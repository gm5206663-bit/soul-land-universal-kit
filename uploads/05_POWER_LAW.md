# 05 — POWER LAW

Anti-nerf, measurement parity, and the ban on plot armor.

---

## 1. THE ANTI-NERF RULE

**Never reduce your protagonist's power to preserve a canon outcome.**

This is binding, not decorative. It is the failure mode that produces the most reader
distrust, because the reader can feel the author's hand.

If your OC is publicly stronger than canon expects, then **public facts cause public
consequences**: paperwork changes, institutions pay attention, training allocation shifts,
trial eligibility moves, rivalries recalibrate. Do not quietly write the OC down so the
canon scene can happen as written.

Concretely, when your OC's power becomes public, ask:

```
- What does the institution record now?
- Who is threatened by this?
- What resource or opportunity opens or closes?
- Which canon character must now treat the OC differently?
```

If none of those change, you have nerfed by omission. Go back.

---

## 2. THE OTHER DIRECTION: NO UNEARNED POWER

Anti-nerf is not a licence. Every gain answers five questions:

```
1. What changed?
2. What caused it?
3. What did they already possess that made this possible?
4. What failed first?
5. What limitation remains?
```

Question 4 is the one everyone skips. A gain that cost nothing and overcame nothing is not
progression, it is a gift from the author.

**Nothing is absolute.** Rank and title labels — King, Emperor, Saint, Douluo — are *estimates
about a fight*, not fixtures. A lower-ranked character with the right counter beats a
higher-ranked one, and the reader should be able to see why afterward.

---

## 3. MEASUREMENT PARITY

Numbers on the page must be consistent with numbers in the ledger, and both must be
consistent with the panel.

Three rules:

**One measurement source.** `STATUS_PANEL.md` states current rank, soul power, and rings.
Nothing else does. Other files derive.

**Public and private values may differ — record both.** A beast recorded at 100 years
publicly and 480 privately is fine, and it is a plot device. A beast that is 100 in one file
and 480 in another with no explanation is an error.

**Every number has a source.** A rank that appears in prose came from a measurement, an
assessment, or a document. Say which, in the ledger.

### Where numbers live

```
PANELS and LEDGERS  → exact figures permitted
PROSE               → round felt counts, spoken or thought by characters
```

A character thinks "he had lived a long time," not "he had lived 743 years." The ledger
carries 743. This is a prose law as much as a measurement law — see `07_PROSE_LAW.md`.

---

## 4. THE RING GATE IS YOUR FRIEND

Soul Land's progression system is a gift and most serials waste it.

You cannot pass a multiple of ten without a soul ring. That means:

- Every tenth level is a **decision point** with real stakes.
- Every ring requires **killing something**, which is a moral event, not a menu selection.
- Every ring's colour tells the reader **what it cost** (white 10 / yellow 100 / purple 1,000
  / black 10,000 / red 100,000).
- Every ring is **public information** — people can see your rings, and they will judge you.

Use all four properties. A ring acquisition is the natural centre of a chapter: the hunt, the
kill, the absorption, the reaction.

---

## 5. NO PLOT ARMOR

Victory, failure and survival each need reasons.

Tests:

- **What could be lost here?** If the answer is nothing, the scene has no stakes.
- **Who could fail?** If nobody can, the scene has no tension.
- **Why isn't this easy?** If you cannot answer, it should be easy — and then it should be
  over quickly.
- **What did it cost?** A victory that cost nothing reads as a gift.

---

## 6. THE POWER AUDIT

Run on every chapter:

```
- Did strength come from an established cause?
- What counters remain?
- Does the world react to what just became public?
- Is any number in prose inconsistent with the panel?
- Has a reserved-for-later power been granted early?
```

That last line is why `STATUS_PANEL.md` carries a **FORBIDDEN NOW** field. Reserve powers
explicitly and the gate can catch a chapter that grants one early. Leave them implicit and
chapter 22 will spend chapter 40's payoff because the scene wanted it.
