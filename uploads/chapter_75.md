Qin Ming did not look back at the gate.

“Blue Tyrant is in the city,” he said. “I have heard the teaching is strict. They take commoners. They do not take nobles. That is the specialty, not a rumour.”

Flender’s mouth found something that had not been there when a prince had said *lousy*.

“Then they can stand across from Heaven Dou Imperial,” he said, “and mean it.”

Dashi’s pencil did not come out. Zhao Wuji made a sound that wanted to be a laugh and had not earned one yet. Shao Xin, Lu Qibin, Li Yusong — the same shape of a mouth, yesterday’s Title still in the teeth.

Rongrong walked. She did not say castle. She had said it yesterday. Flender had said he would not live under someone else’s roof. A clan girl going through a door that spat nobles was a step, not a sentence. Mubai’s princedom stayed in his fists. Zhuqing’s house stayed where it always stayed. Oscar’s box bumped his hip on every other stride. Fatty walked as if a stair were still a legal place to put a body.

Gu Yuan’s hands were in his pockets. Cheap mask. The line under it was a line. Sanpao rode Dashi’s pack and snored like a person who had been told forty and had not become forty. Vision on, because it was on. A city that had filed a black third on a merchant’s paper and in a granddaughter’s mouth. A prince who had never heard of Shrek. Different mouths. He did not make it a speech.

---

Blue Tyrant’s gate had paid to sit on a city wall. The man who met them had a clipboard and the face of someone who had been told to start.

“Yin Shu,” he said. “Examiner. We begin.”

Qin Ming stepped forward first.

The Inferno Grey Wolf came up off his body the way it had come up across a dinner table two months ago, when Gu Yuan had sat opposite it and chosen not to finish a dragon. Yellow, yellow, purple, purple, purple, black. Level sixty-two. The grain was not shock. It was the same Emperor. The same man who had knelt outside a city that morning and said that without the teaching there was no future.

“Qin Ming,” he said. “Power attack. Inferno Grey Wolf. I am here to apply for teacher.”

Yin Shu’s clipboard dipped.

“Spirit Emperors and above are exempt,” he said. “The rule is the rule. You do not need the extra test.”

Flender’s eyes moved once, along his own people.

“Then we are not wasting time,” he said. “Show them.”

Li Yusong’s staff came first — dragon-pattern, a white ring that made Yin Shu’s mouth tighten, then yellow, then the rest, black at the end. “Li Yusong. Sixty-three. Power.”

Shao Xin’s sweet pea put two blacks in the air. Food-type. Seventy-one. Saint. Yin Shu’s clipboard forgot what it was for.

Lu Qibin’s chessboard hung between his hands, sixty-six, control, and Zhao Wuji did not wait to be invited. The Vigorous Vajra Bear took the yard. Yellow, yellow, purple, purple, black, black, black. Seventy-six. Saint. The wood under Yin Shu’s boots remembered a different weight.

Flender went last, because Flender always went last when the purse was watching. Owl. Seventy-eight. Agility. Three blacks. The air over the gate got quieter.

Yin Shu looked at six men who had just made a King into a doorman.

“Deactivate,” he said. His voice had to be found. “Yin Shu. Fifty-four. Power. I cannot rate you. Let me take you to the Dean. She will decide position. And pay.”

Tang San’s mouth ticked, once, the way it ticked when a true thing had arrived without needing a theory. Yesterday a Poison Douluo had made Saints hold a breath. Today the same Saints had made a King step aside. Strength was still the sentence. He did not say seven. He did not need to.

Gu Yuan did not put 幻术 in a fifty-four. He did not 瞳谎步 a recruitment. A door was a door. Pockets. AT silent. Flender watched Dashi from the side and smiled the smile of a man who had already seen the next room.

“Lead,” Flender said.

---

The garden was not the main campus. Dashi asked why, because Dashi asked why when a map was wrong.

“The Dean likes peace,” Yin Shu said. “She stays here. Teachers run the days. Large issues come to her.”

“I understand,” Dashi said.

Then water on flowers, and Dashi’s feet forgot they had a job.

Flender’s hand found the shoulder that had been running.

“When,” Flender said, not loud, “do you stop avoiding. Twenty years, Xiaogang. Can you bear not to see her.”

The eight did not have a name for the voice yet. Yin Shu was already calling toward the house.

“Dean. Spirit Masters applying. Six of them above sixty-one. We cannot decide.”

She was watering.

柳二龙. Dark red. The can forgot the flowers when she saw the two men in the path.

She came the way fire comes when it has been waiting in a grate.

“Xiaogang.” The watering can hung from her fingers and then did not. “Is it you. Am I dreaming.”

Flender got there first because Flender had always gotten there first when Dashi’s mouth would not.

“I didn’t know you were here,” he said. “Years. How are you.”

“You look the same.”

“I’m getting old.” He turned, because an introduction had to be an introduction or it became a wound. “These are the teachers and students of Shrek Academy. We saw a notice on a gate. We came to ask for work.” His hand moved, a small arc that took in Zhao and Shao Xin and eight children who did not fit a house. “This is Liu Erlong. She travelled the continent with us. Last corner of the Golden Iron Triangle. Slaughtering Corner.”

Xiao Wu’s fingers found Tang San’s sleeve. Rongrong’s mouth made a shape that was not a word. Mubai’s shoulders remembered a Title’s glance from yesterday and did not like *slaughter* sitting in a garden. Fatty stood as if a stair would have been easier. Oscar’s box was a box.

Gu Yuan looked.

Fire. Dragon. Not Blue Lightning. Fire. A martial soul that was a dragon-bloodline in a person, watering flowers. Dashi had been told forty and a good dragon-blood ring. This was not that hunt. This was a garden. He did not make her a solution. He did not make a well. The grain sat down.

They greeted. She greeted. Her eyes went over the children and snagged, half a breath, on a cheap mask and a line that was only a line, and then they went on, because an extra student was a chair, not a file.

Flender said, “Are you going to invite us in, or are we applying in the hydrangeas.”

Liu Erlong looked at the house, at the eight, at Flender’s coat that had been a dean’s coat in a village and was a beggar’s coat in a capital.

“Does my house fit this many. What happened to you. When did you fall to this.”

“Long story,” Flender said.

---

Inside, the long story became Heaven Dou Imperial, and fire got mad the way fire gets mad.

“What a great academy,” she said. The cup in her hand had not been poured. “How dare they bully you.” She looked at Flender, not at Xue Xing, who was not there to be looked at. “Don’t worry. My academy is your academy. Everyone stays. This is your place.”

Flender, who had paid ninety percent of a purse to the families of a Fiend team two days ago and had asked at a city gate what a notice cost, put his glasses straight.

“Our pay requirements,” he said, carefully, “are very high. Can you bear it.”

Liu Erlong set the cup down.

“Payment,” she said. “That’s your problem now.”

He stared.

“You’re the boss of the Golden Iron Triangle,” she said. “From now on Blue Tyrant is yours.”

The room did the work of a room that has been given to someone who did not ask.

“Tomorrow I hold a faculty meeting,” she said. “We change the name to Shrek.” She turned her head. “Yin Shu. Announce it. These people are my friends. Canteen, second floor, a feast. And I am Deputy Dean. You will say that when you say the rest.”

Yin Shu said yes as if yes had been trained into him.

Oscar’s stomach remembered it had a job. Fatty’s mouth remembered food the way it remembered a stair.

Flender looked at Dashi.

“Are you still going to remain silent.”

Dashi remained silent.

---

The second floor smelled like oil and peas and a day that had not been a fight.

“We haven’t eaten since morning,” Fatty said, which was a morning that had held a Title, and he sat down as if sitting were still a skill he was allowed. They ate. Eight places. Shao Xin did not put a pea on the table; the canteen had done the work. Rongrong did not offer a clan dish. Qin Ming ate like a man who had resigned from an empire before noon and found a commoner roof by the time the bowls came. Gu Yuan ate because a body eats. Vision on the room: a fire-dragon woman who had given away a school in one sentence; a dean whose purse was already empty and had still asked for high pay; a pig-dragon under the table-line; a brother whose back was about to leave.

Dashi stood.

“Tang San,” he said. “Walk.”

Tang San stood.

Gu Yuan’s chair moved half an inch. Brotherhood. Crises. This was not a spear. This was a twenty-year wound Dashi had chosen one disciple to carry the words of. The half inch went back. Dashi’s glance did not need to be a dismissal. It was a man who had knelt in a yard and still had a story that was his.

At the table Flender turned his glass.

“Aren’t you afraid he’ll run again.”

Liu Erlong watched the garden door.

“Do you think he can still run,” she said, “now that I have seen him.”

Xiao Wu made a sound into her bowl. Rongrong followed it. Mubai, Oscar, Fatty — people who had been told to get out yesterday and had been given a school today. Gu Yuan did not. Hands in pockets under the table. AT filing a fire that was a person.

---

The garden had evening in it.

Dashi did not start with a name.

“You want to ask,” he said. “The relationship. Why it ended like this. Why I avoided her for twenty years. I will tell you a story.”

Tang San said, “Yes.”

“More than twenty years ago a young man with a famous background was rejected by his clan. Mutant martial soul. He left to prove himself. He wanted the world to recognize him. He wanted his clan to. Abroad he met a young Spirit Master with good strength. They became friends. They travelled. In a spirit-beast forest they met a girl.” Dashi’s voice did not decorate it. “Lively. Cheerful. Fire Dragon. She attracted both of them. Neither showed it. Especially the one whose soul had failed him. In time they made a name. Golden Iron Triangle. Feeling that hides gets stronger. One day the strong one proposed. She refused. She loved the weak one who liked to study theoretical knowledge.”

He looked at Tang San.

“You should have guessed. That girl was Liu Erlong. The two men were me and Flender.”

Tang San did not interrupt.

“When I heard it from her I felt a happiness I had not had in my life. Flender withdrew. Readily. He said he would be my brother. She would be his sister-in-law.”

“Flender is a good man,” Tang San said. “Maybe that was the best ending.”

“If it had gone smoothly.” Dashi’s mouth was dry. “Fate is cruel. I remember the night. The stars were exceptionally bright. Flender hosted the wedding. We were happy. The story should have ended. An uninvited guest came. My second uncle. Second Elder of the Blue Lightning Tyrant Dragon Clan. 余洛面.” A pause that was not for effect. “He did not come for me. He came for her.”

“Why.”

“Liu Erlong is his illegitimate daughter. My cousin.” Dashi’s hands were empty of a pencil. “I ran. She tried to follow. He stopped her. I wanted to die. Flender stopped that too. My heart was dead. I could only leave. Later he told me she had fallen out with the uncle and gone looking. I wanted to see her. I could not see her.”

The hydrangeas had a listener in them. Liu Erlong did not announce herself. She had been a corner of a triangle. She knew how to stand in flowers.

Tang San said, quietly, “Is the world’s view really that important. She waited. If you keep avoiding, both of you suffer.”

Dashi looked at the path, not at the flowers.

“It is unfair to her. When we love someone, we don’t have to own them. I hope she can live happily.”

“I don’t care about the world’s view.” Liu Erlong’s voice came from the hydrangeas as if the hydrangeas had been waiting to be a mouth. “I don’t need justice. I just wish to be with you.”

Tang San looked at Dashi. He looked at her. He left them the garden.

He did not tell the story on the path back. He sat down at a table that still had eight places and said, very quietly, “Yuan.”

Gu Yuan said, “Mm.”

Not a drum. A field. A school that would be named Shrek tomorrow because a woman with a watering can had decided it. A man at thirty-eight who had been told forty, whose vitality was going, who had just been asked not to run for another twenty. Sanpao under the table-line. A cheap mask. Somewhere in the city a Title’s granddaughter who had named a plant and a black ring. No well in anyone’s mouth. No forehead. No 八蛛矛. The canteen smelled like oil and peas.

Gu Yuan’s vision had not stolen the garden. It had waited. That was also a job.
