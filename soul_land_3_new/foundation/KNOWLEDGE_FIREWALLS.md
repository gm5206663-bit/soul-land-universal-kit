# KNOWLEDGE FIREWALLS — who may know what, and when it is earned

Rail 6: knowledge crosses a wall only through an on-page, logged route. A breach is
a continuity defect and fails audit. Meta-knowledge (what the AUTHOR knows about
canon) is FW-0 and may never leak into character behavior.

| ID | Wall | Blocked knowledge | Who holds it (canon) | Earned route (examples) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FW-0 | Author → cast | FUTURE canon: Shrek's fall, Wulin/Gu Yue endgame, abyss plans | no character | NEVER for the OC; era-power holders act on in-world evidence only |
| FW-1 | Golden seal | Wulin's Golden Dragon King seals and origin (son of Tang San) | Tang Sect inner circle, Wulin late | deep Tang Sect trust; battlefield witness; late-arc only |
| FW-2 | Silver veil | Gu Yue = Silver Dragon King (Gu Yuena), the beast cause | beast kings, Na'er, late Wulin | bloodline RESONANCE may be sensed dimly by beast-blood OCs (feel, not fact); the FACT stays walled |
| FW-3 | Cult infiltration | Holy Spirit Cult's seats/plants inside legitimate bodies | cult only | evidence chains, captures, defections — each logged |
| FW-4 | Pagoda factions | Qiangu family's deeper agenda; Ascension Platform truths | Pagoda inner seats | employment, clearance, betrayal beats |
| FW-5 | Tang Sect tech | hidden weapons/gray tech | Tang Sect only | membership + rank, never casual display |
| FW-6 | Abyss passage | location/scale of the sealed passage, Blood God Legion mission | military high command | enlistment arcs; need-to-know grades |
| FW-7 | OC's own secret | (fills at R1 — e.g., origin, seal, craft heirloom) | OC ± mentor | reveal COSTS something (lock 6); never accidental, chapter-forty style |
| FW-8 | Separation wall | anything from the Lin Hao branch or other serials | other projects | never crosses (rail 7 / R2 default) |

Logging: every crossing is one line in `foundation/SERIAL_LOG.md` —
`FW-x: <fact> crossed to <character> via <chapter/scene> (date)`. Undated or
unlogged crossings are treated as breaches on audit.

Premise-specific walls (P1 Pagoda experimental stock / P2 Star Luo court secrets /
P3 abyss-mark origin / P4 smith-association trade secrets / P5 beast identity —
the heaviest walling of the six / P6 military tech) activate when R1 lands.
