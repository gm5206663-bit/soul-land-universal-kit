Purpose: kill failure #3 ("no felt progression") and rail 5's twin evils (fiat leaps,
dead seasons). Every gain must have a substrate scene; every scene gain must land in
`STATUS_PANEL.md §2` the same turn.

(spirit-soul purchase, platform trial, contract), never granted between chapters.

## 2. SPIRIT SOULS (this era's rings)

- Ring color = spirit-soul age tier: **white (10y) → yellow (100y) → purple (1,000y)
  → black (10,000y) → red (100,000y)**.
- Sources: Spirit Pagoda products (price-classed; defective stock exists — canon:
  Wulin's Goldsong), Ascension Platform age-raising, rare wild contracts.
- **Firewall:** no OC-first-ring-purple openings without an on-page substrate AND an
  author ruling; era-average first rings are white/yellow. Canon prodigies are the
  exception we must not casually match (anti-centering law).
- Defect/pattern note: a "defective" soul may hide function, but the reveal must be
  earned across chapters, never opened with.

## 3. THE THREE PARALLEL LADDERS (the era's real depth)

Progression in SL3 is three rails, not one. Pick which rails your OC climbs (lock 5).

1. **Soul power ranks** (above).
2. **Craft ranks** — blacksmith 1★–9★ (7 Master / 8 Saint / 9 Divine), or mecha
   engineer classes, or armor designer. Fusion-forging harmony % is the hard metric.
   Craft ranks MOVE on the page (hammers, tests, failed alloys).
3. **Armor track** — one-word → two-word → three-word → four-word battle armor.
   Words are earned with named materials + a Saint-tier crafter + a design; track
   cost in ledgers. Four-word = legend; an OC at four-word needs author ruling +
   arc-length substrate.

Mental-power realms (Spirit Origin → Connection → Sea → Abyss → Domain) gate
multi-soul bonding and control classes; track in STATUS_PANEL only via `[verify]`'d
thresholds.

## 4. MECHA (military mass tech)

Colored classes white → yellow → purple → black → red (red = super-class). Mecha
grade ≠ soul rank; a low-ranked elite pilot with a purple mecha is a real combat
threat to higher ranks — USE this asymmetry for tension; do not let it become fiat.

## 5. BLOODLINES — the suppression law and the ban list

- Dragon > beast suppression is era canon. Beast-bloodline OCs react to Wulin and
  Gu Yue — refer to firewall FW-1/FW-2 for what they may SENSE vs KNOW.
- **HARD BANS (rail 5):** no Golden Dragon King/Silver Dragon King inheritance; no
  second protagonist-grade twin martial soul without author ruling; no "awakened
  god-seat" shortcuts; no absorption/devour mechanics that skip ring economics.

## 6. THE COMBAT-REALISM LAW (rank gaps cost)

- One-word armor ≈ +10–20 ranks of effective threat vs unarmored **[canon-texture,
  verify]**; use it as the measuring stick, not a multiplier slot machine.
- Cross-rank wins need a written reason ON THE PAGE (terrain, prep, craft artifact,
  betrayal, information). A win without a reason is a fiat leap (rail 5 defect).
- The OC loses sometimes. A serial without losses has no stakes (story law 2).

## 7. RECORD FORMAT

Exact figures (rank, ring ages, craft ★, harmony %, armor words, mecha class,
mental-realm points) live ONLY in `STATUS_PANEL.md §2` / footers. Prose carries
felt weight, never spreadsheets (prose law 9). Every figure entry is dated.
