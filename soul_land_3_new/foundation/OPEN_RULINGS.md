## R4 — DIVERGENCE POLICY

| Ruling | Question | Status |
|---|---|---|
| R4 | Fixed canon spine, or Natural-Ripple? | **OPEN** |

- **Fixed spine:** canon events never bend; the OC's story lives in the gaps.
  Safest; lower drama ceiling.
- **Natural-Ripple (recommended):** canon is the spine; it bends only when the ON-PAGE
  simulation earns it, and every bend is logged with cause in the continuity ledger —
  romance included. Nothing pre-chosen (kit natural-ripple principle).
