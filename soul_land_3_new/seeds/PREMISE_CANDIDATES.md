# PREMISE CANDIDATES — pick R1 here, merge, or overrule with your own

Each candidate is built to survive the five failures: a canon anchor (≥5 beats),
a named-face antagonist vector, a visible progression rail set (see POWER_LAW_SL3),
and a NOT list (anti-centering). The author decides; nothing here is locked.

---

## P1 — THE PAGODA'S DEFECTIVE

**Hook:** an orphan raised inside a Spirit Pagoda stock-house is issued a "defective"
experimental spirit soul — the shelf-stock sibling of Wulin's Goldsong luck.
The defect is FUNCTION, not power: the soul records what it kills.
- **Canon anchors:** Pagoda commerce (§4.2), Ascension Platform (§4.3), Shrek exam
  era (§4.4), abyss war late (§4.9), cult infiltration of legitimate bodies (§2).
- **Antagonist with a face:** the stock-house supervisor who wants the recording
  soul back — and the higher seat he answers to.
- **Progression rails:** soul ranks + mental-realm points; craft rail optional.
- **NOT list:** never a combat monster; no ring-skip absorption; the soul never
  grants knowledge FW roads don't earn (it records, it doesn't explain).
- **Risk:** reading soul-recordings can become exposition-on-tap. Cap it: one
  recording per chapter, raw and cryptic (prose law 4).

## P2 — THE STAR LUO REFUGEE

**Hook:** a minor Star Luo house is consumed by a court faction; one child escapes
across the sea to the Federation with a name they can't use.
- **Canon anchors:** Star Luo continent (§2), exchange tournament (§4.6) — from the
  OTHER side, Federation politics, cult court-infiltration (§2), finale (§4.10).
- **Antagonist:** the usurper house's heir, same age, same arena later — a rival
  who was taught the OC is dead.
- **Rails:** soul ranks + one-word armor track; diplomatic craft as soft third rail.
- **NOT list:** no royal bloodline power, no continent-shaking claim; the name is a
  debt, not a kingdom.
- **Risk:** court-revenge melodrama flattening school texture. Cure: entry E1,
  rival lands mid-arc, not ch3.

## P3 — THE ABYSS-TOUCHED

**Hook:** survivor of a small abyssal leak far from the front; the wound left a
cold mark that eats warmth when the OC lies (a curse, not a treasure).
- **Canon anchors:** the Abyss (§2), abyss war (§4.9), Blood God Legion era (§4.8),
  cult's abyss ties (§2), god-level ceiling texture (§4.11).
- **Antagonist:** the containment squad that decides whether the OC is evidence —
  led by a captain who lost a squad to leaks like this.
- **Rails:** soul ranks + military/mecha rail; the mark is a CONSTRAINT register,
  not a ladder.
- **NOT list:** the mark never becomes a power to level (hard ban, rail 5); no
  Golden-Dragon-King-style "seal that's secretly treasure."
- **Risk:** grimdark monotone. Cure: registers law — the mark also creates comedy
  (a lie-detector at the dinner table) as often as it creates horror.

## P4 — THE SMITH'S OTHER APPRENTICE

**Hook:** a forge-row kid apprenticed in the same association circuit as Mu Chen's
line — the kid who was sweeping the floor the day Wulin's first fusion ingot rang.
- **Canon anchors:** fusion forging & smith ranks (§4.5, beats it lives inside),
  Eastsea years (§4.1), Heaven Dou association texture (§2/§3), armor materials
  economy (§5 battle armor), war-material demand (§4.9).
- **Antagonist:** the association's quota-and-contract machine with a face: the
  master who sells apprentices' alloys as his own.
- **Rails:** CRAFT ranks as the primary ladder (rare in the fandom — freshness is
  the selling point) + soul ranks; armor-design late.
- **NOT list:** no Saint-tier talent without decade-substrate; never rivals Wulin's
  ceiling; the alloy never rings for free.
- **Risk:** craft detail drowning story. Cure: the forge decides things — every
  chapter a decision the metal makes worse (scope law).

## P5 — THE BEAST THAT WALKS AS A STUDENT

**Hook:** a remnant spirit beast wearing human form inside the Federation's youngest
generation — while the Silver King builds her cause.
- **Canon anchors:** near-extinction (§2), Star Dou remnant court (§3), Gu Yuena's
  arc (§3/§4.7–4.10), Pagoda-as-enemy (§2), finale (§4.10).
- **Antagonist:** any human friendship — every bond IS the conflict (rail: story
  carries threat+face; here the face keeps changing).
- **Rails:** soul ranks + beast-body second track; mental-realm.
- **NOT list:** never the Silver/Golden King line; never outranks Di Tian's court;
  human-form cost is constant and physical.
- **Heaviest firewalling of the six** (FW-2 adjacency). Only pick with full
  firewall appetite.

## P6 — THE NUMBERLESS

**Hook:** born with innate soul power 0 in the age where soul power is destiny —
the kid who will never awaken, in a world of awakeners, until the machine answers.
- **Canon anchors:** awakening rite era-texture (§5), mecha classes (§4 Power Law),
  Federation military (§2), war economy (§4.9), cult arms race (§2).
- **Antagonist:** the meritocracy itself, plus the mecha-academy examiner who fails
  the OC on principle and becomes the recurring face.
- **Rails:** MECHA class ladder (white→red) as primary — the only candidate whose
  main ladder is not soul ranks; soul ranks stay flat/absent and stay felt.
- **NOT list:** no late "surprise martial soul" — the zero is the point; no god-seat
  ending via tech-godhood without author ruling.
- **Risk:** power-fantasy hunger pushing a mid-arc soul-power retcon. It is banned
  here, in the candidates file, in advance (lock 2).
