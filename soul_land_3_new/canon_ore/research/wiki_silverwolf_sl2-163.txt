ELEMENTAL SILVER WOLF (Sun Moon Empire · Jing Yang Mountain Range · SL2)
- A metal-element soul beast, not ordinary. Feeds on MITHRIL; needs MASSIVE mithril
  to break through its own bottleneck (like humans need soul rings at bottlenecks).
- SIX ELEMENTS: water, fire, earth, wind, light, darkness. Unleashes any at will;
  practically no element can curb it. ONLY THE MOST BASIC ATTACKS per element.
- Race ladder: Silver Wolf (1,000y) -> Silver Wolf King (10,000y, +1 rare advanced
  element) -> Silver Wolf Emperor (100,000y, +1 more, never appeared in canon).
- Rumor: a 10k Silver Wolf King once resisted TWO Soul Douluo, escaped injured.
- Fur/bones = top soul-tool materials; meat delicious, strengthens spirit masters.
