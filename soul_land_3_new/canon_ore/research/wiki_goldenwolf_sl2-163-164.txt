GOLDEN WOLF (Jing Yang Mountain Range · Sun-Moon Empire · SL2)

- Weaker than metal lion/tiger/panther/bear, but MUCH QUICKER; moves in packs.

- REGION LAW: in Jing Yang range, all metal-type beasts fight +~30% ability.
