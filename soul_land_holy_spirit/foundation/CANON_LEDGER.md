  church) is long fallen and the Spirit Pagoda is not yet founded. Canon
  shows awakenings happening but does not name the era's registry
  institution. RESOLUTION for this serial [design]: the empires keep their
  own registries; border-war orphans fall OUTSIDE any registry — Luo Chen
  was never registered by any empire; his awakening in the ruined shrine
  was unwitnessed by any institution. The Cult is the first record of him.
  This is a load-bearing design fact (it is why he can belong to the pit
  with no past) — labelled [design on canon silence].
- RINGS FOR CULT DISCIPLES: canon shows the Cult fields soul masters with
  rings up to Titled Douluo (roster above) but does not detail disciple
  ring-hunts. RESOLUTION [design]: the Cult runs its own hunts out of the
  Evil Demon Forest and its altars — disciples who prove useful are fed
  rings by the organization; the rest hunt or die. Recorded so chapter
  beats never invent a different mechanism.
