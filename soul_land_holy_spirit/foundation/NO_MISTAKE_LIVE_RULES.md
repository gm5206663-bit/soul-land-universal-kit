10. VOICE — WRITE LIKE THE ORIGINAL SOUL LAND (author rulings s10 AND
    s13: "what which angle this story feel like soul land" → RESTART).
    The Soul Land register, not a literary voice in Soul Land clothes:
    (a) SIMPLE, CLEAN, FAST web-novel prose — short paragraphs, direct

        refrains, no paragraph-long meditations;

    (c) THE MECHANICS ON THE PAGE — soul power ranks, methods, rings,
        beasts discussed and shown as mechanics, not metaphors;
    (d) THE PROTAGONIST DEMONSTRATES — he hides his true depths but
        WINS VISIBLY with what he shows (the Soul Land loop:
        underestimate → demonstrate → shock); interior thought stays
        short, sharp, planning;

    NO apparatus: no panels, quote-blocks, footers, anchor lists in
    chapter files; bookkeeping lives only in kit files.
