## META-KNOWLEDGE LAW (this serial's firewall, kit 06 adapted)
- He remembers the SL2 story as a reader — names, arcs, dangers, outcomes.
- KNOWING is not ACTING: every use of foreknowledge must have an in-world
  route (he cannot "know" a person he has never met; he can only act on
  what he has seen, in the shapes memory gives).
- HIS MEMORY INCLUDES THE INTERNET OF HIS OLD LIFE (author ruling): he
  remembers the fan voices as well as the story — the arguments, the
  opinions, the hundred readings of the canon's people (Tang San argued
  a hundred ways and never settled). OPINIONS CAME WITH HIM TOO, and
  opinions are not knowledge: where the book is unsure and the internet
  disagreed, his uncertainty is REAL and stays real — he cannot resolve
  it by wanting to.
- THE TIMELINE PUSHES BACK: every foreknown thing he touches changes; the
  story he remembers degrades as he uses it. What he changes is recorded in
  a DIVERGENCE LEDGER (to be opened at chapter 1).
- He does not know everything SL2 contains — the canon he remembers is the
  canon as told; side lives, back rooms and offstage truths can surprise
  him. Nothing is absolute.
