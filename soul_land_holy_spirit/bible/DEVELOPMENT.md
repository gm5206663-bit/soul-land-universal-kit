# DEVELOPMENT — Luo Chen's arc map
The road, in its locked stages. Details inside each stage are STORY — they
earn themselves on the page; nothing here is granted early (FORBIDDEN NOW
governs).

## STAGE 0 — THE WALK-IN (told in flashes; behind us)
Orphan → awakening (Devouring Dragon + hidden Dragon Heart; innate
fullness) → the hunger → the door with the two-skull coin. Done by
chapter 1's flashback discipline.

## STAGE 1 — THE PIT'S FAVORITE ANOMALY (chapters 1→)
- Rank 0 → the ten-gate. The Cult's methods + the dragon's appetite +
  the Talent's efficiency; the climb shown, not skipped — every rank
  feels its cost and its speed (anti-inflation: the speed IS the design,
  but speed is paid in hunger, attention, and what the Cult asks back).
- The instruction from above lands: he is sorted out of the ordinary
  (the saint-path's shadow: canon's prodigies get groomed; he will be
  groomed and know the shape of the grooming and use it).
- FIRST RING: the Cult feeds useful tools — his first hunt, out of the
  Evil Demon Forest, for a DRAGON-TYPE OR BLOOD-BEARING ring (author
  ruling; like fuel changes him toward what he is). The kill is moral
  event one, and the story does not look away.
- THE WHALE GLUE PROGRAM runs underneath the whole stage (author
  ruling): graded doses, the vessel forged before the gate, the
  interaction of sea-glue and dragon-blood recorded as an experiment.
- The mask deepens: the clean-eater becomes a doctrine of his own making
  — cruelty spoils the meat; the Cult starts to believe him the way
  religions believe useful heresies.

## STAGE 2 — THE CUB OF THE ALTAR (rings two and three, the purple
threshold)
- Usefulness escalates: tasks, missions, the city altars, first contact
  with the empire's interlock (the Mingde Hall's shadow).
- The pervert-streak at its DARK EDGE: desire as temptation, leverage,
  and danger inside a church that trades in minds — scenes and
  consequences, never explicit.
- The book rots: his first real divergences from remembered canon — the
  ledger rows begin to matter.
- Canon adjacency grows: the tournament era approaches; saints get made
  (Tang Ya, Ma Xiaotao shapes); he may meet the shapes before the names.

## STAGE 3 — THE SAINT'S SHADOW (the tournament era, the surfacing)
- The Cult surfaces; the war machinery engages; his position inside it
  is now a load-bearing secret for both sides of his self.
- Zhongli Wu's saint-making eye (canon: he wanted Huo Yuhao as Saint
  Son) — what does the Leader want of a clean-eating dragon?
- The Dragon Heart's first near-exposures; the heart's own hunger begins
  to ask questions the dragon's hunger never asked.

## STAGE 4 — THE ENDING HE REMEMBERS (the fall-horizon)
- Xu Tianran's trap, the war's end, Huo Yuhao's reckoning with the
  Cult — all coming at the book's dates unless he has rotted them.
- The serial's question stands up: does he save the church that fed him,
  burn it from inside, or eat what's left and walk out wearing his own
  name? Author-gated; the road decides in the walking.

## THE CONSTANT THREADS (every stage)
- The five secrets (firewall): reincarnation, the book, the heart, the
  engine, the mask. Each leak, if ever, is earned and costs.
- The private ledger: Shi Tou's name was the first entry. The names he
  keeps are the serial's moral counterweight, and the story spends them
  deliberately.
- Appetite: food, strength, beauty, knowledge, freedom — the man wants,
  and the wanting is the engine of the plot, played straight and played
  dark.
