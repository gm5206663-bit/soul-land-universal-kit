# POWER LAW — this serial's progression rules
Twin law (master §61): NO NERF, NO UNEARNED INFLATION. Every gain answers
five questions: what changed, what caused it, what he already possessed
that made it possible, what failed first, what limitation remains.

## 1. THE TWO ROADS
- THE RING ROAD (the world's road): rank gates at every multiple of ten;
  a ring per gate; the ring's age = its colour = its cost. Rings come
  from kills — his kills, the Cult's hunts, never purchased, never
  gifted without the killing blow's truth (canon: the killer absorbs).
- THE DEVOURING ROAD (his soul's road): kill → devour → convert. The
  species law (DD-03): ~10x the pace of ordinary beasts — HONEST PACE:
  it makes him fast and it makes him hungry, and the hunger is a leash
  the Cult holds and he holds and nobody fully holds. Devouring never
  replaces rings; it deepens what the gates admit.
- The roads never cancel each other. A ring without devouring is a gate;
  devouring without rings has no gate to pass. Both, together, are the
  design.

## 1b. THE FIRST-RING LAW (author ruling — his plan, earned on the page)
- HIS FIRST RING is planned dragon-type or blood-bearing — like fuel
  CHANGES him toward what he already is; strange fuel only fills. The
  ring's AGE is set by what the vessel proves it can carry at the gate,
  never by want: the WHALE GLUE program (canon WG-01..03: whale-brain
  glue, yang-supreme body-forging, mis-sold as aphrodisiac, raises the
  absorbable ring-age; the market misprices it) runs FIRST, graded,
  dosed, recorded — an experiment of glue vs dragon-blood vs engine,
  kept honest.
- Nothing here is granted early: the glue must be bought/earned, the
  hunt must be reached, the gate must be passed. The plan-book lives in
  bible/HIS_PLANS.md; the page pays for every line of it.

## 2. THE HEART'S ACCOUNT
- The Dragon Heart grows on what the dragon's blood trusts — the
  purification principle carried from the dragon serial's DS-10,
  translated to a human soul: lesser fuel feeds, superior fuel changes
  him, nothing mutates him by accident. The heart's steps are author-
  gated; they arrive as EVENTS, never arithmetic.

## 2b. THE SPIRITUAL RIVER (SP-01 canon + author ruling)
- Spiritual power grows naturally with bodily maturation until forty;
  after forty the ordinary decline, soul masters extend to ~60; the
  first soul core must form before forty or never. Realms: Spirit Origin
  → Connection → Sea → Abyss → Domain → Divine Origin.
- HIS river runs full: adult soul + prenatal substrate + the natural
  growth = VERY HIGH spiritual power for his age (author ruling). At
  six: Spirit Connection standing, the Spirit Sea in reach as the years
  give what the years give to everyone — except that his growth is
  riding a grown man's mind, so the realm thresholds arrive in him
  earlier than in anyone the pit has owned.
- Measurement: spiritual power needs its own instruments; the Cult's
  soul-appraisals weigh the SOUL, not the sea — his depth is currently
  UNMEASURED by any eye (firewall: a future scene may measure it, and
  that scene will matter).

## 3. MEASUREMENT
- RANKS AND RINGS: STATUS_PANEL is the single source; prose carries felt
  counts in words only (kit number law).
- Estimates about fights, not fixtures: nothing is absolute — a
  lower-ranked counter beats a higher-ranked soul when the cause is on
  the page, and the reader sees why afterward.
- Public vs private values both recorded: the Cult will measure him;
  what they measure and what is true are two columns in the panel.

## 4. THE PRICE LAW (this serial's own lock)
Every gift the Cult gives is priced. Every ring is a kill he must live
with (or learn to live with — the learning is the story). Every rank
gained inside the church buys the church a claim on him; the claim is
real even when he plans to break it. Power taken from the darkness
always carries the darkness's fingerprints — and the fingerprints are
part of the design, never washed off for convenience.

## 5. ANTI-PLOT-ARMOR TESTS (per chapter)
- What could be lost? Who could fail? Why isn't this easy? What did it
  cost? — all four answered in the chapter's body or its audit, never
  assumed.
- The Cult's methods (drugs, worms, brainwashing) are REAL threats to
  him — the firewall protects his mind as long as it holds, and the
  story must never neuter a canon threat because he is the protagonist.

## 6. THE META-EDGE LAW
- Foreknowledge is an advantage, not a power: it buys preparation, never
  outcomes. Every use of it that touches a canon cause starts a
  divergence row, and the row rots his memory of what came after. By the
  middle of the serial the book he remembers is a book with pages
  missing — that loss is played for real.
