# HIS PLANS — the reincarnator's plan-book (reader-facing reference)
His mind keeps a ledger the Cult will never read. Everything below is
what he INTENDS — not a schedule, not a promise, not a list of things
the story must perform. AUTHOR LAW (s8 ruling): not everything you can
plan you can try to do; THINGS GO NATURALLY ACCORDING TO EVERYTHING. A
plan here may be tried, may wait, may be outgrown, may simply never
find its moment — the serial executes nothing by force, and a plan that
the world refuses is information, not failure. Nothing below is
granted; whatever happens is earned, paid for, or missed on the page.
Plans rot when the book rots (meta-edge law); he knows it and keeps
them loose at the edges.

## PLAN 1 — WHALE GLUE: build the vessel before the gate
- What he knows (the book): whale glue is yang-supreme body-forging,
  mis-sold as an aphrodisiac; the Jubao Pavilion in MING DU once priced
  ten-thousand-year glue as thousand-year because nobody knows what it
  is; Wang Dong'er's childhood dosing let her take a thousand-year ring
  at rank twenty. He is in the same city as the auction house.
- The plan: absorb AS MUCH AS HE CAN, graded — hundred-year stock first
  (market has it; cheap, because the market thinks it is a bedroom
  medicine), thousand-year and above as coin and access grow. Dose,
  absorb, RECORD: the engine keeps the honest ledger of what the glue
  does to this body, this soul, this blood — and he will watch
  especially how the DRAGON'S BLOOD takes to it (a dragon-line body
  forged with whale-blood of the sea: the interaction is an experiment,
  and he runs experiments the way he does everything, with a control and
  a note).
- The purpose: ring-absorption limits scale with BODY and SPIRITUAL
  POWER — his two rivers. The vessel decides the gate. A stronger
  vessel lets him take DEEPER rings earlier, and deeper rings are the
  whole geometry of the road ahead.
- The routes he can see from the pit: coin earned through usefulness;
  the Cult's own sea business (Dongyang City; the Myriad Soul elder's
  sea-beast refining — the church touches the ocean, and the ocean is
  where whales fall); the Jubao Pavilion's ignorance as a discount.
- What can go wrong (kept honest): yang-overload on a child's body if
  the dosing is stupid; the Cult noticing a pit-child buying tonics;
  glue that is fake or low-grade; the market correcting its ignorance
  before he buys his fill.

## PLAN 2 — THE FIRST RING: dragon-type, or blood-bearing
- The principle (his own derivation, the bloodline road translated to a
  human soul): his soul IS the Devouring Dragon — a ring from the
  dragon's own line, or from a beast carrying dragon blood, harmonizes
  where a strange line only fills. Lesser fuel feeds; like fuel
  CHANGES. He wants the first ring to change him in the direction of
  what he already is.
- The candidates he can name: a young DEVOURING DRAGON of the Evil
  Demon Forest — his soul's own species lives in his church's own
  forest (the book's roster of the forest names them: ten-thousand-year
  kinds in the deeps, called Mini Tyrant Dragons, devouring and
  bloodthirsty — and the young of such a line, the hundred-year ones of
  the FRINGES, are the only place a first hunt can breathe); or any
  dragon-blooded beast the Cult's hunt-masters take and he can claim by
  usefulness. What he will NOT touch: the Evil-Eye Tyrant clan at the
  forest's core — the forest's royal house, beloved and terrible; the
  book says what happens to the man who hunts that blood in this wood.
- The ceiling math (honest): ordinary first rings sit low in the
  hundred-year class; exceptional vessels take high hundreds; a
  thousand-year first ring is a legend that needs a forged body — so
  the whale glue comes FIRST, and the ring's age is set by what the
  vessel proves it can carry at the gate, never by want. The target:
  the deepest dragon-line ring the vessel and the hunt allow.
- The position he needs: the Cult feeds rings to useful tools; he must
  be inside the hunt when the right beast dies — which means Plan 4 is
  the door to Plan 2.
- The cost, written down so it is never forgotten: the ring is a taken
  life, and he is a man with a dragon's hunger taking a dragon's years;
  the book never looked away from this price and neither will he.

## PLAN 3 — THE SEA NEEDS A CHART (spiritual technique)
- The river of his spiritual power runs natural to forty — but a river
  without banks floods or wastes itself. The Cult has mind-arts (the
  Sleeping Nightmare Douluo devours mental power; the church breeds
  worms that rewrite memories — minds are its trade). Somewhere in its
  keeping there are cultivation methods for the sea of consciousness.
- The plan: earn library access through usefulness; learn the mind-arts
  of the dark side the way he learns everything — completely, and with
  his eyes open; and defend the sea the same season he feeds it,
  because he intends to live in a church that fishes in minds.

## PLAN 4 — THE USEFULNESS LADDER (the mask's career)
- Clean-eater doctrine as a BRAND: the Cult believes useful heresies;
  he will be its favorite heresy — the evil soul that refuses cruelty,
  explained with a logic the butchers can respect (the hurting spoils
  the meat). Every rung: tasks done perfectly → altar network trust →
  the inner path → the saint-road's shadow, walked on his own feet.
- Defense in the climbing: learn to detect the Cult's own tools — the
  drugs, the memory-worms, the brainwash teas — because he intends to
  be offered them; the engine builds resistance by graded exposure, and
  he will expose himself deliberately, in doses, like the glue.
- The rule of the ladder: never become indispensable for the thing he
  cannot survive losing, and never seem to want the top; the top is
  where the trap is built.

## PLAN 5 — THE CANON CALENDAR (the book's clock, kept in his head)
- The boy with the Spirit Eyes is six somewhere in Star Luo now; at
  eleven he leaves home; the Shrek years follow; the saint-victims
  (Tang Ya, Ma Xiaotao) fall around the tournament era; the church
  surfaces, allies, overreaches, and the emperor's trap waits at the
  end of it.
- His rules of the calendar: stay OUT of the book's gravity until the
  vessel and the rings can stand its weather; watch from inside the
  church, which sits at the dark side of every canon event; and when
  his own actions start rotting the book, re-plan from what is real,
  not from what was written. The calendar is a map of a country he may
  one day have to cross; maps age.

## PLAN 6 — THE RESOURCE MAP (what the book remembers, and where)
- MING DU (his own city): the Jubao Pavilion and its mispriced
  treasures; the three underground powers (one of them, the Xishui
  Alliance, is run by a Cult elder — the church's fingers are already
  in the city's pockets, and he may use fingers that don't know they
  belong to the same hand).
- THE SUN MOON MACHINE: the Mingde Hall, the soul-tool road — the
  empire's interlock with the church is a door that opens both ways;
  soul tools are the one power-system where knowledge beats bloodline,
  and he has knowledge.
- FAR AND PARKED (not for years, written so it is not forgotten): the
  herb-treasures the book names for spiritual power and body; the sea
  beyond Dongyang City; the forest's deep places where the Evil Emperor
  sleeps. Each one parked deliberately, each with its reason.

## PLAN 7 — THE OMINOUS KNIFE: the Soul-Devouring Carving Knife
- What he knows (the book): rank ninety-nine of the carving-knife
  ledger; forged of LIVING METAL; a genius evil soul master's soul-strand
  poured into it; thirty-seven owners, all dead horribly, nine of them
  turned evil; "only the firm of will may wield it, and never often."
  The book's own fate for it: bought by the Spirit-Eyes boy at the
  Starlight Auction House, purged by the necromancer's remnant soul,
  reborn as the Blade of Life's Vigil — a life god-item that heals any
  wound while the sea stands.
- Why he wants it, in his own logic: it is a soul-devouring artifact of
  Living Metal — kin to his own hunger, and a vessel question made
  steel; his soul DEVOURS, the knife DEVOURS, and the dragon's bloodline
  is the kind of thing that does not leave such a chance lying on an
  auction table for someone else. He knows what the book does with it —
  which means taking it is one of the largest divergences available to
  him, and he prices that honestly: the knife is not stolen from a
  person yet, but it is stolen from a STORY, and the story pushes back.
- How a boy of the pit reaches an auction house: he doesn't, not yet —
  the plan is a horizon, and the road to it is coin, rank, and leave to
  travel; the Starlight Auction House stands in STAR LUO TERRITORY (the
  book's geography says so; verify the city before staging), which
  means the knife waits on the far side of the empire that hosts Shrek,
  inside the widest circle of the god's sight (see Plan 9). The
  sequencing is part of the plan: the vessel, the rings, the mask, the
  leave — and only then the knife.
- What can go wrong (kept honest): the curse is real — a soul-strand
  that leads men toward evil is exactly the bait his soul's hunger could
  bite on; the engine can build resistance by graded contact (§43 of the
  master doctrine), but resistance is earned, and the knife will test
  the firmness of his will against the exact thing his road is made of.
  If he takes the knife, the book's Blade of Life's Vigil never happens
  the way it was written — and whatever replaces it is on him.

## PLAN 8 — THE BODY SECT'S SECRETS: for the vessel, and for the heart
- What he knows (the book): the Body Sect hides in the HEAVEN SOUL
  EMPIRE's fortress country; its disciples all carry body souls; its
  secret art grants the SECOND AWAKENING of body souls, gold-grade at
  the summit; eleven Titled Douluo behind one door; and the book says
  the secret CAN travel — the sect master Du Busi gives the Body's
  Secret to the Spirit-Eyes boy before his end.
- Why he wants it: his hidden soul is THE DRAGON HEART — a body-bound
  organ soul; whatever the Body Sect knows about the body's depths, the
  heart is owed a share of it. Body-refining methods stack on the whale
  glue and the training — vessel, river, heart, one design.
- The route he can see: not membership (a Cult-marked child does not
  knock on that door), but the secret's own travel habits — it has
  moved before and will move again; a man who knows where a river
  floods can stand where the water comes. Trade, timing, and patience,
  in that order; the Body Sect acts on its own likes and hates, which
  means it can be met, but never managed.
- Timeline: future — the Heaven Soul Empire is far, the sect is
  secretive, and he is six; the plan is a bearing, not a door.

## PLAN 9 — THE NOT-TO-BE-SEEN LIST
The man from the book knows who is WATCHING this world, and he keeps
the list the way a thief keeps the map of the lamps.
- FIRST, TANG SAN — and the uncertainty is the point (author ruling).
  He is NOT SURE about the god, for two reasons he cannot resolve:
  first, his old life's INTERNET was full of voices about Tang San —
  the fans of his first world argued the god a hundred ways (schemer,
  father, hypocrite, protector, chess-player), and the arguments never
  settled, so no verdict came with him through the rebirth; second,
  he cannot guess what Tang San knows — whether a god's sight could
  ever read a reincarnator like him, whether the divine notices the
  out-of-world at all, the book never said and the fans never agreed.
  What manner of person the god truly is, and what the god knows of
  HIM, are the two questions he has no way to answer — so the ruling
  is the only one an uncertain man can take: NEVER FIND OUT IN
  PERSON. No Tang Sect (not now, and
  not for a long while — its hidden weapons, its people, its altars of
  memory are all places the god's eye may rest); no contact with the
  god's bloodlines (the White Tiger line is the god's family by the
  boy's father, the Wang child is the god's daughter wearing a boy's
  face — he knows BOTH, and he keeps away from both); nothing offered
  to any sea god's altar, ever.
- SECOND, THE BEAST GOD'S COURT: Di Tian watches the Star Dou Forest;
  the deep beasts keep their own lists; a dragon-souled human who draws
  a dragon-line ring had better never be read by the forest's depth
  before he can stand it.
- THIRD, THE CHURCH'S OWN SUMMIT: the Holy Emperor of the doctrines,
  the Limit Douluo above — inside the Cult, the higher the eye, the
  deeper it reads. His usefulness climbs; his exposure must climb
  slower.
- The law of the list: power is not the absence of being seen; power
  is choosing WHEN. The list shrinks one lamp at a time, and only as
  the vessel, the rings, and the mask allow.

## THE LEDGER BEHIND THE PLANS
Shi Tou's name was the first entry. The plans are how the man keeps the
boy alive; the ledger is how the boy keeps the man human. Both are kept
in the dark, and both are his.
