# ERA STATE — what the world looks like at our start (DL ~12667)
All facts canon-verified unless tagged [design]. Sources: CANON_LEDGER.

## THE HOLY SPIRIT CULT — order of battle
- Leader: ZHONGLI WU (Death God Demon Puppet soul; rank 98; son of the
  founder-line; the man who will one day try to make Huo Yuhao his Saint
  Son — and die in the Evil Demon Forest for the Cult's ending).
- Supreme Elder behind him: YE XISHUI (Limit Douluo, Death God Douluo,
  class-9 soul engineer; the true power; the woman whose concealed Bright
  Phoenix soul is the Cult's deepest secret).
- Chief Worship: LONG XIAOYAO (Dark Holy Dragon, Limit Douluo — a dragon
  soul at the Cult's right hand; the walk-in will eventually stand before
  a dragon-line elder, and neither will know it yet).
- Deputy Leader: FENG LING (Evil Phoenix; Zhongli Wu's wife).
- Worships and elders: Zhang Peng (Scorpion Tiger), Wu Yun (Dark Crow),
  Nangong Wan (Heavenly Centipede — also runs the Xishui Alliance, one of
  Ming Du's three underground powers), the Nether/Netherworld/Myriad Soul/
  Abomination/Masked/Sleeping Nightmare Douluo — stationed across the
  empires, Dongyang City, even the Tianhun imperial palace.
- Above the roster, a name only: THE HOLY EMPEROR — known only from
  doctrine and the mouths of the Great Holy Spirits of the branch altars.
  [canon — the Cult's figurehead mystery; story-use author-gated]

## THE EMPIRES
- Sun Moon Empire: ascendant, soul-tool-powered; capital MING DU (Radiant
  City), near the west; the Cult's HQ under the Evil Demon Forest beside
  it; the Mingde Hall (Jing Hongchen) already interlocked with the Cult in
  canon's later events.
- Star Luo, Heaven Soul, Douling: the three native empires the Sun Moon
  war will come for; Shrek Academy stands in Shrek City (Star Luo).
- Shrek: alive; Tang Sect: declined to almost nothing (canon's opening
  state).

## THE FORESTS AND THE BEASTS
- Star Dou Great Forest: Di Tian the Beast God (~811,000 yrs) supreme —
  carried over from the dragon serial's verified era-math.
- Evil Demon Forest: the Cult's hunting ground and shield. THE EVIL
  EMPEROR rules it — the Evil-Eye Tyrant Sovereign, ~800,000 years,
  second of the Ten Great Fierce Beasts, a 300-meter eye with
  eighty-one tentacles, the Sun Moon Empire's totem, and a partner of
  the Holy Spirit Cult itself; his Evil-Eye Tyrant clan holds the core,
  and his beloved son (a 100,000-yr tyrant) lives there too — the
  forest's royal house is not to be touched. The DEEP places hold the
  ten-thousand-year kinds — AMONG THEM THE DEVOURING DRAGON, his soul's
  own species, called the Mini Tyrant Dragon, bloodthirsty, devouring,
  Devouring Heaven and Earth for its innate art; a 20,000-year specimen
  roams the forest in the book's years. The FRINGES hold the hundred-
  year kinds — where a first hunt can breathe, and where a boy's first
  ring will be taken nowhere near the deeps.

## THE REGISTRY GAP [design on canon silence]
- Spirit Hall fallen ~1,000 yrs; Spirit Pagoda not yet founded. The empires
  keep their own registries; border-war orphans fall outside all of them.
  LUO CHEN WAS NEVER REGISTERED — the Cult is the first record of him, and
  that is why the pit can own him with no past to dispute.
