# CANON CALENDAR — the complete SL2 timeline and events map
Purpose: the serial's Plan 5 (the canon calendar). Everything the serial
may touch, when it happens, and what it means for Luo Chen. Dates use
the serial's DL design clock (birth year DL 12661 = Huo Yuhao's birth
year; Luo Chen and Huo Yuhao are the SAME AGE — his age IS the canon
clock's hand). Confidence flags: [canon] = novel-certain; [canon-loose]
= happens, exact year floats in the sources; [design] = serial ruling.

THE NOVEL'S OWN CLOCK IS LOOSE — the book never keeps a strict calendar;
sources disagree by a year or two in places. Rows marked [canon-loose]
may drift ±1 year. The serial writes nothing that a one-year drift can
break (plans-law: intentions, not schedules).

## PART TWO — THE YEAR-BY-YEAR MAP (the canon clock)
=====================================================================
The boy's age = Luo Chen's age = Huo Yuhao's age. DL year = 12661 + age.

### AGE 0–10 (DL 12661–12671) — the hidden years
- Both children are born the same year [design anchor]. Huo Yuhao in
  the Star Luo White Tiger Duke's house; Luo Chen in the border-war
  orphanage, then the walk-in at SIX (DL 12667).
- ~age 10 [canon]: HUO YUN'ER dies — the Duke's house kills her by
  inches. Huo Yuhao's hatred is forged.
- SERIAL STATE NOW: DL 12667, age 6 — ch1–3. The boy is in the pit,
  then the house under the forest. Canon is SILENT here — everything
  the serial does at ages 6–11 is its own country (the divergence
  ledger's free ground).

### AGE 11 (DL 12672) — the story opens
- Huo Yuhao leaves the Duke's house at RANK TEN; in the Star Dou
  Forest the MILLION-YEAR HEAVENLY DREAM ICE SILKWORM fuses into him
  (first ring, million-year, wisdom ring); Electrolux's grey divine
  thought lodges in him; he meets TANG YA and BEI BEI, joins the
  fallen Tang Sect. [canon]
- SERIAL HOOK: the year the book starts, the boy is eleven and the
  Cult's knife of him is five years old. Whatever the house has made
  of him by then must stand the first ring's test — the hunt-master's
  road is in his near future.

### AGE 12 (DL 12673) — Shrek
- Shrek Academy; WANG DONG (Tang Wutong in disguise) is his roommate;
  the martial-soul FUSION discovers itself (Radiant Wings); the soul-
  tool path opens (Xiao Xiao, Xu Sanshi, Jiang Nannan around him).
  [canon]
- SERIAL NOTE: god's daughter and the book's hero share a room — the
  not-to-be-seen list is standing on its strongest leg.

### AGE 13 (DL 12674) — the first tournament year
- THE FIRST ALL-CONTINENTAL SOUL MASTER TOURNAMENT: the main team is
  wounded by evil soul masters on the road; the reserves (Huo Yuhao
  among them) fight up and TAKE THE CHAMPIONSHIP; the NEW SHREK SEVEN
  are named (Bei Bei, He Caitou, Xu Sanshi, Jiang Nannan, Xiao Xiao,
  Huo Yuhao, Wang Dong). [canon]
- THE EXTREME NORTH: the ICE EMPEROR becomes his second martial soul.
  [canon]
- THE TREASURE APPRAISAL ASSEMBLY (Star Luo): at the auctions he buys
  WHALE GLUE and the SOUL-DEVOURING CARVING KNIFE surfaces in the
  market's world (the knife's purchase is placed by most sources in
  the MING DU Starlight Auction House during the later exchange years
  — flagged below). Dai Huabin's assassins hunt him; his domain wakes.
  [canon; knife/auction placement canon-loose]
- MA XIAOTAO's evil fire is suppressed by his extreme ice. [canon]
- SERIAL HOOK (PLAN 1): THIS is the canon proof that whale glue is
  bought openly at auction, cheap, mispriced — the market's ignorance
  is a standing discount. And the knife is MOVING through the world.

### AGE 14 (DL 12675) — destiny and grief
- The THREE-EYED GOLDEN NI (the Imperial Auspicious Beast) is taken;
  the fierce beasts trade for it; Huo Yuhao's THIRD RING + the
  DESTINY EYE. [canon]
- MU EN DIES; the soul in the Golden Tree; his dying word: soul tools.
  [canon]
- THE BODY SECT strikes Shrek; in the chaos Huo Yuhao lifts the SNOW
  EMPRESS embryo from under the world's eye. [canon]
- AT THE HAOTIAN SECT: ELECTROLUX BURNS HIS DIVINE THOUGHT TO MAKE THE
  FIRST SOUL SPIRIT (the Snow Empress); the Ice Emperor becomes a baby
  soul spirit; the third martial soul (necromancy) is bequeathed.
  [canon]
- SERIAL HOOK (PLAN 8): the Body Sect moves openly this year — their
  secondary-awakening secrets walk the continent. The vessel's road
  has a real address in Heaven Soul country.

### AGE 15–16 (DL 12676–12677) — the exchange, MING DU
- The two-year exchange at the SUN MOON ROYAL SOUL ENGINEERING
  ACADEMY: XUAN ZIWEN, JU ZI; the SILVER MOULDED WOLF KING fourth
  ring; the MINGDE HALL; the Body Sect's Du Busi raids the Mingde Hall
  and the 100,000-year SOUL BEAST EMBRYO bursts — its origin-force
  enters Huo Yuhao's spirit sea. [canon]
- THE STARLIGHT AUCTION HOUSE OF MING DU: the Soul-Devouring Carving
  Knife — nine owners dead, the market afraid — is bought by Huo
  Yuhao and purified by Electrolux into the BLADE OF LIFE'S VIGIL.
  [canon; year canon-loose, placed here by most readings]
- He returns to Shrek carrying Xuan Ziwen, Ji Juechen, Jing Ziyan —
  poaching the empire's future in broad daylight. [canon]
- SERIAL HOOK (PLANS 6–7): Ming Du is the Cult's own city, and the
  boy's church owns ground in it. The knife's purchase-year is the
  year a careful road must NOT cross Huo Yuhao's path — the knife is
  spoken for by the book itself; Plan 7 is a plan for what the book
  leaves lying down, priced for divergence.

### AGE 17 (DL 12678) — the girls and the sea
- WANG QIU'ER arrives at Shrek (the Auspicious Beast wearing a face).
- THE SEA GOD'S EDGE MATCHMAKING: Wang Dong's woman-shape uncovered —

- SERIAL NOTE: the book's heart-line is forming; the Cult's saint-

### AGE 18 (DL 12679) — the second tournament, IN MING DU
- THE SECOND ALL-CONTINENTAL TOURNAMENT — held in MING DU, sects

- THE MING DU BLOW-UP: the underground arsenal is bombed; the old SUN
  MOON EMPEROR dies in the chaos; XU TIANRAN ascends; contestants are
  imprisoned; the QIANKUN EMOTION-ASKING VALLEY. [canon]
- THE RESCUE: toward the Cult-held hostages; TANG YA (a Holy Maiden
  now, long-corrupted) is found in the Cult's hands. [canon]
- SERIAL HOOK: the Cult is NO LONGER hidden after this year — the
  church the boy serves steps into the light of the continent.

### AGE 18–19 (DL 12679–12680) — sacrifice, tide, tower
- WANG QIU'ER'S SACRIFICE — the Auspicious Beast gives her all to him.
- THE EVIL DEMON FOREST, FIRST ENTRY: the 100,000-YEAR EVIL-EYE TYRANT
  — the Evil Emperor's BELOVED SON — falls to Huo Yuhao (seventh ring,
  head soul bone); the Evil Emperor's killing blow is escaped by the
  destiny-bone's grace. [canon]
- THE SECOND BEAST TIDE: Di Tian's rage strikes Shrek; Mu En's remnant
  soul and Xuan Ziwen seal the beast god; the SOUL SPIRIT IDEA is
  offered in the tide's smoke — THE SPIRIT PAGODA IS FOUNDED. [canon]
- THE ARMY ARC: Huo Yuhao serves under Star Luo; DAI LUOLI the
  brother; he saves DAI HAO his father; meets YE XISHUI the Death God
  Douluo and escapes her by a hair. [canon]
- SERIAL HOOK: THE FOREST THE BOY LIVES IN IS THE FOREST THAT WILL
  BLEED. The Evil Emperor's son dies in these trees by the book's
  hero's hand; the Evil Emperor's wrath will bend the whole forest.
  The plan-book reads the calendar: what is done TO the forest, the
  forest remembers; a boy of the house must be deep, useful, and
  nowhere near the surface when the book comes to collect.

### AGE 19–20 (DL 12680–12681) — the war's opening

### AGE 20 (DL 12681) — the sea again, and the cores

- Tang Sect rebuilt on pills; Xuan Ziwen reaches the Limit; the

### AGE 20–21 (DL 12681–12682) — the emperors and the knife
- The PHOENIX SOUL ENGINEER CORPS infiltration; the DRAGON EMPEROR

- Sun Moon betrays YE XISHUI; LONG XIAOYAO and YE XISHUI destroy each

- THE HEAVEN-WORSHIP ASSASSINATION: DU BUSI and the Dou Ling super

- SERIAL HOOK (PLAN 8): Du Busi — the Body Sect's summit — spends

### AGE 21–22 (DL 12682–12683) — the burning cities
- The UNDEAD PLANE raids: city after city, granary after granary,

  army to Shrek's wall and asks for Huo Yuhao's head; the YIN-YANG
  SECOND SOUL CORE. [canon]
- XU TIANRAN traps Ye Xishui and Long Xiaoyao in the Ming Du palace

  exact blow; the throne passes to the child-emperor with Ju Zi

- SERIAL NOTE: Ming Du burns. The altar city of the serial's opening
  is inside that fire's reach — the Cult's surface properties are
  about to become liabilities. The boy's investments must be in the
  things that survive a city's burning.

### AGE 22 (DL 12683) — THE HOLY SPIRIT ENDS
- THE SECOND ENTRY OF THE EVIL DEMON FOREST: HUO YUHAO ANNIHILATES
  THE HOLY SPIRIT CULT AT ITS HEADQUARTERS — the headquarters under
  the trees where this serial's boy is being raised; and he slays the
  EVIL EMPEROR himself, the eight-hundred-thousand-year sovereign of
  the forest, taking the ninth ring of the Spirit Eyes and the time-
  space external soul bone from the remains. [canon]
- The Cult's survivors scatter; the five-year peace is made with Ju
  Zi; Huo Yuhao closes himself away. [canon]
- SERIAL HOOK — THE GREAT CLOCK: this is the day the boy's church
  dies, in the house's own forest, by the book's own hero. Everything
  in the plan-book is downstream of surviving this day USEFUL, FED,
  RINGED, DEEP, and UNKNOWN TO THE MAN WHO DOES IT. The Cult's fall
  is not the boy's fall — the Cult's fall is the boy's GRADUATION,
  if the ladder is climbed right and the not-to-be-seen list holds.

### AGE 22–24 (DL 12683–12685) — the ending and the sky
- Ju Zi breaks the five-year word: Heaven Soul is finished; Star Luo
  stands with only its capital; she asks Star Luo for the White Tiger
  Duke's head. [canon]
- THE LAST STAND AT STAR LUO CITY: Huo Yuhao vs Di Tian, the peak of
  the continent; the Emotion God's power fused; the secret of XU
  YUNHAN (the boy-emperor is Huo Yuhao's son, born of the borrowed-
  seed night) stands in the light; the war stops. [canon]
- Three years of seclusion; the third soul core; ASCENSION with Tang
  Wutong — the Emotion God's seat. The six soul spirits go free.
  [canon; the novel's inner clock is loosest here — total age at
  ascension ~23–25 depending on source]

## PART THREE — THE THREAD MAP (who moves when)
=====================================================================

rebuilt on pills and soul tools → war's arsenal → victor's table.

FOREST THREAD: the Evil Emperor's kingdom unchallenged → his son
slain in the forest (18–19) → his wrath on Shrek (the tide) → HE
FALLS in his own forest (22) → the forest's crown gone — the beast
politics of the continent reshuffle toward the Spirit Pagoda age.

DIVINE THREAD (the not-to-be-seen list): Tang San sees the new star
at the story's opening; Tang Wutong is DOWN from before chapter one;
the Emotion God names his heir at 20; ascension closes the book. THE
RULE FOR THE SERIAL: no lamp of this thread ever turns toward Luo
Chen while the author keeps the list shut.

## PART FOUR — THE SERIAL'S READING OF THE CLOCK
=====================================================================
WHERE THE SERIAL STANDS: DL 12667, age six, day twelve in the house.
The canon clock says: five years of silence before the book opens
(free ground); fifteen years before the Cult dies in the boy's own
forest. The plan-book's arithmetic, honestly:
- RINGS (Plan 2): first hunt ~ages 7–9 (house measure + hunt-master);
  the dragon-line choice; the vessel program running all along.
- WHALE GLUE (Plan 1): canon buys it openly at auction in year 13 of
  the clock — the boy needs coin and a city's leave long before he
  could walk to Ming Du's Jubao Pavilion unseen; the Cult's sea
  business (Dongyang City) is the nearer door.
- THE KNIFE (Plan 7): the book's hero buys it at the Starlight
  Auction House in Ming Du around years 15–16 — it is SPOKEN FOR by
  canon; any move on it is a divergence paid in full, and the plans-
  law says a plan the world refuses is information, not failure.
- BODY SECT (Plan 8): Du Busi spends himself at year 20–21; the
  window for their secrets closes THERE.
- THE FOREST'S FUTURE (canon-derived): after year 22 the forest's
  crown is gone — a boy who has spent fifteen years being useful
  under those trees, ringed and deep and unknown, stands in a forest
  with no emperor. That is the graduation the plan-book walks toward.
- THE CULT'S FALL (year 22): the boy must be nowhere the book looks,
  valued by no one the book interrogates, and holding nothing the
  book confiscates. Everything he builds must be built in the private
  place — the place the house will never keep.
