# KNOWLEDGE FIREWALLS (kit 06) — who knows what; locked through session 2

## THE NAME AND ITS LAYERS
- LUO CHEN — the orphan's name; the only name that is his.
- The Cult will name him something else (recruit-number, disciple-name,
  saint-title if it comes) — every name they give him is a collar; the
  story keeps count of which name answers in which scene.

## HE (the reincarnator) MAY KNOW
- The SL2 story AS A READER: arcs, names, dangers, outcomes — fallible
  memory, the shape stories leave, not omniscience.
- What his own senses and memory of his new life have given him.

## HE MUST NOT KNOW WITHOUT AN EARNED ROUTE
- Anything the story never told: back rooms, side lives, offstage truths —
  these can SURPRISE him (nothing is absolute; canon-as-told ≠ canon).
- Current-day specifics his reader-memory never held (exact dates, minor
  names, local people) — only what the page of his new life shows him.

## THE CULT MAY KNOW / MUST NOT KNOW
- May (as earned on page): his soul, his hunger, his rank, his usefulness.
- Must not (without earned route): that he is a reincarnator; that he knows
  their ending; the Dragon Heart; the Adaptation Talent; that his obedience
  is a mask. These five are the serial's loaded chamber — each leak must be
  earned, on page, with a cost.

## THE GOD'S SIGHT (author ruling — the not-to-be-seen list)
- TANG SAN (ascended Sea God / Asura): sees the lower world through his
  descendants, his sect, his arrangements. HE MUST NOT SEE LUO CHEN.
  The reincarnator's uncertainty is double and unresolvable (author
  ruling): the internet of his old life argued the god a hundred ways
  and never settled what manner of person he truly is, and neither the
  book nor the fans ever said whether a god's sight can read an out-
  of-world soul at all — so he cannot guess what Tang San knows of him,
  and refuses to find out in person. Firewall: no
  Tang Sect, no god-bloodlines (the White Tiger line; the Wang child),
  no sea god's altars, nothing that reports upward to the divine.
- The ruling is a STANDING ABSOLUTE until the author opens it.

## CANON CHARACTERS
- Huo Yuhao, Tang Ya, Bei Bei, the Shrek cohort, Ye Xishui, Zhongli Wu,
  Long Xiaoyao: know nothing of him before the page gives them a route.
- His meta-knowledge of THEM never shortcuts the firewall the other way:
  he may act on foreknowledge only through in-world routes (kit 06 §3).

## THE TIMELINE ITSELF
- Every foreknown thing he touches changes; the story he remembers degrades
  as he uses it. The DIVERGENCE LEDGER (opens chapter 1) records each
  change with cause → effect. No forced canon, no forced divergence.
