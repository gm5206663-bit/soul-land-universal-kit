# PLACES (codex — grows from what happens on the page)

## THE NOVICE PIT, Ming Du altar [design on canon base HS-02]
A long hall cut from living rock under the altar city; forty-one children
= the Cult's smallest investment; the morning catechism, the midday bowl,
the evening lesson. The altar city sits over the Cult's true headquarters
in the Evil Demon Forest near Ming Du (canon); the pit has never seen the
forest and does not know what it sleeps under. He does.

## THE CAMP COUNTRY (the before) [design]
The border wars made him alone; the camp burned by men with cold shining
things — not in any book he remembers; the first lesson of the new life:
the edges of the book are sharp. The ruined shrine where the awakening
happened.

## THE HOUSE UNDER THE FOREST [design on canon base HS-02; unstaged — old-register staging parked s13]
Where the instruction sends the chosen of the pit — down the road at the
altar city's far edge, into the Evil Demon Forest, at the edge of the
Cult's true headquarters under the trees. The pit sleeps on top of it and
does not know; the house is where the church keeps the hunger it can
feed.
OLD-REGISTER STAGING (parked s13, reusable): a long low building grown half into the roots of a vast
tree — roots the ribs, bark the walls, a barnacle in the shadow of a
ship; windows low and orange; broth and herbs, and under them the
sweetness of the welcome. Inside: the common room (nine mats, the fire,
the bench), the work yard (beaten ground), the herb room (shown from the
doorway, not entered), THE LITTLE DOOR in the back wall — locked, leads
down; "the kitchen," says Thirteen, with the fear kept. Ten knives with
Luo Chen. Mother Wei keeps it; the cup comes at the evening bell; the
monthly measure sorts the climbing, the standing, and the going-down.
The forest stands at the edge of the yard like a country never unbent.
