## A. MING DU — the city over the boy's head
- CAPITAL of the Sun Moon Empire; the largest city of the continent's
  four empires; soul-tool civilization at its densest there [canon].
- The SECOND ALL-CONTINENTAL TOURNAMENT is held in Ming Du (canon
  year 18 of the clock), with the underground soul-engineer
  tournament run under it by Sun Moon and the Cult together [canon].
- THE HOLY SPIRIT CULT HEADQUARTERS stand under the EVIL DEMON
  FOREST at Ming Du's edge (HS-02; EF ledger) — the boy's church is
  a suburb of the empire's capital.
- AUCTION HOUSES OF THE CITY: the STARLIGHT AUCTION HOUSE (where the
  Soul-Devouring Carving Knife is bought by Huo Yuhao — its placement
  in Ming Du is the majority reading, flagged canon-loose) and the
  JUBAO PAVILION (the mispriced whale-glue house of WG-03 — placement
  in Ming Du per the whale-glue receipt, flagged canon-loose). The
  interior look of the city is design ground: the serial gives it
  steel, lamps, and soul-tool hum where the page needs texture
  [design on canon silence].

## B. HUNTING GEOGRAPHY — where rings come from
- THE THREE GREAT SOUL BEAST GROUNDS [canon]: the STAR DOU GREAT
  FOREST (continent's center, spans three empires; periphery ten-/
  hundred-year kinds, mixed zone hundred-/thousand-/ten-thousand-year,
  core ten-thousand-year+ and the fierce beasts — the gradient is the
  universal law: the deeper, the older); the EVIL DEMON FOREST (Sun
  Moon's first forest, a FORBIDDEN ZONE by imperial decree because of
  the Evil Emperor; it stands DUE WEST OF THE JINGYANG MOUNTAINS);
  the EXTREME NORTH (more 100,000-year kinds than anywhere).
- THE JINGYANG MOUNTAINS [canon]: north-central Sun Moon, vast,
  mineral-rich; beasts there feed on metal energy — the GOLDEN WOLVES
  that eat rare metal, and spirit kinds like the SILVER MOULDED WOLF
  KING (ten-thousand-year). The nearest ordinary hunting country to
  Ming Du that is not forbidden.
- PENNED FORESTS [canon SL1 concept; Sun Moon specimens design]:
  empires keep ROYAL HUNTING FORESTS of raised gentle low-grade
  beasts for first rings (the SL1 Spirit-Hunting Forest needed a
  Spirit Hall token). The Sun Moon keeps such grounds too [design on
  canon pattern] — this is where ordinary children get white and
  yellow rings, and where a knife with no name would never be taken.
- THE MING DOU MOUNTAINS [canon]: the Star Luo–Sun Moon border range;
  bandit and front-line country (the Envoy of Death affair; the later
  war's first front).

## C. THE SHREK SUPERVISORY CORPS (the hunters of his kind) [canon]
- Shrek's continent-wide arm for the atrocities no state will touch —
  FIRST AMONG THEM EVIL SOUL MASTERS. Inner-court students must
  complete THIRTY supervisory missions to graduate.
- They work masked — dedicated robes, masks, cloaks, and a signal
  flare that brings every Shrek student who sees it running. Their
  judgments can make a soul master's name: they once hunted the
  misjudged Wu Yuan, and the continent let them.
- THE ENVOY OF DEATH AFFAIR (Huo Yuhao's first mission, canon year
  13): an evil soul master bandit corps in the Ming Dou Mountains —
  the ambush of the corpse-bombs; one dead, six wounded; Ma Xiaotao
  slew the leader. The boy who wins the book read this ledger young.
- WHY IT MATTERS TO THE SERIAL: the world's answer to an evil-soul
  child is not the academy gate — it is the Supervisory Corps. The
  Cult is the only roof between the boy and that; the boy knows it,
  and the knowing is half of his obedience. His law holds: never
  anything in daylight that a mask-wearer could report — beasts, not
  men, and no evil art in any open place (Plan 4's graded exposure).

## D. SERIAL APPLICATIONS
- THE FIRST HUNT (Plan 2 geography): the Cult hunts in the Evil Demon
  Forest's FRINGES under the Evil Emperor's partnership [design
  inference on EF-01 + HS ledger] — a hundred-year kind from the
  fringe is a first ring the forest itself permits; the Jingyang
  Mountains stand as the alternative ground if the story wills. The
  penned forests are the road he will never be given — and never
  needs.
- SOUL BONES: the boy's long ledger (POWER_CANON §G) — a dragon-line
  kill at the gate may carry more than a ring; the serial does not
  grant what the page has not paid for.
- MONEY: everything coin-shaped in the serial prices in gold/silver
  soul coins (POWER_CANON §I); the whale-glue program needs both coin
  and city-leave (Plan 1).
