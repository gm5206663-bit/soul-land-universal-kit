## C. THE AWAKENING / MEASUREMENT RITE [canon SL1 rite; cult variant design]

  light, right hand extended, the soul manifests behind/in the child);
  the SOUL CRYSTAL BALL then measures innate soul power — light fills

- The Cult runs the same instruments with its own liturgy [design]:
  the altar appraisal hall keeps awakening stone and crystal ball; the

## D. THE EVIL SOUL MASTER LAW (the serial's native country) [canon]
1. DEFINITION (the wide standard): an evil soul master is any soul
   master whose soul is evil-natured — REGARDLESS of how they
   cultivate — or who cultivates by devouring. Seven recognized paths
   into it: (a) INNATE evil soul (born with it); (b) MUTATION of a
   clean soul into evil; (c) CORRUPTION by another's evil work (Ma
   Xiaotao — her fire phoenix darkened into the evil-fire phoenix);
   (d) SELF-FALL through devouring cultivation (Tang Ya — her Blue
   Silver Grass's devouring attribute woke with her rage); (e) FUSION
   with an evil soul; (f) INFECTION by contact with evil soul masters;
   (g) INFECTION by evil-soul methods striking/healing the body.
2. THE ENERGY: evil soul power is a mutated, corrosive energy — not an
   element; it feeds on blood, flesh, souls, soul power, spiritual
   power, negative emotion [canon shape; the author's s14 breakdown].
3. THE BARGAIN: before Titled Douluo, evil cultivation is FASTER than
   honest cultivation — and it is a trap: the soul HUNGERS for what it
   feeds on; the hunger grows with the feeding; stop feeding and the
   evil energy backfires in agony; the mind corrodes with the power —
   paranoia, sadism, madness. Few evil soul masters reach Titled
   Douluo: the hunger spends them or the hunters catch them [canon].
4. THE HUNTERS: Shrek's Supervisory/Enforcement wing hunts evil soul
   masters (canon: the Blood Infant evil soul master case); LIGHT-
   attribute souls counter evil souls (Ye Guyi's Sacred Angel is built
   to kill them) [canon].
5. THE TRAGEDY: many evil souls never chose it — they are BORN
   mutated, and normal society rejects them; the Holy Spirit Cult
   finds them, collects them, and gives them the only door left open.
   This is the Cult's recruitment ENGINE and the serial's pit [canon].
6. EVIL SOUL CATEGORIES (derived): corpse-control, spirit-wielding,
   beast-driving (by evil methods), demon-expulsion, evil-summoning,
   blood-working [canon list]. The Devouring Dragon reads DEVOURING
   TYPE — closest canon kin: the Dark Demon Evil God Tiger, which can
   only advance by devouring [canon beast example].

## G. SOUL BONES (the second harvest of a kill) [canon]
- FORMATION: a soul bone forms only from a powerful soul beast dying
  under special conditions — NOT every beast yields one; it is the
  beast's SOUL CORE turning into its most characteristic part
  (spirit kinds → skull; strength kinds → arms; speed kinds → legs;
  all-around kinds → torso). Ordinary drop chance ≈ one in a thousand.
- THE SIX PARTS, strongest first: TORSO, SKULL, RIGHT ARM, LEFT ARM,
  LEFT LEG, RIGHT LEG. A soul master carries at most six (one each),
  the way he carries nine rings.
- SKILLS: bones of ten-thousand years and under carry ONE skill;
  100,000-year bones carry AT LEAST TWO.
- DROP LAW (100,000-year+): a beast above 100,000 years ALWAYS drops
  a bone, and it is ALWAYS a part the killer lacks (unless he holds
  all six, or the species yields only fixed parts — the Evil-Eye
  Tyrant line yields ONLY skulls or the external eye bone).
- EVOLUTION: a bone grows with its bearer; the earlier it is taken,
  the longer it has to grow. Once absorbed it never leaves — except
  the bearer's death, which drops it ownerless.
- EXTERNAL SOUL BONES: the seventh kind — rarer than one in ten
  thousand, second only to a 100,000-year ring in value; they GROW
  with the bearer and can evolve toward the divine; they can only be
  fused AFTER absorbing the ring of the same beast. Canon examples:
  the Eight Spider Lances, the Darkgold Dreadclaw palm, the Evil Eye
  (of the Evil-Eye Tyrant Sovereign).
- SERIAL NOTE: rings are the boy's near plan; bones are his long
  ledger — and the devouring road is the road of harvests.

## H. SOUL TOOLS AND SOUL ENGINEERS (the age the boy lives in) [canon]
- A SOUL ENGINEER researches, builds, and operates SOUL TOOLS; ten
  grades, graded by the grade of tool mastered; each grade requires
  the matching soul-power tier (grade one ↔ Soul Scholar/Soul Master,
  … grade nine ↔ Titled Douluo, grade ten ↔ Limit Douluo).
- Only ONE grade-ten engineer exists in the book: KONG DEMING — at
  soul power ninety-five, with grade-ten tools, he fought the rank
  ninety-nine Xuan Zi level or above.
- THE MINGDE HALL = the Sun Moon Empire's supreme soul-tool organ
  (the Mirror-Dust family's seat); Sun Moon tooling and certification
  outrank the other three empires' — their engineers regrade DOWN
  abroad. Nine-grade tools are a Sun Moon monopoly.
- SOUL TOOLS divide into FIXED (anyone can use them — no soul power
  needed; the grenade-class: cannons, missiles, bombs) and UNFIXED
  (driven by the user's soul power). MILK BOTTLES are soul-power
  restoring charges that let even weak users run low-grade tools.
- A soul master can force tools one or two grades above himself at a
  cost; much above, and the tool will not answer him.
- SERIAL NOTE: the Cult arms itself with Sun Moon tools (canon: the
  partnership); the altar city's lamps, the gate, the locks — the
  serial stages them as tools where the page needs them [design].

## J. THE DEVOURING DRAGON'S INNATE (his soul's birthright)
[design on species canon: EF-03 + DD species law; innate-precedent
canon: Huo Yuhao's Spirit Eyes carry spirit detection before any ring]
- THE INNATE ART: DEVOURING HEAVEN AND EARTH — the soul's own,
  ringless, untaught: it drains and converts the life force, soul
  power, and flesh of what the soul devours into the bearer's own.
  The church's Formula only opens the road; the WALKING of it is the
  innate. It grows with rank and with feeding.
- DOMINION: the dragon's manifestation carries a pressure of lineage
  — lesser beasts fold before it (the boar's legs gave out from the
  blood's own counsel, not a blow).
- DRAGON BODY: the beast soul strengthens the body when summoned, and
  the dragon's blood thickens as the devouring gathers (design).
- SPECIES PACE (carried from the Devouring Dragon species law): the
  dragon line grows at roughly ten times the common pace — honest
  pace: it makes him fast and it makes him hungry, and the hunger is
  a leash everyone believes they hold.
- THE COST MECHANIC (s18, canon-logic): the devouring eats what rings
  are made of — a devoured beast leaves no ring. The gate wants a
  killing done the right way, the life standing at the taking.
- VISIBILITY LAW: the innate belongs to the PUBLIC soul — the cult
  may see it, measure it, and write reports about it. The Dragon
  Heart's is not their business, and never will be.
