# CHARACTERS (codex — grows from what happens on the page)

## LUO CHEN — protagonist
The reincarnator; adult mind from birth in the SL2 era; full canon meta-
knowledge (fallible memory); Adaptation Talent from the womb (never named).
Public soul: DEVOURING DRAGON (innate fullness read from it). Hidden soul:
THE DRAGON HEART — sunk and held through the assessor's showing (ch1).
Six years old; orphan of the border wars; walked himself into the Cult.
Manner: the truth as the safest mask; never lies where the truth is
stranger; appetite deep as a well; no joy in cruelty — the anomaly.

## FENG SI — novice-master, Ming Du altar [design]
Thin as a knife, cheerful as its owner; soft voice (the soft-voiced
children scream less); manages the Cult's smallest investment; does nothing
by chance. Ch1: the morning catechism, the supper test, the night-brazier
report — "he looks at us the way a man looks at a road he has already
walked."

## THE ASSESSOR [design — deliberately unnamed; NOT in the book]
Old without being bent; grey eyes without warmth; a merchant's eye over the
shipment. Read innate FULLNESS from the one shown soul. Her leash-line:
"the soul is the hunger; everything else is the leash." Unwritten =
dangerous; he never treats her as nothing.

## KUI [design]
Largest boy in the pit; the Cult's only lesson embodied (everything smaller
is food); caught, not brought; swung at the walk-in and found the somewhere
else.

## SHI TOU [design; dead ch1]
The failed novice; his name given back to him in the moment of the taking;
"do the first thing quick." The first name in Luo Chen's private ledger.

## GAO XU — Great Holy Spirit of the Ming Du altar [design man on canon TITLE]
Broad and still; the black-skull mark on the breast; the voice that fills
the hall like water. Speaks the doctrines (the Holy Emperor's light) and
means them the way true believers mean them; the altar's doctrine-keeper
and errand-voice. Ch2: reads the instruction; turns the curtain-question
back on Feng Si — "the hand above the altar is not a thing the altar asks
about"; "the boy is the forest's now."

## MOTHER WEI [OLD-REGISTER DESIGN, parked s13 — reusable — first ch3]
Keeper of the house under the forest. Old the way iron is old; the Cult's
grey with a white apron; the kindness fitted and for use. The seasoning is
hers to give and she gives it as kindness — the kindness real, the
seasoning real, the two the same thing: the shape of the church in one
person. Resolves what the word from above leaves open "to my own comfort."
Knows: the word from above (no number), the boy, the cup's surface effects.
Does not know: anything behind the mask.

## THIRTEEN [OLD-REGISTER DESIGN, parked s13 — reusable — first ch3]
The house's best knife before Luo Chen — perhaps ten years, thin and
straight-backed, the room defers to her. The account-giver of the house's
order; afraid of the little door with the fear kept (face given, fear kept
— which means she will climb). Watches the new one by the house's promise.
Her word: the hunt-master comes at month's end; the boy before who chose a
weak ring went somewhere that is not up.

## THE GREY MAN [OLD-REGISTER DESIGN, parked s13 — reusable — first ch3]
The altar's escort to the two-skull stone; stops at the line he was never
paid to cross. "It hears what it wants to hear." One scene, delivered him,
gone up toward the lamps.
