# GLOSSARY — the Devouring Dragon serial
Terms as THIS serial uses them. Canon terms carry their canon meaning.

- **Devouring Dragon** — his species; see CANON_LEDGER DD rows.
- **Devouring Heaven and Earth** — the species' innate spirit skill (DD-02); how
  it expresses for HIM is a story question, never assumed.
- **Year-tiers** — beast cultivation measured in years: ten (white ring), hundred
  (yellow), thousand (purple), ten-thousand (black), hundred-thousand (red).
- **The bloodthirst** — the species flaw (DD-03/05/06): the rampage-hunger that
  ends only in fullness, then deep sleep. His lifelong adversary within.
- **The deep sleep** — the feeding sleep in which the Talent works conversion.
- **The Talent** — the Adaptation Talent (v2.0). NEVER a word used in prose; this
  entry exists for record discipline only. In prose he simply IS what he becomes.
- **The tribulation** — the heavenly trial at 100,000 years (ER-05); the serial's
  horizon.
- **Distant gravity** — the rule for canon ancients (DS-06): real, nameable in
  human lore, never on stage without author go.
- **DL** — Douluo calendar year. Our year zero: DL 3661.
