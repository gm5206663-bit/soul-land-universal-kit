1. BEAST TRUTH: he is a Devouring Dragon — bloodthirst, devouring, deep sleep,
   the prey-web, men as danger. Never softened into a pet story, never prettified.
2. TALENT DISCIPLINE: v2.0 — non-sentient, holder-dependent, no free output,
   never named in prose. Shown only as the difference between him and his kind.
3. ANTI-NERF / ANTI-INFLATION TWIN LAW: no dead seasons; no fiat leaps. Every
   gain has a substrate story; every season costs something real.
4. PACING RAIL: years 1–5 in detail, then decade jumps; 10,000-year cultivation
   ≈ 25–30 real years; the tribulation is the horizon, unscheduled. No author
   ruling, no deviation.
5. ERA TRUTH: DL 3660s — Tang Sect at height, Shrek living, Spirit Hall gone,
   no soul tools, spirit beasts plentiful. Anachronisms are hard errors.
6. SEPARATION WALLS: nothing from the Lan Shen serial or SL1/SL3/SL4 fics crosses
   in; this root stands alone.
7. KNOWLEDGE FIREWALL: he knows only what a beast of his life can know.
8. OVERLORD DISTANCE: the ancients are gravity, never cast, without author go.
9. RECORD LAW: mirrors updated the same turn; exact figures only in panels/logs;
   zero CJK; English only; no unasked chapters.
10. ENDING LAW: natural happens. Nothing is pre-chosen — not his end, not his
    name, not his nature's resolution.

## PROSE LAW — "don't write boring" (author correction, session 25)
The author rebuked drift into monotone, meditative prose. Standing law:
1. SCENE-LEVEL TENSION in every beat — something concrete is at stake,
   something can go wrong, and at least once per chapter it DOES go wrong
   (a mistake with a cost, a near-miss, a betrayal of ground/wind/body).
2. LEARNING THROUGH FAILURE first, mastery second — the ledger records what
   was PAID, not what was contemplated.
3. SENTENCE VARIETY — short punches beside long flow; no recurring lull
   pattern ("X, the way Y") as connective tissue; fresh images per beat,
   never the same metaphor twice in one chapter.
4. END SCENES ON A TURN OR HOOK, open them in motion — never open a beat
   with a thesis sentence.
5. The beast-POV, wordless-keeping, Talent-never-named, zero-CJK and
   anti-inflation laws all stand unchanged; this law governs HOW the same
   locked story is told.
