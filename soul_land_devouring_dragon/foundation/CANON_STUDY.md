# CANON STUDY — the three adaptation checks (receipts preserved)

Written 2026-09-23, sessions 53–56. This file preserves everything the three
adaptation checks learned, so no future session needs to re-fetch to know why
the prose laws are what they are. Each check was run AGAINST THE SOURCE at the
author's word, and each became law (RAILS: CANON VOICE LAW s53, THE MANHUA
LENS s54, THE DONGHUA LENS s56).

The standing rule the checks share: WE NEVER REORDER EVENTS (the s34/s40/s52
rule — facts, order, causes never change; only the telling).

## 1. THE NOVEL — checked at the author's word: "actually go see how soul land
canonical is written" (s53, 2026-09-23)

Source read: the official Soul Land translation, "Otherworldly Tang San"
(chapters 1–2), WebNovel:
https://www.webnovel.com/book/soul-land--douluo-dalu_22741204305046205/otherworldly-tang-san-(1)_62529266620883250
(fetched 2026-09-23; text read in full, both chapters.)

HOW THE NOVEL ACTUALLY WRITES — the receipts, quoted:

- Geography opens chapters like stage directions, then explains the place:
  "Douluo Continent, southwestern Heaven Dou Empire, Fasinuo province." …
  "The reason why it was called Holy Spirit was because, in legend, a hundred
  years ago a Spirit Sage ranked Spirit Master came from there."
- The narrator EXPLAINS, always; reasons are stated openly:
  "It wasn't because Tang Hao demanded it, but rather because if he didn't do
  it, Tang San would practically never be able to eat his fill."
  "In this world, blacksmith could be said to be the humblest of professions.
  This is because…"
- Feelings are named flatly, not implied by rhythm:
  "Tang San carried no resentment."
- The narrator knows the future and says so:
  "Tang San wasn't clear on what was going on until much later."
- Numbers are carried freely: "three hundred households", "a five or six-year
  old child", "almost six years old".
- The plain subject is repeated constantly: "the boy", "Tang San".
- Similes are simple, concrete, rare: "White qi poured out through his mouth
  like a bolt of unrolling silk."

THE LAW IT BECAME (RAILS s53, CANON VOICE LAW): the narrator explains, never
implies; feelings are named; the plain recurring subject ("the little
Devouring Dragon", author ruling: "Just Said dd, Devouring dragon"); world-
rules stated once plainly at first appearance; canon cadence; chapters end
forward; canon's dialogue channel carried by the narrator (the serial's no-
voices law s45 stands).

## 2. THE MANHUA — checked at the author's word: "Why don't you check manhua"
(s54, 2026-09-23; deepened the same session at the author's second push)

Sources read:
- Soul Land Wiki, Episode 001 (full beat breakdown):
  https://soulland.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_001 (fetched 2026-09-23)
- TV Tropes, Douluo Dalu (Adaptation Distillation): 
  https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Literature/DouluoDalu
- MangaUpdates series page (reader consensus):
  https://www.mangaupdates.com/series/pz0mh84/douluo-dalu

HOW THE MANHUA ACTUALLY TELLS IT — the receipts:

- IT OPENS IN THE CHASE. Episode 1 begins with the elders running Tang San
  down (needles fired, moves named and dodged), not with dawn meditation; the
  jump happens in episode one.
- ACTION IS CALL-AND-RESPONSE. Every exchange is a named move meeting a named
  move, in sequence: Shadowless Needles → Purple Demon Eye + Ghost Shadow
  Perplexing Track → Torrential Pear Blossom Needle → Mysterious Jade Hand —
  and after every exchange a REACTION BEAT ("did he just block the
  Torrential Pear Blossom Needle with his bare hands?").
- GEOGRAPHY IS A LABELED INTERVAL — one insert page ("Douluo Planet → Douluo
  Continent → Heaven Dou Empire → Nuoding City → Holy Spirit Village") — then
  back to the action.
- WORLD-RULES ARRIVE IN ONE PLAIN LINE MID-ACTION, exactly when needed:
  "when everyone grows up, there will be an awakened ability, called the
  Martial Soul."
- THE COLD OPEN IS THE BIGGEST PANEL — the story's largest image first.
- WHAT IT LOSES: "Adaptation Distillation" (TV Tropes); "chapters feel
  disconnected… left guessing the motivations and reasons for many scenes"
  (MangaUpdates); fan consensus: it "doesn't make justice to the epicness of
  the novel." The manhua compresses away the novel's explanations.

THE LAW IT BECAME (RAILS s54, THE MANHUA LENS + its deepening): open every
scene in motion; beats are panels (drawable or cut); world-notes are
intervals; one-line rules mid-action; scenes end on visible turns; loud where
it counts (the slow-motion panel at impact); action as call-and-response with
a reaction beat after each exchange; the biggest panel first. Manhua pacing
STAYS JOINED to the novel's explaining narrator — the thing the manhua loses
is the thing we keep.

## 3. THE DONGHUA — checked at the author's word: "Why don't you checking
donghua" (s56, 2026-09-23)

Sources read:
- r/SoulLand reader thread on the adaptations:
  https://www.reddit.com/r/SoulLand/comments/1p8o5xs/ (fetched 2026-09-23)
- Grokipedia, Douluo Continent (production/pacing):
  https://grokipedia.com/page/Douluo_Continent
- Baidu Baike, Soul Land (the episode-to-chapter map, part one):
  https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Soul%20Land/986042
- Douluo Dalu episode guide (episode one's shape):
  https://shapes.inc/fandom/douluo-dalu-soul-land/episodes-guide

HOW THE DONGHUA ACTUALLY STRUCTURES IT — the receipts:

- FIDELITY: readers hold it "more faithful to the novel than the manhua" —
  it KEEPS the novel's explanations and EXPANDS what the novel left ambiguous
  into full flashback scenes (r/SoulLand).
- PACING: 2–3 novel chapters per episode (r/SoulLand; the Baike episode-to-
  chapter map), with "narrative shortcuts to maintain pacing" (Grokipedia) —
  achieved by MERGING scenes, not by cutting content.
- EPISODE ONE'S SHAPE: a complete mini-arc in one episode — the chase, the
  jump, the rebirth — with the world's fundamental mechanics introduced
  INSIDE the action (the episode guide).
- It also REORDERS some chapters for flow ("the order of some chapters has
  been adjusted in the animation" — Baike). THAT WE NEVER DO: the s34/s40/s52
  rule stands; events keep their order, only the telling changes.

THE LAW IT BECAME (RAILS s56, THE DONGHUA LENS): the opening is a complete
mini-arc (motion, turn, landing, the world-rule inside the action); re-anchor
inside the motion (the "previously-on" as a line, never a summary block);
MERGE, DON'T QUEUE — the lesson learned inside the doing, the loss replayed as
a short memory-panel inside the proving scene; the ceremonial beat (slow panel
+ held stillness after a power moment); no dead air (every scene carries
story, character, or world).
