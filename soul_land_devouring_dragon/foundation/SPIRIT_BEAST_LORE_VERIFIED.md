## 1. THE CORE LAW — REAL AGE vs CULTIVATION AGE
- "The actual age and the cultivation age of the soul beasts are not the same
  sometimes. For the references, the CULTIVATION AGE is used." [wiki, Age sect.]
- Baike states it bluntly: xiuwei-nianshu (cultivation year-count) ≠ cunhuo-nianshu (years lived) (cultivation years are NOT years
  lived). A beast's tier (ten/hundred/thousand/ten-thousand/hundred-thousand)
  is measured in CULTIVATION YEARS. [baike hunshou (soul-beast entry)]
- Cultivation age can run FAR ahead of real age through: DEVOURING (the Deep Sea
  Demonic Whale King and the Demon Empress reached million-year cultivation by
  devouring oceanic soul beasts [wiki]); Emperor Beast presence (doubles the
  cultivation speed of beasts in its habitat [wiki]); immortal herbs and heaven-
  earth treasures; special environments (Ice and Fire Yin Yang Well multiplies
  by ten [wiki]).
- Even DI TIAN: actually lived a few hundred thousand years, cultivation
  800,000+ years in SL2 [wiki]. Divergence is normal at the heights.
- For ORDINARY beasts, cultivation age grows roughly 1:1 with real age (that is
  why the tiers are named in years and why bottlenecks exist — see 3).
- OUR DEVOURING DRAGON: canon gives his species rate — ~1,000 real years to
  reach 10,000-year cultivation (DD-04), i.e. cultivation age runs ~10x real
  age, driven by the devour-and-evolve nature. The Adaptation Talent's honest
  edge multiplies that further (design dial DS-05, current setting 2x species
  = ~20 cultivation-years per real year; one word from the author changes it).

## 3. BOTTLENECKS — beasts have barriers at EVERY tier line
"Soul beasts' bottlenecks didn't just occur at the hundred thousand year level.
For lower-level soul beasts, EVERY HUNDRED YEARS represented a barrier they had
to cross. This barrier continues to every thousand years, and slowly advances
to ten thousand years before reaching a hundred thousand years. For every
hundred thousand years, the difficulty of improving would escalate." [wiki]
→ OUR STORY LAW: his first great barrier is at CULTIVATION-YEAR 100 (the
hundred-year line), then 1,000, then 10,000, then the 100,000 tribulation.
Barriers are crossed by growth (devouring, ambient drawing, survival), not by
rings — beasts have no rings; rings are what they DROP.

## 4. INTELLIGENCE LADDER
- "Soul beasts at a young age are not that intelligent compared to humans. It's
  only when they reach 100,000 years that they gain intelligence on par with a
  human and are able to talk. Before then, they slowly mature. A 10,000-year
  old pink mist lady queen has the intellect of a little kid." [wiki]
- Exceptions: noble/royal bloodlines and heaven-earth treasures can grant
  speech/intelligence before 100,000 [wiki].
- Devilouring Dragons canonically sit at the LOW end of beast intelligence
  (instinct-driven; DD-05).
- CORRECTED (author s18): THE LADDER IS A TENDENCY, NOT AN ABSOLUTE. It
  normally happens, but not for everyone — it DEPENDS ON BLOODLINE AND RACE:
  SPIRITUAL TYPES and very good bloodlines/races gain intelligence EARLY
  (especially spiritual types); and there are beasts with only child-like
  intelligence even at 100,000 years — ER MING in Soul Land 1 is the canon
  example (a 100,000-year king with a child-like mind). Never write the
  ladder as a clock.
- Third-party article (f166): thousand-year beasts open spiritual intellect;
  ten-thousand-year beasts become regional overlords [tier-2 source, canon-soft].
→ OUR STORY LAW: at cultivation-age ~16 (now), his mind is sensation + instinct
+ learned shapes ONLY. Thought deepens slowly with the tiers. Human-parity
speech/thought is a 100,000-year fact unless the author rules otherwise. His
Talent gives LEARNING, not granted intellect — the gap between him and his kind
is that he learns at all, and keeps what he learns.

## 5. THE 100,000-YEAR GATE — tribulation and human form
- At 100,000 cultivation years a beast chooses: (a) rebirth as a HUMAN (recult-
  ivate anew; the first seven rings need no hunting; must stay in contact with
  humans to progress; cannot return to beast form unless killed; past human rank
  60 it is fully human; rank 90+ enters the path to godhood) [wiki + baike];
  or (b) remain a beast and face the HEAVENLY TRIBULATION — it arrives some
  centuries after the 100,000 mark [baike]; survive it = FEROCIOUS/GREAT BEAST
  (200,000-year class); fail it = destruction of soul and form [baike]. Only
  about one in a dozen hundred-thousand-year beasts becomes a Great Beast
  [baike]. Tribulations repeat every 100,000 cultivation years, escalating
  [wiki].
- SL1-era note: wiki says in SL1 a beast choosing the beast road could live
  only another ~1,000 years; later series extended this through surviving
  tribulations. [verify against primary text before the story ever nears this
  gate — it is thousands of real years away.]
- SACRIFICE: only 100,000-year beasts can sacrifice themselves, bestowing ring
  + soul bone on an acknowledged one; cultivation spent; revert to an infant
  [wiki].

## 6. DEATH-DROPS — what he IS to the hunters
- Soul ring ALWAYS drops on death; grade set by cultivation age (not real age)
  [wiki]. Soul bone: CHANCE drop below 100,000 years, GUARANTEED above; seven
  kinds (limbs, torso, head, external); a 100,000-year bone fills an unfilled
  slot [wiki].
→ OUR STORY LAW: if he dies, he is a ring of his cultivation-years. That is the
permanent shape of the danger men represent. At cultivation-age 16 he would drop
a white ring — nothing to a hunter but a morning's wage. His worth to them grows
with his years.

## 7. GROWTH APPEARANCE
"The cultivation of most soul beasts can be recognized through their appearance
— some increase in size when they move into the thousand-year level. Beasts
with pure and powerful bloodlines experience LESS major change during evolution,
because their bodies are already close to perfection." [wiki]
→ OUR STORY LAW: his growth spurts come at tier crossings and are visible
(size, scale, presence); the dragon bloodline means refinement more than
re-shaping.

## 8. EMPEROR BEASTS (distant lore only — DS-06 applies)
Beasts of destiny that beget prosperity; double the cultivation speed of all
beasts in their habitat; double the odds of a 100,000-year beast breaking its
bottleneck; the Three-Eyed Golden Lion (SL2) is one [wiki]. In our era such a
being, if present in the Star Dou Great Forest, is DISTANT GRAVITY ONLY — never
staged without author go. [design caution]

## 9. ORIGINS (deep lore, prologue-grade)
Soul beasts were born of god-aura and divine-beast-aura leaked from the Divine
Realm; the planet's first inhabitants; since the Dragon God's defeat in the god
war, beasts are barred from godhood (plant-types exempt) [wiki]. Not needed in
prose; held for canon-honesty.

## 10. REPRODUCTION
"Soul beasts reproduce mainly the same as normal animals; it depends on the
type." [wiki] Dragon Clan: eggs — the lone egg (DS-09) is design-consistent.
