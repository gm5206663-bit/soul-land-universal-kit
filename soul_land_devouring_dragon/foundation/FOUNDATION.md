## 2. The species — Devouring Dragon (canon receipts; full rows in CANON_LEDGER)
Source: SL2 ch. 391 (wuxiaworld The Unrivaled Tang Sect 391-1) via the Soul Land
wiki; categories: Spirit Beast / Dragon Clan / 10K+ Years / SL2.
- Also called a Mini-Tyrant Dragon; resembles the Tyrant Dragon but smaller.
- Thick dark-blue scales (three layers); upright-walking; very thick lower limbs;
  short small upper limbs, three sharp cold-gleaming claws per limb; ~4 m tall at
  the adult stage in the one canon sighting (a 20,000-year specimen); head about
  one third of the body; thick ~3 m tail. Extremely muscular.
- Innate spirit skill: DEVOURING HEAVEN AND EARTH.
- Innately bloodthirsty: recovers energy and EVOLVES BY DEVOURING enemies it has
  killed (beast or human); stops its rampage only when its stomach is full; then
  enters a DEEP SLEEP after feeding.
- Growth: much faster than ordinary spirit beasts — "only a thousand years to
  reach the cultivation of an ordinary ten thousand years spirit beast."
- Weakness: the bloodthirstiness usually gets them killed by stronger opponents;
  intelligence usually very limited — it relies on instinct to kill (canon ranks
  it inferior to the Dark Demon Evil God Tiger for this reason).
- Even spirit beasts shun the kind as too brutal: in a Devouring Dragon's eyes,
  every creature is a target to kill and devour.
- Bloodline depth (DD-08): he is of the EARTH DRAGON line — the mini-Tyrant-
  Dragon kinship; the Tyrant Dragon stands among the strongest Earth Dragons,
  carries the Golden Dragon King's bloodline, and its wing-evolution lore
  touches the Dragon Kings. A 20,000-yr Devouring Dragon ≈ 4 m: small and quick
  for dragon-kind. The lineage's heights are DISTANT POTENTIAL only — author-
  gated, never plotted.

## 3. The Adaptation Talent — BEAST FORM (v2.0 laws, author-locked; s7 scope fix)
- Definitive Master Foundation v2.0 governs whole (master §73): an innate, pure,
  inseparable, NON-SENTIENT existential principle that continuously evolves the
  holder's COMPLETE BEING along the best presently supportable path. Never named
  in prose; the world sees only a strange, lucky, learning beast.
- THE SCOPE IS THE WHOLE EXISTENCE (author s7, master §8/§48 areas): BLOODLINE
  (the dragon blood refined toward its best supportable shape), CULTIVATION /
  SOUL POWER (absorption, conversion and the deep sleeps all adapt), SPIRITUAL
  POWER / INTELLIGENCE (his mind deepens faster than his species' curve — the
  canon low-intelligence of Devouring Dragons is the species baseline, not his
  ceiling), BODY / SENSES / INSTINCT / RECOVERY, and NATURE-MASTERY (the
  bloodthirst stays — the mastery of it grows). No two Talents are alike: his is
  shaped by his existence — a kinless predator on a Star Luo mountain border.
- Limits are expression-limits (age, substrate, resources, world law), never
  scope-limits. No free output: every gain has substrate. Anti-nerf and
  anti-inflation twin law: no dead seasons, no feast-granted leaps.
- Devouring economy [design, under v2.0 no-free-output]: devouring converts a
  SLIVER of the prey's years into his growth — never the prey's full years, never
  a visible jump; growth reads as "a beast that grows unsettlingly well," not as
  power-ups. Exact magnitudes stay out of prose; records live in ADAPTATION_LOG.

## 4. Pace architecture (author pick: detailed early, time-jumps later)
- Real years 1–5 (DL 3661–3665): IN DETAIL — hatchling survival, first prey,
  first evolutions, the learning of men-as-danger.
- Thereafter: decade-scale time jumps as natural.
- REAL AGE vs CULTIVATION AGE (canon SB-01): tiers are measured in CULTIVATION
  YEARS, not years lived; the two diverge — his species' cultivation age runs
  ahead of its real age (devouring-driven; DD-04: 10,000-year cultivation ≈
  1,000 real years). All records track BOTH separately.
- Pace calibration (CORRECTED s7): the species' ~10x growth line (DD-04) is a
  REFERENCE BASELINE, not an absolute clock (author s7: "this is just
  reference"). NO arithmetic rate exists in this serial — growth is event-
  shaped (devourings, trials, barriers, seasons) and adaptive; panel
  cultivation-age is an honest ESTIMATE moved by events. All derived clocks and
  barrier-age formulas (s5/s6 versions) are RETIRED. Narrative pacing keeps the
  author's pick: years 1–5 detailed, decade jumps after.
- TIER BARRIERS (canon SB-03): bottlenecks at every tier line — cultivation-year
  100, then 1,000, then 10,000, then the tribulation; escalating. Crossed by
  growth (devouring, ambient drawing, survival), never by rings.
- The 100,000-year TRIBULATION is the beast's great gate (canon SB-05) and the
  serial's horizon; nothing about it is scheduled now. Ending: NATURAL (author
  law: no pre-chosen ending, no pre-chosen tone).
- Anti-inflation ledger rule: every growth step needs a prey/survival story;
  no ring-of-years numbers appear in prose; exact cultivation-years live ONLY in
  STATUS_PANEL / ADAPTATION_LOG / ledgers (exact-figures law).
