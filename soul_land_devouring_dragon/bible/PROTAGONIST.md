  never by granted power.

  Forest's outer slopes (DS-02), on the STAR LUO border (author ruling s2).
  THE ONLY EGG (author ruling s2; DS-09): kinless from the first breath.

  deep sleep after feeding (DD-03) — PLUS the Adaptation Talent ACTIVE FROM
  THE EGG (Phase 0 built the foundation pre-hatch; DS-03 corrected s7, s11).
  The Talent is his survival; the bloodthirst is his flaw.
- THE BLOODLINE PATH (author s20; DS-10): he is a SUB-line dragon, not pure;
  the Talent focuses on the best path he has — cleaning the impurities and
  INCREASING THE DRAGON BLOODLINE, fed from every devouring; the absorption

  FUEL for the dragon line (no prey-mutation, no trait-patches); a trait/
  bloodline element gained only from something strong enough or superior,
  harmonized into the line. The bloodline is the beast's most important
  thing.
- The canon contradiction he lives: his kind is "intelligence usually very
  limited, instinct to kill" (DD-05). He is the exception — not by wisdom granted,
  but by a nature that LEARNS: instinct that corrects, appetite that waits, a
  predator that survives its own hunger. The world will read that as cunning;
  nothing is ever granted free.
