# POWER LAW — the Devouring Dragon serial
The operating laws of growth, combat and record. Overridable by AUTHOR RULINGS
only (section C), which are dated and quoted.

## A. Canon laws (binding)
| Law | Statement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Beast cultivation | Spirit beasts grow by YEARS of cultivation; might, mind and standing rise with the year-tiers (ten / hundred / thousand / ten-thousand / hundred-thousand) | SL1 canon |
| Ring law | When a spirit beast dies by a hunt, its years condense as a soul ring for the killer's absorption; ring colors follow the year-tiers (white/yellow/purple/black/red) | SL1 canon (WL-09/10/11 of the sister ledger) |
| Tribulation law | At 100,000 years the heavenly tribulation comes; the beast may face it or choose the human-form road instead | SL1 canon (ER-05) |
| Devouring nature | A Devouring Dragon recovers energy and evolves by devouring killed enemies; fullness ends the rampage; deep sleep follows | DD-03 |
| Growth rate | Species grows ~10x ordinary beasts | DD-04 |
| Intelligence law | Beasts' intelligence deepens with cultivation; Devouring Dragons canonically sit at the low end of it (DD-05) — our OC's learning edge is the Talent's, and must always READ as exceptional-for-his-kind, never as human wisdom granted | DD-05 + DS-03 |

| Real age vs cultivation age | Tiers are CULTIVATION YEARS, not years lived; the two diverge (devouring, treasures, Emperor-beast presence); ALL records track both separately | SB-01 |
| Tier barriers | Bottlenecks at every tier line: cultivation-year 100, then 1,000, then 10,000, then the 100,000 tribulation; escalating; crossed by growth, never by rings (beasts DROP rings) | SB-03 |
| Intelligence ladder | Sensation/instinct now; thought deepens with the tiers; 10,000-year beasts ≈ a child; human-parity speech at 100,000 (bloodline/treasure exceptions); Devouring Dragons sit low-end — his Talent gives LEARNING, not granted intellect | SB-04 |
| Death-drop law | If he dies he is a ring of his cultivation-years (white at ~16 yrs cultivation) plus a chance of bone — his worth to hunters grows with his years | SB-06 |
| Growth-appearance law | Growth spurts at tier crossings are visible (size/scale/presence); dragon bloodline = refinement over re-shaping | SB-07 |
| Species rate is REFERENCE, not clock | The canon line (~1,000 real years to 10,000-year cultivation) is a baseline reference for the species, NOT an absolute — author s7: "10× times just a guess a baseline not absolute fix… this is just reference." NO arithmetic growth clock exists in this serial; growth is event-shaped (devourings, trials, barriers, seasons) and adaptive; cultivation-age in panels is an honest ESTIMATE, never a formula | DD-04 + s7 |

| THE BLOODLINE PATH LAW (author s20 — the spine of the premise) | LOGIC, LOCKED: he is NOT a pure dragon — the Devouring Dragon is a SUB-line of the dragon race (Earth Dragon line, DD-08), dragon blood with impurities. The Adaptation Talent's bloodline work FOCUSES on the best path he has: it CLEANS THE IMPURITIES and INCREASES HIS DRAGON BLOODLINE — every devouring feeds the dragon line, kill by kill and sleep by sleep; the bloodline's direction is up its own origin (Earth Dragon line → Golden Dragon King's blood → toward the Dragon God's own height — the far horizon, author-gated, §41.4). The Talent also PERFECTS THE ABSORPTION (§42 cross-read): his race skill is the legitimate mechanism, the Talent makes the taking truer — less waste, less rejection, nothing lost. WHY HE DOES NOT MUTATE FROM HIS PREY (author s20): he already holds the STRONGER PATH — dragon bloodline; lesser devoured bloodlines and traits are BURNED AS FUEL for the dragon line (fuel for bloodline, body, energy, instinct), not worn as patches — he does not grow his prey's feathers, hides, or instincts. He CAN gain a trait or bloodline element, but only from something STRONG ENOUGH OR SUPERIOR to matter against dragon blood — and even then it is harmonized into the line, never overwriting it (§41.2). The bloodline is the beast's most important thing; everything he devours is measured against it. The serial finds the rest of this logic by itself — this is common sense, not a checklist |
| INTELLIGENCE LAW (author s18) | The intelligence ladder is a TENDENCY, not an absolute — it normally happens, but NOT FOR EVERYONE: it depends on BLOODLINE AND RACE. SPIRITUAL TYPES and very good bloodlines/races gain intelligence EARLY (especially spiritual types); and some beasts hold only child-like intelligence even at 100,000 years — ER MING of Soul Land 1 is the canon example. For him: the species curve is the Devouring Dragon's low baseline (DD-05), but apex dragon blood + the egg-foundation + the spirit-mind road carry his mind early and fast — still gradual, still experience-fed, never granted; never written as a clock |
| STAGE BREAKTHROUGH LAW (author s17) | EVERY stage breakthrough (10→100, 100→1,000, 1,000→10,000, 10,000→100,000, the gate and beyond) is NOT just a capacity step — it carries many things: the whole-being recalibration (§29) AND an opportunity window of AWAKENING / EVOLUTION. A breakthrough written without its opportunity is a nerf; the opportunity is a CHANCE, not a grant — it is seized by foundation, extremity and the Talent, and what awakens must fit the holder's nature and causal chain. Canon ladder anchors (opportunities ahead): the THOUSAND-YEAR crossing NORMALLY opens spiritual intellect (canon; the spirit-mind road's great gate — but see the INTELLIGENCE LAW below: normally, not absolutely); the HUNDRED-THOUSAND gate = human rebirth OR tribulation (verified s15: beast path = 900,000 more years, tribulation every 100,000; human path chosen by 1 in 10, irreversible; at 200,000 the choice returns once, then never). What a stage breakthrough CARRIES (the many things, s18 expansion): (1) the energy/soul-power leap into the new tier; (2) the whole-being recalibration (§29); (3) visible body change — growth, armor, form-markers (SB-07); (4) the ring color/value shift (white→yellow→purple→black→red→ferocious→gold; his worth to hunters changes); (5) the AWAKENING/EVOLUTION opportunity window; (6) the bloodline opportunity — refinement/purification, dormant traits (§41); (7) the MIND opportunity — variable, see INTELLIGENCE LAW; (8) the race skill deepening with the tier; (9) lifespan extending with the years; (10) at the gates: choice/tribulation. HIS FIRST BREAKTHROUGH (10→100, ch6) seized: THE DRAGON'S VOICE (author s21 — "logically he should have a skill after 100 years": the bloodline's first ACTIVE skill — the roar carrying the weight of his blood; hammers the low lives flat, makes equals falter, barely reaches above him; short; paid in breath and blood; does not come at his calling yet — control is the climbing; proven at the long-fang hunt: the falter in the air of its spring, the instant was his), the bloodline step (armor set into the full deep layering of his kind + the FIRST CROWN of the dragon along skull and neck), the SENSE OF YEARS (the world's more carries age; the greater hunger feeds by it), and the race skill's deeper face (the mountain-drawing reach) |
| BREAKTHROUGH LAW (master §29, read s15) | A barrier crossing is a genuine breakthrough = a change in ACTUAL CAPACITY, never a title change. It recalibrates the connected whole (body, senses, mind, energy, healing, core powers, bloodline). §29.2: every inseparable layer must reach minimum foundation to survive the new state — adaptation may reinforce first, slow the transition, or fail it. §29.4: observable consequences AFTER the panel — old threats easier, movement changed, recovery changed, perception wider, costs down, NEW limitations specific to the new state. Governs the ch6 crossing |
| ABSORPTION LAW (master §42.4, read s15) | Absorption is NOT a general Talent function; it needs a legitimate mechanism. His devouring IS that mechanism — the Devouring Dragon nature + its innate skill (DD-02/DD-09) = his species' absorption pathway; the Talent rides and refines it, never replaces it. No reserve becomes infinite because the Talent exists (§42.3) |
| SLEEP LAW (master §35.2, read s15) | Sleep = memory consolidation, tissue repair, energy stabilization, INTEGRATION; the Talent may improve sleep quality. His deep-sleep conversions are canon: the devouring's integration window; reduced sleep would need a replacement for every function sleep serves — never used |
| BLOODLINE-EVOLUTION ANCHOR (master §41.4, read s15) | "An inherited bloodline is a foundation, not necessarily a permanent ceiling. With sufficient growth it may become a unique personal lineage." — the distant, author-gated horizon of the Earth Dragon line in him; reached only by actual inheritance + acquired compatible forces + resources + experience + identity + world laws; §41.1 refinement (defects reduced not erased — bloodthirst ridden, never removed; dormant traits awakened — the wings kept as distant potential) |
| BLOODLINE SUPPRESSION LAW (author s11) | He is a dragon, not a joke: the apex bloodline of beastkind weighs on low beasts — the small lives still and lower before him, from before learning (DD-10). Young and thin at his stage, but real and growing: it scales with cultivation and bloodline depth. Serial consequences: prey reads him before seeing him (his nobility warns the eaten — the predator's paradox); the Talent's nature-mastery/bloodline roads will, when events demand, learn to VEIL the weight so the hunter can close (seed, author-gated, not yet acquired). Beasts above him are not suppressed — the law is one-directional down the bloodline order |
| DIFFERENCE-FROM-HIS-KIND LAW (author s11) | He is DIFFERENT AND SPECIAL from his own race — not by granted power, but by built foundation: the Adaptation Talent was active from the egg ("from mother's womb"), building his complete foundation before the hatch — bloodline expression, vitality, sensory apparatus, spirit-mind substrate, the roots of instinct. What hatched was hunger and flesh with a FOUNDATION under it. The difference reads as QUALITY (health, density, depth, the spirit-mind lifting past the species curve, bloodthirst rideable) — never as free levels; anti-inflation law untouched. This is why his species' fate (dying young of bloodthirst, staying low and shunned) is a fate he can diverge from |
| RACE-SKILL COMBINATION LAW (author s10) | He is a Devouring Dragon: his race has its OWN ability — DEVOURING HEAVEN AND EARTH (DD-09), the domineering innate skill of devouring-and-evolving, carried on the species' heavy defense. The Adaptation Talent COMBINES with the race ability: the race skill is WHAT he can do, the Talent is how it refines, controls, integrates and evolves (master §10.4 powers-and-forms layer; §25 ladder). The Talent never replaces the race skill; it perfects and climbs it. A Devouring Dragon without the Talent dies young of bloodthirst (DD-05); with it, the skill and its bloodline develop instead of dying. Skill development is stage-gated and event-triggered (stirring → unstable → controlled → mastered), never granted |
