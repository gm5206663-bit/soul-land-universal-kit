# WORLD — the Star Dou border country, DL 3660s
One thousand years after Soul Land 1. Canon anchors first, design second.

## 1. The continent (canon)
- Two empires rule: HEAVEN DOU and STAR LUO (canon). THE STAR LUO EMPIRE holds
  our mountain border (author ruling s2) — consistent with canon placing the Star
  Dou Great Forest on the Star Luo side of the continent's heart [canon-soft].
- The TANG SECT stands at the height of its legend (founded by the ascended
  Tang San; ER-02). SHREK ACADEMY lives and is famous (ER-03).
- The SPIRIT HALL is gone (ER-04): its rites, stipends and register customs died
  with it; what replaced them in this century is UNVERIFIED — a design question
  to answer before any human-side scene touches awakening/registration.
- NO soul tools (ER-07): steel, soul-power craft of the old kind, hidden weapons
  of Tang Sect tradition — that is the whole of the age's edge.
- The star of the age's sky is the SPIRIT BEAST: the continent is still wild;
  hunting beasts for rings is the soul master's road exactly as in SL1.
- ERA FACTS (s15): the Sun Moon Continent has NOT yet collided (ER-09: the
  collision falls ~5,000 years after our era) — no Sun Moon presence, no soul
  tools; the continent of the three empires; the Star Luo Empire holds our
  border (DS-02). Da Ming and Er Ming fell a thousand years ago (ER-08); the
  Deep Sea Demonic Whale King fell at SL1's end; the Top Ten beyond them are
  alive in this era (GB-02).

## 2. The Star Dou Great Forest (canon + design)
- The continent's great spirit-beast country: outer forest, mixed forest, deep
  core, and the great lake at the heart (canon shape from SL2; ages intact here).
- Ancient overlords rule the deep (ER-06): DI TIAN, the Beast God, the
  Golden-Eyed Black Dragon King — ~811,000 years in this era (era math s15:
  SL2's 820,000+ minus ~9,000 yrs). With him in the forest's depth, verified
  alive in our era (GB-02): Bi Ji the Jadeite Swan, the Myriad Demon King
  (Demonic Eye Demon Tree), the Bear Lord (Dark Gold Terror Claw Bear), the
  Scarlet King (Three-Headed Crimson Demon Mastiff). OVERLORD LAW (DS-06):
  distant gravity; never staged; never contacted without author go.
- Spirit beasts are PLENTIFUL in this era — the forest is still winning the long
  war with the hunters. Ten-year things swarm; hundred-year things hold ground;
  thousand-year things rule valleys; ten-thousand-year things are the border's
  whispered dangers; anything older lives where men do not go.
- [design] The forest's unwritten law, as beasts live it: eat below you, fear
  above you, hold your ground, avoid the fire-makers.

## 3. The mountain border country (ours — DS-02)
- The outer slopes where the forest meets the passes: steep woods, scree, cold
  streams, wind-broken ridges. No farms. Human presence = HUNTER LODGES and
  pass-traffic (author-locked: humans present from the start).
- [design] The lodges: timber-and-stone camps of fur hunters and ring-hunters,
  occupied by season; spirit hounds, fire, iron, and the patience of men who kill for
  living. Ring-hunters (soul masters working the outer forest for white and
  yellow rings) are the lodge aristocracy. Exact lodge names/placements: at
  Chapter 1's need, logged here then.
- [design] Prey-web of the hatch-slope (to detail in CREATURES.md as met):
  **EVERY creature of it is a soul beast of its own years** — this world has soul
  beasts, not ordinary animals (author correction s32, 2026-09-20); the low ground
  first, blue silver grass and the below-ten-year things — the commonest soul
  beasts there are; then hare-kind, marmot-kind, ground-birds; then the
  mid-predators that eat hatchlings — fox-kind, wildcat-kind, the great-winged
  things of the ridges, and the hundred-year things of the lower woods. Nothing
  invented ahead of need.
- Seasons: the mountain has teeth in winter (deep snow, starvation pressure —
  the hatchling's first true enemy may be cold, not fang).

## 4. The human world beyond the passes (distance, not detail)
- Market towns at the mountain's foot, then the empire's road-country; the Tang
  Sect's and Shrek's names are everywhere as legend (canon). None of it is
  "near" to a hatchling. Detail arrives only when the story walks there.

## 5. Economy of the border (folk level)
- Furs, pelts, dried meat, beast-bone and the occasional ring: this is what the
  lodges live on and what the mountain pays. Exact figures stay in dialogue-folk
  level until the story earns a price (law carried from the corpus).
