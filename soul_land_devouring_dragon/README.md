# DEVOURING DRAGON — a Soul Land serial (1,000 years after SL1)
A Devouring Dragon spirit beast, the only egg, kinless, on the Star Luo border
of the Star Dou Great Forest. The Adaptation Talent (never named in prose)
built his foundation from the egg. Governed by the NO-MISTAKE KIT and the
author rulings in SERIAL_LOG.

> SPIRIT_BEAST_LORE_VERIFIED > RAILS > POWER_LAW > everything else.
