## 2. OC integration plan

Zhou Xu’s presence:

- He is not inserted as a savior who cures the poison.
- He is present in the inner palace because he is an adopted prince, same-age brother-like companion, and refuses to leave Zhou Yuan during the triennial poison outbreak.
- Adults allow him at a guarded distance because he has watched before and because his Clear Origin Eyes can perceive strain/impurity without interfering.
- His eyes should see the blood-red resentment poison as poisonous origin/resentment flow and Qin Yu’s life-force depletion as dimming source warmth, but he cannot solve them.
- His Adaptation Talent passively resists the oppressive resentment aura and records its pressure in his body, but does not steal it or neutralize it.
- He has an innate pure body / no impurities; mention subtly through how blue sandalwood smoke and palace air do not cloud his breathing and how Master Qin trusts his body’s stability near the sickroom.
- Wine trait: include a small jade cup/gourd of watered palace wine as personality flavor; do not make it a power-up.

Natural butterfly effects:

- Zhou Qing and Qin Yu are not only speaking to one suffering son; they also have to decide how much truth the adopted son/brother hears.
- Zhou Yuan’s emotional reaction is still the canon core.
- Zhou Xu’s reaction adds a second witness and future promise: he cannot cure it, but he will not let Zhou Yuan carry the truth alone.
- Zhou Xu’s Clear Origin Eyes make the truth more visibly horrifying to him, strengthening his vow without changing canon outcome.
- The Yuan family dynamic changes: Zhou Yuan is not isolated; he has a brother-like equal-age companion who knows the burden and will affect future public/shared scenes.
