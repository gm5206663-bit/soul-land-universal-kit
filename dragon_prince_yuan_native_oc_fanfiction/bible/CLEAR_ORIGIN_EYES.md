# Clear Origin Eyes — Zhou Xu Special Eyes

Status: **WORKING DESIGN USED LIGHTLY IN CHAPTER 1 / USER MAY STILL CORRECT**

## Name

**Clear Origin Eyes**

Alternative style names if user wants more mystical wording:

- Origin-Cleansing Eyes
- Clear Genesis Eyes
- Pure Origin Spirit Eyes
- Adaptive Origin Eyes

## Core concept

The eyes are a Dragon Prince Yuan-world expression of Zhou Xu's Adaptation Talent and pure innate body. They do not copy another franchise's eye power. They are not future sight and not omniscience.

They perceive the **state and flow of origin/foundation**:

- impurities in body, medicine, food, bloodline, and cultivation resources;
- Genesis Qi flow/stagnation/pressure points;
- hidden strain in meridians/channels/body foundation;
- flaws in technique execution;
- environmental compatibility;
- weak points in attacks by reading Qi rhythm and structural tension;
- possible adaptation paths, but not guaranteed outcomes.

## Passive effects

- Sharper perception of Qi movement and body imbalance.
- Better selection of safe resources/medicines/foods.
- Helps maintain no-impurity innate body by noticing contamination early.
- Supports learning techniques by seeing where force/Qi breaks shape.
- Helps read opponents, but only within his realm and experience.

## Active effects — tentative

Possible active mode: **Clear Origin Sight**.

When activated, Zhou Xu's eyes brighten with clear amber-gold or pale jade-gold light and he can read deeper Qi/foundation patterns for a short time.

Potential uses:

- find the weakest stress point in a defense;
- see if a medicine/resource suits him or Zhou Yuan;
- notice poison/curse/blocked channels earlier than others;
- guide his Adaptation Talent toward a safer breakthrough;
- help allies correct training flaws.

## Limits

- Not future sight.
- Not mind reading.
- Not automatic victory.
- Cannot fully understand powers far above his realm.
- Cannot replace experience, technique, resources, or cultivation.
- Overuse causes eye pain, headache, bloodline heat, dizziness, or temporary blurred perception.
- If user approves, early wine habit may soothe/adapt body heat or become tied to medicinal-wine compatibility.

## Butterfly risks

If these eyes are public, Great Zhou/Yuan family and enemies should react. If hidden, there must be a real reason and discipline for hiding them.
