It may affect:

- No canon memory or future knowledge.
- Not instant mastery.
- Not immunity.
- Not free realm skipping.
- Needs resources, training, danger, recovery, and time.
- Costs/strain may include hunger, sleep, pain, heat, eye strain, exhaustion, or higher resource demand.
