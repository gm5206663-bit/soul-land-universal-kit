# Power Scaling Rules — Dragon Prince Yuan Native OC

Status: generic until OC power path is locked.

## Principles

- Track formal cultivation realm separately from actual combat power.
- Track public reputation separately from private hidden foundation.
- Track resources, talent, technique quality, bloodline/body, treasures, teachers, and experience separately.
- Costs should matter but must not erase real output.
- Major breakthroughs must produce visible consequences.
- The OC must not be nerfed to keep canon unchanged.
- The OC must not randomly surpass major canon pillars without earned setup and user approval.
