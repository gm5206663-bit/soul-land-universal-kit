# Butterfly Effects Protocol — Dragon Prince Yuan Native OC

Because the project is canon-aware and has a native-born OC, every public change must breathe.

Before preserving a canon event unchanged, check:

1. Is the OC present?
2. Is the OC publicly known?
3. Does the OC affect clan/family politics?
4. Does the OC affect cultivation resources or teacher attention?
5. Does the OC affect Zhou Yuan's choices, reputation, danger, or emotional state?
6. Does the OC affect rivals or enemies?
7. Does the OC change timing, training, tests, auctions, sect recruitment, battles, or resource distribution?
8. Are private canon secrets still private?
9. Is canon being preserved by making the OC useless or invisible?

Rule: public facts produce public consequences; private facts require earned knowledge routes.
