## Part 1: The Lecture

Shen Yi gave the lecture on the third day.

She gave it in a hall that had a ceiling, which the working-student dormitory did not, and she gave it to all the new students, and she did not repeat herself, because she said at the start that she would not.

"You are all now members of Shrek Academy," she said. "Whatever you do in the outside world represents Shrek Academy. If you overstep your bounds, the Academy will punish you. The punishment will be unrelated to whatever the Federation decides."

She let that sit, because Shrek was its own country and everyone in the hall was learning it.

"Each of you has a student card with your contribution points in it. You can exchange them for cultivation resources, spirit souls, time in special facilities, personal guidance from a teacher, entrance to the spirit ascension platform." She looked at the working students, who were the ones in the worst seats. "Working students, in particular, need them to buy food."

Xie Xie, beside Lin Hao, made a sound.

"You cannot buy contribution points with money," Shen Yi said. "You earn them by completing jobs for the Academy. Physical labor. Metal forging. Mecha design. Mecha making. Mecha maintenance. As long as you complete the job, you are awarded points."

Lin Hao had stopped listening at *metal forging.*

---

## Part 2: What Lin Hao Heard

He had been a blacksmith since he was six.

His father had put a hammer in his hand at six and not explained anything, and he had made horseshoes, and straps, and simple components, and he had gone to the bottom of it at Old Zhou's forge, and he had made a strap that did not know he was better than the bottom, and he had come to Shrek and found the forge behind Spirit Ice Plaza on his first day.

And now Shen Yi was standing in a hall saying *metal forging* was a job that earned contribution points, and that contribution points bought food, and spirit souls, and entrance to the spirit ascension platform.

Lin Hao had been getting ready for a thing since he was six. He had not known what the thing was. He had made horseshoes because his father made horseshoes, and straps because Old Zhou made straps, and he had come to Shrek because Wulin had wanted Shrek since he was six, and he had found the forge because that was what he did.

And now a woman in a hall was saying that the thing he had been doing since he was six was the road.

---

## Part 3: The Most Appropriate Profession

Shen Yi gave the second lecture the next day, and it was about battle armor.

"If all of you believe battle armor masters are merely that, then you are gravely mistaken," she said. "A mecha pilot puts clothes on their person. A battle armor master puts clothes on their martial soul, because at their core they are still soul masters. One-word battle armor is the beginning. Only at two words, when you are one with your armor and it becomes an extension of your body and martial soul, are you truly a battle armor master."

Lin Hao had seen a two-word battle armor. He had seen it twice. The first time it had been Wu Zhangkong, in a yard, saying two words and becoming a knight of ice. The second time it had been a man at a forge behind Spirit Ice Plaza, making a plate that was a piece of a person.

"There are four levels," Shen Yi said. "One-word, two-word, three-word, four-word. The gap between each is immeasurably deep. If you want to become a battle armor master, you must pick a secondary profession relating to building the armor. The most efficient are mecha maker and mecha designer." She paused. "You are free to become a blacksmith or a mecha mechanic instead. Blacksmithing is both demanding and strenuous, and forging armor is much more difficult. However, it is the most appropriate profession for creating powerful battle armors."

Lin Hao had stopped breathing.

*The most appropriate profession for creating powerful battle armors.*

He was a fourth-rank Grandmaster blacksmith. He was ten. He had been a blacksmith since he was six. And a woman in a hall at the greatest academy in the world had just said that the thing he had been doing since he was six was the most appropriate profession for the thing Xie Xie had told him on a seawall he would make.

*You'll make the metal,* Xie Xie had said. *I'll make the mecha.*

---

## Part 4: What Xie Xie Said

Xie Xie found him after the lecture, because Xie Xie found people, and because Xie Xie had heard the lecture too.

"She said mecha maker and mecha designer are the most efficient," Xie Xie said.

"That's the efficient one," Lin Hao said. "Not the appropriate one."

"And blacksmithing is the appropriate one."

"She said it's the most appropriate profession for creating powerful battle armors," Lin Hao said. "I've been a blacksmith since I was six. I did not know that was a road. I thought it was just what my father did, and then what Old Zhou did, and then what I did."

"And now it's a road."

"Now it's a road," Lin Hao said.

"I'm going to be a mecha maker," Xie Xie said. "I've been saving contribution points. I'm going to make the mecha, and you're going to make the metal, and I said that on a seawall and I meant it, and now there's a woman in a hall saying it's a road, and I want you to know that I said it first."

Lin Hao looked at him.

"You said it first," Lin Hao said.

"I want that on record."

"It's not on record," Lin Hao said.

"It's on record now," Xie Xie said. "I'm putting it on record. You'll make the metal, I'll make the mecha, and a woman in a hall said it's the most appropriate profession, and I want you to know that I have been getting ready for a thing since I was six too, and I did not know what the thing was either, and now I know, and it's a mecha, and you're going to make the metal for it."

Lin Hao had been getting ready for a thing since he was six. He had not known what the thing was. And now two people — a woman in a hall and a boy on a seawall — had told him what the thing was, and it was a battle armor, and he was going to make the metal for it, and he had been making metal since he was six.

"Then it's on record," Lin Hao said. "You'll make the mecha. I'll make the metal. And I have been getting ready for it since I was six, and I did not know it, and now I know it, and I am not going to stop."

---

## Part 5: The Requirement

There was a requirement.

Shen Yi had said it at the end of the lecture, because she said everything, and she did not repeat herself.

"The requirement to become a one-word battle armor master is five soul rings and the third rank in your second profession."

Lin Hao had three soul rings.

He was a fourth-rank Grandmaster, which was past the third rank in his second profession, so he had that. But he had three soul rings, and the requirement was five, and the fourth ring needed a second spirit soul, and a second spirit soul was a thing you did not find at a forge.

He was at the gate. He had been at the gate since the dormitory. And the gate was the last rank before the thing he did not have, and the thing he did not have was a second spirit soul, and the lecture had said the road and the road was long, and he was ten, and the road went to five rings.

He was not frightened. He had been at a lot of bottoms this month. He understood now that the bottom was not a place, it was a thing you stood at until you understood it, and he was standing at one, and the road was long, and he had been getting ready for it since he was six.

## Part 6: What Lin Hao Wrote

He wrote it that night, on the floor of the working-student dormitory, because there were five of them and four beds and the floor was still his.

*Three hundred and twenty-four. One thousand four hundred and sixty-six. Forty.*

*A woman in a hall said metal forging is a job that earns contribution points, and contribution points buy food and spirit souls and entrance to the spirit ascension platform. I have been a blacksmith since I was six. I have been getting ready for a thing since I was six and I did not know what the thing was.*

*She said blacksmithing is the most appropriate profession for creating powerful battle armors. Xie Xie said on a seawall that I'll make the metal and he'll make the mecha. Two people have now told me what the thing is. It's a battle armor. I'm going to make the metal for it.*

*The requirement for a one-word battle armor is five soul rings and the third rank in your second profession. I'm a fourth-rank Grandmaster, so I have the profession. I have three rings. The fourth ring needs a second spirit soul, and a second spirit soul is a thing you don't find at a forge.*

He set the pen down, because there was one more thing.

*I am at the gate. I have been at the gate since the dormitory. The gate is the last rank before the thing I don't have, and the thing I don't have is a second spirit soul, and the road is long, and I have been getting ready for it since I was six, and I am not frightened, because I have been at a lot of bottoms this month and I understand now that the bottom is not a place, it's a thing you stand at until you understand it.*

He closed the book.

The lock at his temple lay gold across his cheekbone, and the marks on his forearms were raised, and the hawk turned over in him — *one thousand four hundred and sixty-six* — and did not pull toward anything, and Lin Hao lay on the floor of a slum dormitory at the greatest academy in the world, and he was at the gate, and the road was long, and he had been getting ready for it since he was six.
