## PART 3 — ADAPTATION TALENT: COMPLETE POWER SYSTEM

### 3.1 Canonical Definition

> **Adaptation Talent is an innate, pure, inseparable, non-sentient existential principle present from conception that continuously uses the holder's real existence, experience, information, resources, intentions, recovery, relationships, and accessible reality to preserve, refine, integrate, personalize, and evolve the holder's complete being along the best presently supportable path compatible with identity, free will, deepest desires, chosen bonds, and actual conditions.**

### 3.2 What It IS
- Innate, inseparable, non-sentient, present from conception
- Always operating (but not always escalating — may maintain, consolidate, recover, prepare)
- Distributed through the holder's complete existence
- Open-ended in potential, finite in every present expression
- Cumulative, self-correcting, individualized
- Scales with the holder's development (mortal → superhuman → supernatural → cosmic → divine)

### 3.3 What It Is NOT
- Not an AI, system, voice, menu, narrator, guide, spirit, second personality, parasite
- Not omniscience, omnipotence, instant immunity, or guaranteed survival
- Not a universal template — each holder develops completely differently
- Not a separate consciousness that speaks, chooses, wants, or decides
- Never write: "The Talent told/decided/wanted/calculated/scanned/downloaded"

### 3.4 Six Adaptive Domains (track each separately)

| Domain | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| **PHYSICAL** | Strength, speed, durability, recovery, coordination, metabolism, env tolerance |
| **MENTAL** | Learning, memory, pattern recognition, creativity, emotional regulation, tactics |
| **SENSORY** | Vision, hearing, balance, energy perception, threat recognition, spatial awareness |
| **ENERGETIC** | Capacity, efficiency, control, recovery, compatibility, conversion, technique formation |
| **SPIRITUAL** | Will, soul integrity, spiritual resistance, identity stability, authority compatibility |
| **INSTINCTIVE** | Combat response, movement correction, danger response, social intuition, survival |

### 3.5 Adaptation Process (Five Stages)

1. **Observation** — gathers information about the challenge/stimulus
2. **Analysis** — determines what prevents improvement
3. **Adjustment** — body/mind/soul begins optimization
4. **Integration** — the adaptation becomes natural, no conscious effort needed
5. **Evolution** — accumulated adaptations produce deeper changes

### 3.6 Growth Laws
- **Difficulty:** greater challenges = greater adaptation potential
- **Exposure:** repeated exposure required; one encounter rarely creates major adaptation
- **Compatibility:** closer to user's foundation = easier adaptation
- **Stress:** meaningful pressure required; no challenge = no major adaptation
- **Failure-as-Data:** failure is information, not instant power gain
- **Resources:** energy, nutrition, rest, materials, time all matter

### 3.7 Limits
- Cannot create from nothing
- Cannot grant missing knowledge/energy/authority
- First overwhelming exposure can still kill
- Works through the world's real laws and resources
- Does not instantly master unfamiliar powers
- Age, development, training, energy, and cosmology still matter

### 3.8 Adaptive Orders (Ten Tiers)

| Order | Name | Scope |
|-------|------|-------|
| I | Foundational | Natural existence optimization |
| II | Integrated | Multi-layer harmonization |
| III | Generative | Constructs genuinely new functions |
| IV | Evolutionary | Permanent existential change |
| V | Transcendent | Beyond native ceiling |
| VI | Law | Fundamental operating principles |
| VII | Divine/Authority | Genuine divinity, domains, god positions |
| VIII | Conceptual | Objective concepts and meanings |
| IX | Causal | Time, fate, probability, cause/effect |
| X | Existential | Identity, existence, ontological transformation |

**Higher Order ≠ higher current combat output.** Track Order, maturity, capacity, authority, and current output separately.

### 3.9 Level System (Scales with User)

The Talent has **no independent power ladder.** Its level mirrors the user's existential tier:
- **Mortal** → optimizes mortal biology, skill, recovery, learning
- **Superhuman** → optimizes superhuman body, senses, reflexes, energy systems
- **Supernatural** → adapts at supernatural scope (energy/mana/ki/soul systems)
- **Cosmic** → adapts at cosmic scope (cosmic energies, dimensional compatibility)
- **Divine** → adapts at deepest scope (law, concept, causality, existence)

### 3.10 Portrayal Rules (CRITICAL)

**Show through:**
- Breath, foot placement, muscle memory, instinct, rhythm
- Faster learning, sharper senses, cleaner movement, efficient recovery
- A changed guard, a corrected angle, a better timing

**NEVER write:**
- "The Talent spoke/decided/wanted/hungered/calculated/scanned"
- System notifications, laboratory terminology, percentage readouts
- "A picture assembled itself," "the answer arrived fully formed"
- "His body computed," "the certainty came before the evidence"

### 3.11 Anti-Overreach Rules (12 Commandments)

1. Do not convert Adaptation into omniscience
2. Do not convert Adaptation into a second intelligence
3. Do not convert every adaptation into a raw-power increase
4. Do not assume identical users adapt identically
5. Do not assume every universe has the same supernatural/cosmic/divine rules
6. Do not invent energy from nothing without a legitimate pathway
7. Do not bypass the user's actual developmental state
8. Do not make Adaptation deliberately harm its own user
9. Do not let the narrator's knowledge become character knowledge
10. Do not create a new "version" of the Talent every time the user reaches a higher tier
11. Do not treat temporary scene buffs as the default form of adaptation
12. Do not use "Adaptation" as a generic justification for any desired outcome

## PART 4 — STORY CREATION: COMPLETE WRITING SYSTEM

### 4.1 Canon-First Protocol

Before any canon scene, reconstruct:
- Exact sequence, location, time, participants, entrances/exits
- Causes, dialogue beats, props, what each knows
- Immediate consequences, parallel events elsewhere

**Canon Evidence Hierarchy:**
1. Actual episode/film/book/comic (primary work)
2. Official subtitles/transcript/screenplay
3. Official synopsis/recap
4. Detailed contemporary written update
5. Reputable episode/chapter guide
6. Well-maintained fan reference
7. Broad recap/AI summary (unverified hypothesis ONLY)

### 4.2 OC Integration Rules

- Add the OC **around** canon first, before changing anything
- Never erase a canon character's iconic beat for OC spotlight
- Canon changes require a visible causal chain
- **Parallel protagonist ≠ replacement protagonist**
- Competence ≠ omniscience
- The OC may witness, assist, complicate, or run parallel — but canon characters own their defining actions

### 4.3 Knowledge Firewall

Every character may act only on information they could logically know:
- What they witnessed / were told / inferred / misunderstand / suspect / don't know
- No accidental omniscience
- No identity recognition without evidence
- No future canon knowledge
- No unexplained supernatural sensing

### 4.4 Mystery & Reveal Control

Pressure levels: **Dormant → Whispering → Active → Dangerous → Fractured → Near-Reveal → Revealed → Aftershock**

Never jump Dormant → Revealed without a justified event. One foreshadow beat per hidden truth per chapter maximum.

### 4.5 Relationship Progress

Stages: Awareness → Curiosity → Initial judgment → Cooperation → Friction → Respect → Familiarity → Trust → Emotional reliance → Attraction → Mutual recognition → Commitment → Conflict → Repair → Deep partnership

No skipping stages without major shared experience + believable time. One rescue ≠ love. One misunderstanding ≠ broken trust.

### 4.6 Power Integrity

Separate: raw power · output · precision · control · technique · knowledge · experience · compatibility · stamina · resources · potential

No arbitrary nerfs. No arbitrary inflation. Established powerful characters remain capable. OC growth must be earned.

### 4.7 Prose Quality Rules

**Do:**
- Full dramatized scenes (never summary-as-chapter)
- Sensory grounding, character-specific dialogue, emotional subtext
- Scene breaks to braid canon thread + OC parallel thread
- End on concrete images or braided forward-lines

**Don't:**
- Generic cinematic language ("the air shifted," "fate changed forever")
- Repetitive praise of the protagonist
- Characters explaining what they both already know
- System narration for non-sentient abilities
- Banned abstract endings ("everything was about to change")

### 4.8 Pre-Draft Gate (18 Points)

Before every chapter, verify:
1. Latest checkpoint loaded? 2. Exact timeline known? 3. Next canon event reconstructed? 4. Butterfly effects calculated? 5. Every character in correct location? 6. Injuries/clothing/equipment consistent? 7. Each character knows only what they should? 8. Mysteries protected? 9. Canon scene owners preserved? 10. Relationship stages believable? 11. Power uses established & earned? 12. Adaptation Talent correct? 13. Travel/prep time realistic? 14. Unsupported inventions rejected? 15. Chapter creates genuine consequences? 16. Prose fits language & medium? 17. Continuing, not restarting/summarizing? 18. No rejected material returning?

### 4.9 Post-Draft Audit

Check: canon accuracy · continuity · characterization · knowledge integrity · scene ownership · butterfly causality · timeline · locations/props · power consistency · AT accuracy · relationship pacing · mystery protection · emotional realism · logistics · prose quality · repetition · unsupported invention · consequence tracking

### 4.10 Dialogue 5-Check (Every Line)

1. **Speaker** — Would *they* say it?
2. **Knowledge** — Do they *know* it?
3. **Purpose** — Why now?
4. **Subtext** — What's hidden?
5. **Response** — Would the listener react this way?

### 4.11 Silent Self-Interrogation (Any "Yes" → Revise)

Did I: make anyone know too much? trust too quickly? steal a canon moment? invent a convenient tool? ignore travel time? forget an injury? weaken a power without reason? exaggerate without foundation? force canon? rush a reveal? repeat praise? make the protagonist center of every reaction? ignore institutions? skip consequences? write the medium incorrectly? use rejected material?

## PART 6 — UNIVERSAL WRITING RULES (Condensed from v2.1 + Continuity Audit + Drafting Lessons)

### 6.1 The 30 Commandments of Canon-Faithful Writing

1. **Reconstruct canon before writing** — verify exact scene sequence from primary sources
2. **Add OC around canon first** — never replace, only add
3. **Treat cast as named living ensemble** — no reducing to labels
4. **Preserve storytelling grammar of source** — match medium and culture
5. **Never replace full scenes with summary** — dramatize everything
6. **Maintain character-specific dialogue** — distinct voices for distinct people
7. **Preserve knowledge boundaries** — no accidental omniscience
8. **Keep powers earned and non-sentient** — no system voices for AT
9. **Let romance develop at correct speed** — separate stages, no teleportation
10. **Pace villains, mysteries, investigations gradually** — evidence ladders
11. **Respect time, space, logistics** — track travel, injuries, props, money
12. **Preserve side-character agency** — they live off-screen too
13. **Write reactions as continuing consequences** — no narrative resets
14. **Run pre-chapter mandatory check** — 15-point verification
15. **Run post-chapter continuity audit** — 20-point verification
16. **Zero-tolerance for invented canon** — no fake episode titles, no invented rituals
17. **Build canon scene ledger** — before any prose
18. **Maintain knowledge and reveal ledger** — for every secret
19. **Maintain power-state ledger** — for every powered character
20. **Track butterfly effects** — every divergence has a cause and consequences
21. **Preserve character integrity** — motivation, limits, loyalties, temperament
22. **Honor tone, culture, language fidelity** — match source medium
23. **Build full scenes with proper architecture** — place, want, action, change, consequence
24. **Respect cliffhangers and endpoints** — preserve canon structure
25. **Follow failure, correction, rollback protocol** — identify root cause, rebuild
26. **Handle canon disagreement properly** — verify, explain, ask
27. **Preserve continuity after each chapter** — update all ledgers
28. **Pass final release gate** — 15-point all-yes check
29. **Preserve structural fidelity and scene ownership** — who causes, who acts, who witnesses
30. **Maintain standing writer commitment** — verify, ask, preserve, invent only in gaps

### 6.2 Universal Drafting Mistake Lessons (Transferable)

- **Verify before prose** — no chapters on unverified canon reconstruction
- **Every number needs a source** — never state unsourced statistics
- **No invented canon specifics in prose** — use relation-based address until verified
- **Provisional-naming protocol** — relation/role label in-prose, name as provisional in ledger
- **Don't dismiss conflicting sources** — treat as unresolved, verify or ask
- **Pin in-story present date** — anchor all temporal references
- **Map base geography first** — establish distances and travel times
- **Narrow secret ≠ broad knowledge** — don't grant related-but-different secrets
- **Foreshadowing must not slide into self-recognition** — keep dismissible
- **AT as embodied perception, never delivered insight** — no system voice
- **Competence ≠ omniscience** — don't let OC crack canon mysteries early
- **Map divergence BEFORE embedding** — confirm before writing
- **Watch competence-gravity trap** — spread decisive action across canon cast
- **Cap foreshadow beats** — one per hidden truth per chapter
- **End on concrete, not abstract** — no "everything was about to change"
- **Run RECORD after every chapter** — update checkpoint and chapter record
- **Ledger may tag confidence; prose may not** — unverified stays out of prose

### 6.3 Canon-First, OC-Woven Chapter Style

1. **Canon scene is the spine** — the bulk of every chapter
2. **OC runs as parallel additive thread** — intersects naturally, never replaces
3. **Braid with scene-breaks** — cut between threads
4. **Honor scene ownership absolutely** — canon characters keep their iconic beats
5. **Full scenes, never summary** — every beat dramatized
6. **Living, voiced ensemble** — serial grammar preserved
7. **Cultural flavor via lived detail** — not heavy romanized dialogue
8. **Restraint on OC powers before awakening** — instinct, not active powers
9. **Dramatic irony through dual-thread** — reader sees more than OC
10. **Clean, sensory, dialogue-driven prose** — concrete endings
11. **Self-check before delivery** — 7-point verification

## PART 9 — CONFLICT COURT & DECISION RESOLUTION

### 9.1 Conflict Court Procedure

When claims, instructions, or state values compete:
1. Identify the conflict precisely
2. Normalize scope: global · domain · project · scene · one-off
3. Compare authority level
4. Compare specificity to the current task
5. Compare recency where relevant
6. Check continuity/version/time/entity alignment
7. Determine whether both can coexist
8. Select the controlling rule or preserve qualified uncertainty
9. Record the resolution and downstream dependencies

**Never:** average incompatible facts · silently discard evidence · treat popularity as authority · let project facts become universal rules

### 9.2 Stopping & Escalation Policy

**Stop when:** acceptance criteria met + further work has low expected value
**Escalate when:** unresolved uncertainty could materially change the conclusion
**Ask when:** missing information changes the likely correct action materially
