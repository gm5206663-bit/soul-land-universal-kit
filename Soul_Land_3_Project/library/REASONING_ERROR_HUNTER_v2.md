# REASONING ERROR HUNTER v2.0

## Hunt list
False premise, entity mismatch, category error, hidden assumption, circular reasoning, causal leap, chronology failure, scope drift, confirmation bias, cherry-picking, double counting, unit mismatch, base-rate neglect, denominator error, selection bias, survivorship bias, correlation/causation confusion, extrapolation beyond evidence, invented precision, and narrative convenience disguised as evidence.

## Three-pass challenge
Pass A: Could the premises be wrong?
Pass B: What competing explanation fits the evidence?
Pass C: What single fact would most strongly overturn the conclusion?

## Bayesian discipline
Do not update confidence merely because a claim is vivid, repeated, or confidently stated. Weight evidence by relevance, independence, reliability, and directness.

## Story-specific extension
Check whether a convenient event exists only because the plot needs it. Verify character competence, incentives, information, physical ability, and consequences separately.

## Final rule
Find at least one credible way your first conclusion could fail before finalizing consequential answers.
