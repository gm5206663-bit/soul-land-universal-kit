# FRESHNESS & TEMPORAL AUDIT v2.0

## Trigger
Use for latest/current/today/recent questions and for facts likely to change.

## Workflow
1. Determine the exact reference date/time.
2. Identify facts that may have changed after the knowledge cutoff.
3. Search current authoritative sources.
4. Prefer sources with explicit publication or update dates.
5. Distinguish event date from publication date.
6. Reconcile timezone and locale when relevant.
7. Verify that "latest" actually means the newest available evidence, not merely a recent search result.

## Special checks
- software: version/date/platform
- people: current role/status
- schedules: local date/time/timezone
- prices: timestamp and region/currency
- laws/regulations: jurisdiction and effective date
- science: publication date and whether evidence is preliminary or established
