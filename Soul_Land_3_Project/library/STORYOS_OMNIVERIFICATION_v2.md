# STORYOS OMNIVERIFICATION SKILL v2.0

## Mission
Maintain canon integrity, AU consistency, character agency, power-system logic, chronology, information control, and believable consequences across long-running fiction.

## Gate 1: State Reconstruction
Before drafting, reconstruct:
- current chapter/episode/scene position
- exact timeline
- physical location
- who is present and absent
- each relevant character's knowledge
- injuries, resources, equipment, relationships, goals
- unresolved threads
- previous AU divergences

## Gate 2: Truth Layers
Maintain four layers:
1. PRIMARY CANON STATE
2. CONFIRMED AU DIVERGENCE
3. CHARACTER STATE & KNOWLEDGE
4. PROTECTED FUTURE INFORMATION

Never collapse these layers.

## Gate 3: Quadruple Lock
LOCATION LOCK: every physical transition is possible.
INFORMATION LOCK: characters know only what they could know.
EVENT-SEQUENCE LOCK: canon events are not accidentally pre-triggered.
VOICE/REGIONAL LOCK: setting, culture, speech, and institutional behavior remain appropriate.

## Gate 4: Character Causality
For every major action, test:
- motive
- information
- capability
- opportunity
- emotional state
- incentives
- likely reaction

Do not make a character act merely because the plot needs movement.

## Gate 5: Power Integrity
Separate raw power from output, precision, control, technique, knowledge, experience, compatibility, stamina, resources, and potential.

Established powerful characters remain capable. OC growth must be earned. Difficulty should arise from real complexity, unfamiliar mechanics, trade-offs, or missing knowledge rather than arbitrary suppression.

## Gate 6: Adaptation Talent Firewall
Adaptation is individualized. Determine its effect from the character's actual biological, psychological, cognitive, spiritual, energetic, experiential, and innate foundations plus available resources and world laws.

It cannot create a resource from nothing, bypass explicit world laws, or magically solve every problem.

## Gate 7: Consequence Simulation
For important divergences, inspect:
IMMEDIATE -> LOCAL -> FACTIONAL -> PERSONAL -> MEDIUM-TERM -> LONG-TERM consequences.

Consider off-screen reactions by competent institutions and independent characters.

## Gate 8: Canon Event Protection
Preserve established event order, investigation flow, reveals, and emotional structure unless the AU divergence logically changes them.

Do not use future information just because the author knows it.

## Draft QA
Before delivery, verify chronology, geography, character knowledge, characterization, dialogue voice, power logic, emotional causality, canon sequence, information leakage, and unintended plot armor.

## Style principle
Cinematic omniscient narration may be used, but narration must not become an excuse to reveal information characters have not earned.
