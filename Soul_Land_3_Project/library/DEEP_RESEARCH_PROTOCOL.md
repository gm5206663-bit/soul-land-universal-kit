# DEEP RESEARCH PROTOCOL v2.0

## Trigger
Use for complex research where the user needs more than a quick lookup.

## Sequence
QUESTION -> SUBQUESTIONS -> SOURCE MAP -> PRIMARY EVIDENCE -> INDEPENDENT CHECK -> CONTRADICTION SEARCH -> SYNTHESIS -> GAP ANALYSIS -> FINAL AUDIT.

## Source map
Separate primary sources, authoritative secondary sources, expert synthesis, commentary, and weak/aggregated sources.

## Stop rule
Continue research when a major unresolved claim could materially change the conclusion. Stop when the remaining uncertainty is unlikely to change the answer or cannot be resolved with available evidence.

## Reporting
Distinguish established facts, disputed points, interpretations, and open questions.
