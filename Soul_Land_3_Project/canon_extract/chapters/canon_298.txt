Chapter 298 - Meteoric Iron
(fetched 2026-09-03 — ORE ch109, workshop world). Wulin is carried to a LARGE MESSY WORKSHOP: two shelves full of rare metals (some near-impossible without a master teacher), a huge sparkling-silver high-grade forging table. The red-haired man: "Can you spirit refine?" Wulin: "I succeeded once by chance." The man: "Show me your luck again." Wulin refuses (no third ring yet; failure = grave injury); man: "Then thousand refine for me. I want to watch." Wulin's condition: let me forge any metal I like and keep it. Agreed instantly. Wulin chooses TWO ashen-gray chunks off the lowest shelf = METEORIC IRON (the most precious in the room; the man panics "NOT those ones").
METEORIC IRON: a first-rate metal not formed naturally on the Douluo continent — only extracted from meteorites; extremely dense/heavy; contains unusual energy; weapons forged from it have a PENETRATING effect (common for powerful mecha weapons); very rare; recognized by the dark-golden lines within (Purple Demon Eyes). It is IMPOSSIBLE to spirit refine, so it is used only for one-word battle armor or mechas — which is why the man ever permitted it. The advanced forging table can calcine FIVE metals at once, with a soul battery reaching higher temperatures. The man threatens: if you can't at least second-grade thousand refine it, you'll work for me until you pay off the two chunks of meteoric iron. Wulin strikes — his SPIRIT-REFINED HAMMERS (the thousand-refined hammers that lucked to spirit-refined) ring out; their wonders revealed in the first strike.
