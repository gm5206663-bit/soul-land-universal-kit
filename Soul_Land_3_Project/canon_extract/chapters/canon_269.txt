CANON 269 — Xie Xie's Terrifying Opponent (readnovelfull ch-272 = novelhall-11723 #269; fetched live 2026-09-01, title-verified; RENUMBERED 09-01 after the canon-index correction — was mislabeled 268 — RESERVED FOR CH88)

Not only had Tang Wulin won... it had been a stomp.

"Was he waiting for this the entire time?" Even though Xie Xie muttered this under his breath, Shen Yi heard him clearly. He's just a thirteen-year-old kid! How could he be so patient? Unknown to Shen Yi, Tang Wulin's second profession was that of a lonesome blacksmith. Since the day he met Mang Tian, his temperament had been taken to the grindstone and continuously sharpened. He had been forging since he was six years old; seven years was more than enough to forever alter a person — let alone the peerless blacksmithing prodigy that was Tang Wulin.

Student number ten soon woke, staring at Tang Wulin in disbelief. No matter how she looked at it, she couldn't understand how he had snatched victory with his soul power depleted. What is up with his body? And just what was that purple light in his eyes? She had fainted in the final moments, and the snow had obscured everyone's line of sight.

"Next," Shen Yi said.

Xie Xie and student number two, the slender one, entered the arena at the same time. Tang Wulin returned to his companions, immediately sat down cross-legged and began meditating with the Mysterious Heaven Method. His opponent had pressured him immensely, draining all his soul power. Two mysterious trials remained; he already had the sixty points required to pass, but he still needed strength to help his teammates. His number one priority was the entire team entering Shrek Academy. For his sake, his teammates had nearly lost their chance at the sacred academy; though he never said it, such loyalty was engraved on his heart.

"Begin!" Both burst into action. Xie Xie charged — an agility-type through and through, close quarters his only option. Student number two rushed as well, his eyes possessing an eerie calm beyond his years. Xie Xie had the edge in speed. Neither had released a martial soul yet; both waited for an opening. Their soul rings appeared when the gap had shrunk to ten meters: identical configurations, two yellow and one purple.

Xie Xie wielded the Light Dragon Dagger; his opponent underwent a monstrous transformation. The instant the rings appeared, student number two's slender body swelled, his purple soul ring flashing. "He's already using his thousand-year soul skill?" His body inflated like a balloon, clothes bursting into shreds, leaving only insanely elastic undergarments. Brown hair covered him; humanoid still, but not human — four meters tall, two burly arms on broad shoulders, the body rumbling with raw power.

Xie Xie wanted to run back and give Tang Wulin a big thumbs up for his suggestion. We really can't judge Shrek Academy students by appearances alone! This guy's martial soul has to be a giant gorilla! Maybe it's even the…

Two massive arms recklessly smashed the ground. Xie Xie leaped, unleashing a golden blade of light from the Light Dragon Dagger; lines cut into the walls and floor, rippling golden light countering the quaking ground. The shock wave left him astonished — a second slower and he would have immediately lost. Student number two didn't dodge the Light Dragon Blade; he met it head-on and shot toward Xie Xie, unaffected, his leap surpassing an agility-type's, arms swinging with the might of a mountain.

Unable to exert force in the air, Xie Xie split into three clones, each kicking off the other two, speeding in three directions. Shock filled the faces of the Shrek Academy students — Xie Xie had actually picked student number two, the strongest of the ten arranged opponents! The three clones didn't faze number two: his first soul ring lit, and he threw three lighting-fast punches, the air hissing — one blast for each clone. Heavens! What sort of monster is this guy? The clones kicked off thin air to escape by a hair's breadth. A trace of surprise flashed through his opponent's eyes — Xie Xie's third soul ring had lit a second time.

Both returned to the ground. Student number two held the upper hand, but not an overwhelming one. The attacker always exhausted faster than the defender; with roughly equal ranks, Xie Xie seized a tiny advantage in theory — reality yet to test it. His clever response had been costly: Light Dragon Clones for breathing room, then Shadow Dragon Clones against the three punches — maybe leaving him more tired than his opponent. He could only rejoice in the Mysterious Heaven Method's recovery power, confident he could barely outlast.

Student number two patiently observed the three clones — he couldn't attack three directions at once. As a Shrek Academy student he had an abundance of combat experience; he had a hunch his opponent would strike soon — sustaining three clones of a thousand-year soul skill had to be a huge drain on Xie Xie's soul power, leaving him no other option.

And student number two's hunch was correct.
