Chapter 290 - Ceramic Mountain
(fetched fresh 2026-09-03, readnovelfull, slug chapter-290-ceramic-mountain — ORE for ch105)

Gu Yue, Xie Xie, and Xu Xiaoyan had gotten trays and sat at a neighbouring table, pretending not to know Tang Wulin out of embarrassment. Wulin, with a free pass in his pocket, was determined to eat a mountain's worth. Xie Peichen stood behind him at first, but as he took in Wulin's gorging, his jaw dropped. People gathered, drawn like moths. Wulin kept sending Xie Peichen for more food — ten orders of the sea-cucumber-looking dish, and again, and again.

The dining hall manager, a middle-aged man with a grim pinched face, was called when the kitchen reported a food shortage. He saw the staggering pile of dishes. "Hey you. Your stomach is beyond normal. The Academy can't feed someone like you. I'll petition to remove your free-meal right." Wulin blinked: "Teacher, don't worry. I'm a working student — I can't eat free starting tomorrow. This is my last free meal."

Everyone's expression changed at "working student." The peanut gallery retreated. "So you're a working student! Alright, have a good meal," the manager said, and left. "Why didn't you tell me you were a working student earlier?" Xie Peichen asked, flustered, then groaned and disappeared into the crowd.

An older girl placed a steamed bun on Wulin's plate: "He's so pitiful, still not full." Others followed. Wulin realized a hundred points was only five or six normal meals; the meal today cost over a thousand points. The cheapest dish was ten points.

Wulin staggered out, bursting. "Everyone in the outer court recognizes you by now," Xie Xie said. They went to Xie Xie's promised treat, strolling the outer-court street of warm orange lights. They found a busy drink shop, got a table, ordered juice and ice cream. Gu Yue and Xiaoyan dug into the ice cream. Xie Xie smirked: "I'm scouting for pretty senior sisters — preparing for adulthood."
