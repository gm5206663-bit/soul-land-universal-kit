CANON 266 — To Battle (readnovelfull ch-269 = novelhall-11723 #266; fetched live 2026-09-01, title-verified; the chapter the canon index revealed was missing — fetched after renumbering)

The opponents class zero would face had only joined the academy a grade before them — not much age gap, but all geniuses who had passed Shrek's entrance exam and been educated by Shrek. This was a trial of true combat, a battle between two soul masters.

Shen Yi, an air of indifference in her words: "You may begin selecting your opponents. Once you've made your choice, you may no longer change your mind — all selections are final. This trial's only rule is that you are not permitted to use soul devices during battle. You have ten minutes to choose."

Tang Wulin gathered his teammates and lowered his voice. "I have already put together an order for us to go in. I'll be the first, second will be Xie Xie, and Gu Yue will follow afterwards. Xiaoyan, you'll go last. Xie Xie, Gu Yue, you two need to drag out your battles for as long as possible. Especially you, Xie Xie. It should be easy for you since you're an agility-type soul master. We need to stall for nightfall so that Xiaoyan can use her Starwheel Ice Staff." Xu Xiaoyan's eyes sparkled as she nodded. Gu Yue also gave a slight nod. "As for selecting your opponents… Just pick whoever you feel is best."

Generally, a soul master's outward appearance reflected the qualities of their martial soul — power-types robust, range/spiritual-types delicate. Xie Xie's gaze settled on a strong-looking student; about to announce it, he was stopped by Tang Wulin's arm and a hushed voice: "Let me remind you all that this is Shrek Academy, the legendary Monster Academy. I'm sure you're aware of the school's motto: they only accept monsters, not ordinary people. That means we can't judge our opponents as we normally would. Looking at them from the opposite perspective might even prove more accurate." Xie Xie was struck as if by lightning and gave a big thumbs up, re-examining the ten. Attribute compatibility was crucial at lower levels: an agility-type facing a control-type would be thoroughly subdued; power-types feared range-types. Despite his confidence, the world had no shortage of geniuses — especially here, in the continent's number one academy.

THE PICKS: Xie Xie pointed: "I choose number two as my opponent." — the slender figure, almost certainly an agility-type (the reverse reading: the strongest hidden in a slight frame). Xu Xiaoyan picked the sturdy man Xie Xie had nearly picked: number six. Gu Yue said, "Number one" — the first student had the most ordinary appearance of the ten, lacking any unique traits whatsoever. Last, Tang Wulin: "I choose number ten" — a tall, slender girl whose eyes displayed a trace of arrogance; looks above average, a notch below Xu Xiaoyan's, about Gu Yue's level.

Shen Yi: "I will be supervising this match. You must listen to my commands during the match and you must stop the moment I tell you to. Understood?" — directed at Tang Wulin, who answered with deference. "Good. Get ready… Begin!"

Without superfluous words, student number ten shot forward like a cheetah — Xie Xie gaped: he had always been confident in his speed, but couldn't even compare with hers. Three soul rings, two yellow one purple; ears twitching, silver streaks in her hair, pupils squished into vertical slits. A beast martial soul — the Snow Leopard, rarely seen, cold climates, speed and ice-attribute attacks. A smile blossomed on Tang Wulin's lips: what agility-types feared most were control-types! As a control-type himself he held the advantage — and he possessed the Purple Demon Eyes. He may be nowhere near as fast as her, but his eyes could keep up.

Two purple soul rings appeared beneath him; Bluesilver Grass shot out like a giant net. Then the startling scene: the girl halted in the air, snowflakes exploding out of her body as her first soul ring lit. The temperature plummeted; popping noises blanketed the air as streams of snow collided with the grass, which turned sluggish — had it not evolved several times already, it might have shattered. Isn't she an agility-type? These snowflakes seem to come from a control or assault-type! She's so quick, yet she isn't approaching like an agility-type? Shrek Academy really is filled with a bunch of deceptive freaks.

The blizzard grew; the grass couldn't penetrate the wall of snow. Tang Wulin turned tail without hesitation — behind him the grass spun like a blender, whipping away snowflakes. "Coward," his opponent coldly snorted, willing the snow to pursue; she vanished amidst the blizzard, the snow gathering into the form of a leopard. The students' task differed from the examinees': they were tasked with ending battles as quickly as possible — the faster, the better the reward. Her second soul ring lit; the leopard's appearance dropped the temperature further, slowing his grass even more; it broke through and rushed him. Tang Wulin fled in a winding path, a sorry figure, grass whipping at the pursuer. The girl held the arena's center: Trying to stall for time? Then let's see just how quick you are!

After running a while, Tang Wulin felt something was amiss. There was something off about the temperature.
