(fetched 2026-09-03 — ORE ch108). OPENS: "Since Elder Cai left the lecture hall, Shen Yi replaced her at the lectern."
Shen Yi's rules: Shrek/Shrek City is a powerful INDEPENDENT org; laws/punishments at Shrek's sole discretion, the Federation has no jurisdiction ("punishment unrelated to whatever the Federation decides"). ID card holds info + contribution points. POINTS buy EVERYTHING in the outer court: cultivation resources, spirit souls, special-facility time, personal teacher guidance, spirit ascension platform entrance, (working students) food. Cannot BUY points with money — earn via JOBS (physical labor, metal forging, mecha design/making/maintenance); points transferable between students (private deals unprotected; public platform at small cost); points valid while Shrek stands; if expelled you get to spend your points first.
CLASSES ONLY IN THE MORNING; afternoons/evenings free (study or go into the city); don't miss morning class; tardiness = absence; THREE absences = expulsion. No bringing Federation-money-bought items on campus — fined TEN times the price in points (smuggling not advised). Working students pay for own meals; off-campus eating wastes TIME (the key resource; fall behind = expelled). ELIMINATION: the BOTTOM FIVE of the yearly minor exam are expelled — "Shrek Academy only raises monsters." Optional courses list later (normal students: some free; working students pay for all optional classes; resources cost points). If you reach NEGATIVE ONE THOUSAND points you are expelled.

"Some of you wonder why the first class is about battle armor masters — because you already learned the basics before you came or you wouldn't have passed." One-word by THIRTY-FIVE = graduate the outer court; only THIRTY-THREE PERCENT of outer students graduate. One-word by TWENTY-FIVE or younger = eligible for INNER COURT. "Unlike what you think, you don't have that much time." Then: "What is a battle armor master? Most of you will say they're just more powerful mecha pilots." (nods) — "Then you are all gravely mistaken."
