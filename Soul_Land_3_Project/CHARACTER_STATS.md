ch 13 to ch 61, Xie Xie at rank 21 from ch 17 to ch 61, Wei Xiaofeng at 22 from ch 21 to ch 61,
Gu Yue was never given a rank at all, and Wang Jinxi / Zhang Yangzi were labelled "gone" /
"~27" with no schedule behind either. Canon has explicit numbers for all of them and explicit
events (two of them transfer out of class zero) that the story never staged.

c323 "not far from rank 30", c335 "broke through to rank 29". The four source PDFs were numbered
independently. **Order by internal evidence (ranks, ages, events), never by file number.**
