## THE INHABITANTS — non-sentient by law (AT §74.4; the Character Law)

The hawk and the jiao never advise, speak, or answer in words. They are **weather and water**:
- **The hawk** = the sky. Its moods are his reflexes and his temper. It *asks* ("Can you see — without me?") the way weather asks — by being a certain way.
- **The jiao** = the coil. Vast, patient, older than the conversation. It turns once in a great while. When it turns, his regeneration runs; when it speaks — and it has TRIED once (ch71, ninety-second mark: "something in him that was also something in it tried, gently, to speak with his mouth") — that is a crossing-class event, never a chat. **THE ABYSSAL SYLLABLE: a planted, unrequested future gate.** The first time the deep water says ONE syllable in his own voice, on purpose, is a power moment comparable to the lightning crossing — held, not scheduled, tracked now as thread C1 below.
- **The adaptation** = the country itself reshaping; visible as the map being different between visits.

## RULES FOR USING THE COUNTRY

1. **Non-sentience absolute.** Weather doesn't talk. No inner dialogues, no tutorial beasts. The Country reveals through image, pressure, temperature, light.
2. **Show once per arc, under growth or extremity** — meditation, a near-loss, a crossing. Never every chapter; a place seen too often loses its depth.
3. **Every sighting, the map has changed** (the adaptation is the medium) — he notices what is different; that IS the growth update, shown not told.
4. **It is not an asspull vault.** Powers are never first used inside it. C3 lets him REHEARSE; the first real use is in the world. It holds promises, it doesn't invent ability.
5. **From his eyes only.** (Perspective panels of it from outside — the medic's mirror-glimpse once, ch102 — are her image, never a tour.)
6. **The image bank (STYLE_GOLD register, grow as written):** the smoked glass · clouds moving the wrong way · lightning fishing far up · a continent turning in its sleep · the salt line · cold with opinions · rain the sea does or doesn't take · the iron that rings without being struck.
