## B. NEW FINDINGS (missed by both passes)

### B1 — The ch-18 deflection is canonically **Wu Zhangkong's hand**, not Gu Yue's (mislabeled "(canon)")
Canon ch 105, exact sequence:
> "一隻修長的手掌悄無聲息的出現在唐舞麟的右臂上，輕輕一撥…" → Wulin carried aside by a soft force → **"'夠了！' 冰冷的聲音在耳中響起…舞長空就站在他身邊，手上還抱着重傷的謝邂。"** → *afterward*: "古月**也**湊了上來" (Gu Yue **also** came up — i.e., following, not the one at his arm).

The slender palm arrives **with** Wu Zhangkong's "Enough!" while he is holding the injured Xie Xie — the natural, near-certain reading is that **Wu Zhangkong deflected the claw one-handed** (Gu Yue only joins after). The fanfic reassigning the deflection to **Gu Yue's arm** is a legitimate AU choice (it powers her L4 "I won't ask / control it" scene), **but the tracking layer calls it "(canon)"** — e.g., "Her deflection of the GDC (canon) spent." That label is wrong and should read **"AU swap: canon deflector = Wu Zhangkong's hand + '夠了！'; Gu Yue's line is canon-exact."** This matters for the L4 ledger: Gu Yue physically redirecting a full-power GDC is an *AU power statement*, not a canon event, and should be tracked as such.

### B2 — "Storm week" vs "hurricane week": canon says **typhoon** (台风)
Canon ch 104 twice roots the week in the **台风**: the crowd feels "仿佛又回到了幾天前台風的時候," and Gu Yue: "台風這幾天…" The project currently uses **"storm week"** (ch 17 fix) and **"hurricane week"** (continuation prompt, Wulin row) for the same week. Unify to one term in v2.26 (suggest keeping "storm week" everywhere, or moving both to "typhoon week" as closest to canon).

### B3 — Knower-list nuance: canon Xie Xie connects the power to the park duel
Canon ch 78, Xie Xie's first line at the scene: **"你又出現那種力量了？"** ("that power of yours — AGAIN?") — he was the ch-48 park-duel victim (saw a golden blur, woke on Wulin's back) and by ch 78 **knows Wulin has "that power."** If fanfic ch 14 kept this beat (the audit calls ch 14 canon-faithful), then "ch7 secret = Wulin + Lin Hao **only**" needs a caveat in the dossier: full secret/shared file = Wulin + Lin Hao; **partial knowers = Xie Xie (since ch7/ch14, knows the power exists, not what it is) and Gu Yue (ch 14, "I won't ask")**; teachers: none until ch 17. Verify ch7/ch14 prose and amend the golden-scales knower list so the ch-19+ "who knows what" ladder stays exact.
