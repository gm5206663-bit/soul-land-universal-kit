- Kind / tags: human soul master, [design], holder of Adaptation Talent (v2.0 Foundation)

  watches weather, soil, animals with a farmer's practical eye; asks one question at a
  time. Not cold — warm at home, slow to trust strangers.
- Voice law: plain village speech; never learned words he hasn't lived; questions over
  declarations; silence is a real answer from him.

## 2. Family (all [design], author may rename or veto)
### Lan He (father)
- A steady, broad-shouldered farmer in his late twenties; can't read; respects the Hall
  the way farmers respect weather — as something that decides things. Loves his son in
  work-shapes: fixing his shoes, saving the best bowl. WOUNDED (ch1-2): spent his pride
  like currency at the gate ("Pay it where it grows") so the debt would not come to his
  son — the speech of the chapter, and the truest thing in it. ANSWERED THE VERDICT IN HIS OWN WAY (ch3):
  teaches fieldcraft in work-shapes, asked the truest question ("what are you looking
  at?") and said "then watch it, and tell me when it does something." The grass-report
  between them is now a standing wire.
### Zhou Shi (mother)
- Sharp-eyed, practical, mid-twenties; the family's planner and counter of grain. She
  noticed early that the boy rarely got sick and never panicked — she calls it "a quiet
  belly" and thinks nothing more of it. Protective in ordinary ways. TEACHING (ch3): opened the
  fire/plant-kitchen school without announcing it — her way of answering the verdict by
  making her son able to feed people.
### Grandmother Lan (deceased before the story)
- Died the winter before the opening year; she once saw a ring-hunter pass the creek road
  and told it as a fire-story. Lan Shen's only inherited object: her habit of watching
  the treeline before sleep. [design] — her story is the village's window on what soul
  masters are.
### Siblings
- None (family decision-line: a single child makes the quota and the farm's future sit on
  one boy). [design]

## 3. Village cast (all [design])
### Old Tao — village elder of Qingni
- Sixties, lanky, neat out of habit; keeps the quota custom and the Hall fee; proud of
  the creek that never froze; fond of Lan Shen without favoring him.
### Tao Wu — the elder's grandson, Lan Shen's age-mate
- Loud, quick, wants to be something. AWAKENED (ch1): brown stubby-tailed field-dog beast
  soul, faint-but-true soul power — cultivable. QUOTA WON (ch2): the village's one
  road is his; departure after harvest. Paid Lan Shen the honey-cake debt and made the
  promise ("when I come back, I'll tell you everything"). Not a villain — a mirror
  with a louder voice, now carrying the first weight of being the one who got out.
  DEPARTED (ch3): left for Nuoding after harvest with the sub-hall clerk and the quota
  receipt; wears Lan Shen's braided grass-ring on his wrist under the city coat's
  sleeve; the road has been silent since — distance, not silence-by-plot. FIRST HOMECOMING (ch4): walked home in the walking season; changed on
  the surface, not in the middle; the grass-ring kept, wearing thin; delivered the
  academy's basic circulation to Lan Shen — the promise paid in full (OPEN #15);
  still no ring (hunts cost masters and money). SECOND HOMECOMING (ch5, autumn DL 2619): at his own wall; carries the
  academy's ring-lore; on the academy supervised-hunt list, grandfather saving;
  promised to ask how the registered get rings. THIRD HOMECOMING (ch6, autumn DL 2620): delivered the truth of the roads —
  WHITE IS EASY (small game, costs little, registered ones walk out and get one)
  while yellow-and-up costs masters/coin/waiting; white rings are laughed at, which
  is why he waits for the worth-having (list slow, grandfather saving); named Lan
  Shen's grass-kind ring as the easy road, the right one; oath deepened — his own
  coming hunt will be the shape Lan Shen will learn from.
### Aunt Zhou — market-row gossip node
- Carries news from Nuoding at cart speed and half accuracy; the village's information
  weather. Useful, honest about her sources being rumors.
### Old Man Cao — creek fisherman
- Knows the hill woods' edges: which hollows the beasts keep, where the white-tier strays
  come down hungry. Folk knowledge, no soul power.
