## Law notes
- Soul beasts release their ring above the corpse at death; unabsorbed rings fade within
  about an hour (WL-11). Hunting customs (who may hunt where) belong to Hall/academy
  law — verify before any hunt scene; until then keep hunts referenced, not staged.
- No soul beast in this file exceeds the yellow tier. Higher tiers live in forests far
  beyond the belt and stay there until causality says otherwise.
- The common Blue Silver Grass itself is everywhere: field edges, creek banks, broken
  walls. Ordinary plant, no soul — until a person's soul chooses it. [canon flavor,
  WL-01/03 context]
