## Verified facts by topic (each receipted)

### Soul power and rings
- Rank ladder Soul Warrior → Titled Douluo, ten-level bands (WL-09).
- Ten-level bottleneck; progress past 10 requires absorbing a hunted beast's ring; each
  ring grants one soul skill from the beast's nature (WL-10).
- Ring color = beast age class: white/yellow/purple/black/red (WL-10/11).
- Unabsorbed rings persist about an hour above the corpse (WL-11).
- Absorption limits are body/soul-dependent; Grandmaster's published age-limit table is
  only a theory, canonically approximate (WL-11).
- Innate soul power (1–10) is measured at awakening and read as destiny (WL-02).
- Era's best ordinary configuration: yellow, yellow, purple, purple, black, black,
  black, black, red (WL-12).

### Soul beasts
- Age classes match ring colors; higher-age beasts live in forests beyond the farm belt
  (WL-10/11 + belt design in CREATURES.md).
- A beast's nature speaks through the absorber's soul: the skill is the beast, filtered
  by the person (WL-10).

### Places and powers-that-be
- Heaven Dou / Star Luo two-empire continent; Fasinuo province SW Heaven Dou near the
  border; Nuoding City and its village belt; Holy Spirit Village 300 households, Spirit
  Sage legend (WL-01).
- Spirit Hall: continent-wide authority, awakening circuit via Sub-Halls, stipends for
  low-rank masters, great prestige (WL-05, stipend clause pending novel verification).
- Academy ladder: village quota → city primary academy → advanced academies (WL-02/04).
- Great clans and imperial politics move above village level (WL-06 context).

### The era's timeline
- Douluo Calendar anchor: Tang San born DL 2631, awakens DL 2637 (WL-07).
- Lan Shen born DL 2611, awakens DL 2617 (user ruling 2026-09-15 + WL-07).
- Era-figure age-lines (Yu Xiaogang exile ~2621, Tang Hao/A Yin 2621–2631, Holy Spirit
  Village settlement 2631) are planning anchors pending novel verification (WL-06 queue).

## Hard rules this fic obeys
- Awakenings happen at six, once a year, by the Sub-Hall circuit; never invent a private
  early awakening.
- No rings without a dead beast and a sit; no skills without rings; no skipping the
  bottleneck.
- Innate soul power is fixed at the awakening reading; it is never re-measured upward.
- Grass is grass: Blue Silver Grass stays the common waste species here; Emperor lineage,
  Blue Silver Domain, and A Yin heritage are Tang San's and never Lan Shen's (separation
  wall, FOUNDATION §6).
- Era boundaries: no soul tools/soul guides, no spirit-spirit (soul-spirit) technology,
  no battle armor — those belong to later eras' canon.
- Information travels at cart speed; rumors degrade honestly.
- Any rule the fic bends is [design] or [user ruling], stated in POWER_LAW.md B/C.
