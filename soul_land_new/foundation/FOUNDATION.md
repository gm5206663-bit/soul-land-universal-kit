## 1. One-line pitch
A farmer's son born with the world's most despised martial soul — Blue Silver Grass — and
innate soul power level 3, carrying an inseparable, non-sentient Adaptation Talent from
conception, lives a full soul-master life in the SL1 era twenty years before Tang San is
born: the world reads him as waste, while his complete existence quietly compounds.

## 6. Universe separation wall (mandatory)
This fic shares the SL1 era with the separate project Gu Yuan and with canon's own
Blue Silver Grass lineage, so TWO walls apply:
- **Against the Gu Yuan fic:** Gu Yuan, his rings/beasts/skills/numbers/events, and that
  fic's diverged canon versions (Starlight Tree ring, Mirror Beast, the Well, Lin Hao
  alias, the mist girl) do NOT exist here. Nothing crosses between the two works.
- **Against Tang San's lineage:** the protagonist's Blue Silver Grass is the COMMON waste
  grass. He is NOT Blue Silver Emperor bloodline, has no Great-Master/sect inheritance,
  and does not obtain Tang San's future items, herbs, beasts, or techniques. Tang San's
  eventual Blue Silver Grass awakening remains fully intact and unaffected.
