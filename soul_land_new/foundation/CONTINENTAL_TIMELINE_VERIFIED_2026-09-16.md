### The one error in the supplied snapshot, corrected with evidence

The snapshot opened from "Tang Hao was 44 when Tang San was born, born DL 2587."
Verification result: **Tang Hao's birth year is DL 2586, not 2587**, and the fandom
chapter-derived table records Tang San's birth under Tang Hao's **age-45 year**.
Completed age at the exact birth date could read 44 or 45 depending on an unknown
birthday month — which is the likely origin of the "44" figure (Sogou Baike's
"age 69 at SL1 completion" also yields 2587 only if the story is counted as ending
in DL 2656; counted at DL 2655 it yields 2586). The three novel-anchored age rows
above (51 at 2637; 63 at 2649; Tang Xiao's 60-at-the-banishment in 2631) all fail
under 2587 and hold under 2586, so **2586 is locked**. Consequences: in DL 2616 Tang
Hao turns **30** (not 29), and everything dated off "29 in 2616" shifts +1 year.
