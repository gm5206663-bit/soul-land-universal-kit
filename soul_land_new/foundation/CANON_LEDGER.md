## Spiritual power era note (verification 2026-09-16)

- In the SL1 era the MEASURED potential at awakening is innate soul power (levels 1–10)
  — it sets cultivation speed and the world's verdict (WL-02/03). Lan Shen's level 3 is
  three times Huo Yuhao's level-1 start (WL-14): a meaningful, honest advantage.
- Spiritual/mental power EXISTS in SL1 as a qualitative layer (focus, will, sense) but
  has NO numeric system in this era (numeric spiritual power belongs to later-era canon).
  Story law: track it qualitatively only; Foundation §72.3 permits refining it (as
  spiritual-sea/will work) through cultivation once a method exists.
