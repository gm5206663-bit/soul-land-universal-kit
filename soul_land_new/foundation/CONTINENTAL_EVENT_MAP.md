## Threads of practical use

### Tournament thread
The Continental Advanced Spirit Master Academy Elite Tournament (2645) is PUBLIC and
continent-famous; winners beat Spirit Hall Academy. Any soul master, village or city,
would hear of it through normal channels. It is the era's loudest public event between
2616's Twin Stars roaming and 2651's sect destructions.

### Spirit Hall thread (institutional pressure visible even at village level)
Pope transition 2631 → Bibi Dong's aggressive era → registered-name records (Wen's
ledger lives inside this institution) → sect strikes 2651 → empire 2656. The sub-hall
circuit that measures Qingni's children is the same institution whose head secretly
kills its former Pope and later burns the great sects. Nothing must be staged from
this; but institutional tone shifts (a nervous clerk, a new notice, a changed quota
mood) are legitimate natural weather.

### Hunt/beast geography thread (for OPEN #16/#17 and future rings)
- Nuoding-belt hunting grounds: where Nuoding Academy takes students (canon pattern);
  the belt's ten-year grass-strain beasts belong here too [design on canon base].
- Star Dou Forest: continent-scale great forest; 100,000-yr kings Da Ming/Er Ming;
  canon hunt parties arrive 2651 (do NOT route Lan Shen anywhere near this).
- Sunset Forest: Ice and Fire Yin Yang Well hidden inside; Dugu Bo's ground from 2644;
  Tang Hao lives there from 2651 — avoid.
- Slaughter City: outlaw city, Hell Road, destroyed 2649 — a rumor-place, not a
  destination for this story unless earned very late.
