# PLACES.md — the map, light

Simple names, one place each, receipt where the place is canon.

## Spirit City and the Hall

- **Spirit City** — the sacred land of soul masters; the Hall's seat. [canon]
- **Pope Hall** — the most magnificent hall; sacred together with the Douluo
  Hall. The OC's seat and desk; angel statues, high windows, light in shafts.
  [canon]
- **The Angel Shrine (传承地) — through the statue [canon, novel ch287, B32;
  NOT YET ENTERED on our pages — the chapter that entered it was deleted]:** the great angel statue in the Worship Hall is a door. Behind
  it, and the statue receives a body like water [novel ch287]. Inside: a
  hexagonal hall floating in a star-field, six golden pillars, no walls,
  feather-pattern engravings, a grey angel statue, and the grey **Angel Holy
  Sword** stuck in the floor with a round transparent gem in its pommel. The
  **nine考 light pillars rise in the great hall before the statue** (B33b), not
  in this hall; the ninth考 — the inheritance — is **closed and unwritten**
  (R22/F18). 千道流 administers the trials before the statue; he **passed eight**
  and never the ninth.
- **Worship Hall** — above the Pope Hall in standing; only Douluos qualify to
  enter. The deep **secret chamber** under it is where the one-month seclusion
  ran — the father's domain, quiet and guarded. [canon + Ch01]
- **Douluo Hall** — the resting place of the dead Title Douluos; the highest
  honor of the Hall. [canon]
- **Elder Hall** — between the Pope Hall and the Douluo Hall; the core power
  of the Hall: round stone seats, about twenty, the seven Worshipers ranged
  higher at the front. The first foundation was debated and passed here.
  [canon + Ch02]

## East of the city — the basin (Ch05, R30)

- **落日森林, Sunset Forest** — in the middle of the Heaven Dou empire, east of
  the capital; a temperate forest, thinner and more open than the southern
  Star Dou forest, its beasts mostly between one thousand and ten thousand
  years. [canon: Baike 魂兽森林 + SL1 ch235]
- **The basin with the two waters — 冰火两仪眼** — in the forest's core, an
  inverted cone of old fire on one side and old ice on the other; one oval pool
  holding two springs, milky-white yin and vermilion yang, whose dividing line
  never moves. Sulphur, and steam straight up off the line. Ten thousand zhang
  below it lie the Water and Fire Dragon Kings, who died together. One of the
  **three great treasure-basins of medicine**: rare plants mature ten times
  faster there, and ordinary plants cannot live there at all. In the SL1 era an
  ordinary man or beast who enters **explodes**. [canon: Baike 冰火两仪眼]
- **What grows there:** twelve herbs of the highest grade, among them
  八角玄冰草 (yin spring centre, ten metres of killing cold) · 烈火杏娇疏 (yang
  spring centre, supreme fire poison) — **the pair taken together neutralise
  each other and forge a body that fire and ice cannot enter** · 幽香绮罗仙品
  (cures no poison, defeats all of them) · 朱砂莲 · 雪蚕 · 九品龙芝 · 八瓣仙兰.
  [canon: SL1 ch63 + Baike]
- **Our era's state of it, on the author's correction:** in 2619–2620 **no man
  alive knows the well is there** — 独孤博 is about fifty-three and not yet a
  Titled Douluo, and canon puts his discovery near 2635. **And there are no
  plant beasts:** the six plant-type ferocious beasts are these herbs after
  they cross ten thousand years and gain sentience, which has not happened yet.
  Nothing here can be hunted, and no ring comes from a plant. What guards the
  rim is the 碧鳞七绝花. [author, 2026-10-09 + Baike 独孤博 + SL4 receipt]
- **Not named on the page:** the continent has no word for it in 2620, and the
  chapter keeps it that way (R29's withholding, applied to a place).

## The Hall's reach

- **Holy Halls** — in the capitals of the two empires (Heaven Dou, Star Luo).
- **Main Halls** — major cities; **Sub-Halls** — medium cities; **Branch
  halls** — every city, a landmark. [canon]
- The two empires: **Heaven Dou** (north) and **Star Luo** (south). [canon]
- **Chenghe** — the city of the first cut: its Main Hall, its steward (stripped),
  its seat (the old elder — left unnamed; canon keeps most elders unnamed), its
  books open five years. [design, Ch03]
- **The guild's pilot room** — a side hall under the Elder Hall: ten rows of
  chairs, a stove, a lectern cut down from an old door; the first teacher's
  board, six characters under his name. [design, Ch03]

## Rooms that matter right now

- **The Saintess's room** — Bibi Dong's locked room in Spirit City under the
  old order's guard: safe, monitored, no harm to the child. Since Ch02 the
  orders around it are his: keep safe, no punishment, resources provided,
  space given; guards at a distance; no one enters without permission except
  food and medicine. He speaks from three steps away. (Ch02; R16.)
- **The Pope's desk** — where the codex is written, volume by volume.
- **The small court behind the Pope's quarters** — flagstones, one bare
  tree, a well nobody used; where the sword was walked as a road, winter
  2619. [design, Ch04]
- **The secret chamber under the Worship Hall** — Qian Daoliu's domain, deep
  and stone; the month of the first core (Ch01) and five days of the second
  (Ch04). [design + canon shape]
