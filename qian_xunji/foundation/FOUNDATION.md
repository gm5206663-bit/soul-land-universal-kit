v1.0, 2026-10-07. Docset v6.0 adopted this day, on the author's order
("Ask questions let's build foundation for fen fiction"). Every ruling lives
verbatim in `RULINGS_LOG.md`. Nothing in this docset is invented: each file is
a restatement of the author's rulings and this project's own record.

## What this serial is

- An adaptation of Soul Land 1's darkest hinge: the night after the assault on
  Bibi Dong. The story opens the morning after.
- Someone from Earth — a lifelong reader, single (the author's own starting
  point) — wakes in Qian Xun Ji's body with both lives' memories: the Pope's
  fifty years, and every Soul Land page he ever read.
- He is not a game. No system, no window, no voice. A talent born in him —
  the Adaptation Talent — works silently, embodied, and only through real
  causes.
- One month of seclusion remakes the body: impurities out, bloodline purer,
  rings and bones fully absorbed, and the first soul core of this era forms —
  the True Angel Core.
- The world has never seen a soul core. The Pope is level 96 now, and stronger
  than any 96 in history — because no one in this era has what he has.
- What happens next is not written ahead. It goes naturally, by butterfly
  effects from what truly happened. The author keeps the pen on the far ends
  (rulings R22–R26).

## The locks (author's rulings; verbatim in RULINGS_LOG.md)

- **Both lives are his.** Qian Xun Ji's memories and the Earth reader's are
  one person now — not a costume, not a possession (R1, R2).
- **Adaptation Talent: Mortal Divine Level — not True Divine.** True Divine
  comes only when he becomes a God; then it can raise ring ages and bone ages,
  their quality, and make everything of his God level, every part (R8).
- **True Angel Core** = blood essence + divine things + martial soul authority
  domain — the angel's counterpart of the True Dragon Core. Formed at 95→96
  (R9).
- **Era law — no one else has a soul core.** No human in this era has one;
  Qian Daoliu (99) and Golden Crocodile (98) have never seen one. The OC is
  the first, and the shock is real (R7).
- **Bibi Dong:** healing, physical only, gentle holy light — never pillar,
  never judgement; no memory wipe; she is never told who he really is; he
  considers her his wife genuinely — guilt, attraction, tolerance extreme; he
  never kills her, in any condition (R4, R11, R16, R23).
- **Qian Renxue:** he knows she will be born; everything she needs through the
  pregnancy and after; a real father — love, not mission (R16, R26-era
  founding order).
- **Spirit Hall:** strict rules, corruption ended, the guild and the full
  codex as the first foundation — truth and reputation, and, secretly, faith
  power toward his own God position (R10, R12).
- **He is much stronger than a normal 96:** quality foundation, divine martial
  soul, very high spiritual power, and the era's only core (R13).
- **The far ends are not pre-decided.** The Tang San era, Bibi Dong's long
  road, and the secret's keeping are carried by the pages, not by a plan
  (R22, R23, R24).

## The laws (short form; full form and checks in RAILS.md)

- **Grey Wolf style gate:** third person limited, past, sensory, grounded;
  body band 2400–3400 words; sentences average ~14–18 words, none over 60.
- **No system; no jargon in prose.** Embodied only — breath, blood, bone.
- **Phenomena level** on breakthroughs and core beats — donghua-level staging,
  receipted in the footer.
- **Canon-strict:** nothing nerfed to protect him, nothing inflated to protect
  a scene.
- **Receipts in the footer, not the prose. Every shipped number measured,**
  never claimed.
- **Read before shipping.** Archive, never delete.
