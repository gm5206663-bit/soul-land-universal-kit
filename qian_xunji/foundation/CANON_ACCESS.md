## The spine vs the anchors

- **Spine (load-bearing):** **Soul Land 1 — the Douluo Dalu era** — its
  people, ranks, Spirit Hall, and clock. Where the story stands is decided by
  this spine: the night with Bibi Dong; Qian Xun Ji 95, Pope, 6-Winged Angel,
  rings 2Y2P5B, son of Qian Daoliu; Qian Renxue to be born; the Tang Hao
  wound and the canon death about eleven years on (canon dates: Renxue born
  2620, Tang San born 11 January 2631); Tang San born into a
  world this story will have changed.
- **Anchors (citable, never load-bearing for beats):** Soul Land 2 and 3
  receipts — used knowingly, because the OC's meta knowledge carries them
  (soul cores: first at 90 in the SL2 era, 70+ in the SL3 era; Blood Essence
  Core; Tang Wulin's Dragon Core); Baidu Baike (EN pages) for biographies and
  ability lists; the Soul Land fandom wiki; donghua episodes for phenomena
  staging (for example SL2 Episode 126); manhua for looks only.
- **Era line:** the world of this story has **none** of the later-era things.
  No human has a soul core — the OC is the first (R7). Later-era receipts
  explain what his core is; they never put later-era objects into this world.

## The ore rule

Anchors may support a claim **with a receipt**; only the spine decides what
beats happen. A conflict between sources is recorded with both readings —
never silently switched. Tag every claim that needs it:

- **[canon]** — the spine says it; receipt in footer/`CANON_GROUND.md`.
- **[fan]** — from fan sources; never load-bearing.
- **[disputed]** — sources disagree; both readings kept.
- **[design]** — ours by the author's ruling; listed in `RULINGS_LOG.md`.

## The contamination lesson (paid for 2026-09-27)

An earlier draft let a **fanfic** fact in as canon — another author's scene of
Golden Crocodile watching a soul-core condensation. It was removed from every
file it had reached (v3 chapter, foundation docs) and the corrections are on
record. **Fanfic is never a receipt.** When something feels like canon but
cannot be sourced, it is [fan] or [design] — never silently canon.
