## "His bloodline runs purer" — WHAT IT MEANS — NOT DECORATION — added 2026-10-07 per author correction

purity an *output* of the Adaptation Talent and listed it as a separate line
next to "forms the True Angel Core". Both halves were wrong. This block is the
corrected law.

**What a bloodline IS in this franchise.** A bloodline is the **concentration
of an ancestor's blood** in a person. The 千 line's ancestor is the Angel God,
so the Angel bloodline is Angel God blood essence carried in a body —
`CANON_HARVEST_2026-10-07.md` B1/B3. Canon measures purity exactly this way and
nothing else: 帝天 is "the purest dragon blood after the Golden and Silver
Dragon Kings" (B22). **"Purer" is therefore a concentration statement — a
measurable thing — not a compliment.**

**What "his bloodline runs purer" means here, precisely:** the Angel God's
blood stands in him at a higher concentration than it stood in any 千 before
him — including his own father at 99, whose blood the elders could feel running
dim gold. Fifty years of the old man's filth had been sitting in the channel.
The filth came out in the seclusion. What was left ran truer.

   blood. It does not "purify the bloodline" as a separate power — the blood
   was never dirty blood; it was clean blood under fifty years of grime.

   what a bloodline *is*.

   Essence core. `GLOSSARY.md`: "Derived from bloodline concentration … formed

   Essence cores "run as parallel structures from bloodline power and are not
   automatically under the ordinary cores' limits" — Tang Wulin's Dragon Core
   came out of his Golden Dragon King blood essence concentration at 59.

   concentration made solid and turned. It is not a storehouse of soul power

   measurement* of the blood — a fact, not a metaphor, which is exactly what
   Ch01 now says on the page: *"A centre made of soul power is a storehouse. A
