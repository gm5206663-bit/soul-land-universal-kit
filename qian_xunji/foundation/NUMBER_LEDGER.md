# NUMBER LEDGER — where every number comes from, in plain words

Written 2026-10-07 on the author's question: *"Tell me sincerely what you give numbers from 10 to what you even create."* Nothing here is spin. Every number that appears anywhere in this project falls into **four buckets**, and this file names them.

## 1. The story's numbers — the ladder (canon)

The world runs on a 1-to-99 power ladder. The number is the person's soul-power rank. Each ten is a stage:

| Rank | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| 1–9 | Apprentice (魂士) |
| 10–19 | Soul Master (魂师) |
| 20–29 | Great Soul Master (大魂师) |
| 30–39 | Soul Venerable (魂尊) |
| 40–49 | Soul Ancestor (魂宗) |
| 50–59 | Soul King (魂王) |
| 60–69 | Soul Emperor (魂帝) |
| 70–79 | Soul Sage (魂圣) |
| 80–89 | Soul Douluo (魂斗罗) |
| 90–99 | **Titled Douluo (封号斗罗)** — a name of one's own |
| 95–98 | the high band (Super Douluo, 超级斗罗) |
| 99 | Peerless Douluo (绝世斗罗) |
| 100 | godhood — off the ladder |

**Where the specific canon numbers on our pages come from:**
- 95 — Qian Xunji's level. Source: Baidu Baike (his item). The story never moves it.
- 96 — the OC's standing level in our story. **Not canon** (canon never states more for Xunji); it is our story's fixed choice, held across every chapter.
- 97 — Chen Xin's level (the Seven Treasures' Sword Douluo). Source: Baike/Fandom.
- 98 — Golden Crocodile. 99 — Qian Daoliu and Bibi Dong (99 = peerless). Sources: Baike.
- The other six 供奉: 金鳄斗罗 **98** · 青鸾斗罗 **97** · 雄狮斗罗 **97** · 光翎斗罗 **96** · 千钧斗罗 **96** · 降魔斗罗 **96** (百度百科 武魂帝国七大供奉). **The 3rd and 4th are disputed** — that table says 97, an earlier note of mine said the novel says 96. Recorded as a conflict in `CANON_MASTER.md` §7; no page may state a number for them without naming its source.
- 95 — Yue Guan and Gui Mei. Source: Baike.
- 90 at his fight — Tang Hao, "newly Titled" when he wounded Xunji. Source: Fandom/Baike.

## 6. Where I am not fully certain — no spin

- I have **not read the novels end to end.** Canon chapter numbers rest on Fandom/Baike pages and their citation lists, plus search snippets — not on me reading the raw text.
- **Thinnest receipts:** the sword-ladder chapter numbers (SL3 ch1249/1515) — recorded from the earlier canon pass, not re-read by me directly. If the novel disagrees with any number here, **the novel wins**, and I fix our record the same day.
- One documented case of bad sources: some "SL4 core" web pages were AI-generated noise; they were **flagged and never used.**
- If any number on any page looks wrong to the author, it is checked against the named source first, then struck or corrected — fix-forward, with the receipt kept.
