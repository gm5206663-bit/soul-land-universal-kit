   2026-10-07 sweep found Ch01's footer claiming ~2700 words for a body over
   3,500 — that is the exact shape of this failure.
2. **A system in the prose.** No window, no voice, no jargon; embodied only.
   k02.
3. **Pre-deciding Bibi Dong's road or the secret.** The author's words,
   2026-10-07: "you can't decide that' completely can't". R23, R24.
4. **Nerfing canon to protect him, or inflating to protect a scene.** k05.
5. **Touching the night.** It is "that thing / that night"; healing is
   physical only; no memory wipe; no telling. k03.
6. **Deleting instead of archiving.** k09.
7. **Unreceipted claims in footers.** Receipts live in the footer, not the
   prose; every claim cites a source or a measurement.
