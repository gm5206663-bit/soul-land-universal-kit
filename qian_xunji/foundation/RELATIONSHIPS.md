# RELATIONSHIPS.md — the map of the cast

Canon facts are receipted in `CANON_GROUND.md`. Below: for each bond — what
canon says, what is locked for us, and what breathes (not ours, not
pre-decided: the pages carry it). The author's law stands over all of it:
nobody pre-decides Bibi Dong's road or the secret (R23, R24).

## Bibi Dong — the center of the wound

- **Canon:** Saintess of Spirit Hall; twin spider souls (Death Spider Emperor,
  Soul-Devouring Spider Emperor); the night left her broken; she endures,
  rises, later becomes Pope and Rakshasa God; mother of Qian Renxue. [canon]
- **Locked for us:** he heals her **physically only** — gentle holy light,
  never pillar, never judgement; **no memory wipe**; she is **never told** he
  is not the real Qian Xun Ji; he considers her his wife genuinely — guilt,
  attraction, tolerance extreme; **he never kills her, in any condition**
  (R4, R11, R16). During the pregnancy: resources sent secretly, pills,
  a safe house through the Qian family, guards for safety — not punishment,
  no pressure. He keeps his distance; he does not force the wife role; he
  does not force love. (R16; Ch02 as written.)
- **What breathes:** everything else. Her road across the story is **not
  pre-decided** (R23 — the author's words: "you can't decide that'
  completely can't"). No forgiveness arc and no revenge arc are planned; the
  pages make it. Write her true in every scene; decide nothing ahead.

## Qian Renxue — the daughter not yet born

- **Canon:** daughter of Qian Xun Ji and Bibi Dong, born hidden, raised by
  the Qian family; 6-Winged Angel; becomes Angel God; a tragic canon road.
  [canon]
- **Locked for us:** he knows she will be born (meta knowledge); he provides
  all resources through the pregnancy and after; **be a real father — love,
  not mission**. (R16-era founding order; Arc 3 as recorded.)
- **What breathes:** how fatherhood actually goes, and everything downstream
  of it. No outcome is pre-decided (R22).

## Qian Daoliu — the father

- **Canon:** Great Worship of the Worship Hall; 99 Limit Douluo; Angel
  Douluo; guardian of the Angel God inheritance; canonically dies in the
  Eight Trials for Qian Renxue. [canon]
- **Ours:** notices his son's bloodline purer, the holy aura heavier, a core
  where no core has ever been — **he has never seen a soul core; this is the
  first, and it shocks him**. Old hope returns: an old father watched his son
  fail fifty years; now he sees improvement. He supports the rules and the
  guild because a pure Hall serves the Angel God. He wants to learn how the
  core formed — he cannot re-condense a second core, because he has no
  first one yet. (Recorded; Ch01–Ch02.)
- **What breathes:** whether and how he learns anything beyond that.

## Golden Crocodile — the 98 who watches

- **Canon:** second strongest of the Hall, one step from 99. [canon]
- **Ours:** felt the aura at the seclusion's edge; **first time seeing a soul
  core in his long life**; shocked, curious; an old warrior who respects
  strength; supports the first foundation after the debate. Would want to
  learn the formation. (Ch01–Ch02.)
- **What breathes:** his long run — like every observer, not pre-decided.

## Ghost Douluo · Chrysanthemum Douluo — loyal ears

- **Ours:** came fast when called; noticed the Pope's voice is calmer,
  steadier — **the difference is visible to those who served him twenty
  years**. They keep counsel; loyalty holds. (Ch02.)
- **What breathes:** what they talk about among themselves — natural.

## The Elder Hall — the political body

- **Ours:** the core power of the Hall: ~twenty elders, seven Worshipers
  above them, Qian Daoliu at their head. The Pope holds only three votes;
  all elders together can dismiss a Pope. The first foundation (strict rules,
  corruption ended) was debated and passed — the corrupt opposed because it
  hurt them, the clean supported because a pure Hall is stronger. Not forced;
  a natural political body. (R12, R10; Ch02.)
- **What breathes:** how deep the corruption runs and how fast it can be cut.
