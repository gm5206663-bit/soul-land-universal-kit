## What the book is, in-world

- Written by the Pope, in his own hand, in the months after the seclusion
  (Ch02: he finishes the first volume and sends copies out — "Ghost Copy
  this. Send to Worship Hall, Elder Hall, Holy Hall, Main Halls. Let them
  read. Then public.").
- Its shock is that it is **true** and it is **complete**: it tells the
  continent what martial souls actually are. [Ch02]
- It deliberately excludes the soul core — no human in this era has one; too
  advanced to share; that truth waits. [R10; Ch02]
- The guild (open to all, not only Spirit Hall branches) is its carrier.
  Public purpose: shared true knowledge, reputation. Secret purpose (kept in
  the OC's chest only): faith power, slowly gathered, toward a God position
  of his own. (R10.)
