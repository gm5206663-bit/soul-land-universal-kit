# RAILS.md — the laws of Clear Sky Lion

Every rail has a receipt (where it was paid for) and a check (how it fails on
bad input). Rails restate the serial's ruled defaults and the method's laws —
nothing here is new, and no rail may contradict a ruling.

## k01 — no System, anywhere

**Law:** Adaptation Talent only (R7). No window, menu, screen, gauge,
percentage, level or stat vocabulary — in prose **or any foundation file**.
The talent embodies silently or not at all.

**Check:** chapter gate word-list (to be built with the gate, Stage 0 step 4);
a foundation-gate pass catches System files appearing (`SYSTEM_SPEC`/`METERS`
must not exist here).

## k02 — rules, not events

**Law:** the OC understands how the world works and has never heard of the
plot (R8). No anticipation of canon events without an on-page cause.

**Check:** firewall audit against `RELATIONSHIPS.md` before drafting; every
canon-adjacent beat cites its earned change.

## k03 — canon is never displaced

**Law:** parallel until the Shrek gates; first crossing at intake; Huo
Yuhao's core beats undisplaced (R13) — Skydream, Wang Dong, Tang Sect entry
all happen as canon has them.

**Check:** canon-coverage receipt per chapter (method's coverage pattern);
butterfly effects computed from actual changes only.

## k04 — panels and figures

**Law:** exact figures live in `STATUS.md`/panels, never in prose. Panels
print only on update/gain beats — full then, **0 lines otherwise** (F22,
R15). Ring colors follow canon color-age law: yellow 100–999, purple 1000+
(R5).

**Check:** panel drift guard when the chapter gate is built; STATUS is the
only home of numbers.

## k05 — honest pace

**Law:** page 1 starts at rank 29 with both rings banked (R9); every level
after that is earned on page — no off-screen jumps (F8).

**Check:** STATUS growth entries carry chapter receipts; unbacked jumps fail
the gate.

## k06 — gate before ship

**Law:** no chapter ships without its gate. The gate does not exist yet —
build it before chapter 1 (Stage 0 step 4). Never weaken a gate to pass it.

**Check:** gate exit 0 before ship; foundation gate green while Stage 0 runs.

## k07 — clean and clear

**Law:** third-limited OC POV, past tense, clean-and-clear house style;
sentence discipline measured, not argued (R15; the method's s40 family).

**Check:** style gate numbers fixed when the serial's gate is built —
band and sentence average recorded there, with receipts.
