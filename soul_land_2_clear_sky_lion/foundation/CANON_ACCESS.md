# CANON_ACCESS.md — how canon ore enters Clear Sky Lion

## The spine vs the anchors (R1, ruled 2026-10-03)

- **Spine (load-bearing):** **the Soul Land II novel** (The Unrivaled Tang
  Sect). Beats follow the novel's order.
- **Anchors (citable, never load-bearing for beats):** the donghua, the
  manhua, the Soul Land fandom wiki, Baidu Baike (EN), secondary timelines.
  They support a claim with a receipt; they never decide what happens.
- **Confidence tags:** every claim in `CANON_GROUND.md` carries
  [canon] / [fan] / [disputed] / [design] per kit `01_CANON_SPINE.md`.
  [fan] or [design] never load-bears a beat.
- **Ore rule:** a conflict between anchors is recorded with both readings —
  never silently switched. The author's word settles it, verbatim, in
  `RULINGS_LOG.md`.
