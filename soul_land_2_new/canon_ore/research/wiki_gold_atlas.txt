== TIER 0 — DIVINE GOLD (era-walled context, SL3+/deep-lore) ==
GOLDEN DRAGON KING (金龙王) [src: fandom/wiki/Golden_Dragon_King]
- Half of the DRAGON GOD, split by the Asura Sword; other half = Silver Dragon King
  (spiritual power + elements)
- Inherits: Dragon God's BODY — ultimate strength / defense / destruction / vast endurance;
  carry-bag of the Dragon God's negative thoughts, madness, resentments of 15 fallen God Kings;
  SL4 adds the TIME element to him (SDK got space)
- Body = god-king tier (physical match of the original Dragon God); needs 2+ God Kings to down
- Appearance: km-scale serpentine golden dragon; diamond golden scales >1m²; horns; spikes;
  five claws; RUBY EYES = destruction intent (links to Blood Dragon state)
- Fate: sealed by Dragon God for human God Kings; freed late; killed by Tang San's
  Sword of Three Trials (Asura+Evil+Goodness fusion); dying, he shot his GOD CORE into
  newborn TANG WULIN (SL2.5/SL3) → 18 seals → inheritor becomes the new Golden Dragon King
- Wulin-era GDK bloodline effects (context-only): str/atk/def doubling, beast-soul and
  dragon suppression ("Dragon Power/Might" — weaker bloodlines submit), destruction affinity,
  blood rings: Golden Dragon Body (2x), Tyrant Body (3s triple-def + counter), Roar,
  Raging Domain, etc. — SEE *this only as card-format reference*, our serial can't claim it

GOLDEN DRAGON SPEAR [src: fandom/wiki/Golden_Dragon_Spear]
- Divine weapon forged from the Golden Dragon King's 49TH RIB (he had 49; descendants 48)
- Wood-like, ~15 ft, double-headed shuttle form, dragon engraving; NOT part of the Dragon God
- Devours energies → filters to spear & user; internal seal from the former Dragon God
- Wieldable ONLY by Golden Dragon martial soul / bloodline
- Canon user chain: Golden Dragon King → Tang San → Tang Wutong → WANG QIU'ER → Huo Yuhao →
  Tang Wutong → Tang Wulin → Tang Xuanyu

WANG QIU'ER GOLD LINE [src: fandom/wiki/Wang_Qiu'er + Baike]
- Three-Eyed Golden Lion (Star Dou's Emperor Auspicious Beast, ~15,000y at SL2 start) +
  GOLDEN DRAGON bloodline; human-form simulated martial soul: GOLDEN DRAGON
- Ultimate attribute: ULTIMATE STRENGTH; ring color rosy-gold
- Canon fate event: ELEMENTAL TRANSFUSION with Huo Yuhao — he absorbed part of her strength →
  his Eye of Life became the EYE OF DESTINY
- Her spear = the Golden Dragon Spear (49th rib, above)

== TIER 2 — LEGENDARY GOLD LINES (extinct / rare) ==
DARK GOLD TERRORCLAW BEAR (暗金恐爪熊) [src: fandom/wiki/Dark_Gold_Terror_Claw_Bear + Bear_Lord]
- Inherited some BEHEMOTH bloodline; METAL element — "tyrannical power, the sharpness of metal"
- Devour-evolution: cultivation ≠ age (a 3,000y-culti bear may be <1,000 actual years old)
- Breeding: estrus once per 10 years, 1–2 cubs → naturally rare
- SL2 canon: ch 139 — Xuan Zi trapped a 2,000+y one at Star Dou → XIAO XIAO absorbed its ring,
  HUO YUHAO got its RIGHT PALM external soul bone (terrorclaw strike grows with him)
- SL2 canon: 10,000y parent bears; twin cubs DA HEI & XIAO HEI (adopted by Huo Yuhao)
- Palm bones = rarest & strongest; famous precedent: "Darkleft Douluo" (left palm bone)
- At 10,000y even a Titled Douluo struggles to kill one; Bear Lord sits at 470k
GOLDEN FUR LION MASTIFF (金发狮獒) [src: fandom/wiki/Golden_Fur_Lion_Mastiff(+/Abilities) + Baike]
- Legendary PROGENITOR of all canine soul beasts — some legends say of ALL LION-TYPE beasts
- ~4 m; brilliant gold fur, black demon-contoured face, explosive musculature
- Innate DOMINATION/DOMINEERING DOMAIN: enlarges with each step; attack & defense surge;
  painless; ALL abilities +50% minimum; gold-ring-with-black-rune halo underfoot
- Lion Mastiff Roar: shockwave roar turning 100 m radius to powder — pure vibration, space cracks
- Temper: fights to the death → enemies everywhere → EXTINCT long before SL3
- SL3 revival only via Spirit Pagoda's TEN THOUSAND BEAST PLATFORM: a 10,000y specimen's raw
  strength ~ Tang Wulin at rank 91; RESISTED Golden Dragon King bloodline suppression;
  at 100,000y it would outrank the Scarlet King among the Ten Beasts
- ERA WALL: extinct in SL2 era — cannot be hunted/met; exists only as legend/bones/simulation
GOLDEN TORTOISE — SL2-era beast that carried a LIFE GOLD chunk from the Lake of Life
(Huo's blade grew by absorbing it) [src: fandom/wiki/Life_Guardian_Blade]. Registered; thin data.
GOLDEN EARTH DRAGON KING (金土龙王) [src: fandom/wiki/Golden_Earth_Dragon_King]
- Golden-NAMED but LIGHT-element land dragon; 10,000y; diluted-but-pure light dragon bloodline

  wooden pavilion with golden sign; underground light so thick it's near-liquid (SL2)
- Holds souls of Shrek seniors; tree-spirit ELDER GOLD later becomes the planet's LIFE CORE
