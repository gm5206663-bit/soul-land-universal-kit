== THE MECHANICS LAW (A) ==
1. ONE skill per soul ring / soul bone. Exception: 100,000y+ carries TWO; a 1,000,000y
   ring may carry up to FOUR.
2. Skill originates from the soul beast — tied to ring age; pattern tailored to the master.
3. SKILLS SCALE: the granted effect is proportional to ring age (base grade) and can grow with
   the user's soul power. Canon yardstick (their doppelgänger example):
   10y → 10% · 100y → 30% · 1,000y → 50% · 10,000y → 80% (→90% w/ matching bone) · 100,000y → 100%.
4. Skills can MUTATE (e.g. Tang San's Blue Silver Cage) — mutations ≠ the second skill of a 100k ring.
5. Ring-merging exists: superimposing rings raises output (Huo Wu; Xu Sanshi's Xuanwu Roar).
6. At rank 70 + 7th ring: Martial Soul True Body / Avatar — qualitative step, huge general buff (E).

== THE WHITE TIGER LINE (B, C, D) — beast-soul measuring stick ==
Type family shown in canon: barrier · wave · transformation · AOE · nuke · avatar.
- Initial: constitution of the white tiger; claws, muscle expansion, strength up
- 1st WHITE TIGER BARRIER/Shield Body Barrier (yellow): white-light layer; strength & defense up;
  classed "self-enhancement/amplification", one of the line's three great enchantment skills
- 2nd WHITE TIGER INTENSE LIGHT WAVE (yellow): ranged light blast from mouth/body
- 3rd WHITE TIGER VAJRA TRANSFORMATION (purple, donor: VAJRA TIGER): body grows, stripes/king-mark;
  +100% physical strength, attack, defense; ~30-minute window; one of the three enchantments
- 4th METEOR SHOWER (purple): AOE light spheres, EACH = one-tenth of user's soul power
- 5th WHITE TIGER DEVIL GOD TRANSFORMATION (black): +150% str/atk/def; STACKS with Vajra Transformation
- 6th ANNIHILATION/DESTRUCTION KILL (black): huge finisher; manhua note: uses 50% of total soul power
- 7th WHITE TIGER TRUE BODY: avatar transformation
KEY PRECEDENT (C): Dai Weisi's 4th = WHITE TIGER EARTHQUAKE + derivative GROUND SEAL —
  donor/expressed EARTH-type control skills worn by a White Tiger ⇒ element expression flows
  through the beast soul from the donor. (Supports L-09 attribute-graft adjacency.)
== ABSORPTION EXTRAS (D/E, soft) ==
Ring absorption raises physique/stats proportional to the beast's own (separate from the skill).
