## 5. The chapter contract (Blue-Silver-derived, author-confirmed method)

Every chapter must satisfy **all seven**:
1. **Canon beat** — a canon event/truth the chapter rests on, verification-tier labeled.
2. **Threat level** — something is at stake physically, concretely, this chapter.
3. **Measured movement** — growth shown in-world (rites length, bouts, work), never tallied.
4. **Opposition with a face** — named rival/antagonist force, not weather.
5. **Butterfly** — his presence visibly changes something, causally, on-page.
6. **Five registers ≥ 3** of: close sense / hard scene / dialogue / other POV / lyrical(≤1 para).
7. **A turn** — the chapter ends on something that was not true when it began.
