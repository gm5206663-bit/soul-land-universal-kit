From the author's own strike to the SL_NEW agent (SARA.md §104: *"when you don't write canon how do natural butterfly effects create everything — you just create it yourself, what is natural there, nothing"*) and the doctrine written to answer it (`SOUL_LAND_NEW/foundation/PANEL_BUTTERFLY_DOCTRINE.md`, itself adopted from the SL3 foundation codex). Plus standing rules from the SARA post-mortems (§163–199, §456–462). These are LAWS for this serial; they sit inside METHOD v4 and are referenced by the gate's self-audit column.

## N-1 THE BUTTERFLY LAW (THE_CODEX v3.06)
A butterfly is not decoration on a canon scene — **it is the reason the scene is different at all.** If any Class-1 scene in our chapters could be read with Jin Yang deleted and *nothing in it changes*, he has been erased from his own story. Test per scene: **what here is different because he is in it?** — answer on the page, not in the footer.
- **Effect without its cause on-page is a claim, not a butterfly.** Cause→effect is written in the chapter footer, panel by panel.
- **Superlative scope check:** no canon line making someone "best/strongest/highest" may be copied into a room that contains Jin Yang unless it survives his presence (rank 23 at entry — Wang Dong's canon's best-cultivation claim stands at 24+, Dai Huabin's standing above both; carded in CANON_LEDGER).

## N-2 THE PERSPECTIVE PANEL DOCTRINE (v3.20)
1. Every chapter stages **≥1 panel belonging to someone who is not Jin Yang**, sourced out of canon where it can be (canon scene / canon-adjacent verified texture), adapted from that character's perspective with the butterflies applied.
2. A panel with no canon behind it is an **invention and must be labelled [design]** in header/footer. A chapter built mostly of inventions is the wrong chapter.
3. The butterfly test extends to every character, not just the OC.
4. **Divergences are an economy** (dust · current · bend · break · STORM): each has a cause, a horizon, a ledger row. **No forced canon, no forced divergence.**
5. **Use everything**: minor POVs (the registrar, the parasol sisters, the broom elder, a soul-tools instructor), letters, objects as second voices (the plaque, the bun, the treaty).
6. Locks still govern: another panel may approach a locked door (Huo's heart, Wang Dong's nature, Shrek's deep secrets) — never open it.

## N-3 LEGEND ACCUMULATES IN OUTSIDE VOICES (STYLE_GOLD §7)
Jin Yang's name grows in other mouths — Qiu Yuan's notebook, Deng Wu's hill letters, the class's nicknames, Zhou Yi's file-notes, Teacher Wang's recollections — **never in his own narration.** The watcher must gain something from having watched (e.g., the registrar got her morning back; Deng Wu got twelve honest laps; Zhou Yi got a bench for her forge).

## N-4 CANON IS ORE, NOT FATE
World-facts are true until touched. When our state (rulings + OC placement + derived consequences) implies an outcome, **ours wins and is derived, never snapped back**; every bend is earned by prior chapters and logged (L-11/L-05 still cap Huo Yuhao's fortunes).

## N-5 STANDING HUMAN RULES (SARA post-mortem §165/183–199/458)
- **When intent is clear, choose the natural & logical thing and GO — show work; author redirects if wrong.** (Don't hand him the fork and stall — R1/R2 resolutions this file, provisional but signed.)
- **Restraint from character and canon logic only, NEVER invented walls** (Tianyu autopsy). If a plot needs the world to restrain the OC, the placement is wrong.
- **Every turn pushes the story forward** — a plot, a person, a system, a cost. Canon checks serve the motion; they never stand in place of it.
- The OC is a real peer: humble, warm, gifted — skilled but never benched by machinery. If he stands aside from a prize, it must be HIS choice on-page with cause, not a rule about him.
- Canon characters always sound like themselves; no edgy dialogue; ordinary life (dorm/roommate/class/food) is never skipped.
