2. **Full trait transfer, re-expressed:** donor traits do not copy-paste; they are re-expressed

4. **Cultivation efficiency:** his body adapts to training load — sustained ~3.5 ranks/year

   elements can still kill him; the talent grants **no free skills** — only better absorption;
   it cannot create a trait the donor never had.
