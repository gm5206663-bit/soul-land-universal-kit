## §1 SPECIES CANON CARD — Golden Lion (soul beast)
Source: SL2 novel ch 148 via pointer ore `canon_ore/research/wiki_golden_lion.txt`.

- **Type:** metal-type lion soul beast — Power-attack frame
- **Bloodline:** not particularly powerful, but above the average soul beast; lions = king of beasts
- **Body:** yellow-golden mane, razor teeth; extreme defensive strength; physical-combat specialist
- **Weapons of the species:**
  - Fur and mane harden into **metal spikes at will**
  - Mane spikes can **fire as a volley** — most effective within **5 m**
  - Golden spikes can erupt from the **shoulders**
- **Temper:** bad, even for a king of beasts
- **Canon sighting:** Shrek Academy Beast Dueling Area, 1,000+ years; acknowledged strongest of the
  four golden-furred beasts (Golden Bear, Golden Tiger, Golden Panther, Golden Lion) during
  Huo Yuhao & He Caitou's soul-tool test.

## §3 ADAPTATION TALENT — edge-case register (extends AUTHORS_LAW L-04)
1. Same-species donor (ring 1) = maximal compatibility = maximal refinement → bloodline jump signed.
2. Cross-species donor (ring 2 drake) = traits re-expressed as lion versions; foreign aspects
   (scales, dragon frame) are NOT worn raw — they translate or fail.
3. Donor ceiling scales with his body: at rank 23 his frame held 500y→1,000y steps; future donors
   priced per rank, never free (L-04.5).
4. Element conflicts (fire vs ice, light vs dark) still kill; adaptation widens doors, it does
   not delete walls.
5. No trait invention: if the drake had no tremor-sense… wait — it does: earth-attribute drakes
   feel the ground. Carded. Anything not in the donor does not appear. Every future card must
   name its donor-trait lineage.

## §5 THE EMPEROR-BEAST DISTINCTION (firewall)
**Three-Eyed Golden Lion (三眼金猊) is a different order of being** — Star Dou's Emperor
Auspicious Beast: ~15,000y at canon start; primary spiritual power with extreme light AND
extreme fire; Golden Dragon bloodline; fate-carrier; human form **Wang Qiu'er**.
Our Golden Lion (species §1, martial soul §2) is an ordinary-kingly metal lion. **No kinship
is claimed.** The romance lane to Wang Qiu'er (L-06) is a meeting of two golden lions by the
world's measure — coincidence and destiny, not blood. If the author ever signs kinship,
it becomes a new ledger row and the ripples get priced.

## §9 THE FORGE OF THE LION (added 2026-09-22, perfect-panel order)
Why the smith road is not garnish but *structure*:
- **Attribute logic:** metal is his primary. Five years of hammer + womb-gift + attribute
  resonance woke the **metal-resonance sense** (grains/stress/heat-band through worked metal) —
  the honest explanation for **grade 3 at 10¾**. Without metal-primary + adaptation, it's a myth;
  with them, it's arithmetic.
- **Conditioning logic:** the anvil builds exactly what a power-attack vanguard needs —
  shoulders, wrists, core, tendon-bone base — years before ranks could (canon-logic echo of the
  SL1 smith-hero road; no blood, no method claimed).
- **Era logic (L-10):** no stipends in this age; coin is a cultivation input. The forge pays gold
  for refined blanks the soul-tool workshops consume — while **the making of tools stays closed**:
  he is a smith, not an engineer (era wall).
- **Doctrine:** every grade is certified, every commission dated, every coin ledgered. The lion
  doesn't eat talent; it eats work.
