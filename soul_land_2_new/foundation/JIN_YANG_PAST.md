## PRICES PAID (the honesty list — nothing above was free)

- 3,000+ conditioning hours under Chu (his childhood's main body)
- Two ring-gambles that were coin-flips from dying (ribs; thigh; the ravine cold-sweats)
- Forge years: burned forearms of an 8-year-old, calluses a farmer's father recognized
- Free time: near zero between anvil, training yard, letters home written by lamplight
- The old wound: lion's scar maps (ribs, left thigh) — body kept them as file-records

## WHO KNOWS WHAT (secret-levels card)

- **Parents:** know their son is special, a smith, and "off to the big academy." Do not know the ring-ages' doctrine violation. Kept unknowing on purpose.
- **Chu Shan:** knows everything (both hunts, adaptation's shape). Sworn to the grave; his letter describes talent, never mechanism.
- **Gongshu Yan:** knows the resonance sense exists; assumes it is a metal-attribute quirk. Correct enough.
- **World/Shrek (as of gate):** will measure rank + rings. Nothing else is volunteered. Public policy `[design]`: rings shown when demanded; doctrine-math questions deflected to "my teacher priced my life into these gambles." Adaptation's name does not exist outside this workspace.

## SHAPE OF THE BOY (who walks into ch 2)

Speaks little, counts everything. Promises like smith strikes — few and final. Ledger-minded
(mother), craftsman patient (Gongshu), doctrine-suspicious but measurement-obedient (Chu).
The lion's pride lives under all of it, quiet as banked coals.
