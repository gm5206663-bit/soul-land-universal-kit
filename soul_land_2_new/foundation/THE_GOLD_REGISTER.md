## TIER 2 — LEGENDARY GOLD LINES

**DARK GOLD TERRORCLAW BEAR** — *the bloodline our line can legitimately dream of.*
Behemoth-blooded, **METAL element like us**: "the tyrannical sharpness of metal." Devours
beasts to outrace its own age. Rarest trophies: palm bones whose terrorclaw skill **grows with
the master** (Darkleft Douluo precedent). Already bleeding into our era's canon: SL2 ch 139,
Xuan Zi trapped a 2,000-year specimen — Xiao Xiao took the ring, Huo Yuhao the right palm bone.

**GOLDEN FUR LION MASTIFF** — *the legend above all lions.* Progenitor of every canine line —
and by some legends of **every lion-type beast, including ours**. Four meters of explosive gold
behind a black demon face; born with the **Domination Domain** (attack/defense surge, painless,
all abilities +50%+). So fight-mad it made the world its enemy and went extinct. One resurrection,
one era too late: Spirit Pagoda's Ten Thousand Beast Platform (SL3). A 10,000-year specimen
matched rank-91 Tang Wulin's strength and **shrugged off Golden Dragon King suppression**.
**Era-walled:** in our time it exists only as legend and bones. If our lion's deep ancestry
traces to it one day, that is an author-signed revelation — not a given.

**Golden Tortoise** — registered; carried Life Gold in canon (thin data).
**Golden Earth Dragon King** — golden by NAME, light by element. The permanent teaching case:
*golden-adjacent words prove nothing; element cards do.*

## GOLD OBJECTS & PLACES IN OUR ERA

- **Life Gold (Life Gold)** — "mother of all metals": life-condensed metal that **evolves other
  metals** and drains metal-life. Canonically Huo Yuhao's road (Eye of Life → Eye of Destiny;
  Life Guardian Blade). For a METAL-soul boy this is the single most resonant material in the
  era — therefore doubly locked: it is **Huo's canon fortune, and L-05 prices any touch.**
- **Ancient Golden Tree / Elder Gold** — planted by Sea God Tang San before he ascended;
  Sea God Pavilion carved inside it at the heart of Shrek; concentrates heaven-earth origin
  energy; keeps the souls of Shrek's elders; destined to be the planet's life-core. Our boy
  will walk beneath it at the gates — **resonance flavor is free; power claims are signed.**
