# PROSE METHOD — SL2 · The Golden Lion

Adopted 2026-09-22 from the author's own projects (study order: "check my projects like fire
phoenix, Lin hao then you understand how to write"). These rules are now GATED — `checks/verify.py`
enforces the machine-checkable ones; the rest bind the writer.

## STUDY SOURCES (what was read)
- `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` — the house prose constitution (soul-land-projects repo)
- `Soul_Land_3_Project/chapters/chapter_99.md` (Lin Hao serial) — living sample of the format at peak
- `soul_land_4_fire_phoenix` (Yan Shuo serial) — per-topic bibles/locks + chapter/canon-coverage pairing

## P-1 LANGUAGE LAW (hard gate)
English only; **no CJK characters anywhere** — prose, panels, ledgers, notes, ores. Names in
pinyin only (Jin Yang, Tang San, Huo Yuhao). Typographic punctuation is fine. Files render
portable; the glossary owns each name's one true spelling.

## P-2 CHAPTER HEADER LAW (hard discipline)
Every chapter opens:
```
# Chapter N: Title
## Canon Reference: <canon ch map> AS ORE (...which canon facts are cited...)
```
with the ore/trajectory split made loud: **canon = ore; facts cited; trajectory OURS.**
(Retrofitted onto ch 1–2 on adoption day.)

## P-3 PARTS STRUCTURE
Chapters run in titled parts (## Part 1: … / ## Part 2: …) when the spine has movements —
a measure, a scene, a cost. One-flow chapters (like ch 1's road) may stay single-breath,
but never shapeless.

## P-4 THE FIVE REGISTERS — use at least three per chapter
Close sense · hard scene · dialogue · other POV (italics) · lyrical.
Dialogue is the one that dies first: **hard floor = 3 spoken lines per chapter** (hard gate).
Ch 1–2 audits: both pass ≥3 registers and the dialogue floor.

## P-5 LENGTH LAW (hard gate, ch 3 onward)
**≥ 2,800 words per chapter** — the Lin Hao pipeline floor. Chapters 1–2 (2,536 / 2,277)
grandfathered and logged as pre-adoption; from ch 3 the floor is law, failure blocks push.

## P-6 CANON-FIRST WRITING (L-11, prose-facing)
Canon lanes default (system scenes: dorm pairing, class, rosters, fees, teachers). The hero's
POV rides on top of canon beats; canon bones never bend for convenience. Design fills gaps only.

## P-7 MEASURED-VOICE DISCIPLINE (house accent from Lin Hao samples)
Numbers carry weight ("blood runs out too, and a boy has less of it than a master"); rooms speak
professions; costs are said aloud in the hero's head. Skill displays keep the canon register:
name → type → effect → cost → duration. No trait-poetry, ever.

## P-8 SYNC PATTERN (from phoenix)
Every shipped chapter lands with: chapter file + CHAPTER CARDS endnote, CANON LEDGER row,
SERIAL_LOG row, panel bump, gate run, push. Never prose without sync.
