# 01 — CANON SPINE

The world. Read this fully before drafting. Getting mechanics wrong is the fastest way to
produce fiction that a Soul Land reader cannot read.

**Confidence tags used throughout:**

- **[canon]** — verified against source material or multiple independent secondary sources.
- **[fan]** — widely repeated, plausible, not verified against primary text. Verify before
  building a plot on it.
- **[disputed]** — sources disagree. Pick a reading, record it, and never switch silently.
- **[design]** — your invention on top of canon. Must be labelled as yours, never as canon.

Never present a `[fan]` or `[design]` claim as `[canon]` in prose or in a status file. The
distinction is the whole discipline.

### Provenance — what these claims were actually checked against

`[canon]` in this file means *multiple independent secondary sources agree*, **not** *read in
the original Chinese novel text.* That is a weaker guarantee and you should know it before
you build a plot on one of these rows.

| Section | Checked against | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| §1 The clock | SL1 timeline compendia; Tang San's birth/age cross-checked at two points | medium |
| §2 Rank ladder | Baidu Baike EN (Soul Master, Titled Douluo), anime setting summaries, fandom rank tables — all agree on the ten bands | **strong** (4+ independent) |
| §2 Translation variance | the same sources, which is *why* the variants exist | strong |
| §3 Ring colours (first five) | Baidu Baike EN, fandom ranking tables, SL2/SL3 glossaries | **strong** |
| §3 Orange / gold tiers | fandom and later-series material only | tagged `[fan]` |
| §3 Beast law | a single project bible compiled earlier from novel + donghua | **weak — one source** |
| §4 Blue Silver line | same project bible, plus a user ruling on the valley name | weak + `[user ruling]` |

**The beast-law rows in §3 are the weakest thing in this file.** They come from one compiled
bible, not from independent agreement, and several are load-bearing for any plant-beast or
slow-cultivation protagonist. Re-verify them against the novel before your story depends on
one.

Nothing in this file was read in the original Chinese. If your project turns on a specific
mechanic, find the chapter. A `[canon]` tag here means "safe to build on, worth re-checking,"
not "settled beyond argument."

## 1. THE CLOCK

Soul Land uses the **Douluo Calendar (DC)**. Anchor everything to SL1's start.

| Anchor | DC | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| SL1 begins (Tang San age 6) | **2637** | [canon] |
| Tang San born | 2631 | [canon] |
| Tang Hao met A Yin | ~2621 (TH age 35) | [fan — fandom timeline] |
| A Yin revealed as 100,000-year | ~2624 (TH age 38) | [fan] |
| A Yin's sacrifice to Spirit Hall | ~2631 | [fan] |

**Working rule: DC ≈ (your anchor year) + elapsed years.** Pick one birth year for your OC,
write it down, and derive every date from it. Do not let two files carry two different
birth years — that single drift caused a hard canon violation in a finished serial.

### The five series

| Series | Era | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SL1 — Douluo Dalu | ~2637 DC onward | Tang San. Best documented. |
| SL2 — Unrivaled Tang Sect | ~10,000 years after SL1 | Sun Moon Empire, soul tools |
| SL3 — Legend of the Dragon King | far future | Soul spirits replace rings |
| SL4 — Ultimate Fighting | same era as SL3 | Lan Xuanyu, Shrek Academy |
| Pre-canon | anything before 2637 | Most freedom, least documentation |

Do not mix eras. Soul tools, soul spirits, Spirit Pagoda and Shrek Academy's modern form do
not exist in SL1's era, and the ring system works differently by SL4.

## 2. SOUL POWER AND HUMAN RANKS

Every human awakens a **martial soul** at age six. Cultivation is measured in **soul power**,
and rank titles change at every ten levels.

| Rank | Soul power | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Soul Scholar | 1–10 | [canon] |
| Soul Master | 11–20 | [canon] |
| Soul Grandmaster | 21–30 | [canon] |
| Soul Elder | 31–40 | [canon] |
| Soul Ancestor | 41–50 | [canon] |
| Soul King | 51–60 | [canon] |
| Soul Emperor | 61–70 | [canon] |
| Soul Sage | 71–80 | [canon] |
| Soul Douluo | 81–90 | [canon] |
| Titled Douluo | 91–99 | [canon] |

**Titled Douluo subdivides:** 91–94 ordinary Titled Douluo, 95–98 Super Douluo, 99 Limit
Douluo. Later series subdivide 99 further into Quasi-Demigod, Demigod and Quasi-God.

### Translation variance — read this before "correcting" a rank name

**The same rank has several English names depending on the translation.** This is the single
most common way two agents end up "fixing" each other in circles. Pick one convention at
project start, write it into the ledger, and never silently swap.

| Soul power | Common variants |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | Soul Scholar · Soul Warrior · Spirit Scholar · Soul Apprentice |
| 21–30 | Soul Grandmaster · Grand Soul Master · Spirit Grandmaster |
| 31–40 | Soul Elder · Soul Honour · Spirit Venerable · Spirit Expert |
| 71–80 | Soul Sage · Soul Saint · Spirit Saint |
| 91–99 | Titled Douluo · Title Douluo · Grand Soul Land Master |

None of these is wrong. They are different translations of the same Chinese terms. A project
that mixes them looks careless even when every individual name is defensible.

**Rank gates are not free.** You cannot advance past a multiple of ten without a new **soul
ring**. A Soul Grandmaster at level 30 is stuck until they take a ring. This is the single
most useful structural fact in the franchise: it gives you a natural chapter rhythm, a
natural source of tension, and a natural reason characters must go hunting.

Use it. A serial where ranks never move on the page is a serial with no progression, and
that is failure mode three.

## 3. SOUL RINGS AND SOUL BEASTS

A soul ring is obtained by killing a soul beast and absorbing it. **Ring colour encodes the
beast's age:**

| Colour | Beast age | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| White | 10+ years | [canon] |
| Yellow | 100+ years | [canon] |
| Purple | 1,000+ years | [canon] |
| Black | 10,000+ years | [canon] |
| Red | 100,000+ years | [canon] |
| Orange | 200,000+ years — special case | [fan] |
| Gold | 1,000,000+ years — god-tier, attribute-coloured | [fan] |

The first five rows are the ones that matter for any story below god tier. Orange and gold
appear in later series and in wiki material rather than the original text, so they are tagged
`[fan]` — do not treat them as load-bearing unless your project's era reaches that far.

**Under-100,000 is where virtually all fan fiction lives.** A red ring is already a
once-in-a-generation event. If your protagonist collects them casually, you have written
yourself out of tension. See `05_POWER_LAW.md`.

**This table is the spine of the world's economy.** Soul beasts die so humans can advance.
Every forest in this franchise is a hunting ground, and every ancient beast knows it. If
your serial is set anywhere near a soul forest and nobody is hunting, you have not written
Soul Land — you have written a nature documentary. See `03_STORY_LAW.md` §2.

### Beast law

| Fact | Confidence |
|---|---|
| Beasts hit a bottleneck every 100 years, escalating by order of magnitude | [canon] |
| At ~100,000 years a beast may take human form; early stage can be exposed by high soul masters; must live among humans afterward | [canon] |
| A 100,000-year ring grants two skills rather than one | [canon] |
| Plant-type beasts grow slowly and are fragile until ~1,000 years | [canon] |
| Animal beasts can fight from ~10 years | [canon] |
| Plant beasts develop cognition slowly; ~100,000 years is normal for human-grade intellect | [canon] |

That last row matters enormously for any plant or slow-beast protagonist. A plant soul beast
that thinks like a person at year forty is a canon break, not a liberty. If you want
early cognition, make it a mutation and label it `[design]`.

## 4. THE BLUE SILVER LINE (worked example)

Included because it is the best-documented beast line and because a whole serial was built
on it. Useful even if you never touch it, as a model of how to research a line properly.

| Claim | Confidence |
|---|---|
| A Yin is a 100,000-year Blue Silver Empress; wife of Tang Hao; mother of Tang San; sacrificed to Spirit Hall; later revived | [canon] |
| She carries the **Blue Silver Domain**, an Emperor-bloodline ability — **not** the King's | [canon] |
| Long-cultivated Blue Silver Grass evolves toward Blue Silver **King**, then **Emperor** at the 100,000 gate | [canon] |
| The King threshold | **[disputed]** — wiki says 10,000 years; Baike says 70,000+. The SL1 canon specimen is an ~85,000-year King. |
| Royal grass is born golden-veined | [canon] |
| Blue Silver Valley / Blue Silver Emperor Valley | **[canon]** — user-ruled canon. Never label `[design]`. |
| A Yin's rooted era ends at her transformation, **before ~2621 DC**; exact year unstated in canon | [fan — corrected] |

The disputed King threshold is a perfect example of why the tags exist. Two sources
disagree by 60,000 years. Pick one, record which, and never quietly switch — a serial that
crossed the gate at 10,000 in one file and 70,000 in another has two protagonists.

## 5. INSTITUTIONS

| Institution | Era | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Spirit Hall / Spirit Pagoda** | SL1 → later renamed | Registers martial souls, issues rankings, controls ring acquisition. The franchise's permanent institutional antagonist. |
| **Shrek Academy** | SL1 onward | The elite school. Modern form is far future. |
| **Tang Sect** | SL1, revived SL2 | Hidden weapons, poisons, the Tang San legacy. |
| **The great empires** | all eras | Heaven Dou, Star Luo, later Sun Moon. Politics and war. |
| **The Clear Sky School** | SL1 | Tang Hao's origin; the Hammer line. |

**The Spirit Hall is your best free antagonist.** It is canonically vast, canonically
bureaucratic, canonically interested in exactly the thing your protagonist is, and it
keeps records. A registry is a plot device: it means somebody wrote something down, and
somebody else will eventually read it.

Use institutions as slow pressure. A report filed in chapter five that gets read in chapter
fifteen is better than a villain who arrives in chapter fifteen.

## 6. RESEARCH LAW

How to establish a fact:

1. **Prefer the donghua/novel text** over any wiki.
2. **Two independent secondary sources** minimum for anything you will build plot on.
3. **Record the receipt** — source, URL or path, date checked, confidence.
4. **Record negatives too.** "Searched X, found nothing" is a finding. Without it, the next
   agent redoes the search or, worse, invents an answer.
5. **Tag every claim.** No untagged canon assertions in any file.

A canon notes file with receipts is worth more than a confident memory. Blue Silver's
`CANON_NOTES.md` carried receipts for a claim that turned out to be wrong — and the receipt
is what made the correction possible in one pass instead of an argument.

### When you cannot verify

Say so, in the file, in plain words. An honest `[fan — unverified]` costs nothing. A
confident invention costs a rebuild, and the reader finds it anyway.
