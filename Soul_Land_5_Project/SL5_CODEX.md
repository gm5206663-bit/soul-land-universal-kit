## 1. THE PREMISE (canon-verified skeleton — every canon fact cited in SL5_RESEARCH.md)

**Canon spine (verified):** Tang San, God King of the Douluo God Realm, gives up his throne when
Xiao Wu dies; his divine consciousness (神识) drifts the cosmos hunting the world of her rebirth. It
arrives at **Falan Star (法蓝星)** — a continent where **humans are the lowest caste**, treated as
sacrificial straw dogs beneath the demon clans (bloodline supremacy, Great Demon Emperors, the
Ancestral Court 祖庭) and the spirit clans. He is **reborn as a human slave baby**. He cultivates the
Mysterious Heaven Method in secret, awakens **bloodline brands (血脉烙印)** beginning with the Wind
Wolf, joins the **Redemption (救赎)** organization — and in **Wind Wolf Town**, during a blood
sacrifice, **his mother is killed before his eyes.** The long road: Redemption Academy → devoured
brands (Wind Wolf · Flash Leopard · Golden Peng · Blue Silver Emperor…) → the Jiali City auction →
the Ancestral Court tournament → the alias Jin Miaolin → the war for the Ancestral Court → Xiao Wu
found, the Sky Fox Great Demon Emperor destroyed, the decree protecting humanity, the return.

**Our divergence — born, not chosen:** the slave woman carried twins. The story starts **at canon's
page one** (R-timeline ruling: same moment as canon — the birth). One brother arrives carrying forty
thousand years of memory and a god's patience. The other arrives carrying **nothing but the talent
in his blood** — and no idea, for a very long time, of what is sleeping in the bunk beside him.

**The engine of the whole book:** Tang San hides what he IS. The twin hides what he DOES. Each
brother spends years protecting the other from a secret the other already half-knows. Butterfly #1
is the twin's bare existence: a God King who planned to walk alone has a brother he cannot shake,
cannot fool cleanly, and — the part canon never priced — **cannot stop loving.**

## 3. THE ADAPTATION TALENT LAW (v1.00 — SL5 instance)

The user's ruling: *"he has adaptation talent from womb — birth mother."*

- **Origin (in-world):** carried in the blood of the **birth mother's line**. 🔴 This makes the
  mother a LINEAGE, not a prop — she must be written as a person whose blood explains him, and her
  canon death in Wind Wolf Town lands on BOTH brothers, differently: Tang San loses a mother he was
  guarding; the twin loses the only person who was ever like him.
- **The three clauses (constitution, inherited from the SL3 framework):** the talent **never creates
  from nothing** · **never exceeds the user** · **never speaks.** It adapts what he is given — a
  brand that shouldn't take, a technique watched twice, a poison survived once — always at a cost,
  always inside his own ceiling.
- **The craft law (SL3-proven):** the talent is **never named in narration** in the foundation era.
  It is SHOWN — the thing that steps forward and looks at everything else. Naming comes late, earned.
- **The two-talents symmetry (the story's deep rhyme):** Tang San's own SL5 path is devouring and
  integrating bloodline brands — a god's version of adaptation. The twin does natively, dimly,
  humanly, what his brother does divinely. Neither of them names the rhyme for a long time. When it
  is finally said aloud, it should feel inevitable.
- 🔓 **UNDECIDED (continuity lock, §5-L1):** whether this talent is an echo of the SL3 talent is
  NOT established in either direction. Written so both remain possible, forever deniable.

## 4. THE LAWS (inherited from SL3, adapted — enforced from chapter 1)

1. **BUTTERFLY LAW v2** — canon is the spine, not the script. Seeds near-verbatim; the butterfly
   surrounds them. Every chapter footer carries a 🦋 BUTTERFLIES SHOWN line from ch1.
2. **THE PRIME LAW / SPECTATOR TEST** — the twin is not a watcher. Active > passive verbs in every
   scene; the wall is a banned position. He cannot out-act a God King — so he acts at his own scale:
   the stolen bread, the memorized patrol, the brother's alibi. Small verbs still pass the test.
3. **NO-CANON LAW** — no canon claim without held text on disk. Wiki-level facts (SL5_RESEARCH.md)
   may shape PLANNING only; PROSE waits for fetched chapters. The SL3 scar this law comes from:
   asserting mechanics instead of looking them up.
4. **ALL-OR-NOTHING STATE LINE** — every chapter footer carries ALL of the twin's tracked lines or
   no numbers at all; holds written HELD (event-gated), never silent. The tracked set is defined at
   ch1 and only grows.
5. **DOC-SYNC LAW** — the footers are the story; `state` derives from footers; every doc that copies
   a number names its source. One truth per fact. (The SL3 rot this prevents: five docs, three values.)
6. **REALM GAP LAW — SL5 instance:** 🔴 **RESEARCH-GATED.** Falan's ladder (brand tiers, the 12
   Ranks, Great Demon / Heavenly Spirit bands) must be extracted from held text before any
   power-comparison sentence is written. No invented brackets, ever.
7. **THE RECEIVER PRINCIPLE** — relationships move by causes shown on-page, never by decree.
   The brothers' bond, the mother's weight, Xiao Wu's thread when it comes — all paid for in scenes.
8. **CHARACTER LAW** — characters are what canon says they are; viewpoints ≠ authorship. Tang San
   in our book is CANON's Tang San: patient, ruthless where it counts, forty thousand years deep —
   seen from the outside by someone who only knows him as "my brother, who is wrong somehow."
9. **STYLE GOLD CARRIES** — the 12 SL3 patterns are portable craft (question-titles · cliff rhythm ·
   callback-objects · perspective panels · the not-irony tag · refrain-then-break…). The MOTIFS do
   not carry — SL5 grows its own motif bank from its own soil (slave-brands, the two heartbeats,
   whatever the text gives us). The talent needs its own grain-read equivalent, found not copied.
10. **THE FOOTER CONTRACT (from ch1):** SPECTATOR TEST: PASS · 🦋 BUTTERFLIES SHOWN · WIRES:
    SPENT/ALIVE/RESTED · the state line (all or nothing) · canon anchors cited to held files.
