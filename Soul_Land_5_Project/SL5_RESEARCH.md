## 2. THE PROLOGUE — verified from the official text (xs8.cn, Qidian mobile)

The opening is Tang San's consciousness drifting space: *"当妻子死去的那一刻，神识逸散，唯有转世重生…"*
— when Xiao Wu died, only rebirth remained. He abandons tens of thousands of years of cultivation
and the God King's throne to follow the pull toward the world of her rebirth. *"等我，小舞！我来了…
你的唐三，为你而重生。"* — the wife-chase is the book's stated engine, from its own first page.

## 3. THE SPINE (QQ Reader official baike — plot skeleton; PLANNING-grade until chapters held)

- **Rebirth & survival:** Tang San is reborn as a **human slave baby** (人类奴隶婴儿) on Falan Star
  (法蓝星); witnesses humanity's misery; cultivates the **Mysterious Heaven Method** (玄天功) early;
  awakens the **Wind Wolf bloodline brand** (风狼血脉烙印); joins the **Redemption** organization
  (救赎组织). 🔴 **In Wind Wolf Town (风狼镇), during a blood sacrifice, his MOTHER IS KILLED before
  him** (目睹母亲被害) — the vow to grow strong is born there.
- **Growth & academy:** the **Redemption Academy** (救赎学院); companions incl. 武冰纪 (Wu Bingji);
  he **devours/integrates multiple bloodline brands** — Wind Wolf 风狼 · Flash Leopard 闪豹 · Golden
  Peng 金鹏 · **Blue Silver Emperor 蓝银皇** …; the **Jiali City grand auction** (嘉里城大拍卖会) →
  the Skyfire Iron Heaven-Breaking Hammer (天火精铁破天锤); the **Ancestral Court elite tournament**
  (祖庭精英赛) — humanity's potential displayed.
- **The Ancestral Court war:** alias **Jin Miaolin (靳淼林)**, cover identity in the **Blue-Gold
  Tree clan** (蓝金树族); the war for the Emperor's seat (占皇之战); the schemes of the **Sky Fox
  Great Demon Emperor** (天狐大妖皇).
- **Godhood & return:** Tang San absorbs the powers of the two Emperors (天阳/地阴), condenses chaos;
  **Xiao Wu awakens the Asura God Sword and kills the Sky Fox herself**; the two ascend, take the
  Ancestral Court's will, **decree the protection of humanity**, leave the Book of Laws (诸法之书),
  and return to the Douluo God Realm. Family reunion ending.
- Tang San's arc-shape: 初为人类奴隶 — "at first a human slave" — i.e., **the lowest caste is the
  starting line for BOTH brothers.** The twin's whole life fits inside his brother's starting line.

## 4. THE WORLD (Baidu baike EN + baike; PLANNING-grade)

- **Falan Star / the Demon Continent:** humans and demons "coexist" with humans as 刍狗 —
  sacrificial straw dogs. Human status: property, offerings, prey.
- **The Demon (Monster) Race:** bloodline supremacy; **Great Demon Emperors** (大妖皇) rule; ranks
  run to a **12th Rank** tier; **eight main cities**, each ruled by the most powerful Great Demons;
  weaker demons form clan-centered villages/towns — **Wind Wolf Town is named as the smallest class
  of town.** Named canon figures: the Splitting Heaven Great Demon Emperor (Behemoth clan, 4th-ranked
  Great Demon Emperor, northeast of the continent); city lord Shen Xiao.
- **The Spirit Race (Ling Race, 灵族):** born of Ancestral Spirits; absorb heaven-and-earth spiritual
  energy; grow into Great Spirits / Heavenly Spirits; also bloodline-supremacist. The Richen Empire
  (日辰帝国?); the Diamond Heavenly Spirit Emperor; deputy city lord Jin Chengwu.
- **The Ancestral Court (祖庭):** the continent's seat of authority; its will can be held (the
  endgame); an elite tournament exists at continental level.
- **Power systems named so far:** 血脉烙印 bloodline brands (awakened, devourable/integrable) ·
  妖神变 Demon God Transformation (Baidu EN: "the power system of Demon God Transformation") ·
  玄天功 the Mysterious Heaven Method (Tang San's import). 🔴 **Mechanics of ALL THREE are
  UNVERIFIED at text level — nothing about how brands awaken, stack, or rank may be asserted in
  prose until chapters are held.**
