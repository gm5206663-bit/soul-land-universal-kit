# Chapter 50 — The Forest Counts Separately

The hall knew Dorm333's score before anyone said it.

It was in the way Lu Qianxun stood behind Lan Xuanyu's team with Ye Lingtong and Chang Jianyi without needing an order. It was in Jin Xiang's closed mouth. It was in the glances toward Liu Feng's covered right arm, toward Qian Lei's feet, toward Lan's back.

Twenty thousand three hundred and forty-one.

No one sensible believed Lan's Martial Soul Fusion technique alone explained it. Rank20 did not simply injure a Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon because a child wished hard enough. Something had happened after the teams separated. Something involving timing, danger, and a method Dorm333 had not yet put into public words.

That uncertainty gave Lan's silence weight.

It also made his command difficult to ignore.

Jin Xiang understood it better than he wanted to. Years ago, provoking Lan had felt easy. Now it looked like trying to trip someone before realizing he could read the entire road. Dorm333's personal strength did not look overwhelming at a glance. But under Lan's command, Qian Lei's impossible summon and Liu Feng's sharp movement had become a champion route.

Dorm336 stood nearby, not behind Dorm333 and not outside the pressure.

Second place, nine thousand eight hundred and forty-six, remained visible without anyone announcing it. Yan Shuo stood calm, Song Yichen at his side, Luo Haoran slightly behind. They had no Lucky Wheel glow, no public treasure, no exaggerated ceremony. What they had was harder to dismiss: teachers who looked at all three when formation was mentioned, classmates who no longer called Song and Luo passengers, and a score that made every other team understand Dorm336 had not survived by accident.

Six teams had advanced from Heaven Luo's Elite Junior Class.

Six teams meant eighteen students.

Six teams meant the old five-team shape did not fit anymore.

Ji Hongbin's gaze moved over all of them.

"The next qualifier is about to begin," he said. "Display your strengths. If cooperation becomes possible, let Lan Xuanyu take overall command."

A quiet stir passed through the students.

Yan did not object. Lu Qianxun did not object. Even Jin Xiang looked more relieved than offended.

For Yan, accepting this was not bowing his own road beneath Lan's. It was understanding. They had grown from first friends into rivals who knew too much about each other's silences to mistake command for worth. Yan could burn through many direct problems better than anyone in the hall; Lan could often hear the shape of a rule before others knew it was a trap.

Lan's command role was not a claim that he outranked everyone in raw power. It was a practical decision in a Shrek test. If the next round hid a rule inside danger, Lan was the one most likely to find it before it ate them.

Mu Zhongtian ended the moment.

"Enter the pods."

Eighteen simulation pods opened.

Qian Lei whispered, "If there are Dragons again, I object in advance."

Liu Feng said, "Noted by no one."

"History will vindicate me."

The pod closed before Qian could say more.

Soul World took them.

A pleasant voice announced the rules.

Each team would be transferred to a random corner of the simulated Star Dou Forest, once the largest soul beast forest on the Mother Planet. They had to go deeper and hunt high-level soul beasts. Each team scored separately. Whoever landed the final kill received the points. Killing between teams was allowed; killing another team's members granted half that team's accumulated points, while the killed team kept the remaining half. The test would last seven days.

Lan listened to every word.

Random transfer.

Separate scoring.

Final kill.

Team-killing half.

Seven days.

Go deeper.

Ji Hongbin had told them to cooperate if possible. Shrek had built the rules so possible would not be simple. Random transfer separated even Heaven Luo's six teams. Last-kill scoring made shared hunts unstable. Team-killing meant a successful team became tempting prey.

The countdown ended.

Light fell.

The world spun.

Fresh forest air struck Lan's face.

He crouched by instinct.

Green surrounded them. Towering trees, dense canopy, thick vines, ferns, moss, roots, no road, no human mark. The simulated Star Dou Forest smelled alive enough to make the word simulated feel inadequate.

Qian appeared beside him, confused.

Liu Feng was already scanning the shadows.

Lan pulled Qian between himself and Liu and moved them to the base of a massive tree.

"Do not rush," he said. "Analyze first."

Qian whispered, "Analysis before being eaten. Approved."

Lan kept his voice low. "Survival for seven days is first. Going deeper should mean stronger soul beasts and higher possible scores. But if another team kills us after we accumulate points, half our work becomes theirs. The announcement did not say soul beasts take points the same way. In the worst case, dying to a soul beast — even ending ourselves inside the simulation — is better than handing another team half our score."

Qian's expression tightened.

Liu Feng nodded. "So we survive, judge direction, hunt carefully, and avoid becoming points."

"Yes. Ancient Star Dou Forest had regions: Peripheral, Hybrid, Core, and the central Territory of Great Beasts. Shrek should not throw us into the Core immediately. We are likely in the Peripheral Region or at most near the Hybrid Region. We scout first."

Liu Feng released the White Dragon Spear. "I'll go."

"Three hundred meters. No farther."

Qian lifted the Coin of Summoning. "I prepare?"

"Prepare. If Liu draws something back, we fight ready."

Liu moved in a flash, stepping on a vine and leaping through the green.

Lan set Qian against the tree and released silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass. Ice awls formed quietly around them, low and angled. Qian closed his eyes, danger sense spreading.

"No dangerous aura nearby," he whispered.

Lan nodded but did not dismiss the ice.

He and Qian had the strongest Spiritual Power perception in the class. That helped here, where sight ended in leaves and sound bent around trunks.

Liu returned soon.

"No soul beasts nearby."

Then he looked into the pathless forest. "But without a map, how do we know which way is deeper and which way is outward?"

"Soul beast strength," Lan said. "In the Peripheral Region, unless our luck is terrible, we should mostly meet ten-year or hundred-year soul beasts. We can handle those. Thousand-year soul beasts require caution. If the beasts grow stronger, we are moving deeper. If they grow weaker or disappear, we may be moving outward."

Qian gave him a thumbs-up. "We listen to you. Wherever you say, we go."

"Then summon a soul beast. We use it to find a path."

Qian's thumb stopped. "Now? My first summoning still has a fifteen-to-twenty-minute idle period. If something worse appears after—"

"You have Replicate as contingency," Lan said. "And a summoned beast ahead of us can take the first attack. This is not wasting power. It is buying information."

Qian considered that, then sighed with relief. "Wonderful. Not using my brain remains a valid strategy."

"Use enough to control the summon," Liu said.

The Coin of Summoning spun out.

Two yellow rings rose under Qian's feet.

They were darker than before.

Still yellow. Not purple. Not a thousand-year breakthrough. But the five-hundred-year realm after Spirit Pagoda processing had given them a deeper, heavier color. It was a sourced change now, not an invented boast: stronger yellow, no exact ages named.

Lan wrapped golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass around the Gate of Summoning.

Qian glanced at him. "Why not silver-patterned grass? Dong Qianqiu's power should have a big effect, right?"

Lan froze for half a breath.

He remembered Heaven Dou, the accident, Dong's anger, his own childish counter, and the awkward apology that had not made the matter simple.

"I offended her," he said. "She probably would not appear now. Besides, Shrek qualifiers should be happening at the same time across the Federation. Shrek has algorithms to keep planets from learning test contents in advance. She should be in her own qualifier."

Qian looked as if he wanted details and valued survival enough not to ask.

Lan added, "She has trained with Teacher Nana longer than I have. I am not worried about her passing."

The Gate roared.

Heat rolled out.

A five-meter fiery-red beast emerged, covered in red scales, spikes along its back, heavy head low, smoke leaking from its mouth.

Qian stared. "Of course. A relative."

"Ground Fire Dragon Lizard," Lan said.

An evolved Ground Fire Lizard, a type of Ground Dragon, weaker than the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon but stronger than ordinary lizards. Broad attack range, fireballs, hard heated head scales. After the last qualifier, the coincidence felt like a joke with teeth.

Qian waved.

The lizard charged forward and broke a path through vines and leaves.

They moved behind it: Liu in front, Qian in the middle, Lan at the rear. Golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass stayed around Liu's waist, ready to enhance him at any moment.

The summon lasted about a minute and a half now. In a seven-day forest, that sounded tiny. In pathless terrain behind a fire-type Ground Dragon, it became distance.

The forest smelled peaceful as they advanced: wet bark, crushed leaves, hidden flowers, clean green air.

Peaceful did not mean safe.

A thick vine ahead suddenly sprang alive and wrapped around the Ground Fire Dragon Lizard's head.

Liu's spear rose.

"Plant-type soul beast," Lan said.

The lizard's scales flushed metallic red. A sizzling sound cut through the forest. The vine turned yellow, then brown, withering as bitter plant aura burst outward.

Fire restrained wood.

The surrounding vines recoiled and separated.

"Follow!"

They ran.

For the rest of the lizard's duration, they used the opening hard. When the summoned beast finally blurred back into the Gate and disappeared, they had moved roughly one kilometer inward.

Lan immediately chose higher ground and stopped them.

A small rise. Roots. Better sightlines. Not safe, but less blind.

Qian sat to recover Soul Power. Liu watched the approach paths. Lan organized what they had learned.

No strong soul beasts at the start.

Weak plant-type soul beast after moving.

Fire restrained it easily.

Likely still outer region.

Direction usable, not proven.

They repeated the rhythm: summon, move, rest, measure the forest by what answered.

The Gate responded a little faster after Qian recovered.

Qian noticed Lan noticing and immediately sat straighter. "Admire quietly."

"It is faster," Lan said.

"A little," Qian admitted, grinning. "The ring improvement helps. Not enough. But real."

For a summoner who had little direct battle power between summons, little was not little.

During the next rest, Qian leaned against a root and looked up. "I really want to go to the Spirit Ascension Platform soon. If my first Soul Ring reaches a thousand years, maybe I could summon without stopping until my Soul Power is gone. If I could call Ground Dragon-type beasts continuously, wouldn't I be the strongest in class?"

Liu Feng snorted. "If thinking made it happen, you would already rule Heaven Luo."

"My imagination is underappreciated."

"Work harder."

Lan did not laugh at the dream.

"It is not impossible in principle," he said. "Just not now. Your summoning depends on several foundations: Soul Power rank, Spiritual Power rank, and Soul Ring strength. Higher Soul Power should reduce the interval eventually, though summon quality may still be uncertain. Spiritual Power clearly has a strong relation too — perception, control, and Gate activation all show it."

Qian became quiet.

A future road had appeared, and unfortunately it looked like training.

Lan rose and looked deeper into the forest.

Somewhere else, the other Heaven Luo teams had been scattered by the same rules. Dorm336 would have its own route, Lu Qianxun his, Jin Xiang his. Lan could not command people he could not reach, and he would not pretend cooperation existed because Teacher Ji had wished for it.

The forest counted separately.

So for now, Dorm333 had to survive separately.

Lan raised his hand.

They moved deeper.
