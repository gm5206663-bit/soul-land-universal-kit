# Chapter 38: Names Borrowed by Children

Lan Xuanyu had survived space pirates, a space hunting net, three pirate mechas aiming at his trapped warship, and a hidden meteor-class attack spaceship firing its main cannon.

Then he saw Nana.

For one bright moment, all the frightening things behind him became smaller than the silver-haired woman waiting beyond the space-center exit.

"Teacher Nana!"

He ran.

He forgot the questioning room, the captain's stunned face, the applause, Nan Cheng's earlier scolding, and the secret blue-haired figure outside the cockpit window. He forgot he was nearly twelve and not the little child Nana had once held in Zi Luo City. He spread his arms wide, because some kinds of safety were remembered by the body before the mind had time to become dignified.

A figure stepped between them.

Arms open.

Dark-blue hair.

Bright dark-blue eyes, narrowed like ice before it cracked.

"What are you doing?"

Lan Xuanyu's feet tried to stop.

His momentum did not listen.

He crashed into Dong Qianqiu.

She had appeared too suddenly and too close. Lan had been running too quickly. He was not yet tall, but rare ingredients, strange bloodline power, and years of training had made his body far stronger than an ordinary child's. The impact knocked Dong Qianqiu backward with a small cry.

Nana moved at once and caught her shoulders.

Lan, losing balance too, reached out by instinct and grabbed the person falling with him.

Their noses touched.

Pain flashed through both of them.

Then, through the thin barrier of Dong Qianqiu's mask, their lips brushed.

It lasted less than a breath.

It was enough to make both children freeze.

From outside, it looked almost like an embrace.

From inside the accident, it felt like nose pain, surprise, embarrassment, and a misunderstanding arriving with no manners at all.

"Aiya!"

Dong Qianqiu shoved him away. Her eyes widened, then filled with fury and embarrassment at the same time. Behind the mask, her face flushed so fiercely that even the mask could not hide the heat in her gaze.

"What are you doing!"

Lan Xuanyu rubbed his stinging nose. "What are you doing?"

Nana laughed.

Not loudly. Not mockingly. But the laugh was bright enough that even Nan Cheng and Lan Xiao, watching from behind, felt the strangeness of the scene soften.

Nana pulled Dong Qianqiu away with one hand and opened her other arm to Lan.

This time, no one blocked him.

Lan hugged her tightly.

"Teacher Nana."

The familiar faint fragrance surrounded him. The warmth in his chest settled. Nana's aura had always done that for him. Beside her, the world seemed less likely to tear open without warning.

"You have grown taller," Nana said, rubbing his head. "And older."

"Not that old," Lan muttered into her sleeve.

Nan Cheng felt an absurd little sting of jealousy.

Her son had just been a hero of a spacecraft. He had been scolded, hugged, questioned, and praised. Yet the moment he saw Nana, he became the child who once refused to let go of a teacher's hand.

Nana raised her head and smiled at Lan Xiao and Nan Cheng.

"Welcome to Heaven Dou City."

There was no Soul Guidance car waiting.

Nana had taken public transport with Dong Qianqiu to the space center, so the Lan family followed them the same way. Heaven Dou City's public bus was crowded, full of ordinary passengers, luggage, announcements, and the gentle sway of a city that did not know one of its arrivals had been chased by pirates days earlier.

Lan stood beside Nana. She held his shoulder lightly.

Dong Qianqiu stood on the other side and, with a face full of unwillingness, tried to squeeze him away.

Lan did not move.

In terms of strength alone, Dong Qianqiu was not his match.

Nana, still smiling, simply held both children: one hand on Lan, one hand steadying Dong Qianqiu before their silent struggle became a public wrestling match.

Dong Qianqiu glared down at him. She was slightly taller, and she used every bit of that advantage.

Lan glared back.

"Why are you looking at me like that? Are you trying to show how big your eyes are? Mine are pretty big too."

Dong Qianqiu's fingers curled. "Just you wait."

"How baffling," Lan said. "Are all you girls like this?"

Her eyes sharpened. "Us girls?"

"Yes. You girls all have such bad tempers."

The words left his mouth before wisdom could catch them.

Dong Qianqiu nearly shook from anger. "My temper is bad? You clearly tried to take advantage of me and now you say my temper is bad?"

"You blocked me," Lan protested. "I was running too fast and couldn't stop."

"Then why did you grab me?"

"We were falling!"

"Hng!"

She turned her head away.

Nana heard every word and did not interrupt. Her smile became softer, as if she understood that children sometimes fought because embarrassment did not know how to stand quietly.

Lan Xiao and Nan Cheng exchanged a glance and nearly laughed.

Dong Qianqiu had not removed her mask yet, but her eyes and dark-blue hair were already enough to show how pretty she was. Their son's argument with such a girl looked endearing from the safe distance of adulthood.

Children did not live at that distance.

To Dong Qianqiu, the mask did not erase the fact that contact had happened.

To Lan Xuanyu, the accident did not explain why she was so angry.

Both were right enough to make the argument useless.

Heaven Dou Academy was older than Heaven Luo Institute in a way buildings could show without words. Its stones looked as if they remembered generations of footsteps. Trees stood around the paths with patient dignity. Nana's specialized dormitory building was simple but well treated, an apartment with three rooms and a cultivation room prepared for someone the academy had reason to respect.

The room was clean, bright, and almost severe. No unnecessary decoration. No softness for the sake of softness. Only essentials, polished windows, spotless surfaces, and the faint fragrance that Lan associated with Nana's presence.

"Qianqiu," Nana asked, "are you going back to your dormitory?"

"Yes, Teacher," Dong Qianqiu replied.

Then her gaze slid toward Lan Xuanyu.

"Can I bring him around the academy?"

Her eyes glistened slightly.

Lan felt warning prickle at the back of his neck.

Nana smiled. "Of course. I will prepare food. Come back for dinner before cultivating tonight."

"Sure!"

After Dong Qianqiu removed her mask, Lan Xiao and Nan Cheng both paused.

The girl was indeed stunning. Fair skin like porcelain, precise features, bright eyes with lashes that seemed to speak even when her mouth did not. Standing beside Lan Xuanyu, she looked so visually matched that any adult fond of beauty might think the same careless phrase.

A golden pair.

Nan Cheng thought it for one second and then remembered the way Dong Qianqiu had glared at her son.

A golden pair could still bite each other.

"Let's go," Dong Qianqiu said, grabbing Lan's sleeve. "I'll show you the school."

Lan did not want to go.

He had finally reached Nana. He wanted to ask about his breakthrough, about his gold and silver vortex, about how soon Rank20 could happen, about too many things that had nothing to do with being dragged around by an angry girl.

But Dong Qianqiu pulled hard, and Nana only smiled.

Lan was dragged out.

The moment they left Nana's door, Dong Qianqiu released his sleeve and walked ahead without a word.

Lan followed.

The academy grounds opened around them: old stone buildings, shaded paths, a forest that gave way to a broad lake, and beyond the lake, a plaza where students moved in clusters after class.

Lan watched everything carefully.

This was a habit now. Classrooms, exits, water, lines of movement. Yin Tianfan had ruined him for simple sightseeing.

Dong Qianqiu's shoulders were too relaxed.

That made him more suspicious, not less.

She stopped near the plaza and turned.

"Lan Xuanyu."

She smiled.

It was a very pretty smile.

For a moment, with the clear lake behind her and Heaven Dou Academy's old buildings beyond that, Lan understood why the surrounding students looked whenever she moved. She was the most beautiful girl his age he had ever seen except for the complicated categories in his own head that did not have names yet.

Then Dong Qianqiu stepped forward and held his arm.

Lan froze.

The plaza quieted.

Dozens of eyes turned toward them.

Some were stunned.

Some were envious.

Many, especially from the boys, became sharp enough to stab.

Dong Qianqiu's plan became obvious.

She wanted revenge.

Not by hitting him directly, because Nana might scold her.

By making everyone who adored Heaven Dou Academy's cold little goddess want to beat him instead.

Lan felt the warmth of her hand through his sleeve and the unnatural stillness of the plaza around them. He also noticed that Dong Qianqiu herself had begun to blush. She was not as comfortable with the plan as she had pretended.

She started to loosen her hold.

Lan's mind turned quickly.

Running now would make him look guilty, weak, and ridiculous. Worse, it would make him look as if Teacher Nana had brought a cowardly student. That was unacceptable.

Yin Tianfan had many bad habits.

Some were useful.

If the other side created the battlefield, change the shape of it.

Lan turned halfway, placed an arm around Dong Qianqiu's waist before she could retreat, lowered his head, paused at the last moment, and kissed her cheek.

Not the lips.

Even in that instant of counterattack, he understood that would be too much, a point of no return.

Her cheek was soft.

The scent was light and clean.

The plaza died.

Dong Qianqiu froze as if an ice skill had struck inward.

Lan released her quickly.

"How sweet," he muttered before thought could stop him.

Then he faced the plaza and shouted, "I am Dong Qianqiu's boyfriend. My name is Lan Xuanyu. Anyone who isn't convinced can come fight me!"

The word boyfriend rang across the stones.

It did not feel like truth.

It felt like a borrowed sword he had swung before knowing whether the handle had thorns.

Some promises in his life were quiet, unfinished, tied to a blue cord and half sentences far away on Heaven Luo Planet. This was not that. This was a tactic, childish and crueler than he had meant it to be.

He realized that a breath too late.

The surrounding students did not rush him.

That was the first problem.

They were too shocked. Some probably believed that anyone bold enough to declare himself like that must have strong backing.

The second problem arrived behind him.

Cold.

Killing intent.

"LAN—XUAN—YU—"

Dong Qianqiu's voice cracked through his name with fury and a hint of a wail.

Lan turned.

Her eyes were red.

Not only angry.

Humiliated.

He had gone too far.

Three soul rings rose under her feet.

Two yellow.

One purple.

Two yellow and one purple together made the gap obvious — two yellow, one purple, exactly the order Lan saw as he retreated.

Cold mist spread outward, dropping the plaza's temperature sharply. An ice spear formed in her right hand as she shot forward.

Lan retreated and released Blue Silver Grass from both hands.

Golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass — golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass in the academy records — wrapped around his right arm. Fine golden scales covered his skin. A single white soul ring emerged from his body.

Dong Qianqiu saw it instantly.

One ring.

After years without being summoned often, she had not known his exact cultivation. Now she knew: he still had not obtained his second soul ring.

Her anger did not vanish.

It gained direction.

The ice spear struck.

Lan punched.

The spear shattered, and Dong Qianqiu paused for a fraction of a second in midair, surprised by his raw strength. Lan used that instant to flee toward the lake.

He could not beat her directly.

Not without the martial soul fusion technique.

And he could not use that.

Using it could drop his Soul Power again. He had come all the way to Heaven Dou to break through to Rank20 with Nana's protection. Losing days now because of a childish fight would be stupidity deep enough that even Qian Lei might not defend it.

Water.

He stepped onto the lake by using his left hand's ice and water control, forming a thin ice platform beneath his foot. The moment his toe touched down, he pushed farther away.

Dong Qianqiu snorted and threw the ice spear.

Lan dodged, skimming across water and ice.

Her second soul ring lit.

The air over the lake thickened with ice mist. Hundreds of ice awls formed within it, sealing the space above him.

Second soul skill: Ice Wave.

A strengthening field for her ice abilities.

Lan dropped low and raised an ice shield while curling behind it to reduce the target area. Ice awls hammered down. The ice platform cracked beneath him and dumped him into the water.

For a silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass user whose first soul skill involved water element control, falling into water was not the same as losing.

A vortex formed around him. Ice awls entering the lake were pushed aside by the water current. Lan drove himself toward land.

Then danger roared through the water behind him.

He looked back.

A dark-blue shark, seven or eight meters long, shot toward him with a wide mouth and killing speed.

It was formed of energy, but the pressure it gave off was not fake.

Dong Qianqiu's Spirit Soul.

Lan's profession lessons and modern cultivation classes collided in his head.

Most modern Spirit Souls strengthened soul skills rather than fighting separately. Manmade Spirit Souls especially lacked true intelligence; controlling them directly cost Spiritual Power, attention, and precision. That was why current Soul Guidance Technology pushed Spiritual Power more often into mechas, battle armor, and the path called Duo Mecha Master. A Soul Master had only so much mind to spend. Many would rather let metal and armor multiply their control than force a manmade Spirit Soul to behave like a living companion.

Dong Qianqiu was controlling hers as a combat body.

And controlling it well.

For one absurd instant, Lan thought of Yan Shuo's Fire Phoenix route far away: living soul-spirit foundations, wing-root interface, frame compatibility, and the academy trying to teach a body and future armor not to lie to each other.

Different profession roads.

Same lesson.

Spiritual Power decided what could be controlled.

The shark rushed closer.

Lan pushed upward with water pressure, bursting out of the lake. The shark's jaws snapped beneath him.

Cold wind descended from above.

Dong Qianqiu appeared with another ice spear, thrusting downward.

Lan hung in midair without support.

The spear hit his right arm. Golden scales trembled. The force smashed him back toward the water. Below, the shark opened its mouth again.

He almost brought his hands together.

Martial soul fusion.

No.

Rank20.

Nana.

Breakthrough.

He could not lose that chance to win a ridiculous fight he had caused.

What do I do?

Falling, Lan took a deep breath.

His blood surged.

A low dragon roar sounded from within his body, deeper and clearer than before. Golden scales spread quickly up his right arm toward his shoulder.

He turned forcefully and punched downward.

Golden Dragon Soar.

As he struck, the image of the enormous golden dragon in space flashed through him: wings spread in vacuum, pirate ships melting, a meteor-class cannon beam stopping in front of impossible power.

The dragon roar in his body became more tangible.

A golden dragon-head aura burst from his fist.

The shark closed its mouth and charged upward. Dong Qianqiu's expression remained furious, but she was not trying to kill him. Her control was sharp even through anger.

Bang!

The Golden Dragon Soar collided with the shark and forced it back into the water. Lan rebounded upward.

Dong Qianqiu borrowed recoil from the lake, caught up, and kicked him in the backside.

Lan flew toward land.

"Ouch, ouch! She's murdering her boyfriend!"

The shout was shameless.

It was also useful.

Pain scattered part of his panic, and the gold-silver vortex in his chest revolved, dissolving the cold force that had entered his body. His body was absurdly tough; by the time he hit land, he tucked into a roll, dispersed the momentum, and ran.

Dong Qianqiu's anger, which had cooled slightly after hitting him, ignited again at the shout.

"Stop right there!"

She chased.

The students by the lake stared as if watching a drama written by a teacher with no understanding of school discipline. The cold goddess of Heaven Dou Academy was chasing a handsome boy across water and stone. The boy looked terrified, ran like a horse, and yelled things that made half the plaza question reality.

Lan remembered the route back after walking it once.

He sprinted.

Dong Qianqiu was powerful, but she was not an agility-type combat Soul Master. In the lake, she could use ice and the shark. Inside the academy grounds, she could not throw lethal soul skills freely. Students might step out. Accidents would become trouble.

That left running.

Lan was very good at running when survival and Nana were in the same direction.

He charged into the building and took the stairs instead of waiting for the lift.

Dong Qianqiu closed the distance behind him.

"Teacher Nana, save me!"

An ice ball struck the back of his head.

Lan covered his head and kept running.

He could have dodged.

He did not.

He had gone too far. If he did not let her vent a little, this would never end.

By the time he reached Nana's floor, he dove toward the door like a defeated soldier reaching a fortress.

"Save me, Teacher Nana!"

He knocked, then crouched on the spot with both hands over his head.

Dong Qianqiu arrived, breathing hard. An ice spear started to form.

Then she looked at him kneeling there, defenseless except for shamelessness.

Her hand tightened.

The spear did not stab.

Instead, she kicked him.

"Speak more drivel! Talk more!"

The door opened.

Nan Cheng stood there.

Dong Qianqiu's leg froze in the air.

Lan lifted his head with the most aggrieved expression he could produce. "Mom, she beat me."

Dong Qianqiu almost fainted from rage.

"Aunty, he kissed me!"

The sentence escaped before she could stop it.

Then her entire face turned red.

Nan Cheng's first instinct had been maternal anger. Her son was a guest here. How could this girl beat him outside the door?

Then the second sentence arrived.

Her son kissed another family's girl?

They were still children.

They had only just properly met outside Nana's home — or so Nan Cheng thought, because she did not know how many times Dong Qianqiu had appeared through the Gate of Summoning.

Nan Cheng looked at Lan.

Lan looked back hopefully.

Nan Cheng quietly closed the door.

Lan stared at the door.

Then he slowly turned toward Dong Qianqiu.

Dong Qianqiu smiled.

It was not a forgiving smile.

Her knuckles cracked.

"Aunty is too kind. Don't worry. On Aunty's account, I won't hit your face."

"Ah! Save me!"

Inside the room, Nan Cheng leaned back against the door and explained quickly to Lan Xiao.

"Nana went out to buy ingredients," she finished in a whisper. "What do we do?"

Lan Xiao listened to the muffled wails outside with the calm of a father who had already survived one impossible revelation this trip.

"He is crouching there and letting her beat him," Lan Xiao said. "That means he knows he did something wrong. Let the children handle it. How bad can it be?"

"That is your son."

"Exactly. His body is stronger than mine ever was. And if he really kissed her first, he deserves a few hits."

Nan Cheng glared at him.

Lan Xiao coughed. "Of course, we must educate him afterward. Strictly. Very strictly."

Outside, Dong Qianqiu beat Lan without using Soul Power. Even without Soul Power, a Soul Master's fists and kicks were not gentle. Lan covered his head and did not retaliate. His skin hurt. His pride hurt more. But there were no real injuries.

At last, Dong Qianqiu sat down on the floor, gasping.

Lan sat nearby, soaked, sore, and not brave enough to look directly at her for several breaths.

Her eyes were still red.

Some anger had left.

The hurt beneath it remained.

Lan's heart softened.

He had thought of revenge in the plaza. He had not thought of how she would return to class after his shout.

"Sorry," he whispered.

Dong Qianqiu glared.

Lan scooted closer. "Why don't you hit me more if you're still angry?"

She pushed him so hard he fell sideways, then punched him several more times. Her voice trembled. "How am I supposed to go back to school? How will my classmates look at me?"

Lan's embarrassment thickened until he wanted to sink through the floor.

"I only thought of getting back at you," he admitted. "I didn't think that far. I don't know how to fix it either."

Dong Qianqiu's lashes were wet.

He lifted a hand without thinking and wiped a tear from them.

She froze, then shoved his arm away. "What are you doing?"

"Nothing. Nothing," Lan said quickly. "What can I do? I can't beat you anyway."

The pitiful tone made Dong Qianqiu want to kick him again.

Lan, still tangled in childish panic, reputation, guilt, and words he had heard adults use without understanding their weight, said carefully, "Then... why don't you be my girlfriend when we grow up? I'll take responsibility."

The words sounded wrong the moment they left his mouth.

Not false exactly.

Just borrowed.

Too large for the small, messy guilt he was trying to cover.

Dong Qianqiu stood at once. "Who wants to be your girlfriend in the future?"

She kicked him a few more times and ran away.

Lan remained on the floor, wet and sore.

He had handled that badly.

Worse than badly.

If Yin Tianfan had seen it, he might have praised the battlefield reversal and failed him in human consequences.

Inside Nana's apartment, the interrogation began after he showered and changed.

Lan Xiao and Nan Cheng sat opposite him with expressions far more dangerous than pirate cannons because they were quieter.

"Tell us," Nan Cheng said. "What happened?"

Lan lowered his head and explained.

Not perfectly.

No child explained his own guilt perfectly.

But he did not hide the main points: the accidental collision, Dong Qianqiu's revenge plan, the arm-holding in the plaza, his cheek kiss, the public boyfriend claim, the chase, and the beating.

Nan Cheng smacked his shoulder.

"You brat. How could you do that? A girl's reputation is important. You shouted that in front of everyone. How could she not be angry? You deserved to be beaten."

Lan Xiao nodded solemnly. "You deserved it."

Lan stared at him. "Daddy, Mommy, how can you side with an outsider? She tried to take revenge on me first. I only fought back. And I got beaten too!"

Nan Cheng snorted. "I saw you after your shower. Not even a proper bruise. She held back."

Lan's eyes widened. "Mommy! I am already so big, and you still look when I shower?"

Nan Cheng's ears reddened. "I am your mother. I care whether you are hurt. What is wrong with that?"

Lan felt wronged from several directions at once.

Dong Qianqiu's hits had hurt.

It was not his fault his body was thick-skinned.

Nana returned with ingredients soon after.

The moment she entered, her look at Lan told him Dong Qianqiu had already complained.

Lan stood immediately. "Teacher Nana, I was wrong."

Nana rubbed his head.

"It was a small misunderstanding," she said gently. Then, with a smile that made Lan's back straighten, she asked, "Do you really want to date Qianqiu when you grow up?"

"Ah?"

Dong Qianqiu had told her that too?

Nana did not wait for him to find an answer.

"Qianqiu is very outstanding and very hardworking. If you truly want to court her, you will need to work hard to catch up. She will enroll in Shrek Academy next. If you want to be beside her in the future, you must also strive to enter Shrek."

The word changed the room.

Shrek.

Lan Xiao and Nan Cheng both reacted before Lan did.

"Shrek Academy?" Nan Cheng stood up almost at once. "That Shrek Academy?"

Nana nodded.

Shrek was only one word.

It carried tens of thousands of years of history.

On the old Mother Planet, Douluo Planet, it had already been the first academy of Soul Masters. Its inheritance reached back more than thirty thousand years. Through eras of soul beasts, battle armor, mechas, star travel, and Federation expansion, the name had not been washed away.

For Soul Masters, it was a sacred place.

Even studying there for one day could become an honor people carried for a lifetime.

And legends said Shrek had god-rank elites.

Nan Cheng's excitement dimmed under practical worry. "But Xuanyu's cultivation is too weak. How could he enter?"

Nana looked at Lan.

"I heard from our academy that Shrek's basic requirement is reaching Rank20 before age twelve. With that, students can participate in the basic assessments held across major planets. Of course, most serious participants are Rank25 or even Rank30 and above. But Rank20 gives qualification. Xuanyu came here for Rank20, and I will help him. He has twin martial souls, and his martial soul is very unique. He may have a chance."

Lan thought of the trial waiting after vacation.

"Our Elite Junior Class has a test when school reopens," he said. "A preliminary test. Could it be for this?"

Nana smiled. "Most likely. Your Elite Junior Class was probably built to select talented students who might attempt Shrek. But standing out among all students of the same age on a planet will be difficult. Are you confident?"

Lan did not pretend.

"No. I only have one ring. Many of the others have second rings, and some have third rings. The gap is big."

His mind flashed through names.

Qian Lei, Rank25, Spiritual Power over six hundred, Gate of Summoning becoming stranger every term.

Liu Feng, Rank26, speed sharpened like a spear point.

Lu Qianxun with three rings and pride refined by defeat.

Ye Lingtong and Chang Jianyi, still dangerous in the right formation.

Yan Shuo, Rank39, wings and Ultimate Fire folded under public restraint, an impossible column in the same class.

Lan did not say all of that aloud.

He did not need to.

The weight of it sat behind his answer.

"That is not definite," Nana said. "We will see whether anything changes after you obtain your second ring. Eat first. Then we begin."

Dinner was made with simple methods.

Nana's cooking was not spectacular.

The ingredients were.

Rare meats, rare vegetables, nourishing materials that made Lan's bloodline respond before his tongue decided whether the flavor was good. Dong Qianqiu had been invited, but after what happened, she did not come.

Lan ate quietly at first.

Then hunger remembered itself and he ate less quietly.

After dinner, Nan Cheng helped Nana clear the dishes. Nana brought Lan into the cultivation room.

The room was clean and empty, built for focus rather than decoration.

"Are you full?" Nana asked, rubbing his head.

Lan hugged her waist. "I'm full. Thank you, Teacher Nana."

"Then let me see whether you have been idle these years."

Lan frowned. "I could have broken through Rank20 earlier. Teacher Ji just refused to give me leave, so it was delayed until now."

There was impatience under the complaint.

He had watched classmates pass him in Soul Power. He had watched one ring become two, two become three, while he stayed Rank19 with a body and Spiritual Power that made teachers stare but a Soul Power number that refused to move when he needed it to.

Soul Power was still the foundation of a Soul Master.

Without it, every other strength felt like standing on one leg.

Nana said, "Sharpening the blade does not delay cutting wood. This may not be bad. Sit. Let me sense the changes in your gold and silver vortex."

Lan obeyed.

He sat cross-legged. Nana sat behind him and placed her palm gently on his back.

Warmth entered.

The gold and silver vortex in his chest slowed, not from suppression, but from comfort. The two colors circled with a steadiness he had not felt by himself. At the center, the tiny multicolored dot remained like a grain of light.

Nana's brows lifted slightly.

She closed her eyes and sensed for a long time.

When she finally withdrew her hand, she said, "Very good."

Lan turned his head. "Teacher Nana, what is good?"

"Your current situation is extremely good," Nana said. "Better than I expected. At present, there should be no danger if you break through to Rank20 by yourself."

Lan blinked. "Ah?"

Nana's gaze softened. "Your aura feels very familiar to me, as if you are similar to me. Perhaps that was why I felt a connection when I first met you."

Lan grinned. "Is it because I am too good-looking? I feel connected to Teacher Nana too."

Nana tapped his forehead lightly. "Naughty child. You are not allowed to anger people with that mouth in the future. I have never seen Qianqiu so angry."

Lan lowered his head.

"But," Nana continued, "we should speak of the important matter."

Her tone changed.

Lan straightened.

"When your bloodline first awakened, it was very dangerous," Nana said. "Most people have only one main bloodline: an ordinary human bloodline, or a bloodline changed by the martial soul. Martial souls can carry special auras and lead to bloodline variation. Most good bloodline mutations can strengthen a Soul Master's body and support cultivation. But your situation is different. You do not have only one bloodline. You have two."

Lan listened without moving.

"They correspond to your two Blue Silver Grass martial souls. One is more affable, more connected to elements. The other is closer to pure strength and power. If only one awakened, it would help you greatly. But both awakened, and their classifications are incompatible. They collide. If that collision goes wrong, your meridians can be injured, cultivation can be crippled, and severe danger can threaten your life."

Lan had known parts of this.

Hearing it plainly still made his back cold.

"So I used a spiral method," Nana said. "The spiral keeps the two bloodlines apart while allowing them to move around each other. They keep distance and do not collide fully. Occasionally, slight contact gives them the possibility of adapting, but the hidden danger remains. Each breakthrough strengthens bloodlines. Stronger bloodlines make stronger collisions. That is why I wanted to be present for your Rank20 breakthrough."

Lan's hand unconsciously moved toward his chest.

"But I did not expect what happened when you used your self martial soul fusion technique," Nana said. "Under another unique force, the two energies fused for a moment. That eruption was powerful because the two bloodlines truly mixed. The small multicolored dot in the center of your gold and silver vortex is the result."

"The rainbow dot?"

"Yes. Because of it, a third bloodline has appeared in your body."

Lan's eyes widened. "Third bloodline? Two are already dangerous. Wouldn't three be worse?"

Nana shook her head. "Not necessarily. This third bloodline is born from the fusion of the first two. Its existence means your body has found a method to mix the two conflicting powers. Every time you use the martial soul fusion technique, you are actually mixing gold and silver again. After each mixing, they should become a little more compatible."

Lan's expression changed slowly.

Fear did not vanish.

It gained a shape.

"So the rank drop..."

"Is not simple loss," Nana said. "Your three years have produced good results. The third bloodline is weak, but after several uses of the fusion technique, it has stabilized. With it, your two bloodlines should not clash too excessively. If my senses are correct, given enough time and cultivation, repeated fusion may eventually let them merge into one. When the third bloodline is strong enough, it may incorporate both powers and become the one you truly use."

Nana paused, as if surprised by her own next words.

"It may be a mighty force. So mighty that even I feel I would submit to it."

Lan stared.

He had seen Nana's strength.

He had also seen Tang Le outside the warship window.

For an instant, the two figures stood on opposite sides of his mind: silver hair, calm eyes; blue hair, golden dragon, and a cannon beam walking backward.

Who was stronger?

The question flashed and vanished.

It was too large for him.

Nana continued, "The process will be very long. Your third bloodline is still weak. There is no way to stimulate it except by using your martial soul fusion technique. My suggestion is this: for every ten ranks of Soul Power, use the fusion technique ten times to mix the bloodlines — ten times per ten ranks. That should make future breakthroughs safe."

Lan opened his mouth.

Then closed it.

Then opened it again.

"Does that mean every ten ranks, I actually have to cultivate twenty ranks? I am already slow. If this continues, won't I fall farther and farther behind?"

He thought of the Elite Junior Class again.

Not as a list of enemies.

As pressure.

Qian Lei might complain about effort, but his Rank25 was real.

Liu Feng's Rank26 was real.

Lu Qianxun's three rings were real.

Yan Shuo's Rank39 and Spirit Sea pressure were real, and so were the profession files that refused to treat him as a normal Soul Elder.

Even outside Soul Power, everyone had routes: summons, speed, lion pressure, dragon-type resonance, Interplanet Command, mecha interface, battle-armor foundations, shield-load maps, sensor lanes.

No one was waiting politely for Lan to catch up.

Nana seemed to read part of the worry.

"Recultivation is faster than true cultivation," she said. "From Rank20 to Rank30, it may take time equivalent to about fifteen ranks, not twenty. And your effort will not be wasted. Sharpen the blade first. When you reach the critical point in the future, you may truly soar."

"How long will that take?"

Nana thought for a moment. "Perhaps a hundred years. Perhaps two hundred or three hundred."

Lan's face changed. "That long? I will be old!"

"You may not be," Nana said. "Your third bloodline carries immense life-force characteristics. Your lifespan should exceed ordinary humans."

Lan did not feel comforted.

He had just seen a man destroy a warship with bare hands.

Waiting hundreds of years sounded like being told the sky existed but stairs would be delivered eventually.

Nana's voice remained gentle. "Do not be impatient with cultivation. A strong foundation is better than reckless speed. Calm yourself. Aren't you curious what your second soul ring will bring? Let us begin."

The disappointment in Lan's chest was swept aside by sudden excitement.

Second soul ring.

Would it appear by itself like the first?

Would he need a Spirit Soul after all?

What would change?

"All right," he said.

On Heaven Luo Planet, while Lan sat before Nana and stared at the edge of Rank20, the academy's west practice wing remained lit.

Vacation did not mean all growth stopped.

It only meant the corridors were quieter when growth hurt.

Mu Zhongtian stood beside a sealed desk while Ji Hongbin read three reports at once: Heaven Dou's preliminary incident confirmation, the upcoming cross-academy trial notification, and an internal profession-progress file that had become too thick to be convenient.

Yin Tianfan's reply to the warship incident had already arrived.

It contained nine lines of insults about emergency protocol, three lines of professional analysis, and one sentence Ji Hongbin read twice despite himself.

If the boy can make a civilian reserve warship live under pirate fire, stop treating Interplanet Command as his consolation prize.

Ji did not like agreeing with Yin Tianfan.

He agreed anyway.

Lan Xuanyu's Rank19 problem was real.

So was the fact that a Rank19 child had entered a real 25m warship, used Spiritual Power and professional control to destroy two pirate mechas, survived long enough for the impossible to intervene, and returned alive.

That did not erase the Soul Master road.

It meant the academy could no longer write the profession road in smaller ink.

On another screen, Yan Shuo's file waited.

Rank39.

SP962.

Fire Phoenix.

Duo Mecha Master / Battle-Armor Interface Foundation.

Wing-root compatibility unresolved but improving.

Ultimate Fire heat-channel discipline: acceptable at low output, prohibited at high output without adult field.

Frame response: stable in training; future battle armor interface requires custom design, living-wing clearance, and soul-spirit resonance buffer.

No armor completion.

Ji Hongbin tapped the table.

"One cannot break through. One is too close to Rank40 for comfort. Both are trouble."

Mu Zhongtian looked at the files. "That sounds like our class."

"Do not sound proud."

"I am trying not to."

Below them, in the profession room, Yan Shuo landed inside Song's ground-return mark.

The landing was quieter than last week's.

Song checked the pressure edge. "Left wing-root recoil is down three points."

"Good," Yan said.

Luo leaned over the sensor terminal. "Right side tried to lie again. Smaller lie. Still a lie."

Yan looked at him. "One day, I will ask Teacher Mu to ban that word."

"Then I will call it poetic misdirection."

"I dislike that more."

The three of them were supposed to be on lighter vacation maintenance.

Teacher Ji's definition of lighter meant no one vomited unless they made a poor choice.

Tonight's assignment was not combat. It was profession foundation: return points, heat channels, wing-root measurement, shield load, sensor lanes. Nothing spectacular. Nothing a crowd would cheer.

The kind of thing that decided whether spectacular power became useful later.

Song's shield did not simply block anymore. It marked where a flying teammate could safely return without crushing a knee or burning an ally.

Luo's calls did not simply say left or right. They named pursuit lines, blind angles, false openings, and the one instant when an aerial Fire Phoenix center had to stop being beautiful and become reachable.

Yan's job was hardest precisely because his power wanted to make hard things look unnecessary.

Wings could cross a field.

Ultimate Fire could frighten people.

Purple Flame could disrupt hostile structures.

None of that meant anything if he abandoned the formation, overheated the frame, or let a future armor plate fight his living wing.

The terminal on the bench remained silent.

No full message from Lan yet.

Yan knew only that Lan had reached Heaven Dou after a delay and questioning. Nothing precise. Nothing about golden dragons, Dong Qianqiu, public boyfriend claims, or Nana's bloodline explanation.

That ignorance was frustrating.

It was also correct.

Lan's sealed doors were Lan's.

Yan adjusted the heat-dulling clasp at his copper-red hair. Soul power stirred, and red-gold glow caught for one breath inside the strands before settling.

"Again," he said.

Song lifted the shield mark.

Luo raised two fingers. "West lane. No poetic misdirection."

Yan launched.

Far away, Lan Xuanyu placed his hands on his knees inside Nana's cultivation room and prepared to attempt the threshold that had waited too long.

Different planets.

Different professions.

Different secrets.

The same future drawing both of them toward tests that would not care how young they were.
