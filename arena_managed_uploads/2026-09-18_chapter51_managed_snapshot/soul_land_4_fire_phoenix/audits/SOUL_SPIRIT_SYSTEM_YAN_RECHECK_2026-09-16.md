## 1. Source/mechanics basis used

System facts applied:

- Soul Spirits/Spirit Souls are spirit beasts or spirit-like entities in energy form used to complement soul rings; they can provide one or more rings and can evolve/grow under certain systems such as Spirit Ascension Platform routes [3](https://soulland.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Spirits).
- Stronger/higher-rank Soul Spirits require stronger Spiritual Power and body capacity; Spirit Sea can support five yellow, three purple, or one black soul spirit [2](https://soulland.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Souls), [3](https://soulland.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Spirits).
- Age/ring grant pattern: 10+ white/one; 100+ yellow/up to two; 1,000+ purple/up to three; 10,000+ black/up to four; 100,000+ red/four-to-five; 200,000+ ferocious/orange/four-to-five; 1,000,000+ divine/gold/special [2](https://soulland.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Souls).
- Low-level/artificial Soul Spirits before 10,000 years may lack true intelligence/spiritual wisdom; stronger higher-grade spirits can have more will/intelligence and may communicate or help under conditions [2](https://soulland.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Souls), [3](https://soulland.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Spirits).
- Battle manifestation is not free: a manifested Soul Spirit can be hurt, and damage can wound the host/impact Spiritual Power [3](https://soulland.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Spirits).
- Compatibility and bloodline counter-resonance can affect martial-soul evolution/growth, so Yan's Phoenix bloodline and perfect compatibility must be central [3](https://soulland.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Spirits).
