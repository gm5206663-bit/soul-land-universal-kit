# Power Law — Yan Shuo'er Route

Live edge: **after Chapter31**.

Purpose: define how power should be judged so Yan is neither nerfed nor made unearned/invincible.

---

## 1. Formal rank is not total combat power

Actual combat power depends on:

- soul power rank;
- Spiritual Power;
- martial soul quality;
- soul-spirit age/compatibility;
- soul skills;
- body condition;
- adaptation/training conversion;
- tactics;
- teammates;
- restrictions/environment;
- information available.

Do not rank characters by soul power number alone.

---

## 2. Current Yan power package

After Chapter31:

- Rank23/SP156.
- Fire Phoenix martial soul.
- Dawnflame Kite 480 private/internal maturity.
- Dawnfeather Mantle.
- Dawn-Iron Phoenix Roc 1,680 deep purple.
- Skybone Phoenix Frame.
- Hidden adaptation foundation.

This places Yan far above ordinary Rank23 reading in direct combat.

---

## 3. Current scaling rules

- Yan can defeat Lu Qianxun in fair direct combat without full fusion.
- Liang Zheng is competent but not near Yan's tier.
- Dorm333 can still threaten/win in special categories because Lan's hidden mechanics are abnormal.
- Lan's dangerous self-fusion-like technique is not a normal repeatable field tool and can be restricted.
- Song and Luo's support affects results; Yan is not always fighting alone.

---

## 4. Costs and constraints

Yan is not invincible.

Valid limits:

- stamina;
- soul power drain;
- body strain;
- recovery hunger;
- heat control;
- public identity safety;
- unfamiliar mechanics;
- multiple enemies;
- battlefield restrictions;
- protecting teammates;
- secret knowledge she does not have.

Invalid nerfs:

- forgetting second ring effects;
- pretending Dawn-Iron is minor;
- ignoring public monitor status;
- making weaker canon peers beat her without tactics/explanation;
- using backlash to erase all growth.

---

## 5. Future power law after Level30

After the future Level30 one-month cocoon:

- old pre-Level30 power ceiling must be rebuilt;
- full Fire Phoenix and Ultimate Fire create a new category;
- true flight changes battlefield geometry;
- Dawnflame and Dawn-Iron must remain integrated;
- public attention/resource classification must escalate.

Do not keep old scaling after that event.
