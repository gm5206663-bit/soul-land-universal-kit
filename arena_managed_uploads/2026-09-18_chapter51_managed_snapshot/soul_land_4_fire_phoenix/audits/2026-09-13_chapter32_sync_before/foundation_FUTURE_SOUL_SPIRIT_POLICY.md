## 2. General future soul-spirit rules

Future soul spirits must:

- fit Yan's Fire Phoenix martial soul;
- deepen existing foundations instead of replacing them;
- create believable resource/institution consequences;
- affect body, skills, training, and combat category;
- respect Soul Land mechanics as much as possible;
- produce public butterfly effects when visible.

Future soul spirits must not:

- be cosmetic;
- erase Dawnflame or Dawn-Iron;
- appear without acquisition logic/resources;
- grant full divine mastery without process;
- be used to nerf Yan afterward.
