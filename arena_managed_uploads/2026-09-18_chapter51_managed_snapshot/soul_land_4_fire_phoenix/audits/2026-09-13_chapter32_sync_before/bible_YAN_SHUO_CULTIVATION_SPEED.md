# Yan Shuo Cultivation Speed

Live edge: **after Chapter31**.

Purpose: prevent both under-growth/nerfing and unearned jumps.

---

## 1. Core rule

Yan Shuo'er grows fast because of:

- Fire Phoenix quality;
- hidden adaptation foundation;
- six years of pre-awakening body readiness;
- Lin Mei's tailored training;
- Yan Ke's resources/safety planning;
- Dawnflame and Dawn-Iron compatibility;
- pressure from academy, Lan, rivals, and Soul World;
- high food/resource conversion.

Growth is earned and costly, not free.

---

## 2. Early growth summary

Known route:

- Awakening: innate soul power level 6.
- Early academy: reaches Rank10 first bottleneck before final exam period.
- Chapter6 after first ring: around Rank12.
- Later training and elite pressure raise her steadily.
- Chapter25 after second soul spirit: Rank23.
- Chapter26-31: Rank23/SP156 current live edge.

---

## 3. Why fast growth is valid

Fast growth is supported by:

- easy early ranks after level 6 awakening;
- body already adapted to fire-bird lineage;
- correct soul-spirit compatibility;
- always-working passive adaptation;
- heavy appetite/resource demand;
- strong family training;
- academy pressure;
- not wasting time at bottlenecks once correct foundation is found.

---

## 4. Why growth still has limits

Yan needs:

- food;
- sleep;
- recovery;
- medicinal/resource support;
- correct pressure;
- safe integration;
- soul-spirit compatibility;
- time at major bottlenecks.

Higher levels require much more soul power/resources/body tempering.

---

## 5. Time-skip rule

Do not freeze Yan during the three-year time skip.

Before writing post-skip:

- plan Rank23 to Level30 path;
- decide when bottleneck occurs;
- account for one-month cocoon at Level30;
- plan Song/Luo growth too;
- compare source post-skip canon values carefully without copying blindly.

---

## 6. Future Level30 event

Level30 is not a normal quick breakthrough.

It triggers the 6,000-year Purple Flame Eidolon Bird fusion and one-month cocoon metamorphosis.
