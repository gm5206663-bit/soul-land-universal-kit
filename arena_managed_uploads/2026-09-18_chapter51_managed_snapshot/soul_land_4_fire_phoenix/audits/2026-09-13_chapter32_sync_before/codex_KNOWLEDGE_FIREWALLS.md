# Knowledge Firewalls and Trust Sharing

Live edge: **after Chapter31**.

Standing rule: **old trust stays real, private secrets stay protected, public consequences must breathe.**

---

## 1. Why firewalls exist

The fic has several overlapping secret systems:

- Lan Xuanyu's origin and gold/silver/private bloodline mechanics.
- Nana's true identity and deeper connection to Lan.
- Dong Qianqiu / Gate / summon-route private material.
- Yin Tianfan's exact training/simulator methods.
- Yan Shuo'er's private girl identity.
- Yan's hidden adaptation foundation.
- Dawnflame's private/internal growth beyond public paperwork.

Secrets must not leak casually.

---

## 2. Firewall is not emotional distance

Lan and Yan are childhood best friends.

Correct:

- they worry about each other;
- they tease each other;
- they compete;
- Lan says what he safely can;
- Yan accepts boundaries while still noticing pressure;
- public facts affect them.

Incorrect:

- making them cold because secrets exist;
- making Lan refuse all warmth;
- making Yan demand exact secret mechanics;
- using secrecy to erase Dorm336's public rights.
