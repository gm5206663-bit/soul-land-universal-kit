## 3. Yan Shuo simulation route

Chosen route: **Yan Shuo runs a parallel survival path in another part of the virtual forest, not Lan Xuanyu's stream/Hippo/Nether Wolf route.**

Reason:

- He is a ten-candidate participant after Chapter 11.
- His corrected power demands visible performance.
- He must not steal Lan Xuanyu's canon Hippo/Nether Wolf/Three-Eyed Witch Ape heroism or coma sequence.

Starting state in simulation:

- Yan Shuo begins already in quiet low-output Dawnflame Form, continuing Chapter 11 ending.
- Dawnflame Kite gives instinctive cues, not speech.
- Dawnfeather Mantle is living feather-layer defense/movement/recoil support.
- Dawnflame Kite and first-ring behavior carry a private/internal actual living age **203 years, yellow-tier** with above-normal practical output; original/legal acquisition paperwork remains 100 until a formal retest or earned special method changes it. The academy basic test does not retest this for every candidate.
- Yan Shuo uses fire intelligently; forest fire is danger, not a cheat.

Suggested encounter path:

1. Yan Shuo appears on a dry ridge/roots above a denser forest section, away from Lan Xuanyu's stream.
2. He immediately reads wind, heat, bird-call absence, and animal scent through Dawnflame Form.
3. He avoids igniting the forest, making a thin heat line/fire-light feather screen rather than burning everything.
4. First threat: a pack of three-to-five hundred-year Thornback Jackals, Ember-tooth Hounds, Windblade Weasels, or Ironclaw Lynxes. Choose one in prose and keep them low/mid hundred-year.
5. He uses low-output Dawnflame Form casually: pounce, shoulder wing-frame turn, mantle deflection, light-feather feint, recoil landing.
6. He protects/helps one nameless older candidate without making that candidate a major character.
7. Stronger threat: a mild thousand-year beast enters his zone, e.g. Stonehide Boar, Ash-Horned Lizard, or Ridgeback Ape. It should force intelligence, not automatic victory.
8. Yan Shuo does not solo-kill the thousand-year beast cleanly. He survives/escapes/delays it by using terrain, heat haze, feather-screen, high-output wing-frame burst, and adaptation.
9. His result impresses examiners because he uses his form naturally and controls fire in a forest instead of causing a disastrous blaze.
10. He earns selection through score, survival judgment, and clean form use.

Recommended chosen beasts for prose:

- **Hundred-year Ironclaw Lynxes**: fast ambush predators with claw attacks; good for showing recoil/mantle/angle correction.
- **Thousand-year Stonehide Boar**: powerful charge and thick hide; good for showing he cannot just overpower everything and must use terrain/wing-frame burst to redirect/escape.

Yan Shuo performance outcome:

- Defeats or drives off multiple hundred-year Ironclaw Lynxes using Dawnflame Form and Dawnfeather Mantle.
- Saves/redirects a panicking older candidate from a charge without turning it into grand heroism.
- Faces a thousand-year Stonehide Boar and chooses controlled evasion/trap/terrain break rather than prideful direct fight.
- Uses true combat Dawnflame Fusion and one high-output wing-frame burst that drains him but proves his current trump-card power can briefly touch ordinary two-ring pressure without becoming a real two-ring state.
- Survives until assessment emergency shutdown after Lan Xuanyu's coma event.
- Exits tired/sweating/strained but conscious; no new numeric upgrade from the simulation, but real combat-control proof is recorded.
