## 2. Corrected canon mechanics understanding

### 2.1 Plus sign meaning

A plus sign is **not merely a category-specific freak mark**.

Canon Chapter 102 says the plus sign is the greatest honor in that test context: teacher recognition that a student has a **rare quality worth grooming**. It is not counted inside the numeric score, but it creates a major practical advantage: it can protect a student/team from end-of-term elimination when the academy rule applies.

Correct meaning:

- numeric score = present measured performance;
- plus sign = teacher/institution flags a rare quality or future-grooming value;
- protection = rule consequence, not raw combat power;
- low raw total can still gain safety if plus signs show special qualities;
- high raw total can still lack protection if no rare trait is marked.

Therefore the old support phrasing that made plus signs sound only like a category-specific freak/outlier marker was too narrow.

### 2.2 No-Soul-Power strength/speed tests

Canon Chapter 101 says Soul Power and soul skills are forbidden in strength/speed tests. But the test still measures the whole real body: arms, legs, waist, bones, posture, tactical stance, willpower, and bloodline/body foundation.

Important consequence for Yan Shuo:

- He cannot activate Dawnfeather Mantle or cheat with Soul Power during the strength/speed test.
- But all passive, long-term, body-built advantages still count.
- Fire Phoenix meridians/body-frame, pre-awakening adaptation, recovery, breath, training, and integrated soul-spirit effects are not active cheating; they are his body.
- Lan Xuanyu's canon strength test proves this: his bloodline body strength and willpower count even without Soul Power use.

### 2.3 Spirit rings and compatibility are foundation, not decoration

Canon Chapter 13 establishes:

- Spirit Masters need a spirit ring every ten ranks to advance realms.
- Every spirit ring provides a soul skill.
- Skill strength relates directly to compatibility between ring and martial soul.
- Training before ring acquisition is not wasted; it accumulates and provides benefit after obtaining the ring.

For Yan Shuo:

- Dawnflame Kite cannot be treated as a simple yellow-ring sticker.
- Perfect absorption matters.
- Perfect compatibility matters.
- The living soul spirit becoming part of Yan Shuo matters.
- Pre-ring training, post-ring training, and adaptation all stack into the body/ring/martial-soul route.

### 2.4 Spiritual Power meaning

Canon Chapter 24 says Spiritual Power is quantifiable, relates directly to martial soul/spirit quality, and can develop through meditation; it also notes age-related growth. It gives the basic realm ladder and says 0-50 is Spirit Origin while over 50 enters Spirit Connection.

Canon Chapter 101 confirms Spiritual Power supports learning/memory/perception and that students over 50 are uncommon in this class context, while Lan Xuanyu/Qian Lei above 100 are exceptional.

Canon Chapter 115 clarifies the stronger range: Spirit Connection is 50-500, with 200 and 350 as internal thresholds, and 500+ Spirit Sea.

For Yan Shuo:

- SP88 is not monster-tier like Lan Xuanyu/Qian Lei.
- It is still strong, meaningful, and above most ordinary classmates.
- It should visibly improve control, perception, learning, fire-light boundary precision, and team reading.
- It must not be treated like only a number beneath the protagonist.

### 2.5 Soul World and simulation pressure are real growth inputs

Canon Chapters 86-87 establish Soul World as a high-realism simulated world for Spirit Masters. Real body data maps into it; pain sensitivity has a minimum, and fighting experience helps in the real world.

For Yan Shuo:

- Soul World pain-data is a real foundation input.
- Simulation combat, fear, pain, and repeated correction should change body memory and later choices.
- The private Dawnflame Kite growth ledger is justified by repeated live-like use, not by empty time passing.

### 2.6 Academy resources and body growth count

Canon Chapters 85, 101, and 113 support this strongly:

- Elite Junior Class has harsh schedules, heavy classes, simulators, and substantial resources.
- Midterms are specifically to measure how much the academy's investment changed the students after three months.
- Chapter 113 uses repeated simulator exposure, appetite/nutrition, strong meridians, and physical adaptation as real improvement signals.

For Yan Shuo:

- The month before midterms cannot be empty.
- Food, sleep, academy pressure rooms, recovery logs, armor/mecha-interface drills, and team practice must produce visible changes.
- His hunger/sleep/recovery should be evidence of growth, not background decoration.

### 2.7 Dragon/phoenix meridian hierarchy is directly relevant

Canon Chapter 106 is very important. It states that Golden Lion King is an extremely strong peak-grade lion spirit and even stronger than ordinary dragon spirits, but its meridian strengthening is **not as strong as dragon or phoenix type spirits**.

This means:

- Lu Qianxun's Golden Lion King is legitimately high-end.
- But dragon/phoenix-type martial souls have a special body/meridian advantage category.
- Yan Shuo's Fire Phoenix cannot be treated as merely a fancy bird flame.
- Even as juvenile/incomplete, Fire Phoenix must carry better body/meridian implications than ordinary beast/fire students.
- This strengthens the anti-nerf argument for Yan Shuo's raw physical marks.
