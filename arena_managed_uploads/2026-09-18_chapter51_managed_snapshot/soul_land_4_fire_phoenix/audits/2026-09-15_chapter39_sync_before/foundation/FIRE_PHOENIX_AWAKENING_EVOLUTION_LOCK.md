## 1. Terminology correction

User correction:

- In Soul Land, a martial soul can have a **second awakening**, and later even a **third awakening** or **fourth awakening**.
- This is martial-soul evolution / bloodline/category awakening terminology.
- It is **not** the same as gaining a separate second martial soul.
- When user says Yan Shuo’er’s beauty increased after “second martial soul awakening,” the intended meaning is **martial soul second awakening / Fire Phoenix evolution**, not a new martial soul.

Correct phrasing:

- “Fire Phoenix second awakening.”
- “Second awakening/evolution of the Fire Phoenix.”
- “Martial soul second awakening.”
- “Full Fire Phoenix category awakening.”

Avoid:

- “Yan got a second martial soul.”
- “Second martial soul body-frame.”
- “New separate martial soul.”
