The process must be:

- cocoon-like;
- about one month long;
- comparable in seriousness to Tang San's Blue Silver Emperor awakening in narrative weight;
- physically/spiritually transformative;
- not a quick ring pickup;
- not a cosmetic fire-color change;
- not handwaved offscreen.

---

At the end of the future cocoon, Yan gains:

- full Fire Phoenix martial-soul awakening;
- Ultimate Fire;
- full wings;
- true flight;
- third ring / third soul skill;
- Purple Flame Eidolon Bird integrated;
- Dawnflame Kite true awakening/evolution;
- Dawn-Iron body-frame integration;
- major body, spiritual sea, soul-spirit, ring, academy, team, family, and romance consequences.

Power can be public after the event.

Yan Shuo'er identity remains hidden unless the user explicitly approves otherwise.

Dawnflame Kite's true awakening/evolution must occur **together** with the Level30 cocoon event.

- earlier than the cocoon;
- minor;

- just a number increase;
