# Power Law / Scaling Rules

Live edge: **after Chapter32**.

Purpose: keep power scaling consistent.

---

## 1. Formal rank is not actual combat power

Soul Power rank matters, but actual combat power also depends on:

- martial soul quality;
- soul-spirit compatibility;
- soul skill design;
- Spiritual Power;
- body/frame strength;
- tactics;
- team support;
- output cost and repeatability;
- context/restrictions.

Yan's formal state after Chapter32 is Rank29/SP392/two rings, but actual combat power is higher than a normal Rank29 two-ring student.

---
