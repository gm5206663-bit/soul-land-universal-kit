A normal Rank39 Soul Elder does not have this stack.

Yan's serious release includes:

1. **True Fire Phoenix martial soul** — apex bird/Phoenix route, not generic fire bird.
2. **Ultimate Fire** — offensive attribute quality that makes low-output contact dangerous and suppresses inferior flame.
3. **Assault-type route** — her build is made to attack, pressure, and break enemy rhythm, not merely sense or support.
4. **Three purple rings / soul skills** — all above normal ring quality for a child and all route-compatible.
5. **Three compatible purple Soul Spirits** — not decorative; they express through mantle, body-frame, wings, pressure, and skill efficiency even without full fusion.
6. **SP962 / Spirit Sea** — exceptional for Rank39 and especially meaningful because Yan is not a spiritual-type Soul Master; it proves the body/spirit foundation can coordinate the load.
7. **Cocoon metamorphosis** — rebuilt Phoenix body/wings/frame, not cosmetic transformation.
8. **Adaptation talent** — long-term multiplier making compatibility, absorption, body adjustment, Ultimate Fire safety, and Soul Spirit expression unusually clean.

Therefore, the no-full-fusion serious state should not be downgraded to merely high Soul Ancestor unless Yan is restricted, exhausted, hard-countered, protecting others under bad terrain, or facing superior true-dragon/divine authority.

## 4. What the floor means and does not mean

The phrase **low Soul King-class effective threat floor** means:

- her serious released attacks/pressure/control are dangerous in the low Soul King band;
- ordinary Soul Elders and Soul Ancestors should be overwhelmed unless they have special counters;
- ordinary/no-special-counter low Soul Kings must take her seriously;
- she can pressure, injure, suppress, or force caution from low Soul King-level opposition in serious exchanges.

It does **not** mean:

- Yan is Rank50;
- Yan has five rings;
- Yan has a Soul King's Soul Power reserve;
- Yan has fourth/fifth soul skills;
- Yan has Martial Soul True Body;
- Yan can sustain like an adult Soul King;
- Yan automatically defeats all Soul Kings;
- Yan has Soul Emperor/Soul Saint parity.

Use this distinction:

> Low Soul King-class threat/output/control floor, not full Soul King rank package.

## 5. Bloodline suppression hierarchy

Bloodline suppression must be ranked correctly.

### Strongest: birds / avian / fire-bird / inferior Phoenix-like targets

Yan's true Fire Phoenix suppression is strongest here because Phoenix is apex among birds and her Soul Spirits/body/wings are all aligned with that route.

Effects can include instinctive fear, wing/rhythm disruption, aura collapse, flame obedience, loss of aerial confidence, and easier domination of battlefield height.

### Strong: inferior fire / ordinary fire beasts

Ultimate Fire is a direct attribute hierarchy advantage. Inferior flame should bend, thin, recoil, lose coherence, or become harder to maintain unless backed by higher realm or special authority.

### Moderate but real: pseudo-dragon / lizard-dragon / impure dragon-flame / lower fire-dragon-type targets

This suppression is weaker than against birds because dragon lineage resists Phoenix authority more than avian lineage does.

But it is not zero. If the target is pseudo-dragon, lizard-dragon, impure dragon-flame, or lower fire-dragon type, Yan still pressures it because true Fire Phoenix + Ultimate Fire outranks inferior fire and lower dragon blood is not equal to true Dragon authority.

Correct shorthand:

> Against dragon-types, Yan's bloodline suppression is weakest compared to bird/fire-beast targets, but inferior or impure dragon-flame still bows. Their fire can weaken while their body remains dangerous.

### Weakest/contested: true dragons / superior dragons / divine dragon authority

Yan does not automatically suppress true Dragons, Dragon God-level routes, divine dragon lines, or much higher-realm pure dragons.

Against those, Phoenix and Dragon are rival apex categories. Purity, realm, authority, attribute, ring count, and divine status decide the result.

## 7. Emerald Demon Bird lesson

Raw cultivation age alone does not decide combat.

A specialized bird-type killer such as the Emerald Demon Bird can threaten much larger dragon-type targets through speed, eye/brain weak-point targeting, ferocity, and timing. That does not mean every thousand-year bird is stronger than every ten-thousand-year dragon; it means species function, weak point, and matchup matter.

Therefore, Yan's bird/Phoenix Soul Spirits must not be dismissed as "only thousands of years" when they are perfectly compatible, routed through a true Fire Phoenix host, empowered by Ultimate Fire, stabilized by SP962, and integrated into a cocoon-rebuilt body.

## 8. Metamorphosis and flight rule

The cocoon metamorphosis is not cosmetic.

A normal/generic golden bird or ordinary avian should not casually fly better than Yan unless one of these is explicitly true:

- it is much older/higher realm;
- it is a specialized speed/wind/aerial species;
- Yan is restricted by rules, teammates, terrain, heat signature, identity concealment, or stamina;
- Yan is deliberately using safe training flight instead of combat risk;
- the bird has a specific skill/terrain advantage.

Otherwise, making an ordinary bird outfly her is nerfing.
