## 1. Root rule

Do not force canon’s original board state by weakening established powers.

Before writing any fight, assessment, Shrek reaction, teacher reaction, ranking, or social consequence, calculate the outcome from the full Soul Land structure:

1. martial soul grade and category;
2. bloodline purity / species hierarchy;
3. attribute quality and elemental restraint;
4. Soul Power rank;
5. soul ring color / age / skill quality;
6. soul spirit age, compatibility, absorption, and living-foundation effects;
7. Spiritual Power realm and control precision;
8. body/frame/wings/physical foundation;
9. active state layer: passive presence, martial soul release, soul skill, partial integration, full trump card;
10. terrain/environment compatibility;
11. team formation, support, and tactical roles;
12. opponent species, purity, rank, numbers, physical mass, skills, intelligence, and counters;
13. rules/restrictions/identity firewalls;
14. stamina/cost/aftereffects;
15. Shrek/teacher/academy observation logic.

If a result contradicts this calculation just to preserve canon, that is nerfing.

---

## 2. Soul Land outcome formula

Formal rank is only one component.

Actual combat result =

**rank + martial soul quality + bloodline hierarchy + attribute hierarchy + ring age/color + soul spirit foundation + compatibility/absorption + Spiritual Power/control + body/frame + skills + terrain + team + tactics + cost + restrictions + matchup.**

No single number replaces the full formula.

Wrong shortcuts:

- “Canon character originally won, so Yan must do little.”
- “Lan/Qian need this beat, so Dorm336 must become passive.”
- “Yan is only Rank39, so a large simulated beast ignores her Phoenix authority.”
- “Full integration is not used, so Yan’s baseline Fire Phoenix is ordinary.”
- “Knowledge firewall means public effects disappear.”
- “Song/Luo are weaker than Yan, so their contributions do not matter.”
- “A Shrek examiner ignores an obvious monster because canon did not include her.”

Correct method:

- preserve canon beats only after running the full calculation;
- adapt beats naturally around Yan/Dorm336’s established effects;
- give every visible effect an academy/social/ranking consequence;
- use real constraints, not artificial downgrades.

## 5. Phoenix / Dragon hierarchy

Phoenix is not dragon-lite.

True Phoenix and true Dragon are both top mythical branches. Which side dominates depends on purity, rank, age, attribute, bloodline, divine authority, and context.

Yan does **not** automatically suppress every Dragon in existence. He should not dominate a superior true Dragon, Dragon God-level being, divine beast, or much higher-realm pure dragon without earned conditions.

But Yan **does** naturally suppress inferior fire beasts, impure dragon-flame beasts, pseudo-dragon beasts, lizard-dragon creatures, and fire-dragon-type targets whose bloodline/attribute quality is below true awakened Fire Phoenix + Ultimate Fire.

### Fire-vs-fire rule

Ultimate Fire is not cosmetic.

When Yan faces inferior fire:

- inferior flame bends, thins, scatters, or loses coherence;
- the opponent’s fire aura becomes harder to maintain;
- fire instincts hesitate or recoil;
- target choice can destabilize;
- heat environments often help Yan rather than only harming him;
- the opponent may still threaten through body mass, claws, numbers, physical shock, higher total cultivation, or non-fire mechanics.

Correct shorthand:

**Their fire bows; their bodies may still be dangerous.**

Wrong shorthand:

**They are bigger, so Yan’s Phoenix fire does nothing.**

Limits are valid only when they arise from the system.

- no perfect mastery;
- high output costs stamina/control;

Invalid limits:

- lower-quality fire bypasses Ultimate Fire;

- Yan becomes passive because canon needs another team to act;
- Song/Luo disappear because Yan is strong;
- Yan needs full integration for every serious effect;
- Shrek ignores public monster data;
- a knowledge firewall erases observable consequences.

## 12. Mandatory pre-writing checklist

Before every new chapter, answer these internally:

1. What is Yan’s current exact power layer in this scene?
2. What part of the Soul Land formula matters most here: rank, bloodline, attribute, spirit rings, soul spirits, Spiritual Power, body, team, terrain, or restriction?
3. Is the opponent inferior/superior/equal in species and attribute hierarchy?
4. If fire/dragon/bird/beast hierarchy is involved, what natural suppression occurs?
5. What can Song and Luo contribute that raw Yan power alone cannot replace?
6. What does Shrek/teachers/classmates observe publicly, and how must they react?
7. Am I preserving canon by adaptation, or forcing canon by weakening Yan/Dorm336?
8. If Qian/Lan need a canon spotlight, how can it coexist with Yan’s natural effects rather than erase them?
9. Are the constraints real system constraints, or artificial downgrades?
10. Does the scene show consequences for Yan’s monster-level public profile without revealing private identity?

If any answer is missing, do not write prose yet. Repair the reasoning first.
