# Canon and Butterfly Protocol

Prepared: 2026-09-10
Updated: 2026-09-13
Status: locked workflow correction from author feedback plus Yan Shuo'er identity correction
Purpose: prevent canon from becoming a checklist and prevent Yan Shuo'er / public Yan Shuo from becoming a spectator.

---

## 1. Error corrected

The author pointed out that the story was not showing canon properly and that natural butterfly effects were not happening strongly enough.

Corrected understanding:

- Canon is not just a list of events to mention in a footer.
- Canon scenes must be written with their causes, emotional pressure, adult motives, public/private boundaries, and consequences.
- Yan Shuo'er is not inserted into every private canon scene, but when public Yan Shuo is naturally present in public/shared scenes, Yan's existence must change some reactions, records, choices, timing, or emotional residue.
- Butterfly effects must be natural consequences of her locked existence: childhood friendship, Fire Phoenix public talent, rank progression, family resources, public boy-cover discipline, no-reveal pressure, school track, Adaptation Talent, first soul-spirit route, and second soul-spirit route.
- Do **not** forcefully stop natural butterfly effects just to protect a prewritten outline. If Yan Shuo is naturally public/present/recorded, people should react naturally.
- Do **not** forcefully create butterfly effects where Yan has no access. Private canon scenes stay private unless an earned route opens them.
- The correct method is cause-and-effect: public presence creates public ripples; private canon beats stay firewalled; separate-instance events can run in parallel without pretending Yan Shuo'er is weaker or irrelevant.

---

## 2. Canon handling rule

For every canon chapter touched, record:

1. What exactly happens in canon.
2. Who is present.
3. What is public information.
4. What is private/family-only information.
5. What emotion or pressure drives the scene.
6. What changes after the scene.
7. Where Yan Shuo'er / public Yan Shuo can naturally touch the event.
8. Where Yan Shuo'er / public Yan Shuo must not intrude.

If a canon beat is private, the reader may see it in parallel prose, but Yan must not learn it without an earned route.

If a canon beat is public or shared, Yan's presence must have some believable effect.

## 3. Butterfly effect categories

Use these instead of random changes:

| Category | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Presence butterfly | Public Yan Shuo is physically present in a public/shared scene and changes how people react. | Teachers track another high-potential child; Ye Lingtong measures Yan as a possible rival. |
| Absence butterfly | Yan's absence or another character's absence changes routine. | Lan Xuanyu's holiday trip removes a familiar pressure environment, shifting Yan's bottleneck training inward. |
| Relationship butterfly | Friendship changes messages, worry, choices, or interpretation. | Lan Xuanyu sends Yan a travel picture; it affects how Yan Shuo'er thinks about a flawed soul spirit. |
| Record butterfly | Institutions record public Yan Shuo differently because she exists under that file while private identity stays protected. | Rank 10 bottleneck before final exam triggers school/Spirit Pagoda attention; Chapter 25 records Rank23/two rings under Yan Shuo without a true-name reveal. |
| Resource butterfly | Family money, access, and status shape available options. | Yan family cannot buy a perfect top-grade spirit soul, so a flawed Dawnflame Kite recorded at exactly 99 years becomes plausible. |
| Training butterfly | A previous lesson changes action later. | Lin Mei's breath training prevents Yan Shuo'er from rushing a first soul spirit. |
| Environmental butterfly | Current environment changes passive adaptation. | Artificial fields, city metal, absence of Lan Xuanyu pressure, and Spirit Pagoda testing all leave body-level effects. |

---

## 4. What butterfly effects must not do

Do not use butterflies to:

- Replace Lan Xuanyu as the canon protagonist.
- Steal Nana, Tang Le, or Lan family private emotional pillars.
- Give Yan Shuo'er canon secrets she did not earn.
- Break canon events only for drama.
- Force Yan into trips, homes, or rooms where logistics say she would not be.
- Make Adaptation Talent a system or all-purpose solution.
- Make Fire Phoenix suppress every dragon or ignore higher bloodlines.

---

## 5. Chapter-writing checklist

Before drafting a chapter:

1. Read the current active continuity guide.
2. Read the last chapter's footer and current state.
3. Check the relevant canon chapters carefully.
4. Recheck high-risk future canon claims against fresh chapter text before use; do not rely on memory for exact mechanics, names, thresholds, or chronology.
5. Create/update a coverage file.
6. Mark each canon beat as public, private, parallel, or butterfly-touchable.
7. Decide Yan's causal role before prose.
8. Make sure her passive always-on adaptation appears in ordinary life.
9. Make sure power progression is simulated, not nerfed and not unearned.
10. Write the chapter as story, not summary.
11. Audit knowledge firewall, power state, CJK, and stale continuity.
