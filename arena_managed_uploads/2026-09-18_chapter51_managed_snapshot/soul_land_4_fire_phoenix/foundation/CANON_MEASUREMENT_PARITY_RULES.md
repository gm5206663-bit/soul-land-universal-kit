## 1. Separate measurement categories

Do not mix these categories:

- **Soul Power Rank / Level**: cultivation level, e.g. Rank39.
- **Spiritual Power points**: measured mental/spiritual strength, e.g. SP962.
- **Spiritual Power realm**: Spirit Origin, Spirit Connection, Spirit Sea, Spirit Abyss, etc.
- **Soul spirit age**: e.g. 100-year, 480-year, 3,100-year, 6,400-year.
- **Ring category/color**: visible soul-ring tier; public record can be protected or incomplete.
- **Soul-spirit grade/capacity**: how many rings a soul spirit can provide and whether the host can bear it.
- **Actual combat power**: integrated result of martial soul quality, rings, soul spirits, skills, body, tactics, adaptation, and team support.

Formal rank is not the same as actual combat power.
