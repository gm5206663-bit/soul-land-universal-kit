# Chapter 50 — A Bone Returned to the Hand

The simulation cabin opened with a quiet hiss.

For one breath, Lan Xuanyu still felt as if color were moving under his feet.

No pattern.

No guarantee.

Then the air changed. The starry sky vanished. The giant wheel vanished. Ling Yiyi's pink radiance vanished with it. What remained was the familiar metallic ceiling of Heaven Luo Academy's simulation room, the faint medicinal-clean smell of maintained cabins, and the heavy silence of three boys who had walked out carrying things they did not yet fully understand.

Qian Lei was the first to sit up.

He clutched both sides of the cabin as though afraid the reward might slide out of his body if he moved too quickly.

"Five hundred years," he whispered.

Liu Feng pushed open his own cabin lid and looked at his right arm.

Nothing had changed there yet.

No silver light.

No new strength.

No bone beneath the skin that had not been there before.

But because nothing had changed, the promise felt even heavier. It waited outside the room somewhere, not yet in his hand, not yet in his body, but already bending the future toward him.

Lan sat up last.

His mind was still on the last reward.

Elementary Spirit Ascension Platform.

The words were clear. Their meaning was not.

He knew the Spirit Pagoda. Any Soul Master student knew the Spirit Pagoda. Soul Spirits, Soul Rings, artificial cultivation resources, simulations, research that had changed the entire world of Soul Masters — all of those shadows gathered behind the name.

But a platform for spirit ascension?

Could it affect his rings?

Should it affect them?

Lan lowered his eyes.

His rings had never come from a Spirit Soul.

They had appeared with him, changed with him, and deepened after breakthroughs in ways that ordinary explanations could not hold. When Qian Lei and Liu Feng had asked before, Lan had only given the safest answer: his first ring had already been close to a hundred years, and after he gained the second ring, both had crossed a line and become yellow together.

It was not impossible enough for them to keep chasing.

Elite Junior Class students having hundred-year rings was normal.

Lan had allowed normality to cover what was not normal.

Now the reward waiting for him was tied to the Spirit Pagoda's mechanisms, to Soul Rings and Soul Spirits and artificial ascension. The blank place on the wheel had opened into a door, and he did not know whether the door would recognize him or expose him.

"Xuanyu?"

Mu Zhongtian's voice pulled him back.

Their teacher stood beside the cabins, eyes moving from face to face. He had the restrained expression of an adult trying very hard not to look like a student who had just heard the words Soul Bone.

Qian Lei did not help him.

"Teacher Mu," he said solemnly, "please confirm that my hearing was accurate. All current Soul Rings. Five hundred years. Not fifty. Not five. Not a coupon."

Mu Zhongtian's mouth twitched.

"Your hearing is fine."

Qian pressed a hand to his chest. "Then my heart is also fine. It was only startled by prosperity."

Liu Feng glanced at him. "You looked more frightened than prosperous."

"Great fortune should be respected."

Mu Zhongtian finally failed to hide the smile at the corner of his mouth.

Then his gaze turned serious again.

"Report clearly. One by one."

Lan did not embellish. He explained the Lucky Wheel, the reward rules, the order of selection, and the three results. Qian Lei added emotion to every sentence involving his own reward. Liu Feng gave the shortest possible confirmation for the Soul Bone.

When Lan said the name of his own prize again, Mu Zhongtian's eyes brightened.

"Elementary Spirit Ascension Platform access?"

Lan nodded. "Teacher Mu, what exactly is it?"

This time Mu Zhongtian did not answer immediately.

He breathed out slowly, and that one breath carried a complicated mixture of envy, delight, disbelief, and a teacher's pride trying to remain dignified.

"Your luck," he said, "is enough to make other academies unable to sleep."

Qian Lei straightened at once. "Then it is good?"

"Good?" Mu Zhongtian looked at him. "Your reward alone is enough to make many students jealous. An increase of five hundred years to all current Soul Rings is not a small matter. It improves foundation directly. But Liu Feng's reward..."

His gaze shifted to Liu Feng.

Liu Feng's fingers curled once against his palm.

"A two-thousand-year Soul Bone," Mu Zhongtian said. "Even if it is man-made, even if it is not a top-grade ancient Soul Bone, it is still a Soul Bone. Do you understand what that means?"

Qian Lei raised his hand. "It means Liu Feng owes destiny a meal."

"It means," Mu Zhongtian said, ignoring him with practiced skill, "that Heaven Luo Academy may have half its teachers staring at him tonight. Soul Bones are rare. The federation has countless Soul Masters across many planets. Demand is always greater than supply. Ordinary Soul Masters may spend their whole lives without touching one."

Lan listened carefully.

Soul Bones were not like Soul Rings.

A Soul Ring was a necessary step on the path. Difficult, dangerous, unequal — but the path existed. A Soul Bone was extra fate. Extra ability. Extra foundation inserted into the body itself.

Mu Zhongtian continued, "Man-made Soul Bones solved part of the rarity problem, but not the cost. Their greatest advantage is not that they are cheap. They are not. Their advantage is control and growth. With modern soul guidance technology and Spirit Pagoda methods, man-made Soul Rings and man-made Soul Bones can have their cultivation age improved later."

Lan's attention sharpened.

"Improved through the Spirit Ascension Platform?"

"Exactly."

Mu Zhongtian's eyes moved to him.

"The Spirit Ascension Platform is one of the Spirit Pagoda's most precious systems. Inside, a Soul Master enters a simulated environment and fights emulated soul beasts. When those soul beasts are defeated, their energy can be absorbed and used to improve the years of Soul Rings, Soul Spirits, and compatible man-made Soul Bones. The opportunity ends when the entrant is eliminated, reaches the limit of what the access allows, or can no longer continue safely."

Qian Lei's eyes became round.

"So if I go in and defeat many soul beasts..."

"Do not finish that sentence with nonsense," Liu Feng said.

Qian looked offended. "I was going to say I would work hard."

"You were going to say you would become rich in years."

"That is another form of working hard."

Mu Zhongtian raised one hand before their argument could grow legs.

"Listen first. A slot is extremely precious. The number released every year is limited, and the price is high enough that most families cannot even consider buying one. Lan Xuanyu's reward may not look as solid as Liu Feng's Soul Bone, but if used properly and with enough luck, its value can be even greater than a fixed five-hundred-year increase."

Qian Lei stared at Lan as though the blank door had suddenly become a treasure vault.

Lan did not feel reassured.

If his rings were ordinary, this reward would be simple.

They were not ordinary.

He kept his expression calm.

Mu Zhongtian noticed the calm, but not what hid beneath it. "Do not rush to use it. Ling Yiyi was right. The Platform takes time, and the next selection stage matters more immediately. A reward wasted through impatience is worse than no reward."

Lan nodded. "I understand."

"Good." Mu Zhongtian looked at all three of them. "Come with me. Vice President Yin and Teacher Ji must hear this directly."

The three climbed out of the cabins.

Qian Lei's feet landed on the floor with suspicious gentleness.

Liu Feng asked, "Why are you walking like that?"

"I am carrying future thousand-year rings in spirit."

"You are carrying air."

"You lack imagination."

Lan smiled faintly, but his thoughts had already moved ahead.

Yin Tianfan would not let rewards remain only rewards.

Ji Hongbin would not let excitement cover foundation.

And Heaven Luo Academy now had more than one team under Shrek's gaze.

Dorm333 had won first.

Dorm336 had taken second through a completely different route, with Yan Shuo's public Fire Phoenix foundation too visible for any serious observer to pretend it was ordinary. That path would have its own review, its own pressure, and perhaps its own price.

But this moment belonged to the three of them.

A summoner's rings.

A spearman's arm.

A captain's door.

They followed Mu Zhongtian out.

Yin Tianfan's office looked unchanged from the outside.

Inside, it felt different.

Perhaps because Lan had heard his teacher's story only a short time ago. The clean desk, the instruments, the discipline in the arrangement of everything — all of it now seemed to hold the shadow of a young man standing before Shrek's final examination and choosing victory without people.

Ji Hongbin stood near the window when they entered. Yin Tianfan sat behind the desk with narrowed eyes, as if he had already guessed that Mu Zhongtian's expression meant trouble, treasure, or both.

"Speak," Yin Tianfan said.

Mu Zhongtian reported.

He gave the rewards in order.

Qian Lei's all-ring increase.

Liu Feng's right arm Soul Bone.

Lan Xuanyu's Spirit Ascension Platform access.

The office became quiet.

Yin Tianfan leaned back slowly.

Ji Hongbin's face did not change much, but his eyes sharpened behind the lenses.

"A Soul Bone," Ji Hongbin said.

Only three words.

Enough.

Qian Lei opened his mouth, probably to helpfully confirm that everyone had indeed heard correctly.

Lan stepped lightly on his shoe.

Qian swallowed the sentence with heroic sacrifice.

Yin Tianfan tapped one finger on the desk.

Once.

Twice.

Then he said, "The Soul Bone should not automatically belong to the person who drew it. It should be assigned to whoever among the three is most suitable. If the best match is not Liu Feng, it must be reallocated. Do any of you object?"

The words fell harder than a scolding.

Liu Feng's head lifted.

For an instant, something flashed through his eyes — surprise, instinctive tightening, the natural reaction of a boy who had just been handed a treasure and then told the treasure might not stay in his hands.

Then it passed.

He looked at Lan.

He looked at Qian Lei.

His answer came without delay.

"I do not object."

Yin Tianfan's finger stopped.

Ji Hongbin's eyes moved fully onto Liu Feng.

Mu Zhongtian looked openly startled.

Yin Tianfan leaned forward. "Think before answering. You know what a Soul Bone is."

"I know," Liu Feng said.

"You know that even a two-thousand-year man-made Soul Bone is something many Soul Masters cannot obtain?"

"Yes."

"You know that if it suits you, it may change your entire combat route?"

Liu Feng's right hand tightened again.

"Yes."

"And if it suits someone else more?"

"Then it should go to that person."

The answer was still quiet.

Still immediate.

No performance.

No forced generosity.

Yin Tianfan stared at him.

For once, even he seemed unsure whether to laugh, scold, or praise.

"You children," he said at last, "are becoming harder to teach in the usual way."

Ji Hongbin's stern expression softened by a fraction. "Why?"

Liu Feng looked down, then up again.

"Because we did not get first place separately. Without Xuanyu, we would not have stayed in the Elite Junior Class. Without Qian Lei's summon, we would not have killed the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragons. Without both of them, I would have been eliminated long ago. The reward came to me because the team reached that wheel. If another choice strengthens the team more, then that is the correct choice."

Qian Lei, who had been trying very hard to look noble, suddenly turned his head aside.

Lan saw it.

Liu Feng had not said everything.

He did not need to.

The room had no reason to mention Ling Yiyi's offer in detail, but the three of them remembered. Qian Lei had been given a straight path into Shrek Academy and had refused to abandon them.

Compared to that, a Soul Bone was still a Soul Bone.

But it was not heavier than the road they had chosen together.

Lan said softly, "Teacher Yin, Teacher Ji, we agreed after the qualifier. Individual gains should make the team stronger. They should not split us."

Yin Tianfan looked at him for a long moment.

The old command teacher's eyes were sharp enough that Lan felt as if the office had become another test field.

Then Yin Tianfan exhaled.

"Good. At least the story I told was not poured into the floor."

Ji Hongbin nodded slowly. "Good children."

Qian Lei immediately straightened. "Teacher Ji, please record that sentence. It may be useful during future punishments."

Ji Hongbin looked at him.

Qian Lei lowered his head. "I will be good silently."

Mu Zhongtian covered a cough.

Yin Tianfan stood.

"If the students are not stingy, the teachers cannot be stingy either."

Lan looked up.

Yin Tianfan's expression had become decisive.

"I will speak to the president. Once the Heaven Luo planet selection ends, if you three enter the top ten, I will find a way to obtain two more Elementary Spirit Ascension Platform slots. If I have to use old favors and empty my pockets, then I will empty them. All three of you will enter together and improve before Shrek's true assessment."

Qian Lei's mouth opened.

No sound came out.

This silence lasted so long that Liu Feng glanced at him in concern.

Then Qian Lei whispered, "Two more slots?"

"If you enter the top ten," Yin Tianfan said.

"All three of us?"

"If you enter the top ten."

Qian Lei placed both hands over his heart. "Today is too much. My greed cannot keep up."

"Then train it," Yin Tianfan said. "No, train your strength. Leave the greed weak."

Ji Hongbin did not smile, but the air around him seemed less cold.

"They earned first place across the federation qualifier stage," he said. "Shrek gave prizes. Heaven Luo Academy should not act poor at the wrong time. If we cannot support students who bring back this result, then we deserve to be looked down on by Ling Tian."

Mu Zhongtian nodded. "The academy already has six shortlisted teams in this continuity. Resources will be tight, but first across the federation cannot be handled like an ordinary pass. Dorm336's second-place route and Yan Shuo's separate Shrek review will need their own arrangement, but that does not reduce what Dorm333 earned."

Yin Tianfan gave a short laugh. "Let Ling Tian watch carefully, then. Heaven Luo may be behind in resources, but this time our students walked ahead."

Lan felt the words settle.

Two more Platform slots.

Not guaranteed.

Not free.

Top ten first.

Then support.

The reward had not become power yet, but a road had opened.

Beside him, Liu Feng's expression had changed in a way only someone who knew him well would notice. His face was still calm, but the tightness in his shoulders had loosened.

If the Soul Bone remained his, he would use it.

If it did not, the team would still gain.

And now there was a chance that all three of them would enter the Platform together.

Qian Lei leaned slightly toward him and whispered, "Giving things up is terrifyingly profitable."

Liu Feng whispered back, "That is not why we do it."

"I know. But if virtue insists on rewarding us, I will not be rude."

Lan looked away before his smile betrayed him.

The morning passed under instructions, warnings, and repeated reminders not to rush.

Qian Lei asked three times whether the Spirit Pagoda procedure for his reward would hurt.

Each time, a different adult told him that pain was not the point.

This did not comfort him.

Liu Feng asked once whether he needed to prepare anything if the Soul Bone proved suitable.

Ji Hongbin told him to adjust his state, keep his mind calm, and not let excitement interfere with fusion.

Lan asked about the Spirit Ascension Platform only in general terms.

No one mentioned his natural rings.

No one asked why his Spirit Pagoda-linked reward made him more cautious than excited.

By afternoon, the prizes arrived.

They did not arrive with music.

No pink light.

No wheel.

No voice from the stars.

A sealed delivery case came under academy authorization, checked by Mu Zhongtian, opened under Yin Tianfan and Ji Hongbin's eyes, and placed on the office table as if the surface of the desk had suddenly become a small treasury.

Two cards came first.

One belonged to Lan.

It was simple, pale, and more heavily protected than its plain appearance suggested. The markings on it belonged to the Spirit Pagoda. The card did not glow dramatically, but when Lan's fingertips approached it, his Spirit Sea stirred faintly, as if the blank door from the wheel had left a quiet echo behind.

One access opportunity.

Elementary Spirit Ascension Platform.

Unused.

Waiting.

The second card was Qian Lei's.

He looked at it the way hungry people looked at food locked behind glass.

"All current Soul Rings," he murmured. "Five hundred years. You are a small card, but your soul is enormous."

Liu Feng said, "Do not speak to the card either."

"You are hostile to communication."

"I am hostile to you being strange before teachers."

"Too late," Yin Tianfan said.

Qian Lei accepted the judgment with dignity.

Then the case revealed the third prize.

The room changed.

Not loudly.

Nothing exploded. No violent aura swept out. Yet the moment the protective layer opened, Lan felt the skin along his forearm tighten.

A silver shape rested inside the case.

It resembled an arm bone, but not a full adult's arm. It was smaller, cleaner, refined until the structure looked more like moonlight given skeletal form than something taken from flesh. A dim silver luster gathered inside it, restrained and cold, not bright enough to dazzle but sharp enough to make the air around it feel thinner.

Qian Lei leaned forward.

Then immediately leaned back.

"It bites without moving," he said.

Lan understood what he meant.

The ache on his skin was faint, but real. Like standing near a blade whose edge had not touched him yet.

Mu Zhongtian unfolded the attached record and read silently first.

Yin Tianfan took it from him and scanned it.

His eyebrows rose.

Then he laughed.

"Good character truly does not let treasure run away. Liu Feng."

Liu Feng stepped forward. "Yes."

Yin Tianfan placed the record where all three boys could see it.

"Silver Moon Wolf Right Arm Bone. Two-thousand-year grade. Spirit Pagoda refinement and adjustment complete. Attached skill: Silver Edge. It can be released through the right arm directly, or guided into an external object. When conducted through a weapon, it forms a silver crescent edge roughly three inches long. It consumes Soul Power during use."

Liu Feng stared at the words.

Silver Moon Wolf.

Right arm.

Silver Edge.

Weapon conduction.

The office seemed to narrow until only those details remained.

Yin Tianfan looked at Ji Hongbin.

Ji Hongbin nodded once.

There was no need for debate.

"It suits him," Ji Hongbin said.

Yin Tianfan's gaze returned to Liu Feng. "Very much."

He pointed at Liu Feng's right hand.

"Your White Dragon Spear route has speed, but your attack power has always been insufficient. Your movement lets you reach places, but reaching a place is useless if your strike cannot decide anything. You rely on Xuanyu's support too much when you need true penetration. This Soul Bone will not solve everything, but it gives your right arm a sharper answer. Silver Edge can conduct into the spear. It fits your problem almost too neatly."

Liu Feng did not speak.

His eyes were bright.

Not loud like Qian Lei's excitement.

Not calculating like Lan's caution.

Bright in the way a blade became visible when light finally struck its edge.

Qian Lei looked between the bone and Liu Feng.

"Then it really is yours."

Liu Feng's throat moved.

"If teachers agree."

"We agree," Ji Hongbin said.

Yin Tianfan picked up the silver bone with care. Even through the protective handling layer, the sharp aura seemed to gather along his fingers.

"Fuse with it now. We will guard you. A two-thousand-year Soul Bone is not effortless with your present cultivation, but this is a refined man-made Soul Bone. Its resistance should be gentler than a wild one. With Old Ji here, the risk is controllable."

Liu Feng bowed deeply.

"Thank you, Vice President. Thank you, Teacher Ji. Teacher Mu."

Mu Zhongtian's eyes warmed. "Keep your mind steady."

Liu Feng nodded.

He walked to the cleared space beside the table and sat cross-legged.

The office became very quiet.

Qian Lei stopped joking.

Lan watched Liu Feng's back.

A Soul Bone was extra fate.

But fate still needed a person to bear it.

Yin Tianfan tossed the protected silver shape lightly toward Liu Feng, not carelessly, but with the certainty of someone forcing a student to catch his own road.

Liu Feng raised both hands and received it.

The moment the Silver Moon Wolf Right Arm Bone touched his palms, his shoulders tightened. The faint ache in the air sharpened. A line of silver reflected across his face.

He took one breath.

Then another.

He looked down at the bone.

For the first time since the reward appeared, confusion crossed his calm expression.

He had studied Soul Bones.

He knew their value.

He knew what part of the body this one belonged to.

But knowing about a miracle and knowing how to place it inside oneself were not the same thing.

Liu Feng held the silver arm bone carefully, almost stiffly.

Then he looked up at Ji Hongbin.

"Teacher," he said, very quietly, "what do I do first?"

No one laughed.

The silver light waited in his hands.
