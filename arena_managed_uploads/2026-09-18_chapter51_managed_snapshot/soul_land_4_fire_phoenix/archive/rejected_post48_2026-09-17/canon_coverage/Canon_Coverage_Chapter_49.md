## 2. Required Chapter171 canon beats

- Dorm333 walks through the pink-light portal.
- They arrive in an unusual Lucky Wheel space:
  - pink halos from all directions;
  - boundless space overhead;
  - a huge colorful wheel beneath their feet;
  - the wheel spins too fast for clear visual identification.
- Ling Yiyi explains the rules:
  - each student may move/run around the wheel and choose a spot;
  - when the wheel/choice resolves, the reward appears;
  - once someone stops, that person may not move again.
- Lan Xuanyu asks whether all three can stand on the same spot.
- Ling Yiyi says no, only one person per spot.
- Lan asks what kinds of rewards are on the wheel and whether they connect to colors.
- Ling Yiyi says she does not know / luck decides; warns them not to stop unless they choose.
- Ling's halo places them onto the wheel.
- Lan immediately tells Qian Lei and Liu Feng not to stop.
- The three keep walking.
- Lan uses his newly strengthened Spirit Sea Spiritual Power to sense aura differences beneath/around the wheel.
- He cannot see exact rewards, but senses rotating categories/auras:
  - sweet/fresh/enticing aura;
  - fierce/ominous aura;
  - resistant/repelling aura;
  - blank/no-aura zones.
- Lan realizes the rotating auras make exact selection nearly impossible.
- Lan asks Qian Lei to stop first.
- Qian Lei stops; purple halo appears; voice announces: **all Soul Rings' cultivation age increased by 500 years**.
- Lan observes that the fresh aura had been four steps away before Qian stopped.
- Discuss why Qian's reward is very useful:
  - strengthens his current soul rings/skills;
  - may improve summoning speed/quality/duration;
  - useful for future Shrek qualifier rounds;
  - collection/activation should follow Ling's later instructions, not be treated as fully applied inside this scene if not yet collected.
- Lan asks Liu Feng to stop.
- Liu Feng stops; Lan senses the wheel speed/change and the ominous aura originally about seven steps away moving to Liu.
- Voice announces: **Spirit Pagoda-made 2,000-year right arm Soul Bone with one Soul Skill**.
- Explain Soul Bones sufficiently:
  - six main body-part categories: head, torso, left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg;
  - soul bones enhance the body part and grant an extra skill;
  - ancient soul bones came rarely from killed soul beasts;
  - soul rings vanish on death but soul bones can be passed down;
  - Spirit Pagoda developed man-made soul bones in recent millennia;
  - man-made soul bones remain extremely expensive and valuable;
  - Liu Feng is excited but cannot move after stopping.
- Lan then chooses for himself.
- He compares Qian's four-step and Liu's seven-step offsets and finds no reliable pattern.
- He continues sensing, then stops.
- Lan feels as if he is absorbed into the ground / choice locks in.
- An aura from at least ten steps away arrives; no pattern works.
- Voice announces: **Access to the Elementary Spirit Ascension Platform once**.
- The three return beside Ling Yiyi in a flash of light.
- Lan realizes he had stopped on a blank/no-aura zone and wonders what the Spirit Ascension Platform is.
- Ling Yiyi says rewards will be delivered to the school later.
- Ling Yiyi says Lan Xuanyu and Qian Lei must go to the Spirit Pagoda to collect their rewards.
- Ling Yiyi recommends collecting after the qualifier/selection round because the Spirit Ascension Platform may take a long time; rushing it wastes the opportunity.
- Ling Yiyi disappears.
