# Chapter 49 — The Second Door Also Opens

The pink light swallowed Dorm333 together.

For a heartbeat Lan Xuanyu heard Qian Lei inhale as if he had stepped into a banquet hall. Then the world opened.

There was no ground in the ordinary sense. Pink halos drifted in every direction, soft and layered, and above them stretched a boundless space without ceiling or wall. Beneath their feet, a huge wheel spun so quickly that colors became a river. Red, gold, blue, purple, white, black — all of them flashed past too fast to name.

Qian Lei's mouth opened.

Liu Feng's hand tightened at his side.

Lan did not move.

Reward spaces were also tests. Shrek did not waste opportunities.

Ling Yiyi's voice sounded from the pink light. "This is the Lucky Wheel. Each of you may move on it and choose a place to stop. Once you stop, you cannot move again. When the wheel resolves your position, your reward will appear. Only one person may occupy a chosen spot."

Qian Lei immediately looked at Lan, not at the wheel.

That alone proved he had learned something.

Lan raised his hand slightly. "Don't stop yet."

Qian froze with one foot half-lifted. "I was only preparing to appreciate destiny."

"Appreciate it while walking."

"Can destiny be dizzy?"

"If it is, don't let it drag you with it."

Liu Feng gave Qian a brief look and began moving carefully after Lan.

Lan asked, "Sister Ling, can all three of us stand on the same spot if we decide that is safest?"

"No," Ling Yiyi answered. "One person, one spot."

"Do the colors correspond to reward types?"

"I do not know. Luck decides. Remember: do not stop unless you have chosen."

The answer sounded simple.

Too simple.

Lan lowered his gaze to the racing light beneath his feet and released his Spiritual Power cautiously. His newly opened Spirit Sea spread perception farther and finer than before. He still could not see the rewards. The wheel did not become transparent, and no generous label appeared to help them.

But there were differences.

A fresh, sweet aura flashed past and vanished. A fierce aura scraped against perception like hidden teeth. Something resistant pushed back. Blankness appeared between them like quiet holes in the flow. All of it rotated too quickly, overlapping and exchanging positions until any fixed pattern broke apart before Lan could catch it.

A true lucky wheel.

Or at least a wheel designed to punish children who believed luck could be bullied.

Lan counted breath, step, aura, rhythm.

No stable rule.

Then he stopped trying to control what could not be controlled and focused on what could be decided: order, risk, and team benefit.

"Qian," he said.

Qian's eyes brightened and dimmed at the same time. "That tone sounds like I am about to become the experiment."

"Stop on my mark."

"Can experiments request insurance?"

"Your insurance is that we all need your reward to be useful."

Qian straightened. For all his complaints, he knew when a moment mattered.

"Mark."

Qian stopped.

Purple light rose around him.

A clear voice announced, "Reward: all current Soul Rings' cultivation age increased by five hundred years."

For a breath Qian Lei did not react.

Then both hands flew to his head. "All of them? All current rings? Five hundred years?"

The pink space did not answer his greed, but it did not take the words back either.

Liu Feng's eyes changed. Even he understood what that meant for Qian. Longer summons. Faster response. Higher quality. Less helplessness when the Gate answered badly. For a summoner whose entire fate often hung on one unstable door, five hundred years on every current ring was not a small reward. It was a foundation correction.

Lan saw the fresh aura he had sensed earlier pass several steps away from where Qian had stopped.

Four steps? Perhaps.

Or perhaps the wheel only wanted him to think so.

He did not trust it.

"Liu," Lan said.

Liu Feng looked at him and nodded.

No question.

That trust was heavier than Qian's jokes.

Lan watched the wheel again. The fierce aura that had scraped his senses seemed to approach and then slip away. Seven steps? Less? More? The colors blurred.

"Stop."

Liu Feng stopped.

Silver light burst upward.

This time even Qian was silent.

The voice announced, "Reward: Spirit Pagoda-made two-thousand-year right arm Soul Bone with one Soul Skill."

Liu Feng stared.

A Soul Bone.

Not a ring. Not a ticket. Not an ordinary cultivation resource.

Every student at Heaven Luo Academy knew the word. Six main categories were taught early: head, torso, left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg. A Soul Bone strengthened the body part it merged with and granted an additional skill. In ancient times they came rarely from killed soul beasts, while soul rings vanished with death and Soul Bones could be passed down. In the modern era, the Spirit Pagoda had developed man-made Soul Bones through enormous cost and technique, but man-made did not mean cheap.

A two-thousand-year right arm Soul Bone was enough to change Liu Feng's road.

Liu's right hand trembled once, then stilled.

He could not move after stopping. The rule held him in place, but his eyes were bright in a way Lan had rarely seen.

Qian whispered, "I suddenly feel very generous about being the experiment."

Lan's mouth curved faintly.

Then it was his turn.

He walked alone across the spinning colors.

Qian's reward had been excellent. Liu's was shocking. Lan did not expect the wheel to become kinder just because he wanted it to. He compared the offsets again — four steps, seven steps, fresh aura, fierce aura, blankness — and found nothing reliable enough to call a method.

A false pattern was worse than no pattern.

So he chose when his perception found a quiet gap, not because it promised the best result, but because forcing certainty where none existed would be stupidity wearing calculation's clothes.

Lan stopped.

For an instant he felt as if the wheel had opened under his feet and pulled his choice downward.

An aura from far away arrived.

No pattern.

The voice announced, "Reward: access to the Elementary Spirit Ascension Platform once."

Lan blinked.

Qian looked baffled. Liu Feng looked thoughtful.

The pink light folded.

A breath later, the three of them stood before Ling Yiyi again.

Qian immediately raised both hands. "Sister Ling, I have a question, a request, and possibly a complaint depending on the answer."

Ling Yiyi smiled. "Ask the question first."

"When do my rings become five hundred years more handsome?"

"Your reward will be delivered through the academy and processed with the Spirit Pagoda. It is not something you complete by shouting at your feet."

Qian lowered his hands with deep disappointment. "Then my complaint is delayed."

Lan asked, "And the Spirit Ascension Platform?"

"You will receive one valid access opportunity. It should be used carefully. Entering the Platform may take time, and the gain depends on what happens inside. It is best not to rush before the next selection round."

Lan nodded, but a private unease moved inside him.

His rings were not normal Spirit Soul-derived rings.

They had appeared with his martial soul and changed after breakthrough. To others, he could explain them as close to hundred-year rings that improved with his second ring, and because hundred-year rings were ordinary enough in the Elite Junior Class, Qian and Liu had not pressed too deeply. But the Spirit Ascension Platform was tied to Spirit Pagoda mechanics. Simulated soul beast energy, ring improvement, Spirit Soul interaction — none of that guaranteed it would treat his self-born rings normally.

Useful reward.

Uncertain reward.

He kept both thoughts hidden.

Ling Yiyi's gaze swept over all three of them. "The prizes will be delivered later. First place across Heaven Luo Planet — and very likely first across the federation qualifier stage — has earned you this opportunity. Use it well."

Qian's chest began to rise.

Liu Feng said quietly, "Don't make a speech."

"I was breathing with dignity."

"Too loudly."

Before Qian could defend dignified breathing, a second projection opened beside Ling Yiyi.

Not pink this time.

Clean white light, edged with Shrek green.

Mu Zhongtian appeared first. Behind him stood Yan Shuo, Song Yichen, and Luo Haoran.

Qian's expression shifted. Liu Feng straightened. Lan's attention sharpened.

Dorm336 had not been brought into the Lucky Wheel space.

Good.

That was Dorm333's champion reward. Stealing it would have made both results cheaper.

But Dorm336 standing here meant something else.

Something that should have happened from the moment second place was announced.

Ling Yiyi turned slightly. "Dorm336's review has also reached a preliminary result."

Song Yichen's face tried to remain serious and failed by three degrees.

Luo Haoran looked as if he had already counted the possible meanings and disliked that several were dangerous.

Yan Shuo stood in front of them with public calm. No wings, no flame, no identity leak. Just the class monitor who had walked out of the qualifier with a second-place score that should not have existed beside canon's old expectations.

Ling Yiyi's voice lost its teasing edge.

"Yan Shuo, Song Yichen, Luo Haoran. Dorm336 placed second on Heaven Luo Planet with nine thousand eight hundred and forty-six points. The result is not being treated as ordinary second place. Your route included Fire Phoenix suppression under dangerous simulated conditions, team survival against fire-dragon-type pressure, and support cooperation from lower-ranked teammates without abandonment. Shrek has opened a special observation file on the team, not only on Yan Shuo individually."

Song's eyes widened.

Luo's hands tightened.

Yan's expression remained controlled, but Lan felt the faint shift in him: not surprise at being noticed, but relief that Song and Luo were not being erased.

Ling continued, "Because first-place and second-place reward structures are not the same, Dorm336 will not enter the champion Lucky Wheel. But second place is not nothing. Heaven Luo Academy, after Shrek review, will provide a special team reward package before the next round."

Qian whispered, "Package. That's a powerful word."

Liu elbowed him lightly.

Ling Yiyi ignored them.

"First: all three Dorm336 members receive a team special-observation mark for this selection period. If you reach the later thresholds, the team route will be reviewed as a whole rather than reducing the result to Yan Shuo alone."

Song's mouth opened.

Yan said, "Thank you."

Two quiet words.

They carried more than Song's half-formed shout would have.

"Second," Ling said, "Heaven Luo Academy will open protected recovery, nutrition, and advanced simulation resources to Dorm336 for the preparation interval. These are academy resources, not Shrek admission promises, but they are not symbolic."

Mu Zhongtian nodded from the projection. "The president approved it personally."

Song's seriousness cracked. "Personally?"

"Personally," Mu said. "So do not waste food, medicine, or simulator hours by showing off."

Song immediately became solemn again. "I am a model of restraint."

Luo murmured, "Historically false."

"Third," Ling said, and three slim cases appeared in the white projection. "Individualized training aids. They are not combat artifacts and cannot be carried as battle equipment. They are calibrated training tools for the routes you already proved."

The first case opened.

Inside lay a narrow dark-red cuff with faint gold measurement lines.

"Yan Shuo. Fire Phoenix output-calibration cuff. It stores no power and grants no new skill. It measures heat layers, wing-root strain, and Ultimate Fire leakage during controlled training. Its purpose is restraint, not attack. A monster who burns everything only proves he has power. A Shrek candidate must prove he can choose how much of that power to release."

Yan looked at the cuff for a long moment.

Lan knew why.

A reward that did not flatter the flame.

A reward that demanded control.

That was more Shrek than praise.

Yan accepted it. "Understood."

The second case opened around a square, heavy-looking training plate marked with impact lines.

"Song Yichen. Shield-load impact matrix. It will record how force moves through your Stoneplate Tortoise defense, where recoil travels, and how your shield position affects teammates behind or above you. Dorm336 survived because your defense had direction, not because you stood there and endured. Train that direction."

Song swallowed hard.

For once, he did not joke.

"Yes."

The third case opened with a set of thin pale-green sensor threads coiled like sleeping grass.

"Luo Haoran. Wind-line reaction threads. They are for sensor-lane and interruption-window training. You saw seams under pressure. You must learn to call them faster, cleaner, and without waiting until Yan Shuo's flame has already committed."

Luo bowed his head. "I understand."

Ling looked at all three. "This is the reward for the team you chose to remain. If you had treated Song and Luo as baggage, this package would not exist. If Song and Luo had hidden behind Yan Shuo, this package would not exist. Second place is not first place. But your second place has weight. Carry it properly."

Silence followed.

It was not empty.

It was the silence of something landing where it should have landed chapters ago in the shape of consequence.

Yan Shuo turned slightly toward Song and Luo.

"We carry it together," he said.

Song nodded too quickly.

Luo nodded once, exactly.

Lan watched from the edge of the pink light, and something in his chest eased.

He had told Yan earlier that Dorm336's route was real.

Now the adults had said it where records could hear.

Qian leaned toward Lan and whispered, "Their package has three cases. We got one wheel. Which is better?"

Lan said, "Ours can change fate. Theirs can change training."

"That did not answer."

"Good. Don't compare too simply."

Liu Feng's gaze rested on the unopened future of his right arm. "Different rewards for different routes."

Ling Yiyi heard him and smiled. "Exactly. Shrek does not only test who is brightest. It tests whether a result matches a road."

Her eyes moved briefly to Lan.

"And whether a road breaks the people walking it."

Yin Tianfan's story was still too fresh inside Lan for the words to feel accidental.

He lowered his eyes.

Qian did not understand that layer, but he understood enough to clap Liu on the shoulder. "Then our road currently contains my improved rings, your terrifying arm bone, and Xuanyu's mysterious Platform door. Excellent. I approve this road."

"You approve because you got rings," Liu said.

"I also approve because you got an arm."

"I had one already."

"A more expensive arm."

Liu's mouth twitched despite himself.

The white projection began to fade. Before it vanished, Yan Shuo looked once toward Lan.

Public space.

Public identity.

No secret words.

Still, the look said enough: neither of them needed the other's road to shrink for their own to matter.

Lan gave the smallest nod.

The projection closed.

Ling Yiyi folded her hands behind her back. "Rewards are not endings. They create pressure. Dorm333, Dorm336 — both of your teams have made Heaven Luo Academy difficult to ignore. The next round will not be kind because of that."

Qian raised his hand carefully. "Sister Ling, if rewards create pressure, may I return some pressure?"

"No."

"I expected that."

Mu Zhongtian's voice entered through the system, tired and proud. "Exit the simulation. Rest. The delivered items and processing schedule will be arranged immediately. Do not cause trouble before the next selection."

Qian looked offended. "Teacher Mu says that as if trouble is usually my fault."

Lan, Liu, and Ling Yiyi all looked at him.

Qian sighed. "Fine. Often. Not usually."

The pink halos faded.

As the simulation released them, Lan kept one thought clear.

First place had opened a wheel.

Second place had opened a different door.

That was how the story should move now: not copying an old path until the world forgot what had changed, but letting every earned result push the next step into a new shape.

When the cabin lid rose and real air touched his face, Lan saw Qian already turning toward him with hungry eyes, Liu sitting up with rare brightness, and across the room Yan Shuo receiving a dark-red training case from Mu Zhongtian while Song and Luo held theirs like proof.

No reward had scattered the teams.

Not yet.

The next test would try harder.
