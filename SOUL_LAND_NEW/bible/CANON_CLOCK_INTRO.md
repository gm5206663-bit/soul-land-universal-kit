## THE WORLD (绝世唐门 era — ~10,000 years after SL1)

- **史莱克学院** — the continent's first academy, at 史莱克城 (east of the plains of 天斗/星罗 border). Two courts: **外院** (the school proper; ~1,000+ students intake a year) and **内院** (elite; entrance standard ~ 20岁前达到6环; legendary). **海神阁** (the supreme council of elders) sits over both; 穆恩 is 海神阁前阁主, 玄子 (玄老) succeeds him; 言少哲 is 武魂系院长 + later 海神阁阁主. Other pillars: 魂导系 (仙琳儿 → 轩梓文), the 监察团.
- **The powers of the era:** 唐门 (fallen, rebuilding — 唐雅 is its young 门主), 本体宗, 日月帝国 (rising, soul-tool powerhouse), the **圣灵教** (邪魂师 cult — 叶夕水 太上教主, **钟离乌** 教主, 98级; the religion of the story's shadow), and the two great empires' academies.
- **The shadow-web (canon):** 叶夕水 (光明凤凰 2nd soul + 血魔傀儡/傀儡 death soul) & 龙逍遥 (黑暗圣龙) are the cult's founding monsters AND 言少哲's ancestors. 穆恩 (光明圣龙) was their brother-friend, then their enemy. 钟离乌 is 叶夕水's son by the previous cult master → **family to 言少哲** (he addresses him as 二爷 in canon). This is the bloodline Tianyu inherits — light AND shadow, both supreme, both his.
