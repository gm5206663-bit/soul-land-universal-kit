## YEAR -1 / 0 — CONCEPTION & THE WOMB (the load-bearing mystery)

- **Who:** 言昭 (father, [AU] 言少哲 & 蔡媚儿's only son) and 苏凝 (mother, [AU] a gentle healer-type soul master from outside Shrek). 言昭 carries the family 光明凤凰 (strong, not ultimate). 苏凝 is not a genius — she is warm, and she is the one who made the impossible child possible. [provisional: their meeting story — see Decisions]
- **Two ultimate souls in one body should kill the fetus.** Ultimate Light/Fire (phoenix) and Ultimate Darkness (dragon) are antipodes; a womb carrying both would normally tear apart from the first weeks. [AU-core] The child is instead **flawless** — because the **Adaptation Talent** (the mother's gift through her line [provisional], active from his first moment of existence) is already refining him in the womb: organs, symmetry, energy pathways, and — the miracle — *compatibility between the two hostile bloodlines*. [User's own system, §32 prenatal; user-confirmed: it is strongest as a forming force here.]
- **The pregnancy is still dangerous** (two suns trying to share one sky) — 苏凝 is sick through much of it; the family healers and 蔡媚儿's quiet care carry her. [provisional: whether 穆恩 ever quietly touches the unborn child to stabilize him — recommend yes, one scene only, framed as the old dragon sensing "a familiar shadow" in the light. This is the seed of Mu En's bond.]
- **[AU] Nobody outside the family + Mu En knows the child will be double-souled.** 言少哲 senses *something* (his own phoenix "weeps and sings at once" near the baby). Mu En, who knew 龙逍遥 (the black dragon's grandfather) as a brother-rival, recognizes the truth the day the child is born and says nothing to anyone but the parents.

## AGE 0–2 — INFANT

- **Born perfect** — the womb talent's visible result: full, healthy, startlingly symmetrical; big bright eyes; warm to the touch (light) but runs *cold at night* (dark) — the first quiet clue only the family reads. [AU body detail]
- **Appetite from birth:** he nurses/eats like a bird with a furnace — the family's old wet-nurse story is "this child eats for a grown man." (See DIET doc — the appetite is the visible cost of two souls idling.)
- **Father's grief-seed:** 言昭 loves the boy utterly and is the one who first sees the dark flicker in his son's eyes one night and chooses — like Mu En — to love it into a cage rather than fear it.
- **Mu En's first touch:** the ancient dragon visits the infant (rare) and simply holds him; the boy stops crying. 言少哲 understands: Mu En has claimed this child as his own responsibility.

## AGE 3–5 — THE HOLE IN THE HOUSE

- **[AU-core] Age ~4: 言昭 dies at a border beast-tide** — publicly a hero's death holding the line. [provisional truth: the tide was *directed* — a probe by forces who had begun to suspect the Dragon line had produced an heir. 言昭 died closing a gap he was told was safe, buying the secret with his life. Sealed. Only 言少哲, 蔡媚儿, Mu En know.]
- The boy does not understand death; he understands that his father stopped coming home and his mother began to fade.
- **Age ~5: 苏凝 leaves Shrek** — [AU] not death, but absence: she cannot raise him inside the walls where his father is not, and the elders fear her grief is carving him hollow. She returns to her own quiet home/clan, writes letters, visits rarely. She knows **nothing** of the dragon (kept from her to protect her — she is not a fighter and the truth would put her in the cult's crosshairs). [provisional: her soul/family/where she lives → Decisions]
- The boy is raised by 言少哲 (stern, brilliant, terrified of failing his son's son) and 蔡媚儿 (the warmth, the one who never lets him forget he is loved, the one who feeds him).
- **First memory-scars:** he learns early that adults around him carry silences. He learns to read the shape of a kept secret before he learns what a secret is. [This is why he will later be so good at hiding — he was raised inside a house of hidden love.]

## AGE 6 — THE AWAKENING (the hinge)

- **Shrek's own ceremony.** [AU detail: the dean's household conducts it privately first, then publicly.] The world sees: **光明凤凰, 先天满魂力 (rank 10)** — ultimate light and fire blazing clean. The heir has inherited; the academy rejoices. [If user wants veiled numbers, replace — see Decisions.]
- **What only four people see:** beneath the light, in the moment of manifestation, a **shadow the shape of a dragon** passes across the boy's pupils. Mu En, 言少哲, 蔡媚儿 see it. The boy feels it as a *cold swallow* behind his warmth and is afraid, briefly, then the light floods back.
- **Mu En claims him openly** — the 龙神斗罗, who has not taken a direct hand in decades, announces he will teach the boy himself. The academy is stunned and thrilled; they read it as the old dragon honoring the dean's line. (It is also Mu En completing his old friend 龙逍遥's bloodline — a debt of a hundred years.)
- **The talent awakens/evolves at awakening** (user's rule): his adaptation, which was a forming force in the womb, now becomes a *living* force — his body begins to "remember" what it meets. The first visible sign: he watches a senior's drill once and can copy the footwork imperfectly but *instantly*. Nobody but Mu En understands what this means.
- **Does the boy learn the truth at 6?** [provisional recommendation: NO — see Age 9. He knows only that he felt something cold, and that Mu En told him, gently, "There is a second room in your house, child. You are not to open the door yet. When you are ready, I will be there." This is the promise the whole childhood hangs on.]

## AGE 6–7 — YEAR ONE UNDER 穆恩

- **Daily rhythm:** dawn with Mu En (before the academy wakes) — breathing, energy circulation, the "empty furnace" method [provisional name] where he learns to settle both flows without letting either flare. Morning with grandmother. Day at the academy's junior study (he is a child of Shrek; he attends like other inner-court children, but his private lessons are Mu En's).
- **Mu En's teaching is not gentle and not cruel.** The old dragon teaches like he forged steel: every lesson has a why, and the why is always "because one day you will need this and I will not be here." The boy does not yet know how literal that is.
- **First ring hunt (age 6–7):** with 言少哲 (Mu En watching from the sky). A **赤羽阳雀** (Red-Plumed Sun-Sparrow) of the Star Dou fringe, **1,300 years (purple)** — a thousand-year first ring, survivable only through ultimate-soul compatibility, the womb-perfected body, and 护法. The boy feels the ring settle and his phoenix *laugh* — first soul skill 「燎羽」 (light-fire feather blade-wall + short launch) forms. After the kill he eats an entire beast's worth of roasted meat and 蔡媚儿 laughs and cries at once. Innate 先天满魂力 = 10, so he stood already at the first-ring threshold the day he awakened.
- **The cold visits at night:** sometimes, asleep, he dreams of a huge dark thing under still water. He tells Mu En. Mu En says: "Good. It knows you are not afraid of it. That is the first lesson."

## AGE 8–9 — THE SECOND RING & THE FIRST SURGE (the childhood's wound)

- **Second ring hunt (age 8–9):** deeper into the Star Dou, a **金瞳耀鸾** (Gold-Eyed Blaze Luan), **3,200 years (purple)**. Second soul skill 「贯日」 (piercing light-lance through shadow and armor) forms. The boy, ~rank 23–25, is proud, growing fast.
- **THE FIRST SURGE — age ~9 (the childhood's defining scene):** on the hunt's return, a stronger beast ambushes them; the grandfather is engaged; the boy — furious and afraid for the old man — feels the cold swallow *rise*. For a few seconds the shadows bend around him and a dragon's weight presses the air. The beast recoils. 言少哲's blood runs cold. Mu En arrives, and only Mu En is calm.
- **That night Mu En tells him the truth** — not the full genealogy (that is later), but the essential: he carries a second soul, 黑暗圣龙, the blood of a man Mu En once called brother. It is not evil. It is a fire of a different color. It will try to answer his anger and his fear, and his whole life's first discipline is: **the light leads. The dark waits. You are the one who chooses which one you feed.**
- **Mu En begins the hidden curriculum:** not to use the dark, but to *meet* it without fear — control exercises done in sealed places, so the boy learns to cage it with his own will, not with seals that could break. (By 11 he has never once let it fully out; he has learned the shape of the cage and respects it.)
- **The cover story** for the near-disaster on the hunt: the grandfather's protection + Mu En's arrival. Two more people quietly learn the truth as arcs demand [provisional: 玄老 by age ~10 — see Relationships].

## AGE 9–11 — THE QUIET YEARS (growth, grief, and goodbye-weather)

- **Rank climbs steadily → 30 at the gate (age 11)** (see STATUS for the curve; rank ~23–25 after the 2nd ring). Two purple thousand-year rings. The boy is unmistakably the strongest of his age in Shrek's living memory, and handles it with the eerie calm of someone who knows what "strong" is *for*.
- **Mu En's fading becomes visible.** The ancient dragon tires; the golden tree's pull grows. Mu En gives the boy the last things he can: the discipline, the name of the balance, and one rule that will outlive him — **"When you cannot hold both, hold the light. The light is the part of you that is yours."**
- **Growing up around Shrek's people:** 马小桃 (fiery big-sister, ~17–19 now — she is ~19 at the intake year; from ~age 9 he discovers his light steadies her 邪火 and they build a quiet ritual — she never tells anyone, and neither does he), 玄老 (the voracious elder who bonds over food and calls him 小饭桶), 贝贝 (the gentle older cousin, Mu En's descendant) **and through 贝贝, 唐雅** — the 唐门门主 who teases him about his appetite and tries to recruit him into her sect. Around 贝贝+唐雅 he watches two people choose each other against the world, and it plants the shape of what he wants someday.
- **The soul-tool gift begins to surface (~9–10, [AU]):** watching the 魂导系 workshops, he grasps core-lines and schematics almost on sight — his spiritual power, precision and dual-vision make 魂导器 work feel like a language he was born fluent in. 蔡媚儿 and 言少哲 notice; it is quietly filed as a talent to develop (never pushed at the cost of his martial path). [Anchor of the gift: see STATUS §7.2 — decide per arc.]
- **The star is felt:** to the old generation he already carries the shape of the 黑白双龙 legend — light and shadow in one boy again. The academy calls him 小明凤 and a golden-grandson; the elders who know the older story see something heavier and say nothing. Wherever he goes, people brighten, trust him, remember him — a presence that is felt before it is understood. (His beauty, warmth and gravity are part of it; see STATUS §8–§9.)
- **The public face hardens into armor:** kind, disciplined, brilliant, hungry, modest. The mask is not false — it is *real*, trimmed. [This is the SL1 lesson applied correctly: the mask is not paint over emptiness.]
- **Age ~10-11:** 苏凝's rare visits become the boy's most treasured days. He does not tell her about the cold. He tells her everything else.
- **The last pre-intake scene (the chapter-1 door):** Mu En, very tired, tells the boy: "Soon you will walk through the gate as a student like the others, and I will watch from where the old ones watch. Do not be the strongest in the room. Be the one who knows what the room is for." The boy does not yet know this is nearly a goodbye. [穆恩's death/merging with the Golden Tree is a canon anchor that lands during the main story — the boy is ~13-15 by then; pre-11 is safe.]

## THE PILLARS OF THE PRE-11 BOY (what he arrives with)

1. **Flawless body, furnace appetite** — built by the womb talent, fed by two souls, costing him hunger. (See STATUS §2.2, §9 — the appetite is the visible bill of the densest foundation alive.)
2. **A mastered public self** — 光明凤凰, 先天满魂力, rank **30 at the third-ring gate**, two purple rings, the dean's golden grandson, Mu En's private student. Everyone knows who he is; no one knows what he is.
3. **A caged second self** — 黑暗圣龙, known to him since 9, never released, trained in secret by Mu En. Fear of it is his oldest fear; control of it is his oldest pride.
4. **A house of silences** — father's death (sealed truth), mother's absence (sealed reason), the family genealogy (sealed, deeper than he knows: the 圣灵教 is his family).
5. **Mu En's last student** — the old dragon's final project, taught to hold the balance of light and dark that the old dragon himself spent a lifetime failing with 龙逍遥 and 叶夕水.
6. **A star, earned** — the era's second light: whole-existence adaptation makes him and the world grow *into* each other; wherever he goes the field bends (马小桃 steadier, 唐雅 trusted, 贝贝 given a little brother). Not luck — cause. (STATUS §13.)
