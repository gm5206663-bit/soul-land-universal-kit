# CANON NOTES — receipts and confidence

Every claim this project rests on, with its source and its confidence. **No untagged canon
assertions in any file.**

**Tags:**

- **[canon]** — verified against source material or multiple independent secondary sources
- **[fan]** — widely repeated, plausible, not verified against primary text
- **[disputed]** — sources disagree; record which reading you chose and never switch silently
- **[design]** — our invention on top of canon; labelled as ours, never as canon

## 5. RESEARCH LAW

1. Prefer the donghua / novel text over any wiki.
2. Two independent secondary sources minimum for anything plot-bearing.
3. Record the receipt: source, URL or path, date checked, confidence.
4. Record negatives too.
5. Tag every claim.

**When you cannot verify, say so in plain words.** An honest `[fan — unverified]` costs
nothing. A confident invention costs a rebuild, and the reader finds it anyway.
