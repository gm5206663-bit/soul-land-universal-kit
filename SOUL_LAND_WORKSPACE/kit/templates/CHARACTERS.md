# characters.md — Template

Copy to `codex/CHARACTERS.md`.

One section per character who is named, or who matters unnamed. A character who appears once
and never again does not need a section — but a character who appears twice does, because the
second appearance is where contradictions start.

---

## Rule

Every claim in a dossier must be either `[canon]`, `[design]`, or `[on page]`. The last means
it happened in this story, with a chapter reference. A dossier is how continuity is kept, and
a dossier that blurs invention with canon is worse than no dossier.

---

## [Name]

**Role in the story:** one line. What function do they serve?

**Identity:** what they are. Species, rank, rings, age. State the number or say it is unknown.

| Attribute | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Rank | | |
| Rings | | |
| Age | | |
| First appears | Chapter N | [on page] |

**What they want:** one sentence. A character without a want is scenery.

**What they know / do not know:** link to the firewalls file. This is where leaks are caught.

**Voice:** how they speak. Short sentences? Formal? Do they use names? Two or three example
lines beat any description.

**Arc:** where they start, where they end, what changes them.

**Absolutes:** things this character will never do. These are the rails that keep a long
serial consistent, and they are the first thing to violate by accident.
