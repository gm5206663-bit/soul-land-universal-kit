# Chapter 49 — No Pattern Underfoot

The pink doorway did not lead to a room.

It led to a place made of light and uncertainty.

Pink halos drifted from every direction. Above Dorm333 was boundless space; beneath their feet spun an enormous wheel of colors so fast the eye could not separate one shade from the next. It was beautiful in the way Shrek's tests liked to be beautiful — enough to make a child reach for it before thinking.

Lan Xuanyu did not reach.

Neither did Liu Feng.

Qian Lei almost did, but Lan's hand rose first.

"Don't stop."

Qian froze with one foot half-lifted. "I was only appreciating reward culture."

"Appreciate while moving."

Ling Yiyi's voice came through the light. "You may move around the Lucky Wheel and choose your own spot. Once you stop, you may not move again. When the wheel resolves, your reward appears. Only one person may occupy each spot."

Lan asked, "Can all three of us choose the same spot?"

"No."

"Do the colors correspond to reward types?"

"I do not know," Ling answered lightly. "It is called a Lucky Wheel. It depends on luck."

Qian whispered, "Luck is the most unreasonable teacher."

"Then do not offend it," Liu said.

They walked.

Lan released his Spiritual Power carefully. Spirit Sea perception spread through the wheel's strange flow. He still could not see the rewards beneath the light, but he could sense differences: a fresh sweet aura, a fierce ominous pressure, a resistant feeling, blank spaces with nothing at all. The auras rotated, slid, and overlapped too quickly for a stable pattern.

A trap for anyone who thought one observation made a rule.

Lan counted several breaths, then stopped trying to force certainty where none existed.

"Qian, choose first."

"So I am the offering."

"You are the first chance."

"That sounds better. I accept."

Qian stopped.

Purple light rose beneath him.

A pleasant voice announced, "Increased cultivation age of all current Soul Rings by five hundred years."

Qian's mouth opened.

No sound came.

That was how Lan knew the reward was excellent.

Five hundred years to all current rings would not magically make Qian reliable, but it would strengthen the Gate, improve summoning duration and possibly quality, and reduce some of the weakness that had always left him exposed between miracles.

"Liu," Lan said.

Liu Feng stopped on his mark.

Silver light erupted.

"Spirit Pagoda-made two-thousand-year right arm Soul Bone, with one Soul Skill."

This time all three stared.

A Soul Bone was not a normal prize. Even young students knew the six main categories — head, torso, left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg — and knew that a Soul Bone strengthened the merged body part while adding an extra skill. Ancient wild Soul Bones were rare enough to become inheritance treasures. Modern Spirit Pagoda technology had made man-made Soul Bones possible, not common.

Liu Feng's face lit with disbelief, then tightened because he remembered he could not move after stopping.

Qian finally found his voice. "I feel generous about going first now."

Lan kept walking.

Qian's fresh aura had not landed from where he expected. Liu's ominous aura had shifted faster than the first clue suggested. No pattern.

No safe trick.

Lan stopped when his perception found a quiet blank, not because it promised anything, but because hesitation had become no better than choice.

The wheel pulled at him.

An aura from far away rushed in.

"Access to the Elementary Spirit Ascension Platform once."

The pink world folded.

They returned beside Ling Yiyi.

Qian immediately looked at her. "Sister Ling, when does my reward happen?"

"Your reward will be delivered to your school and handled through the Spirit Pagoda. Lan Xuanyu, you and Qian Lei will both need Spirit Pagoda arrangements. I suggest waiting until after the next qualifier before using the Spirit Ascension Platform. It may take a long time; ending hastily would waste the opportunity."

Lan nodded.

He did not show the unease that moved inside him.

The Spirit Ascension Platform improved Soul Rings and Soul Bones through simulated soul-beast energy. But Lan's rings were natural/self-born: they had appeared from his own martial soul and bloodline, not from a normal Spirit Soul. To others, the safe explanation remained that his rings had been near hundred-year quality and changed after his second ring. It had been enough so far.

The Platform might not be so easily fooled.

Still, a careful opportunity was not a mistake.

A careless use would be.


Mu Zhongtian explained more after they left the pods.

He was delighted, envious, and trying very hard not to look either.

"Qian Lei's reward is very suitable," he said. "All current Soul Rings increasing by five hundred years can affect his summoning foundation. Liu Feng's Soul Bone is even more valuable. As for Xuanyu's Spirit Ascension Platform access, it may not look as direct as a Soul Bone, but used well, its gains can exceed a fixed increase."

He described the Platform without decoration: emulated soul beasts, killing and absorbing energy, opportunity ending when eliminated or exhausted, rare slots, astronomical value. Man-made Soul Rings and Soul Bones could both be upgraded there, which was why the Platform mattered so much to modern Soul Masters.

Then he brought them to Yin Tianfan and Ji Hongbin.

Yin's first reaction to Liu's Soul Bone was not greed.

It was team calculation.

"We check suitability," Yin said. "A reward drawn by one person may strengthen the team more in another person's body. Any objection?"

Liu Feng was startled only for a moment.

Then he shook his head. "No objection. We earned first place together. Whoever fits it best should use it."

The room quieted.

Qian Lei's earlier refusal of early admission hung there without anyone needing to name it. Compared with giving up Shrek certainty for a team, even a Soul Bone became part of a larger rule.

Ji Hongbin's sternness softened. "Good children."

Qian looked deeply moved. "Teacher Ji, if my five hundred years also needs team calculation, I may become less good."

"It does not," Ji said.

"Then I remain excellent."

Yin ignored him and looked at the adults. "If they enter the top ten in the next selection, Heaven Luo Academy cannot be stingy. We already have one Platform opportunity. I will speak to the president. Even if I go broke, we try to get two more slots so all three can enter."

Ji nodded. "They earned first across the federation. Heaven Luo should invest."

Mu's eyes brightened at the word invest.

This was another kind of reward: not a wheel, not a prize announcement, but adults deciding that the path was worth resources.

It did not belong only to Dorm333.

The academy's food and recovery support had already been expanded to all six qualifying teams. Dorm336's second place changed schedules, too. No pink door opened for them. No Shrek official handed them mysterious items. Instead, Mu and the other teachers called Yan Shuo, Song Yichen, and Luo Haoran for a practical report; medical checks were repeated; formation mistakes were written down; training rooms were assigned with more seriousness than before. Song did not become more important because a teacher gave a speech. He became more important because Teacher Mu asked exactly where his shield line had held. Luo did not become real because of a reward object. He became real because the teachers asked him to draw the route he had seen through heat and impact. Yan did not need another public display to prove he was monstrous. The academy already had his scan, his rings, his score, and his decision not to abandon his team.

That was enough.

Natural consequence did not always announce itself.

Sometimes it simply changed who was called into the room.


The physical Soul Bone arrived that afternoon.

It rested above Yin's table inside a faint containment field: a small silver right-arm shape, light folded within it like moonlight under ice. Its aura was restrained, but when Lan leaned closer, his skin prickled with a cutting ache.

A short introduction identified it.

Silver Moon Wolf Right Arm Bone.

Spirit Pagoda-made, refined through special adjustment, around two thousand years.

Soul Skill: Silver Edge.

It could be released through the right arm or conducted into an object; through a weapon it would form a three-inch silver crescent blade and consume Soul Power.

Yin smiled. "What is yours cannot run away after all. Liu Feng."

Liu stepped forward.

There was no real debate now. Right arm. Weapon conduction. Attack supplement. White Dragon Spear. Liu's greatest weakness had always been that his speed reached places his attack power could not finish without Lan's support. This bone fit too cleanly to refuse.

Ji Hongbin instructed him. "Focus. Release your Martial Soul. Inject Soul Power into the Soul Bone. Follow the sensation. It will hurt. Endure it. I will protect you, but I cannot fuse it for you."

Liu sat cross-legged.

White Dragon Spear appeared across his knees.

When his Soul Power touched the bone, silver light exploded into his palm.

Pain hit so fast that his groan escaped before he could stop it. The Silver Moon Wolf Right Arm Bone dissolved into brilliant light and drilled into his arm. His sleeve tore. His right arm swelled to adult size, silver rays bouncing and cutting under the skin. Sweat gathered on his forehead. His whole body trembled.

Qian's face went pale.

Lan stepped forward, stopped, and looked to Ji.

"Teacher, can I help him? My Martial Soul can guide him."

Ji's eyes moved once between Liu's arm and Lan's hand.

"Careful. Withdraw if the backlash is too strong."

Golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass wrapped Liu's right arm.

The cutting sensation came back through the grass at once. Lan's breath caught. He activated the blood-vital energy in his body, and the golden patterns brightened into a faint halo. The silver violence weakened slightly.

The White Dragon Spear buzzed.

Not a weapon's vibration.

A joyful, dragon-like sound.

White light rose from the spear and circled Liu's right arm like a small dragon leaning toward Lan's golden-patterned grass. Gold, silver, and white moved together. Liu's trembling eased. The silver rays became gentler.

Ji Hongbin watched with narrowed eyes.

Lan was only Rank20.

Two rings.

Yet his support could suppress and harmonize a two-thousand-year Soul Bone enough for Liu's Martial Soul to answer. The mystery of Lan's mutation did not shrink. It grew another branch.

Half an hour later, Liu's swollen arm returned to normal.

Silver crescent patterns remained on the skin of his right arm.

When he opened his eyes, a flash of silver crossed them, and his aura sharpened like a spear point under moonlight.

Ji checked him and nodded. "Rank29. Almost Rank30."

Qian nearly jumped. "Your arm came with levels."

Liu was exhausted, but he smiled. "Your mouth came without restraint."

"My restraint is hidden potential."

Lan looked at Liu's arm, then at his own hands.

Rank29.

Qian's rings would improve.

Yan was already Rank39 and SP962.

Lan remained Rank20.

The frustration was small, sharp, and honest.

He did not let it become envy that hurt the team.

Liu saw him anyway.

"Thank you," Liu said.

Lan looked up.

Two simple words pulled him back to the correct place.

Ji said, "Wash up and familiarize with the Soul Bone. Silver Edge is a trump card, not a toy. Do not show it casually before the next qualifier."

Liu nodded. "Yes."

Qian was taken to Heaven Luo City's Spirit Pagoda division for proper reward processing. He complained during the waiting, stopped complaining during the procedure, and emerged staring at his feet as if they were expected to sing.

His rings did not turn purple.

No exact new ages were announced for him to boast about.

But the yellow deepened, and the Gate of Summoning felt less sluggish when he touched it afterward.

"Still yellow," he said mournfully.

Mu Zhongtian replied, "Stronger yellow. Be grateful."

"I am grateful with disappointment."

Lan spent the remaining time stabilizing his Spirit Sea. Liu learned the feel of the fused right arm without releasing Silver Edge for show. Qian tested the Gate under supervision and discovered improvement was real but not miraculous. Across the training wing, Dorm336 went through recovery checks and report sessions, then returned to formation drills with a seriousness that made Song complain less and Luo speak earlier.

Two days passed quickly.

When the six shortlisted Heaven Luo teams gathered again, the hall no longer felt like it had before the first qualifier.

Dorm333 stood with quiet rewards: Qian's deepened yellow rings, Liu's covered silver right arm, Lan's unspent Platform access and new Spirit Sea.

Dorm336 stood with no flashy prize in hand, but with the weight of second place visible in the way teachers looked at all three of them. Yan Shuo's public presence remained calm. Song and Luo stood straighter because their score was no longer something anyone could call Yan's alone.

Lu Qianxun's team stood behind Dorm333 without being told.

Jin Xiang did not speak loudly.

Everyone remembered the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragons.

Everyone had begun to understand that twenty thousand points meant Dorm333 had done something no ordinary explanation covered.

Mu Zhongtian ordered them toward the pods.

The next round was waiting.
