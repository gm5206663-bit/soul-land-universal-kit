# Chapter 24: Twenty Minutes

Lan Xuanyu stayed.

The rest of the class did not leave all at once. Children rarely obeyed silence cleanly after a result like that. They moved in clusters, looking back over their shoulders, whispering even after Ji Hongbin's warning had cut the loudest questions out of the air.

Dorm 333 had taken first place in actual combat.

The vice president had already raised one hand and told everyone that the full results would be announced tomorrow, and that they could return to rest and cultivate. In the original clean shape of a class, that order should have been enough.

This class no longer had a clean shape.

Some eyes went to Lu Qianxun because his Golden Lion King team had been expected to challenge for champion. Some eyes went to Yan Shuo because the midterm sheet had made Dorm 336 the class monitor's team, the public strongest-overall route. Dorm 333's first place did not merely surprise them; it struck both expectations at once and left the students with no simple ranking to hide behind.

That sentence still did not fit the room.

It did not fit Liu Feng's usual place in the class. It did not fit Qian Lei's unreliable Gate of Summoning. It did not fit the way Jin Xiang stood with clenched fists and a lowered head, burning with anger he did not dare aim at Teacher Ji again. It did not fit the way Ye Lingtong avoided Lan Xuanyu's eyes, nor the way Chang Jianyi kept rubbing a shoulder that no longer carried a wound.

Most of all, it did not fit Lu Qianxun's silence.

Lu Qianxun had come out of the simulation pod without complaining. That alone was enough to unsettle half the students who knew him. He stood with his golden hair returned to its ordinary color and his face held carefully still, but his gaze remained fixed on Lan Xuanyu.

Heat lived in that gaze.

Fear lived underneath the heat.

Inside the Maze, Lu Qianxun had seen a girl appear through a Gate that should not have been able to call her. He had felt his Golden Lion Roar answered by something that did not sound like a boy's throat. He had felt the Golden Lion King inside him recoil as if its meridians had been struck by a command older than pride.

He did not understand it.

That made it worse.

Lan Xuanyu stood near Mu Zhongtian with a pale face and a tired expression, not looking like someone who had done anything impossible. Liu Feng and Qian Lei lingered beside him until Ji Hongbin's eyes moved in their direction.

"Go," Ji Hongbin said.

Qian Lei opened his mouth, then closed it.

Liu Feng gave Lan Xuanyu a look that asked three questions at once and received only a small nod in answer. At last, Dorm 333's other two members followed the flow of students toward the door.

Yan Shuo watched from the side of Dorm 336.

He had heard the public result. He had seen Lan Xuanyu being kept behind. He had also seen Teacher Mu's hand remain close enough to steady Lan Xuanyu if the boy swayed.

Mu Zhongtian's gaze found him through the moving students.

"Yan Shuo," Teacher Mu said, voice low enough not to turn it into another announcement, "take anyone injured to the infirmary first. No crowding around Dorm 333. Detailed rankings wait until tomorrow."

"Yes, Teacher."

The words landed with weight because the class had already accepted that the monitor's job was not decoration. Two boys who had begun edging toward Qian Lei stopped when Yan moved. A girl who had been about to ask Liu Feng a question swallowed it and followed her own team instead.

That was a butterfly too: not a miracle, not a stolen secret, only a public responsibility changing how noise moved through a room.

Concern tugged at him.

So did habit. Lan Xuanyu had been his friend before the academy had learned to measure them with scores, plus signs, ranks, and results. It would have been easy to step closer and ask whether he was all right.

Easy did not mean correct.

Teacher Ji had asked Lan Xuanyu to stay. The vice president waited with the expression of someone who had already chosen his next question. Whatever door had opened after the Maze, it was not a public dormitory door.

Yan caught Lan Xuanyu's eye for one brief moment.

He did not ask.

He only gave the smallest nod.

Lan Xuanyu's tired face softened for a heartbeat, as if he understood the question that had not been spoken and appreciated that it had stayed unspoken.

Then Song Yichen shifted beside Yan and hissed under his breath when the motion pulled at his shoulder.

Yan turned at once.

"Still not that bad?" he asked.

Song's expression became stubborn. "It is a shoulder. I have another one."

Luo Haoran snorted and nearly stumbled as his sore ankle reminded him that mockery required balance.

Yan looked at both of them.

"Infirmary first. Then food. Then review."

"Review?" Luo said. "We survived an underground Maze and you want homework?"

"That is why we review while we still remember which mistake almost ended us." Yan glanced once more toward Lan Xuanyu, then turned away with Dorm 336. "Come on."

They left with the others.

Behind them, Lan Xuanyu remained in the bright room with the teachers and the secret of a roar he did not understand.

---

The vice president walked toward Ji Hongbin with his hands clasped behind his back and a smile on his round face.

"Not convinced?"

Ji Hongbin's expression did not change.

Lan Xuanyu was not sure whether the question was for Teacher Ji, for the class that had just left, or for the Maze itself. He stood quietly and tried not to think too hard about his own body. The strange warmth in his meridians had not entirely faded. His chest still ached faintly, and the little vortex inside him seemed to turn with a rhythm that felt clearer than before.

He wanted very badly to return to the dormitory and test his soul power.

He also wanted to lie down.

He did not get either wish.

"Come with me," the vice president said.

Lan Xuanyu followed at once.

Ji Hongbin followed too.

The vice president stopped after two steps and looked back at him. "Why are you following us?"

"To supervise," Ji Hongbin replied flatly. "In case you corrupt the child."

The vice president's smile disappeared. "Old Ji, is there something wrong with you?"

Ji Hongbin's mouth moved in something that could not properly be called a smile. "Are you still planning to groom another devil incarnate like yourself?"

Lan Xuanyu's tired mind woke up a little.

Another?

Like himself?

The vice president's face darkened, then settled again with visible effort. He gave a cold snort and strode away without answering.

That was even stranger than if he had shouted.

Lan Xuanyu looked between the two adults. Teacher Ji was the Demon King of Heaven Luo Academy, the one whose lessons could make an entire class forget how to breathe. Yet he spoke to the vice president not like a subordinate, nor like someone afraid of authority, but like a man who had known an old wound for many years.

The vice president led them into the lift.

No one spoke while it rose.

Lan Xuanyu watched the floor number climb and tried to stand straight. His legs felt steady enough, but the earlier battle had left a hollow tremor beneath his skin. Every time he remembered Lu Qianxun's Golden Lion Roar, his own throat seemed to remember answering it.

He did not know how he had done that.

He was not sure he wanted to know while standing between these two teachers.

The lift reached the top of the main teaching block.

The office beyond it was larger than Lan Xuanyu expected. It was not merely a place to sign documents or scold students. Sofas sat in one area, a wide desk in another, and bookshelves covered a long wall. Real printed books stood behind glass, their spines neat and old-fashioned under soft light. In an era when Soul Guidance devices could store more text than a person could read in a lifetime, paper books felt almost like relics from a museum.

There were plants too, carefully kept and expensive-looking, their leaves glossy against the clean walls.

The vice president had the office of someone important.

He also had the expression of someone annoyed that Ji Hongbin was still there.

"Are you going to keep standing there and listen to us?" he asked.

Ji Hongbin walked straight to the sofa and sat down.

He did not answer.

The vice president placed both hands on his waist. For a moment, Lan Xuanyu thought the two adults might actually start fighting in the office. Then the vice president inhaled, pressed the anger down, and waved toward him.

"Come here, chap. Stand in front of me."

Lan Xuanyu obeyed.

Up close, the vice president looked even less like the terrifying figure Teacher Ji's tone implied. He was short compared with many adult soul masters, round in body, and his little eyes carried a smiling brightness that made him seem harmless if one had not just heard Ji Hongbin call him dangerous in three different ways.

"Tell me," the vice president said, "how did you think through your tactics today?"

Lan Xuanyu blinked.

He had expected questions about the roar. Or the Gate. Or why his body had collapsed. Tactics were easier.

"I did not think of anything very complicated," he said honestly. "We did not know the Maze, and no one knew when they would meet another team. Since it was a test, the teachers would definitely make us meet sooner or later. Maybe they would even make it easier for teams to find one another. So instead of running everywhere and spending strength first, we waited. If other teams fought each other before they met us, our chance would be better."

The vice president nodded, pleased.

"Correct tactics. You used your head. Not bad." His smile deepened. "Most children who believe they are strong want to prove they are strong. Lu Qianxun's team did. The monitor's team chose a steadier route, but even they still had to keep moving and solve what the Maze gave them. You had the weakest-looking team on paper, yet you dared to wait for everyone else to spend strength first. That is not luck."

Lan Xuanyu lowered his eyes, embarrassed by praise and unsure whether waiting behind rubble counted as something adults should praise.

The vice president's eyes narrowed with interest. "Your martial soul can amplify Qian Lei and Liu Feng?"

"It seems so," Lan Xuanyu said. "But Teacher, I do not really know what that roar was at the end. I only felt suppressed by Lu Qianxun's lion roar. It was very uncomfortable. Then I called out."

The office became quieter.

Ji Hongbin still sat expressionless on the sofa, but Lan Xuanyu felt that he was listening closely.

The vice president studied Lan Xuanyu's face, then gave a slow nod.

"A natural reaction from your meridians. They were upset at being suppressed." He smiled again, but this time the smile was sharper than before. "That means your meridians should be even better than Lu Qianxun's. Craftiness and strength are both important. Strength can be nurtured later, but perception is very hard to train. I like your perceptiveness. Do you want to be my student?"

"Ah?"

Lan Xuanyu stared.

He was young, but he understood the word student in that tone did not mean an ordinary class. A class teacher taught many children. A master was different. A master meant inheritance, responsibility, and care. Even with Soul Guidance technology changing almost everything in daily life, soul masters still remembered the old saying: one who taught you for a day could be like a father for life.

He turned instinctively toward Ji Hongbin.

Teacher Ji sat as if carved from stone.

Lan Xuanyu hesitated. "But I... I already have a master."

The vice president raised his brows and glanced toward Ji Hongbin. "You already have a master teaching you soul skills, ancient martial arts, and battle skills?"

Ji Hongbin did not move.

Lan Xuanyu's mind flashed to a silver-haired figure, quiet eyes, and patient instruction that never felt like ordinary school teaching. He did not explain. That promise and that relationship were not something to spill in front of a teacher whose name he did not even know.

The vice president seemed to misunderstand the silence, then waved one hand.

"Never mind. It is fine if you have a master. I was not planning to teach you martial-soul cultivation anyway." His smile widened. "Did you not apply for warship piloting as your secondary profession? I can teach you that. I will be your master in that aspect."

Lan Xuanyu's eyes lit at once.

Fatigue, chest ache, Maze fear, all of it lost ground before the word warship.

"You know how to pilot a warship?"

The vice president's round face gained a touch of pride. "You do not know this yet. Warship piloting is never a one-man show. Inside a warship, many people have different duties. But there must be one person who gives orders and coordinates the whole warship, or even an entire fleet. I can teach you that knowledge. I can teach you how to move freely through the universe. Are you interested?"

"Yes!" Lan Xuanyu nodded so fast that his dizziness almost returned. "I have liked warships since I was little. Master, please teach me."

The vice president looked toward Ji Hongbin with open triumph.

"Formally acknowledge me as your master, then."

Lan Xuanyu followed his gaze.

This time, Ji Hongbin did not stay completely still. He gave the faintest nod.

Trust settled into Lan Xuanyu's chest. Teacher Ji might scold people half to death, but he would not casually nod him toward harm. Lan Xuanyu took one step back, straightened with the seriousness a child could manage, and bowed respectfully.

"Master, please take me as your disciple."

The vice president laughed and supported him up with both hands.

"Good, good, good. I finally found myself a successor. From now on, you are my pupil." His little eyes brightened. "This teacher's name is Yin Tianfan. It is enough for you to know it. When you are outside, do not let anyone else know that you are my disciple. This will be our little secret, all right?"

Lan Xuanyu was startled.

A new master was already a very large matter.

A secret new master was larger and stranger.

From the sofa, Ji Hongbin said faintly, "Right. Never mention his name outside. Otherwise..."

"No one will treat you as mute if you keep quiet!" Yin Tianfan snapped.

Ji Hongbin stood, smoothing his clothes with calm dignity that only made Yin Tianfan look angrier. "It seems you have changed somewhat. At least you know how to take care of your own pupil. I will leave first. Go ahead with your class, but given your glorious history, think carefully about how you educate this child. Lan Xuanyu is a good kid."

"What do you mean by that?"

Ji Hongbin placed both hands behind his back. His aloof expression returned fully. "Devil Fox Yin Tianfan, are you not very impressive?"

"Get out. Get out of here."

Ji Hongbin actually sighed before leaving, as if disappointed by a junior's manners.

The door closed behind him.

For a short moment, Yin Tianfan did not speak.

Lan Xuanyu saw something pass through the fat teacher's eyes. It was not anger this time. It looked older, heavier, and gone almost before he could name it.

"No matter what," Yin Tianfan muttered, "I do not regret anything. Hmph."

Lan Xuanyu stood uncertainly. "Teacher?"

Yin Tianfan's face changed at once. The warm, harmless smile returned, though now Lan Xuanyu was not foolish enough to believe it harmless.

"I just cannot help it. Every time I see Teacher Ji, I lose my temper. You should know I rarely lost my temper in the past." He waved the matter away. "All right, all right. Since you have nothing much going on, let us begin your class today."

Lan Xuanyu's tired eyes brightened again.

"Teacher, have you really commanded a warship before? How big was it? Tell me about it."

Yin Tianfan squinted with pride. "Of course I have. I have commanded all sorts of warships, even the biggest ones. You know about spaceships, but do you know their different types?"

Lan Xuanyu shook his head. "I have only attended the foundation course. It teaches us how to pilot Soul Guidance vehicles."

"What is the point of that? Not stimulating enough." Yin Tianfan curled his lips. "Shall I bring you to experience what true outer-space travel feels like?"

"Yes!"

"Come."

Yin Tianfan opened a sliding door and led him into the adjoining room.

Lan Xuanyu stopped at the threshold.

A simulation pod sat in the center, but it was unlike any pod he had used before. It was enormous, at least four times the size of an ordinary academy pod, white and streamlined like a small spacecraft sleeping indoors. Its surface was smooth, elegant, and expensive in a way even a child could recognize. It occupied so much space that the room seemed built around it.

"Woah," Lan Xuanyu breathed. "It is huge. Teacher, is this also a simulation pod?"

"Of course."

Yin Tianfan touched his Soul Guidance Communication Device. The pod opened slowly, revealing an interior full of soft light and tiny signal lamps. There was no viscous liquid inside. Instead, the inner lining looked soft enough to swallow the body like a cushion.

"This is a dual-seater simulation pod," Yin Tianfan said. "It allows two people to use it together. It was actually..."

He paused for half a breath.

"Never mind. You lie here. You do not have to do anything else."

"All right."

Lan Xuanyu stepped inside and lay down.

The material beneath him softened further, wrapping around him as if he had sunk into warm clouds. Yin Tianfan climbed into the other side with practiced ease. The pod closed above them.

There was no darkness.

Boundless space opened before Lan Xuanyu's eyes.

Stars stretched into the distance. The blackness between them was deep but not empty, and for one breath Lan Xuanyu forgot the office, the Maze, the Golden Lion Roar, and even the question of what his body had done. He had ridden interstellar civilian vessels before, but this was different. This was not looking through a window.

It felt as if his whole being had been placed in the universe.

A golden scan swept past.

A mechanical voice sounded.

"Scanning. Identity confirmed. Heaven Luo Institute, Lan Xuanyu. Piloting level: zero."

Yin Tianfan's voice came through the simulation. "Ready? We are entering the universe. What you will experience is the sensation of flying inside a space warship in the cosmos. The impact on your body will be strong. Prepare yourself. You can use soul power to protect your body."

"I am ready, Teacher."

Lan Xuanyu gazed at the sea of stars with wide eyes.

"Space warship 128, ready for takeoff."

The world lit.

An immense force struck from behind.

Lan Xuanyu's excitement broke into shock. He wanted to cry out, but the pressure locked him into place so hard that even sound seemed trapped inside his chest. His body felt as if it were being squeezed flat. The soul power within him began to circulate on its own, but under the crushing acceleration even that movement felt slow and heavy.

Then warmth spread from his chest.

It flowed through his meridians, gentle but persistent, easing the worst of the pressure. Lan Xuanyu clung to that warmth without knowing how. The force did not vanish, but it became something he could endure.

Suddenly his body lightened.

The pressure dropped.

Space unfolded ahead.

Lan Xuanyu looked around in wonder. The warship around him seemed transparent, or perhaps the simulation was letting him see in every direction at once. Stars, darkness, distant light, all of it lay around him.

Are we in space?

The thought filled him with such excitement that even the lingering discomfort became smaller.

---

Dorm 336 went to the infirmary before the canteen.

Luo complained until the medic touched his ankle and made him yelp. Song said nothing about his shoulder until the same medic pressed two fingers into the bruise and told him that pretending not to hurt did not make the bruise admire him.

Yan Shuo accepted a heat-dispersion patch for the line of fatigue across his back.

"Overused?" the medic asked.

"Controlled too tightly for too long," Yan answered after thinking about it.

The medic raised an eyebrow.

Yan did not elaborate. It was not a secret in the way Lan Xuanyu's kept-behind conversation was a secret. It was simply difficult to explain that keeping flame narrow could tire the back more than throwing it wide, because every feather-edged line had to be caught before it spilled into a teammate's path.

When the patch touched his skin, the warmth under his shoulders shifted. Dawnflame Kite's response was small and living, like a bird settling its wings deeper into a nest. Copper-red heat gathered, then thinned; pale-gold motes flickered once inside his breath and disappeared.

No soul skill was active.

The ring and soul spirit were still there.

They were part of the way his body accepted the patch, part of how the heat loosened, part of how his next breath arranged itself around relief and hunger.

Luo leaned sideways on the infirmary bench. "So. Review?"

"First point," Yan said. "A loud sound is not a command."

Song nodded at once.

Luo sighed. "I knew you were going to say that."

"You wanted to chase it."

"I wanted to know whether it was chasing us. That is different."

"Then next time, scout for danger without giving up formation." Yan looked at Song. "Second point. The angled shield worked. The flat shield would have jammed us."

Song's face brightened despite himself.

"Third point," Yan continued before Song could look too pleased, "you took the later shoulder hit too squarely because you were proud of the first one."

Song's face stopped brightening.

Luo laughed.

Yan looked at him.

"Fourth point. Your laugh still uses the ankle you injured."

Luo sat properly.

For a moment, the three of them were quiet.

Outside the infirmary, other students' voices passed in broken pieces. Dorm 333. First place. How did they win? Was Lu Qianxun really eliminated? Did Jin Xiang get tricked? Why did Teacher Ji make him shut up?

The rumors had already begun to grow teeth.

Yan listened without feeding them.

He knew only public facts. Dorm 333 had won. Lan Xuanyu had stayed behind. Liu Feng and Qian Lei looked as if victory had frightened them before it delighted them. Anything beyond that belonged either to the teachers or to Dorm 333.

"You are not curious?" Luo asked suddenly.

Yan looked at him.

Luo shrugged. "About how they did it. Everyone is going to ask. You and Lan Xuanyu are close."

"Being close does not make every answer mine," Yan said.

Song looked at him for a moment, then down at his own hands.

Yan's voice softened a little. "If he wants to say something, he will. If teachers tell the class something public, we will hear it with everyone else. Until then, our job is our own route."

"Our own route," Luo repeated, half-mocking and half-accepting.

Yan nodded.

His own route had not ended in an office on the roof. It had not ended with the vice president smiling at him. That did not make it smaller. It only made it different.

Close-body mecha work. Battle-armor interface. Flame that could wrap the body without blinding allies. A back and shoulder frame that would someday have to bear more than a low-output Mantle. Song's shield timing. Luo's interruption angles. A team that survived because its boundaries grew cleaner.

Not every door opened above the Maze.

Some doors were built one careful hinge at a time.

---

A strong force came from behind again.

Lan Xuanyu's gaze snapped forward. Something far ahead grew larger in his vision, too quickly for comfort.

Asteroids.

An asteroid belt spread before the warship like a field of broken mountains. Some rocks were small. Some looked large enough to smash a building to dust. All of them moved, tumbling silently through space.

Lan Xuanyu knew he was inside a simulation pod.

His palms still wanted to sweat.

"Kid," Yin Tianfan's voice said, amused and sharp, "sit tight and watch closely."

The mechanical voice sounded again.

"Entering the battlefield."

Battlefield?

Lan Xuanyu did not have time to ask.

The warship made an abrupt right turn.

His body went left, or tried to. The restraints and pressure held him in place while his stomach seemed to continue in the old direction. Then the ship accelerated and plunged straight into the asteroid belt.

Stars vanished behind stone.

Asteroids flashed past so close that Lan Xuanyu felt his breath stop again and again. The warship slipped between them at impossible speed. A beam of light shot from the front, striking something he could not identify before the ship rolled.

The world spun.

Up disappeared.

Down became a rumor.

Acceleration slammed from behind, then from the side, then from somewhere that made no sense at all. The transparent view became a storm of stone, light, and darkness. Lan Xuanyu tried to follow one asteroid, lost it, found three more, and then lost the idea of following anything.

A buzzing filled his ears.

His soul power crawled through his body, stubborn but slow. Warmth rose again from his meridians, but now even that warmth seemed to be spinning. He gritted his teeth. He would not cry out. He had said he was ready. He loved warships. He wanted to learn this.

The ship rolled again.

His stomach disagreed with every dream he had ever had about space.

A flash of light crossed his vision. Another. The warship fired, turned, dropped, rose, spun, and accelerated in the same breath. If there were enemies, Lan Xuanyu could not see them. If there was a path, he could not understand it. He only knew that Yin Tianfan was guiding them through the chaos with a calm that made the violence feel even more terrifying.

This was not the smooth interstellar travel of a passenger vessel.

This was a battlefield.

This was command, speed, pressure, calculation, and a body being forced to pay the price for every turn.

Time stretched.

Lan Xuanyu lost track of whether one minute or ten had passed.

At some point, he stopped trying to understand the outside and focused only on not breaking inside. Breath. Soul power. Warmth. Do not panic. Do not close your eyes too long. If you close them, the spinning becomes worse when you open them. If you keep them open, the lights stab straight into your head.

Breath.

Soul power.

Warmth.

The warship rolled again.

Lan Xuanyu's thoughts scattered.

---

By the time Dorm 336 reached the canteen, the first wave of gossip had arrived before them.

Some students talked about Dorm 333 as if Liu Feng had suddenly become unstoppable. Others insisted Qian Lei must have finally summoned something monstrous. A few looked toward Lu Qianxun's empty seat and lowered their voices.

More than one gaze came to Dorm 336's table too. Before the Maze, the simplest public guesses had been Lu Qianxun's team, Yan Shuo's team, then everyone else. Now the team with the four plus signs had taken first, Lu Qianxun's team had fallen somewhere no one could yet place, and Dorm 336's result waited for tomorrow's full ranking.

A boy from another dorm finally leaned over. "Monitor, did your team meet Dorm 333?"

"No," Yan said.

"Then if you had—"

"There is no if on the scoreboard," Yan said. "Full results tomorrow. Eat before the canteen closes."

The boy flushed, but he sat back down.

Luo covered his mouth with one hand. "That sounded exactly like Teacher Mu."

"Good," Yan said. "Maybe it will work twice."

Yan took food for himself, then added more after his stomach clenched hard enough to remind him that controlled restraint still burned fuel. The portion was larger than an ordinary student's, but not strange enough to start a new rumor. Song ate with the single-minded focus of someone repairing a shield from the inside. Luo alternated between eating and pretending not to check who was watching his limp.

Liu Feng and Qian Lei came in later.

Lan Xuanyu was not with them.

Yan noticed.

He also noticed that Qian Lei's excitement had frayed around the edges and Liu Feng kept glancing toward the dormitory corridor even while holding his tray.

Yan waited until they passed close enough for a normal question.

"Is he resting?"

Liu Feng looked over.

For a moment, his expression said he did not know how much he should answer. Then he seemed to realize Yan had only asked the part that any friend could ask.

"He came back tired," Liu Feng said. "Fell asleep."

Qian Lei nodded quickly. "Very asleep. Deep asleep. If sleep had a ranking, first place again."

The joke was poor.

It was also an attempt to make worry less visible.

Yan accepted it. "Then let him sleep."

Qian Lei blinked, as if he had expected more.

Yan only lifted his tray and returned to Dorm 336's table.

Luo watched him sit. "That was all?"

"That was all he could tell me in a canteen," Yan said.

Song grunted in approval around a mouthful of food.

The class monitor's seat did not make every classmate's private trouble public property. Yan had learned that before there were plus signs on score sheets. He had learned it in institute housing corridors, in the space between Lan Xuanyu's ordinary smile and the pressure that sometimes made Yan's Fire Phoenix sit very still inside him.

Friendship did not always mean walking through the same door.

Sometimes it meant guarding the hallway and not touching the handle.

---

Earlier that evening, twenty minutes after the simulation began, the pod opened.

Lan Xuanyu came out like a person escaping a beast's mouth.

The room beyond the pod was steady.

His body did not believe it.

The floor rose and fell. The walls tilted. Yin Tianfan's round face appeared somewhere that might have been above him or beside him or spinning with everything else.

"The toilet is over there," Yin Tianfan said.

Lan Xuanyu ran.

He reached the toilet just in time.

The sound that followed was not heroic.

Yin Tianfan stood outside and listened with a smile that could only be called treacherous.

"Hehehehe."

He cracked his knuckles and looked thoroughly pleased. "Not bad. Not bad at all. I think I lasted eighteen minutes the first time I experienced a space warship, and I was twelve then. This boy lasted about nineteen before he truly broke, and he is only eight by the academy's file. Worth teaching. Excellent Spiritual Power, good physical qualities, and even a bit of craftiness. I picked up gold this time."

Lan Xuanyu did not hear the praise clearly.

He was too busy regretting that human beings had stomachs.

When he finally came out, his face was pale enough to make his earlier post-Maze pallor look healthy. One hand touched the wall. His legs felt as if they belonged to someone who had betrayed him.

"Teacher, I..."

"It is all right. Go back and rest properly." Yin Tianfan's smile widened in a way that made Lan Xuanyu's stomach threaten rebellion again. "Come back tomorrow night after school. I will inform Mu Zhongtian."

"Oh. All right."

Lan Xuanyu was not entirely sure how he returned to the dormitory.

He remembered the corridor in pieces. A wall. A light. The lift. Someone asking whether he was all right. His own voice saying something that might have been yes.

Then the dormitory bed was under him.

Liu Feng said something.

Qian Lei said something louder.

Lan Xuanyu heard neither sentence clearly. The world was still rolling through an asteroid belt behind his closed eyes. He had wanted to learn about warships since he was little. He still did.

He just had not known that the universe could spin inside his bones.

He fell asleep without dinner.

Outside, Heaven Luo Academy settled into evening noise, rumors, recovery, and the ordinary hunger of children after a hard test.

Inside one dormitory, Lan Xuanyu slept through all of it.

Inside another, Yan Shuo wrote three lines in his review notebook before sleep pulled at his own sore back.

Do not chase noise.

Keep the boundary clean.

Some doors open for one person.

He looked at the last line for a moment, then closed the notebook.

No resentment came with it.

Only a quiet, steady warmth under his shoulders, copper-red at the center and pale gold at the edge, settling around the living beat of a small bird that was still growing.

Tomorrow would bring the full results.

Tonight, both boys slept on opposite sides of secrets neither of them fully understood.
