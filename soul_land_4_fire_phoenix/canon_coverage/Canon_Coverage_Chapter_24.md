## 2. SL4 Chapter 109 — Accept a disciple

Fetched source:

- `https://novelfull.com/soul-land-iv-douluo-dalu-ultimate-fighting/chapter-109-accept-a-disciple.html`

### Canon beats

Post-Maze class aftermath:

- Lu Qianxun looks at Lan Xuanyu with a blazing/fearful gaze.
- Lu's team is the only team that truly confronted Dorm 333 and knows something abnormal happened.
- Lu still does not understand the girl from the Gate or Lan Xuanyu's Dragon Roar.
- Other students are surprised and do not understand how Dorm 333 became champion.
- Lu has developed fear because his meridians are still surging with terror after the Dragon Roar.
- The vice president says full results will be announced tomorrow; students may return to rest or cultivate.
- Ji Hongbin asks Lan Xuanyu to stay.
- Liu Feng and Qian Lei are excited because Dorm 333's first place is a true counterattack; they know Lan Xuanyu was the key.

Vice president and Ji Hongbin:

- The vice president beckons Lan Xuanyu to follow.
- Ji Hongbin follows too.
- The vice president asks why Ji is following; Ji says he is supervising in case the vice president corrupts the child.
- Their banter implies an old history and that the vice president has a dangerous/unreliable reputation.
- Ji calls or implies that the vice president is like a devil incarnate/devil-style figure.
- The vice president does not properly refute this and leads them to his office.

Office scene:

- The office is on the roof/top of the main teaching block.
- The office is spacious, over 200 square meters, with sofa, desk, bookshelves, and plants.
- Printed books are rare/valuable in the Soul Guidance technology era, showing the vice president's taste/resources.
- Ji sits on the sofa and refuses to leave.
- The vice president asks Lan Xuanyu how he thought through his Maze tactics.
- Lan explains that the Maze was unknown, teachers would make students meet, and waiting at ease for exhausted enemies gave them a better chance.
- The vice president approves his correct tactics and perception.
- The vice president asks whether Lan Xuanyu can amplify Qian Lei and Liu Feng.
- Lan says it seems so, but he does not know what the final roar was; he only felt suppressed by Lu Qianxun's lion roar, uncomfortable, and then called out.
- The vice president interprets it as a natural meridian reaction, implying Lan Xuanyu's meridians are better than Lu Qianxun's.
- He says strength can be nurtured but perception is hard to train.
- He asks Lan Xuanyu whether he wants to be his student.
- Lan knows formally acknowledging a master is serious: one-day teacher, lifetime father-type tradition; a master means inheritance and care, not only class instruction.
- Lan says he already has a master.
- The vice president first assumes this may mean Ji Hongbin.
- Ji gives no answer.
- The vice president says that is fine because he does not intend to teach martial-soul cultivation; he can teach Lan Xuanyu warship piloting / warship command.
- Lan becomes instantly interested because he has loved warships since childhood.
- The vice president explains warship piloting is not a one-man show; a warship/fleet needs someone to command and coordinate roles.
- Lan accepts and formally bows, asking the vice president to take him as disciple.

Fetched source:

- `https://novelfull.com/soul-land-iv-douluo-dalu-ultimate-fighting/chapter-110-space-warship.html`

- He tells Lan Xuanyu it is enough for Lan to know his name; outside, Lan should not tell anyone he is Yin Tianfan's disciple. This is their little secret.

- Ji tells Yin to think carefully about how to educate Lan, given Yin's history, and says Lan is a good child.
- Ji calls Yin Tianfan the Devil Fox.
- Yin Tianfan angrily tells Ji to leave.
- After Ji leaves, Yin briefly shows disappointment/old regret and mutters that he does not regret anything.

First warship lesson setup:

- Lan calls Yin teacher.
- Yin says he cannot control his temper around Ji and then starts the class immediately.
- Lan asks whether Yin has really commanded a warship and what size.
- Yin says he has commanded many kinds, even the biggest ones.
- Lan has only studied foundation courses for piloting Soul Guidance vehicles.
- Yin dismisses that as not stimulating enough and offers to let Lan experience real outer-space travel.
- Yin leads Lan into an adjoining room.
- There is a very large high-end dual-seater simulation pod, about four times the normal pod size and occupying over 20 square meters.
- The pod interior is soft rather than liquid-filled.
- Yin and Lan lie inside.

Simulation content:

- When the pod closes, Lan sees boundless space immediately.
- A scan identifies him as Heaven Luo Institute, Lan Xuanyu, piloting level 0.
- Yin warns Lan that entering the universe/space-warship experience will place strong impact on his body and that he can use soul power to protect himself.
- Space warship 128 takes off.
- Lan feels massive pressure/acceleration from behind, unable to cry out, as if being compressed.
- His soul power moves automatically but slowly under the pressure.
- Warmth from his meridians/chest spreads and helps him endure.
- The pressure eases; Lan sees space and is excited by the immersive cosmic view.
- More thrust comes; an object grows in view and becomes an asteroid belt.
- Yin tells Lan to sit tight/watch closely.
- The simulation announces entry into the battlefield.
- The ship abruptly turns, accelerates, and rushes into the asteroid belt.
- Asteroids flash past quickly; the warship fires beams/lights.
- The ship shakes, spins, and rolls violently while still accelerating.
- Lan becomes dizzy/disoriented with ringing/buzzing in his ears and cannot make sense of the lights.
- The simulation lasts 20 minutes before the pod opens.
