## 2. Mandatory canon beats covered

### Chapter141 — Breakthrough

- Nana has Lan begin the Rank20 attempt under her protection.
- Lan circulates the Mysterious Heaven Method again and again.
- The Rank19 bottleneck / shell finally breaks.
- His soul power rushes and his body initially feels lighter.
- Gold and silver bloodline energy surges from the chest vortex and enters meridians/bones.
- Colliding gold/silver currents create pain, swelling, burning/freezing pressure.
- Nana tells him: `Vortex`.
- Lan focuses on the gold-silver vortex and stabilizes the energies briefly.
- The energy supply becomes too large, the vortex swells, and his body feels overinflated / near bursting.
- Nana provides protection and tells him to merge his martial souls.

### Chapter142 — Second Soul Ring / evolution

- Lan trusts Nana and brings the two Blue Silver Grass martial souls together.
- Gold and silver collide through martial soul fusion; rainbow light explodes.
- Lan experiences a blank/dark/weightless interval and then returns.
- The gold-silver vortex stabilizes and becomes larger/purer; the rainbow core brightens.
- Lan’s breakthrough succeeds, but he stabilizes at Rank20 rather than leaping higher.
- Nana explains the danger of future breakthroughs and the need to preserve enough power for fusion.
- Nana warns him that future ten-rank breakthroughs may be dangerous without repeated martial-soul-fusion tempering.
- The `ten times per ten ranks` rule remains active.
- Lan’s Blue Silver Grass evolves: tougher, cylindrical/structured, sharper, more vine-like rather than fragile leaves.

### Chapter143 — Soul ring evolution and fire element

- Lan releases the silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass.
- The silver pattern now has a clearer net-like/oval structure.
- Two soul rings appear and both are yellow/hundred-year; the former white first ring has evolved.
- Lan is shocked because he did not use the Spirit Pagoda or attach a Spirit Soul.
- Nana strictly warns him not to tell others about natural rings / no Spirit Soul.
- The golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass also has a tougher, structured change with diamond-like patterning.
- Silver first soul ring / water-ice control improves; the grass becomes crystal-like and can act like an ice whip / storage conduit for water element.
- Silver second soul ring is unexpectedly fire-element control; heat rises and fire elements gather.
- Lan is distressed because water and fire appear incompatible.

### Chapter144 — Dao of water and fire

- Nana shows she can also control the fire element and confirms her bloodline similarity with Lan.
- She explains that incompatible elements are not necessarily useless; correct usage can create powerful effects.
- She demonstrates water strengthening flame and fire producing steam/change within sufficient water.
- Nana teleports Lan into the sky/space-like height above clouds.
- She demonstrates rotating/compressed water and opposite-rotating fire, merging them without crude collision and producing a red-blue explosion.
- She states water-fire fusion is extremely difficult, second only to light/darkness, and requires personal comprehension rather than copied technique.
- Returning to the room, Nana identifies space element use.
- Nana displays seven element lights: water, fire, wind, earth, space, light, and darkness.

- Nana says bloodline evolution may let him do similar things in the future, but his own comprehension matters.

- Nana says Lan can contact her through Dong Qianqiu / future summoning.

- Farewell at the space flight hub; Lan apologizes through Nana and leaves.
- Nana remains reluctant but does not leave Heaven Dou for Lan’s peaceful life.
- Yun Yan relays Federation/Shrek testing interest; Nana refuses to go to Douluo/Shrek for testing.
