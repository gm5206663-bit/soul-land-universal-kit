### SL4 Chapter 94 - Testing the Gate of Summoning

Source: `https://novelfull.com/soul-land-iv-douluo-dalu-ultimate-fighting/chapter-94-testing-the-gate-of-summoning.html`

Working summary:

- Chapter begins with Lan Xuanyu disappointed that Nana cannot cross, but glad to speak with her.
- Lava Hounds are already charging.
- Dong Qianqiu tells Lan Xuanyu to talk to Nana and leave the hounds to her; she attacks with the dark-blue spear.
- Liu Feng, enhanced by Lan Xuanyu, follows Dong Qianqiu.
- Nana tells Lan Xuanyu that strange factors are involved in summoning Qianqiu and that she needs him to coordinate tests.
- Nana asks whether they can perform a summon every ten minutes.
- Qian Lei says it should not be a problem if the battle ends quickly.
- Nana tells them to finish the scenario first and says she will explain later.
- With Dong Qianqiu's help, handling the Lava Hounds becomes easy.
- During the scenario, Liu Feng discovers Lan Xuanyu's support pattern: a single enhancement lets him unleash one attack, but sustained wrapping with gold-patterned Blue Silver Grass keeps the enhancement active.
- Enhanced White Dragon Spear attacks come with dragon roars; lethality, speed control, and strength increase by more than twofold.
- After scenario completion, Dorm 333 enters again and repeats the summoning tests.
- They perform three tests: two successful, one failure.
- The fourth summon succeeds.
- Nana asks Dong Qianqiu not to kill all Lava Hounds yet because she wants to speak to Lan Xuanyu.
- Nana explains spatial resonance: two different spaces vibrating at the same frequency produce a channel that connects them; this is extremely rare.
- Nana wants to test how the resonance is produced.
- Qian Lei and Liu Feng are dumbfounded that Lan Xuanyu's teacher is on the other side of the gate.
- Nana instructs them to leave one Lava Hound alive, then asks how long Qian Lei needs to re-summon after a gate disappears.
- Qian Lei says normally about half an hour, but in Soul World they can leave and re-enter to do another summon immediately after reset.
- Nana instructs them to kill the hounds, wait ten minutes, re-enter, and try again; if Dong Qianqiu is not summoned, exit and repeat.
- The second summon fails to call Dong Qianqiu but summons a strong spirit beast by luck; Dorm 333 still passes.
- Ten minutes later, the third summon meets Dong Qianqiu.
- After more repeated summons, Nana says she has hypotheses and asks them to repeat the process more.
- After more than ten summons, even with Soul World logout/reset restoring cultivation and Spiritual Power, Qian Lei is exhausted.
- Lan Xuanyu tells Nana Qian Lei may not last another summon.
- Nana ends the test for the day and tells Lan Xuanyu that the spatial resonance is most probably between Lan Xuanyu and Nana.
- Lan Xuanyu asks whether they could summon Nana over if strong enough.
- Nana says it would be difficult/almost impossible, though she does not know why she feels sure.
- Dong Qianqiu looks curious, then shrugs and leaves.
- Gate disappears.
- Dorm 333 returns to their room; Qian Lei lies weakly on his bed from continuous summoning exhaustion.
- Liu Feng is full of excitement and asks Lan Xuanyu to coordinate with him because Lan Xuanyu's arrival has changed Dorm 333.
- Liu Feng wants to test silver-patterned versus gold-patterned enhancement outside battle in the dorm room.

Canon importance:

- Nana/Lan Xuanyu resonance is a private canon pillar.
- Dong Qianqiu's support and Nana's instruction belong to Dorm 333's private route.
- Qian Lei's exhaustion is required and must not be erased.
- Liu Feng's enhancement discovery is required and should lead directly into Chapter 95.
- Yan Shuo cannot know, hear, witness, cause, identify, or solve this gate/resonance route.
