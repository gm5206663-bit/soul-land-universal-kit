## Working design decision

The user preferred the earlier softer face and clarified that Yan is actually
female and exceptionally beautiful. A beauty-preserved revision was accepted
with “Ok”; “Continue” later resumed broader production. That is a **working
visual direction**, not approval of every new scene or an irreversible model lock.
The accepted portrait became `assets/00-approved-reference.png`.

The eight new scenes were generated after this correction. All character-led
scenes used the working portrait as a visual reference; the Extreme North scene
is a separate discovery setting. The earlier rejected ten-image batch and the
masculinized portrait correction are **not** in this delivery.
