## What is not settled

1. **Exact style match:** these are reference-informed 3D fan illustrations, not
   proprietary Soul Land studio assets. The generator cannot promise identical
   facial rigs, render shaders or franchise production quality.
2. **Design continuity:** new outfits, parents and teammate faces are provisional.
   No complete turnaround/model sheet or official costume system exists yet.
3. **Coverage:** eight scene illustrations are finished. Ten chapter entries use
   them, sometimes sharing an arc keyframe. The remaining 42 chapter entries
   have designed plates and shot plans, not dedicated scene illustrations.
4. **Animation:** slow camera moves and fades only. No body animation, battle
   choreography, lips, moving wings or complete episode production.
5. **Timing:** chapter transitions and English WebVTT subtitles are estimated from
   script length and narration duration, not word-aligned. Spoken chapter cues
   are authoritative. Proper-name pronunciation has not received human review.
6. **Story boundaries:** no new chapter prose, author-only ruling, identity reveal
   in-world, fourth ring, deity transformation or future forest resolution.
7. **Technical QA is not creative approval:** source hashes establish text
   integrity, not perfect visual fidelity. A green check must not erase the
   unresolved design and animation work above.
