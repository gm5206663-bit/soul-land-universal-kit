Chapter 1. Beneath the Same Dawn. A northern research expedition finds a mystery beneath the ice while Yan's protected family beginning unfolds elsewhere. Chapter 2. Warm Hands. Family safeguards and institute housing bring Yan and Lan Xuanyu together through an ordinary, quietly extraordinary childhood. Chapter 3. The Shape Inside. Awakening reveals Yan's juvenile Fire Phoenix, before any soul ring or bonded soul spirit has been acquired. Chapter 4. The First Record. School registration creates the first public records while friendship must survive different classes and hidden family truths. Chapter 5. The Weight Between Them. School rivalry and physical tests teach restraint as Yan reaches the first bottleneck without acquiring a ring yet. Chapter 6. The Right Wings. Yan bonds legally with the flawed Dawnflame Kite, gaining the first yellow ring and an unmastered first skill.
