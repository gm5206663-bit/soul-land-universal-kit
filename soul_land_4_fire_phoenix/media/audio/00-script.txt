Beneath the same dawn, two beginnings take shape. One mystery waits beneath northern ice. Another grows inside an ordinary family, protected by a name the world is allowed to know. Yan Shuo. Behind that public identity is Yan Shuo'er, a girl whose beauty and unusual gifts make protection necessary. The disguise must never become a cage around her strength. A flawed first soul spirit becomes a living bond. Friendship becomes trust, rivalry becomes training, and no earned gain arrives without consequence. Beside Lan Xuanyu's unfolding story, Dorm Three Three Six must keep its own place: Yan, Song Yichen, and Luo Haoran, learning to move as a team. Through a protected cocoon and an earned awakening, red-gold wings finally open. But strength does not solve every problem. At the edge of the published story, two wounded beasts guard an immortal herb, and the children still have to decide what they can honestly afford. Fire Phoenix. Strength has a memory. So does the world.
