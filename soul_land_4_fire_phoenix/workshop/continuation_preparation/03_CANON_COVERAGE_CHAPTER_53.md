## Required character logic

### Lan Xuanyu

His choice must read as tactical calculation under risk, not omniscient author knowledge. He is using the forest’s conflict rather than claiming he can defeat two thousand-year threats through raw force. He keeps a retreat option and should not pretend to have already solved the animals’ problem.

### Qian Lei

This is the first source-backed opening for direct communication with non-summoned beasts. His nervousness is valid. His contribution is neither raw power nor animal speech from earlier chapters; it is Spiritual-Power-mediated intent transmission in this exact source context.

### Liu Feng

Liu’s function is speed/extraction. The retrieved herb is not a Silver Edge feat, not an arm-bone victory, and not permission to declare the encounter won.
