## 2. True Phoenix versus true Dragon

True Phoenix and true Dragon are both top mythical branches. Neither category should automatically erase the other without considering purity, age, rank, attribute, divine authority, and context.

Yan does not automatically suppress:

- Dragon God-level forces;
- superior true Dragons;
- divine beasts;
- much higher-realm pure dragons;
- opponents with stronger bloodline/authority counters.

Yan does naturally suppress:

- inferior fire beasts;
- impure dragon-flame beasts;
- pseudo-dragon beasts;
- lizard-dragon creatures;
- fire-dragon-type targets with lower bloodline/attribute purity than true awakened Fire Phoenix + Ultimate Fire.

---

## 3. Fire hierarchy rule

Ultimate Fire is not a joke and not cosmetic.

When Yan’s true Fire Phoenix / Ultimate Fire meets inferior fire:

- inferior flame bends, thins, scatters, or loses coherence;
- fire aura is harder for the enemy to maintain;
- instinctive confidence can hesitate or recoil;
- target choice can destabilize;
- fire/thermal environments often become usable channels for Yan;
- physical danger can still remain through mass, claws, numbers, terrain, shock, and non-fire mechanics.

Correct shorthand: **their fire bows; their bodies may still be dangerous.**

Yan's no-full-fusion serious combat floor is low Soul King-class effective threat floor against ordinary/no-special-counter opponents when her true Fire Phoenix, Ultimate Fire, SP962 control, three purple rings, three compatible purple Soul Spirits, wings, and cocoon-rebuilt body/frame are active.
