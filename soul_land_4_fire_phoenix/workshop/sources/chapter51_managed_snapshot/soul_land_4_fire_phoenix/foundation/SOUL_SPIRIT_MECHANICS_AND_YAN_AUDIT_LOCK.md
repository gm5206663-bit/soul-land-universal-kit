## 5. Bloodline and compatibility rules

For Yan, Phoenix bloodline is central.

Soul spirits are not chosen by raw age only. A lower-age perfectly compatible Phoenix/bird spirit can be better than a higher-age incompatible beast.

Mandatory compatibility calculation:

1. Is the soul spirit bird/Phoenix/fire/wing/sky/resonance-compatible?
2. Does it support the Fire Phoenix route rather than dilute it?
3. Does true Fire Phoenix bloodline suppress/command/attract it?
4. Does Ultimate Fire refine or conflict with its attributes?
5. Does Dawn-Iron body-frame protect absorption strain?
6. Does Dawnflame boundary/light support safe integration?
7. Does Purple Flame/eidolon memory help separate Yan's will from invading impulse?
8. Does Yan's adaptation talent harmonize without erasing the spirit's identity?
9. Does the spirit accept Yan voluntarily, or is there a valid dominance/rescue/sacrifice reason?
10. Does the result create public record/identity pressure?

Phoenix bloodline effects:

- True awakened Fire Phoenix can overawe inferior birds, ordinary fire-birds, and many predatory avian soul beasts.
- It can suppress/attract Fire Phoenix-compatible spirits.
- It does not automatically enslave divine beasts, superior Phoenix branches, or equal/higher ancient beings.
- For high-grade spirits, Phoenix bloodline creates the right conversation/recognition, not automatic ownership.
