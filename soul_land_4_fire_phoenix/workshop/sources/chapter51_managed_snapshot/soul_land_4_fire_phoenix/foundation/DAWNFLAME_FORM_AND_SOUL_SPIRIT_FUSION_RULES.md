## 3. Dawnflame Form

Dawnflame Form is a controlled higher-integration state between Yan's Fire Phoenix martial soul, body, Dawnflame Kite, and Dawnfeather Mantle.

It may show:

- copper-red/gold warmth;
- feather-line glow;
- shoulder/wing-root pressure;
- improved acceleration and impact handling;
- better fire-light conversion;
- sharper presence.

It must remain pre-awakening:

- partial, not complete;
- costly if overused;
- dependent on breath/body control;
- not true flight;
- not full wings;
- not a Phoenix God form.

## 4. Soul-spirit fusion rules before Level30

Yan can coordinate with soul spirits more deeply than ordinary due to foundation/adaptation, but there are limits.

Allowed before Level30:

- Dawnflame + Dawn-Iron coordination;

Not allowed before Level30:

- complete multi-coloured Phoenix mastery;
- Phoenix God authority.

## 5. Interaction with Dawn-Iron Phoenix Roc

Dawn-Iron / Skybone Phoenix Frame supports Dawnflame by giving the fire-light mantle a body-frame to ride.

Combined effect:

- safer recoil conversion;
- stronger landing/stance;
- better shoulder/wing-root alignment;
- more repeatable burst paths;
- improved close-body combat;
- structural support for future wings.

Limits:

- Dawn-Iron is not external armor.
- Skybone Phoenix Frame is not automatic defense.
- Dawn-Iron does not replace Dawnflame's future awakening.
