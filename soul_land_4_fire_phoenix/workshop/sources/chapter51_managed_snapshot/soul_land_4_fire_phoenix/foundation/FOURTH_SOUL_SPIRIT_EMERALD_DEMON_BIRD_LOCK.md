## 5. Why Fire Phoenix can suppress Emerald Demon Bird

Emerald Demon Bird is a terrifying bird-type soul beast and the nemesis of most flying soul beasts, but Yan is no longer an ordinary flying soul master.

After Chapter35/36, Yan has:

- true awakened Fire Phoenix martial soul;
- Ultimate Fire quality;
- SP962 Spirit Sea control;
- three existing bird/fire-wing foundations;
- full phoenix wings;
- stronger adaptation talent;
- Dawn-Iron wing/body structure;
- Purple Flame memory-wing pressure.

Therefore the correct interaction is:

**True/full Fire Phoenix exerts strong bird-type bloodline/class suppression over Emerald Demon Bird.**

This does not mean automatic slavery or mind control. It means:

- the Emerald Demon Bird cannot treat Yan like normal prey;
- its head-killing instinct is checked by higher bird/phoenix authority;
- it recognizes a superior fire-bird/phoenix category;
- its ferocity can turn from predation into challenge, then submission/pact if earned;
- Yan can force a moment of stillness long enough for will, compatibility, or sacrifice to occur.

Wrong handling:

- Emerald Demon Bird instantly obeys like a pet with no danger.
- Emerald Demon Bird casually kills/cripples Yan despite full Fire Phoenix suppression.
- Fire Phoenix aura has no effect at all.

Correct handling:

- the Emerald Demon Bird is terrifying;
- its speed and killing instinct are real;
- Yan's Fire Phoenix bloodline suppresses and overawes it;
- the pact is earned through dominance, compatibility, and voluntary surrender/sacrifice.

Emerald Demon Bird adds:

- lethal precision against head/core/control points;

It makes Yan's flight route deadlier and faster without turning her into only an assassin.
