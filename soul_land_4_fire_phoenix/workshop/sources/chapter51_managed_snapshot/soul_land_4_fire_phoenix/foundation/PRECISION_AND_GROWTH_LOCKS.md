## 4. Cost cannot erase reward

Costs and backlash may create drama but must not cancel earned growth.

Allowed:

- hunger;
- soreness;
- recovery time;
- control difficulty;
- attention risk;
- resource cost;
- identity pressure.

Not allowed:

- `she gained a major power but cannot use it for the next whole arc` unless user approves;
- `the awakening happened but no one reacts`;
- `the new soul spirit is only cosmetic`;
- `Yan stays behind canon peers by unexplained author fiat`.
