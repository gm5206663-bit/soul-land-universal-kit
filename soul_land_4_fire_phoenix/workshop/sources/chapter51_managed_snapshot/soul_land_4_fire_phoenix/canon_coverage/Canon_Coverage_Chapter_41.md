- At the mountain foot, Ground Fire Lizards swarm densely.

- Liu Feng uses White Dragon Spear recoil plus his second skill White Dragon Return under Lan's golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass amplification.

- While under the dragon, Lan thinks of Tang Le's spaceship stomp/golden dragons but keeps the secret internal.
