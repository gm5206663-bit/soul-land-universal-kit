# Lan Xuanyu Pressure Adaptation

Live edge: **after Chapter31**.

Purpose: define how Yan's body adapts under Lan's abnormal pressure without exposing Lan's private secrets.

---

## 1. Core relationship

Yan Shuo'er / public Yan Shuo and Lan Xuanyu are childhood friends/best friends.

Because of proximity and trust, Yan repeatedly experiences indirect pressure from Lan's abnormal route.

This contributes to Yan's adaptation, but does not reveal Lan's secrets.

---

## 2. Allowed effects on Yan

Yan may experience:

- breath tightening;
- heat drawing inward;
- Fire Phoenix identity becoming steadier;
- mild spiritual heaviness;
- hunger/recovery after pressure;
- improved suppression resistance over time;
- better emotional reading of Lan's fatigue/danger.

These effects are subtle and body-based.

---

## 3. Forbidden effects

Lan pressure adaptation must not grant Yan:

- exact knowledge of Lan's origin;
- gold/silver Blue Silver Grass mechanics;
- bloodline truth;
- Nana's identity;
- Dragon God knowledge;
- automatic immunity to dragon pressure;
- free mastery without training;
- public proof of Lan's secrets.

---

## 4. Relationship balance

Do not make firewall equal emotional distance.

Correct dynamic:

- Lan and Yan care about each other.
- Lan can say what is safe.
- Yan can accept boundaries while still worrying and competing.
- Their friendship can affect choices, training, and interpretation of public events.

Incorrect dynamic:

- Lan acts like Yan is a stranger.
- Yan demands private mechanics as proof of friendship.
- The story erases public consequences because secrets exist.

---

## 5. Chapter31 state

After Chapter31:

- Yan knows Lan used something dangerous in the forced Lu match.
- Yan does not know exact self-fusion/colorful-vortex mechanics.
- Lan and Yan speak with trust and boundaries before/around the restricted assessment.
- Dorm336 wins the restricted direct-combat assessment; this does not break friendship.

## 6. Future cocoon relationship note

When Yan later enters the Level30 one-month cocoon, Lan's best-friend reaction should matter: worry, pride, helplessness if he cannot help, and renewed rivalry after she emerges. The event must not make Lan/Yan cold, and it must not leak Lan's private mechanics or Yan Shuo'er's hidden identity automatically.
