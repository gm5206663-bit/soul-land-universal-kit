# Yan Shuo'er Identity and Appearance Lock

Live edge: after the identity correction, second-soul-spirit evolution repair, and no-reveal correction.

Purpose: lock the corrected truth that the public boy `Yan Shuo` is actually the girl **Yan Shuo'er**, raised under a boy identity for protection, without breaking the public-school continuity already written.

---

## 1. Core identity

- Real/private name: **Yan Shuo'er**.
- Public/academy name: **Yan Shuo**.
- Biological/gender truth: **girl**.
- Public childhood presentation: raised and registered as a boy.
- She knows she is a girl. This is not amnesia, confusion, reincarnation, or identity denial.
- The cover is a family protection strategy, not a personality rewrite.
- Her personality remains the same: bright, loyal, responsible, stubborn, good-hearted, rules-aware, protective, competitive, and practical.
- **No reveal has happened yet.** Chapter 25 does not make her strong enough to publicly or formally reveal the truth.

Use rule:

- In private family/internal-support narration, use **Yan Shuo'er** and **she/her**.
- In public academy scenes before any reveal, classmates and public records still use **Yan Shuo** and may use **he/him** because they know the cover.
- In formal/public institutional contexts before the reveal, the file stays **Yan Shuo** unless the user approves a future reveal route.
- When narration is public-facing or tightly filtered through classmates, `Yan Shuo` can remain natural.
- Do not write future support as if she is truly male.

## 2. Why the cover exists

Yan Shuo'er was unusual from the womb and dangerously striking from birth:

- prenatal heat/breath regulation was too organized for an ordinary child;
- Lin Mei's fire-bird lineage stabilized around the unborn child rather than only burdening the mother;
- after birth, the child carried a warm phoenix-like beauty: too clear, too bright, and too easy to notice;
- early sensors and adult instincts could explain pieces of it, but the total impression was dangerous for a non-elite family;
- a beautiful girl with abnormal fire-bird signs, future Fire Phoenix potential, and unusual adaptation would attract bad people, greedy institutions, predatory nobles/sects, or illegal interest before she could protect herself.

Yan Ke and Lin Mei therefore chose the simplest shield they could maintain:

> Publicly raise `Yan Shuo` as a boy until Yan Shuo'er becomes strong enough to choose and protect her own reveal.

This is a concealment decision made by frightened parents with limited power, not a cultivation nerf.

## 3. Appearance distinction

### Public Yan Shuo

The public academy presentation is deliberately controlled:

- short or practical hair;
- loose uniform and training clothes;
- boyish posture and speech habits taught early;
- bright but contained expression;
- lean, lively, athletic build;
- handsome/pretty androgyny that classmates treat as an unusual boyish look;
- Fire Phoenix warmth held close instead of allowed to bloom outward;
- public aura looks promising, not dangerously beautiful.

### Private Yan Shuo'er

True appearance is much more obviously phoenix-like:

- strikingly beautiful in a clear, fiery, youthful way;
- fine features that look too bright when her martial soul stirs;
- warm skin and eyes that catch copper-red/dawn-gold light under soul-power movement;
- hair and lashes can show red-gold warmth in strong light or during release;
- body-line is more graceful and girl-shaped than the public mask suggests;
- the Fire Phoenix martial soul makes the true appearance feel noble, vivid, and hard to ignore.

After the Chapter 25 second soul spirit/ring, her true appearance and aura become even more refined because Adaptation Talent, Fire Phoenix bloodline refinement, Dawnflame Kite growth, Dawn-Iron Phoenix Roc structure, and the new body-frame all improve together. This increases the pressure on the disguise, but it **does not** mean she reveals.

Do not sexualize her. This is child/young-teen phoenix beauty and danger-of-attention logic, not adult glamour.

## 4. The restriction/mask

The cover uses ordinary and low-level methods, not a divine seal:

- public paperwork and name shortening;
- clothes, haircut, posture, and habit;
- family discipline around voice, expression, and aura;
- minor legal/medical privacy handling through Yan Ke's careful paperwork;
- low-level aura-dulling/heat-control accessories or habits if needed.

The restriction is not allowed to nerf her core cultivation.

Correct effect:

- hides/reduces outward beauty and phoenix aura;
- makes public presentation more boyish;
- teaches restraint and control;
- causes fatigue or discomfort if held too rigidly after major breakthroughs.

Wrong effect:

- sealing away her Fire Phoenix power;
- lowering her actual rank/SP;
- making soul spirits weaker;
- preventing perfect absorption;
- making her less herself.

After the second soul spirit/ring, the mask becomes harder to maintain because her body, martial soul, bloodline, and soul spirits are more complete. It remains a disguise and discipline layer, not a power seal.
