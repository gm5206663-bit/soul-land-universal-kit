The previous intermediate correction downgraded Yan's no-full-fusion serious baseline to high/peak Soul Ancestor-class with only conditional low Soul King threat. That was too low.

- true awakened Fire Phoenix;
- Ultimate Fire;

- three perfectly compatible purple bird/Phoenix Soul Spirits;
- SP962 / Spirit Sea despite Yan not being a spiritual-type Soul Master;

- adaptation talent working across almost eleven years;
- perfect compatibility/full absorption;
- Dorm336 team support when applicable.

## 2. Active corrected rule

Use this rule:

This is not Rank50 identity or automatic victory over all Soul Kings.

## 3. Suppression hierarchy correction

Bloodline suppression is not flat.

- Strongest: lower birds, avian beasts, fire-birds, and inferior Phoenix-like lines.
- Strong: inferior fire and ordinary fire beasts.
- Moderate but real: pseudo-dragon, lizard-dragon, impure dragon-flame, and lower fire-dragon-type targets. Dragon-line suppression is weaker than avian suppression but not zero.
- Weakest/contested: true Dragons, superior dragon bloodlines, Dragon God-level routes, and divine dragon authority.

Correct dragon shorthand:

> Against dragon-types, Yan's bloodline suppression is weakest compared to bird/fire-beast targets, but inferior or impure dragon-flame still bows. Their fire can weaken while their body remains dangerous.

## 4. Soul Spirit / foundation correction

- Three purple Soul Spirits do not mathematically combine into one black Soul Spirit.
- But they are not normal purple add-ons either.
- Because they are perfectly compatible/full absorbed and aligned with true Fire Phoenix, their combined 13,450-year note is a synergy/foundation marker.
- In the exact functions they support — fire boundary, mantle, body-frame, wing-root stability, eidolon pressure, safe Ultimate Fire output — the stack can outperform a higher-age but poorly matched source.

## 5. Emerald Demon Bird lesson preserved

The Emerald Demon Bird precedent teaches that raw cultivation age alone does not decide combat. Specialized species function, speed, weak-point targeting, timing, and matchup can let a bird-type killer threaten a much larger dragon-type body under the right conditions.

This does not mean every thousand-year bird beats every ten-thousand-year dragon. It means Yan's compatible bird/Phoenix soul-spirit foundation must be scaled by function and synergy, not dismissed by age label alone.

## 6. Metamorphosis / flight correction

The cocoon metamorphosis rebuilt Yan's Fire Phoenix body/frame/wings and must not be treated as cosmetic.

A normal/generic golden bird or ordinary avian should not casually fly better than Yan unless it has a clear reason:

- much older/higher realm;
- specialized speed/wind/aerial species;
- terrain advantage;
- Yan restricted by rules, teammates, heat signature, identity concealment, stamina, or safe-training mode;
- a specific skill advantage.

Otherwise, that is nerfing.
