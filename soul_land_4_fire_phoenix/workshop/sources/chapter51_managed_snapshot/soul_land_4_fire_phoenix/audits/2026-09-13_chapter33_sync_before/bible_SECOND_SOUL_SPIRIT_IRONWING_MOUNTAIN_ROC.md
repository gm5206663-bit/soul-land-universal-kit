- supports close-body power transfer;
- improves landing and grounded burst;
- converts some impact/recoil into usable structure;
- improves compatibility with Dawnfeather Mantle;
- now carries seventeen months of repeated direct-combat refinement after Chapter31.

Limits:

- not external armor;
- not an independent moving skeleton;
- not invincible defense;
- not full wings;
- not true flight.
