## 4. Relationship balance

Do not make firewall equal emotional distance.

Correct dynamic:

- Lan and Yan care about each other.
- Lan can say what is safe.
- Yan can accept boundaries while still worrying and competing.
- Their friendship can affect choices, training, and interpretation of public events.

Incorrect dynamic:

- Lan acts like Yan is a stranger.
- Yan demands private mechanics as proof of friendship.
- The story erases public consequences because secrets exist.
