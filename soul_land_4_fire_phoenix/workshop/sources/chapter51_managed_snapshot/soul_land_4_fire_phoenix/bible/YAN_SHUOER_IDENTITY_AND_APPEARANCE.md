## 2. Why the cover exists

Yan Shuo'er was unusual from the womb and dangerously striking from birth:

- prenatal heat/breath regulation was too organized for an ordinary child;
- Lin Mei's fire-bird lineage stabilized around the unborn child rather than only burdening the mother;
- after birth, the child carried a warm phoenix-like beauty: too clear, too bright, and too easy to notice;
- early sensors and adult instincts could explain pieces of it, but the total impression was dangerous for a non-elite family;
- a beautiful girl with abnormal fire-bird signs, future Fire Phoenix potential, and unusual adaptation would attract bad people, greedy institutions, predatory nobles/sects, or illegal interest before she could protect herself.

Yan Ke and Lin Mei therefore chose the simplest shield they could maintain:

> Publicly raise `Yan Shuo` as a boy until Yan Shuo'er becomes strong enough to choose and protect her own reveal.

This is a concealment decision made by frightened parents with limited power, not a cultivation nerf.

## 4. The restriction/mask

The cover uses ordinary and low-level methods, not a divine seal:

- public paperwork and name shortening;
- clothes, haircut, posture, and habit;
- family discipline around voice, expression, and aura;
- minor legal/medical privacy handling through Yan Ke's careful paperwork;
- low-level aura-dulling/heat-control accessories or habits if needed.

The restriction is not allowed to nerf her core cultivation.

Correct effect:

- hides/reduces outward beauty and phoenix aura;
- makes public presentation more boyish;
- teaches restraint and control;
- causes fatigue or discomfort if held too rigidly after major breakthroughs.

Wrong effect:

- sealing away her Fire Phoenix power;
- lowering her actual rank/SP;
- making soul spirits weaker;
- preventing perfect absorption;
- making her less herself.

After the second soul spirit/ring, the mask becomes harder to maintain because her body, martial soul, bloodline, and soul spirits are more complete. It remains a disguise and discipline layer, not a power seal.
