## 1. Core law

Formal Soul Power rank matters, but it is not actual combat power by itself.

Actual combat result must be calculated from:

- martial soul grade/category;
- bloodline purity/species hierarchy;
- attribute quality and elemental restraint;
- rank;
- ring color/age/skill quality;
- soul spirit age, compatibility, absorption, and living-foundation effect;
- Spiritual Power/control;
- body/frame/wings;
- active state layer;
- terrain/environment;
- team formation/support;
- tactics;
- cost/stamina;
- rules/restrictions;
- opponent matchup.

If canon would require ignoring this calculation, adapt canon. Do not weaken established continuity.

## 4. Phoenix / Dragon / fire hierarchy

Phoenix is not dragon-lite. True Phoenix and true Dragon are both top mythical branches.

Yan does not automatically dominate superior true Dragons, Dragon God-level beings, divine beasts, or much higher-realm pure dragon opponents.

But Yan must naturally suppress inferior fire beasts, impure dragon-flame beasts, pseudo-dragon beasts, lizard-dragon creatures, and fire-dragon-type targets whose bloodline/attribute quality is below true awakened Fire Phoenix + Ultimate Fire.

Suppression hierarchy:

- strongest against lower birds, avian beasts, fire-birds, and inferior Phoenix-like lines;
- strong against inferior fire and ordinary fire beasts;
- moderate but real against pseudo-dragon, lizard-dragon, impure dragon-flame, and lower fire-dragon-type targets;
- weakest/contested against true Dragons, superior dragon bloodlines, Dragon God-level routes, or divine dragon authority.

Fire-vs-fire rule:

- inferior flame bends, thins, scatters, or loses coherence;
- the opponent’s fire aura becomes harder to maintain;
- fire instincts hesitate/recoil;
- target choice can destabilize;
- heat environments often help Yan rather than only harming him;
- physical danger may remain through mass, claws, numbers, terrain, and shock.

Correct shorthand: **their fire bows; their bodies may still be dangerous.**
