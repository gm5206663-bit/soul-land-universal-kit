## 1. Core definition

Yan Shuo'er has a passive, always-working adaptation foundation.

It is:

- native-born, not meta knowledge;
- present from the womb/infancy;
- unnamed in-story by characters;
- not a voice, screen, system, or explicit skill;
- not public knowledge;
- not full immunity;
- not instant mastery.

It works by helping her body, meridians, breath, heat, recovery, and training memory adjust under repeated pressure.

Infancy/childhood signs:

- sensitivity to warmth/cold;
- unusual steadiness with Lin Mei's fire-bird lineage;
- body learning not to waste heat;
- breath/movement correction before conscious explanation;
- recovery and appetite changes;

No character should call this a talent by name unless later deliberately discovered.

## 3. Training expression

The adaptation foundation improves through:

- repetition;
- correct pressure;
- recovery;

It converts experience into better fit rather than granting free victory.
