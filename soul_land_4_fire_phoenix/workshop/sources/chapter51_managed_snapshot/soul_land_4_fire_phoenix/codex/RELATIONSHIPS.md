## 2A. Trust/firewall interpretation

The identity and mechanics firewalls must not be written as emotional distance.

Correct reading:

- Lan and Yan trust each other deeply for their age.
- They often cannot tell the whole truth because adults created the cover, bloodline mechanics are dangerous, and public academy/Shrek pressure makes exposure risky.
- Not telling everything is not betrayal.
- Asking less can be care; waiting can be trust.
- Yan's changes since childhood should be felt by Lan through familiarity even when he lacks the private explanation.
- Lan's changes should likewise be felt by Yan through long understanding even when Lan's Nana/bloodline/natural-ring secrets remain hidden.

Wrong reading:

- "They have secrets, so they do not trust each other."
- "No confession yet, so the bond is normal friendship."
- "Public Yan Shuo identity means Lan only cares about a mask."
- "Slow-burn means barely developed."
