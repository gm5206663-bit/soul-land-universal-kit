# Chapter 26: The Test That Chose a Target

Ji Hongbin stood beside the simulation cabin with his arms crossed and his face darker than the cabin lid.

Yin Tianfan pretended not to notice.

This was impossible, because Ji Hongbin's silence had weight. It was not the silence of a person who had nothing to say. It was the silence of a person storing every sentence until it could be thrown with interest.

Inside the cabin, Lan Xuanyu's breathing came through the monitor in short, controlled lines.

The first time Lan Xuanyu had entered this warship program, his body had treated the universe as an enemy. The turns had ripped balance from him. The impacts had driven his stomach into revolt. Even after the cabin opened, the world had continued spinning as if space itself had followed him out.

Now the boy still suffered.

Ji Hongbin could see that clearly.

Lan Xuanyu's face in the monitoring window was pale. His shoulders trembled each time the simulated warship changed direction too sharply. His fingers gripped the restraint rails until the knuckles whitened. Sweat collected at his temples, then ran back because the cabin tilted before it could fall.

But he was no longer merely enduring.

He was reading.

Not well. Not enough to understand Yin Tianfan's piloting. No child at this age should understand that. But his eyes had stopped chasing every light outside the window. His breathing shortened before sharp turns instead of after them. When the warship passed through the meteorite belt and the virtual stars twisted, Lan Xuanyu's torso adjusted half a breath earlier than Ji expected.

The warship fired.

Turned.

Dropped.

Rolled.

Lan Xuanyu's face went whiter.

He did not vomit.

Yin Tianfan's mouth twitched upward.

Ji Hongbin wanted to hit him.

The warship program finally ended. The cabin lid opened with a faint hiss. Lan Xuanyu did not rush to stand. He stayed seated, eyes closed, one hand still holding the rail, and waited until the worst of the dizziness passed.

Ten breaths.

Then he opened his eyes, braced one palm against the cabin wall, and stood slowly.

His knees shook.

He remained standing.

Ji Hongbin's expression changed despite himself.

Lan Xuanyu looked like a child who had just been punished by the sky, but he also looked like a child who had learned where the next punishment would come from.

That was the dangerous part.

"Well?" Yin Tianfan asked.

Ji Hongbin glanced at him. "You are still mad."

"That was not the question."

"It is always the question when you are involved."

Yin Tianfan snorted. "Say what you saw."

Ji Hongbin looked back at Lan Xuanyu. The boy was taking slow breaths, trying not to show too much discomfort in front of two teachers. That was childish pride. It was also useful pride.

"His adaptation is better than I expected," Ji said at last. "His body is learning the pressure. His Spiritual Power is being forced to stay awake under conditions most children would simply reject."

Yin Tianfan spread his hands as if the universe had finally admitted he was reasonable.

Ji's eyes hardened again. "That does not mean your method is safe."

"Safe methods raise safe students."

"Safe students live long enough to become strong students."

"Careful students become careful adults. Then careful adults spend their lives asking why the sky never lowered itself for them." Yin Tianfan's smile thinned. For a moment, the usual lazy roundness in his face could not hide the old edge underneath. "Old Ji, you and I both know where ordinary roads end."

The room quieted.

Lan Xuanyu did not understand every word, but he understood enough to lower his head and pretend he was still dizzy.

Ji Hongbin's mouth tightened. "Do not put your regrets on a child."

"I put food in him," Yin Tianfan said. "And pressure. And I watch whether the two become waste or strength. He eats what other children cannot eat because his body needs it. He consumes it quickly because his meridians are using it. His appetite is a report more honest than most machines."

Lan Xuanyu's ears warmed.

He had doubted whether his teacher had truly been monitoring him. Hearing his own hunger described as a measuring tool made him feel both relieved and embarrassed.

Yin Tianfan pointed at him. "See? He understands."

"He is ashamed," Ji said.

"Also useful."

"You are impossible."

"I am effective."

Ji Hongbin looked as if he wished effectiveness were illegal.

Yin continued before Ji could start another argument. "For now, I only want him to become used to Soul Guidance warship pressure. Years, not days. When his foundation can carry more, he will learn the next thing. I am not throwing him into command tomorrow."

"Good," Ji said. "For once, speak like that before I have to threaten you."

"Your threats lack poetry."

"My threats have survived because they do not need poetry."

Lan Xuanyu nearly smiled, then remembered that smiling might draw attention.

Ji Hongbin walked over and placed one hand on his shoulder. The pressure was not heavy, but Lan Xuanyu straightened at once.

"Next term," Ji said, "the class will begin battle armor lessons. With your Spiritual Power, you should consider a secondary profession connected to battle armor design. Do not think warship piloting removes the need to understand your own body and equipment."

"Yes, Teacher Ji."

"And do not let this fatty make you think suffering is cultivation by itself. Suffering without correction is only damage."

Yin Tianfan protested, "Who are you calling fatty in front of my disciple?"

Lan Xuanyu kept his eyes forward.

Disciple.

The word still felt too large.

Ji Hongbin left after one more warning look, and Yin Tianfan watched the door close with an expression that was half anger and half old loneliness.

Then he turned back.

"Again."

Lan Xuanyu froze.

"Teacher..."

"One more round."

Lan Xuanyu stared at the simulation cabin as if it had betrayed him by existing.

Somewhere in his heart, a small, sincere wish formed: Teacher Ji, please never come to watch again.

---

The end of term arrived without asking whether anyone was ready.

For the Elite Junior Class, readiness was not a feeling. It was a record. It was a number on a testing screen. It was the time a child lasted between closing metal walls. It was the speed of a white spear, the Spiritual Power value under a helmet, the rank written beside a name, and the team standing behind that name when elimination came looking for three people at once.

One whole team would leave.

The rule had been known since the beginning, but a known knife did not feel sharp until it was placed on the table.

In the last month, the class had changed shape again.

Dorm 333 remained low profile. Lan Xuanyu disappeared for meals and after school, returned with better color and worse exhaustion, and sometimes stared at ordinary lecture boards as if they were far away. Qian Lei talked more loudly when he wanted people not to notice worry. Liu Feng trained until his movements looked less like a boy running fast and more like a line being pulled tight.

Lu Qianxun trained with two yellow rings and the pride of a Golden Lion King that had been denied a crown once and had not forgiven the world for it.

Dorm 336 trained under a different kind of attention.

Publicly, Yan Shuo had become impossible to ignore.

Rank 23. Two rings. Deep yellow and deep purple when released together. A class monitor whose team had placed second in the Maze. A Fire Phoenix route that no longer looked only like flame and feather, because a heavier line now lived under every step.

Privately, Yan Shuo'er discovered that victory over the Rank 20 wall did not make mornings kinder.

The first week after the second ring, sleeping on her back was a mistake. The new frame complained from heel to hip to spine to shoulder as if every bone had been given a stern teacher. The collar clasp still dulled warmth and red-gold glimmer well enough for school, but not as easily as before. If she forced the public posture too long, the place between her shoulder blades ached as if the Dawn-Iron Phoenix Roc had set a quiet stone there and asked why she insisted on carrying it wrongly.

Lin Mei noticed every time.

Yan Ke noticed the bills.

Food disappeared faster at home. Recovery medicine that had once lasted a month now had to be counted by the week. Meditation took longer to feel complete because the soul power had farther to travel and more places to fill. When Yan Shuo'er cultivated correctly, the return was astonishing: heat cleaned itself faster, pale-gold light settled along feather-lines without waste, and the earth-metal frame turned impact into instruction instead of bruise.

When she cultivated incorrectly, everything hurt with great honesty.

More than three times the support for one future level.

That sentence had sounded like a measurement in the Spirit Pagoda room.

At home, it sounded like her father reviewing accounts twice after dinner while pretending he was only checking the weather.

Yan Shuo'er hated that part most.

"I can slow down," she said once.

Lin Mei looked up from a bowl of medicinal broth. "Can you?"

Yan wanted to say yes.

Dawnflame Kite stirred under her ribs. The Dawn-Iron Phoenix Roc remained silent, which was worse. A cliff did not argue when someone said they could become lighter by wishing.

Yan Ke set down his account tablet. "Growth is not theft from us. Hiding cost until it becomes injury would be."

"I do not want to be expensive," Yan muttered.

Her father smiled tiredly. "Then become worth it."

Lin Mei kicked him under the table.

"Ow."

"Phrase things like a father, not a loan officer."

Yan laughed before she could stop herself.

The laugh loosened something in her chest, and the two soul spirits settled again.

At school, Dorm 336 learned the new rules of standing near her.

Song Yichen discovered that Yan's first instinct after gaining a deep-purple ring was not to hide behind him less, but to ask more precisely where his shield line ended.

"You are heavier now," Song said during one formation review.

Luo Haoran choked. "That is rude."

"In combat terms."

"Still sounds dangerous."

Yan Shuo, sitting cross-legged on the training mat with one hand pressed to the floor, considered the word. "He is right."

Luo blinked. "You are agreeing with being called heavy?"

"The frame has weight. If I move like before, I steal space from both of you."

Song nodded. "Your recoil used to spread outward through flame. Now it returns through the floor and line. If I stand too close at the wrong angle, I catch the wrong half of it."

Luo pointed at him. "This is why people think tortoises are old men even when they are children."

"This is why mantises lose legs," Song said mildly.

Yan smiled.

It did not last long.

The end-term test was waiting.

---

Mu Zhongtian stood on the platform in front of the class with the expression of a teacher who had seen thirty talented children become thirty nervous problems.

"The end-term examination begins today," he said.

No one whispered.

That alone showed how serious the day was.

"The rules remain centered on teams. One team will be eliminated from the Elite Junior Class. This is not because we dislike any of you. It is because an elite class is not a place where talent can sleep safely just because it once entered the door."

Several children swallowed.

Qian Lei's face looked unusually solemn, which meant he was either frightened or hungry. Possibly both.

"Morning tests: physical fitness and Spiritual Power. The team receiving the most additional recognition from these tests will be safe from elimination. Afternoon test: actual combat. The team judged most outstanding in actual combat will also be safe. Individual talent matters, but if your team fails because you ignored your teammates, that is also your result."

The words crossed the room differently than they had at midterm.

At midterm, Dorm 333 had been a joke waiting to be removed until four plus signs had turned the joke into a rule no one could laugh at.

Now no one knew where to look first.

Dorm 333 had the miracle record.

Dorm 316 had Lu Qianxun's second ring.

Dorm 336 had Yan Shuo standing in front with a public file that said Rank 23 and two rings, while Song and Luo stood behind him with the stiffness of boys who knew other teams had started counting them as obstacles instead of classmates.

Mu Zhongtian's gaze passed over all three teams.

"Begin."

The strength apparatus had not changed.

That made it worse.

The two metal walls waited exactly as they had months ago. They did not care about rumors, pride, team names, or who had spoken bravely the night before. They only cared whether the body between them could resist pressure when soul power was forbidden.

Lan Xuanyu entered again.

The room remembered.

So did he.

This time the teachers did not let him grind himself to the edge of collapse for spectacle. When his body settled into resistance and the timer passed the point needed for additional recognition, the invigilator stopped the test before stubbornness could become injury.

"Lan Xuanyu. Strength component, additional recognition retained."

Dorm 333 exhaled.

Lu Qianxun entered later.

The Golden Lion King did not appear, but pride did. After breaking Rank 20, even without active soul power, his body had changed. He held the pressure with a steadiness that made several students shift uneasily. Five minutes came. The invigilator stopped him too.

Lu walked out without smiling.

He did not need to.

Then came Yan Shuo.

Publicly, he stepped into the apparatus as the class monitor, back straight, collar clasp dull and ordinary at the throat, uniform neat enough that Luo had whispered earlier, "If we are eliminated, at least your buttons will pass."

Privately, Yan Shuo'er listened to both soul spirits settle.

No soul power.

No Dawnfeather Mantle.

No Skybone Phoenix Frame.

The rules were clear.

But rules that turned off active skills did not remove the months and rings that had taught the body how to exist.

The walls began to close.

At first, the pressure was familiar. Legs. Hips. Back. Shoulders. Breath.

The old Yan Shuo would have wanted to answer from flame. The post-Chapter-25 body wanted to answer from the floor. Yan let the soles feel the platform. Earth-brown steadiness did not appear as light. Iron-grey lines did not show. Nothing visible broke the rule.

Inside, the memory of structure guided where strain should travel.

The walls pressed harder.

Her shoulders almost jumped ahead.

She corrected before Ji Hongbin's voice could exist in memory.

Feet first.

Waist second.

Back third.

Shoulders last.

The timer climbed.

Students stopped whispering.

Five minutes.

The invigilator stopped the test.

Yan Shuo stepped out with a calm face and a back that wanted to call him a liar.

"Strength, full mark," the invigilator said, then looked at the note screen and added carefully, "No active soul power detected. Structural foundation excellent. No additional plus sign."

A few students made confused sounds.

Luo muttered, "I would like to complain on behalf of excellence."

Song said, "Do not."

Yan did not complain.

Not every recognition needed the same shape. A plus sign was not a candy handed out because a child wanted one. Dorm 333's plus signs had changed their fate because they proved rare things the academy needed to protect. Yan Shuo's public file was already carrying enough attention to make hallways bend.

More attention was not always a gift.

Speed belonged to Liu Feng.

Everyone knew it before the test began, and the test only made knowing it official again. Liu Feng crossed the measured course like a pale line pulled through air, sharper than at midterm, cleaner after months of training beside Lan Xuanyu's strange pressure and Qian Lei's unreliable miracles. He earned the speed additional recognition without needing to look back.

Yan Shuo's speed remained strong, efficient, and almost irritatingly controlled. The new frame helped landing and direction changes, but it also punished greedy steps. Without active soul power, he could not use the deep-purple ring's visible structure, and he refused to pretend otherwise.

Speed score: nine point five.

Luo looked offended on Yan's behalf again.

"If your face stays like that," Yan said quietly, "Teacher Mu will make you run the course until it improves."

Luo immediately relaxed his face into an expression so fake that Song looked away.

The Soul Power records were simpler and crueler.

Lan Xuanyu: Rank 16.

The teachers had known. His classmates had guessed. Hearing it still created a ripple, because every other part of him argued with the number. A boy who could push walls, change battles, and pull Dorm 333 from last to first should not sit at the bottom of Soul Power.

But the record did not blink.

Rank 16.

Qian Lei and Liu Feng: Rank 17.

Average class level: around Rank 18.

Many students had reached Rank 19.

Lu Qianxun: Rank 20, two rings.

Then the academy screen listed Yan Shuo's existing Spirit Pagoda-verified record.

Rank 23.

Two rings.

The room did not gasp as loudly as it had when the deep-purple ring first appeared, because everyone already knew. But numbers written during an exam had a different pressure from rumors spoken in corridors. Rumor could be exaggerated. A test screen was an adult saying the world had agreed.

Lu Qianxun did not look at Yan.

Yan did not look at Lu.

That was almost worse.

The Spiritual Power helmets waited last.

Qian Lei tried to look casual when his turn came, which made him look like a person carrying a bowl filled to the rim while pretending there was no soup.

The number rose quickly.

One hundred.

One hundred fifty.

One hundred eighty.

It slowed and stopped at one hundred eighty-seven.

"Qian Lei," the invigilator announced, unable to hide approval, "Spiritual Power, one hundred eighty-seven. Excellent improvement. Additional recognition."

Qian Lei's face lit up.

Then he glanced toward Lan Xuanyu with the anxious happiness of a friend who wanted to be first and also wanted not to be alone.

Yan Shuo watched from Dorm 336's line.

One hundred eighty-seven was not a small number. It was a statement. For a child their age, it was the kind of number that made teachers think of future rooms, future resources, future titles. Yan could respect that without wanting it for himself. Her road was not Qian Lei's road.

Still, respect did not make the gap feel weightless.

Yan's own helmet test came before Lan Xuanyu's.

The metal band settled around the head. A faint, cool pressure touched the inside of the skull. The testing stimulus called, and Yan Shuo'er answered while the public file waited outside her name.

The number rose past the old midterm value of eighty-eight without pausing.

Past the Spirit Pagoda pre-fusion recheck of one hundred twelve.

Past the post-fusion stabilization of one hundred forty-two.

It slowed at one hundred fifty.

For a breath, Yan felt Dawnflame Kite brighten and the Dawn-Iron Phoenix Roc become very still. The new frame wanted the mind to be a frame too: not scattered, not overbright, not trying to climb because someone else had climbed.

The number stopped.

One hundred fifty-six.

"Yan Shuo," the invigilator said, "Spiritual Power, one hundred fifty-six. Spirit Connection Realm. Strong post-fusion progress, below the middle-phase threshold. Full mark for the category. No additional plus sign."

Full mark.

No plus.

One hundred fifty-six.

Yan exhaled.

It was enough to prove growth.

It was not enough to become someone else's story.

Song's later record updated to Rank 19 and Spiritual Power sixty-three. He accepted the numbers with the serious nod of someone receiving shield dimensions.

Luo Haoran reached Rank 17 and Spiritual Power seventy-four, then spent the next minute whispering that seventy-four was a suspiciously handsome number until Song asked what a handsome number was supposed to do in combat.

"Distract the enemy," Luo said.

"It is distracting me. Stop."

Dorm 336 had grown.

No one could call them filler.

But the room's attention still turned when Lan Xuanyu sat beneath the helmet.

It always did.

Lan Xuanyu looked tired before the test began. Not weak. Tired. There was a difference that most children did not bother to learn. His face had the pale steadiness of someone who had been made to face fear so often that fear had become part of the daily schedule.

The helmet settled.

The number rose.

One hundred fifty.

Several teachers leaned forward.

One hundred eighty.

Qian Lei's mouth opened.

One hundred ninety.

The room forgot how to breathe properly.

One hundred ninety-three.

One hundred ninety-four.

One hundred ninety-five.

Yan Shuo's fingers curled once, then relaxed.

He is going to pass me, she thought, then corrected herself because that had already happened.

He is going somewhere I cannot see.

One hundred ninety-nine.

The number stopped.

Disappointment crossed the teachers' faces and immediately turned into awe because one hundred ninety-nine was a ridiculous number to disappoint anyone.

Lan Xuanyu sat very still.

Inside him, the world had gone quiet.

The testing pressure no longer felt like a knock. It felt like a door opening into a room he had not known was part of his own house. Gold and silver turned in his chest, no longer only colors passing each other but rhythms that could be watched. At their center, the tiny colorful point became clearer than before.

Blue.

Red.

Green.

Yellow.

Water. Fire. Wind. Earth.

He did not name them because a teacher told him to. He knew them because, for that breath, they were simply there.

A soft tremor crossed his sea of consciousness.

His mind cleared as if someone had washed dust from glass.

The number moved.

Two hundred.

The teachers erupted before the students understood why.

"Middle phase of Spirit Connection!"

"Two hundred!"

"Lan Xuanyu, Spiritual Power two hundred points," the invigilator announced, voice sharper than usual. "Spirit Connection Realm, middle phase. Additional recognition."

The words hit the class in layers.

Two hundred was not Spirit Sea. The teachers said that clearly. Spirit Sea was far higher, an entirely different threshold. But within Spirit Connection, two hundred was the middle dividing line, the kind of step that said the mind had crossed a door early.

Lan Xuanyu had crossed it first.

Qian Lei stared as if betrayed by mathematics, then broke into a grin so wide it nearly split his face.

"You surpassed me," he whispered, devastated and delighted at once.

Liu Feng's eyes burned brighter than his words.

Dorm 333 had four plus signs again.

They were ashore.

Again.

No one could laugh at them.

Again.

Yan Shuo stood in the Dorm 336 line and looked at Lan Xuanyu's pale face when the helmet came off.

The first feeling was pride.

It arrived before thought, warm and familiar, the same kind of warmth that had once made two children sit closer on apartment floors while adults spoke of things they did not understand.

The second feeling was worry.

Lan looked clearer, yes, but also far away. Whatever door had opened inside him had not opened in the classroom where Yan could see it. She saw only the number, the teachers' excitement, Qian Lei's expression, Liu Feng's tightened hands.

The third feeling was sharper.

Not jealousy exactly.

Not fear.

A wish to stand close enough that the distance did not become a wall.

Yan Shuo'er did not know what to call that wish. She was still a child. Lan was still a child. Some feelings were only seeds that had not decided whether they were flowers, thorns, or simply roots holding the ground together.

So public Yan Shuo did the only safe thing.

When Lan looked over, Yan nodded once.

Well done.

Lan smiled faintly back.

Still here.

Neither said anything.

The class did not need to know how much could pass through a small silence.

---

Dorm 316 closed its door before Lu Qianxun spoke.

Ye Lingtong stood with her arms folded. Chang Jianyi sat at the small table with the test records open. Lu remained standing.

Rank 20.

Two rings.

Golden Lion King.

None of those facts had disappeared.

They simply did not feel like enough to make the world arrange itself obediently anymore.

"We need first place in actual combat," Lu said.

Ye nodded. "Of course."

Chang Jianyi did not answer immediately.

Lu looked at him. "Say it."

"Lan Xuanyu is still the key to Dorm 333," Chang said.

Ye's jaw tightened.

Lu's eyes narrowed. "His Spiritual Power?"

"Not only that. The Maze proved it. Your Golden Lion Roar should have ended them. Ye lost battle power. You weakened after releasing the roar. Liu Feng pierced the opening. Qian Lei's summoned girl blocked more than she should have in the earlier fight. Each part by itself looks strange. Together, it points to Lan Xuanyu."

Ye looked down.

"When we were younger," she said slowly, "my martial soul always felt wrong against him. Not every time in the same way. But wrong. Pressed. Restrained. My father taught me to hold soul power inward so I would not collapse as easily. It helped, but it never made the feeling vanish."

Lu's pride did not like the shape of that sentence, but pride was useless if it refused information.

"Then we remove Lan Xuanyu first," he said.

Chang tapped the record screen once. "Yes. If we face Dorm 333, we do not chase Liu Feng. We do not waste time on Qian Lei unless the summon is already forming and we can interrupt safely. We crush the center before he makes the others more dangerous."

Ye asked, "And Dorm 336?"

The room stilled differently.

Yan Shuo's name was not on the screen in front of Chang Jianyi, but the public record might as well have been written on the air.

Rank 23.

Deep yellow and deep purple.

Skybone Phoenix Frame.

Chang leaned back. "Yan Shuo is dangerous because he is visible. We know the second ring is structural. We know the first ring is fire-light. We know he is newly advanced and not perfectly fluent yet. That can be planned around."

"Visible does not mean easy," Ye said.

"I did not say easy. I said different. Against Dorm 336, separate the frame from the team. Force Yan Shuo to choose between anchoring Song's shield line and answering Luo's flank. Make him move before he has rooted. Do not trade cleanly into the frame. Do not let fire-light sit over bone."

Lu listened.

Chang's eyes returned to Dorm 333's record. "Against Dorm 333, the problem is that the visible numbers lie. Lan Xuanyu is Rank 16. That should mean weak. It does not. Qian Lei's summon should be unreliable. Around Lan, it is not. Liu Feng should be fast but fragile. Around Lan, his spear finds places it should not reach."

"So Lan first," Lu said again.

This time it was not anger.

It was a plan.

Ye looked toward the closed door as if she could see the arena waiting beyond it.

"Then this time," she said, "we do not give him time to roar."

---

Lunch tasted like waiting.

That was Qian Lei's opinion, loudly stated before Liu Feng told him food did not have strategic emotions.

"It does today," Qian Lei said. "This bun knows something."

Lan Xuanyu took the bun from him and ate it.

"Betrayal," Qian Lei said.

"You said it knew something. I am investigating."

Liu Feng's mouth moved once. It might have been a smile.

When they carried their trays back, Lan Xuanyu passed close enough to Dorm 336 for Yan Shuo to speak without making it a scene.

"Two hundred," Yan said.

Lan's ears reddened slightly. "I did not know it would happen."

"That makes it more annoying."

Lan blinked, then smiled because the words sounded exactly like old courtyard justice wearing an academy uniform.

Yan lowered his voice. "Teacher Yin's training?"

Lan's smile twisted. "Some of it. Maybe. It is hard to tell what helps and what only makes the world spin."

"Still the simulator?"

"Still the simulator," Lan admitted. "I cannot say the lesson. I can say I hate the part where space decides I am food."

Yan almost laughed, then stopped because Lan looked tired enough that laughter needed permission.

"Teacher Nana would say you should breathe before the turn," Yan said.

Lan's eyes warmed at the name. "She would. Then she would already be behind me when I turned around."

For a breath, neither of them spoke. Nana's absence sat there too, familiar and old, not a secret between them even if the deeper things around her remained beyond Yan's reach.

"If you go too far," Yan said, "say something before you disappear into teacher stuff."

Lan nodded once. "I will say what I can."

It was not everything.

It was enough to be friendship instead of a locked door.

Across the canteen, Yan Shuo sat with Dorm 336 and did not look too often at Dorm 333.

Luo noticed anyway. Luo noticed many things when food did not fully occupy him.

"Are you worried about them?"

Yan drank water before answering. "Everyone should be worried today."

"That was not an answer."

"It was a correct statement."

Song looked between them. "Dorm 333 is safe from elimination after this morning."

"Safe is not the same as unhurt," Yan said.

Luo's expression softened, then immediately tried to hide that by stealing one of Song's vegetables.

Song moved the bowl before he succeeded.

"Your compassion makes you slower," Luo complained.

"Your theft makes me faster."

Yan listened to them and let the ordinary argument settle him.

Under the table, one hand rested briefly near the collar clasp. The morning tests had not exposed anything, but the pressure of so many eyes had made the mask feel thin. Full marks. Rank 23. SP156. No new plus sign. Deep yellow and deep purple in everyone's memory, even if the rings had not been released during the written parts of the test.

The public Yan Shuo file was carrying more weight now.

So was she.

The weight did not frighten her as much as it had before.

That frightened her a little.

---

In the afternoon, Mu Zhongtian announced the actual-combat venue.

"Arena."

Relief passed through the class like wind through grass.

Arena meant open space. Arena meant no hidden maze walls, no stone aspects appearing from blind corners, no strange paths where cleverness could make strength stumble. Arena meant face the opponent and see who stood.

Then Mu Zhongtian continued.

"This examination will use a defending-champion format. Dorm 333 took first in the midterm actual combat and has received the class's strongest rare-quality recognition through plus signs. They will defend. The other nine teams will draw lots and challenge them in sequence. There will be nine matches. After each match, the simulator will restore combat state, but Spiritual Power fatigue will not be erased completely. Performance will be evaluated across the whole sequence. A team that defeats Dorm 333 will receive high evaluation. Dorm 333's own evaluation will depend on how many matches they can win and how they win them."

The room needed time to understand.

Qian Lei understood first and almost visibly lost his soul.

"Teacher," he said, raising a hand with the tragic dignity of someone asking whether execution could be postponed, "can we surrender? We are already safe, right?"

Mu Zhongtian's face darkened. "You may surrender any individual match if you choose. But if Dorm 333 loses more than half of the nine battles, all plus-sign privileges for next term will be canceled and your next-term total evaluation will be reduced by thirty percent."

Qian Lei's hand fell.

"We will not surrender," Liu Feng said quietly.

Lan Xuanyu did not speak right away.

He was looking at the platform floor, thinking.

Nine battles.

Soul Power restored. Spiritual Power not fully restored. Qian Lei's summon could not be treated as infinite. Liu Feng's speed could not be spent carelessly. Lan himself could not lean on brute Soul Power because he did not have enough to waste.

It was unfair.

It was also a chance.

Lan lifted his head. "We will fight."

Mu Zhongtian gave the smallest nod.

Around the room, other students reacted differently.

Some were relieved not to be the target.

Some envied the honor.

Lu Qianxun's eyes burned. Being defending champion meant the school had placed a team at the center and asked everyone else to measure themselves against it. Lu wanted that position so fiercely that wanting it almost had a sound.

Yan Shuo understood the sound.

Dorm 336 had placed second in the Maze. Yan had Rank 23. If the test were only public strength, there would have been an argument for putting Dorm 336 at the center too.

But this was not only about strength.

Dorm 333 had four plus signs, a first-place actual-combat record, the class's strangest support structure, and now Lan Xuanyu's SP200 middle Spirit Connection breakthrough. The academy was not simply rewarding them. It was squeezing them until their shape showed.

A target was also a mirror.

Luo leaned closer to Yan and whispered, "Nine matches. I almost feel bad for them."

Song said, "Almost?"

"I am saving the rest in case we draw early."

Yan did not smile this time.

"If we draw them," Yan said softly, "we fight properly."

Luo sobered.

Song nodded once.

Friendship did not mean pretending a test was not a test.

The lots were drawn.

Dorm 304 would enter first.

Dorm 336's lot was not first.

Yan felt both relief and disappointment, and decided neither feeling was useful yet.

The students moved toward the simulation pods.

Mu Zhongtian's instructions followed them.

"Remember: this is still a test, not a grudge match. The simulator prevents injury, but pain and fear are real enough to teach bad habits if you chase victory stupidly. Fight to show what you know, not what you can ruin."

Qian Lei muttered, "I know fear very well, Teacher. May I submit that as my result?"

No one answered him.

---

The arena formed around Dorm 333 in clean light.

Thirty meters across. Circular. No corridors, no hiding path, no convenient stone wall to turn into strategy. The ground was flat enough to be honest and wide enough that honesty could hurt.

Qian Lei looked around and swallowed. "Do I summon?"

Lan Xuanyu did not answer immediately.

He looked at the empty space opposite them. He looked at Liu Feng, whose spear had already appeared in his hand. He looked at Qian Lei, whose nervousness sat on top of excitement like a lid on boiling water.

"How many times?" Lan asked. "If Soul Power is restored between matches but Spiritual Power is not fully restored, how many summons can you handle?"

Qian Lei's face changed. Numbers gave fear something to hold.

"It depends what comes. If I try to summon Dong Qianqiu, maybe five times if she is willing. But that is the problem. She may not answer every time, especially if we keep calling her."

Liu Feng's eyes sharpened. "And the other method?"

Qian Lei glanced at Lan's hands.

Lan understood.

Left hand, silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass, had once produced one kind of amplification.

Right hand, golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass, produced another.

The Gate of Summoning did not answer both the same way.

"With the right-hand enhancement," Qian Lei said, "I should manage about three solid times. Maybe. It drains me badly, but if Soul Power recovers, Spiritual Power is the limit."

"Then first several matches, we rely on your summons," Lan said. "Liu Feng and I conserve as much as possible. Later, when your Spiritual Power drops, we take more of the burden. If Dong Qianqiu answers once, that can secure one match, but we do not build the whole plan on someone outside the arena being willing to rescue us nine times."

Qian Lei nodded hard. "I like plans where I am important early and allowed to sit later."

"You will meditate later, not sit."

"Cruel details."

Liu Feng stepped half a pace ahead. "If Lu Qianxun's team comes early?"

Lan's eyes narrowed. "Then we deal with the strongest pressure before we are exhausted. That would be best. If they come late, we must still have something left."

Light began gathering across the arena.

"They're here," Liu Feng said.

The three of them moved without another word.

Liu Feng stood forward, White Dragon Spear angled low.

Lan Xuanyu took the center.

Qian Lei retreated behind him, the Summoning Money appearing in his palm as his yellow ring rose.

"Come out," Qian Lei breathed, then louder, because fear liked sound, "Gate of Summoning!"

The coin flew.

Light fell from above and joined it. A door formed in the air, old and strange and completely unreasonable no matter how many times Lan had seen it.

Lan Xuanyu's Blue Silver Grass appeared over both arms.

For a moment he hesitated.

Then his right hand, covered in golden-patterned vines, gripped the gate frame.

The door shivered.

Qian Lei sucked in a breath as if something had pulled at the back of his mind.

A low sound came from beyond the gate.

Not the cold answer of distance.

Not Dong Qianqiu's sharp arrival.

A rough, heavy call rolled through the opening, deep enough that the arena floor seemed to remember it had been stone in some other life.

Lan Xuanyu's eyes focused.

Liu Feng lowered his stance.

Qian Lei's face slowly changed from terror into wild hope.

Beyond the gate, something answered.

And the first match began.
