# Chapter 42: The Pool That Lied

Lan Xuanyu waited for the left foot to rise.

Under the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon, waiting had its own weight. The beast's belly radiated heat above them. Each breath tasted of scorched stone. Each step shook dust into his hair and made Qian Lei's fingers tighten around his sleeve. Liu Feng held the White Dragon Spear so still that the spear looked more patient than any living person had a right to be.

The left foot lifted.

A gap opened behind it.

Not safety.

A direction.

"Get ready," Lan whispered.

Liu Feng's muscles tightened.

Qian Lei took one breath too loudly.

The foot came down ahead of them.

The Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon's body shifted forward, enormous attention bent toward a cluster of Ground Fire Lizards crawling through cooling ash. Its head lowered. Its tail swung the other way.

Lan's eyes sharpened.

"Go!"

Golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass flared from his right hand and wrapped Liu Feng's waist. At the same moment, Lan caught Qian Lei and threw his own weight forward. Liu Feng moved before the word finished leaving Lan's mouth.

White Dragon Spear flashed.

The three of them shot out from behind the Scarlet Dragon's left foot like a piece of white light escaping the shadow of a mountain.

For one breath, the world opened.

Smoke. Charred earth. Hissing lizards at a distance. The massive tail sweeping somewhere behind them. The empty ground near the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon where even Ground Fire Lizards feared to linger.

Lan did not celebrate.

"Qian Lei, summon. Now."

Qian Lei's face still held the terror of the escape, but his hands moved. The Coin of Summoning spun out, golden light opening into the familiar Gate of Summoning.

"If this gives us a rabbit," he muttered, "I will apologize to the rabbit and then die very resentfully."

"Focus," Liu Feng said.

Liu Feng did not stop moving. Even with White Dragon Return faded, his speed under Lan's amplification remained sharp. He cut through the empty zone around the Scarlet Dragon, aiming for terrain rather than distance. Distance alone meant nothing if the beast noticed them. Terrain might give choices.

Choices were currently more valuable than courage.

A rumble came from the mountain where they had been moments earlier.

Not from the first Dragon.

From above.

Lan turned his head.

A crack split the upper wall near Lu Qianxun's team. Stone burst outward. A gigantic head pushed through the mountain from the other side of the peak, dark red scales scraping rock into sparks. Heat poured from its nostrils.

Another Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon.

Qian Lei's voice went thin. "There are two?"

The second Dragon dragged itself over the broken ridge. Its small wings opened and beat once, not to fly but to control the impossible bulk of its descent. Below it, Lu Qianxun, Ye Lingtong, and Chang Jianyi looked suddenly very small.

Then a golden halo swept across the valley.

It did not come from the sun.

It came from the rules of the test.

The light passed through smoke, stone, lizards, and children. Lan felt it brush his skin like a cold measurement hidden inside warmth. The first Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon stiffened. The second did the same. Their eyes changed.

Before, they had hunted clusters, movement, sky loopholes, and obvious targets.

Now their gazes found people.

Not groups.

People.

Lan's chest tightened.

"Second phase," he said.

No one had announced it.

No one needed to.

Both Scarlet Dragons opened their mouths.

Two fireballs launched toward Lu Qianxun's team, one from below, one from above, crossing the mountain wall like judgment written in flame.

Lu's eyes widened, but his voice did not break.

"Jump!"

He grabbed Chang Jianyi. Ye Lingtong moved with him, white light flashing over her body. The three leaped away from the wall just before the fireballs struck.

The explosion tore the ridge apart.

Falling stone chased them. Chang Jianyi's Slow skill spread upward, dulling the speed of one burning slab. Lu Qianxun's Golden Lion Roar shattered it before it could crush them. Ye Lingtong twisted in midair, palm striking the wall to reduce their fall, body glowing with Celestial steadiness even under heat.

They survived the first exchange.

The second Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon jumped after them.

Its wings opened wide enough to slow its fall, turning descent into pursuit. The shadow of its body covered Lu's team. Ground Fire Lizards surged toward the landing line below, drawn by the same golden-halo hunger that had changed the Dragons.

Lan saw all of it in fragments because Liu Feng was still moving.

Qian Lei saw enough.

His lips whitened. "If we had stayed on the wall..."

"We would be there," Lan said.

There was no comfort in the truth.

The Gate of Summoning continued to open.

Lan forced his attention away from Lu's fight and swept the valley ahead. The first Dragon had moved from its original area, leaving behind scorched earth, scattered lizards, and one feature that had not mattered until now.

A pool of magma.

It glowed red-orange in a basin of black stone. Heat shimmered above it. Ground Fire Lizards crawled from its surface and ran outward as if born from fire.

Lan registered the sight, but not the answer yet.

There was too much happening.

On the far wall, Dorm336 felt the golden halo arrive.

Yan Shuo's skin prickled before the light touched him. It did not feel like an attack. That made it worse. An attack could be resisted. This was recognition. A rule opening its eye.

Luo Haoran's head snapped toward the valley floor. "Targeting changed. It sees us through the small-group rule."

Song Yichen shifted his shield. "How much time?"

"Not enough."

Yan looked toward the broken ridge where Lu's team had jumped. Then toward the huge first Dragon whose head was beginning to turn through smoke. His Fire Phoenix wanted to answer the halo with brilliance, to flare, challenge, burn a path.

The memory of Jin Xiang's team vanishing in the sky killed that impulse.

"No full flight," Yan said.

Luo's tense mouth almost smiled. "Good. I was preparing to shout."

"Shout anyway if I forget."

"I will. Loudly."

Song planted his shield against the wall and lowered his stance. "Return point set."

The first Scarlet Dragon's gaze reached them.

Yan felt it like heat on the back of his neck.

He did not release everything.

Instead, red-gold fire folded close around his body and then stretched only as far as Song and Luo. Dawnfeather light layered low and tight, a heat-break rather than a banner. The Fire Phoenix did not make a sky of itself. It became a narrow roof over three children on a wall.

The Dragon opened its mouth.

"Down-left shelf!" Luo shouted.

They moved.

The fireball struck above them, not at them.

That was worse.

Stone became shrapnel. The wall cracked. Heat and impact slammed downward with a roar that swallowed thought. Song's Stoneplate Tortoise shield took the first rush, his boots grinding lines into the rock. Yan's fire curled over the shield, catching heat before it cooked the air behind it. Luo cut two lizards from the shelf with Threadwind Mantis speed and rolled under a falling slab.

They reached the down-left shelf alive.

Alive was no longer a position.

It was a countdown.

At the Gate of Summoning, a low growl emerged.

A seven-meter creature forced its way through the light. Iron-black scales. Long spikes along its back. Thick limbs and a heavy head. Yellow eyes full of fierce obedience.

Ironback Dragon.

A ground dragon of the Yalong species.

Qian Lei nearly cried from relief. "Not a rabbit. Good dragon. Excellent dragon. I take back everything I said about uncertain fate."

The Ironback Dragon shook itself and snarled at the surrounding Ground Fire Lizards.

Lan exhaled. A familiar summon was better than a surprise. They knew its defense, strength, and limits. It could not solve a ten-thousand-year Scarlet Dragon, but it could solve the small bodies between them and an idea.

"Qian Lei, ride it. Charge forward. Liu Feng, follow with me. We head toward the magma pool."

Qian Lei climbed between two of the Ironback Dragon's back spikes with an agility fear had improved dramatically. "Why the magma pool?"

"Because it is there and everything else is worse."

"That is a very low standard for strategy."

"Move."

The Ironback Dragon charged.

It did not dodge. It did not need to. Ground Fire Lizards that leaped at it struck iron-black scales and bounced away or were flung aside by shoulders, tail, and claws. It became a bulldozer through the swarm. Qian Lei held on with both hands, face stretched between terror and triumph.

Lan kept golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass coiled around Liu Feng.

Liu Feng understood without a word.

Every lizard the Ironback Dragon overturned exposed a softer belly. White Dragon Spear flashed. Under Lan's amplification, spear auras pierced and withdrew with ruthless economy. Points mattered now. Survival mattered more, but Lan had not forgotten Ling Yiyi's words. If the path gave points without slowing them, they would take them.

Behind them, Lu Qianxun's team reached the ground.

For a moment, they fought like the best conventional team the Elite Junior Class had produced.

Lu's Golden Lion King pressure crushed the nearest lizards and blasted openings with roar after roar. Ye Lingtong's Celestial Body endured fire better than most and struck with ancient-martial precision, each punch throwing a Ground Fire Lizard back. Chang Jianyi's Confusion turned several lizards on each other, buying pockets of space that should not have existed under that pressure.

But the second phase did not care about should.

The second Scarlet Dragon landed behind them.

The shockwave knocked half their footing away.

Lu roared again, voice hoarse now. Chang Jianyi's face had gone pale from soul-power expenditure. Ye Lingtong's white glow flickered under too many angles of heat, claws, and falling debris.

They killed lizards until killing stopped meaning escape.

Then the Dragon's shadow covered them.

Three elimination flashes rose through smoke.

Lu Qianxun's team was gone.

Lan saw the flashes over his shoulder.

A tightness passed through him.

Lu had not been weak.

That was the frightening part.

The second Scarlet Dragon lifted its head after the elimination.

The first Scarlet Dragon, still pressuring Dorm336's wall, also turned enough for one burning eye to catch the movement of the Ironback Dragon and Dorm333 cutting toward the magma pool.

For one breath, both monsters' attention threatened to meet on Lan's route.

Yan saw it too.

The shelf under Dorm336's feet was cracked. Lizards came from above and below. Song's shield was already ringing with impacts. Luo's breathing had shortened. Every sensible part of Yan knew they needed every scrap of concealment they could keep.

But if both Dragons chose Dorm333 now, Lan's team would be crushed before the magma pool became anything more than a red reflection in his eyes.

Yan did not know Lan's plan.

He did not know why Lan was running toward magma.

He only knew Lan had seen something.

Trust did not always mean following.

Sometimes it meant buying the other path two breaths.

"Song, brace. Luo, mark me a side exit."

Luo's eyes sharpened. "Yan, don't flare high."

"I won't."

He did not flare high.

He flared narrow.

A red-gold line of Fire Phoenix heat slashed across the broken shelf and into a side ridge, bright enough to catch a Dragon's eye, low enough that it did not become sky-bait. The first Scarlet Dragon's head snapped back toward Dorm336. Its jaws opened with furious recognition.

Lan saw the red-gold line.

He saw the Dragon's head turn away from him.

For half a heartbeat, the valley narrowed to that impossible, reckless gift.

Yan.

Liu Feng's voice cut through. "Xuanyu!"

Lan's eyes snapped forward.

The Ironback Dragon was still charging. The magma pool was ahead. The second Scarlet Dragon was now coming from behind after Lu's elimination.

He could not stop.

He could not waste the two breaths Yan had bought by looking back at them.

He could only use them.

Dorm336 paid for those breaths immediately.

The first Scarlet Dragon's fireball struck the ridge Yan had marked. It did not hit them directly, because Luo had already found the side exit and Song had already anchored the shift. But the explosion broke the shelf beneath them and split the wall open into a glowing seam.

Heat poured out.

Not the same as the Dragon's fire.

Older.

Lower.

Geothermal.

Luo's eyes widened. "Artificial thermal seam. Not natural rock. It has a hollow behind it."

Yan understood faster than he could have in any ordinary test because fire had always been his first language. The heat inside the seam did not behave like true molten death. It was too layered. Too arranged. Too close to the false magma smell that clung to the lizards crawling from below.

Lan was running toward the magma pool.

Ground Fire Lizards crawled out of magma.

Shrek had made death-shaped doors.

"Inside," Yan said.

Song glanced at the glowing crack. "That is not an entrance."

"It is if we make it one."

"Of course it is," Luo muttered. "Why would Shrek use normal doors?"

The first Scarlet Dragon inhaled again.

No time.

Song slammed his shield into the broken seam and widened it by force, Stoneplate Tortoise pressure grinding cracked basalt aside. Luo cut the upper lip of the fissure with mantis-speed strikes, knocking loose shards before they could fall at the wrong angle. Yan stepped behind them and let the Fire Phoenix unfold only halfway.

Red-gold wings opened low around his teammates.

The third ring flickered.

Violet shadow lined the inner feathers for one dangerous breath.

It was not a domain.

It was not invincibility.

It was not mastery that solved all fire.

It was an emergency shape: Purple Flame Eidolon Wing bending heat across the wing surface just long enough to make the side fissure's burning air stop tearing at Song and Luo's lungs. Yan folded his own heat down, matching the geothermal rhythm rather than fighting it, hiding their human warmth inside a louder false volcano.

"Now!" Luo shouted.

Song went first, shield scraping sparks.

Luo slid after him.

Yan entered last.

A fireball smashed the outer shelf as his foot left it.

The entrance collapsed behind them.

For an instant, red-gold and violet light vanished inside black stone.

Not elimination light.

A covered ember.

The first Scarlet Dragon roared at the collapsed fissure, clawing once at the sealed rock. But the golden-halo target it had followed had blurred into the thermal background. False magma heat, Fire Phoenix heat, shattered basalt, and the Dragon's own fire tangled together. There was no exposed human target to strike cleanly.

Inside the fissure, Dorm336 landed hard in a narrow pocket of burning air.

Song hit one knee and kept the shield up even though there was no room to raise it properly. Luo pressed a hand to the wall, eyes watering from heat, and listened through vibration. Yan crouched between them, wings folded tight, breath controlled, copper-red hair lit red-gold by the glow around them.

"Status," Yan said.

"Alive," Song answered.

"Annoyed," Luo added. "Which is better than dead. The seam slopes down. I hear liquid movement beneath us. False magma system, probably connected to the main pool."

Yan's chest hurt from the brief Purple Flame use, and the public mask pressed around his posture even here, absurdly stubborn. He let none of it show in his voice.

"Can we move?"

"Slowly," Luo said. "If we open the wrong heat pocket, we cook. If we go back out, the Dragon remembers us."

Song shifted his shield to wedge the narrow passage behind them. "Then we hold."

Yan listened to the muffled thunder outside.

Somewhere beyond stone, Lan's team was still moving.

"We hold," Yan agreed.

On the valley floor, the second Scarlet Dragon's thunderous footsteps closed behind Dorm333.

Qian Lei looked back from the Ironback Dragon's back and made a sound that suggested his soul had attempted to leave first. "It is coming!"

Lan saw the magma pool ahead.

He saw Ground Fire Lizards still crawling out of it.

He saw the second Scarlet Dragon closing from behind and the first roaring at a collapsed side ridge instead of joining the chase.

He saw no side path, no wall close enough, no sky route, no cluster rule to exploit now that the golden halo had rewritten target priority.

A pool of magma waited in front of them.

Impossible.

Ground Fire Lizards emerged from it.

Impossible.

Lan's thoughts collided and then aligned.

Ground Fire Lizards were low-year soul beasts. Ten-year. Hundred-year at most among most of the swarm. They had fire resistance, yes, and volcanic affinity, but true magma would kill them. Even thousand-year soul beasts would not casually crawl out of molten rock. Only a monster like the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon might endure such heat.

So the magma pool was not true magma.

Or not only magma.

It was a test object disguised as death.

The opportunity.

Lan did not explain all of that.

There was no time.

He ran to the edge and jumped.

"Xuanyu!" Liu Feng shouted.

"Boss!" Qian Lei screamed.

Lan hit the magma pool.

For one terrible instant, heat swallowed everything.

His body stiffened.

Then sensation clarified.

Sticky.

Thick.

Hot, yes, painfully hot, but not lethal. It behaved more like heavy water than molten stone. The temperature might have been sixty or seventy degrees, enough to scald an ordinary person and punish a careless Soul Master, but not enough to dissolve bone. Fire element pressed everywhere, strong and uncomfortable.

Lan released water/ice control along his skin.

Cold mist hissed and vanished immediately, but the tiny boundary it created let him think.

He surfaced.

Liu Feng and Qian Lei stared at him from the shore as if he had returned from death specifically to confuse them.

Behind them, the second Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon rounded the bend.

"Get in!" Lan shouted. "Now!"

Qian Lei did not argue.

That alone proved the seriousness of the situation.

The Ironback Dragon reached the edge and stopped hard, claws scraping stone. It growled at the magma and refused another step. Whatever the test had done to the pool, the summoned creature's instincts hated it.

Qian Lei's eyes widened. "It won't—"

"Send it at them!"

Qian Lei slapped the Ironback Dragon's neck. "Charge! Stop them! Be brave! I will remember you fondly!"

The Ironback Dragon obeyed its summoner. It turned from the pool with a roar and hurled itself toward the second Scarlet Dragon.

It was a brave sight.

It was also a very brief plan.

Liu Feng leaped into the magma next. Qian Lei followed with a face that suggested he had many complaints but no time to file them.

Lan's silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass shot out and wrapped both of them before they hit. Ice fog burst around their bodies, thin and hissing. The magma swallowed them in heavy heat, and both boys jerked before Lan pulled them close.

"Breathe now," Lan said. "Then hold."

They drew deep breaths.

The three submerged.

The world became red darkness.

Heat pressed against eyes, ears, skin, and thought. Lan kept the silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass around Liu Feng and Qian Lei, feeding just enough cold through the contact to keep the heat from overwhelming them. It consumed soul power continuously. Not too fast. Fast enough to matter.

Outside, the Ironback Dragon roared.

Then the sound broke.

Qian Lei's hand twitched as his connection to the summon strained and thinned.

The thunderous footsteps reached the pool.

The three children stayed under.

Lan counted heartbeats.

One.

Two.

Three.

A footfall landed close enough that the magma rocked around them. Qian Lei's cheeks puffed with held breath. Liu Feng's hand tightened on the White Dragon Spear. Lan forced himself not to surface too soon.

If the Dragon could sense them through the pool, they were dead.

If it could not, every second mattered.

The footsteps stopped.

No claws entered.

No fireball struck the magma.

No giant mouth plunged down.

Half a minute passed.

Then another.

Lan's lungs began to burn.

He tugged the Blue Silver Grass upward.

The three surfaced just enough to draw air.

Warm steam filled their mouths. It tasted awful. It was air.

They sank again when the second Dragon's head swung near.

The second Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon stood at the edge of the pool, enormous body shuddering with frustrated heat. Its eyes swept the shore. Its nostrils flared. The human targets had vanished. Ground Fire Lizards skittered at a distance, none daring to come close.

The Dragon did not attack the pool.

Lan's guess had been right.

Again.

Qian Lei looked at him through the red blur with an expression somewhere between worship and resentment.

Lan would have smiled if opening his mouth underwater were not a terrible idea.

Inside the side fissure, Dorm336 learned the same lesson through a different kind of pain.

The heat pocket was not safe.

It was survivable.

That distinction mattered more than any comforting lie.

The passage sloped toward a low chamber where the basalt glowed faintly along cracks. Under the glow, the stone breathed with false magma heat. Song's armor-like shield edge softened at the outer rim but did not fail. Luo found narrow cool intervals in the airflow, timing their breathing so the hottest pulses passed before they inhaled. Yan held the Fire Phoenix heat close, not fighting the furnace but matching it, shaping a thin balance around his teammates.

It was exactly the kind of work the west practice wing had made boring on purpose.

Heat-channel discipline.

Wing-root restraint.

Shield-load timing.

Sensor-lane calls.

Not glorious.

Alive.

"If I ever complain about return-point drills again," Song said through clenched teeth, "remind me of this hole."

"Gladly," Luo replied. "If we leave it."

Yan listened to the Dragon outside clawing at stone, then losing interest. The pressure of the golden halo faded from direct focus to a distant itch.

"It lost us," he said.

"For now," Luo corrected.

"For now."

Yan closed his eyes briefly.

Lan had jumped into magma.

Yan had entered a volcanic crack.

Shrek's doors were unreasonable.

But they were doors.

The ground shook suddenly.

A roar exploded above the pool and through the fissure walls at the same time.

The magma surged, throwing Dorm333 sideways. Lan caught Liu Feng with one vine and Qian Lei with another, pulling them deeper before the surface could expose them.

In the fissure, Song's shield slammed against the wall as the entire chamber jolted. Yan spread a low wing over Luo before a crack spat burning droplets across the floor.

When Lan surfaced the next time, he saw the reason.

The two Ground Fire Scarlet Dragons had begun fighting.

The first had left the collapsed side ridge after failing to recover Dorm336's exposed target. The second had failed to find Dorm333 in the pool. With Lu's team gone, Jin Xiang gone, the lower teams gone, and both remaining human signatures swallowed by false geothermal features, the valley remembered territory.

Or the test had decided to reveal its next door.

Either way, the result was brutal.

One Dragon slammed its head into the other's neck. The second raked claws across dark red scales, tearing long wounds through armor that had seemed invincible to children. Tails swept charred earth into waves. Fire burst from open jaws. The whole shore became a furnace and battlefield at once.

Ground Fire Lizards fled from the area completely.

The magma pool, strangely, remained the calmest place nearby.

Qian Lei and Liu Feng stared.

The terror in their eyes changed slowly into understanding.

They had not found a hiding place.

They had found the place from which to watch giants destroy each other.

Lan's heart beat hard.

Ling Yiyi had not wasted words.

Survival time gave points.

Killing soul beasts gave points.

Different soul beasts gave different points.

There were only two kinds of soul beast here.

Ground Fire Lizards.

And Ground Fire Scarlet Dragons.

No direct force they possessed could kill a healthy Scarlet Dragon.

But the test did not require them to be stronger than a Scarlet Dragon.

It required them to be alive when an opportunity appeared.

In the side fissure, Luo pressed his ear to the stone and went very still.

"They are fighting," he said.

Song stared. "The Dragons?"

"The Dragons. Big impacts. Two patterns. They lost exposed targets and turned on each other."

Yan exhaled slowly.

Lan had found one door.

Dorm336 had found another.

But not all doors led to the same place.

Their fissure was narrow, half-collapsed, and angled away from the main pool. They could survive here, maybe move deeper, maybe wait for the next system change. They could not see the Dragons' eyes. They could not strike through stone. If Yan burst out now, he would expose Song and Luo to two monsters whose battle was tearing the valley apart.

The Fire Phoenix inside him hated waiting.

The team leader in him understood it.

"We hold," Yan said.

Song nodded.

Luo's mouth curved faintly. "That seems to be today's heroic strategy."

"Better than heroic dying."

"Barely less dramatic."

Yan almost laughed.

Outside, two monsters tore each other open under Shrek's golden rules.

Inside the false magma, Lan leaned close to Qian Lei, keeping his voice low despite the roar of battle.

"Get your second soul skill ready. Wait for my signal."

Qian Lei blinked. Then his eyes widened as the thought landed.

"Boss," he whispered, "you are terrifying."

Liu Feng looked from the fighting Dragons to Lan and back again.

He did not object.

None of them were foolish enough to think this was safe.

But outside the pool, two monsters tore each other open.

Inside the thermal fissure, Dorm336 breathed in measured intervals and stayed hidden.

Inside the false magma, Dorm333 held its breath and waited for the moment impossibility weakened enough to be bitten.
