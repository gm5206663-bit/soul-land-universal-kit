# Chapter 18: The Bright Edge

Lan Xuanyu wanted Nana to step through the gate.

The wish rose so fast that it almost had no shape. It was not a plan. It was not a calculation. It was only a child's heart recognizing a voice it had missed and reaching for it before fear, distance, or reason could catch up.

Then Nana answered that she could not.

The Gate of Summoning shook in Qian Lei's light. Its rim wavered like heat over stone, but the cold that crossed from the other side made the air in the colosseum tighten. Lava Hounds had already burst from the gates in the walls. Their claws struck sparks from the cracked ground. Their throats glowed with ugly red light.

Lan Xuanyu held the unstable frame with silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass.

His right hand still wrapped gold-patterned strands around Liu Feng's wrist.

Between both hands, the world pulled in two directions.

Nana's voice came through one side.

Battle came through the other.

Dong Qianqiu did not let him choose.

"Talk to your teacher," she said, and stepped past him.

Her dark-blue spear swept in a crescent. Frost flashed across the hot ground, hissing where it met Lava Hound breath. The first hound lunged through the steam and met her spear point in the throat. Ice climbed its jaw before its body broke into motes of Soul World light.

Liu Feng moved half a beat behind her.

Half a beat was all he needed now.

White Dragon Spear answered the gold-patterned grass with a sound that was not quite a roar and not quite metal. The small dragon head near the spear tip seemed clearer than before, as if a sleeping carving had opened a memory. Liu Feng thrust once, and the spear light stretched farther than it should have. A Lava Hound that had dodged his first step still took the edge across its shoulder.

Liu Feng's eyes sharpened.

It was not only faster.

It was controllable.

Before, speed had often thrown him where he wanted to arrive but not always in the shape he wanted to keep. Now the force running through the White Dragon Spear did not scatter at the end of the thrust. It returned along the shaft and guided his wrist into the next angle.

Lan Xuanyu felt none of that clearly.

He only felt Liu Feng become easier to hold.

"Xuanyu," Nana said from beyond the gate, "listen carefully. The connection that summoned Qianqiu involves several strange factors. I need you to coordinate with me for tests."

"All right," Lan Xuanyu said quickly. "But I do not know how it happened either. I wanted to ask you."

Qian Lei, who had been staring between Dong Qianqiu's back, Nana's voice, and the charging hounds, swallowed.

"This is not normal," he whispered.

Liu Feng deflected a hound and shouted, "Focus!"

Qian Lei's shoulders jumped. "I am focusing on how abnormal it is!"

Nana's voice stayed calm despite the battlefield. "Can you perform a summon every ten minutes?"

Lan Xuanyu turned toward Qian Lei.

Qian Lei's expression changed at once. Business, fear, pride, and exhaustion all tried to sit on the same face.

"If we finish each battle quickly," he said, "it should not be a problem. Probably. Maybe. Teacher Nana, I am very young."

"Complete this scenario first," Nana said. "I will explain after that."

"Good," Dong Qianqiu said coldly. "Then fight."

The next few minutes became a lesson in what one additional person could change.

Dorm 333 had fought Lava Hounds before. They had failed before. They had panicked before. Liu Feng had outrun the team. Qian Lei had summoned failure. Lan Xuanyu had been forced to hold together a situation that kept splitting open under his hands.

Now Dong Qianqiu entered the pack like winter falling through a furnace.

She did not waste movement. She did not chase every hound. She cut the front line, froze the ground under the second, used the spear shaft to redirect the third into Liu Feng's path, and left Lan Xuanyu just enough room to keep the Gate stable without being torn away from the fight.

Liu Feng followed in gold-patterned support.

One attack.

Then another.

Then another.

He realized the difference on the third thrust.

When Lan Xuanyu only touched him for a moment, the power rose like a wave and left through one strike. But when the gold-patterned Blue Silver Grass stayed wrapped around him, the wave did not vanish. It remained under the spear, not violent enough to drown his own control, strong enough to keep waking the White Dragon Spear each time he moved.

A hound leaped low.

Liu Feng turned, spear light cutting across its spine.

Another lunged from the side.

Dong Qianqiu's ice drove it into the path of his next thrust.

The White Dragon Spear roared.

Liu Feng's heart roared with it.

For the first time since entering the Elite Junior Class, speed did not feel like an apology for weakness.

It felt like a weapon.

Lan Xuanyu's forehead dampened with sweat. Holding two different things at once was not the same as using one soul skill. The silver-patterned grass at the Gate felt cool and unstable, always needing adjustment. The gold-patterned grass around Liu Feng felt warm and eager, as if Liu Feng's White Dragon Spear was answering a call Lan Xuanyu had not known he made.

He did not understand.

But the team did not collapse.

That was enough for now.

With Dong Qianqiu's help, the last hound fell sooner than any of them had expected.

The Soul World scenario ended.

Light folded around them.

For a breath, Lan Xuanyu felt the Gate vanish from his hand.

Then they were back in the training room, lying in the simulation pods with the familiar heaviness of real bodies waiting beneath virtual experience.

Qian Lei pulled off the connection hood and sat up as if he had survived a formal execution.

"Ten minutes," he said weakly. "Teacher Nana said ten minutes. Why is it ten minutes? Why not one hour? Why not tomorrow?"

Liu Feng was already sitting up. "Because the connection might not wait."

Qian Lei looked betrayed. "You have become cruel since your spear started roaring."

Lan Xuanyu rubbed his temples. He should have been more tired than he was. Compared with yesterday's Golden Dragon Soar, holding the gate and Liu Feng had cost him less. Or maybe the worry of hearing Nana's voice had carried him through the fatigue.

"Can you do it?" he asked Qian Lei.

Qian Lei looked at him.

Then at Liu Feng.

Then at the empty pod light where no one else could possibly know what had happened.

His eyes slowly brightened with greed strong enough to stand even on tired legs.

"If this works," he said, "we are no longer the bottom."

Liu Feng did not laugh.

That made Qian Lei's expression even more serious.

"Ten minutes," Liu Feng said.

They waited.


The second attempt failed.

The Gate of Summoning opened under Lan Xuanyu's silver-patterned grip, shook, deepened, and produced not Dong Qianqiu but a broad-backed spirit-beast soul form with a horned skull and a temper that considered Lava Hounds a personal insult.

Qian Lei stared.

"That is not the ice girl."

The beast roared and charged into the hounds.

Liu Feng's spear lifted. "It will do."

It did.

Luck was still luck, and Qian Lei's martial soul had always loved making a fool of him by becoming useful in the least predictable way. The summoned beast did not understand teamwork. It did not need to. It smashed a path through the first wave, and Lan Xuanyu used the opening to keep Liu Feng's enhancement stable while Liu Feng cleaned the side threats.

They passed.

Qian Lei walked out of the reset dizzy but triumphant.

"See? Reliability has many forms."

"That was not reliability," Liu Feng said.

"It was successful unreliability."

Ten minutes later, the third attempt brought Dong Qianqiu again.

This time she appeared with one hand already forming frost.

"Teacher says continue," she said.

No one asked which teacher.

The hounds came.

They repeated the process.

Lan Xuanyu held the gate. Dong Qianqiu controlled the front. Liu Feng tested how long the gold-patterned wrapping could sustain the White Dragon Spear's awakened state. Qian Lei watched the gate as if his future ranking depended on every tremble.

It probably did.

The fourth summon succeeded.

The fifth failed.

The sixth succeeded.

The seventh opened halfway, spat out a clawed shadow that vanished before the hounds could bite it, and forced them to reset again while Qian Lei argued that partial failure should count as academic progress.

No one agreed, but no one had the energy to answer properly.

By the time Dong Qianqiu stepped through again, Nana's voice followed at once.

"Qianqiu, do not kill all of the Lava Hounds yet. I need to speak to Xuanyu."

"All right, Teacher."

Dong Qianqiu's spear swept once. Frost pinned two hounds to the ground without breaking them completely. Another hound snarled and tried to circle. Liu Feng intercepted it with a thrust that sent white light flashing across the arena wall.

Lan Xuanyu stood beside the Gate, silver grass taut.

"Teacher Nana," he said, "what is going on?"

Nana answered slowly, as if each word had been weighed before crossing the unstable channel.

"The reason we can communicate through the Gate of Summoning is spatial resonance. Two different spaces are vibrating at the same frequency and producing a temporary channel between them. Such a thing is extremely rare. The probability should be almost impossible."

Qian Lei's mouth opened.

For once, no sound came out.

Liu Feng's spear lowered by half an inch before he caught himself.

Lan Xuanyu understood only part of it. Space. Frequency. Channel. Almost impossible. He knew enough from childhood lessons to know those words should not be sitting inside Qian Lei's unreliable soul skill as if they had rented a room.

"So Qian Lei summoned Qianqiu because of space?" he asked.

"Not only that," Nana said. "I need more tests to judge where the resonance is produced. First, leave one Lava Hound alive. When the gate ends, how long does Qian Lei need before summoning again?"

Lan Xuanyu looked at Qian Lei.

Qian Lei found his voice with effort. "If I wait for one gate to disappear normally, about half an hour. But in Soul World, if we leave and re-enter the scenario, the state resets. I can attempt another summon after the reset, as long as I can endure it."

"Then end the battle," Nana said. "After ten minutes, re-enter and try again. If Qianqiu is not summoned, leave, wait ten minutes, and repeat."

Lan Xuanyu nodded. "Okay."

Dong Qianqiu released the frozen hounds only long enough to end them.

The arena dissolved again.

Ten minutes became a shape in their day.

Enter.

Hold the gate.

Summon.

Fight.

Fail.

Reset.

Drink water.

Argue for thirty seconds.

Enter again.

The Soul World restored cultivation and Spiritual Power better than real battle could have, but restoration was not the same as no cost. Qian Lei's face grew paler each time he held the Summoning Money. His jokes came thinner. His fingers trembled when the coin spun. Sweat soaked his hair until it stuck to his forehead.

Lan Xuanyu wanted to stop earlier.

Qian Lei refused the first time.

"Do not look at me like that," Qian Lei said. "I am investing in my future."

"You are shaking."

"Investing is emotional."

Liu Feng stood beside the pod row, breathing hard but bright-eyed. "One more."

Qian Lei pointed at him. "You only say that because you are having a heroic rebirth."

"Yes," Liu Feng said without shame.

Qian Lei stared. "You used to be quiet. I miss him."

But he still lifted the coin.

Again.

Again.

Again.

After more than ten summons, even Qian Lei's greed could no longer keep his body upright. The next time Dong Qianqiu appeared, Qian Lei nearly sat down before the gate finished stabilizing.

Lan Xuanyu tightened his left hand on the silver-patterned grass.

"Teacher Nana," he said quickly, "Qian Lei may not be able to last another summon."

Nana did not push.

"Then we will end here today. I have some thoughts now. Xuanyu, what I can tell you is that the spatial resonance is most probably produced between you and me. After these tests, I am sure of this."

Lan Xuanyu's heart jumped.

"Between you and me? Then if we become strong enough, could we summon you over here?"

There was a pause.

Nana's voice softened.

"That would be difficult. Almost impossible. But I do not know why I feel certain of it. Rest for today."

Lan Xuanyu did not know whether that answer was comfort or disappointment.

Maybe both.

Dong Qianqiu looked at him for a moment. Curiosity sat in her eyes, not warm, not hostile, simply sharp.

It looked as if she wanted to ask something.

Instead she shrugged.

"I'm off."

The Gate of Summoning closed.

The colosseum went quiet.

Then Qian Lei fell backward onto the ground with the dignity of a dropped sack.

"If anyone asks," he said faintly, "tell them I died improving the team."

Liu Feng hooked one hand under his arm. "Get up before the system charges us for occupying the arena."

"Cruel. Reborn and cruel."

Lan Xuanyu helped from the other side.

Together, they returned to the real dorm.


Qian Lei reached his bed and surrendered to it without negotiation.

He did not remove his shoes properly. One hung from his foot by the heel until Liu Feng kicked it off for him. The other fell to the floor later, when Qian Lei turned over and hugged the blanket as if it were a treasure chest.

Lan Xuanyu looked at him with guilt.

"He really is exhausted."

"He wanted to continue," Liu Feng said.

"That does not mean we should let him."

Liu Feng looked at Qian Lei for a moment. His excitement did not vanish, but it settled enough to become thought.

"Tomorrow we control the number of attempts."

Lan Xuanyu nodded.

That should have ended the night.

Liu Feng did not move.

The White Dragon Spear appeared in his hand again, snow-white and slender, the dragon head near the spear tip faintly cold even without combat.

"Xuanyu," he said, voice lower than before, "we tested the gate all day. Are you tired?"

Lan Xuanyu flexed his hands. He was tired, but not in the same way as Qian Lei. The biggest drain had been attention, not soul power.

"I am all right."

Liu Feng's fingers tightened around the spear shaft.

"Then can you coordinate with me once? Not battle. Just the change. In the Soul World, you used your right hand. Your gold-patterned Blue Silver Grass made my spear feel... awake. What about the silver-patterned one?"

Lan Xuanyu looked at Qian Lei.

Qian Lei snored once, turned over, and muttered something about interest rates.

"He will not wake," Liu Feng said.

Lan Xuanyu hesitated. "If it is only testing the martial soul, it should be safe."

Liu Feng nodded immediately.

Lan Xuanyu extended his right arm first.

Gold-patterned Blue Silver Grass curled out and wrapped along his arm. The moment it appeared, warmth surged through Lan Xuanyu's body like fresh blood suddenly remembering sunlight. A faint golden halo appeared around him before he could stop it.

He pressed his hand to Liu Feng's shoulder.

Liu Feng inhaled.

The change was immediate.

Heat rushed through his body, but it was not burning heat. It was force, energy, something vigorous and dragon-like that ran from his shoulder into his arm and down the spear shaft. The White Dragon Spear trembled. Its glow brightened. At the tip, spear light extended three inches, sharp enough that the air seemed to split around it.

The small dragon carving opened its eyes.

Liu Feng's face changed.

All the years of being fast but not heavy enough, sharp but not penetrating enough, present but ranked at the bottom, seemed to gather behind his eyes and break.

"Awesome," he whispered.

Then louder, unable to hold it back, "This is amazing. I feel stronger. I feel like I need to release it."

Lan Xuanyu drew back his hand.

The golden halo faded.

Liu Feng stared at the spear as if afraid it would fall asleep again.

Lan Xuanyu looked at his own palm. "I did not do anything. I did not transfer soul power. I did not give you vitality. It felt like I only touched you."

"Only touched me?" Liu Feng gave a short laugh. "If this is only touching, then everyone else in the class is wasting their hands."

Lan Xuanyu shook his head seriously. "No. It should be your White Dragon Spear changing. I am only... a medium?"

The word felt strange.

It also felt closest.

Liu Feng did not argue. "Left hand."

Lan Xuanyu raised his left arm.

Silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass slid out, cool and quiet. Its touch changed the air at once. When Lan Xuanyu pressed his left hand against Liu Feng's shoulder, the heat vanished from Liu Feng's body as if winter had stepped through him.

A cooling sensation spread down his arm.

The White Dragon Spear answered again.

This time faint ice fog breathed from the spear. The dragon's eyes opened with a gentle silver glow, different from the fierce light-gold of before. Liu Feng moved his wrist once, and a pale mist drifted over the floorboards.

Across the room, Qian Lei shivered in his sleep and dragged the blanket over his head.

"You gave me the ice attribute?" Liu Feng asked, stunned.

Lan Xuanyu's eyes widened innocently.

He had done nothing.

Again.

He had touched Liu Feng, wanted to help, and the martial soul had answered by itself.

"I do not know," Lan Xuanyu said. "Before this, I only weakened other people's martial souls. I have never strengthened anyone."

Liu Feng lowered the White Dragon Spear slowly.

For a while, neither of them spoke.

The room felt colder than before, but Liu Feng's eyes were bright enough to fight the chill.

"Xuanyu," he said finally, "you really are amazing."

"I am not sure it is me."

"Whether it is you, your martial soul, or some affinity between us, the result is the same. You can strengthen me. You can make Qian Lei's summoning reliable. That changes everything."

Lan Xuanyu looked toward Qian Lei's blanket mountain.

"It may not always be reliable."

"More reliable than before is already a miracle for Dorm 333."

Liu Feng took a breath.

The next words came out with the seriousness of someone who had already decided them in the Soul World and only now found the right moment.

"From now on, Dorm 333 will be headed by you."

Lan Xuanyu blinked. "Me?"

"Each dorm should have a head. Qian Lei and I did not have one because one of us could not attack properly and the other could not summon properly. We were the bottom. Now that you are here, everything is different."

"But I just arrived."

"Exactly," Liu Feng said. "And everything changed after you arrived."

Lan Xuanyu did not know how to answer.

He had never wanted to be a head. He had wanted to find Nana, understand his martial souls, train, eat enough, and not cause trouble for his parents. But Dorm 333 was looking at him now even while one roommate slept through the decision.

"What about Qian Lei?"

From under the blanket came a muffled sound that might have been a snore and might have been an agreement to favorable terms.

Liu Feng said, "He will agree if you tell him this helps us win."

That sounded true.

"Also," Liu Feng continued, "we keep this secret. Do not tell the teacher. Do not tell other students. The midterm ranking exam is soon. We have been at the bottom too long. We will practice hard these days and familiarize ourselves with your strengthening. When the time comes, we show them."

Lan Xuanyu looked at his left hand, then his right.

Silver. Gold.

Ice. Heat.

Gate. Spear.

Nana's voice still echoed somewhere between them all.

He nodded.

"All right. We practice. But we do not exhaust Qian Lei like today."

Liu Feng's expression softened.

"Agreed."

Qian Lei snored again.

This time it sounded like a contract seal.


Yan Shuo did not know any of that.

The next morning, he saw only the public version of Dorm 333's transformation.

Qian Lei arrived at breakfast with his hair flattened on one side, his eyes half closed, and the expression of a man who had been betrayed by his own martial soul. Liu Feng walked beside him with a bowl in one hand and a focus in his gaze that had not been there before. Lan Xuanyu carried two plates and looked thoughtful enough to miss the first free seat.

Yan Shuo watched from Dorm 336's table.

Luo Haoran followed his gaze. "Dorm 333 looks like it fought ten battles and won half of them by accident."

Song Yichen said, "That may be accurate."

Qian Lei dropped onto a chair across the room and almost put his forehead into porridge.

Lan Xuanyu moved the bowl in time.

Liu Feng spoke quietly. Qian Lei straightened by one inch, then two, then pointed a weak finger as if making an important argument. Whatever he said made Liu Feng shake his head and Lan Xuanyu smile helplessly.

Yan Shuo could not hear the words.

He did not try.

Chapter by chapter, day by day, that was becoming a harder skill than fire.

Some doors opened because a person was invited.

Some stayed closed because pushing them would break what waited behind them.

Dawnflame Kite pulsed inside him, warm and bright.

Not heat only.

He noticed that now because his mother had said it the previous night after his report, voice sharp enough to cut through pride.

If your soul spirit was fire and light, do not train as if only fire answered.

Yan Shuo had replayed those words before sleep until they stopped sounding like correction and started sounding like a key.

Fire burned outward.

Light drew the edge.

When he had been younger, he had thought brightness was what happened when fire became strong.

Now, standing after breakfast in Dorm 336's assigned drill room, he began to understand that brightness could also be what stopped fire from wasting itself.

The room lights dimmed to practice visibility. Rubber pellets fired from hidden slots along the wall. The exercise was simple: Song held the front, Luo intercepted moving targets, and Yan Shuo indicated safe lanes without burning the floor grid.

It was the same kind of simple that had teeth.

Song's Stoneplate Tortoise shield rose with a heavy gray shimmer.

Luo Haoran leaned on the balls of his feet, mantis-light thin along his arms.

Yan Shuo breathed in.

Dawnfeather Mantle answered.

Copper-red flame came first, close over his shoulders and arms. Then pale-gold motes gathered inside it, not scattering like sparks but settling along invisible feather ribs. White-gold edges lined each feather shape. The mantle did not grow larger. It grew clearer.

Luo turned his head slightly. "That looks different."

"It is supposed to," Yan Shuo said.

The first pellet shot low.

Yan Shuo's old habit wanted to flare and fill the entire left lane. His body remembered the Soul World rats. His breath dropped before his pride could speak.

A dawnfire line slid across the grid.

It was narrow, copper at the center and white-gold at the edge. The light did not attack. It held the flame in place.

The pellet struck the edge, lost speed in the warm shimmer, and entered Luo's reach exactly where Luo's mantis cut waited.

Second pellet.

Third.

Song shifted his shield by half a step.

Yan Shuo did not chase the movement with a larger blaze. He let the pale-gold motes collect over his shoulder, felt the mantle distribute the recoil of his own output through his upper back, and drew a second line only where Song's shield stopped being useful.

Light refined the boundary.

Fire carried the force.

Together, the line held.

Luo laughed under his breath. "You became prettier and more annoying."

Yan Shuo almost lost the edge to irritation.

The white-gold line flickered.

His lungs corrected.

The edge settled again.

"Thank you," Yan Shuo said.

"That was not praise."

"I accepted it anyway."

Song's shield took two impacts. "Less talking. Side slot."

Yan Shuo saw it early this time.

Not because he was faster than yesterday in a way a tablet could measure. Because the dim room made motion and brightness talk to each other. The weak warm light around Dawnfeather Mantle caught the shine on the slot a breath before it fired.

He stepped, marked the lane, and left space for Luo's cut.

The pellet crossed, slowed, and broke under mantis-light.

No one cheered.

They did not need to.

The drill record showed reduced friendly-pressure overlap and cleaner lane control again. The system did not know why. It did not know Dawnflame Kite's actual living age. It did not know that the official paperwork still said one hundred years while the private truth had grown far beyond the first record. It did not know that light had stopped fighting fire years ago and Yan Shuo was only now learning to respect that fact with his hands.

It only knew the result.

Cleaner boundary.

Lower waste.

Better timing.

Yan Shuo released the form after the timer ended.

The flame withdrew first.

The pale-gold motes lingered for half a breath along his shoulders like dawn caught on feathers, then folded inward after it.

His breathing returned faster than it had three days ago.

That, too, was growth.

No new number came with it.

He no longer needed one to notice.


Secondary-profession registration opened after morning lessons.

The academy did not make it feel ceremonial. That almost made it worse. A row of terminals appeared along the side of the classroom. Names lit up one by one. Each student stepped forward, confirmed the required mecha-related selection, chose the second direction, accepted the schedule warning, and returned to their seat with their future made heavier by two lines of text.

Yan Shuo had expected noise.

There was less of it than usual.

Battle choices could be argued. Soul World victories could be bragged about. Secondary professions sounded like hobbies until the academy attached time, money, examinations, elimination risk, and sleep loss to them.

Then even Jin Xiang was quiet for a while.

Dorm 333 went together.

Liu Feng's choice surprised no one who had watched him move: Duo Mecha Master direction. He wanted speed, weapons, and a future body strong enough for the White Dragon Spear to stop being half-awake only when someone else touched it.

Qian Lei chose warship piloting fundamentals and battle armor.

That made several heads turn.

Lan Xuanyu chose the same combination.

That made the room turn more.

Yan Shuo watched from his place in line with Song and Luo.

Warship piloting foundation.

He had not forgotten the threshold. Fifty Spiritual Power just to begin. Real warship piloting needed far more later. His latest formal number, seventy-two, cleared the first gate. The number sat inside him with a quiet kind of pride.

It also did not decide for him.

Qian Lei had the strange high Spiritual Power that made other students stare.

Lan Xuanyu had loved warships before most children could sit still through safety broadcasts.

Yan Shuo had a different problem.

His shoulders remembered wing-roots that were not wings. His back remembered recoil. His first ring remembered a living bird whose fire and light had needed a body that would not tear under their argument. Dawnfeather Mantle wrapped skin, tendons, breath, spine, and aura together so closely that any future armor fighting his rhythm would become a cage.

If he wanted wings one day, real or armored or something between, he first needed to understand what a body could safely carry.

Song went before him and chose a defensive armor/mecha control path without drama.

Luo chose agility-oriented mecha control and a technical route he claimed would make him impossible to hit and easier to admire.

Then Yan Shuo's name lit up.

He stepped to the terminal.

The screen displayed the options with cheerful neutrality.

Warship piloting fundamentals waited near the top.

He looked at it for two breaths.

Then he selected the Duo Mecha Master foundation side.

For the second field, he chose battle armor interface and maintenance fundamentals, the technical subcategory the academy allowed younger students to refine later into design, manufacturing, or deeper maintenance if their talent proved suitable.

Confirm?

Yan Shuo thought of Yan Ke calculating costs with a face that tried to look calm.

He thought of Lin Mei telling him that accuracy let teammates stand closer.

He thought of pale-gold feather edges settling over his shoulders instead of flaring into a useless cloak.

He confirmed.

The terminal accepted the choice.

No light fell from the ceiling.

No destiny announced itself.

Only a schedule appeared, and it was merciless.

Yan Shuo returned to his seat.

Luo leaned closer. "You stared at warship piloting."

"I qualified," Yan Shuo said.

"Then why not choose it?"

Yan Shuo flexed his fingers under the desk. A trace of remembered feather-light moved across his shoulders and vanished.

"Because qualifying is not the same as fitting."

Song looked at him once, then nodded.

Luo sighed. "I hate when answers are good. They make teasing harder."

Across the room, Qian Lei was discovering that choosing a lonely road made one an excellent target.

Jin Xiang approached him before class with a smile that had already failed to become friendly.

"Amazing, Qian Lei," Jin Xiang said loudly enough for nearby students to hear. "I heard you chose warship piloting fundamentals. Not bad. Not bad."

Qian Lei rolled his eyes. "What does it have to do with you? My Spiritual Power is strong and my perception is good. Is that a problem?"

Jin Xiang nodded as if he were being generous. "That is true. Since you are useless in battle and will probably be eliminated during midterm exams, specializing in warship piloting is a good choice. Very practical."

The classroom air shifted.

Old Qian Lei would have exploded because old wounds like being poked.

This Qian Lei still exploded halfway.

"Eliminated? Little guy, I--"

Liu Feng's hand caught his sleeve.

Qian Lei turned on him. "Why are you pulling me?"

Liu Feng did not speak aloud.

He only looked at him.

Something passed between them.

Not words.

Not something Yan Shuo could name.

Qian Lei's anger paused on the edge of his tongue.

Then his eyes changed.

Yesterday's exhausted boy, blanket victim and professional complainer, suddenly looked as if he were hiding a coin under the table and waiting for the price to rise.

He turned back to Jin Xiang.

"See you during the midterm exams," Qian Lei said. "Do not cry then."

Jin Xiang's smile vanished. "Trying to provoke me? How about one-on-one?"

Qian Lei looked at him as if he had been offered a bad investment. "Are you dumb? Class is starting. The teacher is already here. Teacher Ji is here."

Jin Xiang's face changed.

He moved so fast that several students almost laughed, springing back toward his seat like a Flaming Golden Bird avoiding a bucket of water.

Then he realized there was no teacher at the door.

His face darkened.

"Qian Lei--"

The classroom door opened.

No one laughed.

A tall, lean middle-aged man walked in.

His sideburns were touched with gray. His back was straight enough to make the room remember posture. His eyes were bright, calm, and far too sharp. He did not need to release martial soul pressure. The silence arrived before he reached the front.

Yan Shuo felt Dawnflame Kite go still inside him.

Not afraid.

Attentive.

This was not the wild danger of Soul World. It was a different kind of pressure: knowledge, discipline, consequence, and the certainty that excuses would find no place to stand.

Qian Lei slowly lowered himself into his seat.

Luo Haoran, who had been halfway through whispering something, closed his mouth so carefully it became suspicious.

Song Yichen's hands folded on the desk.

Lan Xuanyu looked curious.

Jin Xiang looked as if an old nightmare had learned to open doors.

The man reached the teacher's platform.

The class remained silent enough for the lights to hum.

Yan Shuo kept his breathing low and felt the last memory of pale-gold feather edges settle beneath his skin.

Fire could burn.

Light could draw the boundary.
