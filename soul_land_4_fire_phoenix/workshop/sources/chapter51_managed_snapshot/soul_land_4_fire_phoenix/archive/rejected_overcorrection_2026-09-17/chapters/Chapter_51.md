# Chapter 51 — The Green Road Chooses Back

By the time the six Heaven Luo teams entered the simulation hall, Dorm333's score had become heavier than a number.

Twenty thousand three hundred and forty-one.

It had moved through teacher reports, academy meetings, student whispers, and Shrek review files. It had made Heaven Luo Academy's president order preparations for the next Elite Junior Class before the current one had even finished being tested. It had made adults who once spoke politely about rankings begin using words like federation first.

It also created a question no one could answer cleanly.

How had Dorm333 killed the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragons?

Lan Xuanyu's Martial Soul Fusion technique was strange. His command was terrifyingly calm. Qian Lei's summons could sometimes turn absurd. Liu Feng now carried something silver under his sleeve.

But a Rank20 child should not be able to damage a Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon directly.

So the other students looked at Dorm333 differently.

Lu Qianxun brought Ye Lingtong and Chang Jianyi behind Lan's team without waiting for an order. Ninth place was good; he did not pretend it stood beside first. Jin Xiang, farther back with Tang Yuege and Li Pengbin, kept his mouth shut for once. The memory of provoking Lan years ago sat on his face like bad medicine. It was no longer possible to call Lan merely lucky. Too many teams had survived the first round partly because Lan had read danger faster than they did.

Dorm336 stood a short distance away.

Not behind Dorm333.

Not erased beside them.

Yan Shuo held a dark-red training case at his side, Song Yichen and Luo Haoran their own cases behind him. Their second-place reward package had already been used for two days, and the faint fatigue in all three proved it. The Fire Phoenix output-calibration cuff had not made Yan stronger in the cheap way. It had made him more exacting. Song's shield-load matrix had made him angrier at angles. Luo's wind-line threads had made him quieter and faster to speak when it mattered.

Second place had not become a decoration.

It had become preparation.

Ji Hongbin stood before the six teams. His gaze swept across Dorm333, Dorm336, Lu's team, Jin's team, and the two other surviving groups.

"The next qualifier is about to begin," he said. "Display your strengths. If cooperation becomes possible, let Lan Xuanyu take overall command."

A stir moved through the students.

It was not submission.

It was recognition.

Lan's command was not a statement that he burned hotter than Yan Shuo, or that his Soul Power outweighed Lu Qianxun's pride, or that Qian Lei had suddenly become reliable enough to lead anyone. It was a statement about rules, pressure, and impossible timings. If Shrek hid a road inside danger, Lan was the one most likely to see the edge of it.

Yan's eyes met Lan's briefly.

No challenge.

No surrender.

Only the same public understanding they had carried since the corridor: different roads did not need to shrink each other.

Mu Zhongtian cut through the atmosphere before it could become sentimental.

"Enter the pods."

Eighteen lids opened.

Six teams.

Not five.

The old board had broken when Dorm336 survived second, and the room no longer pretended otherwise.

Qian Lei looked at the pods and whispered, "If this begins with Dragons again, I will file a complaint with destiny."

Liu Feng said, "Destiny rejected your previous paperwork."

"That is why I need persistence."

Lan lay down before Qian could continue.

The lid closed.

Darkness folded into Soul World.

A pleasant voice rang out around them.

"Welcome to the Shrek Academy Heaven Luo Planet qualifiers."

Lan listened as if the voice were a blade being drawn politely.

The rules came clean and merciless.

Each team would be transferred to a random corner of the simulated Star Dou Forest from the Mother Planet. They had to go deeper and hunt high-level soul beasts. Each team scored separately. Whoever completed the final kill received the points. Killing between teams was allowed; killing another team's members granted half that team's accumulated points, while the killed team kept the remaining half. The test would last seven days in Soul World.

Seven days.

Random transfer.

Separate scoring.

Last kill.

Team-killing half.

Go deeper.

Every rule had teeth.

Lan's first conclusion arrived before the countdown ended: broad cooperation would be difficult. Ji Hongbin had said to cooperate if possible, but Shrek had built the forest to make trust expensive. Random transfer scattered them. Separate scoring split incentives. Last-kill rewards poisoned shared hunts. Team-killing made success itself dangerous.

"Ten, nine, eight..."

Lan glanced once at Qian and Liu.

Qian's darker yellow rings had not risen, but Lan knew they were there now, deepened by proper Spirit Pagoda processing until the five-hundred-year realm could show itself. Liu stood sharper after the Soul Bone fusion, his right sleeve repaired but unable to hide the changed way he carried that arm.

"Three, two, one. Begin."

Light fell.

The world spun.

Fresh air struck Lan's face.

He dropped into a crouch before his eyes fully focused.

Green surrounded them: vast trunks, layered canopy, vines like ropes, moss and ferns crowding the ground, no road, no human mark, no mercy. The simulated Star Dou Forest smelled so alive that for a moment it felt less artificial than the academy outside the pod.

Qian appeared beside him, blinking.

Liu Feng was already scanning shadows.

Lan pulled Qian between himself and Liu and moved them to the base of a massive tree. Only after their backs had something solid behind them did he speak.

"Do not rush. We analyze first."

Qian whispered, "Analysis before being eaten. I support this policy."

Lan ignored the wording and kept the content.

"Survival for seven days is the first requirement. Going deeper should mean higher-level soul beasts and higher scores. But team-killing changes everything. If another team kills us after we accumulate points, half our work becomes theirs. The announcement did not say soul beasts take points from us the same way. In the worst case, dying to a soul beast — even ending ourselves inside the simulation — is better than handing another team half our score."

Qian's expression twisted.

Then he nodded.

Liu nodded too. "So we survive, read direction, hunt carefully, and avoid becoming someone else's harvest."

"Yes."

Lan recalled Ji Hongbin's lessons. "Ancient Star Dou Forest had regions: Peripheral, Hybrid, Core, and the central Territory of Great Beasts. Shrek should not throw us into the Core immediately. We are likely in the Peripheral Region or at most near the Hybrid Region. We learn the area first, then decide how quickly to move deeper."

Liu Feng adjusted his grip on the White Dragon Spear. "I'll scout."

"Three hundred meters," Lan said. "No farther. Information first, points later."

Qian lifted the Coin of Summoning. "And I prepare?"

"Prepare. If Liu brings danger back, we fight ready."

Liu moved.

White light flashed as his spear appeared, and he stepped on a vine to launch himself over roots and shadow. The Silver Moon Wolf Right Arm Bone did not announce itself. It did not need to. His movement had become more gathered, more confident in the right side of his body, but Lan was glad he did not show Silver Edge. A trump card shown too early became someone else's information.

Lan set Qian against the tree and released silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass. Moisture condensed. Ice awls formed in a quiet ring around them, angled low where sudden movement would have to cross. Qian closed his eyes, danger sense stretching into the green.

"No dangerous aura nearby," he whispered.

Lan nodded.

He and Qian had the highest Spiritual Power in the Elite Junior Class. Lan's Spirit Sea made the forest clearer; Qian's summoner instincts made danger feel like pressure before it had a name. No danger nearby did not mean safety. It meant the first breath had not killed them.

Liu returned soon, landing lightly between vines.

"No soul beasts in the area."

He looked at the pathless forest. "But without a map, how do we know which way goes deeper and which way goes outward?"

Lan had already thought through that.

"Soul beast strength becomes the map. In the Peripheral Region, unless our luck is extremely bad, we should mostly meet ten-year or hundred-year soul beasts. We can deal with that. Thousand-year soul beasts require caution. If the beasts grow stronger, we are moving deeper. If they grow weaker or vanish, we may be moving outward."

Qian gave him a thumbs-up. "Good. We listen to you. Wherever you say to go, we go."

"Then summon a soul beast," Lan said. "We use it to find a path."

Qian's thumb froze. "Right now? My first summoning still has a fifteen-to-twenty-minute idle period. If I use it and something terrible happens..."

"You have Replicate as contingency. And a summoned beast walking ahead can take the first attack instead of one of us. This is a seven-day test. We will need safe recovery rhythms anyway. Better to spend power for information than bodies for surprise."

Qian considered that.

Then he grinned. "Wonderful. I will not use my brain."

"Use enough not to die," Liu said.

"Minimum brain. Efficient brain."

The Coin of Summoning spun into the forest air. Two yellow rings rose beneath Qian's feet.

They were darker than before.

Still yellow. Not purple. Not a thousand-year breakthrough. But the color had clearly deepened after the Spirit Pagoda processing, heavy enough now to show the five-hundred-year realm. For Qian, that difference mattered more than a pretty color. It meant duration, response, and possibly quality.

Golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass wrapped around the Gate of Summoning to enhance it.

Qian glanced at Lan. "Why not silver-patterned grass anymore? Dong Qianqiu's power should have a big effect, right?"

Lan froze for a fraction.

Memories from Heaven Dou arrived at the worst possible time: accidental contact, Dong's furious chase, his own childish counter, Teacher Nana's door, apology, embarrassment.

"I offended her," he said. "She probably would not appear now. And she should be taking Shrek's qualifiers too. The tests are simultaneous across the Federation, with Shrek's algorithm preventing planets from learning the content in advance."

Liu nodded. Qian wisely did not ask for details.

Lan added only, "She is Teacher Nana's disciple and has trained with her longer than I have. I am not worried about her passing."

That was enough.

The Gate roared.

Heat rolled out.

A five-meter fiery-red figure emerged, covered in red scales, spikes along its back, massive head low to the ground, smoke leaking from its mouth.

Qian stared. "Of all things..."

"Ground Fire Dragon Lizard," Lan said.

An evolved close relative of the Ground Fire Lizard. A type of Ground Dragon. Weaker than the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon, but stronger than ordinary lizards, with broad attack range and fireballs. After the previous qualifier, summoning its cousin felt like Soul World laughing with poor taste.

Qian waved with forced boredom.

The Ground Fire Dragon Lizard charged forward.

Vines snapped. Leaves curled. Its heated head scales shoved a crude path open through the forest.

They moved immediately: Liu in front behind the summoned beast, Qian in the middle controlling it, Lan at the rear with golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass around Liu's waist. The connection stayed loose, ready to enhance or pull warning through the line.

The lizard lasted about a minute and a half now, longer after Qian's ring-realm improvement. In a seven-day test, ninety seconds was nothing. In a pathless forest, behind a fire-type Ground Dragon breaking vines, it was a road.

The forest seemed peaceful as they advanced. Plant fragrance drifted through damp air. Sunlight fell in broken pieces. If the rules had not included hunting, team-killing, and seven days of survival, it might have been beautiful.

Then a thick vine ahead moved like a living rope.

It snapped around the Ground Fire Dragon Lizard's head.

Liu's spear rose.

"Plant-type soul beast," Lan said.

The lizard's body flushed metallic red. Sizzling filled the air. The vine turned yellow, then brown, withering as a bitter plant aura burst from it.

Fire restrained wood.

The principle was basic, but useful. Here in the outer forest, the Ground Fire Dragon Lizard was exactly the right borrowed tool. The surrounding vines recoiled and separated.

"Follow!"

They rushed.

For the rest of the summon duration, they used the safe opening hard. Liu flashed ahead. Qian ran with panicked dignity. Lan kept the rear, watching for a second attack.

By the time the Ground Fire Dragon Lizard blurred back into the Gate beside Qian and disappeared, they had advanced roughly one kilometer inward.

Lan immediately slowed them and chose higher ground.

It was only a slight rise, roots breaking through earth and lines of sight opening a little farther between trunks, but a little mattered. Qian sat down to recover Soul Power. Liu checked the approach paths. Lan studied what they had learned.

No strong beasts at the start.

Weak plant-type soul beast after movement.

Fire easily restrained it.

Likely still outer region.

Usable direction, not proven direction.

Elsewhere in the same simulated forest, Dorm336 arrived under a different canopy.

Yan Shuo's first instinct was to release wings.

He did not.

The previous qualifier had already taught all of them that open flight and high heat signatures invited punishment when Shrek wanted them punished. Instead, he let only a thread of Fire Phoenix pressure pass through his body and watched the output-calibration cuff flash amber, then settle.

"Too high?" Song whispered.

"Too fast," Luo corrected, eyes on the sensor threads at his wrist. "Not too high yet."

Yan lowered the thread until the cuff dimmed.

Song planted his shield where the ground rose. Luo angled them away from a dense patch of vines without pretending he knew whether it was danger or merely caution.

They did not know Dorm333's position.

They did not know Lu's.

They did know the rules.

Random transfer. Separate score. Team-killing half.

Yan looked at Song and Luo. "We do not chase teams. We build a safe route first. If we meet Heaven Luo teams, we judge before trusting. If we meet Dorm333, Lan commands only if the situation lets teams work without feeding someone else points."

Song nodded.

Luo's mouth curved faintly. "That sounds like respecting him without giving him your brain."

"I need my brain," Yan said. "You two keep borrowing it."

Song looked offended. "I borrow shield-related parts only."

The forest did not laugh.

But the tension became something they could carry.

Dorm336 moved as a team.

Not a Phoenix dragging baggage.

A rewarded second-place team proving the reward was not wasted.

Back on Dorm333's rise, Qian recovered enough to summon again. The Gate answered a little faster than before.

Qian noticed Lan noticing and sat straighter. "Yes, admire quietly."

"The activation is faster," Lan said.

"A little," Qian admitted, then failed to hide his delight. "The ring improvement helps. Not enough, but real."

For a summoner with little direct battle power between summons, a shorter gap could decide whether he was a teammate or a target.

They repeated the rhythm.

Summon, move, rest.

Measure the forest by what answered.

It was not glorious. It was better than glorious. It was sustainable.

During one rest, Qian leaned against a root and looked up through the canopy. "I really want to go to the Spirit Ascension Platform soon. If my first Soul Ring reaches a thousand years, how much better would the Gate become? Imagine summoning without stopping until my Soul Power runs dry. If I could call Ground Dragon-type beasts continuously, I would be the strongest in the class."

Liu Feng snorted. "If thinking made it happen, you would already rule the academy."

"My imagination is underappreciated."

"Work harder."

Lan did not dismiss the dream.

"It is not impossible in principle," he said. "Just not now. Your summoning is affected by several foundations: Soul Power rank, Spiritual Power rank, and Soul Ring strength. If your Soul Power becomes high enough, the interval should reduce. The strength of what appears may still be uncertain, because that is part of your martial soul. But if you want faster and more stable summoning, those are the areas to improve. Especially Spiritual Power. Your perception, control, and Gate activation all show Spiritual Power has a strong relation with summoning."

Qian became quiet.

Not sad.

Focused.

A path that belonged to him had appeared, and it looked annoyingly like effort.

Lan stood and looked deeper into the green.

The forest had not given them a map.

It had given them rules, risk, and small answers.

That would have to be enough.

Behind them were rewards that had become weight.

Ahead of them was a road borrowed from fire, vines, luck, and judgment.

And somewhere under another canopy, six-team Heaven Luo continuity moved in directions Shrek had scattered but not erased.

Lan raised his hand.

Dorm333 moved deeper.
