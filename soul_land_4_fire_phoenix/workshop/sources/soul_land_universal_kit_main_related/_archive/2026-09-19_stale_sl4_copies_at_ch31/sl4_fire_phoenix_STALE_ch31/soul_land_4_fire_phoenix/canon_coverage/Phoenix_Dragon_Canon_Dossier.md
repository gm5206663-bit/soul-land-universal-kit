## 2. Direct high-value source: SL4 Chapter 868

Source checked: `https://www.fanmtl.com/novel/soul-land-iv-ultimate-fighting_868.html`.

Working tier: A-minus. It is a direct translated chapter page, but not an official translation.

Key takeaways for this project:

- Blue Demon Birds are described as divine beasts connected to space and time.
- A Space-type and Time-type Blue Demon Bird appearing together can produce a true Phoenix.
- True Phoenix can potentially ascend to Phoenix God.
- Phoenix God is described as being on the same level as Dragon God.
- The former Phoenix God was killed by Dragon God.
- That Phoenix God versus Dragon God battle heavily damaged Dragon God and is tied to the later chance for human gods to arise.
- Dragon God later killed Blue Demon Birds again to prevent another Phoenix God from being born.
- Dragon God controls nine major elements and an extremely powerful physique.
- Phoenix God controls Creation, described as an ability able to simulate existing abilities.

Project meaning:

- Phoenix and Dragon are not casual equals at ordinary soul-master level.
- At divine-beast myth level, Phoenix and Dragon have an ancient rival/enemy relationship.
- A true Phoenix can be terrifyingly high-tier, but this is not the same as a six-year-old with a newly awakened Fire Phoenix martial soul.
- Yan Shuo may inherit instinctive phoenix defiance under dragon pressure later, but he does not receive Phoenix God Creation, Dragon God power, or Blue Demon Bird ancestry as a current fact.

- A Fire Phoenix martial soul is still powerful and meaningful.

- A dragon bloodline user does not automatically reveal all secrets or overpower all scenes; effect depends on stage, exposure, and pressure.

For Yan Shuo and Lan Xuanyu:
