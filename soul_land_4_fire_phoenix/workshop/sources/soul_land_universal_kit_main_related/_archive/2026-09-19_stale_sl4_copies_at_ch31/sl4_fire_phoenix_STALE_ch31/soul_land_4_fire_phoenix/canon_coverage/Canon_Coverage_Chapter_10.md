## 2. Canon pillars to preserve

Horizon Building incident:

- Location: Horizon Building in Zi Luo City, 128 floors, World of Trampolines entertainment area.
- Lan Xuanyu and Nan Cheng are inside the building.
- Lan Xuanyu enjoys trampolines and jumps unusually high because his body has become heavier/stronger after Nana's training.
- City of Sin attack includes technology, mind control/interference, mechas, bombs, internal betrayal/preparation, and a hidden battleship.
- Zhan Jing is a City of Sin envoy/master of crime/mind control and the apparent bait.
- Ye Feng initially traps/captures Zhan Jing, then loses control when King of Eternal Darkness threatens 12,345 hostages and bombs.
- The main crime is stealing enormous digital funds through the Horizon Building/central bank link, not simple robbery of the building account.
- Nan Cheng protects Lan Xuanyu, is injured by the black mecha, and Lan Xuanyu stands before her despite fear.
- Nana arrives at the last moment, stops/controls space-time enough to save them, destroys terrorists, heals injured, and later destroys the fleeing battleship.
- Lan Xuanyu hears Nana say "Don't be afraid" and later sees the distant fireball as "fireworks."
- Public/official knowledge is incomplete and distorted after Nana's citywide mental wave and Federation investigation.
- News later reports 131 dead and over 800 injured, with the City of Sin blamed.

Nana / Lan Xuanyu aftermath:

- Lan Xuanyu comes home shaken and wants to become stronger immediately.
- Nan Cheng now has absolute gratitude/trust toward Nana after the rescue.
- Nana remains calm on the surface but the incident leads to Federation/Heaven Dou investigators finding her.
- Chapter 10 may end with Nana sensing that "they came"; do not complete the departure scene unless Chapter 11.

Ye Feng aftermath:

- Ye Feng is not incompetent but was outplayed by the scale of the City of Sin operation and hostage pressure.
- He is investigated/isolated afterward due casualties and failed operation consequences.
- This can affect Ye Lingtong indirectly, but she should not learn full classified details.

- Has a child's urge to run toward danger because his friend may be there.
- Learns that protecting the people beside him is not cowardice.
- Uses short, controlled Dawnfeather Mantle under stress but does not become a hero of the main incident.

- Enjoys trampolines at first, then faces real fear and helplessness.
- Stands before injured Nan Cheng despite being terrified.
