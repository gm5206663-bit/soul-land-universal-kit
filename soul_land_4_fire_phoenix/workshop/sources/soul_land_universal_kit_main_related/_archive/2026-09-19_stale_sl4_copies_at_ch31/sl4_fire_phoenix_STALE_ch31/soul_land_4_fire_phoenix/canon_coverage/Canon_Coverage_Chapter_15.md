## 2. Canon pillars to preserve

Soul World rules:

- Soul World is not a harmless ordinary game. It has high realism and minimum 30% pain sensitivity.
- Real-body data matters. Real strength, control, and flaws carry into the simulation.
- Training can improve real combat experience.
- Elite Junior Class students have special under-12 access but are limited to the Heaven Luo Institute town unless they earn more permission.
- Soul coins matter. Junior scenario cost/reward creates pressure even for children.

Dorm 333's first scenario:

- Dorm 333 enters as Lan Xuanyu, Qian Lei, and Liu Feng.
- The junior scenario is a colosseum against at least fifty ten-year Lava Hounds and three hundred-year Lava Hounds.
- Liu Feng's speed is impressive but he outruns formation.
- Qian Lei's first summon is useless: a tiny white cat that runs away.
- Lan Xuanyu is the one who begins saving the situation through coordination, ice, Blue Silver Grass, terrain control, and calm orders.
- Liu Feng's White Dragon Flash can redirect fireballs with Lan Xuanyu's help, but it costs him and injures his hands.
- Dorm 333 should feel desperate and unreliable before the Chapter 89 assist-summon beat.

Chapter 89 firewall:

- Yan Shuo must not trigger or explain the silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass interaction with Qian Lei's Gate of Summoning.
- Yan Shuo must not summon or meet the young girl through Dorm 333's gate in Chapter 15.
- Yan Shuo may later hear limited public/class gossip if canon/prose earns it, but he cannot know private mechanics from inside Dorm 333's scenario.
