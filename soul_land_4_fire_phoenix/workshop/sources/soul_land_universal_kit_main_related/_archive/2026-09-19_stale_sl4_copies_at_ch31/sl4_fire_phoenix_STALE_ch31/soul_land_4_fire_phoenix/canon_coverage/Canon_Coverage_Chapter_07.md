## 2. Canon beats to preserve

### Nana leaving Heaven Dou

- Nana is gone from the Heaven Dou Planet institute room.
- Yun Yan finds a note saying Nana found something she wants to do and may or may not return.
- Nana cuts off/turns off her Soul Guidance communication device tracking.
- This is a private Nana/Yun Yan/institute problem and should not reach Yan Shuo.

### Return spacecraft and consciousness training

- On Spacecraft 7703, Lan Xuanyu meditates during the seven-day return.
- Nana contacts him through his consciousness/spiritual space.
- Lan Xuanyu believes Nana may be somewhere else on the spacecraft at first.
- Nana explains that only she can reach him for now and only while the distance is not too far.
- Nana turns teaching into a childlike chase game.
- The footwork is learned through repeated attempts, not an instant download.
- Lan Xuanyu gets tired because Spiritual Power is consumed in the consciousness exercise.
- The game repeats during the return journey and increases his meditation time/quality.

### Return to Heaven Luo / Zi Luo City

- Spacecraft 7703 returns through Heaven Luo Planet's atmosphere and lands at Heaven Luo Planet Space Center.
- Lan Xuanyu looks for Nana after disembarking.
- Nana's voice tells him she will visit him at home.
- Nana later appears in Zi Luo City near the Lan family route; she did not buy an ordinary return ticket.
- Lan Xiao may assume she used high-level resources, but the prose should not over-explain her true method.

### Rank 12 and nutrition

- After returning home, Lan Xuanyu's Spirit Power Test Apparatus shows he has broken through to Rank 12.
- This is slow for innate full spirit power but still meaningful to the Lan family.
- Lan Xuanyu's appetite has increased.
- Nana says he needs nutrients and spiritual energy in food.
- Lan Xiao asks about medicinal cuisine/rare ingredients.
- Rare ingredients are expensive/restricted and associated with high-ranked institutes, mother planet resources, and the two soul beast planets.

### Nana as home tutor

- Lan Xiao and Nan Cheng treat Nana as Titled Douluo-level and address her respectfully.
- Nana does not ask for money; lodging is enough.
- The Lan family gives her a guest room, not the cultivation room.
- Nan Cheng wants to observe lessons because Nana is still a stranger.
- Nana allows observation.

### Pressure-resistant exercise

- Nana applies carefully controlled pressure.
- Nan Cheng participates/observes and learns Nana's pressure precision is far above ordinary understanding.
- Nan Cheng releases her ice martial soul and shows four yellow and two purple rings.
- Lan Xuanyu uses Mysterious Heaven Skill circulation and silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass/water control under pressure.
- Nana shifts pressure direction/intensity to train control rather than simply crush him.

### Golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass and danger

- Under pressure and resolve, Lan Xuanyu's right-hand golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass activates/awakens more fully.
- It brings warmth, confidence, strength, and body lightness.
- The right-hand martial soul also has a white ring but previously no usable skill.
- Gold and silver energies rush back from both arms and clash near his heart.
- Lan Xuanyu's face/body show gold and silver lines, eyes shifting gold/silver, and dangerous swelling/fluctuation.
- Nana intervenes immediately and recognizes two powerful, stubborn, opposing energies.
- The crisis is private to the Lan household; Yan Shuo must not witness it by default.

### Chapter 40 stabilization

- Nana concentrates with Lan Xuanyu in her arms and her hand on his chest.
- Lan Xuanyu dreams/perceives golden and silver lights drawn into a spinning/entangled state.
- The process takes three days before the visible lines vanish.
- Nana explains that one energy alone would greatly help his cultivation, but both together slow/counteract growth and create danger.
- She has stabilized them through a spinning equilibrium with limited contact.
- Future danger appears when the energies release during major breakthroughs or body growth.
- Nana specifically points toward the twentieth-rank stage as a major future check.
- Strengthening his body and meridians is the immediate solution.
- Nana says the energies also nourish his body, especially the golden energy.

## 4. Natural butterfly plan

Chapter 7 must not make Yan Shuo merely observe canon from outside. It also must not let him steal private Lan-family destiny scenes.

Natural butterfly effects selected:

1. **Public record butterfly:** Yan Shuo's corrected Rank 12/yellow-ring record changes teacher attention, school expectations, parent talk, and possible jealousy/curiosity among students.
2. **Friendship return butterfly:** when Lan Xuanyu returns, Yan Shuo has changed visibly through first-ring stabilization, while Lan Xuanyu has changed quietly through Nana's return-flight footwork and later private tutoring.
3. **Mutual-noticing butterfly:** Lan Xuanyu may notice Yan Shuo's yellow ring and warm feather aura; Yan Shuo may notice Lan Xuanyu's step rhythm, increased appetite, or altered familiar pressure without understanding its source.
4. **Training contrast butterfly:** Yan Shuo's family-supervised Fire Phoenix/soul-spirit control contrasts with Lan Xuanyu's Nana-guided pressure training. These should develop in parallel and then affect shared school scenes later.
5. **Resource butterfly:** both boys now need more food/nutrition for different reasons. Yan Shuo's need comes from Dawnflame Kite integration and Fire Phoenix adaptation; Lan Xuanyu's comes from bloodline/body strengthening. Do not merge the causes.
6. **Pressure butterfly:** if Yan Shuo later stands near post-crisis Lan Xuanyu, the familiar hidden pressure can feel sharper or better layered. Yan Shuo's body may breathe, heat, and posture-adjust, but he does not identify gold/silver dragon bloodline.
7. **Social butterfly:** Ye Lingtong, teachers, and classmates should not treat Yan Shuo as a background student anymore. A Rank 12 first-year with a yellow ring is a public classroom fact.
