## 2. SL4 Chapter 106 — Golden Lion Roar

- `https://novelfull.com/soul-land-iv-douluo-dalu-ultimate-fighting/chapter-106-golden-lion-roar.html`

### Canon beats

Lu Qianxun team approach and Dorm 333 exposure:

- Lu Qianxun's team has already defeated one team.
- Chang Jianyi suggests slowing down and preserving peak state.
- Chang Jianyi is not merely carried by Lu; he has strong judgment and SP80, third after Lan Xuanyu and Qian Lei in canon context.
- Ye Lingtong wants to continue fighting, but Lu accepts Chang's advice.
- Ye Lingtong notices rubble/dead end and suggests resting there.
- Lu's team moves toward Dorm 333's hiding place.
- Dorm 333 hears them behind the rubble.
- Lan Xuanyu signals Qian Lei to summon and Liu Feng to prepare.
- Gold and silver patterned Blue Silver Grass wrap Qian Lei and Liu Feng.
- Qian Lei opens Gate of Summoning; Lan Xuanyu strengthens the Gate with silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass.
- Liu Feng receives Lan Xuanyu's support; White Dragon Spear dragon eyes open.
- Lu's team arrives just as Gate completes.
- Dong Qianqiu's voice comes from the Gate: "Not free!"
- Both teams hear it, Dorm 333 is exposed, and the failed summon makes Qian Lei effectively unable to fight.

Lu Qianxun assault:

- Lu releases Golden Lion King; yellow ring rises; body grows to adult-like size; hair turns golden.
- He smashes rubble into projectiles to suppress the hidden team.
- Ye Lingtong releases Celestial Dragon/Celestial Body and follows.
- Lan Xuanyu reacts by throwing Liu Feng upward and using the heavy battle axe to block rocks.
- Liu Feng targets Chang Jianyi because Dorm 333 needs to remove one opponent quickly.
- Chang Jianyi's ring skill creates a stiff human-figure/white-halo defense that slows Liu Feng.
- Liu Feng still pierces Chang Jianyi's shoulder and pins him, but does not immediately finish him.
- Lu uses his first soul skill, Golden Lion Roar.
- Golden Lion Roar is amplified by the narrow passage and functions as both spiritual and soundwave pressure.
- Liu Feng and Qian Lei lose fighting ability temporarily.

Important canon note:

- Chapter 106 says Golden Lion King is true peak-grade among lion spirits and stronger than ordinary dragon spirits, but its meridian strengthening is not as strong as dragon or phoenix type spirits. This supports both Lan Xuanyu's Dragon Roar reversal and Yan Shuo's Fire Phoenix body/meridian significance, but Yan Shuo must not witness or analyze this scene.

Fetched source:

- `https://novelfull.com/soul-land-iv-douluo-dalu-ultimate-fighting/chapter-107-dragon-roar.html`

- Ye Lingtong realizes the hidden opponents are Lan Xuanyu, Liu Feng, and Qian Lei.

- His golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass becomes pure gold; eyes turn golden with madness.
- Lan Xuanyu releases a fierce Dragon Roar.
- Dong Qianqiu immediately retreats back into the Gate and raises an ice wall; the ice wall shatters.
- Lu Qianxun bleeds from seven orifices and stumbles back.

- Ye Lingtong's Celestial Body collapses and she goes limp.
