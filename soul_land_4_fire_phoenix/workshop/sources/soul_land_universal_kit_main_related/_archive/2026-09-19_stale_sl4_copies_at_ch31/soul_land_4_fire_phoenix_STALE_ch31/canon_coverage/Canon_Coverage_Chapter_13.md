## Canon receipts by chapter

### Chapter 71

- Lan Xuanyu remains unconscious after the simulation injury.
- Inside the coma, he experiences a black/dark state, then a vision-like ancient battlefield with enormous dragon-like beings, giant humans, sorrow/anger, and gold/silver conflict.
- In Zi Luo City's hospital ICU, Lan Xuanyu has been comatose for about twenty days when his body suddenly convulses.
- Gold and silver vein-like patterns appear across him, with faint rainbow-colored light at the intersections.
- Doctors calm him with medical measures, but they do not understand the root spirit/bloodline issue.
- Tang Le is performing in Heaven Luo City and singing Remembrance when he abruptly senses something and leaves mid-performance as golden light.
- Le Qingling covers for him by telling the audience it is a surprise.

### Chapter 72

- Tang Le appears silently in Zi Luo City's Huixin Hospital ICU while Nan Cheng is asleep from exhaustion.
- Cameras stop working around him.
- He feels an inexplicable bond with Lan Xuanyu and heals/stabilizes him with golden light.
- He senses the gold-silver vortex/body conflict and suppresses the violent energy reaction.
- Lan Xuanyu's Golden-patterned and Silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass appear.
- Tang Le instinctively manifests his own blue-gold vine-like Blue Silver Grass, which shocks him and deepens his confusion.
- Le Qingling contacts Tang Le urgently because he left the concert for around half an hour; he returns after touching Lan Xuanyu's face.

### Chapter 73

- The Heaven Luo City concert almost becomes a disaster because Tang Le vanished for too long.
- Tang Le returns and salvages the concert by singing a new song associated with protecting time/hope/protecting someone precious.
- His blue-gold vines and voice calm the restless fans.
- The audience accepts the disappearance as part of the performance/surprise.
- The slogan around protecting time and protecting the beloved/important person becomes tied to Tang Le's new song.

### Chapter 74

- Tang Le asks to prioritize the Zi Luo City concert.
- Le Qingling probes him and finds no normal Spirit Power signature.
- Early morning in Zi Luo City, Lan Xuanyu begins moving and wakes.
- Doctors confirm there is no obvious sequela after checks and move him from ICU to general ward.
- Lan Xuanyu remembers golden light entering/protecting his consciousness, but he does not know Tang Le came.
- Lan Xiao warns Lan Xuanyu to rest and avoid using Spirit Power while recovering.

### Chapter 75
