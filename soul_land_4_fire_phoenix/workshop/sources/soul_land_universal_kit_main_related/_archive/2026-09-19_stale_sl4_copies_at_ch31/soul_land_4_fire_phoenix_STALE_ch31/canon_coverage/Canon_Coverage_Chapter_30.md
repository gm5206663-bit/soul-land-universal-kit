- Mu announces to Lan Xuanyu's team that their next match is against Lu Qianxun's team.

- Qian Lei has opened his eyes, but he has not recovered enough Spiritual Power to summon again.

Lu Qianxun team entry:

- Lu Qianxun, Ye Lingtong, and Chang Jianyi appear.
- Lu Qianxun shows two yellow soul rings and Soul Grandmaster cultivation.
- Lu's body swells with Golden Lion King pressure.
- Lu is arrogant/proud enough that he would prefer facing Dorm333 at full strength, not when Qian Lei is effectively unavailable.

- Dorm333 is effectively Lan Xuanyu + Liu Feng versus Lu/Ye/Chang, with Qian not able to summon.
- Chang Jianyi's Slow Down control ability counters Liu Feng's speed route.
- Lu's Golden Lion Roar is a severe threat.
- Ye Lingtong has Celestial Body speed/strength/defense.
- Lan decides they can only fight because a loss would erase remaining matches.

Lan's plan:

- Lan asks Liu Feng to lift all three opponents at all cost.
- Liu worries he may not get close because of Golden Lion Roar.
- Lan chooses to gamble that Lu will not use Golden Lion Roar immediately.
- Lan releases Blue Silver Grass in both hands, boosts/pushes Liu Feng forward, and follows using ice under his feet to glide.

Opening clash:

- Chang Jianyi releases Slow Down.
- Ye Lingtong charges Liu Feng head-on with Celestial Body active.
- Liu Feng still attacks with trust in Lan.
- Liu Feng's White Dragon Spear soul skill lifts within a five-meter radius.
- With Lan's golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass enhancement and Liu Feng risking everything, the rising force lifts Ye Lingtong, Lu Qianxun, and Chang Jianyi.
- Lu uses Soul Power to force himself down and stay grounded.
- Ye is only lifted about two meters; Lu catches her by the back of her top and helps her turn the situation.
- Ye kicks the White Dragon Spear with transferred force, sending Liu Feng flying backward toward Lan.
- Ye had calculated the angle so that Liu Feng would crash into Lan and leave them open to follow-up attacks.
- Ye knows Lu has worked hard for months to wipe away the final-exam humiliation.
