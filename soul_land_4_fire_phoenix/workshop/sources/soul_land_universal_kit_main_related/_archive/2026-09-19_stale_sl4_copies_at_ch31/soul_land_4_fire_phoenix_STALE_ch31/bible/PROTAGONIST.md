Yan Shuo'er is warm, bright, loyal, emotionally direct, and proud in the way a talented child can be proud. She wants to stand beside people honestly instead of being protected as a mascot or used as a replacement.

The correction does **not** erase her established personality. Being raised publicly as a boy adds discipline, practical caution, and a habit of controlling appearance/aura, but it does not make her a different person.

She is brave but not reckless by default. Her growth should show restraint, learning, recovery, and changed choices, not only stronger flame.
