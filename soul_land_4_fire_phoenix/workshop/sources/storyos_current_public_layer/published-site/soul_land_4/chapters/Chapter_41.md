# Chapter 41: No Loopholes in the Sky

The claws came before the bodies.

Lan Xuanyu heard them through the stone at his back: hundreds, then thousands, thin hard impacts striking the valley floor in a rhythm too chaotic to be rain and too numerous to be one beast. Dust trembled loose from the boulder beside him. Heat crawled under the rock as if the valley itself had decided to wake.

Then the first lizard came into view.

It was longer than Lan was tall, skinny but not weak, with brown hide veined by dull red lines that pulsed like magma under cracked earth. Its claws dug into stone. Its narrow head jerked toward the students with an animal hunger the simulation rendered too well.

Another appeared behind it.

Then ten.

Then the slope seemed to move.

"Ground Fire Lizards," Lan said.

His voice came out calmer than he felt.

Qian Lei pressed himself against the boulder beside him. "How many?"

Lan looked down the valley.

The answer did not need numbers.

They came from cracks, vents, ridges, and the red-brown ground itself, a flood of fire-attributed soul beasts spilling in every direction. In ordinary records, Ground Fire Lizards lived near volcanoes in packs of twenty or thirty. They had strong bodies, fire element, and enough volcanic resistance that even low-year individuals were troublesome in groups. If they crossed ten thousand years, legends said they might transform toward Ground Fire Dragons.

This was not a pack.

This was a tide.

"Too many," Liu Feng said.

A shout rang out from the right.

"Upward! Retreat to the peak!"

Lu Qianxun had already moved when he shouted. He was not the class monitor in this world. The badge and public center of the Elite Junior Class belonged to Yan Shuo. But Lu remained Lu Qianxun: decisive, strong, and unwilling to let fear choose for him. Golden light flickered around his body as he led Ye Lingtong and Chang Jianyi toward the steep mountain wall.

The choice was obvious enough that several teams obeyed at once.

The valley floor was death by numbers.

The mountain at least offered height.

Yan's voice cut across the heat a heartbeat later. "Teams of three! Do not bunch together. Keep lanes apart! Song, left shield. Luo, read the high line."

Lan glanced that way despite himself.

Yan stood half-crouched behind a dark ridge, copper-red hair catching firelight in restrained red-gold. He had not released his full Fire Phoenix. That restraint mattered. His body looked ready to bloom into wings, but his team did not move like a phoenix with followers; Song's Stoneplate Tortoise shield was already rising where falling debris would come, and Luo had dropped low, eyes flicking across ridge, vent, and slope as if drawing invisible lines.

Then the second roar struck.

The Ground Fire Lizards surged faster.

The sound that followed was not their claws.

Boom.

Boom.

Boom.

Something huge was walking through the valley.

Lan turned.

A gigantic shadow emerged from heat haze and dust.

For one instant, his mind refused scale. Then the beast lifted its head, and the valley became smaller around it.

It towered over thirty meters. Dark red scales covered a long, powerful body. Spikes ran from the back of its head down to the end of its thick tail. Two massive legs and the tail supported it upright, while a pair of small wings beat behind its back, not large enough for true flight but enough to make each step less clumsy than a body that size had any right to be. Heat distorted the air within ten meters of it. Stone under its feet blackened.

Qian Lei's voice cracked. "What is that thing?"

No one answered.

Not immediately.

Even Lan, who knew more soul-beast theory than most children his age wanted to know, had never seen such a creature. But instinct did not need a name.

Ten thousand years.

At least.

Not an ordinary ten-thousand-year soul beast either.

Its gigantic eyes fixed on the students scattered along the mountain waist.

Then it opened its mouth.

"Run!" someone screamed.

The fireball came like a small spaceship shell.

It grew as it flew, dragging a long blazing tail behind it. The surrounding air burned before it arrived. For a moment, Lan's space-warship training and Soul Master training overlapped so sharply that he did not think of the beast as a creature at all. It was artillery. A huge ballistic threat with splash range, thermal pressure, and terrain damage.

His body moved before fear finished forming.

Left hand caught Qian Lei.

Right hand shot golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass toward Liu Feng.

The vine coiled around Liu Feng's arm.

After Rank20, Lan no longer needed direct physical touch to support his teammates. As long as the Blue Silver Grass connected, the amplification flowed.

Liu Feng trusted him without asking.

White Dragon Spear appeared in a flash. Two yellow rings rose beneath Liu Feng's feet. The spear butt struck the mountain wall.

The three of them shot upward and sideways like a white streak dragging two shadows.

Qian Lei made a sound that was not quite a word.

Lan ignored it.

Liu Feng's speed under amplification became terrifyingly precise. The White Dragon Spear touched stone, bent, released, touched again. Seven meters. Eight. A turn along the wall. Another burst. The fireball struck the place where several students had stood a breath earlier.

Bang—!

The mountain shook.

Heat and shattered rock exploded outward.

Lan's left hand opened.

Silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass flashed with cold light. Icy mist spread from him, thin but immediate, wrapping over Qian Lei and Liu Feng. The heatwave slammed into them before the mist could fully settle.

Lan pulled Qian Lei close and twisted his own body in front.

Ice condensed behind him.

At the same moment, his second yellow ring on the golden-patterned side lit almost by instinct.

Hegemonic Body.

Gold light shimmered over his skin.

The shock struck.

Pain came first, broad and heavy. Then, strangely, strength followed it. Part of the impact sank into him instead of simply breaking over him, sharpening his senses and tightening his muscles. The new skill did not make him invincible. It did not erase heat, stone, or fear. But it let his body take what would have thrown him apart and turn a sliver of it into power.

They were still sent flying.

Liu Feng stabbed the White Dragon Spear into the wall and dragged them back into a controlled line.

Farther along the mountain, Yan's team weathered the same explosion differently.

Song Yichen's shield hit stone with a dull boom, wide enough to cover the fall-line where debris would naturally pour. Luo's voice snapped through the dust. "Right sweep! Low ridge!"

Yan moved with them instead of above them. Red-gold flame flickered over his shoulders in the shape of wings that were not fully opened. Dawnfeather light layered over Song's shield for one breath, not as a boast but as a heat-break. His Fire Phoenix aura cut the worst of the temperature from around his team, and the red-gold glow in his hair brightened under pressure.

Then the gigantic beast's head shifted by a fraction.

Luo saw it first. "Heat signature! Fold it!"

Yan's flame collapsed inward immediately.

The public Fire Phoenix did not vanish. It compressed. The brilliance that had made classmates stare in the academy tucked itself close to bone, breath, and meridian. A heartbeat later, the beast's gaze slid away toward a larger cluster of students scrambling together below.

Lan saw only a flicker of that exchange from the corner of his eye, but he understood enough.

Shrek did not make tests with simple gifts.

Even fire could become a target.

The huge soul beast lowered its head. It bit a Ground Fire Lizard that had failed to flee in time, chewed twice, and swallowed. Its tail swept sideways, crushing and scattering lizards like dry branches. Then it began to run after the fleeing students.

"Xuanyu," Qian Lei said, voice still trembling. "What exactly was that?"

Lan forced his breathing steady.

"I don't know its exact species. It should be mutated from a Ground Fire Lizard line. Ground Fire Lizards can become Ground Fire Dragons after ten thousand years, but this one has wings and red scales. It is moving toward a real fire dragon type. Scarlet dragons are fire-property dragons, so... Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon."

Qian Lei stared at the monster below. "Do you think I can summon one someday?"

Liu Feng nearly slipped.

Lan looked at Qian Lei. "You can consider it after you reach seven rings."

"That is not a no."

"It is a no with a future attached."

"Very encouraging."

"Also," Lan said, remembering suddenly, "the one I asked you to store is still there?"

Qian Lei slapped his chest, almost lost his grip, and immediately hugged the rock harder. "Of course. I am extremely reliable."

Liu Feng said, "Your shamelessness has excellent defense."

Qian Lei snapped, "Get lost."

The joke lasted less than one breath.

Below, four teams that had fallen or chosen to drop toward the lower slope were being surrounded by Ground Fire Lizards. They had gathered together instinctively because numbers felt safe. Shields, attacks, control skills, and cries overlapped. For a few seconds, the combined force pushed the lizards back.

The Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon turned toward them.

Its mouth opened again.

Lan's eyes widened.

"Liu Feng! Wider! Get away from Jin Xiang and Lu Qianxun's teams!"

Liu Feng obeyed without asking why. He changed angle immediately, no longer climbing straight toward the same upper path. The White Dragon Spear struck a side ridge, and the three of them cut away from the other rising teams.

Qian Lei clutched Lan's sleeve. "Why?"

"It is choosing targets by number," Lan said quickly. "Or by clustered presence. The first shot hit where we were gathered. Now it is aiming at the largest group below. If teams stay too close, we become one target."

Below, the second fireball descended.

The explosion swallowed the lower cluster in red light.

Fire waves, stone, and heat burst outward. Ground Fire Lizards flew in pieces. Human figures vanished into light. Others were thrown apart, rolling across rock while lizards swarmed toward them.

Seven elimination flashes lit almost at once.

One-third of the class had nearly vanished in a single judgment mistake.

Lan's stomach clenched.

They were classmates.

Some had argued with him, some had ignored him, some had shared years of training without ever becoming close. None of that mattered under a fireball.

He drew in a breath with all the force his lungs could hold.

"Spread out!" Lan shouted. "Teams of three, separate! Don't gather! The big one targets clusters! Spread out!"

His voice carried strangely in the valley, thrown by stone and heat.

Yan's voice followed from another ridge, sharper and larger. "Listen to him! Three-person lanes! Do not hide behind one team! Separate!"

That second command mattered.

Lan's analysis reached those who trusted his strange judgment. Yan's authority as class monitor reached those who still looked for the public center of the class when panic made thinking small. Between the two voices, hesitation broke.

Lu Qianxun acted first among the nearby teams.

The third fireball had already begun forming, and this time its path threatened the space where Lu's team and Jin Xiang's team had drifted too close together.

"Jump!" Lu roared.

He grabbed Chang Jianyi. Ye Lingtong moved with him without needing explanation. The three of them threw themselves down from the wall.

The fireball exploded above them.

A burning boulder broke loose and fell toward their heads. Chang Jianyi's first soul skill slowed it just enough. Lu lifted his head and unleashed Golden Lion Roar. The boulder shattered. Ye Lingtong's body flashed white as she checked their fall, palm striking the mountain wall to bleed speed until the three of them landed lower, battered but alive.

Lu did not look at the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon first.

He looked toward Lan.

Even from a distance, Lan saw the acknowledgment in his eyes.

Amazing, that look said without needing words.

Jin Xiang's flying team veered away too, startled into obedience. Several scattered survivors below separated into two-person and three-person pockets.

The Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon's behavior changed at once.

It still swept its tail and spewed flame, but its attacks fell among the Ground Fire Lizards rather than tracking the small teams directly. The terrifying beast seemed almost blind to properly separated groups in this first rhythm of the test.

Lan let out a breath.

The guess had been right.

Right did not mean safe.

It only meant they were not dead yet.

Liu Feng continued upward along the side wall, carrying Lan and Qian Lei with practiced bursts. The higher they went, the thinner the lizard tide became, but the steeper the wall grew. Lan kept the golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass coiled around Liu Feng, feeding support in pulses rather than one continuous flood to preserve soul power.

Yan's team climbed on a separate line.

They could have gone faster if Yan opened his wings fully.

He did not.

Song would not keep up in open air. Luo could move fast, but his sensor calls needed footing. More importantly, the moment Yan's Fire Phoenix aura expanded, the huge beast below noticed the heat-signature flare. Once was warning enough.

So Dorm336 climbed like a team.

Song anchored.

Luo called.

Yan moved between air and wall in short, controlled wing-flicks, never long enough to become a bright target in the sky, never far enough to make Song's shield meaningless.

A weaker two-person remnant tried to scramble toward them after losing its third member.

"Class monitor!" one cried. "Let us—"

"No cluster!" Yan snapped, and the harshness in his own voice hurt him more than the heat. "Left ridge! Three rocks down! Song, cover their crossing for two breaths!"

Song shifted his shield, taking a burst of lizard fire on the Stoneplate Tortoise defense. Yan sent a narrow red-gold line across the ground, hot enough to make the lizards recoil without swelling into a beacon. Luo's hand cut through the air. "Now!"

The two students dove for the left ridge instead of hiding behind Dorm336.

One survived the crossing.

The other vanished under a lizard leap half a breath too late.

Yan's jaw tightened.

He did not fly back.

Song's shield was under pressure. Luo had already called the next attack. The class monitor could not save a class by abandoning his team.

Shrek's first lesson had teeth.

Lan's team reached the mountain peak first.

Liu Feng's spear hooked over the edge, and the three of them pulled high enough to see the other side.

Then all three froze.

Another valley lay beyond the peak.

It was not empty.

It was a mirror of their own disaster: Ground Fire Lizards flooding red-brown stone, another gigantic Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon roaring through smoke, young Soul Masters scrambling like frightened insects across the terrain. Fewer remained there than on their side.

Qian Lei's eyes widened.

"Get down!"

Lan did not question him.

He pulled Liu Feng, and Liu Feng pulled the spear free. The three dropped flat against the mountain wall just as two massive fireballs streaked toward the peak from opposite sides.

The explosions crossed overhead and to the side, heat roaring past so fiercely that Lan's hair lifted. He shoved soul power into Liu Feng, and the three slid along the wall rather than away from it.

Lan understood in the same instant Liu Feng did.

The peak was not an exit.

It was a trigger.

Reach too high, and both valleys treated you as a target.

On another line, Jin Xiang's team reached the upper air and saw the same danger. They reacted by doing what flying-type Soul Masters naturally trusted most.

They flew.

For one breath, it looked clever.

Ground Fire Lizards could not fly. The mountain wall could no longer trap them. Jin Xiang's wings spread, and his team glided outward, relieved terror becoming confidence too soon.

Then the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon below lifted its head toward the sky.

A pillar of fire shot upward.

It did not aim at a person.

It aimed at the heavens.

The pillar burst apart high overhead, and fiery meteors began to fall.

Not randomly.

Lan's scalp went cold.

"Close to the wall!" he shouted. "It targets flight!"

The first meteors found Jin Xiang's team like hunting birds.

Red impacts swallowed them before they could scream properly. Three elimination flashes vanished inside the fiery rain.

Qian Lei went pale.

Liu Feng pressed himself flatter against the stone.

Lan stared at the burning sky.

No loopholes.

Only opportunities.

Shrek had prepared for the obvious answer before the students even thought of it.

Yan saw the meteor shower from below the peak.

For half a heartbeat, Fire Phoenix instinct rose inside him in furious answer. The sky was where wings belonged. Fire falling from above should have been something to meet, split, dominate.

Luo's voice cut through that instinct. "High sky kills! Wall line! Heat above us!"

Song slammed his shield into a narrow ledge and braced his whole body behind it. "Return point set!"

Yan folded his wings tight.

The decision hurt his pride.

It saved them.

He used the Fire Phoenix not to challenge the sky, but to manage the heat rolling along the wall. Red-gold feathers flared low, curving over Song and Luo like a brief mantle, while the true force stayed compressed. Meteors screamed past above, close enough that the shock hammered Song's shield and shook Luo's teeth together. Stone fragments cut across Yan's cheek and vanished into simulation pain.

He did not rise.

He did not chase Jin Xiang.

He did not turn the trial into a spectacle of flame.

When the meteor shower passed, Dorm336 was still alive.

Not untouched.

Alive.

Below, the valley had become a sea of fire. The Ground Fire Lizards had curled, scattered, or hidden during the meteor fall. That respite saved some students for a few more minutes, but when the fiery rain ended, the lizards returned in thicker waves.

Lan kept watching.

There were patterns everywhere.

Large clusters drew fireballs.

The peak drew both sides.

Open flight drew meteors.

Wall-hugging at middle height reduced the greatest threats.

Killing lizards gave points but staying alive mattered more.

The test was not asking, Can you defeat the strongest enemy?

It was asking, Can you understand the battlefield before the battlefield understands you?

Lu Qianxun understood enough to copy.

He had seen Lan flatten against the wall before the meteor shower, and he dragged Ye Lingtong and Chang Jianyi into the same posture. Ye Lingtong's Celestial Body endured heat better than most, and Chang Jianyi's Confusion skill turned several Ground Fire Lizards against each other below them, buying space. Lu's Golden Lion King power crushed any lizard that came too close, but he used it more carefully now. Soul power spent too early became death later.

Time crawled.

The lower survivors lasted three minutes longer than Lan expected.

Then one pocket vanished beneath Ground Fire Lizards.

Another tried to climb too quickly, drifted too near a second group, and drew a tail-swept burst of flame from the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon that scattered them into elimination light.

When the valley steadied again, only three complete teams remained on their side.

Dorm333 on the upper wall.

Dorm336 on a separate ridge-wall line.

Lu Qianxun's team lower and to the right.

Lan did not feel victorious.

The empty spaces where classmates had been made the valley larger.

Ground Fire Lizards began climbing.

Lan released silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass.

Cold gathered.

He dragged ice down the wall in a spreading layer, not thick enough to become a shield but smooth enough to ruin claws. The first lizard reached it, slipped, tumbled backward, and struck two more below. A chain of bodies slid down the wall like falling stones.

Qian Lei stared. "I like this plan. This plan involves us not being eaten."

Liu Feng stabbed the White Dragon Spear into the wall, creating a narrow place to sit. "Rest while you can."

"I prefer resting somewhere that does not involve lizards climbing toward my feet."

"Then pass Shrek and complain later."

Across the gap, Lu Qianxun yelled, "Lan Xuanyu! What should we do next? Do you have any ideas?"

Ye Lingtong's glare crossed the distance like a thrown knife. "Less sarcasm from you, string of cash!"

Lu's expression tightened. "Are there loopholes?"

Lan looked up toward the meteor-scarred sky, then down toward the lizard tide, then toward the huge Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon stomping through its own valley like a moving disaster.

"No simple ones."

He was no longer sure they were different things.

Dorm336 had its own version of delay.

Yan could not make ice. He could not turn the wall slippery in Lan's way. Instead, he used heat with a precision that looked almost strange in a fire trial: a low red-gold shimmer across selected cracks, enough to make lizard claws recoil or slip on softened mineral edges, never enough to bloom into a signal. Song held the lower shield angle. Luo called every new lane.

"Above!"

Not from their valley.

From the other side.

Pincered.

This was bad.

Very bad.

Qian Lei looked too.

Longer survival gave points.

Killing soul beasts gave points.

Different soul beasts gave different points.

Different soul beasts.

Maybe.

When it suddenly quickened, Liu Feng dragged them forward before a foot could crush them. Fortunately, speed was not the gigantic beast's strength. Its small wings helped it move, but its size made every change obvious if one watched carefully enough.

On the mountain wall, Yan caught glimpses of them between smoke and fire.

"They're under it," Luo said, disbelief breaking his usual flat tone.

Song's shield rang as another lizard struck. "Under the Dragon?"

"Under its belly," Luo corrected, as if precision made the idea sane.

Yan's eyes fixed through heat haze.

Lan was a tiny shape beneath a monster large enough to swallow teams by accident. Qian Lei and Liu Feng were with him. Alive.

Of course Lan had found the only safe place in a sea of fire and made it the most dangerous-looking place in the valley.

Yan wanted to laugh.

He wanted to curse.

He did neither.

A lizard dropped from above, and he met it with a narrow red-gold palm strike that sent it spinning into the wall below. No full wing. No high flare. No waste.

"We are not following," Luo said before Yan could speak.

"I know," Yan said.

He did.

Dorm333's path belonged to Dorm333. If Dorm336 tried to copy it from the wrong angle, the Scarlet Dragon might notice the extra heat signature or the extra bodies. Song's shield could not protect them under a moving monster. Luo's route lines did not reach that ground safely.

Trust did not always mean moving closer.

Sometimes it meant holding a separate line and believing the other person had seen a door.

The flames outside the Scarlet Dragon gradually died down.

Charred earth spread across the valley where the meteor shower had fallen. Ground Fire Lizards began creeping back from cracks and edges, cautious around the giant beast but hungry everywhere else.

Lan watched the left rear leg.

The beast was turning slightly as it walked, and each turn created a moment when the space behind its left foot fell outside its immediate line of sight. Not safe. Never safe.

Possible.

He leaned close to Liu Feng.

"Get ready," Lan whispered. "When I say go, we slide out from behind its left foot. It is the most likely place it will ignore."

Liu Feng nodded.

Qian Lei tightened his grip on Lan's sleeve with one hand and Liu Feng's belt with the other, as if physical attachment could argue with fate.

Above them, the Ground Fire Scarlet Dragon took another thunderous step.

Outside, lizards hissed from the cooling smoke.

On the walls, the remaining teams fought for breath.

Lan waited for the next footfall.

The first phase of the qualifier had taught them three things.

Do not gather.

Do not trust the sky.

And when no road remained, look beneath the monster.
