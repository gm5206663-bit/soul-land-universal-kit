# Chapter 30: The Color Pressure Found

Yin Tianfan did not look satisfied when children made the correct choice.

He looked interested.

That was worse.

On the observation screen, Lan Xuanyu had just admitted defeat. Liang Zheng's team had already faded from the arena with a clean, earned victory. The concession mark sat beside Dorm333's name like a small stone placed on a scale.

Four victories.

One concession.

Enough to make the class breathe again.

Not enough to make Yin Tianfan stop smiling.

"How many more rounds until Lu Qianxun's team?" he asked.

Mu Zhongtian looked down at the roster. He did not answer immediately, because the question was no longer as simple as it would have been before the semester bent out of its original shape.

In the old class order, Lu Qianxun had been the obvious pressure name: Golden Lion King, rival pride, high cultivation, a team that had once looked like the top of the room. In this class now, another name could not be ignored.

Dorm336.

Yan Shuo.

Rank23. Deep yellow and deep purple. Second place in the Maze. Class monitor. Fire Phoenix pressure that was not merely a number on the roster and not something a normal Rank23 label could explain.

If Yin Tianfan had asked for the heaviest direct-combat hammer, Mu's eyes would have had to stop there.

But Yin had not asked for the heaviest hammer.

He had asked for Lu Qianxun.

And Lu's team was a different kind of pressure: old rivalry, two yellow rings, Golden Lion Roar, Ye Lingtong's stubborn close combat, Chang Jianyi's slow field, and a match-up that attacked Dorm333's exposed shape while Qian Lei still could not safely summon.

Mu understood that.

He still did not like it.

"One more team," he said. "Then Lu Qianxun's team."

Yin Tianfan's eyes brightened as if Mu had handed him exactly the tool he wanted. "Move them forward. Put them in now. Then tell Lan Xuanyu's team that if they lose to Lu Qianxun, the remaining matches count as defeats too."

Mu stared at him.

"Vice president," he said carefully, "that is changing the rules after the exam has begun. The students will be upset. It is not appropriate."

"Appropriate?" Yin Tianfan leaned back. "They just conceded because they calculated the cost. Good. That means they still have mind left to squeeze. If they think they can spend one loss and then wait until Qian Lei recovers, they will hide behind arithmetic."

"Arithmetic is also ability," Mu said before he could stop himself.

Yin's smile widened. "Exactly. Now let us see what happens when arithmetic is cornered."

Mu turned to Ji Hongbin.

It was not that Mu expected mercy from the Demon King. He simply hoped Ji would object because fairness had words even strict teachers respected.

Ji Hongbin looked at the screen where Lan Xuanyu still stood between Qian Lei and the memory of Liang Zheng's guided arrow.

Then he nodded.

"Do it."

Mu's mouth opened slightly.

Ji said, "They have already proved they can use rules, timing, and concession. If this remains a test, we need to know what happens when those paths narrow. It is also unfair to the later teams if Dorm333 can simply spend matches until Qian Lei's mind recovers."

"And if the other students complain?" Mu asked.

Yin waved a hand. "Let them complain at me."

That was not reassuring. It was only convenient.

Mu looked once more at the roster.

The team that should have gone next was not minor.

Dorm336.

Yan Shuo. Song Yichen. Luo Haoran.

Their name glowed directly above Lu Qianxun's line, waiting for the transfer order the original sequence owed them.

That made Yin's command sharper, not softer. Moving Lu forward did not merely adjust a schedule; it skipped the strongest public direct-combat challenger team in the room to use a different key on Dorm333 first.

Perhaps that was pressure.

Perhaps it was debt.

Mu pressed the command.

---

The waiting hall felt the order change before anyone understood it.

Dorm336 straightened as the transfer light began to gather near their side of the room.

Then it stopped.

Yan Shuo's name faded from the preparation board before the light finished forming. A different name appeared in its place.

Dorm316.

Lu Qianxun. Ye Lingtong. Chang Jianyi.

Voices rose at once.

"Wasn't there another team first?"

"Why Lu now?"

"Did the teachers change the order?"

"Because Dorm333 conceded?"

"Because they want Lu to stop them?"

Across the room, Lu Qianxun stood.

He did not look surprised for long. Surprise touched his eyes, then burned away under something harder. He had been waiting for Dorm333 since the Maze, perhaps longer than the Maze. Lan Xuanyu had humiliated his assumptions more than once. A changed order was not fair, but pride did not always dislike unfairness when unfairness gave it a battlefield.

Ye Lingtong rose beside him. Her expression was more complicated. She looked once toward the screen, then toward Lan Xuanyu's name, then away.

Chang Jianyi adjusted his sleeves as if rule changes were only another form of data.

Luo Haoran leaned toward Yan Shuo. "The teachers are cheating now. Educationally."

"They are changing pressure," Song Yichen said.

"That is the polite version of cheating."

Yan Shuo watched Lu's team walk toward the transfer light.

The room's attention followed them. That was natural. Lu still carried weight. Two rings. Golden Lion King. The old strongest-team expectation. The roar that had once turned the maze corridor into a drum.

But Yan did not feel himself placed beneath Lu by the call.

That would have been the old mistake.

Lu was dangerous. Lu was proud. Lu's Golden Lion King pressure could crush weak timing and punish fear. Chang Jianyi's control could break speed routes. Ye Lingtong could turn a clash into a stubborn collision and make the other side pay for underestimating her.

None of that made Lu Yan's ceiling.

In a fair direct fight, without needing a full Dawnflame burst, Yan had routes through Lu's pressure. Fire Phoenix meridians, deep-yellow fire-light boundary, deep-purple skybone frame, and the denser rhythm of two living soul spirits made the comparison more complicated than public rank alone.

But Lan Xuanyu did not fight like Yan.

That was the point.

Yan's fingers rested lightly against his sleeve. Beneath the public boyish posture, Yan Shuo'er felt Dawnflame Kite stir bright and impatient, while Dawn-Iron Phoenix Roc answered with a heavier calm.

Lu was not the highest direct pressure in the room.

He might be the most useful key for forcing open Lan's locked door.

That difference mattered.

"They are not just sending Lu because he is strong," Yan said.

Luo blinked. "That is usually why one sends Lu anywhere."

"They are sending him because of the match-up. Qian Lei has not recovered. Liu Feng hates Slow Down. Golden Lion Roar can interrupt both speed and Spiritual Power. Ye Lingtong can take the first collision without fear."

Song's brows drew together. "And because Lan cannot concede again."

"Maybe," Yan said.

They did not know the full message inside the arena. They only knew the order had changed and that teachers did not move pieces without a reason.

The transfer light swallowed Dorm316.

For a heartbeat, the waiting hall became still.

Then Luo muttered, "I miss when exams only tried to kill our pride in predictable order."

No one laughed loudly.

---

In the arena, Mu Zhongtian's voice arrived before the opponents finished forming.

"Attention, Lan Xuanyu's team. Your next match will be against Lu Qianxun's team. You must win this match. If you lose, all subsequent matches will be regarded as defeats as well."

Liu Feng stopped so suddenly that the White Dragon Spear nearly tilted out of line.

"What?" he blurted. "Why would the later matches count too? Are we being punished?"

Lan Xuanyu stared toward the observation room he could not see.

He was a child. He could understand many things quickly, but not every adult intention could be decoded in the breath before a fight. He knew only the shape of the trap.

They were not allowed to spend this match.

Three figures appeared on the other side.

Lu Qianxun stood in the center, golden hair already brightening. Two yellow rings rose from under his feet, one after the other, clear and undeniable.

Soul Grandmaster.

Beside him, Ye Lingtong's eyes fixed on Lan with an expression that mixed challenge, old confusion, and stubborn resolve. Chang Jianyi stood slightly back, pale and calm, the kind of calm that did not need to be loud to be dangerous.

Qian Lei opened his eyes behind Lan.

Lan looked back at him.

Qian Lei shook his head once and gave a smile so bitter it barely counted as a smile. "I have rested for a while," he said. "Not for a miracle."

That meant no summon.

Not safely.

Not reliably.

Lan turned forward again.

Two against three.

Worse than the last match.

Liang Zheng's team had trapped them with a plan. Lu Qianxun's team brought pressure, pride, and a control route that struck directly at Liu Feng's speed. If Chang Jianyi slowed Liu Feng even once at the wrong time, Ye could collide, Lu could roar, and the whole fight could collapse before Lan found a second answer.

But losing meant the remaining matches were gone.

There was no longer room to choose defeat.

Lu Qianxun's body swelled. The Golden Lion King martial soul did not arrive politely. It pushed out through bone, muscle, hair, throat, and pride. He became larger, broader, more adult in outline, and the pressure rolling from him made the arena air feel narrower.

He did not charge immediately.

That was not hesitation.

It was arrogance with discipline inside it.

Lu Qianxun wanted to defeat Dorm333. He wanted it clean. He wanted Qian Lei at full strength, Liu Feng unexcused, Lan Xuanyu without a reason to say it had been unfair.

But the exam did not care what pride wanted.

Lan's shoulders lifted in a small, helpless shrug.

If the teachers were forcing them, then the answer had to be forced too.

"Liu Feng," he said.

"En?"

Lan's voice lowered. "I will enhance you. You have to lift all three of them. At any cost. Leave the rest to me."

Liu Feng's eyes widened slightly. "All three? With Lu there? His Golden Lion Roar is too strong. If he uses it first, I may not even get close."

"Then we gamble that he will not use it first."

"That is a terrible sentence."

"Can you do it?"

Liu Feng took one breath.

His hands steadied on the spear.

"I can try."

"Trying is enough if it buys the right moment."

Blue Silver Grass unfurled from Lan's hands, gold and silver patterns catching the arena light. He did not explain the rest. There was no time, and perhaps there were no words that would make the shape less dangerous.

Liu Feng moved in front of him.

Lan pushed.

The force entered Liu Feng like a released arrow.

The White Dragon Spear brightened. The eyes on the dragon head lit as if something sleeping in the weapon had been called awake. Liu Feng shot forward.

Lan followed, ice forming beneath his feet in a thin smooth path, letting his body glide rather than run.

Chang Jianyi lifted his right hand.

A pale white halo spread outward.

Slow Down.

The field touched Liu Feng.

His speed stuttered.

For someone else, the pause might have turned charge into failure. For Liu Feng, it became a tearing sound in the space between intent and body. His muscles wanted to go faster. The halo insisted otherwise. His spear still moved.

Ye Lingtong came straight at him.

Celestial Body lit across her frame. Her speed rose. Her strength rose. Her defense rose. She did not evade the White Dragon Spear; she met it like a question she had decided to answer with her bones.

Liu Feng's first soul skill erupted.

Lift.

Air surged upward in a five-meter radius.

With Lan's golden-patterned enhancement burning through him and all of Liu Feng's courage thrown into one sweep, the rising force caught Ye Lingtong, Lu Qianxun, and Chang Jianyi together.

For an instant, Lan saw the possibility.

Then Lu Qianxun scoffed.

Soul Power pressed downward.

His feet slammed back into the same spot. He did not remain untouched, but he refused to be carried. His hand shot out and seized the back of Ye Lingtong's top as her body rose only a short distance above the ground.

Ye understood him at once.

She turned in the air, using the force Lu transferred through her.

Her leg snapped out.

The kick struck the White Dragon Spear.

Liu Feng had spent too much in the lifting sweep. He had left himself no spare angle, no room to soften the return. Spear and body were thrown backward together.

Not randomly.

Ye had chosen the line.

Liu Feng flew straight toward Lan.

If Lan caught him, Dorm333 would still be two against three with Lu's team already rushing. If Lan failed to catch him, Liu Feng would crash badly enough to lose the next breath. Either answer gave Lu's team time to crush forward.

Lu Qianxun's eyes sharpened.

Ye Lingtong landed.

Chang Jianyi's white halo tightened for the next control.

The trap closed.

Lan smiled.

---

The smile reached Lu Qianxun first.

It should not have been there.

Lan Xuanyu did not catch Liu Feng.

He moved aside.

Liu Feng hit the ground behind him and rolled, stunned but not broken. Lan continued forward alone, ice cracking under his last step as he entered the space Lu's team had thought would belong to their follow-up.

For one breath, Lu Qianxun could not understand it.

Alone?

Against three of them?

Had Lan Xuanyu lost his mind?

Then Lu saw the smile more clearly.

There was ridicule in it.

Not mockery of Lu's strength.

Mockery of the idea that strength was the only shape a trap could take.

A chill ran through Lu's instincts before thought caught up.

He opened his mouth.

Golden Lion Roar.

The second-ring Soul Grandmaster pressure drove the sound out harder than before. The roar shook the arena air, carrying both spiritual impact and soundwave force. Even Qian Lei, far behind and only half-standing, clapped his hands toward his head with a pained gasp.

Lan Xuanyu stood only a few feet away.

He should have frozen.

He did not.

His left and right hands met in front of him.

Between them, a tiny point of color appeared.

It was too small for the size of the fear it carried.

Red, gold, silver, blue, green, purple, something that did not separate cleanly into names a child could say in time. The colors twisted together and then opened.

Golden Lion Roar caught in Lu's throat.

Lan's lips moved.

Goodbye.

The arena became color.

A five-meter sphere burst outward, not like flame, not like ice, not like any ordinary first-ring skill the teachers had recorded. It swallowed Lan Xuanyu and the three opponents together. Lu's widened eyes vanished. Ye Lingtong's half-raised arm vanished. Chang Jianyi's pale halo vanished.

For several seconds, the arena held only that impossible brilliance.

Then the light ended.

Lan Xuanyu was gone.

Lu Qianxun was gone.

Ye Lingtong was gone.

Chang Jianyi was gone.

Liu Feng sat on the floor, White Dragon Spear across his knees, staring at empty air.

Qian Lei lowered his hands from his head and stared too.

The arena system announced the result with a calmness no child in the room possessed.

Dorm333, victory.

Fifth match.

---

The observation room did not breathe.

Mu Zhongtian had seen children surprise him before. That was why the Elite Junior Class existed. Surprise was the point.

This was not surprise.

This was a hole opening under the lesson plan.

Yin Tianfan's mouth remained slightly open.

Ji Hongbin's expression had gone so still that the stillness itself became alarming.

On the screen, the replay froze at the instant color opened between Lan Xuanyu's hands.

One teacher whispered, "What was that?"

No one answered immediately.

They had wanted to put pressure on Dorm333. They had wanted to see whether Lan Xuanyu still had something hidden behind concession, summons, speed support, and arithmetic.

They had found something.

They had also found that finding it did not mean understanding it.

"Even a first-ring Soul Master can use that?" another teacher murmured. "The area was around five meters. Lu Qianxun had two rings active. Ye and Chang were inside too. Instant elimination..."

Mu slowly looked toward Yin. "Vice president."

Yin swallowed.

"Do not look at me as if I taught him that."

Ji Hongbin's eyes did not leave the replay. "Self-sacrifice technique?"

Yin's face tightened. "Or Martial Soul Fusion technique?"

"Not self-sacrifice," Ji said.

His voice had become too controlled.

"He was calm. He has used it before. If he had not, he would not have chosen it in that instant. The system likely judged him eliminated because the skill consumed all available energy, but his expression says he knew the price. Liu Feng's first attack was bait. Lift them together, keep them within range, then Lan Xuanyu enters alone and detonates the answer."

"Detonates," Yin repeated faintly. "That is a comforting word to use for my disciple."

Ji turned his head.

Slowly.

"Your disciple?"

Yin Tianfan had one fraction of a second to regret every sentence he had ever spoken in a room containing Ji Hongbin.

Ji's hand shot out and seized him by the collar near the neck.

The other teachers jerked upright.

"Old Ji!"

"Teacher Ji!"

"Vice president—"

Ji ignored them.

"Fatty," he said, voice cold enough to frost iron, "you will give this child to me as well."

Yin wheezed. "No."

Ji's grip tightened.

"Twin Martial Soul. A possible Self Martial Soul Fusion technique. Combat instinct. Willingness to spend himself with calculation. If you keep him only in your warship hole, it is a waste. A criminal waste."

"He is already suffering enough," Yin managed.

"Then he can suffer correctly."

Mu closed his eyes for half a heartbeat.

The other teachers looked at one another and reached the same realization at different speeds.

Lan Xuanyu was the vice president's disciple.

Teacher Ji wanted to fight the vice president for him.

The final exam had somehow become more dangerous after the match ended.

"Let go first," Yin said.

"Agree first."

"You are unreasonable."

"Correct."

Yin struggled, failed, and finally slapped Ji's wrist. "Fine! Fine. Combat training with you. Warship training with me. If he dies of exhaustion, I will haunt your classroom."

Ji released him.

"He will not die."

"That is not the same as kindness."

"Kindness is not the lesson he just asked for."

Mu looked back at the screen.

Dorm333 had won the fifth match.

Lan Xuanyu had forced the exam open again.

The forced-match result would be simple to write.

The class result could not be—not in this class, not with Dorm336 still untested.

---

The waiting hall received the result without receiving the reason.

That made it worse.

Dorm316's transfer light had barely faded long enough for whispers to settle. Some students had not even finished arguing over whether the order change was fair. The board brightened.

Dorm333 victory.

Fifth match.

Opponent: Lu Qianxun, Ye Lingtong, Chang Jianyi.

For a moment, the hall became a room full of children forgetting how to be children.

Then everyone spoke.

"Already?"

"That fast?"

"But Qian Lei could not have recovered!"

"Did they summon?"

"No footage?"

"Lu lost?"

"Lu Qianxun lost?"

"What happened inside?"

No video appeared.

No teacher explanation followed.

The board gave only the result, and the result was a wall with no door.

Liang Zheng stood near his returning teammates. His fifth-match victory had mattered a minute ago. It still mattered. But the shape of it changed under the new line on the board. Dorm333 had conceded to him because Lan Xuanyu had refused to spend the wrong resource.

Then, when adults made spending impossible, Dorm333 had found another resource entirely.

Liang did not look humiliated.

He looked thoughtful.

That was why Yan respected him within his lane.

Across the hall, the transfer light opened again.

Lu Qianxun returned first.

He did not stride out like a defeated boy searching for an excuse. He appeared, stopped, and stood as if some inner balance had been knocked sideways. Ye Lingtong came after him, face pale and eyes bright with too many questions. Chang Jianyi's calm had cracked around the edges; his fingers flexed once as if trying to feel the slow halo he had not been allowed to use twice.

They had not been slowly beaten.

They had been removed.

Luo Haoran's voice came out very quietly. "That was too fast."

Song Yichen did not answer.

Yan Shuo watched Lu's face.

There was no satisfaction in it.

Yan did not need Lu weakened for his own strength to be true. Lu's Golden Lion King was dangerous. His second ring was real. His pride was not empty. If Lu had been beaten by direct pressure everyone could understand, the hall would know what to fear.

But the hall did not understand.

That meant this was not a simple measure of ordinary combat strength.

This was Lan.

A hidden answer. A price. A door that had opened and closed before anyone outside could see the room beyond it.

Yan's fingers tightened once, then loosened.

She could beat Lu in a fair direct fight. That truth did not change because Lan had erased Lu's team with something unknown.

It only meant Lan Xuanyu contained a danger that did not fit the public categories either.

That was not insulting.

It was familiar.

Some people were not measured correctly by paperwork.

Luo leaned close. "You are making the face."

Yan looked at him. "What face?"

"The face that means you know three things and will only tell us one."

"I know one thing," Yan said.

"Impossible."

"Do not assume we can copy what happened."

Song nodded at once. "No footage. No mechanism. Only result."

"And do not assume Lu is suddenly weak," Yan added.

Luo's mouth opened, closed, then opened again. "You are defending Lu now?"

"I am defending correct thinking. It is less annoying than repairing wrong thinking later."

Song's mouth twitched again.

Luo pointed at him. "Stop enjoying this."

Yan looked back at the board.

Dorm333 had five wins now. The pressure that the teachers had tried to create had not broken them. It had forced out something bright enough that even the lack of footage felt like evidence.

The public result moved through the hall in layers.

Some students looked relieved because Lu could lose.

Some looked terrified because Lu could lose quickly.

Some looked toward Yan because his deep-yellow and deep-purple rings were still the most understandable public answer to direct power in the room.

Yan felt those glances and did not flinch.

Public Yan Shuo stood straight, collar neat, expression controlled.

Private Yan Shuo'er held her hidden frame steady beneath it.

No reveal.

No false smallness.

No pretending the world was simple enough to have only one kind of strength.

---

The board tried to close the test.

That was what everyone expected after a result like that.

Dorm333 had beaten Lu Qianxun's team. Lu Qianxun, Ye Lingtong, and Chang Jianyi had been called early by the teachers themselves. The pressure rule had been changed around that fight. If the old class shape had still been the whole truth, many students might have accepted the next line without thinking.

First place. Dorm333.

But the old class shape was not the whole truth anymore.

The roster still had names below Lu's team.

One of them was not a small name.

Dorm336.

Yan Shuo. Song Yichen. Luo Haoran.

Maze second place. Class monitor team. Rank23 Fire Phoenix with a deep-yellow first-ring response and a deep-purple second ring. Stoneplate Tortoise defense anchor. Threadwind Mantis interruption line.

The strongest public direct-combat challenger team in the waiting hall had not received its field.

So when the board flickered, the hall did not merely watch.

It held its breath.

For one heartbeat, three words appeared beneath the result of Dorm333's fifth win.

Next challenger: Dorm336.

The waiting hall exploded.

"Dorm336!"

"They're still going?"

"But Lan Xuanyu just disappeared from the arena!"

"Can he even fight?"

"If Dorm336 doesn't fight, how can the test be over?"

"If they do fight now, isn't Dorm333 finished?"

Both answers were ugly.

That was the problem with adults changing pressure after a test had begun. Once fairness bent, it did not politely stop bending where the teachers wanted.

Song Yichen rose without looking at Yan for permission. He was not captain and never pretended to be, but his shield-line instincts understood when a team had been called. Luo Haoran stood a breath later, mouth open as if a complaint had become too large to choose only one sentence.

Yan Shuo stood last.

Not slowly.

Carefully.

Because the hall was looking at him now, and public Yan Shuo could not afford to move like private Yan Shuo'er felt.

The pressure in her chest was not only competition.

Lan Xuanyu had just used something dangerous enough to remove Lu's entire team from the arena and himself with them. Yan did not know the mechanism. Yan was not entitled to the mechanism. But Yan knew the shape of exhaustion when it reached the bones, and Lan had already been living under Teacher Yin's shadow for too long.

At the same time, Dorm336 was not decoration.

They had trained. They had earned Maze second. They had carried the class-monitor pressure through every public ranking shift. Song and Luo had not spent this whole term becoming a real team so that a board could close before their names mattered.

Yan looked once at Song.

Song's face was steady, but his hands were already set where a shield would answer.

Yan looked once at Luo.

Luo whispered, "If they make us fight a half-dead Dorm333, I am complaining. If they cancel us, I am also complaining. I have range."

"Good," Yan said.

"Good?"

"You finally found the correct number of complaints."

Luo stared at him.

Song, somehow, looked relieved.

The transfer light did not open.

The three words on the board flickered again.

Next challenger: Dorm336.

Then a second line appeared.

Stand by.

That was worse than either answer.

---

In the observation room, Mu Zhongtian's face had gone pale in the way of a teacher who had realized the students were not the only ones being examined.

"We cannot close the actual-combat test," he said.

Yin Tianfan was still rubbing his neck where Ji Hongbin's hand had been. "We can close anything if the child we are using as the defending champion just emptied himself enough for the system to remove him."

"That is exactly why we cannot close it," Mu said. "If I announce an unconditional Dorm333 overall title right now, what do I tell Dorm336? That their match vanished because we changed the order and forced Lan Xuanyu to expose a suicidal-looking technique?"

"Not suicidal," Ji Hongbin said.

Mu looked at him.

Ji corrected, "Not truly suicidal. Dangerous. Costly. All-energy consumption. But not death."

"That distinction will not comfort the waiting hall," Mu said.

One of the other teachers spoke carefully. "In the original ranking pressure, Lu Qianxun's team would have been enough as the final emotional wall for many students. But this semester is not that simple anymore. Yan Shuo's Dorm336 is still outside."

No one argued.

Not because Dorm336 was a copy of Dorm333.

They were a different threat.

They did not have four plus signs or Lan Xuanyu's strange command center. They did not have Qian Lei's impossible Gate or Liu Feng's Lan-assisted speed blade. They did not have the secret colorful thing that had just made every teacher in the room sit up straighter.

They had Yan Shuo.

That was not a small replacement word.

Yan Shuo's public file alone made ignoring them impossible: Rank23, two rings, one deep-yellow response and one deep-purple ring, SP156, Fire Phoenix pressure that did not read like an ordinary Soul Grandmaster label and could not be judged by Rank23 paperwork alone. Song Yichen and Luo Haoran were not empty names attached to him. Their Maze result had already proved team function.

Dorm336 had earned the right to be a problem.

Mu said, "If we send Dorm336 in now, Dorm333 cannot answer fairly. Qian Lei cannot summon. Lan Xuanyu has just used all his energy. Liu Feng has already spent heavily. Dorm336 would receive either a cheap victory or be forced to restrain themselves until the result became hollow."

Yin pointed at him. "Exactly. So close it."

"No," Ji said.

The room quieted.

Yin's finger remained in the air. "Old Ji, if you say one more sentence that gives me more work—"

"Dorm333's victory over Lu Qianxun stands," Ji said. "The forced-pressure result stands. Lan Xuanyu's value is proven. Qian Lei and Liu Feng's value beside him is proven. But Dorm336's right to challenge cannot be erased."

Mu breathed out once.

Ji continued, "Suspend the remaining top-challenger match. Record Dorm333 as the current defending champion of this pressure stage, not as having defeated opponents it never faced. Record Dorm336 as unresolved top challenger with a mandatory deferred match or equivalent direct assessment."

Yin stared at him.

"That is more work."

"Correct."

"You enjoy this."

"Also correct."

Mu did not smile. The decision was too serious for that.

"Then the public announcement must be careful," he said. "If we give Dorm333 an unconditional overall title with no condition, it insults Dorm336 and teaches the class that teacher interference can erase a team. If we say Dorm336 wins by default, it insults Dorm333 and punishes Lan for surviving the pressure we created."

"So say the truth," Ji said.

Yin looked pained. "A terrible habit."

Mu turned toward the control panel.

This time, before pressing the announcement key, he hesitated.

The children outside would remember this.

So would the teachers.

---

The waiting hall fell silent when Mu Zhongtian's voice came through.

"All students, listen carefully. Dorm333's victory over Lu Qianxun's team is valid. The forced match result stands."

A wave of whispers tried to rise.

Mu's voice cut through it.

"However, the actual-combat test is not being closed by pretending the remaining qualified challenger team does not exist. Dorm336 has not been defeated, and Dorm336's evaluation will not be erased."

The whispers stopped.

Yan felt Song's breath settle beside him.

Luo whispered, "Teacher Mu has chosen the rare path of saying a useful sentence."

"Quiet," Song said.

Mu continued, "Because Dorm333 has just used an unknown high-cost technique and cannot fairly continue at once, the academy will suspend the remaining top-challenger match. Dorm333 is recorded as the current defending champion of this pressure stage. Dorm336 is recorded as unresolved top challenger and will receive a mandatory deferred actual-combat evaluation against Dorm333 or an equivalent direct assessment arranged by the academy. The final top comparison will not be decided by ignoring either team."

For a moment, no one moved.

Then the whole hall changed shape.

It was not satisfaction. It was not disappointment. It was the feeling of a knot being tied instead of cut.

Dorm333 had not been robbed.

Dorm336 had not been thrown away.

The competition had not become a show where one team displayed miracles and everyone else became background noise.

Yan did not relax.

Not fully.

But the sharpest part of the insult left his chest.

He stepped forward just enough that Mu, watching through whatever screen the teachers used, would see that Dorm336 had heard.

Then he bowed once, short and formal.

Not gratitude.

Acknowledgment.

Song followed with a steadier nod.

Luo copied them late and muttered, "I would like my future complaint rights recorded too."

"Denied," Song said.

"You have no authority."

"I have proximity."

Yan almost smiled.

Across the hall, Lu Qianxun stood with Ye Lingtong and Chang Jianyi, still carrying the blank-edged shock of their removal from the arena. His eyes moved from Dorm333's result to Dorm336's standby line.

No one in that moment could honestly say the hierarchy was simple.

Lu had lost quickly.

That did not make him weak.

Dorm333 had won suddenly.

That did not make every other team irrelevant.

Dorm336 had not fought.

That did not make them defeated.

The class would have to live with all three truths.

---

Dorm333 returned from the arena into a waiting hall that no longer knew how to greet them.

Qian Lei came first, pale and offended at the universe.

"I would like to announce," he said to no one and everyone, "that if future historians describe this exam as exciting, I will personally haunt their essays."

Luo lifted a hand. "I support this academic position."

Qian pointed at him. "You. Mantis boy. Sensible."

"Never say that again," Song murmured.

Liu Feng followed with the White Dragon Spear dismissed but not forgotten. He looked toward Dorm336 first, because he had seen the standby line before leaving the arena space. His gaze held apology and challenge at the same time.

That was good.

Yan preferred it to pretending nothing had happened.

Lan Xuanyu came last.

He looked smaller when he was tired.

Not weaker.

Smaller, because some inner light had drawn inward to keep itself from spilling.

Students stared.

Teachers watched.

No one explained the fifth match.

That silence became part of the result.

Yan waited until the first wave of noise broke around Dorm333. He did not rush forward like someone demanding secrets. He also did not stay away like someone pretending friendship ended where competition began.

He stepped close enough that Lan could see him without needing to search.

Lan looked up first.

His eyes went briefly to the board.

Dorm336: unresolved top challenger.

Mandatory deferred evaluation.

Then back to Yan.

"Your match," Lan said.

His voice was rougher than usual.

Yan nodded. "Our match."

Qian Lei leaned sideways. "Can our match happen after my Spiritual Power remembers it lives in my body?"

"Preferably," Luo said. "I want to defeat you at full complaint capacity."

"You want to defeat me?"

"No. I want to survive loudly. It is different."

Liu Feng's mouth moved as if he had almost smiled and decided not to spend the energy.

Yan kept his attention on Lan.

There were questions he could ask.

What happened?

What did the teachers force?

Why did Lu's team vanish so quickly?

Why do you look as if winning took something out of your bones?

Why did the board almost close before Dorm336's name mattered?

He asked the first question friendship had the right to ask.

"Are you hurt?"

Lan's gaze shifted briefly toward the observation room doors.

Adult instruction. Team secrets. A thing he might not understand himself. A price the public did not need to hold.

Yan saw the reasons before Lan found the words.

"Tired," Lan said at last. "And... I used something I should not use casually."

That was not everything.

It was also not nothing.

Yan nodded.

"Then do not let Teacher Yin make casual things of dangerous ones."

Lan's expression changed.

Qian Lei made a choking sound that might have been laughter. "Too late. Teacher Ji looked at him like he had found a new way to ruin sleep."

Liu Feng said, "I think Teacher Ji and Vice President Yin argued."

"Argued," Qian repeated. "That is a gentle word for attempted educational murder."

Lan sighed.

The sigh held more than one kind of fear.

Yan understood only the safe edge of it. That had to be enough for the secret.

It was not enough for the match.

"Lan," Yan said.

Lan looked at him again.

Yan's voice stayed low, but he did not soften the words into nothing. "When you can stand properly, Dorm336 still wants its field. Not because I need to take anything from you. Because Song and Luo earned it. Because we earned it. Because if the academy can skip the strongest public challenger whenever your team shows something frightening, then the exam stops being actual combat and becomes a stage."

The words landed harder than Yan expected.

Not because they were cruel.

Because they were true.

Lan's face tightened once.

Then he nodded.

"I know."

Yan watched him carefully.

Lan continued, quieter, "I don't want them to erase your match."

That was the answer Yan needed.

Not an explanation of the colorful light.

Not a confession of the inner mechanism, or of any secret Lan might not even understand.

Only this: he knew Dorm336 mattered.

He knew Yan mattered.

"Then recover," Yan said.

Lan blinked.

"That sounds like an order."

"It is advice. From the class monitor."

Qian Lei looked delighted despite exhaustion. "Can the class monitor also order him to feed me until my Spiritual Power returns?"

"No," Yan said.

"A cruel administration."

"Eat anyway," Yan added to Lan. "Sleep when you can. Tell us if you are about to fall over in public."

Lan's mouth curved faintly.

It was not the smile from the arena. It had no ridicule, no hidden detonation. It was tired and young and almost relieved.

"I will tell what I can," he said.

Yan accepted the boundary.

Best friends did not need to break every lock to prove the door existed.

But best friends also did not pretend a door was a wall.

"Then I will believe what you can tell," Yan said. "And I will wait for what you owe us on the field."

For a breath, Lan looked as if that answer had removed one weight and added another, both deserved.

"En," he said.

Then Mu Zhongtian called for order, and the moment folded back into the larger noise of the class.

---

Later, the official written notice used careful language because careless language would have broken the class more thoroughly than any single defeat.

Dorm333: defending-champion pressure stage leader. Victory over Lu Qianxun's team valid. Lan Xuanyu, Qian Lei, and Liu Feng retained first-stage actual-combat honor for the forced defense sequence.

Dorm336: unresolved top challenger. Mandatory deferred actual-combat evaluation to be arranged after Dorm333's minimum recovery and teacher review.

Other teams: results recorded according to completed matches and teacher assessment.

It was not clean.

It was honest enough to keep the exam from becoming a lie.

Yin Tianfan did not lose Lan Xuanyu.

Ji Hongbin did not let him go.

Compromise, among adults, sometimes meant a child received twice the training and no additional hours in the day.

Lan Xuanyu gained another master.

The academy gained another reason to watch Dorm333.

Qian Lei gained proof that his martial soul was not useless when Lan stood beside him.

Liu Feng gained proof that speed could become a blade when someone knew where to push.

Lu Qianxun gained a defeat with no clean shape to strike back against.

Dorm336 gained something different: not victory, not loss, but an acknowledged claim the academy could no longer erase.

That mattered.

Yan Shuo'er gained no public number.

No new rank appeared beside Yan Shuo's name. No teacher retested his Spiritual Power. No Spirit Pagoda record opened to announce Dawnflame Kite's private living maturity. No hidden identity slipped through the collar and became public truth.

But no-new-number did not mean no result.

That night, in the quiet after the suspended exam, Yan stood in Dorm336's practice space while Song sat cross-legged near the wall and Luo pretended not to watch from the floor.

No ring rose.

No skill activated.

Yan simply set his feet.

The body remembered the day.

Not Lan's secret color. That was not hers to know.

The pressure around it.

Teachers changing rules. Lu called early. A concession becoming victory. A board almost closing before Dorm336's name, then stopping because the room could no longer pretend Yan's team was background.

Dawnflame Kite pulsed once in the spiritual sea, copper-red warmth edged in pale gold.

Dawn-Iron Phoenix Roc settled underneath, earth and metal teaching the warmth where to stand.

Feather over bone.

Fire-light over frame.

Public mask over true body.

Not a seal.

Not a weakness.

A discipline that would have to grow strong enough to survive the day it ended.

Yan breathed in.

The deep-purple second ring did not appear, but the memory of its weight aligned through feet, hips, spine, ribs, shoulders, and the folded places where wings were not yet allowed to be wings.

Luo whispered, "Are you cultivating or threatening the floor?"

"Both," Yan said.

"Good," Luo said. "Threaten the academy schedule next."

Song opened one eye. "Quietly."

"I am always quiet," Luo said.

Neither of his teammates answered.

Yan let their familiar nonsense settle around him like a safe wall.

Tomorrow would bring new schedules, new rumors, new teacher plans, new pressure.

Somewhere else, Lan Xuanyu would be swallowed by Yin Tianfan's warship misery and Ji Hongbin's combat abyss, carrying a secret bright enough to make Lu Qianxun vanish from an arena and dangerous enough that he could only explain its edge.

Yan would not stand still while that happened.

She could not.

Dorm336 would not stand still either.

The Fire Phoenix did not need public permission to keep growing.

But public promises mattered when a team had earned the right to the field.

Yan lowered his stance by a finger's width.

The floor did not crack.

It only felt, for one quiet instant, as if it had been asked to remember the shape of a future impact.
