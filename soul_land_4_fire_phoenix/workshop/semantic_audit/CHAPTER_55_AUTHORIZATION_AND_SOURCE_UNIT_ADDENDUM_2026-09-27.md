- the Purple Zoysia remains held under the C53 six-day condition;

Therefore C55 may take only the **late-Chapter-182 / Chapter-183 human-flight pressure**: a wounded, separated Ye Lingtong pursued by three human contestants. It may reallocate that pressure to Dorm336 because Dorm336’s established route has a causal reason to encounter it. It must not copy the source’s Dorm333 rescue/battle/point sequence.
