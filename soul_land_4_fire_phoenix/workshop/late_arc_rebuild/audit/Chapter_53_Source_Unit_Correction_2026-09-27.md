1. Qian’s intent contact creates a one-move opening with the Gold Silk Ape.
2. Lan’s temporary support and Liu’s herb retrieval destabilize the clearing.

Stopping after Chapter 178 did not protect a meaningful independent Dorm336 endpoint. It merely converted a source page break into a fanfiction chapter boundary. That violates the author’s standing direction that fanfiction is not one canon chapter per fanfiction chapter and that canon is a pressure spine, not a scene map.

## Corrected rule

A source boundary is used only when it protects an earned causal endpoint. It is not, by itself, a reason to stop prose.

For replacement Chapter 53:

- Source Chapters 178 **and 179** are one Dorm333 pressure movement and must be evaluated/written together.
- Dorm336 remains a real parallel route and cannot be reduced to a name-check merely because the Dorm333 source movement is longer.
- Chapter 180 and later remain a fresh later-source fence.
- No current power, identity, reward, future-currentization, or ensemble lock is relaxed.
