### 2. Dorm336 has causal ownership throughout

This is an ensemble route, not a Yan showcase with two bystanders. The C55 corridor and C56 false-trace withdrawal pay the earlier `Hold / Cut / Out` decision in different ways rather than repeating a single action beat.

### 3. Ye’s C55 appearance has the right amount of emotional weight

The current C55 makes Ye’s pre-existing, private crush on public Yan legible at the exact point where being seen injured and frightened costs her most. It does not reduce her to a rescue object: she assesses the routes, chooses the wash, rejects a debt frame, and leaves under her own agency. C56 correctly does **not** manufacture a second encounter or romantic payoff.

The direct description of Yan as the strong, handsome, disciplined class-monitor figure Ye has measured herself against is intentionally clearer than the surrounding emotional register. That clarity is necessary to preserve the author ruling and must not be flattened back into generic familiarity. It should remain a contained C55 spotlight, not become repetitive narration in later authorized work.
