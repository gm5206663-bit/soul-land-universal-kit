# Rebuild Contract — Chapter 51: The Cost of Quiet

**Status:** pre-prose contract for a replacement draft; not public authority.

## Governing question

When caution has kept a team alive, how does it recognize the moment caution becomes quiet surrender—and who has to live with the trace left by the risk they finally choose?

## Required Dorm336 Day-One movement

Start from the Chapter 50 low-canopy route and give it a complete Day-One turn rather than a cutaway.

- The team follows Luo’s vapor/ground reading out of the root basin into a rain-swollen, waterlogged ravine. The ravine offers an inward route but makes a full Fire Phoenix burn or open-wing crossing wrong: fire creates a visible steam column, unstable footing, and blind space around Song and Luo.
- Luo detects both the river’s changing pressure and recent human disturbance on the higher bank. It is evidence of an unknown team, not identity knowledge, alliance, confrontation, or a promised enemy.
- Yan sees a fast high route that his own wings/fire could solve for him. Song makes the actual objection: it has no viable exit for a shield carrier and route-reader, so it is not a team route.
- The three work the slower crossing together. Song anchors against the water/weight line; Luo calls the reliable steps and the moment the ravine is about to shift; Yan uses carefully limited heat to preserve a crossing rather than turn the ravine into a spectacle.
- An unavoidable emergency pulse saves the formation when the crossing changes. It is a meaningful Fire Phoenix advantage, but it creates a red-gold thermal/steam trace that can be seen from the upper bank. No opponent is defeated, rescued, or identified.
- The immediate cost is real: Dorm336 has spent time, exposed a trace, earned no score, and cannot claim the safe high route afterward. Do not compensate them with an item, prey kill, or convenient reward.
