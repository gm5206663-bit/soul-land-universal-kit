## Governing question

What does it mean to open a way for another person when help does not give anyone the right to own the route, the debt, or the choice that follows?

## Starting state: two routes after replacement C54

### Dorm333

- Lan, Qian, and Liu left the Gold Silk Ape resting beneath roots after its unresolved skull-bone fusion.
- The Purple Zoysia remains in Lan’s keeping under the C53 six-day condition. The plant is neither a human cultivation reward nor an available emergency payment.
- The ape is not currently with Dorm333. No recovery, new ability, location, or return may be assumed.
- Qian’s direct beast contact remains limited intent/impression; Liu’s Silver Edge route remains spent for this sequence and may not be repeated.

### Dorm336

- Yan/public he, Song, and Luo travel through the chosen lower watercourse after C54’s bounded scout/descent.
- The prior high-ground group has not been identified, defeated, or proven gone.
- Their field rule remains `Hold / Cut / Out`: a movement counts only if all three have an exit, and Yan’s visible power must have a bounded use/return line.

## Source-pressure allocation: a reallocated flight, not a copied battle

C55 uses only the late-Chapter-182 / Chapter-183 pressure of a wounded, separated Ye Lingtong fleeing three human contestants. The canon source turns that pressure into a Dorm333 rescue and extended fight. This replacement does not inherit that scene ownership or outcome.

### Dorm333: the held condition becomes a burden

1. The Zoysia’s scent/value makes movement harder; it can draw an ordinary forest response without becoming a convenient prey/score event.
2. Liu identifies the physical approach/line; Qian contributes current awareness and keeps the Gate as bounded contingency; Lan chooses a non-lethal, non-exploitative route that protects the condition without claiming the plant as a trophy.
3. No ape return, no early handover, no point total, and no second Silver Edge effect.
4. Dorm333 learns that holding to terms is not passive waiting: it changes what routes and easy answers it refuses.

### Dorm336: give an exit, do not claim a person

1. Luo detects a one-sided pursuit and identifies a narrow watercourse/split with one viable independent exit for a fourth person, without claiming certainty about the pursuers’ identities or total number beyond what the ground shows.
2. Ye arrives wounded, exhausted, and separated. Song, Luo, and Yan all recognize a fellow Elite Junior Class peer through the damage and mud. Ye’s response to public Yan Shuo carries an already-present, private, age-appropriate crush—layered with pride, admiration, rivalry, embarrassment, trust, and the fear of being seen at her worst. It must not be flattened into generic peer familiarity. Yan does not claim or consciously resolve that feeling; neither it nor immediate danger creates a team merger.
3. Song establishes the holding geometry and makes clear that Dorm336 will not sacrifice its own exit or turn a fourth person into bait.
4. Yan uses bounded, current Fire Phoenix heat/wings to make the crossing legible and costly to enter—not to annihilate the pursuers or display a future form.
5. Luo gives Ye the precise route. Song holds one actual probe. Ye chooses the onward fork herself once the immediate line is broken.
6. The three pursuers may withdraw/recalculate when the corridor no longer offers an easy isolated target. Dorm336 must not call this a defeat, score, named-rival victory, or permanent safety.

## Causal bridge

- Dorm333 protects a promise without mistaking it for ownership.
- Dorm336 protects a person’s chance to choose without mistaking temporary cover for possession, alliance, or a right to decide her future.
- Both teams learn that an open way has a cost, but no clean claim attached to it.
