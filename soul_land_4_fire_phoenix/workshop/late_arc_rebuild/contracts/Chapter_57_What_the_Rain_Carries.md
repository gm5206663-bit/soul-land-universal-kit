> **Later-status note (2026-09-29):** This C57 contract created no C58 authority. C58 later received its own direct addendum; this contract grants no C59 authority.

## Governing question

When weather turns every trace into a liability, what can a team carry forward without turning another person, animal, or advantage into property?

## Starting state

- Dorm333 is on the far shelf after C56: the Purple Zoysia remains held under the six-day term; the Gold Silk Ape is unstable, at a self-chosen distance, and not a scout or companion.
- Dorm336 is beyond its under-shelf exit after C56: its own false warmth may be investigated; Ye’s wash is unmarked and no one follows her.

## Required movement

### Dorm333

1. Rain/runoff makes the far shelf unsuitable for the root ball and forces a physical relocation.
2. Liu reads the stable footing; Lan protects the plant without treating it as a weapon; Qian refuses to turn the ape’s reactions into command or reconnaissance.
3. The ape chooses its own response to the weather. It may share a direction temporarily, never a human-owned route.
4. Distant human conflict may be heard as qualifying pressure, but Dorm333 may neither farm it nor make a source score calculation.

### Dorm336

1. The false trace buys only time, not proof that the pursuing figures are gone.
2. Luo identifies the route that rain will conceal without making Song’s shield or Yan’s current fire a disposable answer.
3. Song carries the physical hold/withdrawal; Yan uses only bounded current output to preserve a team crossing, not to flare, hunt, or reveal identity.
4. Dorm336 explicitly leaves Ye’s wash uninvolved and does not turn unknown contestants into prey.

## Fences and endpoint

- No named human opponent, score, kill, item, reward, alliance, forced reunion, source battle, or future-power material.
- No ape scouting/recovery/ownership or early Zoysia resolution.
- End with both routes in separate weather-safe positions before dawn. The pressure remains outside their shelters.
