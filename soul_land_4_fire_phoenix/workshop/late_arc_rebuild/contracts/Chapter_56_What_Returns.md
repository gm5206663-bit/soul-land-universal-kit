## Governing question

When something returns after being left free, how do you answer without mistaking return for ownership, forgiveness, safety, or a claim on the route ahead?

## Starting state: two routes after replacement C55

### Dorm333

- Lan, Qian, and Liu carry the Purple Zoysia at night under the C53 six-day condition. The plant remains unspent, unharmed, and not a human reward.
- The Gold Silk Ape was left resting beneath roots during unresolved skull-bone fusion. C55 deliberately did not decide whether it woke, what it could do, or what it would choose.
- Qian’s direct intent contact remains limited, invited, and impression-level. Liu’s Silver Edge route is unavailable for repeat use.

### Dorm336

- Yan/public he, Song, and Luo remain on the lower-route side of C55’s watercourse after creating Ye Lingtong’s independent exit.
- Ye has chosen her own wash route. No one knows Lu/Chang’s status, the pursuers’ identities/motive, or whether the figures have abandoned the larger area. Her private, age-appropriate feeling toward public Yan Shuo remains unconfessed and is not a C56 lever, payoff, or reason to reverse her autonomy.
- The old high-ground line remains unresolved. `Hold / Cut / Out` still requires an exit for all three, bounded Yan output, and no route that buys safety by spending a teammate or an uninvolved person.

## Source-pressure allocation: return without source restoration

C56 takes a narrow conceptual pressure from the post-fusion Gold Silk Ape hinge in verified Chapter 187: an independent ape may decide to return to an earlier conditional promise.

It does **not** take the causal machinery that makes the source return easy or useful: no preceding source battle, no source score/kill result, no recovered/stronger ape, no scout, no command relationship, no early herb handoff, and no later human alliance route.

## Required Dorm333 movement: a chosen short crossing

1. The ape appears by its own choice after fusion, but is visibly uncertain and physically limited; its brow-light/fusion consequence cannot become a usable new power.
2. It sees the held Zoysia and tests the distance/terms without receiving the plant. Qian may receive only a fragmentary invitation/unease, not fluent beast speech.
3. A short, physical night crossing gives all three boys a concrete role:
   - Liu reads/holds the unstable material route with ordinary spear/body timing;
   - Lan creates a bounded support line that opens a crossing without binding the ape or spending the herb;
   - Qian offers an understandable, non-commanding route image only after the ape chooses to attempt it.
4. The result is not a restored traveling companion. The ape may keep distance while choosing the same temporary direction for one night under the existing condition.

## Required Dorm336 movement: leave a trace, not a person

1. Luo recognizes that remaining human pressure can use the obvious watercourse and Ye’s departure direction to narrow possibilities. He must identify a third route/terrain feature whose cost is readable rather than magical certainty.
2. Song turns that read into physical stealth geometry: a brief decoy/cover action, an actual hold, and a full-team withdrawal. He is not to remain behind, lose the shield, or become a stationary prop.
3. Yan contributes only a minimal current heat/wing use that makes a false trace plausible or a difficult crossing possible. It must not become a flare, air victory, lethal strike, or identity disclosure.
4. The team explicitly refuses to follow Ye, send the danger down her wash, or convert the pursuers into prey. It leaves by its own intact route.
5. The human figures may investigate/recalculate, but no name, rank, defeat, score, kill, or future guarantee may be claimed.

## Causal bridge

- Dorm333 learns that a return can be a choice without becoming a surrender of freedom.
- Dorm336 learns that protecting an exit can mean leaving a trace behind without attaching danger to the person who used it.
- Both routes make a limited answer and retain the discipline to leave its larger meaning unresolved.
