## Governing question

When the rain removes every easy hiding place, what can a team leave behind without abandoning the people or conditions it has chosen to carry?

## Starting state

- Dorm333 has a pre-dawn hollow behind an uprooted tree. The Purple Zoysia remains held, rain-soaked but unspent; the Gold Silk Ape has independently gone toward higher ground.
- Dorm336 has a boulder pocket beyond its second weather crossing. An unidentified shape occupied the first obvious dry hollow; no one knows whether it is from the old pursuit or another team.

## Required Dorm333 movement

1. Dawn shows that the Zoysia’s root ball needs drainage and stable soil before further travel. This is maintenance of the held condition, not handover, cultivation use, or reward.
2. Liu must locate usable terrain; Lan must protect the plant without making it a weapon; Qian must resist turning the ape’s absence or nearby human movement into a contact/scouting shortcut.
3. The boys leave the night hollow by their own terrain read. The ape remains absent/free; no one follows it.

## Required Dorm336 movement

1. Luo must establish that the watercourse/first hollow is no longer a viable static shelter without claiming the figure’s identity.
2. Song must make the full-team physical exit possible; Yan’s current Fire Phoenix use must support a route all three can take, not become a flare, flight victory, or hunt.
3. The immediate pressure must be broken through terrain and timing, without killing, scoring, naming, defeating, recruiting, or spending Ye’s route.

## Fences and endpoint

- End with a real dawn change: the Zoysia is safely re-secured for travel; Dorm336 has left the watercourse pressure behind by an earned route; neither result is called permanent safety.
