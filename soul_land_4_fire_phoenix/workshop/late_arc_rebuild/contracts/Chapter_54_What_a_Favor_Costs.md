## Governing question

When a temporary arrangement is asked to carry somebody else’s old loss, what does help cost if no one is allowed to turn a living ally—or a teammate—into a tool for easy progress?

## Starting state: two routes after replacement C53

### Dorm333

- Lan, Qian, and Liu have a six-day conditional arrangement with the wounded, independent Gold Silk Ape.
- The Purple Zoysia remains in Dorm333’s keeping under those conditions. It has not been casually handed over.
- The ape may leave against a threat it judges impossible. Qian’s contact remains limited intent exchange, not speech, control, or trust.
- Liu has made one source-backed Silver Edge finishing act. His arm-bone route is not to be repeated, mastered, or made routine.

### Dorm336

- Yan/public he, Song, and Luo have left the C53 stone throat by the slower lower-ridge route.
- The unnamed high-ground pressure disengaged rather than fighting. It remains unidentified, undefeated, and not reliably gone.
- Song’s shield-bearing hold cost real effort; Luo’s exit reading and Yan’s bounded heat preserved the formation. The working rule remains `Hold / Cut / Out`.

## Source-pressure allocation: one complete ape-enemy movement

C54 uses the natural causal movement that crosses verified source Chapters 180–181 and the immediate skull-fusion consequence at the opening of Chapter 182. It is deliberately not limited by the number of source chapters.

### Retained Dorm333 pressure

1. The Gold Silk Ape leads Dorm333 toward three Gold-eyed Leopards it recognizes as personal enemies. Qian receives only limited emotional/image pressure—not fluent exposition—about the old loss behind the ape’s hatred.
2. The leopards are fast, coordinated, and carry the Gold-eyed Stare freeze threat. Lan must price their field before agreeing to an answer; the ape’s anger is not a command Dorm333 must obey.
3. The ape can use itself as bait. Lan’s golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass may alter one lethal line or briefly reinforce the ape, but cannot make it safe, tame it, or erase the leopards’ danger.
4. Qian may use the already-established Gate of Summoning in a bounded current way. He may not use the Emerald Demon Bird/Replicate shortcut or turn his contact into beast control.
5. Liu must contribute through current movement, timing, and the White Dragon Spear—not a second Silver Edge/arm-bone showcase. Lan may use current grass, ice, or known Golden Dragon Soar only if the moment earns it; no power escalation is allowed.
6. Exact source kills, point distribution, score total, and pacing are not mandatory outcomes. If lethal results occur, they must arise from the altered arrangement and be kept non-numeric. The ape’s agency remains central.
7. A rare Gold-eyed Leopard skull Soul Bone may appear as the immediate consequence. In this simulation it is not a human loot/reward route. Lan may recognize that the ape can fuse it and return it to the ape rather than turn it into a Dorm333 prize.
8. The ape’s fusion/rest is the close of this movement. Because C53’s six-day Zoysia condition remains active, the herb stays held; no early Zoysia handoff is allowed. Dorm333 must make a bounded, ethical decision about how long to guard/wait without pretending it owns the ape or may suspend the round indefinitely.

## Required Dorm336 movement: chosen sight, not a chase

Dorm336 needs a real continuation that follows from C53 rather than an invented compensation battle.

- Luo identifies a terrain situation in which the lower route can be read from above only until a short, exposed break in the canopy. His reading must create a limited safe window and a real next route, not supernatural certainty.
- Song establishes a physically defensible return line and states the formation condition for any scout movement. He cannot be left as an immobile shield while Yan solves the forest alone.
- Yan uses a tightly bounded current wing/height action to see enough of the ground beyond the break. It must be voluntary, timed, and returnable—not a broad flare, aerial victory, identity leak, or effortless solution.
- A small terrain consequence (a loose descent, shifting stone, or other physical pressure) must require Song’s anchor, Luo’s timing/read, and Yan’s precisely limited heat to make the new route shared rather than merely visible.
- The scene may establish that the unknown pressure is not currently visible; it must not claim the group has been defeated, identify it, manufacture a pursuit, award a score, or treat temporary absence as safety.

## Causal bridge

- Dorm333 learns that accepting the ape’s request does not entitle them to its obedience or make its grief a score route; helping creates a debt of care when the skull-bone leaves the ape vulnerable.
- Dorm336 learns that refusing to be steered by visibility does not require hiding Yan completely. A bounded, team-held sightline can serve the whole formation when its exit is planned first.
- Both routes choose a limit: Dorm333 will not turn the Zoysia bargain into leverage over a sleeping ally; Dorm336 will not turn Yan’s wings into a solo answer.
