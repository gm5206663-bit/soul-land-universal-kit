## Governing question

Once being seen has become dangerous, can a team choose the slower route rather than buy time by steering that danger onto children it does not know?

## Starting state: two routes after C59

### Dorm333

- Lan, Qian, and Liu have exited the slate-basin cleft into older forest. The Purple Zoysia remains held, dry/re-secured, and unspent under the active six-day condition.
- The Gold Silk Ape remains absent on its own higher line. It is neither a route, scout, companion, contact, nor a solution.
- The unnamed C59 contestants know that Dorm333 carried something unusual but have not received a name, agreement, score, defeat, or permanent connection.

### Dorm336

- Yan/public he, Song, and Luo have left the fern gap below the ridge and entered older forest. They remain on a separate line and have no knowledge of Dorm333’s encounter.
- An unnamed C59 group knows Yan Shuo’s public name and reads Fire Phoenix visibility as a possible route/answer, but is neither attached to the team nor a settled enemy.
- Ye’s wash remains unmarked, off-route, and closed to a second encounter or relationship payoff.

## Required Dorm333 movement: do not turn the condition into a decoy

1. A nearby human cutting/track line and an unsafe, faster bypass must make a deliberate false trail plausible. The possible advantage comes from letting other contestants believe the team carrying the Zoysia chose the unstable route.
2. Lan must refuse to use the held condition, its trace, or the team’s tracks as bait. He keeps the root ball safe while accepting the loss of the quick line.
3. Qian must establish real human proximity through limited present perception without a loud summon, Gate shortcut, ape contact, or generalized animal control.
4. Liu must read and make the slower physical route workable without a kill, a repeated Silver Edge, or a false claim of safety.
5. Dorm333 exits with the Zoysia still held, the six-day term unchanged, and no unknown person deliberately directed into danger.

## Required Dorm336 movement: do not turn public fire into a lure

1. A visible-fire decoy must offer a believable way to buy time from nearby/inferred human movement, while Luo identifies why anyone following that visible answer would enter genuinely bad ground.
2. Song must articulate and physically maintain the boundary that prevents another team from becoming cover for Dorm336’s withdrawal.
3. Luo must distinguish the dangerous apparent route from the difficult team-usable route and set the crossing order/timing.
4. Yan must refuse the flare/false route, then contribute only bounded current Fire Phoenix output that serves Song/Luo/Yan’s own exit rather than showing flight, overpowering anyone, or creating a spectacle.
5. Dorm336 exits as three, with no ally, score, named rival, rescue debt, victory claim, or promise that the human pressure is gone.

## Causal bridge

- C59 establishes that the Zoysia and Fire Phoenix are public pressures. C60 tests whether the teams will turn that knowledge into a weapon against someone else.
- C56’s contained own-route false trace stays context, not a repeatable trick: C60’s moral turn is that human children now understand Fire Phoenix fire as a road and could suffer for following it.
- Both teams accept a concrete cost—slower ground, lost daylight, and a harder crossing—rather than purchase time with an unknown child’s risk.
