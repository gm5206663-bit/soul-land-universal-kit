## Governing question

When other people see a valuable thing or a powerful person before they see the team carrying either one, how does a group refuse to become a resource without treating strangers as prey?

## Starting state: two routes after C58

### Dorm333

- Lan, Qian, and Liu have left the rain-made hollow through a dry cut among older trees. The Purple Zoysia remains held, drained, re-secured, and unspent under the active six-day condition.
- The Gold Silk Ape has independently chosen higher ground and is absent. It is neither a route, scout, companion, nor an answer to the team’s next decision.
- Unidentified contestants used the abandoned stone overhang below. Dorm333 has not been seen clearly, has no score information, and cannot assume those figures are the same people who pressured Dorm336.

### Dorm336

- Yan/public he, Song, and Luo have left the rain-carved spillway by a fern-covered ridge. They do not know who used the boulder pocket or earlier hollows after them.
- Ye’s wash remains out of their route and unmarked. No second Ye encounter, pursuit, or emotional resolution is permitted.
- `Hold / Cut / Out` remains a field rule: a route is only real if Song, Luo, and Yan can all leave it, and Yan’s visible power cannot turn either teammate into cover for a solo answer.

## Required Dorm333 movement: refuse a price on the held condition

1. A small, unnamed human group must identify that Dorm333 carries something unusual enough to price. It may test, ask, block, or calculate; it must not receive a source identity, rank, Spirit Soul, score, motive exposition, or defeat.
2. Liu must read the physical exit and make it usable without a kill or repeated Silver Edge.
3. Qian must identify the human shape/closing line through present perception and resist using the absent ape or an oversized Gate answer as a shortcut.
4. Lan must protect the root ball and create a bounded route without turning the Zoysia into bait, payment, weapon, or a claim that strangers owe them distance.
5. Dorm333 exits with the plant still held. No one is named, beaten, scored, recruited, or declared permanently gone.

## Required Dorm336 movement: refuse to become a lead or weapon

1. A separate unnamed human group must recognize or infer Dorm336’s public Fire Phoenix visibility and try to use its route, strength, or presence as a shield, lead, or calculation.
2. Song must set the physical boundary that keeps strangers from attaching themselves to the team’s formation.
3. Luo must identify the terrain/timing by which an apparent shared route would turn Song and Yan into someone else’s disposable cover.
4. Yan must make the refusal legible with bounded current Fire Phoenix force, not a flare, flight victory, hunt, identity exposure, or unilateral command. He cannot agree to lead strangers through ground he has not priced for his own team.
5. Dorm336 exits through a route all three can take. It gains no ally, score, prey, named rival, victory claim, or future guarantee.

## Causal bridge

- Dorm333 learns that honoring a conditional promise makes it visible, but visibility does not make the condition a public resource.
- Dorm336 learns that public strength invites people to assign it work. The team can refuse that assignment without making a spectacle or treating other children as enemies to harvest.
- Both routes leave the chapter with human visibility established as an active cost, not a source alliance or battle result.
