# Fire-Light Attribute Correction - 2026-09-11

Status: Active project correction after author challenge.

## Author challenge

Yan Shuo has been handled too much as "fire, fire" in later explanations. Since Dawnflame Kite was a fire-and-light soul spirit and Yan Shuo perfectly absorbed it, the light component must remain visible and must influence future development.

## Canon check

Checked SL4 canon support:

- SL4 Chapter 13: soul rings provide soul skills, and soul-skill strength/path is directly related to compatibility between the spirit ring and martial soul. This supports Yan Shuo's perfect absorption changing how Dawnflame Kite's fire-light nature expresses.
- SL4 Chapter 143: fire is treated as a distinct element; water/fire conflict is real and can make skills interfere.
- SL4 Chapter 144: Nana gathers fire element particles and later displays Water, Fire, Earth, Wind, Light, Darkness, and Space as distinct elements; Light is shown as gold and warm/holy, not just ordinary flame brightness. Darkness-light merger is treated as especially difficult.

Conclusion: Soul Land 4 elemental logic treats light as a real attribute/element. Yan Shuo's light side cannot be dismissed as decorative glow.
