## 2. SL4 Chapter 96 canon beats - Teacher Ji's demon lesson

Must preserve:

1. Qian Lei blurts that Teacher Ji is a demon.
2. The class is shocked and silent.
3. Ji Hongbin calmly confirms that, to them, he is indeed a demon.
4. He says the afternoon belongs to him: first half lesson, second half assessment.
5. Students can eat lunch only after passing; those who fail must retake until they pass.
6. Class groans; pressure rises immediately.
7. Lan Xuanyu quietly asks Qian Lei what Teacher Ji teaches.
8. Qian Lei explains:
   - Teacher Ji teaches basic spirit-beast knowledge.
   - Students once thought the course useless because they encounter spirit beasts mostly in simulation pods.
   - Ji is strict, covers complicated material, tests after every lesson, and punishes failures.
   - Ji is said to be the strongest man in Heaven Luo Academy and a vice president.
9. Lan Xuanyu relaxes because his family background gives him unusual spirit-beast familiarity: Lan Xiao and Nan Cheng worked at the Ancient Spirit Beast Research Centre.
10. Ji lectures on spirit-beast cultivation ranks and ring colors:
    - 10-year / white.
    - 100-year / yellow.
    - 1,000-year / purple.
    - 10,000-year / black.
    - 100,000-year / red.
11. Ji adds special ring-color history:
    - Orange-gold rings are linked to Ferocious Beasts, especially 200,000-year+ spirit beasts, with calamities that Spirit Pagoda simulation technology cannot truly imitate.
    - 100,000-year red rings generally provide two soul skills; orange-gold/top rings may provide more.
    - Million-year ring color has uncertain legends: gold, white/natural, white-gold, or varying colors. Tang San and the Deep Sea Demonic Whale are mentioned.
    - Huo Yuhao / Spirit Ice Douluo and the Daydream Ice Worm are mentioned.
    - Tang Wulin / Dragon Emperor Douluo has a unique green-golden ring linked to life force, details Federation secret.
12. Ji speaks about Ferocious Beasts in detail, including Beast God Di Tian / Golden Eye Black Dragon.
13. After half an hour, Ji announces a ten-minute break before assessment.
14. No one leaves the classroom; even Jin Xiang remains seated and thoughtful.
15. Qian Lei explains Teacher Ji's terrifying punishment story: Jin Xiang once failed/boasted the class was useless, so Ji threw him upward out the window hundreds of meters, caught him, and repeated it more than ten times.
16. Ji frames this as useful weightlessness training because everyone will eventually learn to fly through mecha, battle armor, or their own abilities.
17. Ji returns and calls Lan Xuanyu first because he is new.

Yan Shuo insertion for this chapter:

- Yan Shuo may be present in the public class and feel the pressure like everyone else.
- He must not become the star of the spirit-beast knowledge lesson; Lan Xuanyu's family-background advantage is a canon beat.
- Yan Shuo can understand some bird/fire-beast and ring-age logic because of Lin Mei, Dawnflame Kite acquisition, and Spirit Pagoda exposure, but his knowledge should be practical and narrower than Lan Xuanyu's encyclopedic family exposure.
- Ji's line about future flight through mecha, battle armor, or ability can naturally strike Yan Shuo because his wing-frame is not true flight and because Chapter 18 locked his close-body armor/mecha-interface orientation.
- The ring-color lecture can remind Yan Shuo of the gap between legal paperwork and private living response, but no public 248-year soul-spirit/ring reveal occurs.
