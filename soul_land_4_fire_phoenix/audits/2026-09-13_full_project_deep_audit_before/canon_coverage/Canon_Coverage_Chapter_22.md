- Mu Zhongtian watches Lan Xuanyu's group closely due the previous simulation accident and because Lan Xuanyu received additional points twice.
- Mu sees Lan Xuanyu and Liu Feng defeat the stone aspect.
- Mu notices Liu Feng's offensive ability has suddenly increased and wonders what happened.
