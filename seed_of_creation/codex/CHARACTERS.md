## The protagonist

**Role in the story:** POV. The crime the premise is built on.

**Identity:** a genuine divine being from the first moment of his existence, son of Hui Mie and
Sheng Ming, carrying both their laws in one body, plus Adaptation Talent at divine tier. Sealed.

| Attribute | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Name | **AWAITING USER RULING** — candidates in OPEN_DECISIONS.md §2 | — |
| Rank | none. Not measured, not registered | `[design]` |
| Rings | zero. None has landed | `[design]` |
| Martial soul | proposed: a seed-form soul the Spirit Pagoda cannot classify, price or match | `[design]` |
| Age | twenty years by divine reckoning; twenty days by Douluo reckoning at the ascension | `[user ruling]` + `[canon]` #20 |
| Divine core | sealed, stage zero | `[design]` on `[canon]` #15, #37 |
| Position | **AWAITING USER RULING** — OPEN_DECISIONS.md §3 | — |
| First appears | not yet | — |

**What they want:** *unknown until the spine is ruled on.* This is deliberate. A want invented
before Lock 4 is a want that will have to be retconned, and Blue Silver's rebuild was exactly
that. The three candidate spines in OPEN_DECISIONS.md §1 each imply a different want:

```
Spine A → to keep existing, in a realm whose surviving law says he should not
Spine B → to become undeniable, for a succession that canon has already given away
Spine C → to find out what he is for, before something else decides it for him
```

**What they know / do not know:** `KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALLS.md` Layer 2 and F1–F3, F6, F8. He is an
expert on a world that no longer exists. That disadvantage is permanent and does not decay.

**Voice:** *undecided, and it should be.* Two constraints are already locked: he must be given
at least three spoken dialogue lines per chapter (kit hard gate), and he must not sound like a
twenty-year-old who has lived twenty days, nor like a god slumming. The second constraint is the
interesting one — he has twenty years of memories in a body the world reads as three weeks old,
and the mismatch should be audible in how he speaks before anyone names it.

**Arc:** *not drawn.* Drawing an arc before the spine is chosen draws it for the wrong story.

**Absolutes:** Lock 7, verbatim. Never kills a soul beast for a ring. Never takes a god position
by inheritance, claim or conquest. Never uses his parentage as an argument — if he says who he
is, it is a confession with a price.

## Hui Mie — the God of Destruction

**Role in the story:** father. Absent. The reason the era is what it is.

| Attribute | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Position | one of five God Kings; a Law Enforcement God | `[canon]` #4 |
| Artifact | the Scepter of Destruction, a super divine artifact | `[canon]` #4 |
| Subordinates | the Seven Original Sin Gods, plus the God of Damage; Zhou Weiqing | `[canon]` #4, #18, #19 |
| Marriage | to Sheng Ming. She calls him Little Purple | `[canon]` #3, #7 |
| Fate | fused with his wife into the Power of Creation; reduced to the Seed of Destruction, consciousness surviving, ten thousand years to return; his core left in Tang San's keeping | `[canon]` #9, #10, #11 |
| Succession | entrusted the Seed to Tang San to find a successor; Tang San chose Tang Wulin | `[canon]` #12 |

**What they want:** to expand the Divine Realm, because he believes the gods should be stronger
and more numerous. Canon is explicit that he is **not a bad person but too radical**, unable to
sit down and properly discuss things with Tang San, and that he deeply regretted the disasters
his expansion triggered. `[canon]` #2, Baidu *Legend of the Divine Realm*.

**Why that matters to this serial:** he is the antagonist of canon's own era and he is not a
villain. Any character drawn from his faction must be written as *correct*, not evil — including
whatever enforcement reaches the protagonist. And the protagonist is his son, which means the
protagonist inherits not a legacy but a mistake.

**What they know / do not know:** whether his seed-consciousness knows his son exists is
**proposed, not ruled** — see STATUS_PANEL.md and OPEN_DECISIONS.md §5. The two options produce
different serials: a farewell in act one, or an unreturned question across the whole span. They
do not both work in one story.

**Voice:** `[canon]` gives one direct line — "It's my fault, my Destruction Intent keeps us from
having a child" (#6) — and it is tender, plain and self-accusing. Use that register. He is not
thunderous.

**Absolutes:** his fate is immune (Lock 8). He fuses. He does not survive intact, and he does not
skip the sacrifice to raise his son.

## Sheng Ming — the Goddess of Life

**Role in the story:** mother. Absent. The law the protagonist carries and the one the era
needs.

| Attribute | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Position | one of five God Kings; a Law Enforcement God; presides over creation | `[canon]` #4, #8 |
| Marriage | to Hui Mie. He calls her Little Green | `[canon]` #3, #7 |
| Stance in the war | would not help against her husband, and would not help him either | `[fan]` #14, DD2.5Timeline |
| Act of grace | kissed the infant Tang Wulin's forehead and gifted him vitality — a gift even Tang Wutong did not receive, read in the recap as an apology for everything | `[fan]` #14 |
| Fate | fused with her husband; reduced to the Seed of Life with revival potential | `[canon]` #10 |

**What they want:** canon never states it, which is an opening rather than a gap. What canon
*shows* is that she refused both sides, and that her last recorded act was to give vitality to a
child who was not hers, as an apology. `[fan]` for the motive, `[canon]` for the act.

**Why she is the emotional centre of this serial:** the protagonist is her son, and he lands on a
plane whose **Life Core is A Yin** (#33) — a canon character defined by life, sacrifice and a
ten-thousand-year vigil, merged into a tree, who is also the Blue Silver Empress the user's own
earlier serial was written around. That resonance is noted in the project README and must be
handled as *craft reference only*, never as shared canon. But thematically it is exact: the son
of the Goddess of Life arrives on a plane whose life is being held together by another woman who
gave everything to stay rooted in it.

**Absolutes:** her fate is immune. She fuses.

## Tang Hao — Plane Master of the Douluo Star

**Role in the story:** the reason the protagonist's arrival is not private. Firewall F4.

| Attribute | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Canon identity | Tang San's father; the Clear Sky / Haotian Douluo; Haotian Hammer martial soul | `[canon]` #33 |
| Divine status | made a second-level deity after Tang San ascended; **chose not to ascend** | `[canon]` #33 |
| During the turbulence | could not enter the swept-away Divine Realm | `[canon]` #33 |
| Office | Plane Master of the Douluo Star, installed by Tang San | `[canon]` #33 |
| Mandate | care for the infant Tang Wulin; regulate the plane to prevent collapse from human over-exploitation; plot the annexation of the Abyss Plane | `[canon]` #33 |
| Power | actual combat power far exceeds an ordinary second-level deity | `[canon]` #34 |
| Later | condensed into form, resurrected the dead of the Abyss War, killed the Abyss Saint Emperor with one hammer strike, then fell into deep slumber with A Yin | `[canon]` #34 |

**What they want:** to hold a dying plane together for ten thousand years, quietly, while raising
a grandson who cannot know why.

**Why he is the most important canon character in this serial and also the least usable:** he is
everywhere and nowhere. He is a *plane function*, not a walking person, and he later sleeps.
Firewall Layer 1 draws the line exactly: he may register that something divine entered the plane;
he may never identify it as Hui Mie's son without an earned crossing; he may not act on it
immediately, because he has a catastrophe to hold back. **Watching is not a leak. Reporting is.**

**Absolutes:** immune. He becomes Plane Master, he kills the Abyss Saint Emperor, he sleeps.

## A Yin — the Life Core of the plane

**Role in the story:** the deepest resonance in the project, and the quietest. Firewall F5.

| Attribute | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Canon identity | Tang San's mother; the Blue Silver Empress; a 100,000-year soul beast who sacrificed herself | `[canon]` #33 plus SL1 |
| Divine status | made a second-level deity; chose not to ascend | `[canon]` #33 |
| Office | merged into the **Golden Ancient Tree at Shrek Academy**, becoming the plane's Life Core | `[canon]` #33 |
| Fate of her tree | the Ancient Golden Tree dies in Shrek Academy's destruction, along with Yun Ming | `[canon]` #36, `[disputed]` date |
| Later | falls into deep slumber with Tang Hao after the Abyss War | `[canon]` #34 |

**What they want:** canon never says. What canon shows is a woman who died once for her son, was
brought back, declined godhood, and then merged herself into a tree to keep a plane alive.

**Why she matters here:** the protagonist is the son of the Goddess of Life. She is the plane's
life. Firewall F5 permits her to *sense* him — wordlessly, as a tree, as a life core — and
forbids her from testifying. That is an entire relationship built out of something that cannot
be spoken, which is the register this serial otherwise lacks.

**Cross-project note, binding:** A Yin is also a central figure in `blue_silver/`, where the
serial's protagonist is a Blue Silver King of the Sunweave line heading toward a meeting with
her. **That project is craft reference only and never canon for this one.** Repo law is explicit
(`SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT` authority order; `STATE.md` rule 3). No character, event, name or
outcome from `blue_silver/` may be imported. The resonance is thematic and must stay that way.

## Tang Wulin

**Role in the story:** the one who gets it. Spine B's rival, and the mirror of the premise.

| Attribute | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Birth | born in the Divine Realm during the crisis; kissed by Sheng Ming, gifted with vitality | `[canon]`/`[fan]` #14, #15 |
| Nucleus | the Golden Dragon King's soul nucleus struck him and was absorbed; it could not be removed | `[canon]` #15 |
| Descent | sent down to Douluo by Huo Yuhao, sealed | `[canon]` #15 |
| Seals | eighteen placed by Tang San; **every two broken granted one Blood Qi Spirit Ring** | `[canon]` #37 |
| Succession | chosen by Tang San as the God of Destruction's successor; the Golden Dragon King bloodline stripped from him at that point | `[canon]` #12, #13 |
| Reserved | does not appear in this serial until the far horizon, ~22,650 DC | Lock 8, STATUS_PANEL FORBIDDEN NOW |

**Why he is the structural twin of the protagonist:** both are divine children sent down to
Douluo sealed, in the same event-window, by the same catastrophe. One is canon and gets the
succession; the other is ours and gets nothing. That symmetry is the whole of Spine B and it is
available as irony in Spines A and C. **He is immune.** He is never displaced, and he is never
met early.
