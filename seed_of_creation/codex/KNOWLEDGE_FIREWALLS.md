# KNOWLEDGE FIREWALLS

A firewall is a fact one character knows and another does not. Fan fiction collapses when a
character reasons from information they have no way to possess. Every firewall below is a place
this story must not leak.

**This era has an unusual advantage: the firewall is enforced by canon.** The Divine Realm was
swept away with every god in it, and no god is reachable from Douluo for the whole span. Nobody
on the plane can be told anything about the Divine Realm by anyone who was there. That is not a
convenience the author invented; it is receipt #15 and #33 in `CANON_NOTES.md`. Use it, and do
not break it for a scene.

---

## Layer 1 — Canon characters do not know about the OC

He does not exist in canon. No canon character may reason about him as though they had read the
source material. They may learn things the ordinary way: being told, seeing, deducing,
investigating.

**Test:** for every inference a canon character makes about him, name the in-world source. If you
cannot, it is a leak.

**The hard case in this project is the Plane Master.** Tang Hao regulates the entire Douluo Star
and his perception of it is canonically plane-wide (#33). A divine being falling onto that plane
is precisely the kind of event he would register. So the firewall is *not* "Tang Hao does not
know something fell." The firewall is narrower and must be written deliberately:

```
Tang Hao registers that something divine entered the plane.     — permitted
Tang Hao identifies it as a child of Hui Mie and Sheng Ming.    — NOT permitted, ever,
                                                                  without an earned crossing
Tang Hao acts on it immediately.                                — NOT permitted while he is
                                                                  diffused, regulating, and
                                                                  charged with a dying plane,
                                                                  a sealed grandson and an
                                                                  annexation plot
```

That third line is the load-bearing one, and it is canonically supported rather than invented:
Tang Hao is not a walking person during this era. He is a *plane function*, and he and A Yin
later fall into deep slumber (#34). A Plane Master with a twelve-million-death catastrophe ahead
of him, an Abyss to annex and an infant grandson to keep alive does not go and fetch a stranger.
He notes it. He may watch for a thousand years. **Watching is not a leak; reporting is.**

## Layer 2 — The OC does not know the future

He has memory, not prophecy. His twenty subjective years happened *before* the war and the
turbulence, in a hidden place. So he knows some things and cannot know others, and the line
between them has to be drawn before chapter one, not during it.

```
HE KNOWS:      the Divine Realm as it was. His parents. That his father wanted expansion and
               that his mother would not take a side. The war, if the hidden place let him see
               it. The turbulence, and the moment he was released.
HE CANNOT KNOW: what his parents became — the Seeds, the cores in Tang San's hands, the
               ten-thousand-year return. That happens as he falls.
               that a successor will be chosen for Destruction, or who it will be.
               that the Divine Realm is gone rather than merely unreachable.
               anything at all about Douluo's ten thousand years — spirit guides, the
               Federation, battle armor, the Spirit Pagoda's grown monopoly, the state of the
               beasts. He arrives in a world that did not exist when he was hidden.
               that Shrek Academy will be destroyed, that the Abyss will attack, or that the
               Silver Dragon will wake.
```

**Test:** for every decision that conveniently anticipates an event, name what he knew
beforehand and how. Silent foreknowledge is plot armour wearing a costume.

**The productive consequence, which should drive chapters:** he is an expert on a world that no
longer exists. Every institution he understands is gone, and the plane he lands on runs on
machinery, registries and an economy of manufactured spirits that his Divine Realm upbringing
gives him no vocabulary for. That is a real, permanent disadvantage, and it does not decay.

## Layer 3 — Ordinary characters do not know extraordinary things

A Spirit Pagoda clerk does not know what a God King is, beyond temple-story level. Common
knowledge in this era is narrower than the author's in specific, classifiable ways:

```
WHAT EVERYONE KNOWS:   the gods are gone and have been for as long as anyone can count.
                       No one ascends. The Spirit Pagoda prices and sells spirit souls.
                       Soul beasts are dying out. The Federation rules.
WHAT THE LEARNED KNOW: the Spirit Pagoda's founding by Huo Yuhao, ten thousand years back.
                       The mural tradition. That artificial spirits need beast genes and the
                       gene pool is failing (#30).
WHAT NOBODY KNOWS:     the war, the fusion, the Seeds, the Golden Dragon King's escape, the
                       infant sent down. All of it happened in a realm that then vanished, and
                       every witness left with it.
                       That the plane has a Plane Master and a Life Core. This is the deepest
                       firewall in the project: canon never shows the population knowing it.
```

**Test:** is this something a person of this rank, in this place, would have heard?

## Layer 4 — The reader is not a character

The reader knows he is the son of two God Kings. The reader knows Tang Wulin will be chosen.
That is dramatic irony and it is allowed — it is in fact the engine of Spine B. What is not
allowed is the narration quietly adopting the reader's knowledge and letting him benefit, or
any character on the plane acting as though they had read `CANON_NOTES.md`.

## Layer 5 — the project-specific firewall: **the age arithmetic**

This one does not exist in the kit's template and it is the most likely leak in this serial.

He presents as a young man of about twenty. If anyone competent works out that he has been on
Douluo for twenty *days*, and that he was already twenty when he arrived, then his existence is
impossible in mortal terms — and "impossible" points straight at the Divine Realm.

```
The firewall: nobody performs that arithmetic on page until the story intends the reveal.
What makes it hold: in an era of universal registration he either has forged papers or none,
                    and a person with no record is assumed to be a person from somewhere no one
                    bothered to check — not a person who is three weeks old.
What breaks it:     a clerk, a doctor, a soul-power assessor, or anyone who measures bone age,
                    spiritual power development, or growth. Canon's era has all of these and
                    uses them constantly.
```

**This must be planned, not discovered.** Before chapter one, decide which of the three
institutional encounters he cannot avoid — registration, assessment, treatment — and how each
one is survived. That decision is downstream of OPEN_DECISIONS.md §2 and §3.

## WHAT COUNTS AS EARNED

A character may cross a firewall only by one of:

1. **Being told** — by someone who knows, on page.
2. **Seeing** — witnessing the thing directly, on page.
3. **Deducing** — from evidence the reader has also seen, with the reasoning shown.
4. **Investigating** — spending time, resources or risk, with the cost shown.

Off-page revelation is not earned. "He had heard rumours" is not earned unless the rumour is
shown arriving.

**Note the scarcity this creates, and use it.** Firewall F3 concerns facts held only by beings
who are (a) seeds in a god's keeping, (b) unreachable, or (c) asleep inside a tree. There is
almost no legitimate route to that knowledge on Douluo. So when the protagonist finally learns
what his parents became, the crossing itself must be a major event with a visible cost — and it
should not happen in act one.
