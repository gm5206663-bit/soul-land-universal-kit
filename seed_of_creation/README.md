# SEED OF CREATION — a child the Divine Realm was never supposed to have

### Era: Soul Land 2.5 (Legend of the Divine Realm), running into the ten-thousand-year gap before Soul Land 3.
### Protagonist: the son of the God of Destruction (Hui Mie) and the Goddess of Life (Sheng Ming). Created 2026-09-18.
### Not the Blue Silver story. Not Fire Phoenix. Those projects are REFERENCE ONLY — craft, never canon.

## WHAT THIS IS

A Soul Land serial set in the least-documented era of the franchise: the stretch after Huo Yuhao
ascends to the Divine Realm, when the God of Destruction starts his war against Tang San, the
Time-Space Turbulence swallows the whole Divine Realm, and ten thousand years pass on Douluo
before Tang Wulin's story begins.

Into that gap falls a child who should not exist. Canon says his parents **cannot** have one —
Hui Mie's own words in the source text are that his Destruction Intent keeps them childless, and
that is repeated across every secondary source. So his existence is not a detail of the premise.
It is the crime the premise is built on: a Law Enforcement God broke the law he exists to judge,
and hid the evidence on a plane where no god would ever look.

He is a god from birth. He is also, on the page, close to powerless — because a divine core in a
mortal plane has to be sealed or it destroys the body that carries it. That is not a nerf and it
is not an author's convenience; it is the same mechanism canon uses for Tang Wulin, whose golden
dragon nucleus had to be sealed and sent down to Douluo because divine power was eating him alive.

## THE TWO CANON COLLISIONS IN THE ORIGINAL PREMISE, AND WHAT WAS DONE ABOUT THEM

Stated plainly, because a premise that quietly contradicts canon is how a serial gets rebuilt.

**Collision one — the childlessness.** Canon states, in Hui Mie's own voice, that he and the
Goddess of Life cannot have a child; every secondary source repeats it. Their childlessness is
a named fact of the Divine Realm, not an absence of information.
**Resolution:** the premise does not dodge this, it uses it. He exists *because* a Law Enforcement
God broke his own law and hid the result. Canon is preserved through causes, not forced outcomes
(Master Foundation §75.47). Recorded as `[user ruling]` over `[canon]`, with the cost written down.

**Collision two — the age.** The user's ruling was "twenty years old when Huo Yuhao ascends."
On one clock that is impossible: the ascension is a single moment, and twenty years of Douluo
time before it lands inside SL2, where the Divine Realm is not yet at war and no child of the
gods could be hidden. But canon supplies the way out — **one day in the Divine Realm equals one
year on Douluo**, stated by Tang San in the source text.
**Resolution:** twenty years of *divine* reckoning, which is twenty *days* of Douluo time at the
ascension. Both numbers are true; the question "how old are you?" has no clean answer, and that
is deliberately the structural hook of the character. Full derivation in `foundation/CONTINUITY.md` §1.
