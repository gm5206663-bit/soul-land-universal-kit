## 1. ONE-PARAGRAPH STATE

A new Soul Land serial, scaffolded and not yet drafted. Era: **Soul Land 2.5** — the Divine
Realm's internal war, the Time-Space Turbulence that swallows every god, and the ten-thousand-year
gap on Douluo before Soul Land 3 begins. Protagonist: the son of **Hui Mie** (God of Destruction)
and **Sheng Ming** (Goddess of Life) — a child canon explicitly says cannot exist — who is a god
from birth, carries **Divine Adaptation Talent** because the talent mirrors its holder's tier, and
is released onto Douluo sealed, in the same event-window that canon sends the infant Tang Wulin
down. **No chapter has been written.** Chapter one is blocked on four user rulings in
`foundation/OPEN_DECISIONS.md`: the spine (Lock 4), the name (Lock 6), where he lands, and what
his rings come from. If you stop reading here, the one thing to know is: **do not draft until
Lock 4 is filled.** Blue Silver shipped 90,000 words with that lock empty and was rebuilt.

## 6. OPEN QUESTIONS — each with what it costs

```
Q1. Which spine? (OPEN_DECISIONS.md §1)
    A — THE UNSANCTIONED GOD (recommended): the surviving law wants him unmade. Cost: needs a
        reachable antagonist, which means inventing how enforcement survived the disappearance,
        on a [fan] single-source row; and the antagonist must be written as CORRECT, not evil,
        because canon says Hui Mie's faction was radical rather than wicked.
    B — THE DISINHERITED SON: Tang San keeps the Seed from him. Cost: the payoff is fixed and
        cannot be won (canon immunity forbids rerouting Wulin's succession), so it runs toward a
        loss; and it risks turning Tang San into a villain, which canon does not support.
    C — THE ONLY VESSEL: every remaining power wants to know if he can hold creation. Cost:
        flirts with chosen-one destiny, which removes agency unless the price is stated and paid;
        and three antagonists in chapter one usually means none by chapter thirty.
    D — the user's own sentence, which outranks all three.

Q2. What is his name? (OPEN_DECISIONS.md §2)
    Five candidates given. Cost of leaving open: none yet, and it blocks chapter one absolutely.
    Also required: what the Spirit Pagoda's records call him, if anything — forged papers or no
    papers decides whether the institution is hunting him or has never seen him.

Q3. Where does he land? (OPEN_DECISIONS.md §3)
    Five options costed. Recommendation, not locked: a Spirit Pagoda city, because the
    classification failure is a scene rather than a backstory and it gives chapter one a face
    with a clipboard. Star Dou Great Forest is available but is blue_silver's ground.

Q4. What do his rings come from? (OPEN_DECISIONS.md §4)
    Four canon roads, all receipted. Recommendation, not locked: seal-break rings (canon
    precedent: Wulin's eighteen seals, one Blood Qi Spirit Ring per two broken) combined with
    restoration (he is the son of the Goddess of Life in an era of extinction, and canon's own
    answer to extinction is spirit souls that are not killed). Cost: it must never become a ring
    menu — every ring still needs a hunt, a cost, a moral event and a public consequence.

Smaller, non-blocking: how much of the hidden twenty years he remembers; whether his parents'
seed-consciousnesses know he exists; whether the Scepter of Destruction is ever mentioned on
page; words per chapter; whether the Abyss gets a face in act one; book structure.
```
