# OPEN DECISIONS — what is undecided, and what each option costs

Four rulings block chapter one. Each is written out with its cost, because an undecided thing
that gets invented quietly in chapter nine is how a serial earns a rebuild.

Nothing here is hidden uncertainty. Everything below is *known* to be undecided.

---

## §1. THE SPINE (Lock 4) — the one that matters most

The kit's rule: fill all three blanks or you do not have a story, you have a setting.

> **"______ wants ______ from my protagonist, and will ______ to get it."**

Blue Silver shipped 90,000 words with this sentence empty. All three candidates below are
compatible with every lock already set, and with the canon receipts in `CANON_NOTES.md`.

### Spine A — THE UNSANCTIONED GOD *(recommended)*

> **The law the Divine Realm died enforcing wants the unsanctioned god unmade, because a divine
> birth outside the sanctioned order is the same dilution Hui Mie went to war over — and it will
> send what is left of its enforcement down to Douluo to erase him before anyone learns that the
> god who judged every other god broke his own law.**

**Who has the face:** not an abstract law. Enforcement that survived the turbulence — a Deity
Patrol Hunter or God Officer cut off from the realm, an Original Sin God that fell separately,
or the Seed-consciousness of Hui Mie himself, which is canonically alive in Tang San's keeping
and canonically a Law Enforcement God. Also available: the **Plane Master**, who is charged with
regulating the plane and would register an unauthorised divine presence immediately.

**Cost of choosing it:** the antagonist must be *reachable* on Douluo, which means inventing how
enforcement survived the disappearance — `[design]` territory resting on the `[fan]` single-source
row #14. It also risks making Tang San's side look vindictive; canon is explicit that Hui Mie
"was not actually a bad person, but too radical," and that Tang San never hated him. An
antagonist drawn from that faction has to be written as *correct*, not evil.

**Why recommended:** it is the only spine where the opposition is generated by the premise
itself rather than bolted onto it. His existence is the crime. Every chapter has a threat and a
face without inventing new enemies, and the moral problem is real: the law is not wrong to want
him gone.

### Spine B — THE DISINHERITED SON

> **Tang San wants the Destruction succession to go to the child who earned it through ten
> thousand years of endurance, and will keep the Seed out of the hands of the son who was born
> to it — so the protagonist spends ten thousand years becoming undeniable, and the succession
> still goes to Tang Wulin, because canon says it does.**

**Who has the face:** Tang San, holding both Seeds. Canonically alive, canonically the one who
names the successor, canonically unreachable during the gap era — which is the problem.

**Cost of choosing it:** the payoff is **fixed and cannot be won**. Canon immunity (Lock 8)
forbids rerouting the Wulin succession, so this spine's engine runs toward a loss. That can be
magnificent — a ten-thousand-year story about being passed over — but it demands the reader
accept a protagonist who never gets the thing he wants, and it risks turning Tang San into a
villain, which canon does not support. It also needs a *reason* Tang San does not choose him
that is not mere ignorance, or the reader will blame the author instead of the world.

**Choose it if** you want tragedy and a rivalry with Tang Wulin as the emotional centre.

### Spine C — THE ONLY VESSEL

> **The Power of Creation wants a body that can hold destruction and life without unmaking
> itself, and every remaining power — the Seeds' own consciousness, the Plane Master who
> guards them, the Golden Dragon King that broke out of the same turbulence, the Abyss that is
> annexing the plane — will take the protagonist apart to find out whether he is that body.**

**Who has the face:** several, and they can be played against each other. The **Golden Dragon
King** is canonically loose, canonically wants the Silver Dragon to become whole, and
canonically may have engineered the catastrophe — a real antagonist with a real plan. The
**Abyss Plane** is canonically attacking across this era. Both are on Douluo. Both want things.

**Cost of choosing it:** it flirts with a chosen-one premise, which the kit treats as a trap —
"a destiny that picks the protagonist" removes agency unless the price is stated and paid. It
also spreads opposition across several antagonists, and a serial with three enemies in chapter
one usually has none by chapter thirty. Needs one face promoted to primary.

**Choose it if** you want the largest canvas and the most canon contact.

### Spine D — yours

Say the sentence in your own words and it outranks all three. `[user ruling]` beats everything.

**A note that applies to all three:** canon receipt #33 means the plane is *not* unguarded. Any
spine built on "nobody powerful is watching" is already false and must be discarded.

## §2. THE NAME (Lock 6)

The parents' canon names, in pinyin per the Language Law, are **Hui Mie** — *hui mie*,
destruction — and **Sheng Ming** — *sheng ming*, life. Canon gives them no child, so there is no
canon name to inherit. The choice is free, and it is the one lock that cannot be changed later
without a correction entry in `SERIAL_LOG.md`.

Names are written in pinyin everywhere in this project, never in characters
(`SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §1). The glosses below follow that rule.

Candidates, all pinyin, all ASCII-safe:

| Candidate | Reads as | Why it works | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hui Sheng** | destruction + life, in his father's surname-slot first | both laws in two syllables; the order says destruction came first, which is the tragedy | sounds like a title, not a person |
| **Sheng Hui** | life + destruction | the same idea inverted; life first, which is the hope | — |
| **Hui Yin** | destruction + hidden | names the concealment, which is the crime. Also quietly rhymes with **A Yin**, the plane's Life Core — a resonance, not a relation | could be misread as an A Yin connection |
| **Mie Sheng** | "extinguish life" | brutal, memorable, and exactly what the Committee would fear he is | the protagonist may not want a name that reads as a threat |
| **Chuang** | creation | the thing his parents died to make, and his ceiling | one syllable; thin as a daily name |

**Also required:** what the Spirit Pagoda's records call him, if anything. A person with an
unclassifiable soul in an era of universal registration either has a forged record or none —
and which one it is decides whether the institution is hunting him or has never seen him.

**Cost of leaving it open:** none yet, and it blocks chapter one absolutely. A protagonist
cannot be written as "he" for fifteen chapters and named in sixteen; Blue Silver could, because
a nameless grass being *given* a name by A Yin was the payoff. Here the naming would have to be
the same kind of event, or the name should simply be chosen now.

## §3. WHERE HE LANDS

He falls to Douluo in the same event-window as the infant Tang Wulin. Canon does not say where
Wulin landed beyond "sent to Douluo," and Tang Hao is installed as Plane Master partly *to care
for him* — so canon's own machinery puts the child somewhere Tang Hao can reach.

| Option | What it gives | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| **Star Dou Great Forest** | the last stronghold of the beasts; the Silver Dragon wakes there angry about extinction (#32); a son of Life among dying beasts is thematically exact | the user's Blue Silver serial already owns this ground — risk of treading your own previous work, and repo law says earlier projects are craft-reference only |
| **Shrek City / the Academy's edge** | A Yin is *literally* the tree there (#33); the greatest concentration of canon power on the plane; the Academy's destruction later gives the span a catastrophe to run toward | he is within immediate reach of the Life Core, which makes concealment hard to justify |
| **A Spirit Pagoda city** | the institution that prices every soul on the plane meets a soul it cannot price; registration, records, spirit-soul commerce, an antagonist with a desk | urban, political, slower opening; less wilderness danger in chapter one |
| **The Dou Spirit Continent** | Spirit Pagoda HQ (#42); the centre of the era's power | `[fan]`-tagged geography — a whole setting resting on one source |
| **The Extreme North** | canon's empty margin, Snow Emperor territory in SL2, minimal collision | nothing to push against; violates "if canon says nothing happens here, you chose the wrong place" |

**Recommendation, not locked:** open in a **Spirit Pagoda city** — the classification failure is
a scene, not a backstory, and it gives chapter one a face with a clipboard and a reason to be
suspicious. Move toward Shrek and the Forest as the span widens.

## §4. THE RING MECHANISM — what his rings come from

Lock 7 forbids killing a soul beast for a ring. Canon offers four roads, all receipted:

| Road | Canon basis | What it costs him | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seal-break rings** | Tang Wulin's eighteen bloodline seals granted a Blood Qi Spirit Ring per two broken (#37) | each break must be earned by a real condition — canon lists herbs, trials, rage, rescue as triggers | **strong.** Directly precedented, and it makes progression a structure of gates rather than a shopping list |
| **Spirits that choose him** | Overlord Dragon offered itself; the Damask Tulip, King of Immortal Herbs, offered itself on learning he was the Child of Nature (#38) | he must be *worth* choosing, and a spirit that leaves takes its rings with it forever (#28) | **strong.** Gives him faces who are not enemies — which the dialogue register needs |
| **Companion soul spirit** | requires a sincere, heartfelt contract; they never leave each other's side (#29) | total commitment; loss is permanent | good for one bond, not for nine rings |
| **Restoring years** | `[design]` on canon: he is the son of the Goddess of Life in an era of extinction, and canon's own solution to extinction is the spirit-soul system (#27, #31) | every ring is a beast he *saved* and that chose to stay — and the Spirit Pagoda cannot sell anyone else that | **the differentiator.** No rival in canon, and it turns Lock 7 from a restriction into the thesis |

**Recommendation, not locked:** combine the first and the last. Seals break on earned conditions;
when a seal breaks, what emerges is a spirit he has *restored*, not taken. The Spirit Pagoda's
whole economy is built on spirits it can manufacture, rank and sell — and he is the one person
whose rings cannot be bought, replicated, or explained. That is Lock 4's pressure applied to
Lock 9's measurement system, and it makes the institution's interest in him structural rather
than arbitrary.

**One thing this must never become:** a ring menu. Every ring needs a hunt, a cost, a moral
event and a public consequence, exactly as the kit demands of the kill-based gate
(`05_POWER_LAW.md` §4). Removing the kill does not remove the requirement that a ring be a
chapter's centre.

## §5. SMALLER UNDECIDED THINGS — none of these block chapter one

```
- How much of the hidden twenty years he remembers, and whether memory is reliable.
  Firewall L2 governs: memory is not prophecy. Decide before the first flashback, not after.
- Whether Hui Mie's and Sheng Ming's seed-consciousnesses know their son exists.
  Marked "proposed" in STATUS_PANEL.md. If yes, act one can carry a farewell; if no, the whole
  span carries an unreturned question. Both work. They do not both work in one serial.
- Whether the Scepter of Destruction still exists anywhere. Canon does not say (§3 negative
  finding). Lock 5 forbids him ever holding it. Safest ruling: never mention it on page.
- Words per chapter. Blue Silver ran ~2,200.
- Whether the Abyss gets a face in act one or is held as weather until act two.
- Book structure: how many books, and whether act one (the war and the fall) is a book of its
  own told from inside the Divine Realm before he ever reaches Douluo.
```

## WHAT HAPPENS WHEN THESE ARE RULED ON

Each ruling gets a row in `NO_MISTAKE_LIVE_RULES.md` under USER RULINGS with its date, quoted
verbatim; a receipt entry in `SERIAL_LOG.md`; and the corresponding lock is updated in place.
Then, and only then, chapter one is drafted and the gate is run on it.
