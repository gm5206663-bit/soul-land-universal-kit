## ABSOLUTE CURRENT RULES

- The live edge is **the foundation**. There is no chapter one. Do not write as though there is.
- `chapters/` is empty and stays empty until the spine is ruled on. A chapter written before
  Lock 4 is filled in is the exact failure that cost Blue Silver a 90,000-word rebuild.
- The protagonist's parentage is **not public knowledge** and does not become public without an
  explicit user ruling. Nobody on Douluo can deduce it: the gods are gone and stay gone for the
  whole era. See `codex/KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALLS.md` F1–F4.
- The divine core is **sealed**. No unsealing without a real cost, a prior failure, and a
  remaining limitation (Master Foundation §5 questions 1–5).
- Two numbers must never be mixed: **divine years** and **Douluo years**. One day in the Divine
  Realm equals one year on Douluo. Any sentence containing an age must say which clock it means
  or be unambiguous from context. This is Lock 9 and it is the most likely arithmetic bug in
  this project.
- `blue_silver/ADAPTATION_TALENT.md` is the locked universal core. `bible/ADAPTATION_TALENT_MODULE.md`
  is this holder's module. The module may be amended; **the core may not**. Nothing in this
  project may rewrite the universal core to justify one scene (Master §76).
- Nothing enters `codex/` until it happens in a chapter. The dossiers currently in
  `codex/CHARACTERS.md` are canon characters and the protagonist only; every invented character
  is added when they appear on the page, not before.

## THE LOCKS (verbatim, never paraphrased)

### 1. ERA

```
Soul Land 2.5 — Legend of the Divine Realm, and the ten-thousand-year mortal gap between the
end of Soul Land 2 and the beginning of Soul Land 3.

Opens at Huo Yuhao's ascension to the Divine Realm (end of SL2). The Divine Realm's internal
war, the Time-Space Turbulence, and the disappearance of every god all fall inside the first
act. The remaining span is the Douluo of the gap era: the Spirit Pagoda grown to a ten-thousand-
year institution, soul beasts going extinct despite the truce, the plane ruled by a
technological Federation, and no god reachable by anyone.

One day in the Divine Realm equals one year on Douluo. The Divine Realm's entire DD2.5 arc is
roughly one divine year, which is the whole ten-thousand-year mortal gap. The protagonist
experiences the war; Douluo does not.
```

### 2. PROTAGONIST

```
The son of Hui Mie (God of Destruction) and Sheng Ming (Goddess of Life).

WHAT HE IS: a genuine divine being from the first moment of his existence, carrying both
parents' laws — destruction and life — in one body, plus Adaptation Talent (divine tier by
inheritance, per the Master Foundation's rule that the talent mirrors the holder).

WHAT HE IS NOT:
  - NOT a mortal who becomes a god. He is not on the path to godhood; he is on the path to
    being able to survive what he already is.
  - NOT unsealed. His divine core is sealed for the same reason canon seals Tang Wulin's
    nucleus: unsuppressed divine power destroys the body carrying it.
  - NOT a soul master by the ordinary road. He does not kill beasts for rings. See Lock 7.
  - NOT a transmigrator, a system holder, a reincarnator, or a person with foreknowledge.
    He has memory, not prophecy. See firewalls L2.
  - NOT canon's successor. The Seed of Destruction is entrusted to Tang San, who chooses
    Tang Wulin. That happens. Our protagonist does not take it, and does not get offered it.
  - NOT a character canon knows about. No canon character may reason about him as though
    they had read the source material.
  - NOT someone whose parentage is discoverable by a competent mortal. The gods are gone.
```

### 3. CANON ENTRY — the beats this serial actually touches

```
C1. Huo Yuhao inherits the God of Emotions and ascends; Tang Wutong takes the Butterfly God
    seat. The Divine Realm gains its newest gods.
C2. Hui Mie demands the Divine Realm's expansion and the spirit world core; Tang San refuses
    on a premonition and offers a one-year bet.
C3. The God of Destruction's war: the Seven Original Sin Gods plus the God of Damage move;
    Xiao Wu is taken; Tang San's power is sealed; Huo Yuhao and Tang Wutong escape with the
    Desert Universe Hood and try to raise the Goddess of Life and Rong Nianbing.
C4. Tang Wulin is born in the Divine Realm during the crisis; the Goddess of Life kisses his
    forehead and gifts him vitality.
C5. The Time-Space Turbulence arrives. Hui Mie and Sheng Ming fuse into the Power of Creation
    to resist it and are reduced to the Seed of Destruction and the Seed of Life, consciousness
    surviving, their cores left in Tang San's hands.
C6. The Golden Dragon King breaks its seal in the Forbidden Land during the turbulence, and
    its soul nucleus is forced into the infant Tang Wulin. Huo Yuhao sends Wulin down to Douluo.
C7. The whole Divine Realm is swept away. Every god with it. On Douluo, ten thousand years pass
    with no god reachable and no ascension possible.
C8. The Seeds' return takes ten thousand years; canon entrusts the Seed of Destruction to Tang
    San to find a successor, and Tang San chooses Tang Wulin (paid off in SL4).

C1–C7 are on-page or near-on-page in act one. C8 is the horizon the whole serial runs toward.
```

### 5. POWER CEILING

```
CEILING: he becomes the only being able to hold destruction and life as one law without
unmaking himself — i.e. a vessel for the Power of Creation his parents died to make. That is
the top. It is reached only by paying for every stage.

NEVER GETS:
  - the Scepter of Destruction (a super divine artifact; canon leaves it with the succession)
  - an inherited god position — he never occupies a seat, his parents' or anyone else's
  - a divine core unsealed for free, or unsealed to save a scene
  - omniscience, or any system-like readout, voice or interface (Master §4)
  - the ability to create from nothing (Master §5)
  - immunity he has not paid for; overload may only occur with a traceable cause (Master §1.1)

FLOOR: at the start he can express almost nothing. His tier is genuinely divine; his OUTPUT is
near-mortal, because the seal limits what can be expressed through the substrate available.
Hold both truths at once — this is the same dual-truth structure Blue Silver used at M1.
Never write him as a mortal who is secretly special, and never as a god who is briefly weak.
```

### 6. IDENTITY

```
Public name:  *** AWAITING USER RULING *** — candidates in OPEN_DECISIONS.md §2.
True name:    the same, once it is given. He has no other name; he was never registered in the
              Divine Realm, which is precisely the crime.

### 7. ABSOLUTES — what the protagonist will never do

```
- He never kills a soul beast to take its ring. Not once, not under threat, not to save
  someone he loves. This is the era of extinction and the Spirit Pagoda's own betrayal; a
  child of the Goddess of Life who killed for a ring would be a contradiction the reader
  could feel. His rings come by another road — see OPEN_DECISIONS.md §4.
- He never takes a god position by inheritance, claim, or conquest.
- He never uses his parentage as an argument. If he says who he is, it is a confession with a
  price, never a trump card.
- He never prevents a canon character's fate. Tang Wulin's nucleus, the fusion, the turbulence,
  the Golden Dragon King's escape and the Wulin succession all happen.
- No identity reveal without approval.
- Power is never reduced to protect an outline (kit 05_POWER_LAW §1), and never increased
  without a cause, a failure and a remaining limit (§2).
```

  not skip the sacrifice to raise their son.
- Tang San keeps the cores and entrusts the Seed of Destruction's succession; Tang Wulin is

- The Golden Dragon King escapes and its nucleus enters Tang Wulin.
- The Divine Realm is swept away with every god in it. No god returns to Douluo during the gap.

- The Spirit Pagoda's history — founded to avert extinction, later complicit in slaughter —

```

### 9. MEASUREMENT — the dual clock

```
TWO CLOCKS, NEVER MIXED.

Divine clock:  the protagonist's lived years. One day in the Divine Realm equals one year on
               Douluo. He is twenty by this clock at the ascension.
Douluo clock:  the plane's years, measured in DC (Douluo Calendar). At the ascension he has
               existed for twenty DAYS by this clock.

Exact figures live ONLY in STATUS_PANEL.md, CONTINUITY.md, TIMELINE.md and chapter panels.
In prose: round, felt, spoken or thought by a character. A character thinks "he had been alive
longer than the town had been standing, and had been standing here for three weeks" — never
"he was twenty divine years old, i.e. 7,300 Douluo days" (kit 05_POWER_LAW §3).

Rank and rings: this era uses spirit rings granted by SOUL SPIRITS through the Spirit Pagoda,
not rings taken from kills. Record every ring's colour, source, and the chapter it landed in
STATUS_PANEL.md and nowhere else.

Seal stages: each unsealing is a numbered stage with a cost, a failure that preceded it, and a
limitation that remains. Stages are recorded in STATUS_PANEL.md FORBIDDEN NOW until they land.
```
