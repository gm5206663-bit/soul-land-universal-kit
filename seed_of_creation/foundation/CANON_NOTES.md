**Tags:** `[canon]` verified against source text or multiple independent secondary sources ·
`[fan]` widely repeated, plausible, not verified against primary text · `[disputed]` sources

## 0. THE THREE FINDINGS THAT CHANGED THIS PROJECT

Recorded at the top because each one reshapes the story and none of them were in this repo
before 2026-09-18.

### Finding one — the era is not godless. Douluo has a Plane Master and a Life Core.

`[canon]` Tang San became a god, and Tang Hao and A Yin were conferred as **second-level
deities** — but they did **not** ascend. They chose to keep living in seclusion on the Douluo
plane. When the Divine Realm was swept away by the space-time turbulence ten thousand years
later, they were **unable to enter it**. Tang San then assisted Tang Hao in becoming the
**Plane Master of the Douluo Star**, and **A Yin merged into the Golden Ancient Tree at Shrek
Academy, becoming the Life Core of the plane.** Their stated purposes: to care for their
grandson Tang Wulin, sent down to the Douluo planet; to concentrate power in regulating the
whole plane **to prevent its collapse from humanity's excessive exploitation**; and to plot the
annexation of the Abyss Plane. Tang Hao's actual combat power far exceeds an ordinary
second-level deity. Later, at the Douluo–Abyss final battle, he condensed into form and killed
the Abyss Saint Emperor with one hammer strike, then he and A Yin fell into deep slumber.

**Why this matters:** the earlier draft of this project's premise assumed "the gods are gone,
nobody can help or judge him." That is **wrong** and would have been a continuity disaster.
The Divine Realm is gone. But the plane itself has two canon second-level deities embedded in
it, and one of them is a **life core** — for a protagonist who is the son of the Goddess of
Life. And a divine being falling onto a regulated plane is exactly the kind of public fact that
must cause public consequences (`SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/05_POWER_LAW.md` §1). The plane
notices him. That is not a nerf; it is the anti-nerf rule operating at the highest level
available.

**Also:** A Yin merged into the *Golden Ancient Tree at Shrek Academy* — and that tree is
destroyed in the Academy's destruction, along with Yun Ming. See finding three.

### Finding two — the Abyss Plane is an active threat across this entire era.

`[canon]` Tang San plotted the annexation of the Abyss Plane while installing Tang Hao as Plane
Master. `[canon]` In SL3, the Abyss invades: the Abyss Saint King accelerates the fusion of the
Abyss Plane with the Douluo Planet, and the decisive battle ends SL3. `[canon]` In SL3 the
Blood River Killing God Great Array is built from a million souls of victims of the Shrek City
explosion, and the Federation, Shrek Academy, the Spirit Pagoda, the Tang Sect and the hidden
sects all fail to damage it until Ling Zichen detonates a twelfth-rank fixed soul bomb inside
herself.

**Why this matters:** it supplies a hostile, named, faceable opposition on Douluo during the
ten-thousand-year gap, which is precisely what Lock 4 needs. An antagonist does not have to be
a god to be a threat; it has to want something and be able to hurt someone.

### Finding three — Shrek Academy and Shrek City are destroyed during this era.

`[canon]` Two God-Killer-grade fixed soul guided shells, used by the **Holy Spirit Cult**,
destroyed Shrek Academy. The Ancient Golden Tree and Yun Ming died defending it, along with
almost all teachers and elders and more than twenty Titled Douluo. Shrek City deaths exceeded
twelve million, injured about two million; less than a third of the city remained. A crater
filled with water and became part of Sea God Lake. The masterminds were the Holy Spirit Cult,
**secretly supported by the Qiangu family of the Spirit Pagoda** and powerful factions inside
the Sun Moon Federation. Yun Ming briefly ascended to godhood to block the shells and fell.
`[canon]` The **Life Seed was placed in the centre of the lake to grow into the new tree of
life** and purify the radiation.

`[disputed]` **When.** SL4 has a character say "ten thousand years ago, Shrek Academy was
destroyed by god-level soul guided missiles… also in that age the Abyss Plane attacked and
almost destroyed the Federation," and describes the Shrek Seven Monsters' captain of that
generation rebuilding Shrek and founding Shrek City — which reads as Tang Wulin's own era
(SL3), not ten thousand years before it. Baidu Baike instead says "twenty thousand years later"
measured from SL1, which lands in SL3's era. Both readings put the destruction **inside SL3**,
i.e. near the *end* of our serial's span rather than inside it.

**Chosen reading:** the destruction of Shrek Academy occurs in SL3's era, at the far horizon of
this serial — **not** during the early gap era. Recorded in §2. If a chapter wants to set the
Academy's destruction on page, this row must be re-verified against SL3's text first, because
the two sources disagree by roughly ten thousand years.

## 5. RESEARCH LAW

1. Prefer novel or donghua text over any wiki. This file has exactly two source-text receipts

   here; weight arguments accordingly.

   source are marked and listed in §6.
3. Record the receipt: source, URL or path, date checked, confidence.
4. Record negatives too.
5. Tag every claim. Never present `[fan]` or `[design]` as `[canon]` in prose or in a status file.
