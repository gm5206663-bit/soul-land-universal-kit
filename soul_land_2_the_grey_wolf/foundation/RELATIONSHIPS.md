# RELATIONSHIPS — the map of the cast

v1.0, 2026-09-25. Canon facts receipted (CANON_GROUND.md); every
relationship below ours to build (or not) as the story walks beside
canon. Nothing forced; the author steers every meeting.

## His circle (before Shrek)

- **Ye Shan (father)** — teacher of the craft; the man whose plain
  competence the grown mind measures all fathers against, and finds
  wanting nowhere.
- **Zhou Xia (mother)** — the still center; the one person for whom the
  counting stops.
- **Grey Ridge** — the village that shrugs at a rank-one wolf, and does
  not know it is raising the most dangerous kind of patient man.

## The canon circle (the years ahead — all receipted, all PROPOSED as to
closeness)

- **Huo Yuhao** — the protagonist of the book he read; same years, same
  academy. He knows the boy's whole road: the mother, the mansion, the
  forest, the million-year gift waiting for him. What he does with that
  knowledge is the serial's biggest question — and the author's.
- **Wang Dong** — Yuhao's roommate; the haughty first meeting; the
  secret the reader knows and the village boy will pretend not to.
- **Bei Bei & Tang Ya** — the Tang Sect's heirs, the declining sect's
  hope; canon brought Yuhao to Shrek through them.
- **Ma Xiaotao** — the academy's elder-sister flame.
- **He Caitou, Xiao Xiao, the freshman class** — the year's cast.

Canon bonds are canon's — Yuhao's story keeps its spine. Our boy earns
his own place in the margins of it, at the author's hand.
