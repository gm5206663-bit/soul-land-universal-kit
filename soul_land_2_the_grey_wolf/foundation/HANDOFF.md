   fix the work or fix the panel, never the check. (k06)
2. **A bare panel line, or panels off-gate.** FULL panel at gate/gain beats,
   zero lines otherwise; bare (unquoted) panels fail the style gate. (k01, k02)
3. **A number that is not current.** `STATUS.md` is the truth; if a number
   is not there, it does not exist. (k05)

   unverified. (k04)
5. **Walking through canon instead of beside it.** Huo Yuhao's beats stay
   undisplaced; butterfly effects are computed and logged where the road
   touches. (k07)
