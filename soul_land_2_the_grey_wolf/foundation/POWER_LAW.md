# POWER_LAW.md — how power works in The Grey Wolf

## The unit

Soul master ranks with the canon ladder (innate soul power at awakening;
ranks step in tens; bottleneck every ten). Ring colors by age — white 10+,
yellow 100+, purple 1000+, black 10000+ — with the canon absorption optima
this serial keeps to: first ring ≤423y, second ≤764y (recorded as canon
receipts in `CANON_GROUND.md`; current rings yellow 120y/603y, inside the
limits, per `STATUS.md` §0).

## The shape of growth

The Ledger's evolution chain (F14/F23 family, verbatim in `RULINGS_LOG.md`):
**no terminal** — Low runs 1–100% and the instant it touches 100% it becomes
the next grade under a new name (Basic Walking Low → Silent Wind Step Mid →
Ghost Frost Storm Step High), and suitable techniques **fuse** — the footwork
fusion (F26) is law here: three Low footwork fuse into one Mid, not three
parallel masteries. Every meter in `METERS.md` keeps its own pace law;
everything that grows feeds every open meter.

## The system declaration

**This serial has a system: The Ledger** — the silent counting panel, fully
specified in `SYSTEM_SPEC.md` (both system-pack files exist and must both
exist). The meters are in `METERS.md`. This file is the law above them;
SYSTEM_SPEC is the implementation; on disagreement this file wins and the
other is fixed the same turn.

## The ceiling

- **Current position:** Level 30, Great Soul Master, two rings (`STATUS.md`
  is the single source).
- **Ceiling and end-state:** **not ruled as a fixed cap at adoption
  (2026-09-30)** — nothing here may claim a ceiling that `STATUS.md` and
  `RULINGS_LOG.md` do not carry. Proposals on file (ring aging to purple,
  concealment dimming) are PROPOSED, not law, until ruled.

## What never happens

- A resting terminal (k03, F14 family).
- A ring above the canon optima without a logged, receipted cause — the
  absorption record is hybrid/modified-body exceptions only, and this serial
  has not ruled one for Ye Cang.
- Numbers in prose: exact figures live in STATUS/meters/panels, never in the
  chapter body (k02).
