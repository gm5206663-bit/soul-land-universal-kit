# CANON STUDY — THE BEAST-TYPE MARTIAL SOUL

The research file, made permanent (Lesson 6: research everything completely
before starting anything). This is the first serial's Oscar study mirrored:
there the author demanded "go check how even food type martial soul" works;
here the soul is a beast-type, so the beast-type is studied whole before any
chapter. Sources: Baike (Beast Martial Soul, Martial Soul, Dai Mubai, White
Tiger), the fandom wiki (Wolf, Ghost Wolf, Silver Moon Wolf King, Demon
Wolf, Mutant Wolf Ape, Episode 011), gathered 2026-09-25.

## 1. POSSESSION (武魂附体) — what the release IS

"A Spirit Master using 'Possession' to attach the attributes of the animal
corresponding to their Martial Soul to themselves, enhancing their physique
and sharing damage... Beast Martial Souls achieve unity of human and soul
through Possession." (Baike, Beast Martial Soul)

The White Tiger attachment, verbatim (Baike, Dai Mubai): "When the Martial
Soul is attached, a pale white light erupts suddenly, muscles expand
abruptly, his golden hair instantly becomes mixed black and white, a
king-shaped pattern appears on his forehead, his hands double in size and
become covered in white fur, and sharp claws resembling 20-centimeter-long
daggers extend from his palms. His pupils become a deep, profound dark
blue."

So: light, muscle, fur and claw, changed eyes — the man wears the beast,
and the beast shares the damage. The first skill 「The Wolf」 is exactly
this, counted and metered.

## 2. THE BODY LAWS

- **Beast bodies run robust** — "the bodies of Beast Soul Masters are
  generally extremely robust," released or not. (Baike, Martial Soul)
- **THE PRE-30 WINDOW** — "At lower levels (before level thirty), Beast
  Martial Souls hold an advantage due to directly enhancing physical
  strength, but the gap with Tool Martial Souls narrows at higher levels."
  (Baike, Beast Martial Soul) — his whole village decade sits inside the
  window.
- **Tool souls are the opposite trade:** the tool leaves the body, the
  master's own flesh is never enhanced; poison in the tool never touches
  the host. (Baike, Martial Soul)

## 3. HOW CANON'S BEAST MASTERS ACTUALLY FIGHT (the receipts)

**DAI MUBAI — the White Tiger (Beast, Power Attack System, Light).** The
full ring table (Baike): 1st **White Tiger Protection Barrier** (a light
barrier; self-enhancement) · 2nd **White Tiger Fierce Light Wave** (a
ranged light sphere) · 3rd **White Tiger Vajra Transformation** (from the
Vajra Tiger; the body transforms — "for thirty minutes, all Base
Attributes doubled," abnormal-status resistance) · 4th **White Tiger
Meteor Shower** (area barrage) · 5th **White Tiger Devil God
Transformation** (self-strengthening) · 6th **White Tiger Destruction
Kill** (the heavy finisher) · 7th **Martial Soul True Body: White Tiger
True Body** (the giant tiger). Plus the **Nether White Tiger** — the
Martial Soul Fusion Skill with the Hell Civet Cat.

The template, read plainly: **possession is the birthright; the rings
build the named arsenal** (enhancement → ranged → transformation →
barrage → stronger self → finisher → true body); the third ring's
attribute-doubling transformation is the classic mid-game spike.

**The others, for the spread:**
- **Zhu Zhuqing — the Hell Civet Cat** (agility): the fusion partner;
  speed-type beast souls fight the same possession war, faster.
- **The Sharp-tailed Swift** (Baike, Martial Soul): pure speed; the true
  body is the full bird — arms to wings, legs to tail feathers.
- **The Great Strength Diamond Bear** (Baike, Martial Soul): defense-type;
  at attachment the body furs over and passes 2.5 meters; the true body
  passes 5 — "a true giant bear."

## 4. THE WOLF RECEIPTS (canon's wolf country)

**The martial soul (fandom, Wolf):** "a beast spirit which transforms the
body constitution into that of a wolf, giving sharp claws and talons,
boosting their strength." Users: **Su Yuntao — SL1's own traveling
awakening examiner** — Xiao Chenyu, Jia Long. Variants (design-doc only):
Inferno Grey Wolf, Stormwind Doubleheaded Wolf, Thunder Wolf, Green-faced
Wolf, Lone Wolf, Soul Wolf, Dragon Wolf. **No plain Grey Wolf breed
exists — the Ye line's grey is fur, not a breed.**

**The beasts:**
- **Ghost Wolf / Spectre Wolf (幽冥狼)** — iron-grey, green eyes, packs;
  "innately suspicious... prefer to exploit a target's weaknesses...
  willing to go through mutual attrition with their prey until the prey
  tires itself out or exposes a weakness." 10–1,000+ years; the waist is
  the vital part — "copper-headed, iron-boned, bean-curd waist." SL2
  receipt: Huo Yuhao and He Caitou fought a thousand-year Spectre Wolf at
  Shrek's Beast Dueling Area.
- **Golden Wolf, Cyan Wolf, Ghost Wolf, Elemental Silver Wolf, Purple
  Spirit Wolf, Wind Spirit Wolf, Stormwind Demon Wolf** — the forest's
  wolf country, ten years to the thousands.
- **Mutant Wolf Ape** (10,000+, Star Dou) · **Demon Wolf** (100,000+) —
  the deep end, later arcs.
- **The Silver Moon Wolf King** (20,000+, Jing Yang Mountain Range) —
  seven elements, immense defense, "could enslave other wolf-type soul
  beasts... Under its guidance the abilities of the entire wolf pack
  increased by about twenty percent" — a Golden Wolf at a hundred years
  holding a hundred and twenty. Deep-forest lore for the far arcs.

## 5. WHAT IT MEANS FOR YE CANG (design, PROPOSED — the author rules)

- The Wolf is a **Power Attack System** beast soul: possession gives
  strength, speed, the sharpened senses, the claws — the pre-30 window is
  the village decade's home ground, and the story should feel it.
- Ring-skill design follows the White Tiger template: the rings build the
  named arsenal on top of possession; the third-ring transformation is the
  classic spike. The first ring is the author's to rule (Issue #1's canon
  menu).
- The Ghost Wolf's way of war — patience, attrition, the strike at the
  weakness, the waist as the vital — is how a hunter's son already fights;
  the meeting of craft and soul.
- Mutation paths exist (Baike: "may undergo mutation... stronger or
  weaker") — the author's to rule, if ever.
- The True Body (武魂真身) waits at the far end — the seventh ring's
  country, decades away, design-doc only.
