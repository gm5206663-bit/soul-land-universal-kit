8. Chapter 08 — Road To Shrek

The third village fell behind by mid-morning, hem road thinned again where market town wall stopped. Hard ground, beaten flat, grass in middle beaten down again, he walked it because it was honest. Because his father had said hem north never in trees, because road no one owned asked no questions.

He carried half loaf gone, dried beans in cloth, ink cake in hem, road coat weighted with pelts. Spear on shoulder, two rings yellow within limits, wolf ice thin, level thirty, two slots. Spirit Connection two hundred, body near a hundred kilos, robust, he did not count aloud, ledger counted.

First day after third village was short, rain from night before left field edges soft. He walked edge where ground held, his feet threw no dust even carrying weight and road coat. That was because footwork was one line now, not three, walking and running and mountain stride had all been footwork. Each had hit a hundred at eight, nine, ten, they did not go to High alone. They fused into one quiet step, which fool masters so many step techniques separately, they fuse. Now quiet step seen-late, ground did not know he passed.

He passed fourth village before noon, twenty roofs, well with loose rope, children with no shoes. He mended rope because hands knew work, point leading, thrust recover low guard, boar point leads moving thing hand's width. Hands knew work. "Drink," the woman at the well said, and did not ask where boy eleven learned to splice rope so it held. He drank and walked. "Where you walking," a boy with no shoes asked. "East," he said, and did not stop.

Pool was dark dense now, water of deeper well fed by bloodlines and seventeen poured levels. Reborn mind wasted no hour and no motion. Method ran while he carried water, while he sat black pools, while he slept. Where other souls worked one hour, his worked every one, and it compounded. That was why perception was ten to twenty meters, house fly detecting hand before motion, he did not show it. On hem road, power flowed where eye went, body arrived where mind went first, that was Hunt's own effect.

Fifth village came with evening, forty roofs, forge with cold hearth, smith with burned hand. "Bellows drags," the smith said. "Hand's no good." He set bellows straight with one lift because flowing body made it easy. Wrist no longer sprained, back held winter wood without complaint, a hundred kilos lift at eleven, three times normal boy, denser bone, quicker muscle. Smith let him sleep by forge for it, night was cold, pool ran. He slept back to wall, spear point leading, and forge warmth made road coat steam.

Sixth village was larger, almost town, with cart track joining hem road, he heard track before seeing. Loose rim, dog favoring left hind, river before bend because air changed, that was senses at work, not numbers. He did not use burst, burst was for being seen late, here being unremarkable mattered more.

He kept rings hidden, to world, two yellow hundred-year rings showed, true yellow hundred-year within limits only Ledger saw. A boy showing two thousand-year rings would not be allowed to mend fences for bread. He learned that at second village.

He read signs without moving lips, one sign said Shrek City east, eight days by cart. Another said forest road closed after dark, spirit beasts, he understood.

He could sit hem road edge whole short hour, spear across knees, counting cart ruts and wind and field birds without fidget. That was ghost line at work, quiet step seen-late patience limb extremely patient willing mutual attrition until prey tires exposes weakness. He cooked at villages because hands knew that too. Camp cooking Mid, not feast cooking yet, but enough to make beans edible and bread warm. He cooked beans from old woman at third village in small pot borrowed from smith at fifth village, returned clean. That was honest.

He did not use burst on hem road, burst was ten meters blink that leaves afterimage one second. At purple it added wind blades and wings flight fifty meters fifteen seconds. Future tornado layer intense cyan light exploding into dazzling tornado, he did not use it. Hem road wants patience not burst, patience as limb.

He walked.

Seventh, eighth, ninth villages — he stopped counting villages after sixth and counted days instead, that was faster. Season to city if honest, father had said, hem roads around forest skirts. Academy sits northeast of Great Star Dou Forest, he walked north, then east around forest hem, never in trees. Trees were beasts country, hem was honest.

Some days he worked, mended fence, straightened gate, lifted axle, set post, carried water, flowing body made it easy. He was eleven but shoulders had freight height old line gave at High plus thousand-year rings, amber eyes grey tint. Village dogs stopped lifting heads when he passed, wood passed him at peace. Some days he did not work. He sat at culvert, spear across knees, and let patience count cart ruts wind field birds without fidget. In town, you learned where crowd would give, quiet step taught both.

He walked stone road with quiet step even though stone road wanted noise. Stone road had carts heavy, wheels iron, horses tired. He walked edge where ground still honest, not middle where carts beat dust. His feet threw no dust even on stone road edge, that was quiet step.

By fourth week, fields ended, city began as smell before sight. Smoke, iron, people, animals, soul power in air thicker than ridge, then wall. Shrek City wall was not village low wall built for goats. It was city that grew around academy for ten thousand years. Grey stone, high, with gates wide enough for carts two abreast and guards with spears who looked bored and dangerous both. He had read about it twice on Earth. Book this world lived inside had described it as place where Tang Sect declined beside academy and Sun Moon Empire rose on soul tools.
