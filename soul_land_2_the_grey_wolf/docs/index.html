The Grey Wolf

a Soul Land 2 fan fiction — a rank-one wolf, a silent Ledger, and the road beside Huo Yuhao

A tallykeeper dies on Earth and is born again on the Douluo Continent, six years before the story he has already read begins. A wolf, a single rank of innate soul power, and a thing behind his eyes that counts everything — slots, meters, and a full status panel. He knows this world's book. He intends to live past his page in it.
