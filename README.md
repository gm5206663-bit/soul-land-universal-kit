## The house rules (obey, never re-derive)

**Authority order:** author word > the serial's RAILS / FOUNDATION > its STATUS_PANEL >
its codex > the kit > this map.

**One copy of one thing** — a fact kept in two places will be wrong in one of them.
**Receipts, not memory.** No chapter ships without its gate passing; never weaken a gate to
pass it. **Add-only housekeeping** — superseded material is archived with dated receipts;
byte-verify before removing anything that looks like a duplicate. **Sync every mirror the
same turn** you change state. **Canon from primary text only.** **`_archive/` is never
current** — read it as history; the two parked serials are the one resumable exception (each
has its own PARKED note).

**Recovery, if ever needed:** everything deleted in the 2026-10-12 one-copy pass is
retrievable in-repo — `git show 9379d40^:_archive/2026-10-12_one-home/<path>` — and the
weekly account sweep keeps clone evidence for whole-repository loss.
