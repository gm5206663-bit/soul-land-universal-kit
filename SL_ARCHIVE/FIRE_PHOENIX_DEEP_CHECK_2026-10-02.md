## 4. Power system — the laws that must never drift

### 4.1 Ring/soul-spirit ladder (canon receipts the project uses)
- 10+ white · 100+ yellow · **1,000+ purple** · 10,000+ black · 100,000+ red · special/higher colors beyond.
- Soul-spirit **maximum conventional ring supply** by category (white 1 / yellow 2 / purple 3 / black 4 / red 4–5; ferocious/divine tiers further out).
- **No late color fixes.** A purple acquisition must be purple *before* fusion; evolution = dormant-trait awakening (e.g., skybone), not retroactive color surgery.
- Threshold breakthroughs (100→1,000→10,000) are **evolution opportunities**, not cosmetics — and never grant free extra skills/flight/Ultimate states.
- Soul spirits are **living and growing** ("part of the holder's living growth system"); they need nourishment, training, recovery, compatible inputs. They feel; they do not human-talk.

### 4.2 Staged actual-combat power (the anti-nerf grammar)
Formal rank is paperwork; combat power is **stage-scaled**:
1. Public restrained / no active Soul Power — reads as strong presence only.
2. Normal released combat, no full fusion — already above the paper rank; **beats Lu Qianxun fairly**.
3. **Dawnfeather Mantle** — real deep-yellow fire-light amplifier (not a decorative ring).
4. **Skybone Phoenix Frame** — deep-purple structural/body-line amplifier (not a posture trick).
5. Mantle + Frame layered — serious rank-crossing pressure; ordinary third-ring/Soul-Elder pressure is **not an automatic wall**.
6. Low-to-serious **Dawnflame synchronization** — living combat state.
7. **True Dawnflame Fusion burst** — major above-rank trump-card state. Costs proportional to duration/output; normal controlled use is not self-crippling.
Balance guard: this never becomes auto-victory over elite third-rings, veterans, or Dorm 333's hidden mechanics — and it is **never** nerfed to protect canon.

### 4.3 Growth economics
- From ch25 on, each single-level breakthrough costs **more than 3×** normal resources/soul power/body tempering/recovery — while adaptation, bloodline refinement, conversion, and integration **also increase** (the law forbids both free growth and nerfs).
- Perfect compatibility / perfect absorption = permanent integrated foundation: ring and soul spirit are **never "off"** when the visible skill is off.
- The Adaptation Talent gives no instant mastery, no immunity, no system/voice/screen; its effects must be shown as constant embodied micro-adaptation, not only under pressure.

### 4.4 Spirit Ascension Platform (the ch31 arc's engine)
Tickets are single-admission and precious; the platform is half-real/half-simulated; ~50% pain fidelity; soul beasts behave realistically; killing releases energy that feeds rings/soul spirits; cooperation raises efficiency; nearby participants share released energy; purge/alarm/ejection rules exist. Pacing rule after the post-ranking rebuild: **don't default to one canon chapter per fic chapter** — compress routine, expand only change. No invented reward ceremonies or items "just to prove a butterfly".
