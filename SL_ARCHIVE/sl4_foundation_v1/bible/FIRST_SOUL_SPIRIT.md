Dawnflame Kite's flaw is a fire-light split.

Its fire nature wants to burn upward and outward.
Its light nature wants to refine, brighten, and stabilize.
The two should support each other, but in this soul spirit they reject at the 100-year growth line. Every time it approaches the century level, the fire scatters the light and the light thins the fire.

Result before Yan Shuo:

- Recorded at exactly 99 years before absorption.
- Output is unstable.
- It flares beautifully, then collapses.
- It has strong instincts but weak growth direction.
- Spirit Pagoda evaluation marks it as risky or inefficient.
- Ordinary fire martial souls may burn with it; ordinary light martial souls may thin it; neither solves both sides.

This flaw is the reason it can be available to Yan Shuo's non-elite family.

The correct phrase is not "compatibility does not matter." The correct phrase is:

Compatibility matters at first, but Adaptation Talent makes long-term perfect compatibility inevitable for a soul spirit that has truly become part of Yan Shuo.
