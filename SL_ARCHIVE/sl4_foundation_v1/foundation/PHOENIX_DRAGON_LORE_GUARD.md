- high-level for a six-year-old, but not a mature True Fire Phoenix;
- not Ultimate Fire;
- not Phoenix God bloodline;
- not a full bird-king authority.

Correct wording:

- "Yan Shuo's Fire Phoenix is awake but young, still growing toward its name."
- "The record says Fire Phoenix; the power does not yet deserve every myth attached to that name."

- "Yan Shuo's Fire Phoenix is already equal to a True Phoenix."
