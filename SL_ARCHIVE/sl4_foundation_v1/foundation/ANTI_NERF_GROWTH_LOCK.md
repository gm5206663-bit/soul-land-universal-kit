# ANTI-NERF GROWTH LOCK

Live edge: after Chapter 14.

Purpose: prevent repeated accidental nerfs of Yan Shuo's growth, especially when canon introduces Lan Xuanyu or Qian Lei with high Spiritual Power values.

## 1. Core correction

Yan Shuo must not be written as ordinary, smaller, or weak just because another character has a special value in one category.

Current live state:

- Rank 16 / Level 16.
- One-ring Soul Master.
- Spiritual Power 61 / Spirit Connection Realm.
- Juvenile/incomplete Fire Phoenix.
- Yellow first ring with legal/original age exactly 100 years.
- Dawnflame Kite legal/original age exactly 100 years.
- Dawnflame Kite/first-ring private/internal 203-year yellow-tier equivalent practical-output maturity estimate.
- Perfect Dawnflame Kite compatibility.
- Dawnfeather Mantle improved but unmastered.
- Low-output Dawnflame Form casual/normal under academy rules.
- True combat Dawnflame Fusion is a real costly trump card that can briefly touch ordinary two-ring combat pressure.

This is not an ordinary one-ring child.

## 2. Spiritual Power comparison rule

Qian Lei around 130-140 Spiritual Power and Lan Xuanyu around/public over-100 Spiritual Power are raw Spiritual Power specialty lanes.

They do **not** mean:

- Yan Shuo is weak.
- Yan Shuo is ordinary.
- Yan Shuo's growth is poor.
- Yan Shuo's hidden adaptive foundation stopped working.
- Yan Shuo's Fire Phoenix/Dawnflame combat route is smaller overall.

Correct interpretation:

- Yan Shuo's SP61 is already Spirit Connection and clears the warship-piloting foundation threshold of 50.
- Yan Shuo's major strength stack is broader than SP alone: Rank 16 soul power, Fire Phoenix quality, body growth, Dawnflame Kite compatibility, Dawnfeather Mantle, Phoenix seed, recovery, instincts, and fusion rhythm.
- In combat, Yan Shuo should feel like a dangerous standout, especially in close-range pressure, fire-light defense/movement, recoil handling, and short-burst Dawnflame Fusion.

## 3. Growth-without-new-number rule

A chapter can keep Rank/SP/ring unchanged while still giving Yan Shuo real growth.

Always distinguish:

- **No new numeric upgrade**: no new rank, SP value, ring, soul spirit, skill, or formal soul-spirit age retest.
- **Real growth still present**: better control, faster body correction, clearer Dawnflame Kite response, stronger restraint, combat-reading improvement, recovery consolidation, social pressure adaptation, and Soul World experience.

Do not write "no advancement" by itself for a live chapter. Say what growth occurred and what numeric upgrades did not occur.

## 4. Chapter 14 correction

Chapter 14 is not a Yan Shuo nerf chapter.

It gives him:

- restraint under public fire-bird comparison pressure;
- body/Dawnflame Kite processing of Jin Xiang's one-ring wing stabilization;
- clearer understanding that wings are not true flight at one ring;
- secondary-profession awareness around body fit, battle armor, mecha control, and warship piloting thresholds;
- Soul World/pod readiness through his own team route;
- proof that he can support Lan Xuanyu without stealing Dorm 333's first proving moment.

It does not give him a new numeric upgrade because the chapter span is short and contains no formal Yan Shuo measurement, no new ring acquisition, and no earned retest.

## 5. Forbidden framing categories

Avoid live-state wording that frames Yan Shuo as lower overall just because another character has a higher value in one category. Also avoid wording that makes his current Fire Phoenix/Dawnflame state sound plain, decorative, or stalled.

Specific danger categories:

- raw-SP comparison treated as full talent ranking;
- one-number comparison used to erase combat/body/fusion growth;
- metaphors that make his current power sound tiny or fading;
- ordinary-body framing after the first ring and Dawnflame integration;
- live chapter summaries that say only what numeric upgrades did not happen while omitting the real non-numeric growth.

Allowed balance phrasing:

- separate standout route;
- raw Spiritual Power specialty is not overall combat ranking;
- elite child, not adult/limitless;
- no new rank/SP/ring, but real embodied growth;
- dangerous one-ring with short-burst ordinary two-ring pressure through true combat Dawnflame Fusion;
- strong but controlled.
