## 3. Soul power vs Spiritual Power

Never confuse these:

- Soul power / Rank / Level: cultivation strength, e.g. Yan Shuo Rank 12 / Level 12.
- Spiritual Power: mental/spiritual perception stat, e.g. Lan Xuanyu's test values in canon.

When writing status, put soul power rank first and visibly.

When writing tests, specify what is being tested:

- soul-power rank test.
- Spiritual Power test.

## Spiritual Power numerical lock

Do not confuse soul power rank with Spiritual Power points.

- Soul power rank/level: Rank 12 / Level 12, Rank 10, and similar cultivation-stage numbers.
- Spiritual Power: mental/spiritual measurement points and realm, such as 36 points / Spirit Origin Realm.

Current Yan Shuo Spiritual Power: **61 points, Spirit Connection Realm** after Chapter 11 and unchanged through Chapter 14. Historical Chapter 7 value was 36 points / Spirit Origin Realm.

Canon parity rule: when a school or family scene numerically tests Lan Xuanyu's Spiritual Power, Yan Shuo needs his equivalent Spiritual Power value if he is in the same public testing context.
