## 3. Yan Shuo butterfly simulation for Chapter 9

Chapter 9 spans three months. Yan Shuo cannot be frozen in place, but he also cannot copy Lan Xuanyu's Nana/private food path.

Yan Shuo's independent growth route:

- Class A / attack-type training after Chapter 8 treats him as a real but unsafe young yellow-ring asset.
- Gong Yinghao restricts Dawnfeather Mantle usage after fraying in Chapter 8.
- Lin Mei builds a boring but effective mantle-root routine: breath, shoulders, ribs, ankle, step, stop.
- Yan Ke tracks food/recovery and refuses expensive rare-food shortcuts the family cannot sustain.
- Dawnflame Kite's bond stabilizes; its measured age remains exactly 100 years, but response quality improves.
- Yan Shuo learns not to chase Lan Xuanyu's secret and not to mock Ye Lingtong.
- School rivalry continues through safer drills, not full secret-revealing duels.

Planned exact earned outcome by end of Chapter 9:

- Soul power: **Rank 13 / Level 13**.
- Spiritual Power: **39 points / Spirit Origin Realm**.
- Dawnflame Kite: still exactly **100 years** by original acquisition record; better compatibility/response, no age jump.
- Dawnfeather Mantle: improved control, still unmastered; can hold a school-safe short form longer, but no true flight/full wings.
- No second ring.
- No second soul spirit.
- No new soul skill.

Why this is balanced:

- Rank 12 to Rank 13 over three months is plausible for early-rank one-ring training with Fire Phoenix quality, hidden adaptive foundation, family training, academy drills, and soul-spirit integration.
- It is not automatic matching of Lan Xuanyu: Lan Xuanyu's route uses Nana, rare food, vortex/gold-silver danger, and bloodline techniques; Yan Shuo's route uses normal training, recovery, school controls, and steady integration.
- Yan Shuo remains in Spirit Origin at that historical Chapter 9 point, before his later Chapter 11 Spirit Connection breakthrough; Lan Xuanyu's hidden bloodline abnormality remains a separate protagonist mystery, not a measure that makes Yan Shuo ordinary.
