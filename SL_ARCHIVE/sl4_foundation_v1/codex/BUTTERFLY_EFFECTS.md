## 8. Butterfly-writing checklist

Before writing a scene, ask:

1. Is this scene public/shared or private canon?
2. If public/shared, what changes because Yan Shuo exists?
3. If private canon, why is Yan Shuo absent or what limited surface version reaches him?
4. Does canon measure a number/status? If yes, where is Yan Shuo's exact equivalent/absence note?
5. Does Yan Shuo's reaction show his hidden adaptive foundation without naming it?
6. Does the scene preserve Lan Xuanyu's protagonist mystery?
7. Does the scene preserve Yan Shuo's limits: no second ring, no second soul spirit, no true flight, no full wings, no Ultimate Fire, no mature True Fire Phoenix, and no unlimited high-output form?
