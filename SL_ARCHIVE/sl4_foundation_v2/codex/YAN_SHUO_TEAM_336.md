# Yan Shuo Elite Junior Class Team - Dorm 336

Live edge: after Chapter 14.

Purpose: define Yan Shuo's separate Elite Junior Class team so Chapter 15 can use Soul World without accidentally moving Yan Shuo into Dorm 333 or stealing Lan Xuanyu/Qian Lei/Liu Feng team beats.

## 1. Team lock

Yan Shuo's team/dorm is **Dorm 336**.

Dorm 333 remains canon-protected:

- Lan Xuanyu.
- Qian Lei.
- Liu Feng.

Yan Shuo must not join Dorm 333, replace one of them, or solve their first Soul World teamwork problem.

Dorm 336 is an OC/non-Dorm-333 team slot created by replacing one nameless male candidate/classmate from the original broad class background. If later canon reveals a direct conflict with this exact dorm number, preserve the team concept and adjust only the label.

## 3. Why this team assignment works

- Dorm 333's empty seat was preserved for Lan Xuanyu.
- Yan Shuo needed a team where his Fire Phoenix pressure mattered without becoming Lan Xuanyu's roommate.
- Song Yichen gives defensive shape and adult-approved restraint.
- Luo Haoran gives mobility/interruption and can create mistakes if he overextends.
- Yan Shuo gives decisive pressure, controlled fire, and emergency burst potential.
- The team naturally creates Chapter 15 parallel Soul World stakes without interfering with Dorm 333's Lava Hound problem.

## 4. Team dynamic

Song Yichen:

- Calls Yan Shuo back when pride or friendship might pull him out of formation.
- Respects Yan Shuo's power but does not worship it.
- Uses short commands and expects obedience.
- Can clash with Yan Shuo over when to burst and when to hold.

Luo Haoran:

- Curious about Dawnflame Form and sometimes jokes too close to danger.
- Likes speed and hates waiting behind a shield.
- May compare himself to Liu Feng because both have speed issues, but Luo's route is interruption rather than pure spear-speed.
- Gives Yan Shuo a peer who can admire him without making him flawless.

Yan Shuo:

- Must learn that his role in Dorm 336 is not only to hit hardest.
- His hidden adaptive foundation should process team rhythm: shield timing, mantis feints, fire restraint, and burst windows.
- He can be strong while still learning how not to burn Dorm 336's formation.
