# 📖 THE GOLDEN LION — Soul Land 2 fan fiction
## Start here. Chapters live in this repo; one click each.

**The story:** Jin Yang, eleven, a smith's boy from Jinxi Village, walks to Shrek Academy with a
500-year lion ring and a nearly-millennial drake ring and a rule from his grandfather:
*you never walk past a man going down on the road.* Canon stays canon around him; the world
is different because he is in it — that is the only promise this serial makes.
