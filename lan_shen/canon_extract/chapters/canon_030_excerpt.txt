1. THE PIECE, FACE-ON (continues 029's night visit — Tang Ziran is present)
=====================================================================

He goes to it without being urged. One circle smaller than the raw lump; the lustrous
silver has gone **dull grey**; the whole impression: deep, reserved, simple. Engraved
across the surface: **endless layers of a wave-like pattern**, "as though it represented
unlimited life force." 🔴 The connection, stated on page: looking at it, **it felt as
though that piece of metal were a part of him.**

2. THE PRAISE THAT COSTS A MASTER Something
=====================================================================

Mang Tian (rare register — Tang Ziran is visibly astonished at the height of it):
he had never expected a first-try success; the result owes partly to the body and its
physical abilities, most of all to the boy's **comprehension of the process while he
refined it**. *"I did not recognize the wrong apprentice; in this regard, you are a

=====================================================================
4. DOCTRINE: HUNDRED PURIFIES, THOUSAND ENLIVENS
=====================================================================

*"Do you understand what the Thousand Refinements are now?"* — his answer: *"It was as
if the metal had gained a life of its own. Every time I struck it, I could hear the
sounds it was making."*
Canon then codifies, in Mang Tian's mouth:
- **The Hundred Refinements purifies and removes impurities.**
- **The Thousand Refinements bestows life into the metal** — originally a method
  created by blacksmiths to infuse life into metal.
- Metal that has life is the most precious to blacksmiths; living metal can then
  **awaken its innate special qualities.**

=====================================================================
5. 🔴🔴 BLOOD SACRIFICE — NAME, TRADITION, LAW (the title's cargo)
=====================================================================

- Tradition: a blacksmith's **first Thousand Refined work undergoes the Blood
  Sacrifice**, to forever be part of the blacksmith's belongings.
- Mechanism, stated: the metal **becomes one with your blood vessels — a part of your
  body**; combined with a Thousand Refined metal, **your blood vessels receive the same
  improvement**, able to bring out stronger innate qualities.
- Tang Ziran's civilian objection: *"If every Thousand Refinements requires the Blood
  Sacrifice, how much blood will a blacksmith lose?"* Mang Tian: *"If you don't
  understand, don't blabber rubbish."*
- THE LAW, complete: the first work must (tradition). Thereafter, rarely — only when
  wholly satisfied. 🔴 **A Blood Sacrificed metal can only be used by the one whose
  blood it took; others cannot forge with it — the metal would shatter; it recognizes
  only its master.** (Client exception: a client may offer their own blood.)
- Warning to the boy: do not easily use the Blood Sacrifice in future — **it harms
  vitality**. Wulin promises, nodding furiously; his eyes never leave the piece.

=====================================================================
6. THE GIFT AND ITS RULES
=====================================================================

*"It's yours."* — *"But Master, I cannot afford it."*
Rule, stated outright: 🔴 **regardless of who provided the material, the first work
produced by the Thousand Refinements belongs to the blacksmith who made it.** The Heavy
Silver has a price; the Thousand Refinements is **priceless**.
- Size/plan: this piece will make **two hammers**, a perfect fit; injunction: **before
  leaving for intermediate academy, finish crafting them**; Mang Tian wants to see the
  awakened special quality.
- The weight, jaw-dropped on page: **approximately 150 kilograms** — he hugs it home.
- 🔴 Quiet recognition beat: only **after** the Thousand Refinements did Mang Tian truly
  recognize him as disciple — from then on the master stops his own work to teach
  patiently.
