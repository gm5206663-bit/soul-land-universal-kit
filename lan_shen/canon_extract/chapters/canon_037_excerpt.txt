2. LONG HENGXU'S COURT (the rulings, all canon)
=====================================================================

"What's going on?" — a firm voice in the hall. Ten minutes later, the intermediate
teaching building: four boys in a row; Yun Xiao narrates honestly.
**Director Long Hengxu**, frowning "like a drop of water": *"So in conclusion, this
tragedy was triggered over a blanket?"*
- **Zhou Zhangxi:** "originally with a martial soul like yours, you would have been
  assigned to class three — however, your soul power rank was too low. Now it seems
  your moral conduct is equally as low. Getting beaten up serves you right. **Class
  five permanently.**"
- **Yun Xiao + the arrogant youth:** "Fighting as soon as you enter the academy.
  You'll both be in class five." To Yun Xiao's "I'm only a spectator": "You're all in
  the same dorm room. If you see them fighting and don't take any action to stop it,
  how can you say you are the righteous one? You're so sly at such a young age — how
  could you grow up to be any good."
- **Tang Wulin:** "You won a fight, one against two. This time's matter can't be
  blamed on you as you were provoked. It's good that a man is brave, so I won't
  punish you. However, your martial soul is just Bluesilver Grass — **so you're stuck
  in class five.**" (His astonished "Thank you for your fairness" is on page.)
- 🔴 **THE COLD CLAUSE:** "You can continue fighting if you want, but next time it'll
  be added to your record. **Battle results and the record of your talent toward
  becoming a Mecha Master will affect advanced-academy acceptance.** If you have the
  courage, then go fight — but don't let the academy find out, otherwise severe
  punishment." (The academy's tolerated-underground engine, canon-decreed.)
- 🔴 AND THE BILL: he'll be given the costs of the window, bed, and walls "so you can
  pay it back." His interior panic: he has exactly **3,000 federal coins**, his
  father's pocket money for the whole semester. (Compulsory education covers food and
  living — the academy economics beat, stated.)

Grass accomplish this? Moreover, he cultivated a trash martial soul like Bluesilver
Grass to rank 11 at nine years old. **Just this would make him a prodigy.** Contrary
to what you might think, we must pay close attention to him."**
