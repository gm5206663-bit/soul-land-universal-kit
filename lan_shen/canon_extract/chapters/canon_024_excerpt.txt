TIMING: days after fusion (canon 020-022) — a morning some nights later. "Early in the
morning, Tang Wulin awoke from his meditation and discovered himself SURROUNDED BY GRASS."
His post-fusion cultivation in the garden is much faster (Soul Master realm + martial soul

🔴 "Lin Lin! Lin Lin!" Lang Yue's anxious voice — THE SECOND STREET-CALL, echoing canon
015's dawn. This time: "Something's wrong. Na'er, Na'er… She— She's gone. Na'er is gone."

🔴🔴 THE LETTER ON HER BED, VERBATIM (must be re-voiced if ever staged):
"Mom, Dad, big brother, I'll be leaving now. Thank you for taking care of me these past
few years, but I remember who I am now. My family has come to pick me up, so I have to go.
I was really happy during the days I spent with you all. Extremely happy. I really didn't
want to part with you all, but from the memories I've recalled, I know that I must leave.
I have many things I must do. Big brother, I will always remember how you looked when you
protected me from those bad guys. I will always remember the taste of all the treats you've
bought me."
— Na'er.

🔴🔴 SHE IS TAKEN, AND HER ESCORT SPEAKS: against a wall, crying, clutching a crude rag
doll — violet eyes, silver hair THAT WULIN DYED HIMSELF — the first present he bought her
with his monthly wage; it looks just like her. Tear after tear. Wulin's image revolving in
her mind: the smiles he worked to put on her face, his stubborn protecting. "Big brother,
big brother…" — then, from somebody present and unnamed: 🔴 "Young Lady, we must leave now."

🔴🔴 LEFT BEHIND: a small silver pendant necklace, an inlaid silver gem, perfectly round,
with a silver thread around its edges. 🔴 SEVEN rays of light faintly distinguishable,
twinkling within the gem. Obviously of considerable value.

WULIN: scours the whole town, sobbing screams "Na'er, Na'er", along the coastline, hoarse;
no trace. For days: prowls streets to late night, forging mistakes, firmly scolded by Mang
Tian, working hours +3 daily. Recovers after about a whole week.

THEN THE SCHOOL WINDOW: Red Mountain Academy — Lin Ximeng hands Wulin the special
RECOMMENDATION LETTER (any Soul Master with a ring can register for an intermediate
academy). 🔴 This signifies the conclusion of elementary academy days.
WAN YUNCHAO (canon-named classmate, fat, winks): "We're graduating now. Let's go swap
pointers later." Spar arranged after school in the large grove (Nature Studies ground).
His short blade now carries a white 10-year ring and extends fifteen centimeters when the
ring moves onto it. (Spar continues into canon 025 "Thousand Refinements".)
