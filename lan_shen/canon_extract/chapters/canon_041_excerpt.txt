1. THE ROOM AND THE RULES
=====================================================================

- The testing room **is a forging room** — "the same as Mang Tian's"; for the first
  time since arriving in Eastsea he stands in a familiar environment, and his
  nervousness eases. 🔴 (The forge as refuge-beat begins here.)
- 🔴 **THE SURVEYOR:** a middle-aged woman who evidently knows Cen Yue; experienced;
  her tease lands — "If he was just a bit younger, he would be able to attack the
  President's record." 🔴🔴 **(The youngest-testee record of 040 is THE PRESIDENT'S.)
- THE TEST, canon rules: **15 kinds of metal**, each chunk one-third meter squared;
  pick one and **purify** it; graded on the metal chosen AND the degree of
  purification; **score over 60 = rank 1 blacksmith title.** The metals are
  unidentified — identifying them is part of the exam; quality and purification are
  coupled (harder metal drags the score).

=====================================================================
2. 🔴 HEAVY SILVER, CHOSEN WITHOUT A SOUND
=====================================================================

- He picks from the middle of the shelf: a chunk twinkling faint silver light.
  Both witnesses reveal shock — such a thick and heavy choice.
- 🔴🔴 **The chunk weighs OVER 300 KILOGRAMS; he picks it up and sets it on the forging
  table without the slightest sound.** Mang Tian's discipline quoted: forbidden to
  let metal collide with metal unless forging. Surveyor: "Little guy, your strength
  truly isn't small!"
- His reason (canon, internal): **not to show off** — it is simply the last metal he
  forged; he knows its characteristics; the chunk even resembles "the one he had
  forged with originally." ("Like seeing someone else hunting and being excited by
  one's memories of the thrill of the hunt.")
- 🔴 **Mang Tian's law, restated on page:** he must not use his **Thousand Refined
  Heavy Silver Hammers** unless alone — **not until he is fifteen years old.**
  (The blood-sacrificed pair stays hidden; the TUNGSTEN pair is the public tool.)
- Time limit: **one hour**; calcining time counts against it.
