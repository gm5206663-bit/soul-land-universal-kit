1. 🔴🔴 GEOGRAPHY CARD — EASTSEA CITY, ON THE RECORD
=====================================================================

- **Eastsea City: the second largest seaside city in the Sun-Moon Federation.**
- Main feature: its **port** — a nexus for travelling the seas and exploiting the
  ocean's resources.
- **Population over three million.** Even federation-wide it counts as a **second-tier
  city**, prosperous off the ocean.
- Long history, simple and unadorned style preserved: the federation has protected the
  ancient buildings these last several hundred years; **millennia-old buildings stand.**
- **Soul trains: dark blue**; nearly all soul trains in Eastsea City are blue. The
  station ceiling = metal tubes **cast and pressed like ordinary metal** (his
  blacksmith's eye reads the building — first city-apprentice beat in canon).
- The scale-gap, stated: the station alone is grander than the largest building in
  Glorybound City; skyscrapers in the distance; soul cars constantly speeding;
  the streets "countless times busier" than Glorybound's.

=====================================================================
2. THE ALONE-NESS, STAGED PLAINLY
=====================================================================

- Nine years old, sent **alone**: dad said he was a big kid and should experience it
  himself; they bought the ticket and saw him onto the train. Lang Yue's tireless
  instructing until departure; his mind goes blank on arrival — perplexed, frightened,
  gloomy.
- The father's slip of paper, first line: 🔴 **"Don't trust strangers easily."** — and
  canon tests it at once: a beaming slim middle-aged man ("first time? where are your
  parents?") gets a head-shake and a quick walk-off.
- He consults the **Administrative Law Enforcement** tower instead: two uniformed
  officers point him to the bus. To "where's your family?" he straightens and answers
  the chapter's signature line: 🔴 **"Uncle, I'm not little anymore. Thank you."**

3. 🔴 THE YOUNG MASTER ON THE PLATFORM (UNNAMED, FILED)
=====================================================================

The crowd splits around a polished **black soul car parked on the platform** — slender,
sleek, four wheels **on caterpillar tracks** (all-terrain wealth); two men in black
suits. They call out **"Young Master!"** — and the target is **a boy his own age**:
dressed head-to-toe **in blue**, short brown hair, fair white skin, straight nose,
slightly sunken eyes, long curled lashes, 🔴 **eyes of deep green.**
The collision: Wulin is shoved by someone, stumbles into the young master's shoulder.
The youth turns — handsome, and **cold and arrogant in temperament**; the expression
sours, he glances once, and walks on. 🔴 **Not indifference — disdain.**
Wulin's "My apologies!" earns him a black-suited shove back into the crowd and a
fierce **"Be careful you hick."** The car rumbles off. His rub of the stomach and his
verdict: *'These city people are truly too tyrannical!'*
