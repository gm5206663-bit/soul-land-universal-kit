THE AWAKENING LAW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"All of the people residing upon the Douluo Continent possessed something called a
'martial soul.' The martial soul was a part of the body which could be awakened when they
became six years old, regardless of whether they were animals or plants."

"the martial soul had long since become the most significant factor in determining
someone's life."

🔴 "However, the emergence of soul power was even more important. ... Although everyone
possessed a martial soul, ONLY ONE IN A THOUSAND PEOPLE MIGHT POSSESS SOUL POWER."

"The soul power rank represented someone's future and power."

"over the 100,000 years the Douluo Continent has existed, the most noble profession has
been that of a Soul Master."

"even commoners would look forward to their children becoming six years old ... If they
possessed soul power, their lives would be transformed, benefitting them and their whole
family."

THE STREET, AT DAWN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"The moment dawn arrived, the streets in front of the Red Mountain Academy were packed.
The streets were flooded with parents and their children who had come to participate in
the Awakening."

"There was an unceasing torrent of parents and children passing as they exited the
academy. The overwhelming majority of them all left with sighs of disappointment, but
occasionally one of them would be overjoyed. There was no need to ask why; their martial
soul must have given birth to soul power. This immediately attracted the envious and
jealous gazes of others."

TANG WULIN, AGE SIX — CANONICAL DESCRIPTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"A small boy, just barely six years old, said with excitement as he looked towards his
father. He loved to listen to his father's stories about Soul Masters. He had a pair of
large eyes filled with a longing for the future."

"The young boy had a head full of short black hair and a stature slightly taller than
others of the same age. However, his most attractive feature were his large, clear black
eyes and long eyelashes which wouldn't lose to a girl at all. Even with his commoner's
clothing, his outstanding appearance could easily draw the eyes of others."

Dialogue:
  "Dad, what do you think my martial soul will be?"
  "Dad doesn't know either. It all depends on luck."
  "I'll definitely have soul power, right?"
  "Regardless of whether or not you have soul power, dad will always love you."

TANG ZIRAN (father): "a middle aged man who shared a similar appearance. He had a medium
build and an average height, everything about him was just average."
  — "His father released a gloomy sigh within his heart. Actually, the Awakening Day was a
     decisive day that decided one's life and future."
  — "Fortunately, in this era those who were unable to become Soul Masters were still able
     to have a pretty good future."

RED MOUNTAIN ACADEMY — GROUNDS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"Red Mountain Academy was an elementary grade comprehensive academy able to hold two
thousand students. The buildings within the academy were all simple and unadorned with
white roofs and red walls. The academy grounds were exceptionally clean. Awakening Day was
right before the start of a new term. In comparison to the clamor outside of the walls, the
academy seemed unusually peaceful."

🔴 THE SPIRIT PAGODA IS IN THE ROOM — THE FAR SPINE, CANONISED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"The teacher brought them to the front of a circular building before telling Tang Ziran,
'Would the child's parent please wait here for a moment?'"

"Every single elementary academy would have this circular building. It was called the
Awakening Chamber, and it was specifically used to aid six year old children in the
awakening of their martial soul. BEFORE THE DAY OF THE AWAKENING CEREMONY, THE ACADEMY
WOULD INVITE A SPIRIT MASTER FROM THE SPIRIT PAGODA TO CONDUCT THE AWAKENING CEREMONY.
Decades ago, he had the same hopes when he entered the Awakening Chamber."

"As he watched his son follow the teacher into the circular building, a trace of
disappointment and frustration appeared within Tang Ziran's eyes as he reminisced of the
past."

"There wasn't just a single awakening area within the Awakening Chamber, rather there were
SEVEN FLOORS and within each one was an Awakening Chamber. Tang Wulin was brought to the
Awakening Chamber on the THIRD FLOOR."

"All of the floors, the walls, and even the ceiling had wonderful inscriptions carved into
them to create an elegant, decorative design."

"Within the Awakening Chamber was a middle aged man wearing a SIMPLE ORANGE ROBE, that
featured the EMBROIDERED FORM OF A SOUL BEAST."

"These Spirit Masters from the Spirit Pagoda were powerful and mysterious existences. They
were an exceptionally special group of Soul Masters and held an extremely majestic status
on the Douluo Continent. However, this was the first time he had ever seen one."

"'Hello.' Tang Wulin shyly replied."
"The Soul Master wore a gentle smile on his face. 'Come here child. Stand in the m[iddle]'"

WHAT THIS SETTLES FOR THE PROJECT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. OPEN_DECISIONS #1 IS ANSWERED BY CANON. Glorybound City is named in the novel's FIRST
   LINE, on the eastern coast of the Sun Moon Federation, bordering the ocean. Red Mountain
   Academy is in it. The existing Soul_Land_3_Project's "Eastsea" is a different AU.
   → RULING: Glorybound City. Receipted, not inferred.

2. THE FAR SPINE IS CANON, NOT INVENTED. The Spirit Pagoda personally sends the Spirit
   Master who performs every child's awakening. The man who sees the golden lines on
   Wulin's forehead IS a Spirit Pagoda man, standing in a school building, on the annual
   day the whole city's children are measured and recorded. The report needs no invention.

3. SOUL POWER IS ONE IN A THOUSAND. This reframes rank 1: Lan Shen is not a failure for
   having soul power — he is a miracle who happens to be the smallest miracle possible.
   His family will be overjoyed. That joy is the emotional engine of chapter one.

4. CHILDREN ARE AWAKENED ALONE. Canon ch2 shows Wulin alone in the chamber with the Spirit
   Master; parents wait outside the circular building. → Lan Shen CANNOT witness the golden
   lines directly. He can only witness the aftermath. This is stronger and it is canon-safe.

5. THE TEACHER IS UNNAMED AND APATHETIC. Carrying a list. Do not invent a name for him
   without labelling it AU.

6. TANG ZIRAN'S OWN AWAKENING. "Decades ago, he had the same hopes" — and disappointment
   and frustration in his eyes. Tang Ziran is a man whose own number did not save him.
   Free, canon-grounded depth for the neighbour father.
