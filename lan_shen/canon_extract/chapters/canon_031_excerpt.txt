1. THE PAIR, DESCRIBED
=====================================================================

Grey exterior — at first glance **ordinary forging hammers**. Closer: **dark wave-like
patterns cover the whole body**; the patterns on the handles seem to nurture an endless
strength. A **spiral pattern twists along each handle to a sharp point** at its end.
He spent **three whole days** forging them, little by little — and felt in his blood he
was doing **half the work for twice the results** even so.

=====================================================================

- Left hammer: **152 kilograms**. Right hammer: **166 kilograms.** Appearing small;

  pair. The **blood connection makes them feel lighter**; with soul power added, his
  grip becomes exceptionally steady.
- Canon self-awareness: his strength-growth is not small, yet not enough alone — the

3. THE TEST — ONE TAP, THREE NOTES
=====================================================================

**_ding ding ding!_ THREE brittle notes for a single tap.** Mang Tian's pupils contract.
Again: three-for-one. On Mang Tian's "Use a heavy strike!": right hand trails an arc —
**_DANG DANG DANG!_** and this time it is VISIBLE: 🔴 **two blurred phantom images of
the hammer appear behind the real one, producing the extra sounds.**

- The luck ledger (canon's own summary): bad luck in martial soul and spirit soul;
