1. WHY HE FUSES A DEFECTIVE SPIRIT SOUL — THE REASONING IS THE SCENE
=====================================================================

He cries on Na'er's shoulder first. "Crying really was the best way to vent one's feelings."

    "Na'er, am I weak?" Tang Wulin asked after he raised his head and wiped away his tears.
    Na'er shook her head vigorously.

He sends her out of the room before he does it:

    "You can go first, Na'er. I want to fuse with this spirit soul."
    Na'er hesitated and said, "But, this one isn't any good?"

His reason is money and family, not resignation, and it is stated in full:

    "It's already difficult for mom and dad to provide for us, so I can't increase their burden.
    Dad said he would help me earn the money to buy another one just now, but our family doesn't
    have much money to begin with. I can't let mom and dad carry another burden for my sake. Even
    if it's not too good, it's still a spirit soul. It'll still let me become an official Soul
    Master. Also, dad won't have to face any more difficulties after I fuse with it."

    "Big brother..."

    "I'm fine. It was only a dream of mine anyways. Maybe becoming a master forger is most
    suitable for me after all."

🔴 THE LOAD-BEARING SENTENCE FOR THIS SERIAL — he names the forger path himself, in the same
breath as giving up the Soul Master dream:

    "At this moment, Tang Wulin's profound smile was engraved within her heart. It was the kind of
    optimistic smile of relief and helplessness which masked the depth of his sorrows."

    He knew that after fusing with this spirit soul, he would lose any chance of achieving his
    dream of becoming a powerful Soul Master. But what could he do?
