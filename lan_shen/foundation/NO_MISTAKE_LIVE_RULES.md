## 2. PROTAGONIST — and explicitly what he is NOT

**Lan Shen.** Six years old at chapter one. A reincarnator.

**What he IS:**
- Reborn with the memories of a young man from our world who had read/watched the whole
  franchise. That past life is the author's own — handled as a character, not a biography.
- **Full meta-knowledge:** SL1, SL2, SL2.5, SL3 (mostly), SL3.5, SL5, and roughly half of
  SL4, plus "all the important things." Bounded by Lock 6 and `codex/KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALLS.md`.
- **High spiritual power** — an adult soul in a child's brain. This is his one real gift,
  and it is **expensive**: the body cannot carry it. Cost is written in Lock 5.
- **Six years of deliberate body training** before awakening, from the first year he could
  walk. He is physically far ahead of any six-year-old in Glorybound City.
- Martial soul: **Bluesilver Grass — ordinary.** No gold at the root. No lineage.
- **Innate soul power: rank 1.** The floor. *Below* canon Wulin's rank 3 (receipt R1).

**What he is NOT — only what the author actually ruled:**
- ❌ **No adaptation talent.** This is the author's explicit ruling and the one deliberate
  divergence from the Lin Hao serial. *"no adaption telent"* — author, 2026-09-19.

**Ruled by the author, 2026-09-19 (character, not power):** imaginative in both lives ·
**interest-driven, not hard-working** — he does what he is interested in, and for those things
the word "hard work" barely applies · **playful and good company with boys his own age** ·
🔴 **uncomfortable with girls his own age** (a standing trait, not a flaw to be cured) · loves
growth · genuinely loves tasty food · **cooks**, in both lives · **staff now, spear later** ·
plans a **full life path**, six tracks, not a min-max. All detailed in
`codex/CHARACTERS.md` and `foundation/THE_PLAN.md`.

**Everything else is open.** An earlier version of this lock also barred a bloodline, a
system, a second martial soul, divine tier and being "chosen." The author never ruled any of
those, and they were removed on 2026-09-19. Lock 5 now governs power as a **baseline, not a
ceiling**; Lock 7 governs **conduct**, which is the agent's only legitimate constraint.

If a future session wants to add a "what he is NOT" line, it must quote an author ruling.

**He cannot say what he knows.** Every advantage he has is also the one thing that, if
revealed, destroys him — an adult soul in a child is exactly the kind of anomaly the far
spine hunts. So he must win with rank 1 soul power, an ordinary grass, and a mouth he cannot
open. That is the engine.

## 5. POWER — BASELINE, NOT CEILING

🔴 **REWRITTEN 2026-09-19 at the author's direction.** The first version of this lock
hard-capped him at rank 91–95, barred godhood, forbade any upgrade before Book Four, and
declared the grass permanently ordinary. The author's ruling: *"I never said or select… I
said everything go naturally."*

The author is right, and the caps also contradicted the kit's own **Unfixed Law** — *"for AT
holders nothing is fixed; traits are dated states; power is a **baseline**, never a
ceiling."* Every hard cap is removed. What remains is where he **starts** and how anything
he gains must be **paid for**. Neither is a limit on how far he goes.

### The baseline (Year 0 — this is all that is fixed)

| Axis | Starting state | Fixed? |
|---|---|---|

| **Martial soul** | **Bluesilver Grass, ordinary** — no gold at the root (contrast Wulin, R2) | ❌ No. Nothing is declared permanent. |
| **Spiritual power** | high for a child; unmeasured until the academy instrument (canon ch17 era, age nine). 🔴 **This is the real edge** — canon gates soul-spirit grade on spiritual power, so it is the one door rank-one soul power does not close. See `foundation/THE_PLAN.md` Track 1 | ❌ No |
| **Body** | six years of deliberate pre-training; still a child's body | ❌ No |
| **Rings** | none | ❌ No |

### The method (these are rules about *how*, not *how much*)

1. **Earned on the page.** No limit on how high he climbs — but no gain the reader did not
   watch him pay for. This is the only real constraint.
2. **Nothing is fixed.** Every number is a dated state, not a destiny. If the grass changes,
   it changes because a cause was shown, not because a plot needed it.
3. **Canon mechanics still govern the world** — ring colours by beast age, the rank bands,
   ten rings maximum. Those are the physics of Douluo, not a cap on Lan Shen.
4. **The body pays for the soul.** Spiritual power beyond what a child's body can carry
   costs something visible. A cost rule, not a ceiling.

## 6. IDENTITY

- **Public name:** Lan Shen, child of Glorybound City, rank 1, Bluesilver Grass.
- **True self:** an adult who remembers another world. **Never spoken aloud. Not once.**
- **What the reveal costs:** everything. The far spine hunts exactly this. If it is ever
  revealed, it is a catastrophe he survives, not a triumph — and it requires an author
  ruling written into this file before the chapter is drafted.
- **Rule:** he may act on knowledge. He may never *explain* knowledge. Every time he is
  right about something he should not know, someone must notice. That noticing is tracked in
  `codex/KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALLS.md`.

## 7. ABSOLUTES — what he will never do

1. **Never kill for a soul ring.** Rings come from soul beasts; the hunt is canon. But he
   never kills a person, and never kills a sapient or bonded beast for convenience.
2. **Never reveal the reincarnation** without a written author ruling (Lock 6).
3. **Never reroute a canon character's fate** without logging the butterfly cause and cost in
   `CANON_LEDGER.md` first.
4. **Never let power drop to protect an outline.** If a plan fails, it fails forward.
5. **Never use meta-knowledge as a cheat that skips a scene.** Knowledge tells him *where to
   look*; the work still has to be shown on the page.
6. **No fourth wall.** No "in canon", no "I know how this ends", not even in internal
   monologue phrased as a reader. He experiences it as memory, not as fiction.

## 8. CANON IMMUNITY — whose fate may not be rerouted

**Immutable (locked):**

- **Gu Yue's nature and her six elements** (fire, water, earth, wind, light, space — **metal

**Rule:** canon silence is freedom, not a trap. But every divergence gets a cause and a line
in `CANON_LEDGER.md`.

## 🔒 LOCK 11 — THE HOUSE SPELLING (added 2026-09-19, after a full-project audit)

**"Bluesilver Grass" is ONE WORD.** Canon's own text, verbatim
(`canon_extract/chapters/canon_002_excerpt.txt` §5):

> *"Bluesilver Grass was so common that it covered the whole Douluo Continent as well as the
> Star Luo Continent and Sky Dou Continent. Even the lawn of Red Mountain Academy was covered
> in Bluesilver Grass."*

This project therefore writes **Bluesilver Grass**, **Bluesilver Emperor**, and all skill
names in that form. It does NOT write "Blue Silver Grass".

Two exceptions, and only these:
- **Verbatim quotations** from a secondary source that spells it as two words stay exactly as
  the source has them. A receipt is a receipt; editing a quote is a canon-integrity failure.
- The `blue_silver/` project name and any path or filename, which are already locked.

**Audit finding this replaces:** 34 instances of the two-word spelling across 10 project files,
corrected in one pass. `tools/style_gate.py` now fails a chapter containing it.
