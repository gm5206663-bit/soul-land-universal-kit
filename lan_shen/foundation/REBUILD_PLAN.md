## 🔴🔴 THE CANON-PARALLEL DOCTRINE (author ruling 2026-09-23 — supersedes the first V2 ch1)

The author's own binding words: *"you just creating everything, there is no canon …
preserve canon completely while do things for oc in parallel — there is no natural
butterfly without it."* Golden_lion's card is the citation: **"Canon shown, never
skipped; the OC added, never centered."** The FIRST V2 ch1 violated this — canon
re-voiced into crowd-noise, lore planted early (the queue-speech ladder), OC family
featues centered. That draft was rebuilt the same day.

**THE LAW, four clauses:**

1. **CANON RUNS ON THE PAGE in its own POV track, complete and unskipped.** Wulin's
   scenes are staged in his close-third, faithful to the receipts — beats, numbers,
   dialogue intent, emotional turns. Our own prose, always (the re-voice law governs
   words, never facts). Canon scenes are cut for length only, never for convenience.
2. **THE OC TRACK RUNS PARALLEL, same clock, same streets.** OC domestic/economic life is
   legal and wanted. What is NOT legal: OC lore-planting (canon facts arriving early or
   altered *through* the OC), OC rescuing/carrying canon scenes, OC absorbing canon
   emotional beats meant for canon characters.
3. **A BUTTERFLY IS CONTACT-MADE, NEVER CHOICE-MADE.** Log a butterfly row ONLY when the
   two tracks meet — shared room, traded words, a changed mind with a scene cause. The
   premise (Lan Shen exists, grass, rank one) is declared once at ch1 and is NOT a
   butterfly. Everything downstream of contact gets a receipt; everything else is canon,
   marked HELD/staged.
4. **FIREWALLS APPLY PER TRACK.** The reader may see whatever canon shows (the golden
   lines exist on the page for the reader). Lan Shen's track may never touch it. A canon
   scene staged in Wulin's POV is canon reaching the reader, not knowledge reaching Lan
   Shen — the two layers are kept separate forever.
5. 🟢 **THE NATURALNESS CLAUSE (author ruling 2026-10-10. Binding. The four clauses above
   are read through this one.)** The author's own binding words: *"parallel when needed —
   not it's mean you force it; everything natural accordingly to everything; you can't
   force anything, completely not anything. You should have enough logical and canon and
   common sense."*
   **PARALLEL IS A CAPACITY, NOT A QUOTA.** The OC track enters a beat only when the beat
   calls for him and logic, canon, and common sense approve the entry. If a canon scene
   needs no OC presence, none is manufactured: canon runs alone. Some chapters the tracks
   sit close (Awakening Day, the school day, the silver find — real contact, real lanes).
   Some chapters the OC lives his domestic line while canon moves in the next street.
   Forcing presence, forcing coincidence, forcing contact is the same error class as the
   first draft's OC-centeredness — it is scene-planting under a friendlier name and it is
   banned.
   **TEST ON THE BOARD, BEFORE DRAFTING (the prewrite gate):** every planned OC beat must
   answer, in one line each — (a) logic: does the world that stands already make this
   happen by itself, (b) canon: does the receipt tolerate it on this date, (c) common
   sense: would a sane reader nod rather than wince. Any answer NO → the beat is dropped,
   no exception. CANON still runs complete and unskipped (clause 1 is untouchable); the
   amendment governs the OC's footprint only.
