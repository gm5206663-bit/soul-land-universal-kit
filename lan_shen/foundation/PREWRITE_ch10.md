## 1. CANON BEAT

**Canon 012, held as a receipt:**

> *"In the second year that Na'er stayed with the Tang family, she had attended the Awakening
> Ceremony and brought about a marvel. This marvel wasn't that her martial soul was exceedingly
> powerful. Instead, **she didn't possess any martial soul at all.** This was something that
> was rarely seen throughout the history of the continent."*

Calendar anchor, canon 011: *"Next year's Awakening Day, Na'er will also awaken her martial
soul."* Na'er arrived in Year 0 at five and a half (canon 006's receipt); "the second year she
stayed with the Tang family" is **Year 1**. ✅ Confirmed by `CANON_NOTES.md` FINDING 2.

**How it stays true:** Na'er attends the Awakening Ceremony in Year 1 and is recorded as
possessing no martial soul at all. The record is rare, and it is noticed.

🔴 **What must NOT happen:** canon 014's seven-coloured ring (yellow, green, red, blue, purple,
gold, silver) is **Year 3, and nobody is in the room.** Firewall 4. He must never see it. This
chapter may not hint at it beyond what ch3 already staged visually — the mist behind the pupil,
which he saw and does not understand.

## 2. THREAT — must be a **person** (Face Law, #26a)

🔴 **Yan Song.** Register-keeper and director of the Soul Master class at Red Mountain
Academy. Forty-ish, thin, ink on one cuff, the flat pleasant expression of a man who has been
saying the same sentences since before sunrise.

**He wants the register clean.** He never raises his voice in Book One. Fifteen children
awakened soul power in Glorybound City in Year 0 and fifteen is the number he reports upward;
a rank-1 Bluesilver Grass in that fifteen makes his numbers look like padding.

Now he must write a **blank** — the one entry that cannot be padded, explained, or sent
upward as a good number. *"Rarely seen throughout the history of the continent"* is a
sentence that arrives in a small city's register once, and it arrives on his desk.

And standing in the same yard is the boy who read the seventh line of his own notice aloud to
him a year ago, and whose family's measure the bursar's office now specifies **in writing**.

## 3. FACE — named, with at least three spoken lines

- **Yan Song** — the register, the column, the blank. Reasonable, which is worse than cruel.
- **Qin Wu** — fourth-rank Spirit Master, has awakened thousands and tens of thousands of
  children, filled an anomaly form twice in twenty-three years. He conducts the ceremony.
  Carries the **Other POV** register.
- **Na'er** — six and a half. Speaks. In ch3 she said *"En."* and *"Five and a half."* She is
  not a prop and this chapter must not make her one.
- **Wulin** — seven. He is her brother by choice and he does not yet know what has happened.
- **Lang Yue / Tang Ziran** — the parents who asked her, in ch3, whether she wanted to stay.

## 4. MOVEMENT — a **number must move** (Number Law, #26b)

| Number | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴🔴 **Na'er's record** | a child awaiting a martial soul | **blank. No martial soul, no soul power, no rank.** A zero where the register expects a value |

**Rank does not move and cannot.** Rank ten requires a ring, a ring requires a kill, and he is
seven. `#15` binds. **The number that moves is Na'er's, and it moves to nothing** — which is
the point: this serial has spent a year on a boy hiding a number, and now hands him a girl
who was never given one.

## 5. BUTTERFLY — what does his existence change about canon? 🔴 NEW, answered pre-draft

In canon, Na'er's blank is reported in retrospect by a narrator following Wulin. Nobody in
Glorybound City has any reason to connect it to anything else, because nothing else in that
city is unusual.

**In this serial there is a second unusual thing in that city, and it is one year old, and
Yan Song knows its name.**

```
trigger              a child with no martial soul, written into his register
immediate effect     the register is not clean, and cannot be made clean
secondary effect     Yan Song has a frame for "impossible measurement" he did not have in
                     canon: the Lan family's measure, which his own bursar specified in
                     writing, and a boy who read the seventh line of a notice at six
character reaction   he does not accuse. He is not that kind of man. He LOOKS — and being
                     looked at by Yan Song is the first step toward being looked at by the
                     thing above Yan Song
institutional        Qin Wu files a third anomaly form. Two are in a drawer. Three is a
reaction             pattern, and patterns get read
long-term            🔴 the drawer is the far spine. A report about too much (Wulin's gold,
consequence          ch1) and a report about too little (Na'er's blank, ch10) now sit in
                     the same drawer, in the same small city, in the same two years
```

`03_STORY_LAW.md` §4: *"A rule that is never broken is not a butterfly — it changes nothing
downstream."* This one propagates. **And it propagates wrongly**: Yan Song will connect two
things that are not connected. Lan Shen's concealment has been so successful that the only
explanation available to a reasonable man with a register is the one that points at the family
that has been supplying his academy.

🔴 **The sense reading nothing is load-bearing and safe:** Na'er has no martial soul, so there
is nothing lit to feel. It does **not** pre-empt canon 014's seven colours, which are Year 3
and unseen by anyone. It does put Lan Shen in the position of holding **three records that
disagree** — the register says nothing, his sense says nothing, and his eyes saw mist in ch3.

## 7. TURN — what is true at the end that was not true at the start

**At the start:** Lan Shen has spent a year being unremarkable, and it has worked. The academy
holds his family's measure as a specification **for jars**. Nobody at Red Mountain has any
reason to look at him. Na'er is a girl two doors down who is going to awaken.

**At the end:**

- 🔴 **A girl has been written into a register as nothing**, and the man who wrote it has
  looked up.
- 🔴 **The gauge is ceremonial.** His stick has reached a fourth office, and the fourth one is
  a room where children are measured.
- 🔴 **Yan Song has started looking at him instead of through him** — which `CHARACTERS.md`
  names as the Book One climax arriving early, because being noticed by Yan Song is the first
  step toward being noticed by the thing above Yan Song.
- 🔴🔴 **TRACK 8 FIRES.** That night he tells Na'er a story **out loud**. Not written. No
  manuscript before Year 2, and this is not a manuscript. And he obeys the governing rule:
  **he may write anything except the truth** — so what he tells a girl who has just been
  recorded as empty is a story about a thing that looks like nothing and is not.
- 🔴 **"There is one thing. I am not going to do it yet."** Still unspecified. Do not specify.
