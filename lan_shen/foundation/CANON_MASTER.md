## 3. THE HELD CANON, CHAPTER BY CHAPTER

### 001 · "Awakening Day" (partial hold + 2026-09-23 addendum)
- **First line of the novel:** Glorybound City is a small town on the border between the
  ocean and the eastern coast of the **Sun Moon Federation**. Annual **Awakening Day**.
- **The law:** everyone has a **martial soul**, awakening at **age six**; 🔴 **only one in a
  thousand** has **soul power**; rank = future; Soul Master = noblest profession for 100,000 years.
- Tang Wulin: barely six; short black hair; slightly taller than peers; large clear black
  eyes, long eyelashes; commoner's clothing; outstanding appearance. Tang Ziran: medium
  build, average everything; *"Regardless of whether or not you have soul power, dad will
  always love you."* — and an internal gloomy sigh: 🔴 *"Decades ago, he had the same hopes."*
- The call: an **unnamed, apathetic teacher** with a list (do not name him without AU tag).
- **Red Mountain Academy:** elementary comprehensive, 2,000 capacity; white roofs, red
  walls; exceptionally clean.
- 🔴 **The Spirit Pagoda is in the room from page one:** every elementary academy has a
  circular **Awakening Chamber**; before the ceremony the academy **invites a Spirit Master
  from the Spirit Pagoda**. Seven floors; Wulin to the **third**. Inscriptions on floors,
  walls, ceiling. The Spirit Master: middle-aged, **simple orange robe, embroidered soul
  beast**. **Parents wait outside.**
- Addendum (novelhall/wuxiaworld mirrors): the eye-martial-soul joke (*"your eyes are so
  pretty, it would be just perfect if you got a martial soul related to your eyes"* —
  canon-sequenced cruelty before "Bluesilver Grass").
