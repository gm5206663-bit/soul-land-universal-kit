## 2. THREAT — a person (Face Law, #26a)

🔴🔴 **OLD PEI — NEW. AU-ORIGINAL. THE CARRY TRADE GIVEN A SPINE.**

The man who comes from out of the city with a case, twice a year — named in ch13 as
infrastructure, given a face here. He is not kind and he is not cruel. He is **empty
capacity with opinions**. His spine is bent from the case. What he wants is not to help a
boy; what he wants is for the case to come home with something in it, because it comes home
empty either way and empty is a waste of a road.

His question — the Zhou-form question, different register:

> **"What do you have that is finished."**

Not *what are you writing*. **Finished** is the load-bearing word, and it detonates the
whole chapter: the Na'er story is finished and is not a product; the story the market might
buy is a product and is not finished. 🔴 **He comes before the frost; the frost comes
whether the book exists or not.** The deadline is the villain. Pei is only its voice.

🔴 **Do not give him a backstory.** (The Zhao rule.) He gets a spine bent one way, a case,
a trader's arithmetic, and one truism that is also the chapter's LAW panel line: *prices
are the one thing nobody charges you for; asking is free.*
