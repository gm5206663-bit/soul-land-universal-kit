## 0.5 🔴 SENTENCE-LEVEL DNA — measured 2026-09-20 against the author's own prose

**Why this section exists.** §0 measures *shape* — dialogue share, sentence length, chain rate.
It does not measure *voice*. The audit of 2026-09-20 measured the sentence-level constructions
that actually make the house sound like the house, against **`uploads/chapter_75.md`** — the
SL1 顾渊 chapter 「二龙」, which the author's own `REFERENCE_MAP.md` names as the
*"quality bar: voice, full-play, consequence prose."* **This is not SL3's ch75.** Different
serial, different file, and §0's row for "SL3 Lin Hao ch75" is a separate measurement.

Per 1,000 words of prose body (panels, headers, tables and footers excluded):


### What the numbers say

🔴 **1. THE TREND IS REAL AND IT IS UPWARD.** Negation density runs 5.6 → 11.7 from ch1 to
ch13, and `a X, not a Y` crosses his bar at ch13. The style work has been compounding, not
stalling. **Do not "fix" ch1–4 up to ch13's profile retroactively** — the drift is the record
of the learning, and rewriting shipped chapters to a later voice is the rebuild trap that cost
`blue_silver` 90,000 words. Apply the targets **forward**.

🔴🔴 **2. THE remembered/forgot CONSTRUCTION IS ALMOST ENTIRELY ABSENT FROM THIS SERIAL.**
0.7 per 1k at ch1, and **0.0 at ch13** — against 3.8 in his bar. This is one of his
signatures, and it does a specific job: **it puts memory and attention into bodies and objects
instead of into the POV character's head**, which is how he gets interiority without interior
monologue.

> *"Oscar's stomach remembered it had a job."*
> *"The wood under Yin Shu's boots remembered a different weight."*
> *"Yin Shu's clipboard forgot what it was for."*
> *"The can forgot the flowers when she saw the two men in the path."*
> *"Dashi's feet forgot they had a job."*
> *"Mubai's shoulders remembered a Title's glance from yesterday and did not like slaughter
>   sitting in a garden."*
> *"Flender's mouth found something that had not been there when a prince had said lousy."*

The grammar is: **[body part or object] + [remembered / forgot / found] + [the thing it is
still holding].** It is used for *reaction*, never for exposition. **Target: 2–4 per 1,000
words, forward from ch14.**

🔴 **3. NOUN-PHRASE FRAGMENTS ARE ALSO ABSENT** (0.3 → 0.0, against 5.1). His bar uses them as
a flat filing rhythm, usually inside the POV character's scan of a room:

> *"Pockets. AT silent."* · *"Fire. Dragon. Not Blue Lightning. Fire."* · *"Cheap mask."*
> *"Not a drum. A field."* · *"Oscar's box was a box."*

**Target: at least one such cluster per chapter, in the protagonist's scanning beat.** Note the
constraint — this serial's Language Law still forbids digits in prose, so the cluster carries
no numbers.

🔴 **4. HE WRITES INTERROGATIVES WITH A FULL STOP.** Zero question marks in 2,373 words:
*"Are you going to invite us in, or are we applying in the hydrangeas."* · *"Does my house fit
this many. What happened to you. When did you fall to this."* · *"Can you bear it."* · *"Why."*
Ch13 is already at zero and ch1 at four. **Target: zero. A question mark in this house is a
shout, and the house does not shout.**

✅ **5. TWO THINGS ARE ALREADY RIGHT.** Exclamation marks: zero in both, against zero in his bar
(the four in ch3 were canon transcription and were removed with it — see LOCK 16). Plain
`said` as the default speech tag: 12.6–13.3 against 14.3. **His tags are boring on purpose;
the emotion is carried by the action beat around them, never by the verb.**

⚠️ **6. EM-DASHES: his bar uses 1.3 per 1k, `blue_silver` uses 7.7–11.6, this serial sits at
0.4–5.4.** There is no gate here and there should not be one — but a chapter above ~5 is a
chapter leaning on the dash instead of the sentence.

## 1. 🔴 THE VOICE LAW (SL3 CODEX, locked v3.10)

The author's own correction, verbatim, which produced it:

> *"the writeing style of your is very bad you should write like you write Frist three chapters"*

**Chapters 1–3 are the voice. Everything is measured against them.** Not "the early chapters
are charming and we grew out of it" — they are the standard, and drift away from them is the
single largest craft failure the author has ever paid for. The drift was measured:

| | ch1–3 | ch59–62 |
|---|---|---|
| *"I would like you to notice"* | **0** | **43** |
| *", and that"* chains | **10** / 10,516w | **117** / 11,561w |
| record-keeping meta-voice | **0** | **26** |
| dialogue share | **25–35%** | **13–26%** |
| median sentence | **9–11 words** | **13–20** |

> *"The narration stopped being a story and became a record of a story: a first-person journal
> in which the protagonist summarises his own bookkeeping to the reader, in which every scene
> exists to produce a number, and in which nobody talks."*

### The six rules

1. **Third person, close.** He does not narrate to a reader. He is not writing a memoir.
2. **Scene and dialogue carry it.** If two people are in a room, they talk.
3. **No journal tics.** Forbidden outright — `tools/style_gate.py` fails a chapter containing
   any of them: *"I would like you to notice"* · *"on the record"* · *"this is also a fact"* ·
   *"I have stopped needing to know"* · *"I have written that sentence N times."*
4. **His inner life is SHOWN.** A decision. A physical beat. A line of dialogue. Something he
   does with his hands. No summary. No numbers narrated at the reader.
5. **Sentences vary, and short ones do the heavy lifting.** Median 9–11 words.
6. **Bold in prose is for a term, not for emphasis.**

### What the voice bar does that drift abandons

- **Wit.** *"That was a good spoon." / "It was a spoon, Wulin."*
- **Concrete nouns.** Forty-one windows. A worn heel. Two-across, one-along bricks.
- **Aphorism that earns its place** — one, rarely two, never a speech.
- **Other people existing.** Lin Mei cooking for nine when told there would be four.
- **Warmth.** *"The book is funny and it is kind, and the drift made it neither."*

## 2. 🔴 THE SPECTATOR TEST

> **The protagonist is never a watcher.**

From `SL3_FOUNDATION_LESSONS.md`: *"The OC is never a watcher across a whole chapter. Even when
canon owns the scene, Lin Hao acts, chooses, negotiates, withholds. His 'observer' moments are
half-beats, never a chapter posture."*

The first draft of Lan Shen's chapter one failed this outright. He watched Wulin's awakening
from a queue, watched his mother, watched a Spirit Master, and made a private vow. That is a
watcher chapter, and watcher chapters do not ship.

**The rebuilt chapter gives him five acts, all his own:** he reads a notice pinned to a table,
he negotiates a fee, he changes a line in a register, he asks a question nobody has ever asked,
and he proposes an experiment at a dinner table in front of witnesses.

Machine-enforced: `style_gate.py` counts the protagonist's attributed spoken turns and **fails
below four**.

## 3. THE DIALOGUE DIAL (STYLE_GOLD)

> *"Choose the chapter's dialogue load like a camera chooses its lens."*

```
lesson    44–66%   a chapter that teaches: the price ladder, a law, a mechanism
standard  13–30%   the default; a street day, a family, a team
hush       8–15%   grief, absorption, a miracle, deep time
```

SL3's lifetime average is 24.5% across 92 chapters, band 5.5–66.6%. **Every chapter declares
its dial in the panel** — `DIALOGUE : standard 13-30%` — because an undeclared chapter cannot
be audited. `style_gate.py` warns on an undeclared dial and fails a chapter below its floor.

⚠️ The dial is a floor for hush chapters too. A **human** protagonist in a crowd may not run
at 2.7%. That number belongs to a plant with no mouth.

## 4. APPARATUS LAW (failure mode five — revoked)

The author lost a 90,000-word serial to this and rebuilt 33,000 words to fix it.

```
PANEL  — slim, at the top, 8 fields maximum:
         STATUS / KIND / AGE / CLASS / THREAT / BOND / LOSS / LAW  (+ DIALOGUE, TURN)
FOOTER — NONE. Never. Not one line.
◆      — exactly once per chapter. A book-end card belongs to a BOOK's last chapter only.
```

Measured across `blue_silver/chapters_rebuilt/`: chapters 1–14 carry **one** `◆`. Chapter 15,
the last of the book, carries **two** — the panel and the book-end card.

> *"A footer in the chapter file is apparatus outgrowing story. It is failure mode five and it
> is revoked."* · *"Bookkeeping lives in the kit, in `SERIAL_LOG.md`, where a reader never sees it."*

The first draft of chapter one shipped a **fifty-line footer** carrying CANON TOUCHED,
BUTTERFLY, PANELS, FORBIDDEN STILL, NUMBERS and REGISTERS USED. `verify.py` passed it, because
`verify.py` permits one card and does not require zero. **All of that content now lives in
`foundation/SERIAL_LOG.md` and `foundation/CANON_LEDGER.md`, which is where the law puts it.**

⚠️ Note the history: the SL3 project's chapters *do* carry large footers, and
`SL3_FOUNDATION_LESSONS.md` §3 documents them leaking end-of-serial state into chapter one
(the REALM GAP LAW said Lin Hao was a Soul Elder with three purple rings at age six). The kit
is the later, distilled doctrine and it revokes the footer. **The kit wins.**

## 5. STYLE_GOLD — the twelve patterns

> *"One or two patterns breathing per chapter beats a checklist stamped whole."*

The gold is **consulted, not decorated with**. Do not stamp all twelve. Pick one or two and
let them breathe.

| # | Pattern | Deployment rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Question-Title** | the title is the chapter's one-sentence answer, unknown until the end |
| 2 | **Arithmetic as Soul** | emotion becomes cost, cost becomes grammar. Never two price-clauses in a row |
| 3 | **The Grain-Read** | the talent's voice without a voice. Extend, never repeat — each extension reads a NEW class of thing |
| 4 | **The Drawer** | a motif with a life; once per 8–12 chapters, evolved one step |
| 5 | **The First Hand In** | brother grammar, never announced, always positional. *The love is in logistics* |
| 6 | **The Hall That Hears Everything** | institutions personified as quiet listeners; vary the institution |
| 7 | **The Perspective Doctrine Panel** | the legend accumulates in outside voices, never in his |
| 8 | **Cliff Rhythm** | alternate long and short; after a long-lyrical cliff take a short one; end on process when the threat is offstage |
| 9 | **The Callback-Object Economy** | every arc spends one old object and banks one new one |
| 10 | **The Not-Irony Tag** | deadpan measurement humour, one per scene maximum, and it belongs to the WATCHER |
| 11 | **The Refrain, Then the Break** | one refrain live at a time; the break must cost something on-page |
| 12 | **The Ongoing Image** | when the feeling is too large for any character, give it to the setting |

### Used in chapter one (two, deliberately)

- **#2 Arithmetic as Soul** — *"Four hundred and eleven people. Sixty-two of them children.
  Fifteen of those children were the right age, which meant roughly one of them, statistically,
  would come out with soul power."* And the price-clause: *"the honest version is cheaper for
  everybody."*
- **#5 The First Hand In** — *"Wulin's hand was on his sleeve and then it was on his hand, and
  it was the first hand in, because it always was."* Positional. Not announced.
- **#12 The Ongoing Image** carries the cliff — *"The water in the cups was still moving."*

### Banked for later, not yet used

#4 the drawer · #6 the hall that hears · #7 the perspective panel · #9 the callback object
(the two cups are **banked** in ch1 and must be **spent** later) · #10 the not-irony tag ·
#11 the refrain.

## 6. THE TEN CRAFT LAWS (`SL3_FOUNDATION_LESSONS.md` §2)

1. The OC is never a watcher across a whole chapter → **§2**
2. **Talent is embodied, never named.** No system voice. No narrator explaining "his gift means…"
3. **World rules arrive as priced experience** — each delivered through a character who *pays*
   for the knowledge. Not stated. Costed.
4. **Choice > trait.** Every chapter gives him a decision with a consequence and a stated butterfly.
5. **Secondary cast have real interior.** A character who appears for one chapter may still get
   an origin. Even the surveyor has a personality.
6. **Withholding is a tool.** Name the big thing and hold it back so its first use matters.
7. **Friendship is mutual and equal.** 🔴 *The canon MC is not worshipped at a distance.*
8. **Theme is earned in scenes, not declared.** Nobody delivers the thesis in a speech.
9. **Chapters end in shared warmth or forward motion, not solitude.**
10. **Bookkeeping stays tight** — and now stays *out of the chapter file entirely*, per §4.

## 7. 🔴 THE PARROTING WOUND

From `UNDERSTANDING.md` §2, the author's own list of recurring wounds:

> *"You just do what I say; you can't understand meaning of others."* Agents repeatedly
> **parroted the author's own explanations back**, or used skill names as stickers instead of
> showing what the thing means.

This is the deepest one and it is not a prose problem. It shows up as:

- echoing a ruling back at the author with receipts attached, instead of building with it
- naming a power instead of staging what the power *does to a room*
- presenting files instead of talking
- letting a status document lag the story, so the story becomes opaque to its own author

**The discipline:** understand the *thing*, not the words. When the author rules something,
the ruling goes into the codex once and then stops being discussed. What ships is prose.

Also from the same file, on quality: the author rejected *"thin hinges, copy-machine rooms,
kit-recap-as-story, skill-name stickers, audit-as-story, robot chapters."*

## 8. THE PER-CHAPTER CONTRACT (check BEFORE drafting)

```
1. CANON BEAT   — what canon does this touch, and how does it stay true?
2. THREAT       — what can hurt someone here? Not "tension" — a thing.
3. FACE         — who opposes, named, with dialogue and a motive?
4. MOVEMENT     — what changes: rank, ring, skill, bond, resource, knowledge?
5. BUTTERFLY    — what does his existence change, visibly, causally?
6. REGISTERS    — ≥3 of: close sense / hard scene / dialogue / other POV / lyrical
7. TURN         — what is true at the end that was not true at the start?
```

Plus, from this file: **declare the dialogue dial**, **pick one or two gold patterns**,
**check the cliff rotation against the last chapter's cliff**, and **set the panel endpoints
before writing any date**.

> *"A chapter that is only interior meditation fails and does not ship. That is a hard rule."*

## §0.7 FIRE PHOENIX CALIBRATION — THE READABILITY CEILING (author ruling 2026-09-21)

The author read the ch15 first draft and ruled it unreadable, pointing at his own Fire
Phoenix serial as the target register: *"why can't you write like my fire phoenix fen
fiction … i don't want big fen fiction, i want to complete."* Measured drift proved him
right: similes/1k ran 5.0 (ch01) → 2.8 (ch10) → **14.7** (ch15 first draft), `which`
17 → **29**, double-simile sentences 1 → **6**, one 59-word sentence with two similes and
three trailing phrases. The §0.5 DNA had FLOORS only — nothing capped the mannerism, and
the drift accumulated chapter by chapter. §0.7 is the ceiling. Floors + ceilings = the band.

**CEILINGS, binding from ch15 on:**

1. **Sentence average ≤ 11 words** (Fire Phoenix runs 9.0–9.5; ch15 shipped 8.5). A
   sentence over 30 words needs a payoff the author would read aloud twice, and there may
   never be two of them in one chapter (ch15 shipped zero).
2. **Similes (like / as if / the way) ≤ 6 per 1,000 words.** One image per paragraph at
   most; never two in one sentence. Keep the best; kill the explanation after it.
3. **"the way" ≤ 6 per chapter.** It is a crutch. Say the thing plain.
4. **Say it once.** No telling what was just shown ("which was a joke, because…"). The
   reader got the joke or the joke failed; a filing note fixes neither.
5. **Short paragraphs: one to three sentences.** One action per line at emotional beats.
6. **Dialogue carries scenes.** Feelings may be said plainly ("something in her chest
   hurt"). Plain is not failure. Plain is the job.
7. The §0.5 floors stay (fragment clusters where they hit, remembered/forgot band,
   zero '?' and '!', said-tags, no digits in prose, no CJK). The gates now measure BOTH
   ends of the band.

Lesson banked: **floors without ceilings measure what I wish I wrote, not what I wrote.**
Forward only — no retroactive rewrite (blue_silver rebuild-trap law, same as §0.5/§0.6).
