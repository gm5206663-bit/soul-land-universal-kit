**Written before drafting**, per `OPEN_DECISIONS.md` #26(d).

## 1. CANON BEAT

Canon supplies **nothing** for Year 1 — canon 012 is *"Three Years Later."* So the beat is not
an event, it is a **precondition**, and it is canon 004 + canon 012:

- **Canon 004:** Lin Ximeng is the Soul Master class homeroom teacher, three years running.
- **Canon 012:** three years on, *he discovered the cause of Tang Wulin's fast cultivation
  speed — this child's spiritual power was far higher than his peers.*

**A man does not discover that out of nowhere.** He discovers it because he has been keeping a
file. Ch11 stages the file being kept, in the year it starts to have content.

🔴 **How it stays true:** Lin Ximeng remains **male** (canon 004's receipt — canon 012 says
"she" four times and the contradiction is held verbatim in `CHARACTERS.md`, ruling MALE). He
does not solve anything. He accumulates. And what he accumulates in this chapter is
**four hours a week of a boy standing still within reach.**

Also held: **canon 017's spiritual power test is Year 3 and Wulin measures 38 at age nine.**
Nobody is measured in ch11. Rank stays one.

## 2. THREAT — a person (Face Law, #26a)

🔴 **Lin Ximeng.** THE DETECTOR. Homeroom teacher, three years, a small black book.

His want is not malice and it is not suspicion. **He wants an explanation.** Ch8 gave him six
months of nothing — *"six months of nothing is a shape"* — and he chose not to fill the gap
because he could not think of anything worth the risk of being wrong about. Now the boy whose
family supplies his academy is standing in his building for four hours a week, washing jars,
carrying crates, standing where a man tells him to stand.

🔴 **He does not raise his voice either.** Two men in this serial now do not raise their voices,
and they are the two most dangerous people in it.

**Secondary pressure, at home: Su Wan.** `CHARACTERS.md`: *"the most dangerous person in Book
One — not because she hunts him, but because she notices things. Do not soften her into
obliviousness."* Her son has just agreed to put his name in a column, and she is the person in
that house who would ask why a boy who has spent a year being unremarkable would volunteer.

## 5. BUTTERFLY — answered pre-draft

In canon there is no Lan Shen, so there is no working studentship, and Lin Ximeng's attention
stays **entirely** on Tang Wulin for three years — which is how canon 012's discovery happens
cleanly.

```
trigger              a boy volunteers to go on the books to save his father a fee
immediate effect     he is inside the academy four hours a week, in the east wing, within
                     reach of the man who has been watching him for three years
secondary effect     Lin Ximeng's file stops being about absence. Six months of nothing was
                     a shape; four hours a week of something is a RECORD
character reaction   Lan Shen discovers, after he has agreed, that the work is in the east
                     wing — and that the thing he bought with four hours a week was not
                     invisibility but proximity
institutional        🔴 TWO ANOMALIES IN ONE SMALL CLASS. Canon 012 has Lin Ximeng explaining
reaction             Wulin's speed. Here he has two boys he cannot explain, in the same room,
                     in the same year, and one of them is on his own books
long-term            the black book becomes the thing that is read later. Qin Wu's drawer has
consequence          forms; Lin Ximeng's book has hours. 🔴 BOTH POINT UP, AND THEY HAVE NOT
                     MET YET
```

`03_STORY_LAW.md` §4: *"If your divergence changes nothing, do not claim it changed the story."*
This one changes canon 012's discovery from a solo investigation into a comparative one — and a
man comparing two unexplained boys will find the difference between them faster than he would
find either alone.

## 7. TURN

**At the start:** the offer is his to refuse and he intends to refuse it, because he has spent
a year not being in a column.

**At the end:** 🔴 **he has said yes — himself, for reasons that are sound — and discovered what
he actually bought.** Four hours a week in the east wing, within reach of the only man in
Glorybound City who has been counting him. His name is in a column with a number of hours
against it. And Lin Ximeng's six-month hole has started to fill.

He is not caught. **He is available.** Those are different, and the chapter must not confuse
them.
