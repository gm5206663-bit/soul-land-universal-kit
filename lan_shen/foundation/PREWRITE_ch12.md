## 1. CANON BEAT

**Canon 012, held:** *"As Lin Ximeng guided Tang Wulin, she discovered that this child had a
gift for meditation... Moreover, his meditative state was exceedingly focused. **Later, she
discovered the cause of Tang Wulin's fast cultivation speed. This child's spiritual power was
far higher than his peers.**"* (Gender held **MALE** per canon 004.)

Canon reports the guidance as an established fact and never shows how it began. **Ch12 stages
the beginning.** And already established in this serial:

- **ch2:** *"In the afternoon they learned to meditate."* And: *"The grass in him did nothing at
  all. At the end of the afternoon he could not have said whether he had felt anything, or
  whether he had decided that he ought to have."*
- 🔴 **ch4:** *"Lin Ximeng walked the rows. He put a hand on a shoulder. He moved a wrist. **He
  spent nine seconds on Wulin — seven more than the first day.** Lan Shen noticed that too,
  wrote it down in his head, and then hated himself a little for writing it down in his head
  when he was supposed to be meditating."*
- **ch1:** the academy grounds are full of Bluesilver Grass — *"came up through the base of the
  stone, along the ditch, in through the gate, and across the whole of the clean grounds
  beyond."* The east-wing classroom is on the **ground floor** with **tall windows**.

🔴 **How it stays true:** Lin Ximeng begins guiding Wulin. He does not discover the cause —
that is canon's "later," and canon 017/018 put the actual measurement at Year 3. **No spiritual
power reading happens in ch12.** What happens is a teacher with a stopwatch and two boys he
cannot explain.

## 2. THREAT — a person (Face Law, #26a)

🔴 **Lin Ximeng, escalating from observer to instrument.**

His want has changed. Ch11 left him with *"two pupils he cannot explain, in the same room, in
the same year, and one of them on his own books."* He has been to the office twice and been
told courteously that there is no procedure.

🔴🔴 **So he does what any honest record-keeper does when he has one unexplained measurement: he
takes a second one.** To explain Wulin he needs a comparison. He needs a **control.** And the
obvious control is the other unexplained boy — the one who is already on his books, four hours a
week, forty feet away, and who cannot be refused without the refusal itself being a fact worth
writing down.

**He never raises his voice. He is not suspicious. He is methodical, which is worse.**

## 5. BUTTERFLY — answered pre-draft

In canon, Lin Ximeng guides **one** boy and eventually explains him. Here he guides **two**,
because a second anomaly exists, and the second one is deliberately bad at the thing the first
is good at.

```
trigger              a teacher needs a control for a measurement he cannot explain
immediate effect     Lan Shen is guided too — closer observation than he has ever been under
secondary effect     🔴 A COMPARISON INVITES A DIFFERENCE. Canon's Lin Ximeng found one fast
                     boy. This one will find a fast boy and a slow boy who is not slow —
                     because the slow boy's numbers will be too consistent
character reaction   Lan Shen must perform mediocrity, on purpose, at the thing he is best at,
                     in a ground-floor room with tall windows above a ditch full of grass
institutional        the black book gets a second column of numbers. Two columns that disagree
reaction             are a shape, and Lin Ximeng draws shapes
long-term            🔴 canon 012's discovery changes kind: not "why is Wulin fast" but "why
consequence          is Wulin fast and Lan Shen exactly, suspiciously, average"
```

`03_STORY_LAW.md` §4: *"A rule that is never broken is not a butterfly."* This one propagates
**and it propagates against him** — being deliberately average for a year produces a flatter
line than any child actually has, and a flat line is the one shape a record-keeper cannot miss.

## 7. TURN

**At the start:** Lin Ximeng watches from the rows. Lan Shen is on the books but unmeasured —
sixteen hours in a column, and nothing else anywhere.

**At the end:** 🔴 **he is being guided.** He has a number in a black book, taken by a man who
is comparing him to Tang Wulin, and he chose it — because refusing would have been more visible
than accepting, and because the arithmetic was sound, and because he is seven going on eight and
he has now spent a year making himself unremarkable so successfully that the only interesting
thing left in Lin Ximeng's classroom is how unremarkable he is.

🔴 **And the flat line.** He lands in the middle, every time, to the second. **That is the
failure disguised as the success**, and the chapter must not notice it. Nobody says so. He
writes it down himself, at the end, in the smaller hand — and then does not understand what he
has written.

## 🔴 DEVIATION FROM THIS BOARD

**This board ruled that Other POV would deliberately NOT be used in ch12**, because ch10 (Qin
Wu) and ch11 (Lin Ximeng) had already used it twice running.

**The Lin Ximeng italic section was written anyway.**

Why: the flat line is the chapter's discovery, and it is only visible from inside the man
keeping it. From Lan Shen's side four minutes looks like success. Only Lin Ximeng can see
fifteen children scatter and one not move. Cutting the section would have cut the chapter's
only dramatic irony and left *"why is this straight"* with nothing to be ironic against.

Legal under `#26(c)`, which sets a floor (once per three) and no ceiling. **But three
consecutive chapters makes it the default, and a default register is not a register.**

🔴 **This produced `OPEN_DECISIONS.md` #28 — a ceiling where #26(c) had only a floor. From
ch13, Other POV runs at most once per three chapters, and the prewrite must state why Lan
Shen's own register cannot carry the discovery. CH13 IS LAN SHEN'S OWN REGISTER. BINDING.**

🔴 **Lesson banked in LOCK 15: a prewrite ruling that gets overridden must be recorded, not
quietly dropped.** A deviation nobody logs becomes a habit nobody chose.

## What the board got right

- **#25 compression.** Eleven scenes, fifteen months, no calendar. The chapter states one date
  in the whole of it — "the winter of the second year" — and nothing needed another. **This is
  now the model for the unspent half-year between here and canon 013.**
- **The stopwatch.** Lin Ximeng escalating from observer to instrument was the correct move and
  it produced the serial's best discovery without moving a single rank.
- **Lan Yue as the #27 instrument.** She got a method, not a scene: the noodle ticket. #27 is
  now a physical object in the house.
- **Not resolving #27.** He brought four substitutes, she rejected four, nobody agonised.

## What the board missed

🔴 **Check (d) found thirteen defects the gates could not, and the board's own day arithmetic
was not detailed enough to prevent them.** The worst four:

1. 🔴 **A unit collision on a loaded motif.** The draft wrote *"The street was eleven"* inside a
   passage measured in minutes. Ch11's eleven is **seconds**. The bare number silently converted
   the motif and destroyed it. **Board fix: any number that carries meaning must carry its
   unit — and the board should list the units in play before drafting, not after.**
2. 🔴 **A knowledge-firewall breach.** Lin Ximeng's interior recalled *"the week his sister
   started work at the harbour."* He has no route to that fact, and the fact was wrong — Lan Yue
   washes dishes on the noodle street (ch5, ch8). **An Other-POV section is a firewall audit.**
3. 🔴 **Class arithmetic.** "Fifteen of them... sixteen the year before... one family gone...
   one new boy" = 16, not 15. And ch11 fixes Year 1 at sixteen, so the fifteen can only exist
   in Year 2 — which moved the measuring, which moved three other things.
4. 🔴 **A gender error.** Jiang Ning is female (ch2). The draft said "he" three times.

🔴 **Seventeen defects total, four from gates, thirteen from the manual read.** Full list in
`SERIAL_LOG.md`.
