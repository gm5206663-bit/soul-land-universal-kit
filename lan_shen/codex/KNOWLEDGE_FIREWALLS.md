# KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALLS.md — what Lan Shen knows, and who has noticed

**This is the most important file in the project.** A reincarnator with full franchise
knowledge is either the most interesting character in the room or the most boring one, and
the only difference is whether the knowledge has a wall around it.

The kit's rule: knowledge is a **firewall**, not a faucet. Every wall has a list of who is
on each side, and every time something crosses, the crossing is logged with its cost.

---

## FIREWALL 0 — THE ABSOLUTE WALL

**What is behind it:** the fact of the reincarnation. That he lived before. That he is an
adult. That another world exists where this one is a story.

**Who is on the other side:** nobody. Not his parents. Not Wulin. Not the reader-facing
narration of any other character's panel.

**Rule:** this never crosses. If it ever must, it requires an author ruling written into
`NO_MISTAKE_LIVE_RULES.md` Lock 6 *before* the chapter is drafted, and it must be a
catastrophe he survives rather than a triumph.

**Narration rule:** he experiences the past life as **memory**, never as fiction. He may not
think "in canon", "in the novel", "the plot", "the author", or "I read this". He thinks
*"I remember"*, *"I know how this went"*, *"this happened before, to him, and I watched it."*
No fourth wall. Ever. (Lock 7.6)

## FIREWALL 1 — WHAT HE KNOWS, PRECISELY

Per the author's ruling: **SL1, SL2, SL2.5, SL3 (mostly), SL3.5, SL5, and roughly half of
SL4, plus "all the important things."**

That ruling is generous, and generosity is a trap. So it is bounded here:

| He KNOWS | Confidence | Bound |
|---|---|---|
| The major beats of SL3 — Wulin's road, the seals, Gu Yue, Shrek, the Abyss | **High** | He knows *what* happens, not always *when* or *how it felt* |
| That Wulin carries the Golden Dragon King's seals and that they break on a clock | **High** | He does not know the mechanism in detail |
| Tang San, the Tang Sect, Bluesilver Emperor lore, the Clear Sky Clan | **High** | Historical. He cannot meet any of it. |
| Ring/beast pairings — which soul beast at which age gives which skill | **Medium** | He knows the famous ones. He does not have an encyclopaedia in his head. |
| Soul power / spiritual power mechanics, cultivation method, the rank ladder | **High** | This is his real edge — method, not destiny |
| SL4 (half) and SL5 | **Partial** | Gaps. He knows things ended; he does not know everything about how |
| Exact dates, exact numbers, exact dialogue | **LOW** | 🔴 **He is a reader, not a database.** He misremembers. He confuses arcs. He does not know chapter numbers. |
| Anything about **himself** | **NONE** | 🔴 **He does not exist in the story he remembers.** This is the single most important firewall. |

### The last row is the engine

Lan Shen is **not in canon**. He has no entry, no fate, no recorded future. Everything he
knows is knowledge about *other people*, and every single thing he does from chapter one
onward makes his remembered future less accurate.

So: **his knowledge decays.** Every butterfly erodes it. By Book Two he is working from a
map of a country that no longer exists, and he knows it. That is not a weakness in the
premise — it *is* the premise, and it is what stops a meta-knowledge OC from being
omniscient and therefore boring.

Tracked in `foundation/CANON_LEDGER.md` as the **decay column**.

### 🔴 FIREWALL 1 — FIRST LIE TO A CANON CHARACTER (ch2)

Wulin asks, on the walk home: *"You looked at my grass for a long time today."*

Lan Shen says: *"No."*

Firewall 0 held — the reincarnation is still unspoken, and nothing in the lie gestures at it.
But this is the first time he has told a canon character something untrue about **canon
content he is hoarding**, rather than about himself. The distinction matters:

- Lying about himself protects Firewall 0. The cost is personal and slow.
- 🔴 **Lying about the gold protects nobody.** Wulin would be no safer knowing. Lan Shen told
  the lie because a gold root is a report, a report is a man in an orange robe remembering a
  question, and he had already spent one crack in his cover on the ceremony floor.

He weighed his brother's hope against his own anonymity for about a second and a half and he
chose himself. **The chapter does not let him feel good about it, and neither does this file.**
It is logged as the first debt in the brotherhood, and every future scene where Wulin trusts
him is drawing on an account that is already overdrawn.

## FIREWALL 4 — THE REVERSE WALL (what he must NOT be told)

A firewall works both ways. Canon characters have knowledge he does not, and he must not be
handed it for free:

- **Wu Zhangkong's realm and history** — not revealed early
- **Shrek's inner workings** — he knows the reputation, not the reality
- **The Spirit Pagoda's internal rot** — he knows it happens, not when or where
- **Gu Yue's nature** — 🔴 he may *suspect* from meta-knowledge, but suspicion is not proof,
  and acting on suspicion about a child who has done nothing is a moral line the book will
  make him pay for
- **Na'er** — he knows she matters. He does not know what she is. Reserved.

**Rule:** meta-knowledge gives him a *direction*, never a *fact about a person standing in
front of him*. People are allowed to have changed. That is what makes the butterflies real.
