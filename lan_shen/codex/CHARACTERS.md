## THE PROTAGONIST

### Lan Shen — AU-ORIGINAL
Six years old at chapter one. Reincarnator. Bluesilver Grass, ordinary. **Innate soul
power rank 1** — the floor, and *below* canon Wulin's rank 3.

The name carries the joke and the thesis: **Lan** means *blue*, for the grass; **Shen**
means *deep*, for what is underneath it. Everyone in Glorybound City will read the name as
*"blue, deep"* and think of a weed. He is the deep thing in a shallow vessel.
🔴 Written in pinyin everywhere, never in characters — Language Law, `07_PROSE_LAW.md` §1.

- **Voice:** adult narration, child dialogue. The gap is the book.
- **Want (stated, chapter one):** to be in the room when his brother's fate is decided,
  instead of reading about it afterwards.
- **Need (unstated):** to stop treating the people around him as characters.
- **Flaw:** 🔴 **he does not grieve.** He has already read how this ends, so he has started
  spending people instead of meeting them. The book's job is to make him pay for that.
- **Wound:** he remembers a world where all of this was entertainment, and he was happy there.

#### THE PERSONALITY (author ruling, 2026-09-19 — supersedes any inference above)

- 🔴🔴 **HE WAS AN AUTHOR. (author ruling, 2026-09-20 — the root of the personality, not an
  addition to it.)** In his last life Lan Shen wrote. He read and watched enormously across every
  form — novels, drama, serials, film, many genres — and he could create his own work and
  **sell** it. He was a professional storyteller.
  **Everything below is a symptom of that.** The externalised memory, the line-level attention,
  the habit of comparing a scene to the version he remembers, the editing instinct, the book he
  keeps in the open — these are not quirks of a careful man. **They are craft habits.**
  🔴 See `foundation/THE_PLAN.md` TRACK 8, including the ruling that `THE_PLAN.md` itself is a
  **plot outline** he wrote for his own life: eight tracks as arcs, load-bearing beats marked, a
  character sheet for his own soul, and an audience of one.
- **Imaginative.** In the previous life too. He does not only remember the story, he
  *plays with it* — he invents routes nobody wrote, and invention is his default response to a
  closed door. This is why the six-track plan exists at all.
- **Unique perspective, from the reasons.** Not just knowledge — a different *angle*. He sees
  the world as somebody who has already seen how this kind of world ends.
- **🔴 Interest-driven, NOT hard-working.** He does not grind. He does the things he is
  interested in and likes — and *for those things the word "hard work" barely applies.*
  Everything he has built was built this way. **This reframes the four years of body
  training:** he was not disciplined, he was absorbed. Write the difference. A disciplined
  child is admirable; an absorbed child is strange, and strangeness is what Su Wan has noticed.
- **Playful with boys.** With male friends he is good company — quick, teasing, warm, up for
  anything. This is the register chapter one has not used yet, because in chapter one he has
  no friends. 🔴 **It must arrive, and it must arrive early.** A reincarnator who is only ever
  watchful and controlled is a bore, and the author has ruled that this one is not.
- **🔴 Uncomfortable with girls his own age.** In the previous life he was a virgin and was
  never at ease with girls of his own age. This is not shyness to be cured by a love interest;
  it is a standing trait with a history. Consequences the story must honour:
  - He is **worse** with girls his age than with boys, and better with *adult* women (his
    mother, teachers) than with either — because adults do not make him a peer.
  - Canon puts **Gu Yue** beside him for nine school years. He knows exactly what she becomes.
    He will be *unable* to act normally around her, and the reason will look like awe or fear
    rather than what it is.
  - 🔴 **Romance is a blocked axis, and the block is the point.** Do not resolve it with a
    confession scene. Do not hand him a love interest to fix him.
- **Loves growth.** The core drive. Not power for its own sake — *becoming*. He will take a
  visible step forward over a safe plateau every single time, which is a flaw as much as a
  virtue.
- **Loves tasty food, genuinely.** Not as a strategy. He likes eating. And he knows food is
  canonically load-bearing for cultivation, so the pleasure and the plan point the same way —
  which is why Track 5 is the track he enjoys.
- **Cooks.** Learned it in the last life and again in this one. See `foundation/THE_PLAN.md`
  Track 5 — income, cultivation, and a social door, and a serious Firewall 2 leak risk.
- **Weapon: staff now, spear later.** Track 6. A staff is a child's legal weapon and a control
  weapon; the spear is where the grass eventually points.

#### WHAT CHAPTER ONE ACTUALLY DID WITH THIS (rebuilt 2026-09-19)

The first draft was written before this ruling existed and staged him as a **watcher**: quiet,
controlled, alone in a crowd, staring at Wulin from across a wall, saying nothing, ending in
solitude. It measured 5.2% dialogue across 21 spoken lines. It violated the Spectator Test,
the Voice Law and M1/M7/M8 of `SL3_FOUNDATION_LESSONS.md` (the source doc was later removed;
the M-list survives in `STYLE_LAW.md` §6) simultaneously, and it was
**rebuilt, not revised**.

The shipped chapter one carries every ruling on the page:
- **playful with boys** — the wall banter, the spoon, the optimistic empty hand, the race of
  wits with Wulin. He is quick and teasing and warm with a boy his own age from line one.
- **uncomfortable with girls his own age** — the green coat. Four things to say, all rejected,
  ears the colour of the radishes. Played for comedy, established as a standing trait.
- **interest-driven, not hard-working** — four years of cups on a windowsill, and the reason
  given on the page is that *he liked it*. Nobody calls it discipline, including him.
- **imagination** — the two-cups experiment is his invention, made at a table, aloud, in front
  of witnesses. He does not remember a route; he designs one.
- **loves food, cooks** — the egg with scallion and the soft ginger. His father tastes it
  twice. The meal is the chapter's warmest scene, not a footnote.
- **the staff** — the callus on the heel of his right hand, mentioned once, unexplained.
- **he is never a watcher** — he reads a notice, negotiates a fee, changes a number in a
  register, proposes an experiment, and says the thing out loud. Five acts, all his own.

### His family — the Lan household, AU-ORIGINAL
Neighbours of the Tang family. **A small herb shop.**

This is load-bearing, not decoration:
- A plant martial soul in a plant family reads as *natural* to everyone — which is exactly
  the cover he needs. Nobody questions a herb-shop boy who knows what a root is for.
- It gives him legitimate, non-suspicious access to botanical and soul-beast knowledge.
- It gives the family a reason to be poor, literate, and careful with records — which
  rhymes with the near spine (a man with a register).

harder than an enemy. Do not soften her into obliviousness.
