# KNOWLEDGE FIREWALLS — who knows what
### Soul Land 2 · The Unraveled Tide. Levels L1–L4.
### A character never acts on knowledge they have not earned. Reader layer,
### character layer, and truth layer are tracked separately.

## L1 — The Adaptation Talent
- **Truth:** the AT has been building Jiang Che's soul (and, around it, his
  body and mind) since conception; martial-soul-centered; perfect-integration
  post-absorption.
- **Who knows:** the reader (as effect, never as named system); the fox
  (pattern-recognises it: "different currencies"); **Jiang Che himself
  experiences the results and never the engine** — he does not name it in
  thought on the page, and never in speech.
- **Who must not know:** every other character. No examiner, no elder, no
  canon character has read it. It is invisible, non-sentient, and never a
  system/voice/menu.
- **Leak route:** none designed. If a character notices something, it is
  noticed *as a result* (a mended wound, a vine that re-knits), and the
  attribution is left open.

## L2 — The fox's true years
- **Truth:** Xiao Jiu is nine-tailed; her true age is sealed (L4 in the
  v2.0 codex framing); she was healed by the OC at 7 and has ridden with him
  since.
- **Who knows:** the reader (partially — "nine" is spoken to Wang Dong; the
  *count* is known, the *years* are not); the fox.
- **Who must not know:** everyone else, including Jiang Che (he knows what
  she is in shape, not in ledger). Her first on-page reveal of the years is a
  reserved beat, not yet written.

## L3 — The red veil (the True Cascade)
- **Truth:** the fox's archive: "the red one from the lake; two hundred days
  since the ice pressed her fire flat; she does not know what pressed it."
  Ma Xiaotao's fire was suppressed by the ice-soul; the cascade is running
  toward the public debut (ch18) and its aftermath.
- **Who knows:** the fox (archive); the reader (via the fox's beats).
- **Who must not know:** the cast. The near-recognition in ch18 is a blink,
  not a disclosure. The ice-soul who did it is not named on-page.

## L4 — The white ring (the ice worm's choice)
- **Truth:** at the baboon ambush, a ten-golden-ring white-jade ice beast
  chose Huo Yuhao, poured itself away, and left him a single lustrous-white
  ring (canon ch 3 aftermath, held whole in our ch1). "Their story will be a
  baboon and a lucky ring, and the story will hold."
- **Who knows:** the reader and the fox (the secret's second keeper, "purring
  against his collarbone"); the ice worm's choice is real but veiled.
- **Public layer:** Yuhao's public account is "shock of my first kill"; the
  world's version is a ten-year ring. The veil is kind to the boy and holds.
- **Leak route:** none written. The fox keeps it; the boy carries it as
  "something chose him."

## Standing firewall rules (this project)
1. **No romance is pre-decided.** The Natural-Ripple Principle governs:
   nothing aimed, nothing pre-decided; canon spine + earned causes only.
2. **No canon beat is staged before its readiness** (the ring hunt and the
   knockout bracket are not triggered; Xiao Xiao's flute is held to her
   decisive fight, per her doctrine).
3. **A public fact must produce a public consequence** (records, attention,
   training, comparisons) — the firewall protects secrets; it never freezes
   the world.
4. **Author knowledge is never character knowledge.** What the reader knows
   from the codex is not what the characters know.
