## 🔴 1. THE FOURTH RING IS HARD — and this is directly our arc

> *"…she had to find a martial soul that matched it. However, she sought a spirit soul for her **fourth
> soul ring**, which was a **drastically different situation** than Xu Xiaoyan getting one for her
> third."*

> *"After helping **Yuanen Yehui obtain her fourth soul ring**, Yue Zhengyu understood **the gap that
> existed between them**."*

> *"Though Tang Wulin was strong, she thought he wouldn't be able to match her, especially since **she
> just got her fourth soul ring**."*

**What this means for us:** canon treats the fourth ring as a genuine step-change, and getting a
*matching* soul for it as much harder than the third. **Our plan has Lin Hao absorb a ten-thousand-year
ice+water dragon as his fourth at rank 40.** That is not a small claim — it should be *felt* as
impossible by everyone watching, and the difficulty canon describes is the yardstick.

## 🔴 2. YUANEN YEHUI — the benchmark, verified

> *"She's only **fifteen**, yet she's now **rank 40**! **I can't even dream of reaching rank 40 by the
> age of fifteen!**"* — Tang Wulin
> *"Yuanen Yehui had **three purple soul rings** and **two powerful martial souls**."*
> *"Kids are so advanced these days! She's so strong with just four rings! Extraordinary!"*
> *"That soul skill of hers is just too **domineering**! It's even mightier than my Hell White Tiger."*

**Canon's once-in-a-generation prodigy: rank 40 at fifteen, three purple rings, two martial souls.**
**Our Lin Hao goes 40 → 45 at eleven with three purple rings.** Use her by name — a real canon figure
beats an invented yardstick, and the reader can feel the size of the gap.

Note: canon itself uses **"domineering"** for an overwhelming soul skill, which supports naming the
fourth ring's skill **DOMINEER**.

## 🔴 3. SOUL POWER COMPRESSION — the mechanism we were missing

> *"In order to maximize their strength, they **compressed their soul power** so that a denser, more
> potent form of the energy filled them."*
> *"**The value of compressed soul power became more apparent with strength.**"*
> *"…it wouldn't have been able to stand on the level as Yuanen Yehui."*
> *"the root of the issue lay in the **difficulty in compressing soul power**"*
> *"…he could only let his **blood essence** continue to gather and **compress**…"*

**This is canon's answer to "how do you fight above your rank," and our story never had one.** Adopted
into `THE_CODEX.md` §SOUL POWER COMPRESSION. It is also the bottleneck — which explains the ch22–39
rank stall far better than "the ring count caps it."

## 🔴 4. SHEN YI AND ZHUO SHI — Wu Zhangkong's teachers

> *"Don't you know how much **your teacher** grieved after you left?"* / *"**I'm not worthy of my
> teacher.** I'm too ashamed to go back."* — Wu Zhangkong, to **Shen Yi**
> *"Are you raising these children to send off to the academy as an **apology to your teacher**?"*
> *"Spectating from the side were Wu Zhangkong and Shen Yi, who had awoken by the time the
> **granddisciple and grandteacher** pair stepped foot into the courtyard."*
> And separately, **Zhuo Shi** — *"Hello, Tang Wulin…"* — who teaches Wu Zhangkong and is addressed as
> his teacher in the Sea God's Island material.

**Load-bearing and our story has never stated it:** in ch61 Shen Yi assesses Lin Hao *in front of* Wu
Zhangkong. Her verdict is therefore not an inspector's opinion — **it is his teacher judging whether the
one thing he built turned out worth building.**

## 🔴 5. GU YUE — what canon actually does with her

> *"…round, **silver scales** had appeared on the back of Gu Yue's hand."*
> *"Her pupils instantly turned into **vertical slits**… the scales on the back of it flickering with
> **silver light**."* (ch596)
> *"But I can still sense the **Golden Dragon bloodline** in you. It's so pure, so **I'm always drawn to
> you**…" Gu Yue blushed, averting her eyes*
> *"She kept it a secret that few were privy to. If she did use it, **her true gender would be
> revealed**."*
> *"…everything will come to light. By then, if you can **absorb the bloodline**, then maybe you can—"
> "Enough!" Gu Yue's tone was ice cold*
> *"What was that **seven-colored light**? And those **multi-colored scales**?"*

**Her nature surfaces in behaviour and body, never in exposition.** That is the correct way to write
her, and it is how our story already handles the lock. Note also canon's *"No one is allowed to hit you
other than me"* (ch598) — possessive, not sentimental.

## 6. TANG SECT STRUCTURE

> *"I'm **Guo Xiaoxu, the general manager of this Tang Sect headquarters**"* — regional HQs, not one
> temple
> *"he would return to the **Tang Sect headquarters in Shrek City** to obtain the other two spirit items
> needed to **shatter the fourth seal**"*

**Spirit items break the seals**, and the Tang Sect supplies them. Relevant to any future seal work.

## 7. WU ZHANGKONG — confirmed

> *"This man is a **Soul Sage from Shrek Academy**!"*

**He is a Soul Sage (71–80) and he is from Shrek.** Our lock holds: canon ch204 is the scheduled
reveal, we are at ch62, so **Shrek is still not named on-page.** 🔴 **And ch62 has made the lock load-bearing: Shen Yi — his teacher — is standing in the yard saying *"Congratulations, Zhangkong"*, and nobody says where either of them came from.** Six rings, two of them ten-thousand-year,
is consistent with a Soul Sage.

## ⚠️ 8. DOMAIN — a caution

Canon's *"within the domain, the ability user had absolute control"* is real, but in **337–600 the word
"domain" is mostly used in its ordinary sense** (*"beyond the domain of her current abilities"*). There
is **no named martial-soul domain system** in this range. **Our Frost Abyss Domain is an AU invention**
and must be labelled as one — it is not a canon mechanic we can lean on.
