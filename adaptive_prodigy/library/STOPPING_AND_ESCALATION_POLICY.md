# STOPPING & ESCALATION POLICY v4.0

## Mission
Balance rigor with efficiency.

## Escalate when
- a material claim is uncertain
- sources conflict
- the task is current/niche/high consequence
- the user's wording is ambiguous in a way that changes the result
- the task depends on a file, tool, or external state
- a known historical failure mode is present
- multiple downstream dependencies exist

## Stop when
- the acceptance criteria are met
- remaining uncertainty is unlikely to change the conclusion materially
- additional evidence is redundant
- the user asked for a bounded answer

## Exception
For explicit deep-research requests, continue until the requested research boundary is satisfied.
