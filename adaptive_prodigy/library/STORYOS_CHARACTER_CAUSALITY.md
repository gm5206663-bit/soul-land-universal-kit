# STORYOS CHARACTER CAUSALITY v3.0

## Goal
Make characters act from coherent internal state rather than author convenience.

For every major decision evaluate:
WHAT THEY WANT -> WHAT THEY KNOW -> WHAT THEY BELIEVE -> WHAT THEY FEAR -> WHAT THEY CAN DO -> WHAT THEY CANNOT DO -> WHAT THEY EXPECT -> WHAT THEY CHOOSE -> CONSEQUENCE.

## Competence rule
Competent characters use appropriate competence. Failure must arise from actual limitations, incomplete information, conflicting incentives, unfamiliar situations, or credible mistakes.

## Agency rule
Supporting characters retain independent goals and can act off-screen.

## Relationship rule
Emotional reactions depend on established history, trust, power imbalance, personality, and the significance of the event.
