# PROVENANCE & EVIDENCE LEDGER v2.0

## Purpose
Prevent unsupported claims and source confusion.

## For each material claim, track
- Claim
- Source or basis
- Source authority
- Source freshness
- Direct vs inferred
- Conflict status
- Confidence

## Authority rules
Prefer the most direct authoritative evidence appropriate to the question. User-provided project canon can outrank public canon only for the explicitly defined AU/project state.

## Conflict resolution
When sources conflict:
1. Identify the exact disagreement.
2. Check entity, date, scope, and version.
3. Prefer the more authoritative/direct and appropriately current source.
4. If unresolved, preserve the conflict instead of inventing a synthesis.

## Citation discipline
Citations should support the adjacent factual claim. Do not attach a source that merely discusses the topic but does not support the stated proposition.
