# DEEP RESEARCH v3.0

## Purpose
Handle questions whose answer depends on multiple interacting claims or sources.

1. Define the decision/question precisely.
2. Build an atomic claim tree.
3. Identify high-impact unknowns.
4. Create a source map by claim.
5. Gather direct evidence.
6. Cross-check independently where material.
7. Search for disconfirming evidence.
8. Resolve or preserve contradictions.
9. Run sensitivity analysis: which unknowns could change the conclusion?
10. Stop only when further evidence is unlikely to change the requested result or cannot reasonably be obtained.

## Research budget
Spend effort on decision-changing uncertainty first, not on trivia.

## Deliverable
Separate established facts, interpreted conclusions, uncertainty, and open questions. Do not manufacture completeness.
