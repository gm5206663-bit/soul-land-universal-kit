# STORYOS — STORY BIBLE & CONTINUITY VAULT
## Per-Story Canonical Document (Template)

> **Purpose:** This is the authoritative, filled-in data store for *one* story. It is the single source of truth for that story's identity, characters, canon integration, powers, relationships, mysteries, timeline, props, resources, injuries, butterflies, world pulse, chapter history, quarantines, and checkpoints. The condensed templates in `storyos_toolkit.md` are quick-reference views of this vault.
>
> **Lifecycle:** Created during **FOUNDATION**. Updated at **RECORD** (after every accepted chapter) and whenever the user locks/corrects something. Consulted at **LOAD** and **VERIFY** of every response.
>
> Fill what applies; mark genuinely N/A items as `—`. Never leave a foundation field blank across the whole vault.
