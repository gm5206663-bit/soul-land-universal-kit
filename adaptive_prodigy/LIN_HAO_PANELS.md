## THE LAW THIS PANEL ENFORCES

**A physical line is a progression line.** It is audited every chapter like rank, spiritual power,
swordsmanship, blacksmithing and the hawk. Specifically:

1. **A measured value is a hard floor** (AT §61.4). 2,612 kg may never be under-performed under equal
   conditions.
2. **Physical lines must move at least as fast as spiritual power** across any arc, because the body is
   the primary target of the Talent. A chapter arc where spiritual power grows and the body does not is
   a sick chapter.
3. **Never invent a number to fill the panel.** Unmeasured stays unmeasured until a scene measures it —
   and then it becomes a hard floor with a named source.
4. **Record defects as well as gains.** The Unnamed Stroke's unrepeatable nature is a tracked weakness.

## 1. THE SPECIALTY — corrected

**Previous framing (WRONG):** spiritual power was treated as his headline number.

**Correct framing:** spiritual power is **not** his speciality. It is one line among many, and it is
neither his highest nor his fastest.

> ### ⚡ HIS SPECIALITY IS THAT **EVERY** LINE IS HIGH AND **NOTHING** IS LOW.

Other cultivators are specialists. Wang Jinxi has strength and almost no spiritual power (18). Xie Xie
has speed and average spiritual power. Gu Yue has overwhelming spiritual power (153 at ten) and had to
be told to use less of it. **Lin Hao has no spike. He has a plateau — and the plateau is above everyone
else's.**

**Why this follows from the Talent.** AT §73: the Talent *"develops the complete holder across body,
mind, soul, identity, energy, powers, resistance."* It does not optimise one axis. It removes
weaknesses. **So the signature of an Adaptation Talent holder is not a high number — it is the absence
of a low one.**

**How to write it:** never introduce him by his biggest number. Introduce him by the thing that has no
answer — *"there is nothing about him you can attack."* A specialist can be fought at their weak axis.
He cannot, and that is what makes him frightening rather than impressive.

**The one exception, recorded:** **Reliability.** The Unnamed Stroke cannot be summoned on purpose. That
is the only genuinely low line, and it stays in the panel as a defect.
