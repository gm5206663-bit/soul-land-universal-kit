# RELATIONSHIPS — the web and who knows what

v1.0, 2026-09-25. Full texture lives in the chapters; this is the continuity
card — read before writing any scene. Companion to CODEX §VI.

## THE WEB

**Su Ping ↔ The System.** The one relationship with no voice. It counts; he
farms. No gratitude, no trust, no conversation — only the numbers, and the
steadily deepening reliance. The name is the author's gift, awaited. He
knows exactly what it is (the genre carried in his old world's stories) and
tells no one, ever.

**↔ Headmaster Lin** (field-hoe soul, three rings, Spirit Elder). Teacher,
then protector. The only man who has seen the impossible twice: five levels
in a school year, and a ring that changed color in its seat. Holds both in
silence — "the yard's memory is short and kind. The Hall's is neither."
"Not yet" (the letter to the Hall) is his held thunder. Trajectory: the
wall between Su Ping and the Hall, thinning year by year.

**↔ Wen Bo** (ox-calf soul). First customer, partner, the one-in-ten — now
growing a merchant's education ("the shape of a plan, not a dream"). The
honest heart; knows the business, never the secret. Trajectory: the shop in
the prefecture city — the orchard is growing a merchant.

**↔ Ma Fei** (iron hammer, innate 4). The rival who became the wall-class
mate. Not quick but honest; borrowed Su Ping's own arithmetic to defend him
twice ("I took the top of what was mine"). Ringed at nine — the east-slope
boar, forty years. Trajectory: the ordinary road's finest — fast, and never
fast enough; the clean rivalry, four levels wide and widening.

**↔ Father and mother** (Fruit Creek). The letters; the purses; the lower
meadow leased and planted to sapling stock. "Apple trees are patient. We
are patient. It runs in the family." The parents price their son in pride
and worry, not arithmetic.

**↔ Fruit Creek** (Old Shi, Old Gao, Deacon Bao's one visit). The blessing
story the village tells; the granary roof the apples bought. The deacon's
return is an open thread — the Hall's registry holds his name.

**↔ The Hall.** Weather, not plot — for now. Knows exactly one line: a
three-innate food-type in the mountains. The day it learns more, the
politeness begins. "Not yet."

**↔ The porters, the town, the over-the-hill trade.** The street name ("a
night's sleep in a fruit"); the second basket; the market finding its own
edges.

**↔ Tang San (off-page).** The comet. Same age, another village, never met,
never referenced. One crossing, far away, if ever — the Cameo Law.

## TRAJECTORIES (one line each)

Lin's "Not yet" → the Hall, someday, with Lin writing the letter himself.
Wen Bo → the prefecture shop; the merchant grown. Ma Fei → the honest wall,
climbed at the honest pace. The Hall → the polite knock. The green apple →
the closed hand, until the author opens it. The System's name → the
author's, whenever he gives it.
