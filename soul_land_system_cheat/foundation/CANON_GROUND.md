## The laws the System must respect (canon, non-negotiable)

1. **Awakening:** every person's martial soul awakens at six; the System
   changes nothing about the holder's awakening (it is not a martial soul —
   author ruling).
2. **Ring absorption:** a spirit ring is absorbed at a rank breakpoint
   (10, 20, 30 …) — and a ring too old for the body can kill the absorber.
   The System's aged rings do not bypass this. Ever.
3. **The body bears nine rings** (mortal); the tenth is divine.
4. **Soul bones:** six positions — head, torso, right arm, left arm, right
   leg, left leg — non-overlapping; external bones exist in canon as rare
   exceptions.
5. **Killer's right:** a ring goes to the one who delivered the killing
   blow. The System ages rings; it does not take them.
6. **Cultivation is the floor of everything:** spirit power, body, and soul
   are grown; the System accelerates the holder's growth only through the
   channels that already exist in canon (training, rings, bones).


## CORRECTIONS FROM THE AUTHOR (SC4, 2026-09-24 — law, from the Chapter-1 strikes)

2. **THE SCALE:** innate spirit power runs one to ten and is spoken with awe. Nine is the holy maiden of the Hall herself. Ten is once a generation on the whole continent. A rural deacon reads power at all perhaps twice in a spring circuit.
3. **THE RARITY LAW:** a village produces a power-child once in a generation at most. Village ceremonies test the whole cohort; almost all awaken a soul with innate ZERO (souls without power — the common lot, canon's own arithmetic: "8 villages, only 1 child").
4. **TIMELINE PURITY:** no canon character may be referenced before their canon fame. (Oscar is years older than Su Ping and unknown at Su Ping's awakening.)
6. **THE POOL LAW (SC5):** spirit power is a well, not a ration. Soul arts draw it down; rest returns it; cultivation grows it. Output counts are well ÷ cost — discovered in the story, never prescribed as fixed daily numbers, and never dictated by a character who could not know them.
5. **THE RECOGNITION LAW:** the reborn carry their whole past life and use it from the first breath (canon precedent: Tang San — complete memories, the Mysterious Heaven Skill from childhood). A reincarnator who has read the old world's stories KNOWS what a system is.

7. **THE PLAIN-SPEECH LAW (SC6 + SC7):** Hall officials and spirit masters are EDUCATED PROFESSIONALS — the universal mechanics are their profession's first lesson, and they say them plainly, in Soul Land's own terms: spirit power is fuel; using a soul's power spends it; rest recovers it; cultivation climbs it one level at a time (levels 1–100); every tenth level earns a spirit ring; the ring gives the soul skill. NO invented metaphors (cups, basins, wells, snails), NO homesy aphorisms, NO false ignorance — and equally no character explaining what is genuinely unknowable to them; the truly unknown (e.g., a food-type's ring outcomes, unrecorded in the region) is stated as the plain rarity it is. *Canon anchors: spirit-power levels and the ring road (Tang San ch. 1–2); "It can produce various kinds of abilities depending on the Spirit Ring absorbed" (Sausage, fandom).*

8. **THE FUTURE-KNOWLEDGE LAW (SC9):** no in-world mouth speaks canon's future — godhoods, endgames, inheritances, the God Realm. Canon characters' destinies (e.g., Oscar's, decades ahead) are not history; they are not knowledge at all until the story lives them. 9. **THE ENGINE ARITHMETIC (SC10, sharpened SC11):** ordinary cultivation is 3–5 hours a day; early spirit-power levels climb easiest (canon's own curve). System-assisted gains are paced honestly: at the opening era, one month of the 24-hour best-speed engine ≈ an ordinary person's year — the first term reads level 3 → 7 — and the walls slow it later as they slow everyone. The ratio is a CURRENT reading, never a constant (SC12: there is no fixed thing).

10. **THE BODY-DRAFT LAW (SC11 — author canon for this serial):** the body is the draft on the furnace — body strength feeds cultivation speed. The strong-soul classes burn hot; the support classes run thin; food-type soul masters cultivate slowest of all (their bodies are not as strong). Su Ping is exempt: the body was the first thing his System ever built — six years of farm childhood before it woke, the yard work every evening since, the body-conditioning meter ticking underneath — so his class's headwind was cut before it ever touched him.

11. **THE NAMED-METHOD LAW (SC12):** a foundational stage completed at 100% does not upgrade to the generic next stage — it becomes a NAMED method, perfectly built for the holder, as if he had created it himself. Completing a stage fully is the rare, inward "big thing" most practitioners never do; the finished road becomes his.

12. **THE APPLE ECONOMY (SC13):** the base apple works on ANYONE — stamina, fatigue, minor hurts; the villagers' blessing was only the first case. Its only cost to the maker is soul power, refilled by meditation. A food-type soul master sells from the start: open, sanctioned, respectable — "a food soul master, not a joke." The mockery-to-market turn is the class's own canon arc, run from month one.

13. **THE LIFE-SKILLS LAW (SC13):** soul power control, observation, running/walking, reading, body control — and everything else a life teaches — are real entries on the System's list. Unslotted, they increase normally through living (living is their only engine); and they influence: each leans on the others and on every art (observation feeds learning; control feeds cost; the body feeds everything).

14. **WHAT A SOUL RING GIVES (SC14 — the ordered check, receipts):** on absorption, a spirit ring gives: (1) **A SKILL** — "under normal circumstances, it confers one skill" (Baidu Baike, Soul Ring); the skill comes from the beast, expressed through the martial soul (a Datura Snake ring on Blue Silver Grass = Blue Silver Bind). (2) **THE BOTTLENECK BROKEN** — without a ring, spirit power STOPS at every 10th level; the ring unlocks the next title (fables.gg lore page). (3) **A SPIRIT-POWER BOOST** — "the older and more powerful the Spirit Beast... more potent skills and a larger boost to Spirit Power" (cultivation guide). (4) **BODY NOURISHMENT** — the early rings feed the body and the six spirit bones' growth (fan-compiled rank pages, novel lore). (5) **ABSORPTION LIMITS** — a ring too old for the body = severe injury or death; the true limit runs on martial-soul quality, physique, and spiritual power (Yu Xiaogang's 423/764/1,760… table is a rule of thumb, not a law — Baidu Baike). White (10–99): "the most common and also the lowest-grade Soul Ring. Soul Masters with the means would not choose to absorb this type" — which makes the PEAK white (99 years) both the ceiling of its class and the overlooked maximum. **Two more of the check's receipts (played in Chapter 3):** the ring comes only to the one who delivers the killing blow — "The Spirit Ring can only be absorbed by the person who delivers the final blow and kills it" — and the taker must stand at a bottleneck (every tenth level) to absorb at all (WebNovel rank compilation); and the ring rises at the beast's death and does not wait — the absorption begins at once, under the escort's watch. **THE IMMEDIATE-SKILL RECEIPT (SC15, the strike that corrected Chapter 3):** the skill is the ring's own gift, obtained and USABLE the moment absorption completes, and it arrives knowing its own work in its master's hands — Tang San's Datura Snake ring breaks the first bottleneck and Blue Silver Bind is his that same day (Episode 007: "the 1st Bottleneck... has finally been broken" + the skill manifesting); "the soul master... can immediately obtain a certain soul skill of the spirit beast"; a food-type's every ring is a new WORKING product with its own incantation from the first hour (Oscar: Big Recovery Sausage, ring one — each sausage its own words). A ring's skill is never dormant, never unknown to its master, never deferred.

17. **THE FULL GAINS OF A RING (SC16 — "check everything... there is many things"; the completed list, receipts on the Soul Rings reference page and the property receipts):** on absorption a soul ring gives, all at once: **(1) the skill** — immediate, usable, own incantation for food-types (SC15); 19. **WHAT SOUL POWER IS AND HOW IT WORKS (SC17 check):** "Soul Power is primarily energy derived from the Martial Soul by absorbing the essence energy of heaven and earth" — the well, filled by meditation through a cultivation method, spent on the soul and its skills, recovered by rest (World System compilation; fables lore). QUANTITY is the level, 1–100. Innate soul power (0–10, read at the awakening) is the starting speed — zero means no cultivation at all. At the top, soul power liquefies, and a SOUL CORE compresses it solid — storage and recovery in one. Our serial's terms (the Plain-Speech Law) are canon's terms: fuel, spend, rest, climb — and the quiet law underneath: the method you build with decides the quality of what you build.

20. **METHODS, TECHNIQUES, AND THE MEANING OF MASTERING BASICS (SC17 check):** three families of power. **(a) Cultivation methods** — the arts that grow soul power: the BASIC methods (the academies' plain meditation: slow, common quality) against the ADVANCED — sect inheritances, "an extremely precious inheritance" (fan consensus on the guarded methods): Xuantian Gong, nine levels, whose unblocking of the Eight Extraordinary Meridians expands capacity, accelerates recovery, sustains more skills — and "the energy level of the Soul Power cultivated through Xuantian Gong is higher than that of ordinary Soul Power" (Baidu Baike). Methods are treasures; the Hall and the upper sects research them deepest. **(b) Soul skills** — ring-given, cost soul power, scale with rank and years. **(c) Techniques** — learned arts, trained by repetition, no ring needed: Purple Demon Eye (a dawn-facing eye art), Ghost Shadow Perplexing Track (footwork), Mysterious Jade Hand — and self-created techniques (Tang San's hidden weapons). Plus the later families: martial soul avatar at rank 50+, bone skills, domains at Titled, fusion skills. The System's slots take (a) and (c), rings, bones — soul skills arrive with their rings. **THE COMPLETE MEANING OF MASTERING BASICS:** canon is unambiguous — "there are no shortcuts in cultivation, and if they are, they can be damaging"; rushed cultivation cracks the foundation and forces re-founding; the pure, dense, step-by-step foundation is what raises the ceiling (the concentration law, 19). To finish the basic method fully is to hold the cleanest foundation the soul can carry. OUR LAWS' TIE: the meter measures exactly this; 100% is canon's perfect foundation; and the NAMED METHOD (SC12) is the perfection made personal — soul power built on a finished basic runs PURER than the same levels on a good-enough road (an advantage no crystal reads, compounding for the rest of the story — the long-game reason the Mastery Law is an event).
