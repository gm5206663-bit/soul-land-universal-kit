# CHARACTERS — the cast cards

v1.0, 2026-09-25. Voice, want, knows, arc — the four lines that keep every
character alive and law-abiding. The who-knows-what matrix is in
`RELATIONSHIPS.md`; the plot web is there too.

## THE HOLDER

**SU PING** — the apple's master. Two ages in one boy: seven years old and
forty, and both very still.
- **Voice:** plain, short, exact. Answers questions with facts or with
  nothing ("I sit still well, headmaster" — true, and not an answer). Never
  explains the System; never will. Prices like a farmer.
- **Wants:** the family farm secure; the road finished properly; the levels
  — in that order, though the order gets tested every arc.
- **Behavioral rules (enforced):** files feelings away carefully "where he
  could find them later" (they return, weighed); does not chop — he
  undermines (farmers' arithmetic in everything); gratitude is paid in
  coppers and trees, not words; the closed hand stays closed.
- **Arc:** the orchard — patience at compound interest, from a village's
  blessing to a continent's quiet necessity.

## THE SCHOOL

**HEADMASTER LIN** — field-hoe soul (white, yellow, yellow), Spirit Elder;
the farm boy who walked the plainest soul in the registry to a headmaster's
office.
- **Voice:** the register-voice — plain, level, the truth at arm's length
  ("Rings do not age."). Dry as a ledger; startles expressed as a second
  cup of tea or a very steady pour.
- **Wants:** the school's children through their walls; the Hall's rules
  kept; and — undeclared — to see how far the apple boy can go before the
  world notices.
- **Knows:** the levels, the yellow ring (kept silent), the sanction's
  worth. Never the System, never the green fruit.
- **Arc:** "Not yet" — the held letter to the Hall, thinning year by year.

**MA FEI** — iron hammer, innate four, the school's best reading in five
years. Not quick, but honest.
- **Voice:** slow, plain, a farmer's arithmetic when he bothers to use it.
  Snorts first, thinks after — then says the fair thing anyway.
- **Arc:** the clean rivalry — the ordinary road's finest, walked in good
  order ("I took the top of what was mine"); the wall-class mate; four
  levels behind and pulling the other way.

**WEN BO** — ox-calf soul; could sell a coat to a goose.
- **Voice:** careful, commercial, warm underneath; says plans as if they
  were observations.
- **Arc:** the one-in-ten → a purse of its own → the prefecture shop: the
  merchant the orchard grows.

**THE SIX BEGINNERS / THE YARD** — the porch rails that laughed, then
bought; the queue at the laundry; the yard that learned the arithmetic.
Chorus, not characters — but the chorus is the economy's scoreboard.

## THE HOME

**THE FATHER** — orchard ladder shoulders; speaks in deeds (the walking
stick; the four-page letter; the leased meadow). **THE MOTHER** — collars
set square; the letter-reader. Both price their son in pride and worry, not
arithmetic. **FRUIT CREEK** — Old Shi (the headman's festival line), Old
Gao (the footwork teacher), Deacon Bao (the Hall's one visit — and its
registry line: innate three, food-type).

## THE POWERS

**THE SPIRIT HALL** — weather, not plot (for now): the awakenings, the
permits, the licensed grounds, the strongest force alive. Its politeness is
the fuse's lit end. **THE PORTERS AND THE TOWN** — the street name ("a
night's sleep in a fruit"), the over-the-hill trade, the second basket:
the market as a character. **THE COMET** — off-page forever until the Cameo
Law's one crossing.

## THE CHARACTER WITH NO VOICE

**THE SYSTEM** — silent by nature; law, like weather. Its characterization
is timing alone: when the panel comes, and when it does not. Never explains
itself, ever — not because it is hiding, but because it cannot. Its name is
the author's gift; until then, "the readout."

## THE CAST LAW

Minor characters enter with a job and a voice, never a name-tag. No
character explains what they cannot know (the Ignorance Law — check the
matrix before every scene). No canon cast on page or in reference. Nobody
in this era speaks of anything past Soul Land 1's horizon.
