## The flows

Queue → coppers → **the purses home** (New Year: the first money Fruit
Creek ever received from a child away at school; June: heavier) → **the
lower meadow** (leased off Old Shi; sapling stock planted — five years to
bear) → someday the orchard's own trees. Wen Bo's tenth → a purse of its own
→ **the prefecture-city shop** (a plan, not yet a dream). The porters carry
the keeping apples over the hill to the next valley — the market finding
its own edges.
