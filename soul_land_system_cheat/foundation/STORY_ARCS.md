# STORY ARCS — the plan of the whole serial

v1.0, 2026-09-25. WRITTEN is fact; PLANNED is PROPOSED — every beat, number,
and shape below can be struck or re-ruled by the author at any time (the
Nothing-Fixed Law applies to this file above all). The arcs follow the
TIMELINE's age table; the current readings live in CODEX §VI.

## ARC 1 — THE AWAKENING DAY (Ch 1 · WRITTEN)

The ceremony, the apple, the village's blessing, the System waking in the
dark. Ends on the cart to Yunxi. **Spine: the blessing.**

## ARC 2 — YUNXI JUNIOR ACADEMY (Ch 2–ongoing · WRITTEN through Ch 4)

**Spine: the orchard planted** — the three engines started one by one, and
the boy they grow.

*Written:* the one-slot choice; the economy opened; the life-skills list;
the wall at ten and the first ring (the 99-year grass); the green apple
live; the 100% event and the named method; the purer water; the yellow
crossing seen by the yard; Ma Fei ringed; the wall at twenty at nine.

*Planned (PROPOSED):*
- **Ch 5 — the second hunt**: the east-of-the-river stands; the escort and
  the higher fee; the second beast (the author's ruling, awaited); the
  third slot; the intermediate method seated; the second cultivar — the
  ring decides.
- The third ring and the walls of the twenties; the walls slow him as they
  slow everyone (the timeline's law).
- The intermediate-academy tests at twelve — the first gate the world runs;
  Lin's letter to the Hall, still held ("Not yet") growing heavier.
- The first team (Ma Fei's hammer, Wen Bo's ox-calf?); the
  mockery-to-indispensable turn completed.
- The economy maturing: Wen Bo's prefecture shop; the parents' orchard
  bearing (the five-year trees); the red apple a small institution.
- **Arc ends (~age 12, rank ~30): leaving Yunxi** — the school outgrown,
  the town too small, the farewell to Lin.

## ARC 3 — THE WIDE WORLD (12–16 · PLANNED)

The advanced academy in the city; ranks 30s–40s; rings 4–5. The seed law
discovered (an apple has seeds; a seed, fed for years, sprouts). His name
begins traveling in small circles. **THE CAMEO** (one crossing, across a
field, no entanglement — the era's monsters seen once at the continental
competition). **Spine: the orchard flowers — and the world begins to smell
them.**

## ARC 4 — THE ATTRITION YEARS (16–25 · PLANNED)

The Spirit Hall's war as weather, not his battlefield. A support master's
war: the wounded, the roads, the hungry. Rings 6–7; ranks 50s–60s; the
orchard's first true harvest; the fuse burning — the green apple's value
impossible to hide forever, and the choice of how it meets the world
(the author's, when it comes). Canon Part 1 ends (~25): distant thunder, a
light in the sky, the comet gone. **Spine: what an orchard is FOR.**

## ARC 5 — THE ORCHARD (25+ · PLANNED)

The world after canon — the story's own canvas, the part no comet ever
wrote. The climb to Titled Douluo by orchard arithmetic; the Food God
direction (the food-type line's ceiling — design-doc knowledge, arrived at
honestly); the Golden Apple question; the ring's King line (a 99-year grass
aged toward ten thousand) closing its long circle. **Spine: patience,
rewarded at compound interest.**
