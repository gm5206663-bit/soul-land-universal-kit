# GLOSSARY — the serial's words and the world's, plain

v1.0, 2026-09-25. The Plain-Speech Law's reference: every term as the story
uses it. Era-true (SL1 only).

## The serial's own terms (with first use)

- **The readout / the System** — the silent thing behind his eyes (Ch 1).
  Its name is the author's gift, awaited; "the readout" in all prose.
- **The well** — his spirit power: filled by the method, spent by the soul
  and its fruit (Ch 1, the pool law).
- **The engine** — the System's 24-hour training at his best speed (Ch 2).
- **The meter** — the percentage readout of a slotted thing (Ch 1).
- **The slots** — 1/1/1 at Scholar, +1 per rank-up, every list (Ch 1).
- **The orchard** — the compounding whole: well, fruit, engine, body, each
  feeding the rest (Ch 3: "The well fed the fruit. The fruit fed the
  engine...").
- **The draft (on the furnace)** — the body (Ch 2, the Body-Draft Law).
- **The closed hand** — the green apple withheld from the world (Ch 3).
- **The wall / the gate** — every tenth level; the ring is the key (Ch 3).
- **The laundry line** — where the apple queue stands (Ch 2).
- **The small cold stone** — the arithmetic feelings he files away (Ch 4).
- **"Not yet"** — Lin's held letter to the Hall (Ch 3).
- **The fourth list / the grass in the blood** — the bloodline list,
  unlocked when the ring's essence rooted into his blood (Ch 4); "the ring
  was the branch. The bloodline was the root."
- **Bloodline** — a line of power in the blood, inherited in canon; his is
  ROOTED — grown from the Blue Silver Grass ring's essence, the System's
  fourth category (SC20).
- **The panels** — 「...」 printed at key beats only, cold and quiet.

## The world's terms as the story uses them

- **Spirit power (soul power)** — the fuel every spirit master runs on;
  drawn from heaven and earth through the soul, by meditation. Quantity is
  the level (1–100); quality (purity, density) is the ceiling.
- **Meditation (the method hour)** — the sitting that draws the water.
- **Innate soul power** — the awakening's reading, 0–10. Zero = no
  cultivation, the common lot. Three = a village's generation. Nine = the
  holy maiden. Ten = once a generation, and the Hall knows within the month.
- **Martial soul** — tool soul (the hoe, the apple) or beast soul (the
  sparrow, the hammer); grades common upward; the food-type is tool,
  support class.
- **The titles** — Scholar 1–10 · Master 11–20 · Grandmaster 21–30 · Elder
  31–40 · Ancestor 41–50 · King 51–60 · Emperor 61–70 · Sage 71–80 · Douluo
  81–90 · TITLED 91–99 · god at 100. A ring at every tenth level; the ring
  decides nothing less than the title.
- **Soul ring** — the beast's cultivating given at the kill: a skill
  (immediate), the gate broken, the well deepened, the soul's own nature
  raised by the ring's years, the beast's properties, the body fed. Bands:
  white 10–99 · yellow 100–999 · purple 1,000–9,999 · black 10,000–99,999 ·
  red 100,000+ (two skills) · orange 200,000+ · gold 1,000,000+ (all past
  red is design-doc knowledge in our era).
- **Soul beast** — the continent's other cultivator; the forests are theirs.
- **The Spirit Hall** — the church of spirit masters: the awakenings, the
  registry, the tests, the licensed grounds, the strongest force alive.
- **The chant** — spoken in his mind, never aloud ("ripe, and given"; the
  green apple's own words came with it).
