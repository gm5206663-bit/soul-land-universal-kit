## The receipts

- **"Sausage is a top-class Food Spirit."** (1) Food-type is not a joke
  class; it is a legitimate top-class line — support-class.
- **The ring shapes the food.** (1) "It can produce various kinds of
  abilities depending on the Spirit Ring absorbed." Oscar's ladder: 1st
  ring — **recovery** (wound healing, stamina, soul power, fatigue, pain,
  satiety; "effective if consumed within 24 hours of creation" (3)); 2nd —
  **detox** (mild poisons only (1)); 3rd — **flight** (wings, one minute,
  very fast — from a Phoenix Tail Crest Serpent (1)); 4th — **buff**
  (+10% all attributes, must be eaten within one minute of production (1));
  8th — heal 50% / restore 50% stamina, costs 50% of his soul power, one
  per seven days (3); 9th — within three seconds, half of physical
  strength, soul power, and mental power restored (3).
- **Production laws.** (1) "It requires an incantation to be produced and
  has time limits for usage." Food-type output costs soul power, needs a
  chant, and spoils. (3: 24-hour effectiveness window on the recovery
  sausage; blood-mixing personalization on advanced outputs.)
- **Scaling.** (2) "His Halos expand the variety and potency of his
  sausages… Older rings turn good sustain into game-defining sustain."
  Ring AGE deepens food-type power — the fact the System's ring slots are
  built on.
- **The arc is canon-proven.** (2) "Oscar proves that a 'silly' martial
  soul can be one of the most important… sustain wins wars of attrition."
  (4) "an excellent support-type soul master in a team" — the
  mockery-to-indispensable turn is the food-type's own canon arc.
- **The ceiling is divine.** (3) Oscar's title: **Food God** — the Food God
  descended and offered him the inheritance at level 99. The food-type line
  ends at godhood.

## What these receipts became (design translation)

- The apple is a **top-class food-type tool spirit**: support-only, honest,
  and village-priceless in its base form.
- Each ring = **a new cultivar** (the beast taken shapes the effect family);
  potency scales with ring age → the System's aging slots are the apple's
  long fuse.
- Production laws held: soul-power cost, a plain incantation, a day's
  spoilage.
- The apple's own edge over the precedent: **the seed** — food-types
  output consumables; an apple outputs plantable futures. (Agent design,
  on-canon in spirit: the seed law, the orchard, the Golden Apple — see
  PROTAGONIST.md.)
- The endgame direction aligns with canon's own: the Food God inheritance
  is the food-type line's ceiling; ours passes through the orchard.
