## III. THE SYSTEM (locked)

- Not a martial soul. **Silent** — a readout, not a personality; it cannot
  ever explain itself. No shop, no quests, no points, no intelligence.
- **Slots:** 1/1/1 at Scholar → +1 per rank-up → 10/10/10 at Titled — one
  number, every list (techniques, rings, bones, and the categories to come).
- Slotted techniques train **24/7 at his best speed**; progress always kept;
  free switching; stages upgrade at 100%.
- **THE ACQUISITION LAW:** seeing once is enough — witnessed arts become
  slottable; every art still opens at its true current mastery.
- **THE PASSIVE CULTIVATION LAW:** a slotted method generates real spirit
  power around the clock.
- **THE RESONANCE LAW:** slotted things influence each other, across all
  categories — the loadout is one living formation.
- **THE AGING LAW (rings and bones):** stored-only aging, organic — the
  user's strength, the ring's own potential, bloodline, and what the world
  weigh; NO CEILING; aging never changes skill identity, only depth.
- **Panels:** printed at key beats only, minimal lines, cold and quiet. The
  System's NAME is the author's own gift, still awaited (placeholder in all
  prose: "the readout").
- **OPEN:** the second beast — Chapter 4 ends on the question; the author
  rules it, as he ruled the first.

## IV. THE STYLE LAWS

- Chapter band **2,400–3,400 words** (measured after every edit; the footer
  records honestly if a strike-pass carries it over).
- **No prose sentence over 60 words.** Average mid-to-high teens; medians low.
- The **"the way [clause]" simile construction is banned** (idioms like
  "all the way" excepted).
- Dialogue ~5–10 lines per 1,000 words.
- Numbers in prose carried freely (canon voice); **the System's digits live
  in the panels only.**
- The footer contract on every chapter: word count + metrics; chapter time;
  LAWS APPLIED; beats; PROPOSED (author may strike or keep); every
  correction recorded with its strike number.
- Editing discipline: grep the exact on-disk text before every
  assert-replace; writes sit at the end of the script (an assert failure
  means no partial writes); measure; commit early, push at the end; never
  force-push.

repo's `_archive/`. Author-gated forever: the System's name; the
forever-canon rulings; every beast he chooses to rule; anything marked OPEN
