## THE ANCHOR (PROPOSED)

**Su Ping is born the same year as Tang San.** Two villages, one continent,
one quiet generation: Holy Spirit Village's smith's son and Fruit Creek's
orchard's son are the same age, and their lives run parallel — one a comet,
one an orchard. The story opens the same season canon does: awakening year,
the boys both six.

The thesis the timeline carries: **Tang San is the comet** — innate full
power, twin souls, Titled before thirty, gone by twenty-five. **Su Ping is
the orchard** — average talent, compounding time, behind the monsters at
every age, passing everyone in the long game the comet leaves behind. When
Part 1 ends and the comet ascends, the orchard is barely at its first
flowering — and the story keeps growing into the years canon never wrote.

## THE PACING LAW (PROPOSED)

Ranks arrive by orchard arithmetic, not comet arithmetic: roughly a rank a
year through the teens (accelerating as the snowball compounds — the
cultivation-speed apple stacked on passive cultivation stacked on growing
strength), slowing where canon slows (the 40s and 60s are walls for
everyone), Titled Douluo an old man's crowning by monster standards — and
arrived at exactly when the orchard says so. Every number in the age table
is tunable at drafting; the SHAPE (behind the monsters at every age,
passing everyone in the long game) is the story's spine.

## THE CAMEO LAW (LOCKED boundary)

Paths cross once, at the elite competition, across a field — no entanglement
before or after. Canon's cast is the weather of his era, never his plot.
