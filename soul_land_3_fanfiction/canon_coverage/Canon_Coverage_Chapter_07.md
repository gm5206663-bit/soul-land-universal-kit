## Divergences logged (licensed or deliberate)

1. **Rank: canon says eleven, we say twelve.** Canon ch 34's form read gives rank 11; this serial's spine has run rank 12 since ch 1 (the licensed fracture). Chapter keeps rank 12 in the form read, in the timeline header, and in Long Hengxu's file-side speech ("a trash martial soul at rank twelve at nine years old"). Nothing else in ch 34–37 depends on the number. **Logged, not patched.** *(Tang Wulin is the boy, not the rank; the fracture stays consistent across every in-house station.)*
2. **Canon's quiet dragon-roar hint (ch 36) is not staged — deliberate.** In canon, the youth's throwing-off carries a faint dragon note that will matter later in the boy's own line. Our chapter stages his golden dagger and yellow ring as a family soul-skill (which is what the room can see) and leaves the dragon note for its own window. **Held, not erased.** To be re-checked at the ch 8+ slice; the dagger's fail-to-cut result is the beat this chapter needed.
3. **The 3,000 federal coins per semester (ch 37) is not stated.** Canon gives the academy's pocket-money figure in the verdict scene; our chapter leaves the boy's money to his savings and the coast shop's wage, because the ch 7 scene's engine is the bill, not the stipend. **Omission logged.**
4. **The campus vehicle ban (ch 35) is not stated.** Canon notes soul vehicles aren't allowed on the grounds; our bus reaches the gate and the walk is the boy's first campus mile. No rule broken on the page — the ban is simply not raised. **Soft handling, logged.**
5. **The youth's hair color is not restated.** Ch 6 logged the canon divergence (source text says brown at the station; the character's page says blonde). Ch 7 describes black clothing, fair skin, long lashes, deep-green eyes — the station boy's face without re-picking the hair. **No new divergence.**
6. **Yun Xiao's name-character breakdown (云 cloud / 小 small-big) is not narrated.** Canon introduces him with the character-by-character gag; our chapter gives it through voice and the "experiment in this room" line. **Staging shift, logged.**
