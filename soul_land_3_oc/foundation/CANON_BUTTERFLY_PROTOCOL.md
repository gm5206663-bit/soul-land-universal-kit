## 1 · The corrected understanding (our working form)

1. **Canon runs complete, in order, unskipped** (R12) — and canon scenes are written with their causes, pressure, and consequences, not as summaries.
2. **Su Yan is never a spectator in his own story.** In every shared/public scene he plausibly attends — the chamber, the queue, the classroom, the town, the market, the office, the wall — something must be **different because he is in it**, and the difference must be **on the page**.
3. **The butterflies are natural consequences of his locked existence** — the illness and its healing, the physician's good house, the grain-house mother, the sums page, the wages, Luo Sanpao and its nose, the feed and the pot, the academy seat, the high private mind. Nothing descends from nowhere.
4. **Felt, not footnoted.** A reader must *feel* the world went its own way because he lives in it — a changed habit, a moved price, a second recording, a stranger who knows him, a door that opens easier. Ledgers are receipts of what happened on the page, **never the butterfly itself** (P-13).
5. **Cause → effect, both on the page.** If a consequence needs a paragraph of explanation to be believed, it is not a butterfly; it is a claim.
6. **Time passes like weather.** Every chapter carries the season, the school's clock, the market's rounds — and the growth paid for in R18/R19 terms.
7. **Consequences are debts.** What his existence sets moving must keep moving; a line never dies quietly. Silence is not a decision.

## 2 · The canon-handling rule (run per canon chapter — goes in the coverage doc)

For every canon chapter touched, mark each beat:

**public · private · parallel · butterfly-touchable**

and answer eight questions:

1. What exactly happens in canon?
2. Who is present?
3. What is public information?
4. What is private/family-only?
5. What pressure drives the scene?
6. What changes after the scene?
7. Where can Su Yan naturally touch it (shared/public only)?
8. Where must he not intrude (private — parallel reader-view only, no earned route = no knowledge)?

**If the beat is private:** reader may see it in parallel; Su Yan must not learn it. **If the beat is public/shared and he is plausibly there:** his presence must change something — reaction, record, choice, timing, or mood — **without touching the beat's essential canon outcome.**

## 4 · The magnitude economy (his scale, adopted)

Every butterfly is one of: **dust** (a small permanent change in a life) · **current** (a standing change in a system or a room) · **bend** (an earned change in a shared course of events — with on-page cause) · **break / STORM** (setting-scale — **author-gated only; never ours to declare**).

- Our chapters may freely make **dust and current**. A **bend** needs the full cause chain on-page and a ledger row.
- **Every butterfly traces to a ledger row.** A butterfly that traces to no row is invention — label **[design]** or cut.
- **Scale-true:** this boy is quiet and small. Big effects arrive **by accumulation** — hundreds of small true things — never by display.

## 5 · What butterflies must not do

- Steal canon's center: no canon event's essential result changes; no canon character's private pillar is touched; no canon secret is ever free.
- Force rooms, trips, or scenes where logistics say he would not be (no coincidence-magic).
- Give knowledge across the firewalls (`bible/KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALLS.md`) without an earned route.
- Make the Talent a system or an all-purpose solution — the Talent works on *him and his parts*, never rearranges the world (`ADAPTATION_MODULE` v3; MF §62.3 is about causes he genuinely moves).
- Replace felt beats with footnotes; inflate a quiet boy into a spectacle; spend the seals (LSP-10 · R13/DRG-01 · NAE-02) for a cheap effect.
- Use "months passed" as a skip: time moves like weather, and each year of it is paid.

## 6 · The drill (bolted onto `REGISTER_AND_STYLE.md` §5)

**Before writing:** mark each canon beat (public · private · parallel · touchable); choose our boy's causal role **before prose**; pick the chapter's felt beats (dust/current; a bend only with the chain); check the ledger's open obligations for this window.
**After writing:** update every touched line's **Last echo**; enter each new divergence **with its obligation named at birth**; pay what fell due; mark what is deliberately not paid (a decision, dated — never silence); run the three questions:
1. What changed since the last chapter — what already did, not what is planned?
2. Which open consequences fire on those changes?
3. What does the reader now know that the characters don't — and the reverse?
**Per shared scene, add one line to the coverage doc:** *what is different here because he is in it?*

## 7 · The deletion test (the gate that fails first)

For every scene containing Su Yan or set in his plausible orbit: **read it with him deleted.** If nothing changes — the room, the record, the price, the habit, the mood all identical — the scene fails audit and is rewritten.
