## A · Luo Sanpao — the soul itself (SL1 canon, the boy's martial soul)

| Row | Fact | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| LSP-01 | **Luo Sanpao** (罗三炮) — a **Beast Martial Soul** (Power Attack System); a **mutation of the Blue Lightning Tyrant Dragon** aimed at a *higher* dragon that **failed to complete** — fandom: "not only towards a low-level mutation, but also a mutation of a higher-level dragon, but failed to succeed"; baike: "a benign mutation… the mutation halted halfway," leaving a soul "neither fish nor fowl" | canon (fandom `Luo_Sanpao`; baike EN `Luo Sanpao`) |
| LSP-02 | Unlike ordinary beast souls, it **manifests outside the body** — a separate creature. **It has a consciousness of its own.** If it is injured or poisoned, **it affects its master too.** | canon (fandom) |

| LSP-04 | Faculties: **extremely keen sense of smell** — can find spirit-beast tracks and judge their strength; **very low soul-power consumption** → it can stay released outside the body for long periods. | canon (fandom; baike) |
| LSP-05 | Its own attack is a gas release (the sources say it plainly: it attacks by "farting"), and it can attack **only three times** before it needs **nourishment** (canon feeding: white radishes); its canon cry sounds like "luo luo" — which is why it is named **Sanpao, "three shots."** **House note: the three-shot ceiling is the soul's canon baseline — how this boy's version grows past it is the story, author-gated (see LSP-08).** | canon (baike; fandom) |
| LSP-06 | **Yu Xiaogang's innate soul power: 0.5** — the recorded floor of this soul's history; the fandom soul-power table names his case: "Disadvantageous Martial Soul mutation: Luo San Pao." | canon (fandom `Soul_Power`) |
| LSP-07 | Yu Xiaogang's own rings granted him two named skills — **Thunder Shock** (1st: shock-wave) and **Mesmerize** (2nd: sleep). **House firewall: those are HIS acquisitions; this boy's skills will be his own — earned ring by ring, never copied.** | canon (fandom `Yu_Xiaogang/Abilities`) |

## B · The Awakening — SL3 canon as SL3 runs it (ch 1)

| Row | Fact | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| AW-01 | **The annual Awakening Day**; a martial soul awakens when its holder turns **six**; the ceremony is how. It is the day a family's future is decided. | canon (SL3 ch 1) |
| AW-02 | **"Only one in a thousand people might possess soul power."** Everyone has a martial soul; almost no one has the power to cultivate it. (House reading, confirmed by canon example: Huo Yuhao's innate **1** — SL2's protagonist — is a one. A one is the **door**, not a verdict.) | canon (SL3 ch 1; fandom `Soul_Power`) |
| AW-03 | Held at the **elementary academy**: the streets outside packed with parents and children; the majority leave disappointed, occasionally one family leaves overjoyed. | canon (SL3 ch 1) |
| AW-04 | The ceremony takes place in the **Awakening Chamber** — a **circular building** every elementary academy has; canon's Red Mountain Academy's chamber building has **seven floors, an awakening chamber on each**; floors, walls and ceiling carved with **inscriptions**; the parent waits outside the room. **Addenda (verified 2026-10-02, wuxiaworld `ldk-chapter-1` + fandom `Red_Mountain_Academy`):** the academy is "able to hold two thousand students"; its buildings are "simple and unadorned with white roofs and red walls"; the grounds "exceptionally clean"; canon's Wulin was brought to the **third floor** chamber; canon line *"Come here child. Stand in the middle."* | canon (SL3 ch 1) |
| AW-05 | The ceremony is conducted by a **Spirit Master from the Spirit Pagoda**, invited by the academy for the day — canon: a middle-aged man in a **simple orange robe** embroidered with the form of a soul beast. His instruction to the child: **"Come here child. Stand in the middle."** Then he begins. | canon (SL3 ch 1) |
| AW-06 | Two outcomes exist in the same room: an ordinary awakening (Wulin: **Blue Silver Grass, innate 3**) and, for a special soul, an abnormal one (Wulin's awakening brought **intense pain** and a flash of **golden veins** — noted as a sign of a powerful spirit, however the reading still came out 3). | canon (SL3 ch 1 + spoiler summary) |
| AW-07 | Soul power **nourishes and upgrades the martial soul**; only those who possess it can become Soul Masters. **The measured innate is the starting rank** (Wulin's 3 began him at rank 3; "Innate Full Soul Power" = 10; canon examples across books: 0/0.5/1/3/5/7/9/10). | canon (SL3 ch 1; fandom `Soul_Power`) |
| AW-08 | The sky was expected at the top of the ladder: rank 10 is a **bottleneck** — the first soul ring is required to advance past it. | canon (fandom `Soul_Power`; canon usage) |
