# CHAPTER TEMPLATE — the bones every chapter is built on

*No chapter is authored without the author's word (the gate: `OPEN_RULINGS` G12). This file is the checklist that runs **around** the prose; the prose rules themselves are `HOW_TO_WRITE.md` (R21) and the drill in `REGISTER_AND_STYLE.md`.*

## Before prose — on paper first

1. **The canon slice:** fetched live the same day (STRICT RULE); every beat marked **public · private · parallel · butterfly-touchable** before a word of prose (the ch5 plan in `scratch/ch5_plan.md` is the model).
2. **The OC's turns (R21):** the chapter carries **≥ 2 costed OC events** and **≥ 1 canon reaction** to him; canon's share of the words **≤ half**; the failure state comes early (STORY_METHOD §16); each scene changes a value (§17); conflict rotates (§42/43).
3. **The six lines:** confirm each moves or explicitly rests — rank · spiritual power/mind · body · the pair · the soul · the hands (`SU_YAN_STATUS` §3–§8); every movement priced in the same chapter (R14).
4. **Voice pre-check:** the targets copied forward — ALL avg **≤ 12** (aim 8–10), median ≤ 8, **0 over 60**, no "the way" past 2, dialogue-forward, **CJK 0**, no stat-speak.
5. **Seals copied forward** from `SU_YAN_STATUS` §13 (LSP-10 · R13/DRG-01 · NAE-02 · R5 · WUL-01/04 · Q-2) and the butterfly obligations that fall due this chapter (`BUTTERFLY_LEDGER`).

## Header — exactly two blocks, no more

> **Canon reference:** SL3 chapter(s) — the beats carried; fetched live YYYY-MM-DD (slug).
> **Timeline:** the age and season; the edge the chapter opens.

## Body

Plain register; the dual track (canon adapted whole, in order, unskipped — the OC's line does its own work in the foreground). Scene ownership (R12): canon's scenes stay canon's; the OC stands in them only where the town's own logic puts him. Close on a load-bearing line — the count, the door, the ask.

## After prose — same turn, all of it

- **Measure:** `python3 tools/measure_sl3p.py <chapter>` — fix every over-60 and every "the way" past 2.
- **Receipts:** `canon_coverage/Canon_Coverage_Chapter_0N.md` + `audits/Chapter_0N_audit.md` (with the **R21 tests first**: actor · reaction · event count).
- **Refresh (the drill 5c set):** `SU_YAN_STATUS` (the win-copy) · `RELATIONSHIPS` · `TIMELINE` · `CANON_CHARACTER_STATE` · `GROWTH_LOCKS` §6 row · `BUTTERFLY_LEDGER` (fired + born, obligations named at birth) · `SERIAL_LOG` · `NEXT_STEPS` · `CHANGELOG`; `MISTAKES_LEDGER` if anything was learned.
- **H14 before delivery:** every claim in every panel and receipt is **line-checked against the chapter it cites** — the pages are the source; memory is not.
- **Commit** in the house style.
