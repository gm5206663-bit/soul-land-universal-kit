# NO_MISTAKE LIVE RULES — *One in a Thousand* (armed 2026-10-02, founded)

> The standing rails this serial is written under, in the form that bites. **Author's word outranks every line.** Each rail is a default unless the author rules otherwise — except where marked **FIREWALL** (house strikes, author-endorsed).

## R1 · CANON-FIRST (the STRICT RULE)
Before any canon-touching passage: fetch the source page **and** check this spine — both, always; receipt it in `canon_coverage/`. Never cite canon from memory. New facts enter `foundation/CANON_SPINE.md` as rows with their fetch date. *Nothing ships on an unfetched claim.*

## R2 · THE WORLD-TEXTURE LAW **(earned 2026-10-02, the strike that founded this serial)**
A chapter can be canon-clean, well-written, and still fail — if it reads like the real world instead of **Soul Land**. Every chapter: the power system on-page as canon runs it (Awakening Day, the Chamber, the Pagoda's spirit master, soul power, ranks, the ladder); soul-master culture visible (souls, rings when they come, academies, the era's institutions); no real-world naturalism standing where the fantasy's furniture belongs. **If a chapter of this serial could be mistaken for a mundane drama, it fails — whatever its numbers say.**

## R3 · THE SPEC IS LOCKED (author's core)
Same year as Tang Wulin · good family · **Luo Sanpao** · **innate 1** · Adaptation Talent (Tier 0 weak/spent; Tier 1 stronger) · recovered just before the awakening. Nothing is added to this spec — no second soul, no hidden bloodline gift, no destiny voice, no shortcut. The deviation family ("make him special-er") is the enemy.

## R4 · THE SOUL'S CANON LAW **(FIREWALL)**
Luo Sanpao per LSP rows: manifests **outside** the body; **its own consciousness**; its injuries transfer to its master; **pale purple, gentle-eyed, dog-faced hog**; **keen nose**; **cheap to keep out**; its own strike is a gas attack with the canon **three-shot ceiling** and hunger after. **The boy's named combat skills will be his own — earned ring by ring. Yu Xiaogang's skills are NOT his** (LSP-07 firewall). **The completion horizon (LSP-08: the Golden Sacred Dragon) is never named in prose, never foreshadowed aloud, never promised.**

## R5 · THE TALENT IS UNNAMED **(FIREWALL)**
Never in prose as a name, a system, a voice, or a readout. It reads as consequence: he survives what he shouldn't; he learns what others don't; he recovers, corrects, endures. The boy knows only what a boy knows.

## R6 · THE FAMILY'S LAW
A **good** family, warm, ordinary, proud of him in the specific way they choose moment by moment. No misery-porn; no saint-parents; the world's pity (and the relatives' arithmetic) is the pressure — love is the answer, not the price. The Creed (LSP-09) is not quoted until the story earns the right.

## R7 · THE FUTURE IS HELD
Canon figures (Wulin, canon classmates, adversaries) stay out of the light until the author opens the door (G1/G6). Canon's events belong to the world and to their own story; this serial spends none of them early. The reader may know what Luo Sanpao's history holds; the story may not.

## R8 · THE REGISTER GATE (per chapter, before ship)
Plain language; measured (avg ≤ 25, **no sentence over 60**, dialogue-forward where people exist); no meta-narration; **no count-numbers as stat-speak**; **CJK 0 in prose** (annotations live in the files); no copyrighted text. Numbers posted in `audits/` with the chapter.

## R9 · THE LEDGER LAW
Growth may be earned, fed, inherited, or granted — "priced" is the house taste and the default, not physics (author, 2026-09-29: *"everything possible, nothing is absolute"*). What is absolute: **the bookkeeping** — every real movement of state logs its cause and its price.

## R10 · THE SISTER LAW
The author reads the chapters; the files never argue with him. Veto = one word = the files change the same turn, receipts written, no silent edits.

- **Held beats:** the recovery's unexplained timing (the family's unanswered question) · the soul's three-shot ceiling and its nose (canon faculties, spent slowly) · the horizon (LSP-08) · Wulin.
