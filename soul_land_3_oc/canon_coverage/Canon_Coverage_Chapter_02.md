## 5. Handling notes (divergences and how they are kept)

1. **The class count:** canon says the year's Soul Master class had fifteen students. In this world the roster also holds Su Yan. To keep canon's own sentence intact, **no total is printed on page**; the record difference is held here and is author-movable with one word.
2. **Free tuition:** canon ch4 states elementary and intermediate academies are free and compulsory. Chapter 1's promise ("pay the term") is reconciled as **books, coat, and the noon-meal account** — what the law does not cover. No canon line is contradicted.
3. **The childhood nickname:** the translation's "Lin Lin" is treated as a translation artifact and is not used; the mother's call is staged without it.
4. **The outside-body beast:** canon's own ch4 rule (beast = on the body) meets this boy's canon-wild soul. No character teaches or resolves it; only the reader holds the contradiction (seal discipline).
5. **Su Yan's soul is not displayed in class.** Its specialness stays unshown; no teacher doctrine, no record entry, no early reveal.
6. **Canon's own quotes are short lines only**; all other prose is ours. No passage of canon is reproduced.
