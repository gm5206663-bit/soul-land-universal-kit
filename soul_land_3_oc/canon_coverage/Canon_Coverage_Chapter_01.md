A teacher reads a name off the list — *a common name, two notes the wind takes*. Su Yan does not know him; the meeting is not a meeting. Grounding: canon SL3 ch 1 shows Wulin's family poor (patched clothing, father in shirtsleeves) and Wulin cheerful and certain that morning. Neither coat nor mood is invented. The name is never given.
