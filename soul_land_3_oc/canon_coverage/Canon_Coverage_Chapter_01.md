## 4. The Tang Wulin glimpse (G1 resolved staging)

One moment, deliberately undefined: a patched coat, a tired man in shirtsleeves, a boy laughing with a whole mouthful of teeth and certainty. Su Yan does not know him. The name is not given. The meeting is not a meeting — it is a minute of shared morning. When the two-tracks ruling asks for it later, the boy in the patched coat is already in the reader's memory.

Grounding: canon SL3 ch 1 shows Wulin's family poor (patched clothes, his father doing odd jobs) and Wulin cheerful that morning. Neither the coat nor the mood is invented.

## 5. Lines to protect (checks at each future chapter)

1. **Luo Sanpao's voice** — soft, low, *luo* syllables; never given full speech in ch 1.
2. **The illness language** — "the veins never took root" stays the *family's belief*, not a verdict. His father holds it carefully, without diagnosing.
3. **Innate one** — no self-pity scene, no destiny talk. This is a book about people who work.
4. **The examiners' ledger** — the reading is official and permanent: the brass plate + the single character. No mulligan, no rewrite.
