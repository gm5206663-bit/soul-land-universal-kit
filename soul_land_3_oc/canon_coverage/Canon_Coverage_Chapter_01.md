## 0. The two tracks (structure of v5)

**Canon track (the spine):** Tang Wulin's Awakening Day, adapted from SL3 ch1–ch2 with every canon beat carried.
**OC track (parallel):** Su Yan's own morning in the same town, the same academy, the same day — his own queue, his own chamber, his own reading.
**Where the tracks touch — exactly twice, both allowed by the morning itself:** (1) the queue — the two families stand four places apart; the boys exchange a few words and a handshake; nothing about canon changes; (2) the gate — as Su Yan's family leaves, the teacher calls *"Tang Wulin!"* and Su Yan sees him go in. **Nothing else.** Canon's scenes are otherwise untouched, in order, as the source runs them. No character gains knowledge across the tracks.

## 4. Firewalls held

1. **No knowledge transfer across tracks.** Su Yan hears only the name called at the gate; Wulin sees only a boy leaving. Neither knows the other's result.
2. **The golden lines** remain unnamed, unexplained, seen only by the master.
3. **The Creed** unquoted. **Luo Sanpao's past** (Yu Xiaogang, the sealed gold, the completion horizon) untouched — the master's stillness and one old word are all.
4. **No futures spent.** No rank-ten material, no spirit souls, no academy entry, no Wulin's family home.
