## 6. Handling notes (the deliberate choices)

1. **No meeting inside canon's scene (R12).** The Su family's visit to the counter is a *separate day, separate hour, same counter*; canon's day (movement Two) is played whole and alone. The two families are never in the hall together; nobody in canon's scene knows Su Yan exists. The contact is structural — the same wall, the same master, the same clerks — not personal.
2. **The wall is canon's own price** — 70,000 · 1,000,000 · 30,000 — spoken by canon's master and then written into the Su house's book; **no new price is invented**, and no number appears in the OC's line that the week does not earn.
3. **The bands vs canon's slip:** the chapter renders **Spirit Origin 1–50 with 38 in the advanced band**, and puts canon's own line for the strength (*"You have the best spiritual power of every Soul Scholar I have tested in the past several years"*).
4. **The gold stays canon-own** (WUL-01/04): the world glimpsed under the helmet is shown exactly as canon shows it — no witness, no naming, no narration wink; the page never connects it to the Su line.
5. **The defective is rendered as canon renders it** — cheap, harmless, common, useless, the drum's hundredth slot, twenty-four hours; canon's explanation carried as canon gives it (*"They made spirit souls out of genes that never came from a soul beast at all"*); the pity and the tears are the family's, and the reader's, on the page.
6. **No idle stretch:** the OC's week is four costed beats (the fee from the tin; the father's errand; the reckoning with its law; the paid night-job), each with a price and a change.
7. **No futures spent:** nothing of the Wyrm, the first ring, or the platform appears; the chapter's last frame is a boy, a ball, twenty-four hours, and a tin.
