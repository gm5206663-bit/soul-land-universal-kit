# CANON COVERAGE — index and the fidelity method

> **Purpose:** how this serial proves it carries canon whole, in order, on the page — and where every chapter's proof lives. The law: **R12 · THE DUAL-TRACK LAW** (canon complete on the page; the OC runs parallel, never instead). The strikes that made it: ch 1 v1 *"pure nonsense"*; the predecessor *"you completely can't create fen fiction without following canon."*

## 1 · The method (each chapter, every time)

1. **Fetch live, read fully** (R1) — the chapter's canon block, from the working source (register row 1: `wuxiaworld.com/novel/legend-of-the-dragon-king/ldk-chapter-N`, live chunk list). No canon from memory, ever.
2. **List the beats** — every scene, choice, and named detail of the canon block, in order, including the small ones (a coin count, a scolding, a lunch bag).
3. **Adapt all of them** — the beats run *on the page*, in order, unskipped. Compression is a retelling choice; omission is a fidelity failure. Nothing is demoted to a footnote.
4. **Run the OC parallel** — what our boy is doing in the same days; scenes out of canon's window; **never crowding or rerouting canon's beats**.
5. **Write the coverage doc the same turn** — `canon_coverage/Canon_Coverage_Chapter_0N.md`: which SL3 chapters covered, source lock, beat-by-beat table (canon beat → how it is carried on our page), fidelity notes, and what is deliberately **not** touched.
6. **Receipt the ledger row(s)** — `SERIAL_LOG` line; ledgers updated; audit written (`audits/Chapter_0N_audit.md`); **one commit**.
7. **Re-measure** after the last text edit; receipts must equal the final numbers (`REGISTER_AND_STYLE` §5; `MISTAKES_LEDGER` row H2).

## 4 · Standing fidelity notes

- **The clock wins:** when our pages and canon's days collide, canon's calendar is law — reconcile our clause, never canon's scene (precedent: ch2's softened evening clause, LOG 018; `MISTAKES_LEDGER` H1).
- **The record shows:** where canon measures (the Chamber, the office, the school), our boy's reading is **exact, absent-with-reason, or a locked private note** (R14) — ch 1's plate, ch 3's records beat.
- **No sidelining** (author): canon is not a checklist and Su Yan is not a spectator — his parallel must *do* work in each chapter while canon breathes (`CANON_SPINE` §C; `MISTAKES_LEDGER` strike 8).
- **Parallel, not overlapping:** the two lines can be in the same town, the same crowd, the same week — but they never take each other's scenes (no knowledge, no saves, no crossings) — seals per `bible/KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALLS.md`.
- **Do not re-stage:** the ch3 propagation wave is applied (coverage3, NEXT_STEPS, LOG 018, G12, spine FND/NAE) — new chapters get *new* receipts, not retro-edits.
