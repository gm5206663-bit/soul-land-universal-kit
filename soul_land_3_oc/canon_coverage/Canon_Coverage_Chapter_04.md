## 6. Handling notes (the deliberate choices)

1. **Rank held, no crossing, no print.** Four canon chapters, no measurement scene anywhere canon-side; the private curve moves (stage receipts in the ledgers) but the boy's count holds at **nine ranks to go** — motif intact, no chapter measure invented (R14: numbers only where earned). The first public spiritual-power test stays in canon's later hands.
2. **The golden traces are canon's own** — Wulin's spine (ch9) and forehead (ch11), shown as canon shows them: unseen by the boy, unnoticed by the father, entirely un-named, un-theorized (WUL-01/WUL-04; R13). The father's suspicion is kept exactly canon-shaped: *a soul that heals* — Bluesilver Grass "can't" — a question kept open, never answered on our page.
3. **Na'er (NAE-02):** shown as canon shows her — the smile, *"am I stupid?"*, the spoon, the promise — and never named, hinted at, or suspected beyond that; no narration winks; no character theorizes.
4. **The door talk:** the mother's refusal (*"I don't care what kind of master he is…"*) and the father's reasoning carried through the wall, as canon sets that scene — the words that reach the children's table are the children's share.
5. **Mang Tian's row first (R17):** register row 38 written the same turn as the chapter (row before prose), with the six-star rank, the trial, the trial's true design, and the forging doctrine.
6. **No skip spent:** canon ch12's *"Three Years Later"* is not touched; nothing from later chapters (the six-year awakening, the night the girl leaves, the first ring, the smith's ranking) appears even as a hint.
