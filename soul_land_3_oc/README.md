## The premise in one breath

A boy of **Tang Wulin's year** — a good family, a small city, a father who is its healer — spends his first six years losing a war against his own body. The thing that keeps him alive spends everything it has doing it; there is nothing left over for anything else. The spring before the Awakening Day, he gets well, and his family dares to hope. Then the ceremony finds the bill: a martial soul out of the oldest stories in the world — **Luo Sanpao**, the half-finished dragon, the soul the world still jokes about twenty thousand years after the Grandmaster carried it — and **innate soul power: one.**

The world knows this soul. History wrote down what it cost the last man who carried it. What history never saw was the other thing in this boy — the one that spent six years on survival and is only now, the author has ruled, **getting started.**
