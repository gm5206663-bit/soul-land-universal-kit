## I · THE SELF

**1 · Martial soul.** *Is:* innate supernatural manifestation; everyone has one; awakens ~age 6 at the ceremony, where innate soul power is also measured (most awaken with none). *Work:* the cultivation vessel — rank is counted in soul power, but the soul carries attributes, form, bloodlines, potential; rings, skills, physique, spiritual power, method, and comprehension all modify its expression. Mutation, evolution, bloodline awakening, and second awakening can transform it later. *Gates:* whether you cultivate at all; what you can become. *Classes (verified):* by form — **Beast** (possesses the body; degree of fusion = how much power comes out), **Tool** (manifests outside; weapons, treasures, plants, food, elemental), **Body** (rare; a part of the body itself; second awakenings graded Iron/Bronze/Silver/Gold). By role — Power Attack, Control, Agility, Defense, Auxiliary, Food, Healing, Spiritual. By quality — **Trash → High-Level → Super** (incl. Ultimate, God-Bestowed, Divine Beast with sealed bloodlines) and **Mutant** (incl. Evil, **Twin** [second soul raised later], Companion). *Canon example:* Blue Silver Grass, the trash soul that can become the Blue Silver Emperor; Luo Sanpao — a Beast soul that manifests **outside** the body, which canon itself calls particularly unusual. *Here:* Su Yan's soul is Luo Sanpao. The external manifestation is canon-rare; his rank-1 reading is a receipt, not a verdict.

**2 · Soul power (魂力).** *Is:* the energy every master cultivates to raise rank and nourish/upgrade the martial soul; counted 1–99 (cap ~120 in late SL3 after the planet's upgrade). *Work:* fuels skills, pressure, short-range communication, pushing/pulling; powers soul devices. *Gates:* everything measured — academies, rings at bottlenecks (10/20/30…), stipends, titles. *Costs/limits:* easy for the first 40 ranks, then **compression** becomes the wall; ranks 50–60 are the famous watershed (Tang Hao: five years from 50→60 was considered fast); **concentration level** (SL3: quality over quantity — each level ≈ a rank; e.g., level-6 concentration on a 4-ring base reads overall ≈ Soul King; climbing past level 5 is brutal); at rank 70 a **soul core** can form, and a second at Titled Douluo — even its embryo removes bottlenecks to 95. *Canon examples:* Wulin innate 3, rank 10 at six; Yuhao innate 1. *Here:* the number is the world's lock on him — never his engine.

**3 · Spiritual power.** *Is:* mind-and-soul energy (distinct from soul power and blood essence), concentrated in the **Spiritual Sea** — which stores parts of the soul and houses the spirit souls. *Work — author's order:* **memory · intelligence · comprehension** (memory lives in the sea; thinking and inspiration scale with it; law comprehension is itself a spiritual cultivation method), then perception/sensing, control over soul skills, mind offense/defense, and the **spirit-soul gate** (soul's grade ↔ spiritual power; ring grade ↔ body). *Gates:* spirit-soul ceiling, precision professions, later domains/cores. *Costs:* slow at the top; trainable via meditation, pressure, skills, skull bones, treasures, and **herbs/materials that nourish the Sea**. *Canon example:* Wulin's 38 at rank 10 = intermediate Spirit Origin → a permitted 100-year soul; Thought Concretization projects past memories (quality scales with spiritual power); damaged spiritual power can erase portions of memory (Yuhao). *Here:* Su Yan's private 33 — a mind whose memory, intelligence, and comprehension run ahead of the record, in the band canon says can already carry a yellow soul. Full doctrine: `SPIRITUAL_POWER_LEDGER.md`.

**4 · Blood essence & bloodline.** *Is:* the body's vitality and the inheritance line — the third stat every master carries (with soul power and spiritual power). *Work:* bloodlines express as **suppression** (a stronger bloodline weakens the weaker-blooded side) and **enforcement** (a stronger bloodline can boost a weaker-blooded ally) — canon's own mechanism. *Gates:* power ceilings and identity; the deepest layer. *Canon lines:* Golden Dragon King (destruction/physical half; spear; bloodline seals), Silver Dragon King (elements/kindness half; Na'er → Gu Yuena; the two combined in SL3), Blue Lightning Tyrant Dragon (pinnacle true dragon; famously high mutation rate in its clan; only a few fully unlock it). *Here:* the three-child resonance (Wulin · Na'er · Su Yan) is sealed (**R13**); direction — Su Yan's strengthening bloodline touches theirs (**DRG-01**) — and enforcement gives that a canon-shaped engine.

**5 · Physique & body.** *Is:* the vessel everything flows through. *Work:* carries rank progress (bottlenecks are bodily too), gates high-age absorption (a strong body is required alongside spiritual power), feeds skills. *Costs:* injury/illness leave real marks; recovery is bought with food, medicine, rest. *Here:* his body failed first — the soul-veins failed — and was quietly rebuilt for six years.

**6 · Will, mind, comprehension.** *Is:* the fourth axis of cultivation. *Work:* breakthroughs need more than energy — canon: progress comes from a breakthrough in soul power, or in comprehension of laws, intent, spiritual power; the mind governs correction, control, method. *Here:* his unseen tutor.

**7 · Awakening ceremony & innate soul power.** *Is:* the age-six rite; soul manifests, innate power measured (0–10; 10 = Innate Full Soul Power; most awaken with none). *Work:* sets the public starting line; opens or closes academy lists; begins the record. *Canon examples:* Wulin 3; Yuhao 1; Tang San 10. *Here:* Su Yan's 1 was read under the brass plate for the whole town; nobody asked his spiritual power — the ceremony measures soul power only.

## II · THE MILESTONES

**8 · Soul rings.** *Is:* remains released by a soul beast at death, sacrifice, or contract; only the one who lands the killing blow can absorb it; absorption only at a bottleneck rank. *Work:* one skill per ring (house law L-08: 100,000-year rings give two; million give four); attributes scale **quantitatively** by the beast's cultivation — canon's own arithmetic (a suited 10-year ring ≈ +10 to a trait, a 100-year ≈ +100). *Costs:* historically = killing what you can face; in SL3 the beasts are nearly gone, so rings come from spirit souls. *Here:* rank 10 → first ring → the Wyrm moment.

**9 · Spirit souls (魂灵) — the era's core.** *Is:* a soul beast's energy form bound to a master — animal, plant, tool, material, elemental; it changes form, not existence; spiritual + life origin bind to you. *Work:* **it carries your rings** — 10y→1 white max; 100y→2 yellow; 1,000y→3 purple; 10k→4 black; 100k→4–5 red; 200k→orange; 1M→gold (divine beast). Upgrades happen mainly in the **Spirit Ascension Platform**. **Bloodline merge both ways:** it merges its bloodline into you (high compatibility can evolve/awaken your martial soul) and receives counter-resonance from your growth — one canon case grows literally off *funded food* and rank. *Costs:* money (70,000 / 1,000,000 / 30,000 lottery with defective odds); spiritual power + body gate grade; **24h to absorb or it dies**; if the soul leaves, those rings are lost forever; battle use risks it and your spiritual power. *Types:* Defective · Unyielding (prideful; forced fusion life-threatening) · Inherited (no rings; boosts a special trait) · Companion (mutual sincere contract) · Bonded (the first, growing with you) · Orange. *Rules:* under 10k = no intelligence; above purple can speak to the host; four ways in — free first soul for exceptionally fast geniuses, purchase, mutual contract, Tower Attack. *Institution:* the Spirit Pagoda runs it all (Huo Yuhao + Di Tian, from Electrolux's technique; Snow Empress first). *Here:* when the Sun-Branded Wyrm comes, this is the machinery that prices and carries it; Luo Sanpao's bond follows the same law-shape — one row apart from the adaptation module.

**10 · Soul bones.** *Is:* crystallized beast remains absorbed into the body — skull, torso, arms, legs. *Work:* grant skills and strengthen; skull-type bones especially feed spiritual power. *Costs:* extremely rare; high-age beasts only; era scarcity. ⧗ depth check at first use. *Here:* horizon, not early arc.

**11 · Soul core.** *Is:* condensed soul power formed at rank 70; a second at Titled Douluo. *Work:* the high-rank engine; a second core's embryo removes bottlenecks to 95. *Here:* far horizon — but "scatter condensed into something solid" is the shape of everything Su Yan quietly does.

## III · THE WORLD'S RULES

**12 · The ladder.** Ranks easy → 40; compression wall; 50–60 watershed; 91–95 the easy part of Titled, then chasms — over 95% of Titled never reach Hyper; 99% of Hyper never reach Limit. Cap 99 (120 after the SL3 upgrade; the planet can't host 120+). Spiritual-power tier gates: Titled needs Spirit Abyss (almost all have Spirit Domain), Hyper needs Spirit Domain, Limit needs its peak. *Here:* growth is never nerfed, never fiat — every stage gets page time (**R14–R16**).

**13 · Suppression & enforcement.** As row 4; the reason a child's presence can matter to stronger-blooded people without touching canon's outcomes.

**14 · Lifespan & Flame of Life.** ~100 years for non-Titled; up to ~300 at rank 99. Soul King and above may ignite their **Flame of Life** for a burst of power — burning life force and aptitude; death follows fast. *Here:* the world's stake-raiser, used only when earned.

**15 · Dark paths.** Dark Spirit Masters use dark methods for faster growth than normal (even faster than spirit-beast-turned-human), at the price of corruption and a harder climb at high ranks. Exists in canon; awareness only in our early arcs.

## IV · SOCIETY & ECONOMY

**16 · The profession.** Soul Masters are the noblest profession; registered; stipend **1,000 federal coins/month**, barely covering intermediate-academy tuition, rising at Grandmaster. SL3 splits Battle vs Utility masters; spiritual masters are the spiritual-power specialists.

**17 · Academies.** Elementary (three years) → intermediate → advanced; advanced requires a second profession — blacksmithing, mecha designing, mecha mechanic, mecha manufacturing — to build battle armor and/or mecha. Shrek Academy is the legendary apex. *Here:* the coming arc — a school where numbers get compared out loud.

**18 · Spirit Pagoda.** Records (including spiritual power and the first soul), sells souls, runs the Spirit Ascension Platform and Tower Attack — the era's gatekeeper with a near-monopoly on souls. *Here:* the record is a story object — it holds what the town doesn't know.

**19 · Mecha & battle armor.** Soul devices let ordinary humans match a Soul Grandmaster; a mecha equals a Soul King; a Soul Sage in battle armor can fight a normal Titled Douluo. Advanced students pick a craft; Duo Mecha Masters (armor + mecha) are elite, expensive in time and materials. *Here:* era flavor and later a real option layer; ⧗ deeper check at first use.

**20 · Money.** Federal coins; souls at 70,000 / 1,000,000 / 30,000 lottery; 1,000/month stipend; school fees, medicine, herbs, food. *Here:* the price list is the pressure table of the whole early story (**D-07** feeding economy).

**21 · Food, medicine, nourishment.** Food-type souls are rare, hard to cultivate, and strategic (a Soul Grandmaster sustains ~100 people; rank 30 feeds 500+). Beast-soul bodies burn food as fuel (canon hunger). Herbs and medicine nourish body, blood — and even the Spiritual Sea. *Here:* the family's kitchen and clinic are mechanical power, not scenery (mother's cooking; father's medicine).

## V · NATURE & HISTORY

**22 · Soul beasts.** The source of rings, and of spirit souls; by SL3 nearly extinct — which is why the era runs on manufactured souls (later eras: hunting banned, artificial souls capped at 10k). *Here:* the reason the modern economy exists at all.

**23 · Gods & the cap.** Rank 99 → godhood needed inheritance and trials; the Divine Realm vanished (SL2.5), freezing mortals at 99; SL3's planetary upgrade raised the cap to ~120, comparable to gods up to First Class — but not true gods. ⧗ politics/timeline depth at use.

**24 · Dragon lines (sealed).** Golden Dragon King / Silver Dragon King / Blue Lightning Tyrant Dragon — as row 4. Never named in prose before canon does (**R13**).
