## 1 · What the Talent is — the whole of it

**Classification:** Inseparable Recursive Whole-Existence Adaptive Evolution. **Definition (§2):** an innate, pure, inseparable, non-sentient existential principle present from conception that "continuously uses the holder's real existence, experience, information, resources, intentions, recovery, relationships, and accessible reality to **preserve, refine, integrate, personalize, and evolve the holder's complete being** along the best presently supportable path." It is not a system, AI, voice, or automatic-training cheat (IATS separate, §28). **It is not one job: it is one law with many faces.**

**Three domains (§10):**

1. **Direct domain — his own existence, every part.** Body and biology · **genetics, race, bloodline** · brain and nervous system · mind, cognition, memory · emotions and personality · instinct and habit · **the soul/spirit layer where real** · will and identity · senses · energy systems · powers, techniques, skills · healing, resistance, immunity. "This is where adaptation is deepest and most automatic."
2. **Interface domain — what is genuinely connected without becoming him:** bonded living things and **symbionts, spirits, summons** · bonds (family, team) · equipment · institutions. Here the Talent works on his **compatibility, communication, use, protection, synchronization, understanding, contribution** — and the other being keeps its own agency. **Luo Sanpao lives exactly here — and precisely because it is his soul made manifest (soul bond, living interface), the work on it is real and deep; it is also a *part of him*, not a project bolted on.** It keeps its own consciousness (canon): partner, never puppet.
3. **Authored derivative domain — horizon only:** a sufficiently advanced holder who possesses the power, knowledge, authority, and control may *create* — bounded adaptive forces, adaptive bloodlines or tendencies, empowerments, avatars, constructs, self-repairing gear, adaptive systems, domains. Products of earned reach, never of this era.

**The twelve faces (§12):** maintenance · developmental · directed · reactive · anticipatory · integrative · recovery · exploratory · environmental · relational · evolutionary · recursive. (It maintains while it refines; it anticipates; it integrates body–soul–bloodline–power; it recovers; it explores; it adapts to climate, city, and system; it deepens through relationships; it reconstructs at breakthroughs; it gets better at adapting.)

**The ladder (§25):** latent revelation → refinement → specialization → recombination → derivation → synthesis → pathway construction → permanent evolutionary ability → personal law → authored derivative. **"Cannot create from nothing" never means "can only improve old things" (§75.13).**
