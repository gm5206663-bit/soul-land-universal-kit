# HOUSEHOLD LEDGER — the money, and the kitchen that moves it

> **Purpose:** the author's own bookkeeping law (R9: *the money is real*) applied to the Su house: what the family has, what it spends, how the feed is paid for, and every figure that has stood on a page so far. **No misery-porn (R6):** the money is pressure, not the story's price; love is the answer, not the cost. **No page prints a price a scene did not earn** (R14).

## 1 · Standing (fixed)

- **The house:** above the clinic; a stove room, a shelf of books, three lives and now a fourth mouth that does not sit at the table. **Good, not rich.** Six sick years bled the cushion; the recovery season spent what was left into hope. No clan treasures, no elite backing.
- **Income:** the father's clinic (the city's physician — fees, medicines, calls; ordinary practice, steady) and the mother's hand on the grain-house books (her family's warehouses; rates, seconds, seasons).
- **The house's own line, said at doors and at the table:** *"We are still richer than most."* (Zhou Hui — motif.)

## 3 · The shapes the ledger keeps (for future chapters)

- **Weekly:** market line (fish row · radish basket · extras) + feed line (the pot's ingredients) — the boy's own sums page mirrors both, in his handwriting, in the back of the practice book.
- **Monthly:** the term's incidentals, medicine stores, the lamp oil, the small repairs (a soul-machine man's house, a physician's stores).
- **Seasonal:** the winter coat, fuel, the sick season's extra calls at the clinic (both income and drain).
- **Rule:** a chapter may print one price at most, and only where the scene earns it; the running shape stays here. When the numbers of a scene are invented, they are invented **once** and recorded here, so the next chapter can add to them without contradicting.

## 4 · The kitchen line (his mother's craft — author directive)

What Zhou Hui actually teaches, shown as behavior: knife-work (the two corrections she gave and never gave again), **broth** (bones, water to cover, a long low simmer, lid cracked), steaming and timing, the arithmetic of a pantry (what stretches, what must be fresh, what a second-grade crate is worth). The kitchen is **mechanical power**: a soul's food made digestible, a body's recovery cooked in, a household's week balanced on a pot. Canon shadows this at the Tang house too (Lang Yue, who *"could cook like a woman born humming"*) — **two kitchens, both feeding a growing thing** (motif).

## 5 · The rules this ledger serves

**R9** (bookkeeping absolute) · **R14** (numbers exact where measured; printed only when earned) · **D-07** (the feeding economy: as much as the family can afford, and no more) · **R6** (no misery-porn) · the sums page is the reader's window into it, and the boy's own discipline is the receipt.
