## R23 · THE GROUNDING LAW (armed 2026-10-03)

**No original element enters this story without a reason that can be stated in one line.** A reason is one of exactly four things:

1. **Canon** — the element exists in SL3 (or SL1–SL2) and we are using it correctly, cited.
2. **The author's lock** — an explicit ruling (the OC spec, the house ruling, the bloodline ruling).
3. **The house's own law** — the family's stated rules (the pot, the door open when money is counted, the pig's wage).
4. **The engine** — the two things the serial runs on: **the pig's nose as trade** and **the boy's road to his first soul** (author-approved in (L-2): canon whole, the OC doing story work with stakes in every chapter).

**Everything else is cut.** If an element cannot be filed under one of the four, it does not go on the page.
