> **Why this file exists (the author's strike, 2026-10-03, verbatim):** *"Tell me what the hell he is doing three years and what adaption telent three years seriously what the hell what is nonsense… He learn nothing, what his spiritual power is joke."*

> **The answer, in one line:** the three winters were never only level growth. Rank 2 → 9 is the *smallest line* of those years. The rest of it — a whole kitchen, a whole herb cabinet, the clinic's day-book, the school's lists, a trade, a mind, and a body that stopped dying — is receipted below, and from 2026-10-03 it is **on the page** (ch 5, the three-years pass; G18).

## The house's three laws that came out of these years

1. **Feeding first** — *"Feed the pig before you feed the page."*
2. **The book is the record** — the sums page, the clinic's day-book, the father's page *The boy*.
3. **No fiat** — every rank, every coin, every lesson has a scene and a price.
