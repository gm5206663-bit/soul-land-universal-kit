## 4 · The chapter engine (the checklist a chapter passes before it ships)
1. **Name the movement** — hard (a measurable step) or soft (control, correction, knowledge, recovery, relationship).
2. **Cause** — the canon-shaped method that bought it.
3. **Price** — money, time, sleep, or risk; written into the household sums and the ledgers.
4. **Behavior** — at least one scene where the reader *sees* it; never a narration of the mechanic.
5. **Receipts** — ledger lines and the chapter's log entry updated the same turn.
6. **No dead chapters; no futures spent** (seals, §7).
