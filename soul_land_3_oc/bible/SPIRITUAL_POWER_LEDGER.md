## 1 · What it is

- **Spiritual Power** (精神力 — "Mental Power") = the mind-and-soul energy of a living being; distinct from **soul power** (the martial-soul energy) and **blood essence** (body, vitality, bloodline).
- It is concentrated in the **Spiritual Sea** (Sea of Consciousness — the mental realm in the head), which also **stores parts of the soul**, houses a **Spirit Core** that speeds its recovery, and is **where fused spirit souls reside**.
- It develops naturally with maturity (ordinary people to ~40; Soul Masters to ~60) and is trained through: meditation · spiritual-pressure methods · spiritual-type skills · certain rings and bones (**skull-type bones** especially) · Heaven-and-Earth treasures · **herbs and materials that nourish the Spiritual Sea** · and **law comprehension itself**. Higher levels get progressively harder.
- After the first spirit-soul fusion, the **Spirit Pagoda records** spiritual power, the soul's level and type; the Federation can read a master's maximum.

## 2 · What it does — memory · intelligence · comprehension · and the others *(author order)*

**The three first:**
1. **Memory** — capacity, retention, recall. Canon: the Spiritual Sea stores parts of the soul; **Thought Concretization** displays a master's memories, with image and sound quality depending on spiritual power; and destroying separated spiritual power can make **portions of memory disappear** — memory is lived in the spiritual sea.
2. **Intelligence** — thinking, processing, judgment, reaction, inspiration. Canon: spiritual power *aids in improving thinking and having inspiration*; and intelligence scales with it in living things — a soul spirit under 10,000 years has no intelligence, and above **purple** rank one can communicate with its host.
3. **Comprehension** — insight, understanding, learning speed; the mental half of cultivation itself. Canon: progress comes from a breakthrough in soul power **or in comprehension of laws, intent, spiritual power**; **law comprehension is a spiritual cultivation method** in the SL3 era; a Spiritual Domain is formed out of comprehension of laws — and a domain contest is settled by each side's **comprehension + spiritual power**.

**And the others:**
- **Perception & sensing** — control over one's own emotions; focus; observing the surroundings; later: the minute changes inside one's body, disturbances far away, an opponent's next move, danger intuition; at Spirit Domain: reading thoughts, telepathy, distinguishing the elements in the air.
- **Control over soul skills** — precision, timing, layering; canon notes this control "begins" at **Spirit Connection**.
- **Mind offense / defense** — spiritual pressure, attacks that touch another's spiritual sea, and resistance to the same.
- **The spirit-soul gate** — the grade of spirit soul one can fuse ↔ spiritual power (soul-ring grade ↔ the body). The higher the soul's grade, the higher the mind required. **This is how a "rank one" body could one day hold a soul its record denies.**
- **Later transformations** — **Spiritual Concretization** (thoughts, memories, and emotions becoming externally perceptible near the peak of Spirit Domain) → **Divine Consciousness** beyond it.
