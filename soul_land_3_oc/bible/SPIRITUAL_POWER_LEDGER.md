# SPIRITUAL POWER LEDGER — Su Yan (苏晏)

> **Purpose:** the boy's mind has a number, and — per the author's correction, *"what is meaning of having High spritual power, what you understood no you don't, so please do, understand meaning and work and effects"* — this file first defines **what spiritual power is, what it does, what it gates, and what each band means, with canon examples**; then locks Su Yan's ledger on the **SL3-era scale**. Prose prints a number only in a scene that earns it.

## 1 · What it is (canon, verified 2026-10-02)

- **Spiritual Power** (精神力 — "Mental Power") is the mind-and-soul energy of a person: it governs **perception, mental awareness, concentration, thought, spiritual sensing, and control over spiritual phenomena** (fandom `Spiritual_Power`).
- **It is not soul power and not blood essence.** Soul power is the energy cultivated through the martial soul; blood essence belongs to body, vitality, and bloodline. Spiritual power belongs to **mind and soul** and is concentrated in the **Spiritual Sea** (Sea of Consciousness — the mental realm in the head).
- **Spirit souls live in the Spiritual Sea.** Fused soul spirits reside there; spiritual-type attacks, mental abilities, and projections originate from it.
- **It is the gate for spirit souls:** fusing an ever-stronger spirit soul requires correspondingly stronger spiritual power (canon: the ranking system was invented for exactly this, and completed only in the last ~2,000 years — the SL3 era). **Spirit-soul grade is gated by spiritual power; soul-ring grade is gated by the body.**
- **It develops independently of rank:** two soul masters of the same soul-power rank can be worlds apart in spiritual cultivation. It grows naturally with maturity (ordinary people ≈ until 40; Soul Masters ≈ until 60) and can be cultivated: **meditation · spiritual pressure · spiritual-type skills · certain soul rings and bones (skull-type bones especially) · Heaven-and-Earth treasures · herbs and materials that nourish the Spiritual Sea · comprehension.** Higher levels get progressively harder (canon).
- **After the first fusion the Spirit Pagoda records it** — strength of spiritual power, level and type of spirit soul — and the Federation can read a soul master's maximum (canon, ch17 cluster).

## 2 · The scale (SL3 era — corrected)

**Spirit Origin 1–99** (elementary 1–30 · **intermediate 31–60** · advanced 61–90 · peak 91–99) → **Spirit Connection 100–499** → **Spirit Sea 500–4,999** → **Spirit Abyss 5,000–19,999** → **Spirit Domain 20,000–49,999** → **Divine Origin 50,000–99,999** → **God King 100,000+**. (Sources vary slightly on the Connection entry — 100, or 51 in one table; the ch17-era text reads 1–99/100+, and canon's own usage is the authority. **House note: the SL4 project's 0–50 Spirit Origin is the LATER-era variant — never mix it into SL3 prose.**)

**What each band means in-world (canon):**
- **Intermediate Spirit Origin (31–60):** can support a **yellow (100-year) spirit soul** (canon: Wulin at 38 → allowed a 100-year soul; "yellow" support begins at intermediate).
- **Spirit Connection (100–499):** *"when one reaches the Spirit Connection rank, that is when their ability to control their spiritual power begins"* — control for one's own purposes; ~200 points can support a **3,000-year spirit soul**; peak-range values are the foundation for a **legendary spirit soul**; one glossary states any level of spirit soul (even orange/red) becomes possible here — with the canon nuance that fusing an orange/red soul can leave remaining growth at the Spirit Sea band.
- **Spirit Sea and above:** the spiritual sea itself transforms in scale and density; professions of extreme precision (calculation, coordination, control) and spiritual-type paths open; later, **soul cores** (a spiritual soul core, a Spiritual Dragon Core) can form in the sea.

## 3 · What having high spiritual power DOES (the meaning)

1. **It buys the spirit soul.** Grade of spirit soul ↔ spiritual power. This is the single most career-defining effect: a strong mind can fuse what a weak one cannot — and a boy whose martial soul is "trash" and whose rank is "one" can still, one day, hold a soul spirit that shouldn't be his.
2. **It controls soul skills.** Better precision, timing, and multi-layered control of every skill he'll ever learn.
3. **It widens perception and processing** — reading a room, a fight, a person; learning faster; holding focus; correcting mistakes (a Talent-shaped student is a *correcting* student).
4. **It resists spiritual attacks** and unlocks techniques that touch other minds.
5. **It feeds the impossible professions** — perception-, calculation-, coordination-, and control-heavy work (canon: "particularly important … to professions or abilities requiring exceptionally precise perception, calculation, coordination, and control").
6. **It is trainable — with material the house can actually supply** (this is why the feeding economy also feeds the mind): meditation (canon: taught on the first day of class), spiritual pressure, **herbs and materials that nourish the Spiritual Sea** — his father's clinic and his mother's grain-and-goods trade — later, skull-type bones and treasures.
