## Absolute current rules

- The serial is being REBUILT in `chapters_rebuilt/`. `chapters/` is the superseded first
  draft — readable as history, never as continuity, never as the source for a new chapter.
- One chapter per pass. Audit before starting the next.
- **He never kills.** Not hunters. Not beasts. Not the deep. No exception, no "this once,"
  no accidental kill. If a scene seems to need it, the scene is wrong.
- The Blue Silver Valley / Emperor Valley is **CANON** (user ruling 2026-09). Never call it
  a [design] name. Never write "canon nowhere names it."
- He is a **Sunweave Blue Silver Grass** — a real elemental mutant of the COMMON line. No
  royal gold, no dragon blood, no devour trait. Nothing is fused into him.
- The Adaptation Talent effects everything and never conjures from nothing. Real material:
  sun, water, soil, centuries, the dead things that fall into his ground.
- No grass network. He cannot sense through other grass. Ground-language travels through
  soil and his own root. The Empress's domain reading grass is HERS, an Emperor-bloodline
  ability, never his.
- Ritual powers stay dormant. He is never the elder King. He never performs an awakening or
  condenses anyone's ring.

## Canon never staged

- A Yin's transformation / human-form era, Tang Hao, Tang Xiao, Tang San, her sacrifice.
- The elder Blue Silver King's SL1 role (Tang San's second awakening, the 25,000-year ring).
  He stays a legend of a far region; no meeting, no name, no road.
- Spirit Hall politics invented as fact. Its *presence* in Star Dou is canon and is used;
  its internal hierarchy beyond what is verified stays vague on purpose.

## Numbers

- Exact figures live in BLADE_STATUS §0, TIMELINE, and the chapter panel. Never in prose.
- Prose uses round felt counts ("four hundred years" spoken by a hunter is fine — it is his
  speech and his arithmetic, not the narrator's ledger).
- When any anchor changes, every mirror updates the same turn.

## The spine must be visible

Every chapter carries: a canon beat, a threat, measured movement, an opposition with a face,
a butterfly, at least three registers, and a turn. A chapter that is only interior
meditation fails the contract and does not ship.

## Prose

- English only. Zero CJK anywhere, including filenames.
- No literal backslash-n artifacts.
- Upload-safe ASCII filenames only.
- Slim panel at the top. **No chapter footer** — the bookkeeping lives in the kit.
