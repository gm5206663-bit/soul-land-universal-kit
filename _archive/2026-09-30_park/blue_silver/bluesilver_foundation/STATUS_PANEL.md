race/variant, bloodline, numbers (actual age, carried cultivation, class, stage, next
gate), role, and current status — written plainly, not scattered. The narrative prose stays
literary and free of exact numbers (prose laws unchanged); the numbers live HERE, above the
chapter, in one clean panel.

**Rule:** starting with Chapter 10, every chapter file opens with this panel, placed under
the title and ABOVE the italic epigraph/curtain. It is meta-text, outside the story voice —
it is for the reader's bookkeeping, like a codex entry, never something he sees or thinks.

## Fill rules
1. **Numbers change with the ledger.** Pull the current actual age and carried
   cultivation from BLADE_STATUS §0 / §6 every chapter; never guess in the panel.
2. **Prose never repeats the numbers.** The panel is the only place exact figures may sit
   inside a chapter file. Narrative paragraphs stay free of "7,000 years", "Stage 2",
   ring colors, and calendar dates — as the prose laws require.
3. **"Carried cultivation" ≠ actual age.** Carried years are what his essence would grade
   him by (and what a ring would measure); actual age is how long he has really lived.
   Both appear, and the gap is the point of the serial.
4. **Kind row — what he is, short.** He is a **Sunweave Blue Silver Grass** — a real,
   nameable elemental mutation of the common line (every such mutation type has a name:
   his is the Sunweave): core LIFE/vitality · WOOD developed · LIGHT + WATER secondary
   via photosynthesis · EARTH partial. He is a mutant by nature — never reduce the row to
   "common grass + talent" and never "a mutant the talent only touches": BOTH are false.
   The Adaptation Talent effects EVERYTHING — it is the engine that carries a real common
   grass through real sun, water, soil, and centuries INTO a genuinely elemental Sunweave
   mutant (Master §5: it never conjures from nothing — the elements are real because the
   material is real). Formal species title still comes only by evolving: Blue Silver King
   at ~10,000. Full law: ADAPTATION_TALENT.md.
5. When a chapter crosses a **gate** (the King evolution is the big one), the panel is
   where the class/stage fields visibly change.
6. Each chapter's SERIAL_LOG entry copies the panel's numbers so the log stays a true
   record.
