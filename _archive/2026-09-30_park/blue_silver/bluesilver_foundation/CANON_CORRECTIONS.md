## What I had WRONG (nonsense to purge from prose/status)
1. **"Grazing-deterrent that turns the herd aside by age 40."** FALSE. Plant-type soul
   beasts generally cannot protect themselves until ~1,000 years of cultivation (Tree
   Monster page states this for plant beasts broadly; animals fight from ten years, plants
   not until ~a thousand). A 40-year-old grass having a repelling "skill" contradicts canon
   plant weakness and the pacing of beast power. **Fix: no deterrent; grazers still crop
   him.**
2. **"The glade's beasts stare at / avoid his patch by 40; badger pauses, birds land
   oddly."** FALSE timing. A young soul beast's presence does not command the forest's
   notice. His true age ~40 puts him at the bottom of the beast ladder regardless of his
   strangeness. **Fix: he stays effectively invisible for a long age.**
3. **"Crossed the hundred-year gate at ~40."** FALSE mechanics. Beast tiers are cultivation
   classes: ten-year (10–99), hundred-year (100–999), thousand-year (1,000–9,999),
   ten-thousand-year (10,000–99,999), hundred-thousand-year (100,000+). There is no
   "hundred-year gate"; and calling his essence "100-year grade at 40" was made-up
   shorthand. **Fix: speak in canon cultivation classes only.**
4. **The grass "network hum" as a living presence that "paused / sensed him back."**
   FALSE. Ordinary Blue Silver Grass has no soul power and no mind; it is not connected to
   anything. Gathering "life force and information" of surrounding Blue Silver Grass is an
   **Emperor-domain ability** (A Yin's Blue Silver Domain, canon), not a property of grass
   itself. **Fix: no sentient carpet. His loneliness is real — his kindred are truly
   nothing.** (Network sensing belongs to his far-future royal-domain arc, if earned.)
5. **"Faint pale sheen as if holding light" at 40; royal-looking markers.** FALSE timing.
   Royal grass shows difference across ages; a 40-year-old showing luminous markers is
   power-inflation. **Fix: no visible sheen in the first centuries.**
6. **"Essence draw makes the air heavier around him."** FALSE. No canon basis for ambient
   pressure at low tier. **Fix: remove.**
7. **Scar-ledger "layer under layer like rings" implying permanent compounding "armor"
   each event.** Overstated as usable defense. His adaptation remembers and leans — but
   canon plant fragility means those leannings do not become combat abilities for a very
   long time. **Fix: keep memory/leaning internal; physical hardening stays minor for
   centuries.**

## Correct canon model (verified; this is what the story now runs on)
### Soul-beast growth
- Beast power is graded by **cultivation years, not actual age** ("xiuwei years are not the years actually lived").
  Cultivation rises by absorbing heaven-and-earth essence (plants: light, soil, water) and,
  for some, by devouring or lucky environments. [Baike Soul Beast; Soul Land wiki Soul Beast]
- Cultivation classes: 10 / 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 years, etc. Ring colors track
  these (white/yellow/purple/black/red). Power roughly maps to human tiers only at high
  classes (100k ≈ Titled Douluo; 10k ~ upper 60s–95; thousand ~ 40–60; hundred ~ 20s–30s).
  [Baike; zhihu mapping — fanon scale, used only as feel]
- **Intelligence is slow for every beast, worse for plants.** Verified canon rule: young
  soul beasts are far less intelligent than humans and mature slowly; only around **100,000
  years of cultivation** do they reach human-grade intellect and the ability to speak; a
  10,000-year beast may still have the intellect of a small child (canon example: the Pink
  Mist Lady Queen). Royal/unique bloodlines — and heaven-and-earth treasures — are the
  exceptions that speak and think early (the 85,000+ Blue Silver King is fully articulate;
  the regrown royal A Yin converses with her family in SL1 while still short of her former
  power). An earlier note claimed "even Emperor-grade Ah Yin needed 10,000+ years to regain
  basic wisdom (donghua ep. 122)" — that quote is UNVERIFIED and conflicts with the novel's
  royal-exception scenes (SL1 ch.266); it is removed and not reused. [Soul Land wiki Soul
  Beast: Intellect; SL1 ch.266]
- **Bottlenecks and tribulations exist at great thresholds**; a 100,000-year beast that does
  not take human form faces tribulation and, in SL1-era terms, a hard life-limit beyond.
  [Baike Soul Beast]

### Plant-type soul beasts
- Plants grow slower and are more fragile than animals when young; **~1,000 years of
  cultivation before a plant can protect itself** (general rule, Tree Monster page).
- Abilities arrive by species and cultivation: tree monsters attack at ~1,000, grow feet at
  ~3,000, eyes at ~10,000 (each eye adds strength). Evil mutations can devour to speed up.
  [Tree Monster]
- Plant life force is exceptional (a plant beast's greatest gift), but that means
  survivability/vitality, not early power. [SL1 ch.266: plant vs animal difference = life
  force]
- Rootedness is species-specific: the Blue Silver King is rooted and immobile even at
  85,000+ years; tree monsters can walk from ~3,000. Grass is rooted by nature. [Blue
  Silver King; Tree Monster]

### Blue Silver Grass specifically
- The commonest, most resilient plant on the continent; **ordinary grass has no soul power
  and never becomes a soul beast**. Grass cultivating into a soul beast at all is
  vanishingly rare (it is eaten, overshadowed, and "grass rarely cultivates"). [Baike;
  Blue Silver Grass wiki; sina fan writeup of the King's rarity]
- A grass that does endure for ages climbs the grass ladder:
  - Blue Silver **King** (Blue Silver King): a grass that cultivated for tens of thousands of years —
    the known SL1 King is **85,000+ years**, vine-bodied, rooted, human-face marks, fully
    intelligent, speech + telepathy, subordinate to the Imperial bloodline. Fan-writeups put
    King-grade evolution around 70,000–90,000 years; one source's "10,000-year King" is
    softer and disputed (see receipts open item). [Blue Silver King; Baike Blue Silver
    Grass; sina]
  - Blue Silver **Emperor** (Blue Silver Emperor): the royal grade, tied to ~100,000 years of cultivation
    and to the imperial bloodline (Ah Yin; inherited by Tang San/Tang Wulin; a separate
    190,000+ year Emperor exists in SL2). Emperor bloodline commands the grass domain.
    [multiple]
- **King and Emperor coexist** in canon (the 85k King served the Empress; later a 190k
  Emperor exists separately from Tang San's line). The "only one Emperor, next appears on
  the last one's death" wording is from the mobile game, NOT the novel — **do not use it**
  (user-corrected). [game lore flagged as non-canon]
- Grass royalty (Ah Yin) is described as born with a **golden tint** / different from common
  grass — an origin marker of the royal blood, not something a common grass gains at 40.
  [Ah Yin plot readings — medium confidence, useful]

### Ah Yin timeline (corrected)
- Ah Yin is a 100,000-year Blue Silver Empress. **She took human form (hua xing / chong xiu — human-form rebirth) BEFORE
  meeting Tang Hao** — a transformed 100k beast must live among humans to re-cultivate, so
  she entered the human world and met the brothers by chance; she was ~level 59 (Soul King)
  when she revealed herself to Tang Hao (Tang Hao age 38, ~2624 DC). [Baike A Yin; Ah Yin
  wiki; Tang Hao timeline]
- She therefore left her rooted forest life at her transformation, **some years before
  ~2621** (the exact year is not stated in canon). The old note "rooted until ~2621" is
  revised: her rooted era ended at her transformation, before she ever met Tang Hao.
  [corrected]
- During a deep era ~800 years before SL1 (≈1837 DC), if her cultivation is near 1:1 with
  years she sits close to the 100,000-year decision for the OC's entire first age — this is
  the **era-span question** the user must settle (see next steps).

## Consequence for the serial (design decisions forced by canon)
1. **The OC's early power must come from cultivation essence and mind, not combat.** For
   centuries he is a fragile grass by body even as his essence and awareness climb. His
   wins are survival, learning, bonds, and outlasting — not fighting back.
2. **The talent's visible miracle is speed of cultivation** (he is a true soul beast with
   real essence at an age when grass should have nothing), expressed in canon classes, not
   invented powers.
3. **Becoming a "Blue Silver King" in canon terms is a tens-of-thousands-of-years
   achievement.** This must be reconciled with the serial's timeline (see next steps). The
   OC cannot be the SL1 King (already alive at 85k) — he is separate, and any King-grade
   rise must either take place over millennia or be redefined with the user.
4. **The "Emperor senses the King through the grass"** device remains valid only as an
   Emperor-domain ability in the far future, not as an early grass property.
