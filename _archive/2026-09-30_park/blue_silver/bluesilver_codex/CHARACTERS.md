## 2. The mantis (years ~40–90 — gone)
> **Profile:** insect soul beast of the grass carpet **[design]** · lifespan ~decades (~30–50 yrs, design working) · below hundred-year class · senses: motion & heat · role: first bond / first grief · status: gone (~90), husk, molts, egg-case shell kept.
The first bond. A jade-green mantis, a soul-beast insect of the grass carpet, one of a
hundred.
- Senses: motion and heat. She saw the world as pattern — a wind-blown leaf, a prey's leg
  tensing, the stillness that meant a mantis-shaped shadow overhead.
- Mind: quick, alien, fond. No words. She knew the tuft by the water was safe the way she
  knew the hollow log was good shelter — by pattern, not understanding.
- What she became in him: the reason he learned to make a shelter on purpose; the reason a
  patch of grass became a sanctuary; the first shape of grief. Her husks and molts and the
  empty egg-case shell stay at his base.
- Death: old age, at his base, held by his blades — she did not wake. Her line went into
  the grass after the fire; whether it ever returns is open. **[wire]**

## 3. The night-walker, "the old one" (years ~140–375 — gone)
> **Profile:** original keeper beast, last of its kind **[design]** · cultivation ~60,000+ carried (design working figure — exact never in prose) · ten-thousand-year class, upper range · senses: age-deep, wordless, deliberate ground-taps · role: teacher, second bond, former keeper of the boundary · status: passed ~375; body given to the ground; its place kept in his green.
The second bond and teacher. An original keeper of the middle/deep boundary, last of its
kind, its years in the high tens of thousands (unstated). No species name, no name — like
him, never named.
- Senses and mind: wordless, age-deep. It reads the glade by pattern and the deep by
  centuries of keeping. It communicates only by deliberate taps through the ground — a
  language it built with the blade — plus presence, weight, and the long slow drum of its
  heartbeat.
- Character: patient, fearless, alone in the way he is alone — it outlived its whole kind
  and carried the memory of them alone. It had stopped expecting anyone to answer. It found
  something new to keep.
- Its gift to him: acknowledgment; the ground-language; the hardest-winter vigil; the
  deep-country memory (the grass once had kings and queens — myth-level, no canon claim);
  the keeper's whole knowledge; and the keeping itself. *You are the keeper now.*
- Going quiet (~300–355): its taps grew fewer until a season passed with none; it kept
  only its breath. On the morning the rot broke it tapped its last two blows (*you are the
  keeper now*) and spent its final speech.
- The last vigil (~360–375): its breathing faded by degrees through the long quiet and, on
  a spring night, did not resume — the last of its kind finished its going. He kept the
  vigil; his green went thin for a season; and he let the ground have its body whole —
  did not draw its returned years into his well. Where it lay, the densest silver-green in
  the glade grew: its place, kept.
- Now: gone, and the keeper is alone on the line, the whole of its knowledge carried in his
  roots. What its kept place and carried knowledge become is story-earned. **[wire]**

  winter and gave it back to the soil in spring — the first of his kept lives to end in his
  keeping, and the first thing he ever gave back. Its place is still kept.

An armored, patient deep-band burrower, drawn by the open well — the sweetest root in the

The first creature of the deep he has KEPT, not held out. A small gray grazer of the deep

  It crossed the boundary because there was nowhere else left, and the green took it in.
- What it is to him: a kept life of the deep — proof that his keeping is a door as well as
  a wall, and that the deep is not one thing. In the long quiet it grew steady and

  here / home / safe / danger / the new single blow) — and learned to speak for the green
  to the small and lost of the fringe.
- Its end (Ch11): it grew old in his keeping, taught the drum to the fringe young until
  the door had a hundred voices, drummed its last *here* against the silver over the old
  one's place, and passed. Given to the ground, not drawn into his well. Grief carried,
  not adapted away. Its teaching outlives it — the door is now a people, and when the King
