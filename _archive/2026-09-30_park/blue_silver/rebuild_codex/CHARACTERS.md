## THE PROTAGONIST

### Home

**Role in the story:** the viewpoint. A Blue Silver Emperor grass who becomes a keeper.

**Identity:** `[on page]` Blue Silver line, real elemental mutant with Adaptation Talent.
Common line — no royal blood, no dragon blood, no devour blood, nothing fused in. `[user ruling]`
Takes nothing for 775 years. Reaches ≈10,000 years at year 665 (King gate, Stage 3).

| Attribute | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Rank | King gate, Stage 3 at yr 665 | [on page] |
| Rings | none taken, ever | [on page] |
| Age at naming | 753 years | [on page] Ch15 |
| Birth | ≈1837 DC | [user ruling] — never move |
| Named | year 753, ≈2590 DC | [on page] Ch15 |

**What they want:** to keep. Not to grow, not to win, not to avenge.

**Knows / does not know:** cannot read through grass — only A Yin can `[canon]`
(`CANON_NOTES.md:186`). He can go where she cannot. Does not know he is an Emperor line
until the naming.

**Voice:** no speech for most of the serial. Ground-language, eleven words, delivered by
tap → pulse → stamped foot. Interior register is patient, concrete, unhurried.

**Arc:** a thing that is eaten → a thing that keeps → a thing that is named.

**Absolutes:**
- **Never kills.** No exception, no accident, no self-defence. `[user ruling]`
- Takes no years from the dead.
- Never abandons a post while anything still lives on it.

### The surveyor — Ch5, year ~193

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Sex | she `[on page]` Ch5 |
| Age | past seventy `[on page]` Ch5:46 |
| Rings | **four — purple, purple, purple, black** `[on page]` Ch5:46 |
| Rank | Soul Ancestor (4 rings ⇒ level 41–50) `[design]` derived, not stated on page |
| Affiliation | Spirit Hall |
| Function | the first deliberate look |

Stood inside him all afternoon and found nothing. Surveyed grid 19; marked it
surveyed-and-unresolved and stayed that way for 147 years. `[on page]` Ch5:138

She is the same officer who wrote up a mauled cat's leg on a scree slope thirty years
earlier — that is the shadow cat, year 163, which fixes this scene at ~193. `[on page]` Ch5:44

**Voice:** procedural, dry, unwilling to overreach. `"You're right. It's grass."`

**What they want:** to close a grid. **Knows:** nothing. He was grass, and grass is empty.

**Why she matters:** the closest he ever came to being found by someone actually looking.
She did not fail because he was hidden. She failed because nobody had ever been told to look
for him. `[on page]` Ch5:130

> **Continuity note.** A black fourth ring (10,000 years) is above the canonical optimal
> configuration for a fourth ring (3,000–5,000 years), so she is an exceptional cultivator
> rather than a typical one. This is on page in Ch5 and is not an error, but it should not
> be quietly "corrected" downward by a later editor.
