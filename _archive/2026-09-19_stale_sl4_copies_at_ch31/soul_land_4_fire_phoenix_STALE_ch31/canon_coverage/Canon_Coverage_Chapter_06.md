## 2. Canon beats to preserve

- Lan Xiao and Nan Cheng had booked the trip in advance.
- They live on Heaven Luo Planet; the trip goes through Zi Luo City/Heaven Luo City to Heaven Dou Planet.
- Interstellar travel is expensive; the family uses ordinary/lowest grade cabin despite upper-middle income.
- Spacecraft 7703 has automated belts, protective covers, oxygen/pressure controls, safety instructions, and rescue capsule information.
- The journey to Heaven Dou Planet takes seven days.
- Lan Xuanyu becomes interested in battleships and wants to become a battleship pilot.

### Space meditation

- Takeoff pressure scares Lan Xuanyu, but right-arm warmth helps him settle.
- In space, he meditates under protective cover.
- He perceives silver space-element lights for the first time.
- Nan Cheng recognizes them as space elements.

### Tang Le

- Tang Le debuts through Xing Tang Media press conference.
- Bei Tianhong announces heavy company support and multi-planet release.
- Tang Le's appearance/aura stuns viewers and reporters.
- Nan Cheng becomes a fan despite earlier dismissive comments.
- Lan Xiao reacts with humor/jealousy.
- Lan family notices possible resemblance between Tang Le and Lan Xuanyu.

### Heaven Dou/Nana

- Lan family arrives at Heaven Dou City and visits migration museum.
- Museum explains Blue Silver Grass's importance in early terraforming/migration because of vitality/adaptability.
- Nana hears/feels Blue Silver Grass as familiar.
- Nana reacts strongly to Lan Xuanyu: headache, emotional shock, tenderness.
- Lan Xuanyu touches/holds her silver hair and calls her beautiful; she removes mask.
- Nana later keeps thinking of him and can sense his location.
- Lan Xuanyu practices improved water/ice control; Nan Cheng realizes she cannot teach enough.
- Lan Xiao discusses possible exploration work and future tutoring because Lan Xuanyu needs combat training/teaching.
- Lan Xuanyu becomes interested in Dragon Emperor Douluo and battle armor kits.
- Nana buys/offers the Silver Dragon Emperor Douluo kit; Lan Xiao pays instead.
- Nana reacts coldly when a salesperson calls Lan Xuanyu a cheeky little monster, then restrains herself.
- Nana asks to become his teacher, gives her device/credentials, and claims water-element/ancient martial arts teacher background.
- Nana demonstrates nine water dragons from the mall fountain without apparent spirit-power fluctuation; Lan family interprets her as Titled Douluo-level.
- Lan family cannot easily reject her, but return travel creates a temporary uncertainty.

Chapter 6 must not make Yan Shuo a spectator.

Natural butterfly effects selected:

1. **Relationship butterfly:** Lan Xuanyu sends Yan Shuo child-safe travel pictures/messages. This does not expose secrets but keeps the friendship alive during the trip.
2. **Absence butterfly:** Lan Xuanyu's absence makes Yan Shuo feel the missing familiar pressure; his body shifts adaptation inward toward the bottleneck and first soul-spirit readiness.
3. **Record butterfly:** Yan Shuo's rank 10 bottleneck before final exam triggers academy/Spirit Pagoda referral pressure and family decision-making.
4. **Resource butterfly:** Yan family cannot buy a perfect top-grade soul spirit. This makes the flawed Dawnflame Kite plausible and important.
5. **Canon emotional butterfly:** Lan Xuanyu's picture/message about Blue Silver Grass's adaptability at the Heaven Dou museum helps Yan Shuo emotionally accept a flawed, stuck soul spirit rather than chasing a perfect-looking one.
6. **Power butterfly:** Dawnflame Kite absorption happens while Lan Xuanyu is away meeting Nana, creating parallel growth paths that will affect the next school term.
