## 1. Canon span touched

Chapter 1 uses the opening Soul Land 4 discovery era as the world door.

- Novel Chapter 4: the object is an egg-like form retrieved from ice; it later hatches in the aircraft.
- Novel Chapters 5-6: the baby appears normal, eats the eggshell, and the remaining shell later disappears.
- Novel Chapter 7: Lan Xiao and Nan Cheng move toward adopting/naming/legalizing the baby as Lan Xuanyu.

Parallel but not staged directly:

- Novel Chapter 2: Nana's cryogenic awakening on Heaven Dou.
- Novel Chapter 3: Tang Le's beach awakening / Le Qingling thread.

Reason not staged: Chapter 1 focuses on Yan Shuo's birth foundation and the Lan Xuanyu discovery thread. Nana and Tang Le are part of the living world, but Yan Shuo has no contact with them and no knowledge of them.
