## 2. SL4 Chapter 100 — The possibility of the Self Spirit Fusion technique

### Canon beats

Lan Xuanyu after the Chapter 99 rainbow/rank-drop event:

- Continues meditating after discovering his soul power dropped from Rank 14 to Rank 13.
- Notices the gold/silver energy disparity has lessened while revolving.
- Trusts cultivation intuition because Nana once told him intuition during cultivation can be reliable/enlightening.
- Realizes the soul-power decrease may not necessarily be bad.
- Notices a tiny colorful light point deep inside the gold/silver vortex.

  - The new point is colorful, needle-tip small, and did not exist before.
- Understands the issue involves his bizarre Twin Spirits and that school teachers may not be able to explain it.
- Initially considers Mu Zhongtian, but decides Nana is more appropriate because other teachers would be suspicious.
- Recognizes this is a spirit/bloodline secret and should not be known even by Qian Lei and Liu Feng.

Letter route:

- Lan Xuanyu realizes Dong Qianqiu can be summoned.
- He thinks he can write a letter for Dong Qianqiu to carry to Nana through her storage device.

- He eats all he wants while classmates stare.

- Three days later, Nana's reply arrives.
- Nana explains the situation could be a **Self Spirit Fusion technique**.
- His golden-patterned and silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass may be able to fuse.
- The two energies may have homologous origin but conflicting attributes.
- Under battle danger, the barrier between them broke and produced the fusion.
- Using Self Spirit Fusion at just over Rank 10 is already incredible.
- The Rank 13 drop is probably a change caused after spirit fusion.
- The decreased conflict and tiny colorful point may mean the two energies are beginning to fuse, which could be good.
- It may strengthen cultivation/spirit in the long run, but may affect cultivation speed.
- Nana advises him to cultivate first and see whether he can recover to Rank 14 faster.
- Nana also reminds him that the conflict between gold and silver energies is his greatest hidden danger; spirit fusion may become a solution.
- Next time he uses spirit fusion, he should tell her what changes happen.

One-month bridge:

- After several days, Lan Xuanyu discovers recovering from Rank 13 to Rank 14 is faster than expected, possibly because his meridians had already widened once.
- Lan Xuanyu, Qian Lei, and Liu Feng privately practice coordination outside normal lessons.
- Lan Xuanyu becomes the well-deserving core of Dorm 333; Liu Feng and Qian Lei's combat power increases greatly with him.
- One month passes.
- On midterm exam day, Lan Xuanyu's rank is finally back to Rank 14, preventing teacher attention.
- The tiny colorful light point in his vortex does not grow with the recovered soul power.
- Elite Junior Class curriculum is intense, with sociocultural theory and real combat both important from morning to night.
- Lan Xuanyu senses his own progress and the improvement of classmates.
- Everyone is focused on studying/cultivating due elimination pressure.
- Ye Lingtong does not look for Lan Xuanyu again or ask what he wanted earlier; she studies and cultivates diligently.

## 3. SL4 Chapter 101 — First test

Fetched source:

- `https://novelfull.com/soul-land-iv-douluo-dalu-ultimate-fighting/chapter-101-first-test.html`

### Canon beats

Pre-midterm/class pressure:

- Qian Lei says Ye Lingtong and Lu Qianxun's group topped the beginning-of-semester assessment and works hard to keep first place.
- Dorm 333 had been considered bottom-ranked and likely to be eliminated.
- Lan Xuanyu and Qian Lei feel pressure from choosing warship piloting fundamentals.
- Warship piloting fundamentals begin in simulators; they have tried driving three types of Soul Guidance cars.
- The route consumes extra time and competes with class/training time.
- Lan Xuanyu slightly regrets the choice because his low soul power already creates a gap.
- Duo Mecha Master students also learn mecha piloting, but their path more directly increases battle power; warship piloting includes mecha plus more piloting skills and more effort.
- Lan Xuanyu and Qian Lei were permitted to choose warship piloting because their Spiritual Power surpassed 100.

Important canon measurements:

- Lan Xuanyu's latest Spiritual Power test shocks him: **137**.
- Qian Lei is higher: **152**.
- Among students, not many are over Spiritual Power 50.
- Teachers still have hope for Dorm 333 because of Lan Xuanyu/Qian Lei's outstanding Spiritual Power, despite Dorm 333 appearing weakest.

Midterm structure:

- Midterm exams are the first test of Elite Junior Class results after three months of learning/training.
- Academy wants to see improvement after heavy resource investment and revise teaching accordingly.
- Exam parts: sociocultural test, physical data test, actual combat practice in teams of three.
- Entire assessment happens in one day to avoid delaying further learning.
- Sociocultural and physical data tests happen in the morning; actual combat in the afternoon.
- Sociocultural exam is easy for Lan Xuanyu and Qian Lei because high Spiritual Power helps learning/memory/perception.
- Spirit Connection starts at 50 points; Spirit Sea starts at 200.
- Lan Xuanyu and Qian Lei are above 100 and not too far from Spirit Sea.

Physical data test setup:

- Components: strength, speed, Spiritual Power, Soul Power.
- Students do not need more detailed data at their age.
- Strength test prohibits using Soul Power; same rule later for speed.
- Strength apparatus has two metal walls closing inward; testee must resist with pure bodily strength as long as possible.
- Qian Lei result before Lan Xuanyu: **17 seconds**.
- Lu Qianxun result: **2 minutes 32 seconds**, current best at that point.
- Ye Lingtong result: **1 minute 17 seconds**, best among girls, reflecting Celestial Dragon strength.
- Lan Xuanyu copies Lu Qianxun's stance, using arms and legs against walls.
- Pressure increases about every ten seconds.
- Lan Xuanyu's bloodline strength begins to boil; he remembers Lava Hound combat and notes blood-vessel strength exceeds Soul Power strengthening.
- The teacher is impressed when Lan Xuanyu passes 1.5 minutes; he is registered as a control-type Soul Master.
- Chapter 101 ends with Lan Xuanyu likely reaching beyond 2 minutes.

### Chapter 101 implications for Yan Shuo

High-risk measurement issue:

- This chapter creates new formal Spiritual Power values for Lan Xuanyu and Qian Lei.
- If Yan Shuo is present for the same physical-data test, the same-context measurement rule applies.
- The story currently has Yan Shuo's latest formal SP at 72 from Chapter 13. Natural growth continues, but a new exact value requires an actual formal test scene and support-file update.

- Song Yichen's latest formal SP48 and Luo Haoran's SP57 should also be handled carefully if Dorm 336 cards/results are shown.

Safe writing choices:

- Keep Chapter 20 mainly on Chapter 100 private letter and one-month bridge; end at announcement of midterm physical-data test before Yan Shuo's detailed measurement.
- Or show Yan Shuo watching Lan Xuanyu's strength test publicly without yet giving Yan Shuo's own card/result.
- Or write Dorm 336's formal values deliberately and update ledgers afterward.

Dangerous writing choices:
