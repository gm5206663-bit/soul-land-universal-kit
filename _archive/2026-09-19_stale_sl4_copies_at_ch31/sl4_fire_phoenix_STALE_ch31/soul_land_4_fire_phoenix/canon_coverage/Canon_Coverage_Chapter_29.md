- Lan and Liu attempt the same first-move tactic from the fourth match.

- The new trio has clearly watched the released video and prepared.
- The opponents are stronger than Jin Xiang's team.

- Teammates: **Wang Lijian** and **Chen Yuelin**.
- Liang Zheng's martial soul: **Half Moon Blades**, a variant martial soul. Together they form a shield; separated they become large offensive weapons.
- Liang blocks Liu Feng's ferocious spear aura with the shield, retreating four steps but successfully resolving the attack.
- Chen Yuelin is a control-type Soul Master with **green ivy** martial soul; he sends vines outward and makes a spider-web-like net to constrain Liu Feng.
- Wang Lijian is Rank18 with **Offensive Moon Bow** martial soul; his first soul skill is **Guided Arrow**.
- Wang's guided arrow targets Qian Lei, who is meditating/recovering Spiritual Power.

- Lan gives up his attacking plan and rushes to protect Qian with an ice awl.
- Liang follows with first soul skill **Lunula Slash**.
- Lan avoids the slashes and uses an ice awl to knock away the guided arrow above Qian.
- Lan then shouts for the match to wait and admits defeat without hesitation.
- Liang Zheng relaxes, stops, and nods.
- Lan's reasoning: Qian cannot currently summon; opponents have long range, control, and close-range strength; Dorm333 is effectively two against three; forcing the fight would cost too much and victory is uncertain; with four wins already, no need to waste Spiritual Power/energy here.
- Liang Zheng's team is one of the stronger ordinary challenger teams in the canon class and wins through preparation/control/targeting, not because Liang is near Yan Shuo or because Lu Qianxun remains the class ceiling.
- Teachers sigh in relief. If Dorm333 had continued to win too much, especially five consecutive wins, it would have heavily damaged other Elite Junior Class students' morale.
