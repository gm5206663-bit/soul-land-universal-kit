# 言天宇 — RELATIONSHIPS (age 0–11) — who surrounds him before the gate opens
### Each entry: who they are (canon-verified where possible), what they are to him, what they know, and the grain of the bond. [AU] = original design.

---

## 穆恩 (Mu En) — 龙神斗罗, 99级, 光明圣龙 — MASTER, THE OLD DRAGON
- **Canon:** Shrek's soul, former 海神阁阁主; teacher of 言少哲/蔡媚儿/仙琳儿/钱多多, later 霍雨浩; the White Dragon of the "黑白双圣龙." Was brother-rival to **龙逍遥** (the black dragon's ancestor) and loved **叶夕水** — the very triangle his two bloodlines now carry.
- **To 天宇:** claims him at the awakening. Teaches him daily from 6. Knows **everything** — both souls, the bloodline, the father's death, the talent.
- **Why he takes the boy (the hidden why):** it is not charity to the dean's line. Mu En spent his youth as the White Dragon beside the Black Dragon, and lost that brother to a century of silence. In 天宇, the black blood has come home to the white — and Mu En is the only man alive who can teach a black dragon's heir to be a good man, because he is the only man alive who loved the last one. Teaching 天宇 to cage the dark is Mu En finishing the friendship he failed with 龙逍遥.
- **The grain:** fierce tenderness. Mu En is not soft; he is *certain*. He calls the boy [provisional: 小宇 or a private name; recommend he never uses a pet name in public — the old dragon does not do pet names where people can hear]. His promises are few and absolute: *"When you are ready, I will be there."* The boy will spend the rest of his life wishing he had asked one more question.

## 言少哲 (Yan Shaozhe) — 明凤斗罗, 武魂系院长 (later 海神阁阁主) — GRANDFATHER
- **Canon:** 光明凤凰, inheritor of 叶夕水's second soul (so the dragon line and the phoenix line are BOTH his ancestry). Teacher 穆恩. Wife 蔡媚儿.
- **To 天宇:** raised him after the parents were gone. Stern, exacting, terrified — a man who lost his son and is terrified of losing his grandson to the blood that took... everything.
- **What he knows:** everything (Layer A + B). He sees the phoenix and, in his worst moments, sees 叶夕水 and 龙逍遥 staring back from his own grandson's eyes.
- **The grain:** the hardest love in the boy's life. 言少哲 pushes because he is afraid; the boy, who reads silences, knows his grandfather is afraid and loves him *more* for it, not less. Their unspoken pact: the boy will be so good, so controlled, that his grandfather will never have to be afraid again. (This is a pressure that will break open in the main story.)
- **His integrity (story-relevant):** he will NOT let his grandson walk into Shrek's freshmen class by name alone — 天宇 must earn the gate like anyone else. [This is a founding scene of year one.]

## 蔡媚儿 (Cai Meier) — 武魂系副院长 — GRANDMOTHER, THE WARMTH
- **Canon:** wife of 言少哲; [provisional: strong soul master / 破邪龙魂枪 per one game source — verify before asserting in prose]. Teacher-circle of 穆恩.
- **To 天宇:** the one who fed him, held him, and never once flinched from the cold. She knows everything and has chosen love over fear every single day.
- **The grain:** she is the only person who treats him as *just a boy* — scolds him for not eating enough (impossibly), fusses over his cold nights, hides tears when he sleeps. When the world later learns the truth and recoils, she will be the one who never stopped seeing the child. **She is also the one who knows 苏凝's heart best** (the two women share letters) — the bridge to his mother.

## 苏凝 (Su Ning) — MOTHER [AU, provisional core design — see Decisions]
- **Who:** a healer-type soul master, gentle, not a genius, from outside Shrek. Loved 言昭; he chose her against every expectation; she was the quiet miracle behind the impossible child (her line's gift → the womb talent). After 言昭's death she left Shrek rather than raise the boy inside her grief.
- **To 天宇:** absence with love. Letters, rare visits, a warmth he treasures and a hole he never names. She knows nothing of the dragon, the genealogy, or the true cause of 言昭's death — kept from her to keep her alive and clear-eyed.
- **Why it matters (engine):** she is his soft spot and his vulnerability. In the main story, the cult learning of *her* is a lever they will pull. And the boy's deepest unspoken fear is that she left because of something wrong in *him* — which is false, and which he will only learn is false when the truth finally comes out. ## 言昭 (Yan Zhao) — FATHER [AU] — THE SEALED HOLE
- **Who:** 言少哲 & 蔡媚儿's only son; 光明凤凰 (strong, not ultimate); brave, warm, ordinary-brilliant. Saw the dark in his son's eyes at a few months old and chose love.
- **Death (public):** hero's death at a border beast-tide when 天宇 was ~4.
- **Death (truth, sealed):** [provisional — see Decisions] the tide was a directed probe from forces who suspected the dragon line had produced an heir; 言昭 died closing the gap he was told was safe, buying the secret. He is the reason the boy lives.
- **To 天宇:** a face in stories, a warmth in the grandmother's voice, a shape the boy has tried his whole childhood to grow into. The boy's public perfection is, in part, an attempt to be worthy of a father he barely remembers.

## 玄老 (玄子) — 饕餮斗罗, 98级 — THE GLUTTON UNCLE [canon]
- **Canon:** Mu En's 师侄, Shrek 宿老, succeeds Mu En as 海神阁阁主. 饕餮神牛 soul. Voracious. Gruff, warm under the gruff.
- **To 天宇:** learns the truth at ~age 10 (Mu En tells him — the circle must outlive Mu En). Bonds with the boy over food before he ever bonds over power. Calls him 小饭桶.
- **The grain:** the only adult who jokes with the boy like an equal. He is the living proof that knowing the truth does not have to mean fear — 玄老 knows what 天宇 carries and still sees a hungry kid who needs a second helping. After Mu En falls, 玄老 becomes the boy's shield in the old generation.

## 马小桃 (Ma Xiaotao) — 邪火凤凰, 言少哲's 徒弟, inner-court terror — BIG SISTER / MIRROR [canon + butterfly]
- **Canon:** descendant of first-gen 马红俊; 邪火凤凰 (a fire that fights her); 言少哲's disciple, raised by him from small; ~19 at the story's start; the "百年来第一天才" of her generation (before 张乐萱); later corrupted to 黑凤凰 → 圣灵教's 圣女 → saved by 霍雨浩's ultimate ice → pure ultimate fire phoenix.
- **To 天宇:** the older sister he grew up under. She babysat him (badly), terrorized anyone who bullied him, taught him (by example and by threat) that crying in front of juniors is forbidden, and is secretly the person he measures his own fire against.
- **🔴 THE BUTTERFLY (user-directed):** from ~age 8–9 he can *sense* her 邪火 (dual-vision) and finds — by accident — that his 光明凤凰's light steadies it. A private ritual forms: every few weeks, when her fire flares, she finds her "junior sun." It is NOT a cure (the 邪火 is fundamental); it makes her early years steadier and binds them across a real need. **Reserved (canon spine):** when 审判之剑 tips her (canon), his light is NOT enough — a wound he will carry ("my light couldn't save her"); her fall is the deepest mirror of his own secret. 霍雨浩's ultimate ice later does what light alone couldn't. She must never know his secret pre-main-story.
- **The grain:** she loves him like a real little brother and has no idea how much of herself she is showing him. She calls him 小宇; he calls her 桃姐.

## 贝贝 (Bei Bei) — 蓝电霸王龙 (later light-dragon path), Mu En's descendant — COUSIN-BROTHER [canon]
- **Canon:** Mu En's 玄孙; gentle senior; 唐雅's disciple-lover; Tang Sect 大师兄; ~16 at intake; his dragon blood is stabilized toward the light by Mu En at the old dragon's end.
- **To 天宇:** the older boy who is family in a different branch — both are "Mu En's boys," one openly a dragon line, one secretly. Bei Bei teaches him manners-by-example, takes him to the city, and is the first person 天宇 ever sees *choose* gentleness over strength.
- **With 唐雅 (user point):** Bei Bei and 唐雅 come as a pair — so 天宇 knows her too (see 唐雅 below). Through them he watches two people choose each other against the world, and quietly learns what he wants someday.
- **The grain:** a quiet, uncomplicated love — the kind 天宇's life has very little of. They will not be classmates (Bei Bei is a senior), but Bei Bei is the first person outside the secret circle who will ever feel like *safe*. When 贝贝 later brings 霍雨浩 into Shrek, he is the bridge between the two boys' worlds.

## 唐雅 (Tang Ya) — 唐门门主, 贝贝's girl, outer-court senior — THE BRIGHT-SAD SISTER [canon]
- **Canon:** 唐门's young 门主 (her parents killed, the sect's site stolen by 铁血宗); studies at Shrek's outer court; 贝贝's disciple-lover; vivacious on the surface, burning with a hidden drive to restore her sect; later overreaches and becomes a 邪魂师 (暗黑蓝银草), taken as 圣灵教's 蓝银圣女, eventually saved by 霍雨浩.
- **To 天宇:** met through Bei Bei. She calls him 小明凤 or teases him about his bottomless appetite; she cheerfully tries to recruit him into 唐门 — *"your flames could light our sect's return!"* He cooks with/for the pair, spars gently with Bei Bei while she heckles, and — because he reads shadows — sees the grief and desperation under her cheer. He never pries, never judges; he simply *stays*, and that steadiness means more to her than she ever says.
- **The mirror (reserved):** like 马小桃, she is a bright person who falls to the dark. When she becomes the 蓝银圣女, Tianyu faces the pattern again — light cannot save everyone, and some fires are only doused by those who understand them. His bond with her also seeds his later link to 霍雨浩 (who becomes 唐雅's student).
- **The grain:** the person who makes him laugh hardest and whose hidden sadness he reads clearest. He thinks of her as family-by-贝贝 — and family is the word that hurts most when it falls.

## The junior-study children & Shrek's people — THE PUBLIC
- To the academy, 天宇 is the 少院主 — the golden grandson, Mu En's student, kind and brilliant and hungry. Half the junior children worship him; a few resent the prince who gets everything. He handles both with the same even warmth.
- **The kitchen staff** adore him. The **inner-court servants** know which nights he sleeps cold and say nothing. The **city children** he meets with 贝贝 know him as "the polite one from the academy."
- **No close same-age friend yet** [provisional — this is deliberate: the secret isolates him, and Mu En's training consumed his hours]. His first true peer-bond is waiting at the gate in the main story — that is the story's gift to him.
