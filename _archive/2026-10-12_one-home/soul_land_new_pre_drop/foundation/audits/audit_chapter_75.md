## B. CONFIRMED ERRORS (fix these)

### B1. ❌ Wrong canon name: 「余洛面」 → should be **玉罗冕**
ch75 (Dashi's garden story): *"My second uncle. Second Elder of the Blue Lightning Tyrant Dragon Clan. 余洛面."*

Canon: the Second Elder of the 蓝电霸王龙 clan — 柳二龙's father — is **玉罗冕 (Yù Luómiǎn)**.
"余洛面" is a by-ear misspelling (玉→余, 冕→面). This is exactly the "match the franchise's exact terminology"
error class.
**Fix:** 余洛面 → **玉罗冕**. (If this name was deliberately changed in the project's divergence ledger, record that
explicitly; a silent near-spelling is an error either way.)

### B2. ⚠️ Canon-knowledge inconsistency: Flender and "I didn't know you were here"
Canon (novel, Baidu 弗兰德): Flender **knew 蓝霸学院 was 柳二龙's**, deliberately **did not tell 玉小刚**, and
steered the group there to heal Dashi's heart-knot.

ch75 makes Qin Ming the one who knows about Blue Tyrant ("I have heard the teaching is strict…"), and has Flender
say to Erlong: *"I didn't know you were here."*

Either:
- **(a)** Flender is lying on purpose (canon-accurate & poignant), or
- **(b)** this AU moved the knowledge to Qin Ming — which **contradicts canon's reason the school gets chosen at all**
  (the chapter's own ending leans on Flender "already seen the next room" + healing Dashi).

**Fix:** make the concealment explicit — Flender chose Blue Tyrant knowing it was hers, hid it from Dashi, and the
"I didn't know" line reads as the tactful lie it is. That turns a possible error into the chapter's strongest beat.

### C2. Li Yusong's "white first ring" (a 63-year master starting on a ≤100-yr white ring)
Unverified canon detail. Possible, but unusual for someone who reached 63. Verify or mark as deliberate characterization.

### C3. Erlong fire-only vs donghua fire+lightning
Donghua 柳二龙's 火龙 carries both fire and lightning (e.g., 第三魂技 怒火惊雷). ch75's "Not Blue Lightning. Fire."
is thematically true (her soul is a mutation of 蓝电霸王龙) but if donghua-first, her lightning side exists. Minor; your call.

### C4. The singing beat replaced with watering
In canon/donghua, Erlong is heard singing & murmuring Dashi's name before the group meets her — the reader's first
proof she still loves him. ch75 gives us silent watering + the hydrangeas confession instead. Fine as a **deliberate
divergence** (keeps her identity a surprise) — record it as one.

### C5. Ambiguous antecedent: "The same man who had knelt outside a city that morning…"
Grammatically it points at Qin Ming ("the same Emperor"). Qin Ming kneeling is not in canon and not explained in this
chapter. Clarify who knelt, and to whom — or this reads as an unexplained referent.

### C6. Minor translation drift
邵鑫's soul 糖豆 = sugar/candy beans, not "sweet pea" (香豌豆). Cosmetic, but term-precision matters to this project.
