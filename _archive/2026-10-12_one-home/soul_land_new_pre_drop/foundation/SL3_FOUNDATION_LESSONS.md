## 2. THE LAWS THE FIRST FIVE EMBODY (evidence inside the prose)

1. **The OC is never a watcher across a whole chapter.** Even when canon owns the scene, Lin Hao acts, chooses, negotiates, withholds. His "observer" moments are half-beats, never a chapter posture.
2. **Talent is embodied, never named.** The Adaptation Talent appears as counting, reading grain, seeing movement a beat early, arithmetic-as-thought. No system voice, no narrator explaining "his gift means…"
3. **World rules arrive as priced experience.** The 1-in-1000 rarity, the spiritual ceiling, the ring price ladder, the rank-20 wall — each is delivered through a character who *pays* for the knowledge (Wulin's three years of coins; the clerk's "you can't afford it").
4. **Choice > trait.** Every chapter gives Lin Hao a decision with a consequence and a stated butterfly. The reader knows him by what he picks.
5. **Secondary cast have real interior.** Mu Xi gets her full origin (father's "not suitable for girls," the box, the two years) *in the same chapter she appears*; even "the surveyor" has a personality. Named faces recur with their own drives.
6. **Withholding is a tool.** The big skill is named and *held back* so its first use will matter. Reader anticipation is engineered.
7. **Friendship is mutual and equal.** Wulin and Lin Hao train each other, carry each other, choose together; power never flows one way. The canon MC is not worshipped at a distance.
8. **Theme is earned in scenes, not declared.** Mang Tian's "one hears it, the other is better" is a scene-judgment. Nobody delivers the thesis in a speech.
9. **Chapters end in shared warmth or forward motion**, not solitude (the two boys on the roof; "a good shape for a life to have").
10. **Footer discipline is deep but must stay tight.** Per chapter: canon chapters cited + corrections logged, canon-preserved/not-triggered lists, AU divergence locks, single-source-of-truth pointers, butterfly effects traced.

## 3. A WARNING SEEN IN THE MODEL ITSELF (learn it, don't copy it)

Chapters 1–5 of the SL3 project carry a **footer template that leaks end-of-serial state**: the "REALM GAP LAW" line says Lin Hao is a Soul Elder with three purple rings — at chapter 1 he is six with no rings; the ensemble boilerplate (class zero, Shrek dorm of four) is story-future pasted into the opening. The project later had to retrofit ("v2.42 added during the integrity audit"). Lesson for us: **centralized CURRENT_STATE + a per-chapter state line is the right medicine; never embed end-state ensemble blocks in early footers.** Our design already does this better — keep it that way.

## 4. GAP ANALYSIS — our 绝世唐门 chapters 1–3 vs this bar

Our project runs under a STRICTER canon law than SL3 does (user-locked: Yuhao's canon events, ranks, and feats stay unchanged and unshared; the OC may not join his team or alter his outcomes). So we cannot import SL3's *story* solutions (co-protagonist in every canon scene, OC-injected divergences). We import its *craft*. Measured against the foundation:

### Mistakes / problems in our chapters 1–3

- **M1 — The OC is a watcher for whole chapters, not half-beats.** Ch2 and ch3 both resolve with 言天宇 watching 霍雨浩's canon feat from the rail. Intended ("the watcher" arc) — but the foundation's law says a watcher posture is a *half-beat*, never a chapter's spine. Our chapters 1–3 have no chapter where his own driven want moves events.
  - **RESOLVED (2026-09 spine correction):** the false "bench-the-golden-boy" rationale that produced the watcher posture is gone. The OC is not on this year's ladder because he is wall-capped (no level until 穆恩's hunt) and the room's selections belong to the room's climb — never because "too strong to be allowed." Ch2 he RAN the hundred laps with the room (holding back; 周漪 saw it). Ch3's single rail-morning is his own plain choice, spent in service (report, water, warmth). His own driven want moves events from ch4 on (the wall-fire for the ones who eat last; the stall with 霍雨浩) — acting lives on this side of his wall, never in the seats.
- **M2 — Talent is named more than shown.** Our narration says "he read the room," "his dual-vision," "two furnaces," "the cold at his spine stirred." The foundation shows Lin Hao's gift as actions only. Our kit-vocabulary in narration is closer to telling than SL3's standard.
- **M3 — No priced world rules.** The rank-30 wall, the culling, Shrek's economy are stated, never *costed* for the reader through a character who pays. (SL3: Wulin's three years of coins for the draw.)
- **M4 — Theme by declaration.** 周漪 and 穆恩 deliver the chapter theses in speeches ("a man being forged… you have never been spent"). The foundation earns the same idea through two boys at an anvil.
- **M5 — Side cast too thin for a "know the room" protagonist.** 徐磊, 温宁, 萧萧 are sketches. If 言天宇's whole skill is knowing every child, the reader should *meet* several children memorably (SL3 gives a full origin to a character who appears for one chapter).
- **M6 — No skill has ever been shown; none is withheld with intent.** 燎羽/贯日/敛焰步/厨艺 are reputation-only. SL3 shows Gale Talon in ch5 and explicitly withholds Hawk-Soul Union to build anticipation. We have built zero anticipation engine.
- **M7 — Friendship is unidirectional in the shown scenes.** 言天宇 gives (feeds, defends by proxy, water cup); we have no scene where 霍雨浩 or 王冬 gives him something real.
- **M8 — Chapters bookend in solitude.** Each chapter ends with him alone (dawn/room/wall). The warmth of ch3 Part Four is immediately traded for a solitary wall-vigil resolution. The foundation ends chapters in shared warmth.
- **M9 — No butterfly with visible field-change yet.** His presence has subtly helped Yuhao (not alone in the hall), but no *structural* change to the room exists — the thing the bible's own "Star" doctrine promises (people around him rise or reveal themselves).
- **M10 — Chapter footers lack the model's canon-coverage checklist.** Ours have butterflies/wires/state; the model adds explicit "Canon preserved / NOT triggered" and AU-lock lines per chapter.

### Missing things (foundation-level)
- A positive, present-tense want that produces a task with a price (his only want is negative: not-a-trophy; his project is the distant ring hunt).
- Physical/sensory grounding of Shrek spaces within canon-allowable texture (the moved-into dorm room, the dining hall's windows, the market).
- One named big skill held back + one used cheaply (anticipation engine).
- Mutual exchange with the canon trio (a scene where they give to him).
- A room that changes because he's in it (his own butterfly).
- Endings that land on belonging, at least sometimes.
