## LAW 1 — THE BUTTERFLY LAW (borrowed: THE_CODEX v3.06)
> **A butterfly effect is not a decoration added on top of a canon scene. It is the reason the scene is different at all. If a canon scene can be dropped into the story unchanged, the butterfly effect has not been applied — the story is canon with an extra character standing in it.**

- **The test, for EVERY canon beat and EVERY canon character:** *what does this scene look like differently because 言天宇 is standing in it (or because our named divergences happened)?* If the answer is "the same," the beat has not been adapted.
- **Effect without its cause shown on-page is not a butterfly; it is a claim.** The cause → effect chain must be written, panel by panel, in the chapter footer.
- Canon's superlatives must be **scoped**: any line that makes someone "the strongest/best/highest" in a group the OC belongs to must survive his presence. (The foundation's founding failure: copying canon's *"Xie Xie's soul power is the highest"* into a class that contained Lin Hao — that is transcription, and it erases the OC from his own story.)

## LAW 2 — THE PERSPECTIVE PANEL DOCTRINE (borrowed: THE_CODEX v3.20, user directive)
> *"I think you don't show other characters their own things — that is why no natural butterfly effects are forming. You should also [write] others accordingly, like canon chapters with butterfly effects — multiple perspectives and panels and many things; that feels more real. You should use everything."*

1. **Every chapter stages at least one panel that belongs to someone who is not 言天宇**, and the panel's scene must be **checked out of canon itself**: take the verified canon source (donghua episode synopsis / novel passage), verify the scene, and adapt it *from that character's perspective* with the butterflies applied.
2. **A panel with no canon chapter behind it is an AU invention and must be labelled AU in the header/footer.** A chapter built mostly of such inventions is the wrong chapter. (Foundation's own wound: ch65's dock, letters, bay master and dawn slots were "inventions wearing panel titles — the same disease as transcription, mirrored: invention instead of sourcing.")
3. **The butterfly test extends to every character**, not just the OC. The cause → effect chain is written in the chapter's footer, panel by panel.
4. **Divergences are an economy** — a ledger of standing changes to initial conditions, each with magnitude (dust · current · bend · break · STORM), status, horizon, and last-paid. Both directions must derive: **no forced canon, no forced divergence.**
5. **Use everything:** canon episodes, ensemble states, locks, **minor POVs** (an examiner, a kitchen elder, a 魂导系 apprentice, a market vendor, a letter-writer), panels, letters, machines. The world is full of people who do not know 言天宇 exists — show them.
6. **Locks still govern.** Another character's panel may *approach* a locked door (霍雨浩's secret cold, 王冬's true nature, 穆恩's history, 马小桃's fate) but never open it.

## LAW 3 — LEGEND ACCUMULATES IN OUTSIDE VOICES (STYLE_GOLD §7)
The world seen side-on through strangers who owe the OC nothing. The "legend" (the star, the golden grandson, the furnace) grows in *other people's* observations, never in his own narration. One such panel per era; **the stranger must gain something from having watched.**

## LAW 4 — CANON IS ORE, NOT FATE (DIVERGENCE_LEDGER preamble)
World-facts are true until touched; canon's plot is one timeline's path. When our state (user locks + OC placement + their derived consequences) implies a different outcome, **OURS wins and is derived, never snapped back**. Pay a row and it may spawn 0–2 children — keep the world alive.
