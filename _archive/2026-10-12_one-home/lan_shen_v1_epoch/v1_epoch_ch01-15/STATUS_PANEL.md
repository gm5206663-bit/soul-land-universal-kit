## THE OPENING IMAGE — CHANGED BY CANON, 2026-09-19

The plan was: *Lan Shen stands in the chamber and watches the golden lines cross his
brother's forehead.* **Canon 002 forbids it** — Wulin is alone, no parent, no queue, no
other child. The planned image was canonically impossible.

What replaced it is better and it is legal: **Lan Shen never sees the miracle. He sees what
the miracle did to a man who has conducted thirty thousand ceremonies** — and then, eleven
minutes later, he asks that man a question nobody has ever asked him.

The most important image in the opening is therefore not the golden lines. It is
**two blades of grass in two cups of water on one windowsill**, which is a six-year-old's
experiment and the seed of the entire six-track plan.
