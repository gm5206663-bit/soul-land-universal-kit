# CANON_LEDGER.md — every canon beat touched, and what it cost

**Canon is ore, not fate.** Every beat this serial touches gets a row: what canon says, what
we did, why, and what it cost.

The **decay column** is the one that matters. Lan Shen's meta-knowledge is a map of a
country that stops existing the moment he changes it. Every butterfly erodes what he knows.
If the decay column stays at zero for twenty chapters, he has stopped being a reincarnator
and started being an author, and the premise is dead.

three times, and acting costs accuracy:

- **B2** puts a sentence about Lan Shen in a Spirit Pagoda man's handwriting. Canon has no
  such sentence. It does not change an event; it changes what a man might later remember.
- **B3** moves a small amount of money and creates an obligation between the Lan household

  later want one, and then the fee is not what canon says it is.

  happens — *"success is 99% effort and 1% talent"* is not stolen, it is now said to a friend

  from here on every Wulin interior beat is a guess rather than a memory.
- **B4, B6, B7** are ~1% each. A roster number, a minor grudge, a private document. None of
  them touches an event he remembers. B7 will, eventually — a written secret has a way of
  being read.
- 🔴 **B8 and B9 are the crossing.** A canon event — the single most important childhood
  beat in the first eight chapters — now happens **one day late** and **with an extra
  witness**. That is the ~15% tier by this file's own scale: *a canon event happens EARLY,
  LATE, or DIFFERENTLY.*
- **B10 is the clever one and the worrying one.** Lan Shen did not change *whether* the thugs
  back off. He changed *how fast*, and by what instrument. Canon's incidental bystanders are
  now his deliberate tool. That is a control-system mind doing control-system work, and it is
  exactly the kind of change that compounds.
- **B12 is the largest single addition to the serial.** A rank-one child who can feel the
  street's grass and his brother's soul through a wall is not in the book Lan Shen read. From
  here, every prediction he makes about cultivation is a prediction about a world that
  contains him. Ruled and bounded in `OPEN_DECISIONS.md` #15 — **the bounds are load-bearing
  and must not be quietly relaxed.**
- **B14 is a small fuse with a long burn.** A fifteen-year-old with a knife now has a face to
  attach to a grievance. Do not spend it inside ten chapters.

Both are **~2% each, not additive to 4%** — decay measures how much of his *map* is wrong,
and neither of these touches an event he remembers. The rule stands: decay never resets.

## DECAY — how it is measured

```
0%      chapter 1. Everything he remembers is still true.
~5%     first butterfly logged. Small — a person's mood, a day's timing.
~15%    a canon event happens EARLY, LATE, or DIFFERENTLY.
~30%    a canon event does not happen at all.
~50%    🔴 he can no longer predict a major beat. He is working from a dead map.
```

**The rule:** decay never resets and never decreases. It only accumulates. A chapter that
changes nothing is a chapter where the reincarnation is decoration.

When decay passes **50%**, the far spine changes shape: he is no longer a man exploiting a
story he read, he is a man improvising in a world he broke. That transition is the
mid-serial turn and it should be planned for from chapter one, not discovered at chapter
two hundred.

## 🔴 THE DIVERGENCE ECONOMY — standing rows

**Why this section exists.** Seventy-four B-rows are **events**. The doctrine this project
imported wants a layer above them: the **standing changes to initial conditions** that events
trace to, each with a magnitude, a horizon, and a record of when it was last paid. Without
that layer, a butterfly that traces to nothing is indistinguishable from invention — and the
imported rule is explicit: *a "butterfly" that traces to no standing row is invention and must
be labelled AU or cut.*

dust     a day's mood, a single sentence, no downstream
current  a relationship or a record that persists and keeps being read
bend     an institution's behaviour changes shape

   schedule. It records that the condition still holds and the world has not yet reacted.
