## 3. BATTLE ARMOR — the defining system of SL4 (verified, fandom wiki)

Six levels; each adds a word (character) to the armor's name. Grades work like soul rings
(white/yellow/purple/black/red/gold).

| Level | Grade | Requirement / note |
|---|---|---|
| **One-word** | White (yellow/purple rare) | 11 components. **6 rings → +10 levels of amplification** |
| **Two-word** | Purple | Armor gains **wings**. 8 rings → +9 |
| **Three-word** | Black | Soul-refined metal, **self-repairing**, crystal-like. Titled-Douluo combat power. **A second life** |
| **Four-word** | Red | **Semi-divine**, possesses a degree of sentience |
| **Five-word** | Gold | **Cannot be crafted** — evolves through divine consciousness. Divine level only. +110 |
| **Six-word** | Gold | Evolves from five-word. Super-divine. +120 |

**Key rule:** a battle armor master is **20 ranks stronger** than a soul master of the same soul power
rank. Five rings + one-word armor ≈ seven-ring combat power.

**Creation requires all four crafts professions** — designer, **blacksmith**, manufacturer, mechanic.
**The blacksmith's skill is explicitly the tested one at two-word and above** (fuse forging /
harmonizing refinement).

**⚠️ THIS IS THE DIRECT PAYOFF FOR LIN HAO'S BLACKSMITH LINE.** Our fic has been seeding his
battle-armor-smith path since ch 20 (the armor lecture) and ch 34 (3rd-rank Grandmaster, Thousand
Refinements). In SL4 that profession is the bottleneck for everyone. **A Lin Hao-line smith in SL4
would be exactly as valuable as his whole arc has been building toward.**

## 4. SHREK ACADEMY STANDARDS (verified, allnovel ch 303 full text)

Teacher **Xiao Qi**, Battle Armor Master course, first year:

> *"When the second academic year is over, the assessment is fixed in advance, that is, to have your
> own one-star Battle Armor, or also known as a one-word Battle Armor. In other words, you have two
> years to learn, prepare and build your own Battle Armor. **If you can't finish it by then, you can
> leave Shrek.**"*

> *"In the sixth year… To qualify for the Inner Court's assessment, the most basic criterion is to
> become a **three-word Battle Armor Master**. The basic criterion for soul power is **rank 70**. If you
> don't meet them, you won't even have the qualifications for taking the Inner Court's test."*

> *"In Shrek Academy, being a Battle Armor Master is the foundation for all disciplines… **Strength is
> the foundation of everything.**"*

- Age 14 → must have a one-word battle armor or be expelled.
- Age ~20, sixth year → three-word battle armor **and** rank 70 to enter the Inner Court.
