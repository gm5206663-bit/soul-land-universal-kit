# ENTITY & SCOPE RESOLUTION v3.0

## Purpose
Prevent correct facts from being combined about the wrong entity, version, edition, timeline, or scope.

## Resolve before synthesis
- exact entity identity
- aliases/names
- universe/version/edition
- jurisdiction
- date/time window
- geography
- product/platform/model version
- canon branch/AU branch

## Collision checks
Search for same-name people, organizations, characters, products, locations, remakes, adaptations, translations, and different releases.

## Scope rule
Never combine evidence across scopes until the relationship is established.
