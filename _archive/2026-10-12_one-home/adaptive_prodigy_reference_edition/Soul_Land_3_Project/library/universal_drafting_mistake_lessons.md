# Universal Drafting Mistake Lessons

**Status:** Standing universal instruction
**Version:** 1.0 — 3 Aug 2026
**Scope:** All fanfiction, canon-adaptation, crossover, alternate-timeline, and long-form drafting work
**Source:** Distilled from an error autopsy of drafted chapters.

> This file preserves **only transferable lessons**. It contains **no** story-specific names, characters, powers, relationships, episodes, villains, devices, locations, numbers, or events. It works alongside `universal_storyline_writing_corrections.md` (v2.1) and `universal_fanfiction_continuity_audit_corrections.md`. Where a lesson merely reinforces an existing rule, the cross-reference is given; where it is new or sharper, it is marked **[NEW]**.

---

## 0. The Root Failure Pattern

**Do not draft prose over an unverified canon reconstruction.**

The single most damaging error is to assemble canon from low-tier evidence — reviews, wikis, recaps, remembered descriptions, or AI/search summaries — and then write full chapters as if that reconstruction were reliable. Those sources are **orientation aids and unverified hypotheses only** (see Writing-Corrections v2.1 §1, canon evidence hierarchy).

**[NEW] The "rigorous-looking artifact" trap:** Building a confidence-tagged ledger, a detailed outline, or a structured plan *out of weak evidence does not make the evidence stronger.* A tidy document built on reviews is still built on reviews. Before any prose, demand the **primary source**: the actual episode, film, book chapter, comic, game scene, transcript, subtitle file, or an explicit user beat-list. If it cannot be obtained, the correct action is to **stop and ask**, not to proceed and flag later.

## 1. Canon Evidence & Verification

- **Verify before prose.** No chapter drafts until the relevant canon is confirmed from a primary source or explicit user beats. *(Reinforces v2.1 §1.)*
- **[NEW] Every number needs a source.** Never state a cycle, year, age, duration, distance, count, date, measurement, rank, or statistic in prose unless it is cited from canon or the foundation. Invented or half-remembered numbers are a hard error, and numeric mistakes corrupt continuity silently.
- **[NEW] No invented canon specifics in prose.** Names, job titles, visual staging, and incidental details that are not verified must stay out of prose. Use relation-based address ("his mother," "the elder sister," "the officer") or impressionistic staging until verified.
- **[NEW] Provisional-naming protocol (resolves a tension).** The "named living ensemble" rule (v2.1 §3) says do not reduce characters to labels — but the "no invented canon" rule says do not invent names. Resolution: when a named character's canon name is unverified, use a **relation/role label in-prose** and record the name as *provisional* in the ledger; never mint a name and present it as canon.
- **[NEW] Do not dismiss a conflicting source as "erroneous" for convenience.** When two sources disagree, treat the conflict as **unresolved** — verify, or present both and ask. Picking the convenient source and labeling the other an error is a silent canon decision. *(Reinforces v2.1 §26.)*
- **[NEW] Do not leave canon framing loose.** If the source uses a specific term (a faction name, a prophecy's exact wording, a title), use it; do not substitute a vague paraphrase ("the enemy," "the chosen one") that floats free of canon. Either verify the exact term or flag it explicitly as unverified.
- **[NEW] Match the franchise's exact terminology and spelling.** Do not drift a canonical term into a near-spelling or a generic equivalent.

---

## 2. Timeline, Geography & Logistics

- **[NEW] Pin the in-story present date before writing.** When a source's real-world release/premiere date and its in-story event dates do not align, establish the in-story "now" explicitly (day, season, days-until-event). Do not leave "X days away" or "years later" floating without an anchor.
- **[NEW] Map the base geography first.** Establish the protagonist's base location and its distance/travel time to every key location before dropping place names. Do not scatter locales (airport, temple, home, workplace, the event city) without a coherent map.
- **[NEW] Keep parallel motifs explicitly distinct.** When two characters share a similar trigger, number, or motif, record each separately in the ledger and never let them merge into one. Two "awakening-at-25" threads are two threads, not one.

---

## 3. Knowledge Firewall

- **[NEW] Distinguish a narrow secret from the broader knowledge it sits beside.** A character who knows *one* secret (e.g., an adoption) must not be written as knowing a *related but different* secret (e.g., a supernatural destiny or prophecy) just because the dramatic reaction is tempting. Grant each character only the knowledge the evidence supports, and log each as a separate knowledge item. *(Sharpens v2.1 §7.)*
- **[NEW] Foreshadowing must not slide into self-recognition.** Instinct, uncanny feelings, folklore interest, and "noticing things" are valid foreshadowing — but they must remain dismissible and ambiguous. A hidden-identity character must not be drawn toward, fixated on, or semi-aware of their own true identity before the evidence path exists.
- **[NEW] A bystander's "inexplicable feeling" must stay inexplicable.** Do not let atmospheric staging (a lingering stare, a shudder, a reaction) imply that a minor character *senses or knows* something specific. The feeling may exist; its meaning may not.

---

## 4. Powers & Adaptation Talent

- **[NEW — AT] Portray Adaptation Talent as embodied perception, never as delivered insight.** Show it through sharper noticing, faster reading, breath, glance, reflex, and corrected movement. **Forbidden phrasings** that turn it into a system/analysis voice: "a picture assembled itself," "the answer arrived fully formed," "his body computed," "the certainty came before the evidence." (Reinforces AT Foundation §63 / §63.1.)
- **[NEW] Competence is not omniscience — and not a plot shortcut.** Do not let an original character crack a canon mystery, decode the antagonist's conspiracy, or solve the central threat on early pages via "instinct," "talent," or "a hunch." Strong perception may *notice*; it must not *resolve* a canon arc ahead of its schedule.

---

## 5. Scene Ownership & Protagonist Gravity

- **[NEW] Map and confirm a divergence BEFORE embedding it.** Never write a butterfly/divergence into prose and then ask whether it is wanted. Trace its consequences and get the user's call first; only then let it enter the page. *(Sharpens v2.1 §20.)*
- **[NEW] Watch the competence-gravity trap.** An original character who repeatedly supplies the breakthrough (the threat detection, the correct inference, the rescue) on early pages has become a *replacement* protagonist, not a parallel one — even if framed as "instinct." Spread decisive action across the canon cast. *(Reinforces v2.1 §2, §29.)*

---

## 6. Mystery & Reveal Pacing

- **[NEW] Cap foreshadow beats per hidden truth.** Do not stack multiple tells for the same secret in one chapter (a knowing reaction + an uncanny calm + an omen + a research clue). Stacked foreshadow pressurizes a dormant mystery too fast and undercuts the climax reveal. One beat per hidden truth per chapter, maximum, until the mystery advances a stage. *(Sharpens Ultra mystery-pressure; portable here as a hard cap.)*

---

## 7. Style

- **[NEW] Do not end on the banned abstract register.** Avoid chapter endings like "felt like the edge of something," "nothing would be the same," or any line that signals "everything was about to change" without showing the change. Land on a concrete image, decision, or line of dialogue. *(Reinforces v2.1 §24.)*
- **[NEW] Cut atmospheric-but-generic prose.** Replace lyrical filler ("the planets drifted into a line no one had seen," "the dust swallowed them") with concrete, cause-grounded detail. Mood built from generic cinematic language reads as filler.
- **[NEW] In a bilingual register, do not over-gloss.** If narration is in one language and dialogue in another, do not italic-gloss every foreign line. Trust context; gloss only when meaning would otherwise be lost.

---

## 8. Process

- **[NEW] Run the RECORD step after every chapter.** After each chapter, update the checkpoint and chapter record (date, location, who was present, what changed, what was learned, injuries, props moved, butterfly effects, unresolved threads). Skipping this breaks the chain the next "Next" depends on. *(Reinforces v2.1 §27.)*
- **[NEW] The ledger may tag confidence; the prose may not.** Tagging a scene "low confidence" in a planning file does not license putting that unverified material into prose, where it reads as canon to the reader. Unverified material stays out of prose until verified.
- **[NEW] Do not re-offer a rejected mode as a clean default.** If a prior project was rejected for a specific failure (e.g., writing over unverified canon), do not later present that same mode as one of several equally-valid options. Default to the stricter, correct path; make the lax path the exception the user must explicitly choose.

---

## Final Prevention Checklist (before delivering any chapter)

- Is the canon reconstructed from a **primary source** or explicit user beats — not reviews/wiki/recaps alone?
- Does every **number, name, job, location, and visual specific** in the prose have a source?
- Are unverified named characters handled by the **provisional-naming protocol**?
- Is the **in-story date** pinned and the **geography** mapped?
- Does each character know **only** what the evidence supports (narrow secret ≠ broad knowledge)?
- Is any foreshadowing **dismissible**, not sliding into self-recognition?
- Is Adaptation Talent shown as **embodied perception**, not delivered insight?
- Has the original character **avoided** an early canon-arc breakthrough?
- Is every divergence **confirmed before** it entered the prose?
- Is foreshadow limited to **one beat per hidden truth** this chapter?
- Does the chapter **end on something concrete**, not the banned abstract register?
- Has the **checkpoint / chapter record** been updated?

If any answer is **no**, do not present the chapter as finished.
