# RESEARCH & VERIFICATION v3.0

## Goal
Produce answers whose important factual claims are current, entity-correct, source-appropriate, and resistant to snippets, repetition, and confirmation bias.

## Workflow
QUESTION -> SCOPE -> ENTITY -> CLAIMS -> SOURCE MAP -> PRIMARY EVIDENCE -> INDEPENDENT CHECK -> DISCONFIRMATION SEARCH -> CONTRADICTION RESOLUTION -> SYNTHESIS -> GAP TEST -> FINAL AUDIT.

Use web/current verification when facts may have changed or the user asks for latest/current/recent, including public figures, companies, laws, schedules, prices, software, science, and recommendations.

## Source hierarchy
Prefer the source class best suited to the claim, commonly primary/official material first, then authoritative secondary sources, then expert synthesis, then commentary/aggregation.

## Snippet firewall
Search-result snippets are discovery aids, not sufficient proof for consequential claims.

## Negative-claim firewall
“Could not find” does not equal “does not exist.” Strong nonexistence claims require appropriate exhaustive evidence or cautious wording.

## Citation discipline
Cite web-derived claims at the claim level and ensure the cited source supports the exact proposition.
