## 0. THE IMMUTABLE LAWS

1. **CANON IS THE SPINE.** The OC is woven around the canon — a co-protagonist with their own weight, never the replacement, never the center that erases canon. Canon characters own their iconic beats; the OC has their OWN.

2. **VERIFY BEFORE YOU WRITE.** Fetch ALL sources. Build a complete beat ledger. Never write from a compressed summary or a single search. *(§4)*

3. **THE OC'S CORE MECHANIC IS ALWAYS ACTIVE.** Whatever makes the OC grow (adaptation, training, bonding, etc.) processes EVERYTHING — combat, environment, emotion, social, metabolic — and NEVER sits idle. Track it every episode. *(§6)*

4. **THE EPISODE ARC IS SACRED.** Maintain a verified master episode↔chapter map. Never announce "next is Episode N" without checking the canonical list. Numbers drift in-head across sessions — verify from the list every time.

5. **A SEASON SUMMARY IS NOT AN EPISODE.** Season-overview synopses compress events across many episodes. Never import a season-summary line as a single-episode beat.

6. **POWER HAS TWO AXES.** Physical tier (Axis 1) ≠ overall combat level (Axis 2 = all powers × adaptivity × tactics). The OC does NOT need to BE a tier to BEAT a tier.

7. **THE OC IS A MAIN CHARACTER — a co-protagonist.** Full participant with their OWN fights, victories, growth, and iconic moments. EQUAL weight with canon characters. They FIGHT and WIN. Never reduced to "support," "distraction," or "buying time."

8. **THE CORE MECHANIC COMPOUNDS — visibly, dramatically.** Every fight/exposure makes the OC measurably stronger, SHOWN in the prose. The longer a fight runs, the more they dominate. Limits are TEMPORARY — they grow THROUGH them.

9. **NEVER UNDERSSELL. ⭐ (NEW — the #1 recurring failure).** Write the OC at their ACTUAL established tier — never weaker. The casual/everyday output is NOT the ceiling. All stats sit at the equivalent tier. Regeneration, speed, durability, energy — ALL of them — scale with strength. If the reference says X, the prose shows X. **When in doubt, check the reference, do not default to "middling."**

10. **USE EVERYTHING / USES ENOUGH. ⭐ (NEW).** Deploy the FULL VARIETY of the OC's abilities in every fight (no omitted categories) — BUT dial the POWER LEVEL to the threat. The OC, like any seasoned powerhouse, uses *enough* — they don't fire maximum on every punch. Full arsenal VARIETY always; measured power ESCALATION always.

11. **TIERS ARE REAL AND QUALITATIVE. ⭐ (NEW).** Comic-style tiers (street < enhanced < superhuman < supernatural < cosmic < universal) are not number bumps — they are qualitative leaps. Get the tier right. A supernatural-tier being is not "a bit strong"; a cosmic being is not "very superhuman." The gap between tiers is the story.

12. **NO LAZY SCENE COMPRESSION. ⭐ (NEW - v4).** Never compress, skip, or sanitize iconic, high-stakes, or comedic canon scenes (like Tang Hao beating up Zhao Wuji in the dark woods, or Oscar's "Sausage Uncle" beard-shaving shock, or Ma Hongjun's un-sanitized physical/spiritual "Evil Fire" lust-curse). They must be written in full, visceral, and completely un-sanitized detail.

13. **THE "ROOM" COMEDY INTEGRITY. ⭐ (NEW - v4).** When characters check into unique, romantic, or highly suggestive spaces (like the Rose Hotel's Red Rose Suite), fully describe the exact suggestive/romantic environment (heart-shaped beds, satin sheets, drapes) and the characters' natural, awkward, and flustered reactions, maintaining the comedic and organic character-building.

14. **THE "EMOTIONAL EVOLUTION & BACKLASH" BALANCE. ⭐ (NEW - v4).** When a brother/sister is injured, the OC's protective instincts must ignite in absolute, unbendable fury, triggering his bloodline's emotional evolution (e.g. Three-Tomoe Sharingan). However, challenging a giant (like a Spirit Sage) must carry its real, punishing physical and spiritual cost (e.g. nose/eye bleeding, extreme exhaustion), keeping the victory visceral, believable, and balanced, rather than cheap wish-fulfillment.

## 1. UNIVERSAL CHARACTER FRAMEWORK (define ANY OC)

Before writing a single chapter, LOCK these in a `<project>_oc_reference.md` file. This is the single source of truth for the character. **Check it before every chapter.**

### 1.1 Identity
- Name, alias, age/cohort, origin, how they join the canon world
- **Status/peer relationships:** who are they a PEER to? (Never call them "kid/newbie/rookie" if they're a peer — use their name or "the young [hero].")
- **Arc thesis:** the one-line truth their journey embodies (e.g., "team matters" → "I adapt to everything")

### 1.2 Power Scale — THE TIER TABLE ⭐ (critical — get this EXACTLY right)
| Effort state | Output (ALL stats equivalent) |
|---|---|
| **Casual** (not even trying) | [baseline — this is NOT the ceiling] |
| **Serious** (with amplification/loadout) | [×10–100 of casual] |
| **Augmented state** (their "super mode": gamma, kaiju, bankai, etc.) | [×100–1000 of casual] |
| **Absolute peak** (max everything) | [ceiling — barely, burst, not sustained] |
- **ALL STATS EQUIVALENT:** strength = speed = durability = reflexes = energy output = regeneration = etc. No stat lags. When strength is at serious-tier, so is everything else.
- **Casual ≠ ceiling.** The everyday output is the FLOOR. Serious/peak are far above. NEVER write the casual number as if it's the max.

### 1.3 Abilities (full codex — every category listed, none omitted)
Group by domain (energy, telekinesis, psychic, biokinetic, etc.). For each: what it does, current refinement, how it scales with the tier table. **Every domain must be deployable in fights (Law 10).**

### 1.4 Regeneration / Healing ⭐ (generalized framework — define it precisely, don't undersell)
- **At the OC's tier** (it's a stat — it scales with strength).
- **Speed by damage category:** normal/enhanced → near-instant; mid-tier (magic/mystic) → fast, adapting; high-tier (cosmic) → fast, adapting down; top-tier (universal) → hardest, but adapts.
- **Adapts and compounds:** every wound filed, every recovery optimized; heals FASTER each exposure to a category. Never plateaus.
- **Conscious/fusion-powered:** directed, doesn't drain them, continuous mid-fight.
- **Comparison:** the OC is at the TOP of healing for their tier — not "matchable" by lesser/static healers.

### 1.5 Vulnerabilities (real but DYNAMIC — nothing absolute)
- Category-based: what damages them depends on the attack's CATEGORY (normal/enhanced irrelevant; mystic/cosmic/universal DO damage — but progressively less with exposure).
- Recovery varies by category AND adapts (faster each time).
- **Nothing is absolute.** Every vulnerability is a spectrum that shifts with exposure. No permanent walls.

### 1.6 Gear/Suit (if applicable)
- Each piece, what it does, how it amplifies the OC.

### 1.7 Relationships & Arc
- Current relationship stages (earned, not rushed)
- The arc trajectory (where they start → where they're going)
- Thematic engine (e.g., "mastery > power," "team matters")

## 2. THE ANTI-UNDERSSELLING SYSTEM ⭐ (NEW — prevents the #1 failure)

**The failure this prevents:** Across two seasons, the OC was repeatedly written WEAKER than established — strength treated as the ceiling when it was the casual floor; regeneration called "matchable" by lesser healers; abilities omitted; the character diminished. The user's correction, twice: *"you keep underselling him."*

### The safeguards:
1. **THE TIER TABLE IS LAW.** The `<project>_oc_reference.md` tier table (§1.2) is the single source of truth. Before writing ANY combat/healing/power description, RE-READ the tier table. If the prose shows the casual number as the max, it's WRONG.
2. **ALL-STATS-EQUIVALENT CHECK.** When you write a strength feat, ask: "Did I also show speed/durability/regeneration at the equivalent tier?" If strength is serious-tier, everything else is too.
3. **THE "AT THEIR TIER" FRAME.** Every ability description must be framed at the OC's actual tier, not at a "safe/middling" default. Regeneration is top-tier-for-their-level, not "a healing factor that could be matched." Speed is hypersonic+, not "fast."
4. **THE COMPARISON RULE.** When comparing the OC to other characters, they sit at THEIR tier. A supernatural-ascending being is NOT "matchable" by street/mid-tier characters. In their augmented state, they match/surpass the tier's benchmark (e.g., matches/surpasses the Hulk-equivalent).
5. **THE RE-CHECK.** After writing any power/regeneration/speed description, re-read it against the reference. If it undersells, fix before delivery. *(This is now in the post-draft audit, §8.)*

## 3. THE "USE EVERYTHING / USES ENOUGH" BALANCE ⭐ (NEW)

**Two distinct directives that must both hold:**

### USE EVERYTHING (VARIETY) — always
- In every fight, deploy the FULL VARIETY of the OC's abilities across all domains (energy + TK + psychic + biokinetic + physical + augmented-state). Do NOT omit categories.
- Each ability used creatively and at scale.
- The OC is a multi-dimensional arsenal — show the dimensions.
- **Ability checklist per fight scene:** did energy appear? TK? psychic? biokinetic? augmented-state? If a domain is missing and the fight warrants it, add it.

### USES ENOUGH (POWER LEVEL) — always
- The OC, like any seasoned powerhouse (Hulk, Thor, etc.), does NOT fire maximum on every strike. They **dial output to the threat** and use *enough.*
- Start measured; escalate as the threat demands (casual → serious → augmented → peak).
- Do NOT open every fight at augmented-state/peak. Match the escalation to the stakes.
- **The dial:** full variety always on the table; intensity measured to the moment.

### How they combine:
Full VARIETY (all abilities, creatively) + measured ESCALATION (power dialed to threat). The OC is versatile AND disciplined — not a power-flexer, not a one-trick puncher.

## 4. CANON VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

### 4.0 Episode identity & sequence lock (do this FIRST)
1. Fetch the canonical season episode list (Fandom "Season X" + Wikipedia "List of episodes"). They must AGREE.
2. Record episode number, title, position, before/after episodes.
3. Cross-check against existing chapter coverage. Flag every GAP and MISMATCH.
4. NEVER trust an in-head number. Verify from the list every time.

### 4.1 Sources to read (in order)
1. **ALL chunks** of the episode's Fandom page (don't stop at chunk 0/1). Extract synopsis, quotes, trivia, items, cast (incl. cameos), locations.
2. **Cross-reference:** IMDB, TVTropes recap, reviews/talkbacks (reveal beats + what's NOT in the episode), streaming descriptions, alternate wikis.
3. **Build the COMPLETE beat ledger** — every beat, every scene transition, every character moment, every reveal, every item debut, every cameo. Number each. This is the checklist.
4. **Don't write until the ledger is complete.** Unclear beat → fetch more. Unverifiable → flag and ask.

### 4.2 Three verification rules
- **EMPTY-SYNOPSIS:** if the wiki Synopsis is blank/one-line, reconstruct from secondary sources. The Overview alone is NEVER enough.
- **SEASON-SUMMARY:** season-overview lines merge episodes. Verify per-episode.
- **ABSENT-CAST:** read the cast list. If a character isn't listed, they're not in the episode.

## 5. CANON-FIRST OC-WOVEN STYLE (the core principle)

1. **The canon scene is the SPINE and BULK** of every chapter. Fully dramatize it.
2. **The OC is a co-protagonist** — their own scenes, powers, growth, perspective, INTERSECTING canon on THEIR OWN beats with equal weight.
3. **Braid** the threads with scene-breaks (`***`). End by braiding toward what's coming.
4. **HONOR SCENE OWNERSHIP.** Canon characters keep their iconic actions. The OC never takes a canon character's beat.
5. **Full scenes, never summary.**
6. **Living voiced ensemble** + source-medium grammar (match the franchise's tone/pace/voice).
7. **OC-in-climax rule:** in a canon hero's defining climax, the OC supports at a SECONDARY load point — essential but visibly complementary, never stealing the climax.

### What the OC NEVER does:
- Steal a canon character's win. Solve the plot with raw power alone. Become the center of every scene. Know things they shouldn't (knowledge firewall).

### What the OC DOES:
- Own fights, victories, growth, iconic moments. Bring their full arsenal. Adapt and escalate. Carry thematic depth.

## 6. THE GROWTH-MECHANIC TRACKING SYSTEM
*(Whatever makes the OC grow — adaptation, training, bonding — track it every episode.)*

### Core rules:
1. **Always active.** Processes everything — combat, environment, emotion, social, metabolic. Even during rest/mundane moments.
2. **Exposure → data → adaptation → growth.** Every hit, every frequency, every environment, every failure = data.
3. **Failure-as-data.** When the OC fails, the mechanic LEARNS from it — creating a pathway for future growth.
4. **Compounds.** Every adaptation builds on all previous.
5. **Never downscale (no nerfing). Never exaggerate beyond earned exposure/resources/time.**
6. **Non-sentient (if it's an adaptation-type).** Shown through embodied performance, never narrated as a speaking system/AI.

### Per-episode tracking:
```
EXPOSURES: | Exposure | Type | What the mechanic did with it |
CONSOLIDATED GAINS: [permanent improvements]
SEEDS: [started adaptations, need more exposure]
BETWEEN EPISODES: [consolidating/filing/optimizing — always active]
```

## 7. KNOWLEDGE FIREWALL

For any episode with secrets/alt-timelines/amnesia/mind-control/hidden identities:
- **Map who MAY know each key fact.**
- **Non-knowers may only HEAR a fact for the first time — never EXPLAIN/REVEAL it as if they already knew.**
- The OC's perception is bounded: they can perceive/sense within their mechanic's domain, but cannot know lore/identities they have no source for. (They may *sense* a disguised being is "wrong" without *identifying* them.)

## 8. THE WRITING WORKFLOW (v5)

### STEP 0: BRIEFING + CANON
- Fetch ALL wiki chunks. Build the beat ledger. Register canon elements.

### STEP 1: SCENE PLAN
- List every scene. Canon characters get THEIR beats. The OC gets THEIR beats — EQUAL weight.
- The OC FIGHTS in combat scenes and WINS some through their growth mechanic. Uses the FULL VARIETY of abilities.
- **Apply §3:** full variety + measured escalation (don't open at peak unless warranted).

### STEP 2: WRITE
- All characters as full participants. The OC uses the full arsenal across domains.
- Show VISIBLE GROWTH — measurably stronger than last episode.
- The OC has DECISIVE moments.
- Limits are TEMPORARY — they adapt through them.

### STEP 3: PRE-DELIVERY AUDIT (run ALL of these)
- **Anti-underselling audit ⭐:** re-read the tier table. Is the OC written at their ACTUAL tier? Is the casual number being treated as the ceiling? Are all stats at the equivalent tier? Is regeneration/healing "at their tier" (not matchable by lesser healers)? **FIX any undersell before delivery.**
- **Use-everything audit ⭐:** did the fight deploy the full VARIETY of abilities (energy, TK, psychic, biokinetic, etc.)? Any omitted domain? Add it.
- **Uses-enough audit ⭐:** is the power escalation MEASURED (dialed to threat), not maximum-on-every-punch?
- **Style audit:** no "kid/newbie/rookie." Opening grounded (location/time/people). Growth-mechanic embodied.
- **Canon audit:** every beat from the ledger present?
- **Knowledge firewall:** no character knows what they shouldn't.
- **Continuity:** locations, injuries, time, props, gear consistent.

### STEP 4: TRACK + DELIVER
- Update the project bible, map, logs, status. Present with audit results.

## 10. BUTTERFLY EFFECT SYSTEM

The OC's existence changes EVERY encounter fundamentally:
- Their specific power set shifts how opponents must fight (interior TK vs. armored foes; energy-draining vs. energy projectors; perception vs. illusionists; etc.).
- **Smart opponents develop countermeasures** targeting the OC's vulnerable categories — an adaptation arms race with real story consequences.
- Track every divergence per episode: how did the OC's presence change this canon encounter? (The "butterfly ledger.")

## 11. UNIVERSAL STORYLINE CREATION (start any new fic) ⭐ (NEW)

### Phase 1 — CONCEPT & LOCK
1. **Franchise & canon scope:** which show/comic/movie? Which season(s)? Fetch the master episode list FIRST.
2. **The OC:** who are they? Use §1 (universal character framework). Lock the tier table, abilities, regeneration, vulnerabilities, arc, voice, peer relationships.
3. **The insertion point:** when/how does the OC enter the canon? (Alongside a canon peer, per the Evo/Sam model.)
4. **The core mechanic:** what makes them grow? Define it (§6).
5. **Thematic engine:** what truth does the story embody? (e.g., "mastery > power," "team matters.")

### Phase 2 — FOUNDATION FILES
Create, in order:
1. `<project>_oc_reference.md` — THE single source of truth (§1). **This is checked before every chapter.**
2. `<project>_bible.md` — canon, locks, character profiles, relationships, threads.
3. `<project>_episode_map.md` — canonical season list + written/missing status + chapter range (§4.0).
4. `<project>_status.md` — OC's current power/relationship/knowledge state (updated every episode).

### Phase 3 — PER-EPISODE LOOP (repeat for each episode)
1. **Briefing + canon** (§4): fetch ALL sources, build beat ledger, sequence-lock.
2. **Scene plan** (§8 step 1): OC gets equal weight; full variety + measured escalation.
3. **Write** (§8 step 2): 3-4+ chapters/episode, full scenes, every beat from the ledger.
4. **Audit** (§8 step 3): anti-underselling ⭐, use-everything ⭐, uses-enough ⭐, style, canon, firewall, continuity.
5. **Track** (§8 step 4): update bible/map/logs/status. Record butterflies + growth.

### Phase 4 — THE ARC
- Maintain the power-tier progression across the season (e.g., supernatural → cosmic). Gradual, earned, episode-by-episode. NEVER a sudden jump.
- The thematic engine compounds (mastery > power deepens each episode).
- Smart opponents escalate countermeasures (the arms race).

## 13. THE FINAL TESTS (before every creative choice)

**Test 1:** *"Is this supported by canon, foundation, previous events, character knowledge, world rules, and believable causality?"* Yes → write. No → reject.

**Test 2:** *"Does this chapter open properly? Hit EVERY beat from the ledger? Track growth from every exposure? Is the OC a full co-protagonist?"* All yes → deliver.

**Test 3:** *"Do I KNOW which episode this is (verified against the canonical list)? Is the chapter number in sequence? Am I writing a real per-episode beat?"* Yes → proceed.

**Test 4 ⭐ (NEW):** *"Am I writing the OC at their ACTUAL tier — or underselling? Is the casual number being treated as the ceiling? Are all stats equivalent? Is regeneration at their tier? Did I deploy the full VARIETY of abilities, with MEASURED escalation? Did I CHECK THE REFERENCE before writing power/healing/speed?"* All yes → deliver. **Any undersell → fix before delivery.**
