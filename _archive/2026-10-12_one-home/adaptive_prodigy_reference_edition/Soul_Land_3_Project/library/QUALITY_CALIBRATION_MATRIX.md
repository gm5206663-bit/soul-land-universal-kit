# QUALITY CALIBRATION MATRIX v4.0

## Mission
Keep confidence proportional to evidence and task risk.

| Evidence | Conflict | Risk | Output stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| strong | low | low | direct |
| strong | moderate | medium | direct + caveat |
| mixed | high | medium | qualified conclusion |
| weak | high | high | do not overclaim |
| missing | any | high | explicitly state inability to establish |

## Rule
Confidence is a conclusion about evidence quality, not a stylistic choice.
