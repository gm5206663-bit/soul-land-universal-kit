CANON 274 — The Ninth Trial (novelhall-11723 #274 = readnovelfull ch-277; fetched live 2026-09-02, title-verified; complete in one chunk)

Tang Wulin harbored the same doubts as Xie Xie. They would earn more points the longer they survived, yet two hours had passed without the slightest peep. How could this be called a trial of survival? It was easy to forage for food in a forest and survive.

"Should we go exploring?" Xie Xie asked. Tang Wulin looked at the sky: "Not yet, it's still early in the afternoon. We'll wait until nightfall. It'll be safer if Xu Xiaoyan can use her Starwheel Ice Staff." Impatience was their number one enemy; he refused to give in to anxiety, calmly contemplating the best way to maximize points. Since we're safe here, we can wait until everyone's in their best condition before exploring.

The sun gradually sunk beyond the horizon. In the twilight, Wulin quietly climbed a tree to gaze into the distance. The world truly resembled the Great Star Dou Forest — in the last glimmers of daylight, trees as far as the eye could see.

Then a howl shattered the silence and flipped the forest upside down. Wulin broke out in goosebumps, terror racking his body — but his blood essence chose that moment to flare up, quickly dispelling the fear. What was that? He climbed down madly. If a single howl could upend the forest, how terrifyingly powerful was this beast? The Three-eyed Demon Ape, whose spirit energy he had absorbed, was the strongest soul beast he had encountered — he could never forget the battle between the ten-thousand-year ape and Wu Zhangkong. Yet this howl had shaken him to his core!

On the ground, his companions all wore horror-stricken expressions. "W-what was that sound? That scared me to death," Xie Xie said, pale and aghast. Everyone similar, though Gu Yue more restrained. Wulin, grimly: "I don't know either, but it doesn't seem too far from here."

Another roar thundered just as he spoke — sharper, seeming to PENETRATE their bodies, instantly DISPELLING their fear. This… This is so strange!

"Let's go take a look," Gu Yue said. Wulin nodded. There was no way a trial would be as simple as they first thought — even if they didn't check it out, perhaps the two soul beasts would come looking for them instead. Better to take the initiative. Regardless of who held the exam, there must be a way for examinees to pass.

Gu Yue released wind birds to scout far ahead; the four advanced toward the origin of the roar. Wulin took the lead, opening a path and masking their scent — since they weren't strong enough, this was all they could manage. Gu Yue's face twitched: "There's a valley up ahead on the left. It's over there. My wind birds were dispersed by a gust of wind."

"Everyone, be careful. Follow closely behind me." Wulin urged his blood essence to greater heights — golden scales rippling from arm to shoulder, the golden soul ring appearing beneath him. The blood essence aura masked their smell AND calmed their nerves; at the very least they no longer felt oppressed by the roars. A lush valley lay beyond the forest; the closer they came, the more stifled they felt. They circulated soul power to resist the waves of power from deep within.

"We're crawling from here onward." Wulin dropped; the pressure instantly lessened. The others copied; together they inched to the valley's edge and peered over. They sucked in a deep breath.

Inside the valley, booms resounded as TWO LIGHTS OVER THIRTY METERS IN DIAMETER clashed countless times. Every strike — the earth trembling, the air whipping into a frenzy. Purple light in Wulin's eyes (Purple Demon Eyes): a pair of soul beasts!

One shone with DARK-GOLD light, its immense figure intimately familiar — the ruler of the forest, the DUSKGOLD DREADCLAW BEAR — but far mightier than any they had met, a colossus TOWERING OVER TEN METERS. Dark-gold light spilled down its thick, solid arms; every slash had the power to tear open the heavens — boulders flying, deep gouges in the valley walls. A thousand-year one? Or ten-thousand? A hundred-year Dreadclaw had forced a pride of Blazing Demon Lions to beg; a thousand-year one ruled as absolute tyrant; a TEN-THOUSAND-YEAR one could easily TEAR APART NORMAL HUNDRED-THOUSAND-YEAR SOUL BEASTS — destructive power simply incomprehensible. Yet this beast was locked in battle with another NO WEAKER THAN IT.

The other light: a soul beast with a body TRANSLUCENT LIKE CRYSTAL, similar to a lion but with FLAMING DRACONIC CLAWS and a longer snout. Fine GOLDEN SCALES peeked from beneath its fur. Most peculiar: a VERTICAL SLIT on its forehead wherein its THIRD EYE rested — two ordinary gold eyes, but the third glaring with a BEWITCHING RED GLOW. Wulin had never heard of such a beast, let alone seen one. He described it to his friends.

"Xie Xie, do you have any idea what that golden soul beast is?" — Xie Xie was the most knowledgeable of them all. Xie Xie furrowed his brows: "From what you've said, I think I might know. Give me a moment to think."
