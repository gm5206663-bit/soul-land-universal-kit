To young boys and girls like Tang Wulin and the others, gems were nothing more than pretty stones — they were far more interested in the kind of place a Three-eyed Golden Lion would choose to reside in. "Do you think we can take the gems?" Xie Xie gazed longingly. "Don't be so greedy," Tang Wulin said. "Do you see me trying anything? If anyone could just waltz in and take those gems, why would Shrek even leave them for us? Use your common sense."

Past another bend the cave opened into a wide space — and a growl; a figure shrouded in golden light POUNCED. Goosebumps: Wulin's instant conclusion — a SECOND Three-eyed Golden Lion lived here (beasts that fight ten-thousand-year Dreadclaw Bears head-to-head!). No chance to escape; it had noticed them. "Run!" — but he exploded with golden light himself: Golden Dragon Body, right arm swollen, hand a claw, three-meter dark-gold blades of light from the swipe. He held nothing back in the face of death. The figure dropped with a metallic thud near him; Wulin retreated seven or eight steps before finding footing, blood essence rolling off him. His teammates formed up instead of obeying — their refusal filled him with joy; Gu Yue's water shield covered his retreat (her action delighting rather than surprising him).

The beast: similar to the lion above but MUCH smaller — under one and a half meters tall, shoulders about two meters wide; aside from size and its CLOSED third eye, identical. "A JUVENILE Three-eyed Golden Lion?" Xie Xie cried. "Could we be any luckier?" His analysis: the third eye is the strongest trait — opened only at a certain level, super-powerful once open, bringing good fortune to companions; THIS one: ten-year level, maybe hundred-year at most. "An amazing opportunity! Xiaoyan — don't let the hundred-year rank bother you; if you can absorb its soul ring, it'll be more powerful than a ten-thousand-year soul ring! It could even yield a soul bone — Three-eyed Golden Lion bones are ranked number one!" Gu Yue (furrowed, whispered): "Is this a good idea?" Wulin: "I think we can do it."

The first clash had left deep claw marks in its scales (no match for the Golden Dragon Dreadclaw); Wulin a bit weaker than it, negligibly. He beckoned, welcoming the challenge: "Come. Let's fight." Grass flew; teammates released souls. PEAK CONDITION NOTE: first time since breaking the second seal, he used bloodline AND martial soul together — blood essence enveloping the Bluesilver Grass, slowly changing its innate nature (an evolution seeded; completion not soon — like the first seal, adaptation takes time). Bind! The lion retreated in a flash and fired a lightning-like beam; Wulin snorted, stood his ground, full Golden Dragon Body, claw strike — explosive boom, a few steps back but overpowered this time. The Dreadclaw soared: nails an inch longer, and (crucially) consuming much less soul power — fueled by blood essence, usable freely.

Gu Yue: stone spikes sprouting around the lion, imprisoning it. Golden light flared, demolishing the prison instantly; freed, a dangerous light in its eyes — then A WAVE OF DIZZINESS struck Wulin and Gu Yue (its spiritual attack), two steps backward, and the lion slashed at Wulin's belly to disembowel — THREE FIGURES descended like a tornado to obstruct it: Xie Xie, Light Dragon Clones + Light Dragon Storm combined, a knife-tapping-glass onslaught holding the lion mid-air. The juvenile's profile, proven: defenses sturdy but body strength NOT impressive — quick, physical + spiritual attacks, but not tough enough to endure endless assault from the team.

"Xiaoyan, we're blitzing it!" — second ring, Bluesilver Impaling Array's spiky embrace. Blitzkrieg was the only option: the adult could return any moment; once it did, no hope of survival. Bizarre golden light gushed from the lion to hold off the grassy spears — the grass went limp at the touch, BARELY failing to pierce.

But at that moment — A STARWHEEL appeared beneath the Three-eyed Golden Lion, and its body instantly went stiff. In a flash of silver, two figures appeared right in front of it: Gu Yue and Tang Wulin. Gu Yue's control over space allowed her to bring one person along on short teleports. No ability could have been more appropriate.
