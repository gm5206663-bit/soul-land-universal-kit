CANON 275 — Auspicious Emperor (novelhall-11723 #275 = readnovelfull ch-278; fetched live 2026-09-02, title-verified; both chunks, seam into 276 'Three-eyed Golden Lion')

"These two soul beasts are too powerful," Gu Yue said. "They could instantly eliminate all of us with a single swipe. I don't think being here will help us pass this trial, but why else would they have us discover these soul beasts?" Tang Wulin shook his head: quietly observe first — they seemed evenly matched; wait until both are exhausted, maybe reap rewards — "if they defeat each other and only have a single breath of life left, even we should be able to deal with them!" He knew his team's strength better than anyone; facing these lords head-on would be foolish. THE SINGLE DIFFERENCE from the spirit ascension platform: NO ESCAPE BUTTON. Dying was the only way out early — death a very real possibility, not even a scrap of their corpses remaining. Extreme caution.

The four peered again. The clashes grew fiercer; neither side's advantage readable. Suddenly both lights blazed and collided explosively once more; after separating, the lights dimmed and revealed the beasts. The Duskgold Dreadclaw Bear was in terrible condition; its opponent showed no weakness, spirited as a horse — its third eye shone brilliant red. FEAR crept into the bear's eyes as its dark-gold light dimmed further.

"I've got it!" Xie Xie blurted. "I think that golden soul beast is the auspicious beast of legends!" — The legend: in the Great Star Dou Forest there once lived an auspicious beast, the AUSPICIOUS EMPEROR. While it lived, fortune smiled on all beasts of the forest and their cultivation advanced faster; when it died, misfortune plagued the forest and drove the beast tide against Shrek City (from the Spirit Ice Douluo's legend). "The Auspicious Emperor is a THREE-EYED GOLDEN LION. The legends say the Spirit Ice Douluo absorbed its soul ring and soul bone — it provided a skull, the strongest spiritual-type bone, always ranked number one. No wonder even the Duskgold Dreadclaw Bear can't beat it — the Auspicious Emperor is on a whole different level!"

Xu Xiaoyan: "It looks so cool! It'd be great if it could become my spirit soul." Xie Xie scoffed ("Keep dreaming") — a beast supposedly smarter than humans. He then remembered: in the Spirit Ice Douluo's biography, the Three-eyed Golden Lion TRANSFORMED INTO A HUMAN and fell in love with the Spirit Ice Douluo; it later sacrificed itself to become his soul ring and save him from a deadly crisis — and that selfless act led to the forest's decline and the beast tide. Wulin: "Why haven't I seen that in the biography I read?" Xie Xie: "My family collects biographies — ones normal people never touch."

Gu Yue, pensive: "Soul beasts are nearing extinction because that lion sacrificed itself? Why would the Auspicious Emperor sacrifice itself for a single human? Didn't it consider its fellow beasts?" Xie Xie: "Logic doesn't matter when someone falls in love. A person would forsake everything for love." — Love? Gu Yue couldn't understand why, but she suddenly glanced at Tang Wulin.

The earth shook; a deep rumble. Wulin attached Bluesilver Grass strands to his teammates, keeping them safe as they crawled away. The wind grew; the two beasts took their battle elsewhere — the valley's end nowhere in sight, the sounds dwindling. "Are they gone?" Collective relief. They were ants before those two; lucky not to be noticed. Xiaoyan: "Aren't those two the reason we met no soul beasts?" Wulin: the valley and surroundings likely one's territory; the other came to seize it or some benefit. Xie Xie's eyes lit: if the region belonged to the lion — a sign of good luck — there'd be treasures; "didn't they say we can bring anything we find back to the real world?" Wulin: "How about we go take a look?" Xie Xie, bursting: "This is an illusory world anyway — even if we die here, we don't really die. An amazing opportunity. If we don't go, we'll regret it forever!" Gu Yue nodded; Xiaoyan smiled ("It's up to you, Captain"). "Then we're going!" — fire in his eyes. Like newborn calves unafraid of lions: no real consequences, so confidently into what could be the Auspicious Emperor's lair.

They rappelled down the valley side on Wulin's Bluesilver Grass — steep but no great challenge, slow and steady to the bottom. Mist thick in the distance; no trace of the beasts. Guards up, scanning different directions, hearts thumping louder each step. The battle fresh in their minds — truly terrifying. COUNTLESS DEEP SCARS wound through the valley; the stone had been no different from tofu before those attacks. The floor was bare of vegetation — the work of the heaven-shaking battle.

"Captain, look!" Xiaoyan pointed. Not far: a CAVE, entrance about five meters tall, darkness shrouded within. Wulin: could this be one beast's lair? Xie Xie ran ahead, scouted, waited. "Captain, I think this could actually be the lair of the Three-eyed Golden Lion! Since we've fought a Duskgold Dreadclaw Bear, you might remember its rancid odor — this cave smells FRESH, yet a bit MUSKY. Legends describe the Three-eyed Golden Lion smelling like this." Wulin decided immediately — no time to hesitate, in case the two beasts returned. They dove in, advancing swiftly. Pitch-black, like a different world; the ceiling grew past five meters, opening into a wide space. Around a bend: TWINKLING LIGHTS — GEMSTONES littering the ground, shining with myriad brilliant prismatic lights.
