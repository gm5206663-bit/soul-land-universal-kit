The instant Xie Xie's feet touched the ground, he shot toward his opponent; with both Light and Shadow Dragon Clones active, he had no time to dally. His opponent was a power-type soul master who also had frightening ranged options — these two traits placing him in a league of his own. Without the clones Xie Xie would have found it difficult to endure the onslaught, but with them out, his soul power was quickly running out.

The three visible clones, now much faster, charged at the solitary figure in the center. Xie Xie rushed in, this time without the Light Dragon Blade. Student number two simply stood there, grim face and titanic figure lending the impression of an immovable mountain. As Xie Xie's second soul ring lit, his three Light Dragon Clones burst into action to unleash a Light Dragon Storm. He was an agility-type with exceptionally high attack power; his opponent had equally impressive defense, to the point of shrugging off the Light Dragon Blade. But Light Dragon Storm was on a completely different level — Xie Xie was confident it would injure him if it landed.

Student number two responded, the motion of his burly arms all too familiar. If Tang Wulin weren't currently meditating, his eyes would definitely have gone wide — student number two was using a TANG SECT SECRET ART, CONTROLLING CRANE, CAPTURING DRAGON! An aura expanded violently outwards; upon impact, the clones were knocked out of Light Dragon Storm. Then a pressing motion with his hands as his second soul ring lit — the air in the arena changed, rumbling like thunder, and Xie Xie's three Light and three Shadow Dragon Clones were sent flying backwards, converging in the air to reform into one.

Student number two stomped off the ground, launching himself like an artillery shell. His first soul ring lit; he threw a punch. A shockwave propagated outwards. Xie Xie was in no position to dodge — repeated soul skills had left his soul power and mind in chaos, not to mention the damage from being sent flying. A GREEN LIGHT flashed over, dragging Xie Xie toward the ground and out of the shockwave's path. (Shen Yi's intervention.) Student number two ceased his advance, bowed to Shen Yi, and returned to his fellow students.

The match was over: Xie Xie's defeat. This was the strength of a Shrek Academy student — in the face of true strength, twin martial souls or type compatibility meant nothing.

Xie Xie's defeat wasn't disgraceful — he displayed his full strength, but his opponent was overwhelming.
