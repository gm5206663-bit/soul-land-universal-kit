seasons) and real cost (denser essence draws danger: it is what brought the eater). Kept
honest: when the old one's returned years lay in his own ground, he did not draw them in —
he let the ground have the body whole. It is the inside-shape of the talent — consolidated
through the ten-thousand-year gate, held whole in the King body, never fed by taking.

(roots woven through the stream bend and along the seam, so that since the old one's
passing the line is held by the ground itself) · the **boundary** (the keeping, held alone).
