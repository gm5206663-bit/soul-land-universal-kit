# THE BLADE — OC concept (nameless for a long age)

Status: concept sheet. Prose may call him **the blade**, **the weed**, **the impossible one**,
until someone names him. We do not name him ourselves.

## What he is
- A soul beast whose body is **Blue Silver Grass** — the commonest weed on the continent.
- By every rule of the world, a Blue Silver Grass is not a soul beast. He is. That single
  contradiction is the seed of the whole story. **[AU premise]**
- Born in **Star Dou Forest**, middle depth, a quiet glade on the bend of a slow stream at
  the inner edge of the middle band, where his ground meets the deep band (GREEN_WORLD).
- Male in identity and voice, though his body is a plant. The world's creatures will not
  know him by gender; the prose treats him as "he" because he is our protagonist. **[AU]**

## His awareness
- **Aware from his first sprout** — but dim and dreamlike, the way a seed's memory is dim.
- Senses run through his whole body: roots taste the soil, leaves feel light, wind, rain,
  the passing of hooves and feet; he "hears" through the ground.
- He does not think in words for a long, long time. Early life is need, warmth, fear,
  memory, pattern. Language enters him slowly — from beasts who speak, from the forest's
  sounds, and eventually from A Yin.
- Voice in the story: **hybrid** — mostly his raw senses from inside; the narrator zooms out
  only at the seams of an arc to carry time and scale.

## His talent — the Adaptation (full doctrine in FOUNDATION)
- The talent touches **every part of his existence** — body, senses, mind, energy, skills,
  identity, bloodline. Nothing is outside its reach. **[user's core rule]**
- He is not "lucky." He is *caused*: he is a **real elemental mutant** — the **Sunweave**
  (core LIFE/vitality · developed WOOD · LIGHT + WATER through photosynthesis · EARTH
  partial) — and the talent is the all-effecting engine that makes a weed a soul beast at
  all and carries that elemental reality across centuries. Real mutant AND talent-effects-
  everything: both true, one whole (module M1).
- It is not a system voice and not free power: every gain is paid for; every change leaves
  him more than he was and more than he can fully understand.
- Most importantly it works on his **bloodline** — not toward royalty: he never becomes the
  Emperor's blood, never royal, never dark, never dragon. The talent refines and stabilizes
  his own Sunweave-mutated common line, which is how "a common grass rises to King-tier one
  day" is even possible (the King is the line's own canon evolution at ~10,000 years).

## His path (form-destiny)
- **Beast road.** He is not pointed at becoming human. Canon's variety of beast forms —
  half-human figures, full human figures, clone-like bodies — exists for high-tier soul
  beasts; we leave those doors open and decide each one in-story, never by decree now.
- Horizons, in order of story: survive → become → endure epochs → rise toward the grass
  throne → the meeting with the Empress → whatever comes after. Exact span: open
  ("let it go naturally").

## Relationship to A Yin (per the user's design)
- She, as the Empress, can sense her blades through the Blue Silver connection. She notices
  him when he is no longer ignorable — after he has **evolved into a Blue Silver King**
  (his ten-thousand-year-class breakthrough — the user-locked King gate, 2026-09). That is
  the rank where her domain can sense him as a King, not a blade — and where his **bloodline
  sensing** (a verified canon King trait) can recognise HER line, so the meeting is two-way
  at the root. At the gate he is a COMPLETE King: the verified canon King grade (intelligence,
  telepathy, true speech, bloodline sensing) plus his own Sunweave/talent gains. The wider
  ten-power kit is [design] territory only, never asserted as canon (BEAST_LAW §10 ledger).
- They meet **later**, when both are still in the forest (he rises before she takes human
  form — see WORLD.md timeline guess).
- Their bond type is deliberately not pinned to a single title: she is elder and kin and
  possibly the one who names him. The bond's texture will be earned across the story.

## Design rules (from the mistakes of the old project — non-negotiable)
1. He lives the ordinary life of his world first: soil, seasons, hunger, neighbors. We never
   invent reasons for the world to bench him or to lift him.
2. Everything about him is caused on-page; nothing is asserted in footers only.
3. No invented "walls" or pseudo-rules. When he cannot do something, the reason is real
   (he is rooted; he is young; the cost is too high; he is afraid).
4. He is humble, curious, warm — a natural center of whatever small society he finds — not a
   pampered prince and not a mystery the world tiptoes around.
