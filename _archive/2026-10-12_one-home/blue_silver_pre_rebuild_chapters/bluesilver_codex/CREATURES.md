## Rules this registry respects
1. **Original creatures are tagged [design] and never passed off as canon.** The night-walker,
   deep serpent, and eater are originals; canon never names them.
2. **Canon beings (A Yin, the elder King) are never staged in our deep era** beyond the
   rails WORLD.md allows.
3. **Mind law:** only the blade has an awake human-grade interior (by the talent). Beasts
   are wordless; their interiority is rendered as sensation and pattern, or by narrator
   zoom (noted as such). No speech below canon thresholds.
4. **Numbers are working figures, never prose.** Approx cultivation years for original
   creatures are internal design estimates so status is readable; they are tagged
   "design working" and never appear as exact ages inside the story prose.
5. **The blade has never killed** — enemies are turned, driven off, or released. That is a
   character law, not a world rule.
