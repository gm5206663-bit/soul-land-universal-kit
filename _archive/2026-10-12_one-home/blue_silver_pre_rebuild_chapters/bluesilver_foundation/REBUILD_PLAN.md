## 2. THE SPINE — "The Hunters"

Canon fact that was never used: **soul beasts are killed for their rings.** Every spirit
master in Star Dou is there to kill something. That is the world's arithmetic, and the
serial never once showed it.

The spine across 15 chapters:

- **The line is being culled.** Blue Silver Grass is the world's commonest plant — but a
  *cultivating* Blue Silver Grass that reaches a thousand years, ten thousand years, is a
  rare ring. Hunters who learn what a King-grade grass is worth do not leave it standing.
- **He does not know this for most of the story.** He thinks he is invisible because he is
  nothing. He is invisible because no one has yet priced him.
- **Spirit Hall's reach extends into Star Dou across the era.** Scouts map the bands.
  Reports are filed. An anomaly in the plant-type records draws attention upward.
- **The payoff (Ch14–15):** the crown ground is not just where the line's great ones stood.
  It is why they dwindled. **The dwindling was not natural.** A Yin knows. That is what she
  has carried alone for an age — and it recontextualises the entire serial on the last page.

This gives the deep era a canon spine without violating the user-locked era window, and it
makes the mercy law *dramatic* instead of decorative: a protagonist who will not kill is
faced with people who kill for a living and want his ring.

## 3. Per-chapter contract (every chapter must satisfy all seven)

1. **Canon beat** — which canon truth the chapter rests on (ring economy, Star Dou bands,
   Spirit Hall, plant-beast law, A Yin's era), and its tier of verification.
2. **Threat level** — what is trying to harm him or his ground, this chapter, concretely.
3. **Measured movement** — class gate, ring tier, or hunter rank that changes on the page.
4. **Opposition with a face** — a named or individually-rendered antagonist, not weather.
5. **Butterfly** — his existence changes something in the world, visibly, causally.
6. **Five registers** — at least three of: close sense / hard scene / dialogue / other POV /
   lyrical. No chapter may be all five paragraphs of the same voice.
7. **A turn** — the chapter ends on something that was not true when it began.

## 4. Measurement law (SL4-style, in-prose where earned)

| Axis | Rule |
|---|---|
| His class | Stated at each gate, in-world ("ten-thousand-year class"), never as a bare number in narration |
| Ring tiers | white 10 / yellow 100 / purple 1k / black 10k / red 100k — **shown being taken from real beasts** so the reader learns the economy |
| Hunter ranks | Spirit masters carry ranks and rings; stated naturally in their own dialogue |
| Carried cultivation | Ledger only (BLADE_STATUS §0). Never in prose. |
| Kill count | **Zero, permanent.** He never kills. This is a story law, not a soft preference. |

## 5. Five-register rule

- **Close sense** — root, water, soil, the body he actually has.
- **Hard scene** — physical, present-tense-feeling, bodies moving, consequences.
- **Dialogue** — humans speak; beasts speak in ground-language; conversations have friction.
- **Other POV** — hunters, Spirit Hall, the deep's ancients, the door-people. The world
  looks *at* him.
- **Lyrical** — the old register, used as seasoning, never as the whole meal.

### Two failures found by audit after the fact, not during drafting

1. **17 arithmetic errors** across Chapters 3–13 — the mantis's lifespan, the vegetation
   note's age, the night-walker's arrival, the found one's lifespan, the length of the going,
   the pull's duration, the remaining-years figures. All patched.
2. **One hard canon violation.** The first pass put the naming at year 833 = 2670 DC, which
   is **49 years after** A Yin's rooted era ends (canon: before ~2621 DC). She could not have
   been rooted in the valley to meet him. Chapters 10–15 were compressed to pull the naming
   to year 753 = **~2590 DC**, 31 years inside her era. Ch15's forward reference was
   corrected in the same pass: Ohn files ~2590 DC, "in forty-seven years" → 2637 DC, which
   matches the canon date for Tang San's emergence exactly.

**Root cause of both, and the rule that replaces it:** dates were written as prose first and
reconciled afterwards. **Set the panel endpoints first, then write every in-chapter date
against them.** This is recorded in `REBUILD_CONTINUITY.md` §7.

### What the spine bought

The diagnosis in §1 said the serial had no opposition because it never used the canon fact
that Star Dou is hunted. The rebuild runs that fact end to end: a hunting party in the first
year, a cat driven in by hunters, a surveyor who nearly finds him, a party that comes for the
black ring, a records clerk who finally connects the files. The payoff in Chapter 14 is that
**the line's dwindling was not natural** — 391 of 400 great ones were harvested, one season
at a time, and A Yin has carried that alone for an age.

Kill count: zero. Permanent.
