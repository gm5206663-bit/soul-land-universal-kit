Student number one's eyes glinted as his first soul ring lit. He shot his hand into the air, an illusory sphere of light materializing close to his palm, strands of blue, green, and white light dancing around his fingers. He chanted a few verses and a protrusion clawed its way out of the sphere; once it reached the ground it transformed into an enormous illusory hound, its body wreathed in flames — yellow and orange growing brighter and more turbulent until its shape resembled a lion. Smaller than lion-type soul beasts, but domineering. A lion mastiff? Is this a soul skill or a spirit soul? Tang Wulin was in awe — a summoning-type martial soul? But this doesn't look like something a Soul Elder should be able to summon! The lion mastiff roared at the heavens, killing intent almost tangible.

"Materialize!" Student number one jabbed a finger at the beast, conducting rays of light into its form, making it more corporeal. "Little Lion, go!" — dead set on Gu Yue's defeat; the mastiff pounced like a flash of lightning. Wulin's read: three purple rings means only one spirit soul; since the summoned beast has a name, maybe that IS his spirit soul? Never before had he witnessed someone using their spirit soul as the primary means of battle — but nothing was too bizarre at Shrek Academy.

Gu Yue waved her hands in a controlled beat, her three twinkling soul rings revolving — then her rhythm changed and ALL THREE lit at once, trembling in unison. Which soul skill she was using was anyone's guess. In a flash of azure light she conjured a half-meter ball of ice in her palm and slapped it again and again, the sphere trembling with each strike. The charging mastiff's fur stuck out like the tail of a blazing meteor. Gu Yue was the picture of boredom — to her this was nothing more than a chore. She shot an ice ball at the beast when it neared — and then the incomprehensible: she began walking back toward Tang Wulin WITH HER BACK TO THE LION MASTIFF.

While everyone was flabbergasted, the mastiff stretched its maw wide and fired a scorching golden-red fireball. The meeting of fire and ice played out breathtakingly: the ice ball exploded into countless icicles, all homing toward the mastiff — jaws dropped as the projectiles FLICKERED FROM FRIGID BLUE TO DEEP GREEN mid-flight; at the same time a THIRD OF THEM VANISHED IN A FLASH OF SILVER, REAPPEARING RIGHT IN FRONT OF STUDENT NUMBER ONE'S NOSE, surrounding him in a dome and attacking on the spot. "Not good!" — his first time encountering anything like it; having prioritized his mastiff's offense, he had left himself open. Several green wind icicles melted on impact, but some evaded that fate, spiraling with gusts of wind toward the mastiff, which endured the barrage — roared, its coat of flames diminishing, its figure growing transparent. And then it disappeared. The battle ended. Before student number one was impaled, SHEN YI JUMPED IN TO PROTECT HIM — lucky, or else he'd be a glorified pincushion.

Shen Yi had not been optimistic about his chances from the very beginning: Gu Yue's control of the elements utterly suppressed him — she was someone the Silver Moon Douluo had recognized! Though even Shen Yi hadn't expected the match to finish so quickly. By the battle's official end Gu Yue was standing at Tang Wulin's side, calm as ever. SHE HAD DECIDED ON AN INSTANT VICTORY SINCE NIGHT HAD ALREADY DESCENDED. Full ten points — total forty-eight; her chances of entering the outer court hopeful with two trials left.

Xu Xiaoyan was the last to go. Tang Wulin whispered a few words into her ear; she raised her head in astonishment: "Really? Like that?" He raised a brow, gentle smile: "Just try it out. It's time for you to go." "Okay."

She didn't have much confidence — among class zero she was undoubtedly the weakest; assisting in team battles was doable with her control abilities, but one-on-one was practically out of the question. To make up for her weaknesses she focused on her strengths [canon compares: 'than her brother, as evidenced by the differences between their Ice Spears — her brother's focused on penetrating power, Xu Xiaoyan's was all about freezing' — THE BROTHER IS OTHERWISE UNESTABLISHED IN CANON-ON-DISK; held, never built on]. Her opponent: the muscular young man, student number six. "Begin," Shen Yi promptly declared. Two yellow soul rings materialized around Xu Xiaoyan, making her seem weak and pitiful; she stumbled to catch the item conjured in her hands. Her opponent hesitated at her near-topple — the frail little girl screamed outlandish. After releasing her rings she held her staff — but her right hand gripped a JET-BLACK HAMMER, its heaviness noticeable from the distance, since she had to lean on her staff for balance. Just what is going on? Is that a soul device? But this trial forbids them! Shen Yi eyed her, then held her tongue: the hammer was NOT a soul device — it lacked the slightest bit of soul fluctuations. After her first ring lit, a massive ice wheel appeared before her (diameter no less than a third of a meter) — and she DUCT-TAPED THE HAMMER TO IT. She levitated the wheel, which swayed from the added weight. Looking like a struggling kitten, she raised her staff and directed the contraption at her opponent. Her actions went beyond strange; they transcended to the realm of bizarre. Bearing the hammer's weight, the ice wheel flew forth LIKE AN INEBRIATED SNAIL, looking like it would drop at any moment. T-this is how she's attacking? The Shrek students watched with odd expressions, furrowed brows and pinched noses — astonishing things seen before, never something so uncanny. An unprecedented battle style. He expects to win like this? Will that toy even reach him? Her second soul ring lit — a spear of ice congealed before her.
