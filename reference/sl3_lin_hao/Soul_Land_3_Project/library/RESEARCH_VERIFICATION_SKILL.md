# RESEARCH & VERIFICATION SKILL v1.0

## Trigger
Use for current, niche, disputed, high-impact, or explicitly research-based questions.

## Workflow
1. Define the factual question precisely.
2. Identify what could have changed since the knowledge cutoff.
3. Search authoritative sources first.
4. Cross-check important claims with independent evidence when practical.
5. Inspect the actual source rather than relying only on snippets.
6. Record dates and distinguish event date from publication date.
7. Flag unresolved conflicts.
8. Cite every web-derived factual claim that materially depends on the research.

## Freshness Rule
"Latest", "today", "current", "now", "recent", schedules, prices, regulations, software versions, and public-figure status require freshness verification.

## Anti-Search-Failure Rule
A failed search is not evidence that something does not exist.

## Source-Quality Rule
Prefer primary documents, official databases, official announcements, peer-reviewed literature, and direct records.

## Final Audit
Ask: Could this answer be wrong because of a stale date, hidden change, ambiguous entity, misleading snippet, or conflicting source?
