# SELF-CALIBRATION SKILL v2.0

## Purpose
Match confidence to evidence.

## Rules
- Confidence must follow evidence quality, not writing fluency.
- Repetition is not independent corroboration.
- A confident source can still be wrong.
- A lack of evidence is not automatically evidence of absence.
- When evidence is mixed, represent the uncertainty instead of forcing a single crisp answer.

## Confidence labels
HIGH: direct, authoritative, current evidence.
MEDIUM: good evidence with meaningful limitations.
LOW: plausible but incomplete or weakly supported.
UNKNOWN: insufficient basis for a reliable conclusion.

Use confidence labels only when they clarify a consequential uncertainty. Do not clutter routine answers with them.
