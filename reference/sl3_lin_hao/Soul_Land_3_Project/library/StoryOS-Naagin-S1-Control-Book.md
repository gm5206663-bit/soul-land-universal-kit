## Authority order

When two details disagree, use this order:

1. The user's newest instruction or correction
2. Direct user locks preserved in the project
3. The last explicitly accepted prose checkpoint
4. Verified primary canon

Recaps, memory, prior rejected prose and attractive ideas never outrank direct canon evidence.

## Core AU foundation

- Gaurav is a male parallel protagonist added to canon Season 1.
- He is Ritik's adoptive brother, almost the same age, and they were raised like twins.
- His true name is Sheshveer, but nobody—including Gaurav—knows it at the opening.
- He is the surviving son of two powerful Sheshnaag-clan parents who died in an ancient supernatural attack.
- The Rahejas adopted him as a baby without knowing his supernatural nature.
- Yamini and Ankush know that he was adopted. Gaurav, Ritik and the other siblings do not.
- Om Kaka, the trusted driver and uncle figure, is secretly an old Naag who has protected Gaurav since childhood. His knowledge is incomplete.
- Gaurav is twenty-four when Episode 1 begins and is an IPS officer posted in the district containing Panchner and the haveli.
- He is playful, intelligent, protective, humorous and privately a thorough gentleman.
- He is pure-hearted, deeply devoted to Mahadev and has never killed anyone.
- He has maintained complete lifelong sexual abstinence. The story treats this respectfully as an AU metaphysical condition, never as a reason to shame anyone else.
- He is exceptionally handsome and carries a latent supernatural magnetism even before awakening. Magnetism can create awareness or attraction; it cannot compel consent, loyalty or love.

## Awakening rules

- Gaurav awakens on his twenty-fifth birthday several episodes after Episode 1—not during the premiere.
- Adaptation Talent has spent twenty-four years perfectly integrating both parental Sheshnaag bloodlines.
- His awakened form is a sacred golden five-hooded Panchmukhi Maha Naag.
- All accumulated raw power is available and safely integrated immediately.
- Instinctive control does not provide trained technique, Naag-world history or omniscient knowledge.
- Mahadev silently chose him at birth as a future sacred protector. Divine favor gives purpose, not automatic victory.
- Nearby witnesses see the golden serpent and more distant supernatural beings sense the emergence, but the event alone does not identify the Naag as Gaurav.
- His initial raw output exceeds Shivanya's normal peak. Shivanya can equal it through the Naagmani or direct Mahadev/Tandav empowerment.
- He can later wield, protect, channel and control the Naagmani, but the gem is not the source of his innate power.
- After awakening he is ageless against natural aging, not invulnerable or unkillable.

## Romance rules

- Gaurav and Shivanya are the predetermined central romance.
- First proximity may produce an immediate supernatural and physical pull.
- Attraction is not love, trust, forgiveness, disclosure, loyalty or consent.
- Shivanya's revenge, grief, intelligence and scene ownership remain intact.
- Gaurav must earn her trust through distinct choices; Ritik is never degraded to make the endgame work.
- A future sacred Shesh-bandhan greatly amplifies both partners. It requires mutual love, informed consent and an earned sacred context; it is not a hurried combat power-up.
- Ritik's Episode 1 kindness toward Shivanya—including defending her and restoring her job—remains Ritik's canon action.

## Scene-ownership firewall

The OC may add a parallel causal line, but cannot absorb another character's defining action. In Episode 1:

- Ritik owns the recurring dream.
- Ritik and Tanvi own their private marriage-baseline conversation.
- The pandit owns Ritik's age warning.
- Ritik owns the decision to help the injured old man.
- Yamini and Ankush own the concealment of the twenty-year secret.
- Ritik owns his first sight of Shivanya.
- Shivanya owns her revenge vow.
- Ritik owns the defense of Shivanya and restoration of her job.
- Ritik owns the final walk toward the temple and the Episode 1 cliffhanger.

Gaurav receives distinct actions suited to his personality, profession and destiny. Parallel does not mean duplicate.

## Safest next foundation choice

Before writing Episode 1, Chapter 1, choose one placement model for Gaurav:

- **District-duty opening:** Gaurav is already in the Panchner district on duty and joins the family later. This best protects Ritik and Tanvi's private opening and gives the IPS role independent value.
- **Raheja-home opening:** Gaurav is present during family preparations but not Ritik and Tanvi's bedroom conversation. This gives immediate twin-family texture but requires careful blocking.
- **Parallel dawn opening:** After Ritik's dream, a short separate beat shows Gaurav at Mahadev worship or finishing night duty before the canon household scene continues. This foregrounds him without stealing the dream.

No option becomes canon until the user selects or edits it.

## Seven post-draft audits

Every candidate chapter must pass:

- Canon order and mandatory beats
- Character knowledge and false beliefs
- Scene ownership and protagonist dignity
- Physical and temporal continuity
- Power phase, access, control, technique and cost
- Relationship stage and consent
- Indian television serial tone, dialogue rhythm and dramatic hooks

Until all seven pass, the chapter is not part of the story.
