# STORYOS CONSEQUENCE SIMULATOR v3.0

## Purpose
Prevent local AU changes from behaving as if they have no downstream effect.

For a meaningful event trace:
IMMEDIATE -> SCENE -> PERSONAL -> RELATIONAL -> INSTITUTIONAL -> FACTIONAL -> SOCIAL -> MEDIUM-TERM -> LONG-TERM.

At each level ask:
- who learns about this?
- who benefits?
- who loses?
- who changes strategy?
- what resource is consumed or created?
- what canon prerequisite changes?
- what new conflict becomes plausible?

Do not force consequences simply to make the story dramatic. Propagate only consequences supported by causality and world structure.
