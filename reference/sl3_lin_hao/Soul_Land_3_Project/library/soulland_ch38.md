# Adaptation — A Soul Land (斗罗大陆) Fanfiction (feat. Gu Yuan)
## Donghua Episode 13-14 — "索托大魂场" (The Suotuo Great Arena of Souls)
### Chapter 38 — 大魂场的注册与“绯影”的诞生 (The Registration of the Great Arena and the Birth of 'Crimson Shadow')

As night fell over the outskirts of Suotuo City, the simple, quiet thatched cottages of Shrek Academy dissolved into the darkness. 

Principal Flender led the six combat-system students—Dai Mubai, Tang San, Gu Yuan, Xiao Wu, Zhu Zhuqing, and Ma Hongjun—down the wide cobblestone road toward the center of the city. The cool night breeze rustled their clothes, carrying the distant, roaring hum of a massive crowd.

"Look ahead," Flender said, pointing toward the center of the city skyline. 

Rising out of the merchant quarters was a colossal, circular stone building that looked like a magnificent, ancient coliseum. It was lit by dozens of massive, magical crystal spotlights that shot columns of brilliant white light straight into the dark sky. The entire building hummed, the sound of tens of thousands of cheering fans vibrating through the stone foundations.

This was the **Suotuo Great Arena of Souls (索托大魂场)**—the grandest stage of combat on the entire border of the two empires.

"In this arena, your duty is to fight," Flender said, his voice flat but carrying an absolute gravity. "The rules are simple. Every Spirit Master must register and obtain an Iron Spirit Fighter (铁斗魂) badge. As you win matches, your points increase, allowing you to rise through the ranks: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, and all the way to Diamond."

Flender stopped, looking at the newcomers with a sharp, hawk-like intensity. "Shrek Academy has a strict graduation rule. To graduate from our school, you **must reach the Silver Spirit Fighter (银斗魂) rank**! Tonight, you will register and begin your first 1v1 Solo matches."

The group walked into the grand, marble-paved registration hall of the Arena of Souls. The hall was massive, crowded with thousands of Spirit Masters, fully armed warriors, and elegant nobles placing bets behind iron grates.

They approached the registration counter. 

Tang San registered his real name, obtaining his first dark-iron badge. Xiao Wu registered, her big eyes sparkling as she held her badge. 

Then, Gu Yuan stepped up to the counter. 

"Name, age, and rank," the elegant female registrar said, her eyes widening slightly as she looked at Gu Yuan's exceptionally handsome features and his tall, athletic build. 

"Gu Yuan," he said, his voice calm and steady. "Twelve years old. Rank Thirty Spirit Elder."

"Rank... Rank Thirty?!" the registrar’s hand froze mid-stroke. She stared at the twelve-year-old boy in absolute disbelief, before hurriedly stamping his dark-iron badge, registering him as a **Rank Thirty Control and Spiritual-system Spirit Scholar (Control/Spiritual-system)**. 

Beside him, Zhu Zhuqing stood quietly. She looked at Gu Yuan’s dark-iron badge, her cold heart racing slightly as she realized he was indeed a monster who transcended their entire generation. 

"Next," Flender said, his eyes glinting behind his square glasses. "You may also participate in **2v2 Duo matches (双人对决)**. It is a highly efficient way to practice coordination and earn double points."

Tang San turned to Xiao Wu, a warm, brotherly smile on his face. "Xiao Wu, let's register a Duo team. We've been training together for five years."

"Hehe! Yes, brother!" Xiao Wu hopped happily. "Let's call ourselves... the **San-Wu Combination (三五组合)**!"

The registrar quickly stamped their duo plaque. 

Dashi and Flender then looked at Gu Yuan and Zhu Zhuqing. 

"Gu Yuan is Rank Thirty, and Zhu Zhuqing is Rank Twenty-Seven," Dashi suggested, his voice quiet and analytical. "Zhuqing is an agility-system master, and Gu Yuan specializes in spatial speed and Sharingan precision. Your attributes match perfectly. A speed-precision duo will be an absolute nightmare for any opponent in the arena."

Gu Yuan turned his head, his dark eyes meeting Zhu Zhuqing's cold, silent gaze. He smiled gently, his tone carrying a warm, respectful reassurance. "Would you like to partner with me, Zhuqing? I'll make sure your back is always protected."

Zhu Zhuqing’s cold, defensive posture softened fractionally. She looked into his calm eyes—completely free of the greasy, predatory lust she usually faced from other men—and felt a sudden, deep sense of safety and absolute trust. 

A faint, natural blush brushed her porcelain cheeks, and she nodded slowly, her voice soft but firm. "I would be honored, Gu Yuan."

"Excellent," the registrar smiled, looking at the beautiful, dark-clad girl and the tall, handsome young master. "What is your Duo team name?"

Gu Yuan thought for a moment, looking at Zhu Zhuqing’s shadow-like Hell Civet presence and his own crimson Sharingan. 

"Let us call ourselves... the **Crimson Shadow Combination (绯影组合)**," Gu Yuan said, his voice smooth. "Crimson for my eye, and Shadow for her claws."

"Crimson Shadow..." Zhu Zhuqing tested the name on her tongue, her heart giving a quiet, fluttering beat of absolute satisfaction. It was a beautiful, elegant, and powerful name that bound their identities perfectly. 

The registrar stamped their gold-bordered plaque, officially registering the **Crimson Shadow Combination** in the annals of the Suotuo Great Arena of Souls. 

The stage was set, the dark-iron badges were in their hands, and the first night of the gladiatorial arena was ready to explode. Under the blinding crystal spotlights, the monsters were ready to unleash their claws, and the continent was about to hear the roar of the Crimson Shadow.
