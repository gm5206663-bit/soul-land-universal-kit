# STORYOS OMNIVERIFICATION SKILL v1.0

## Purpose
A universal fictional-story verification layer for canon-faithful alternate universes and original expansions.

## Mandatory Pre-Draft Gate
Before drafting:
1. Reconstruct the current story state.
2. Lock canon anchor.
3. Lock exact timeline/date/episode/chapter position.
4. Lock physical location and travel chain.
5. Build character knowledge matrix.
6. Separate confirmed canon, confirmed AU divergence, character state, and protected future information.
7. Audit ages, relationships, factions, abilities, equipment, injuries, resources, and unresolved plot threads.
8. Identify what the user changed from canon.
9. Identify what remains untouched.
10. Only then plan the scene.

## Information Firewall
Every significant fact must be classified as:
- KNOWN
- SUSPECTED
- UNKNOWN
- HIDDEN / FUTURE

No character may use future information unless an explicit mechanism gives it to them.

## Canon Preservation
Never move, trigger, reveal, or resolve major canon events early unless the AU explicitly requires that divergence.

## Character Integrity
Established characters remain competent according to canon. Do not weaken them merely so the OC can win.

## Power Integrity
Separate:
- raw power,
- maximum output,
- control,
- precision,
- technique,
- efficiency,
- experience,
- knowledge,
- compatibility,
- stamina,
- potential.

## Consequence Engine
Every meaningful intervention should be checked for immediate, medium-term, and long-term consequences across affected characters and institutions.

## Living World
Other characters and organizations continue acting off-screen according to their own goals, information, resources, fears, incentives, and competence.

## Adaptation Talent Safeguard
Adaptation is not a universal multiplier. Its result must emerge from the individual's current foundation, available resources, world laws, experience, intent, and actual limitations.

## Final Story Audit
Before delivering a chapter, check continuity, voice, chronology, location, knowledge, power logic, emotional causality, canon event order, and unintended information leakage.
