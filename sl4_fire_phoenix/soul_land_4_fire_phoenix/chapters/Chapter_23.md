# Chapter 23: Ten Seconds

The Gate answered.

It simply did not answer with a soul beast.

Silver-patterned Blue Silver Grass tightened around Qian Lei's wrist, and the illusory door that had opened in the dimness behind the rubble shivered as if someone on the other side had turned away before even looking through. Qian Lei's face had just begun to brighten when a cold, clear, utterly impatient voice came from beyond the light.

"Not free!"

The words were not loud.

In the narrow dead-end passage, they were loud enough.

Outside the rubble, Lu Qianxun's advancing footsteps stopped.

Ye Lingtong's eyes sharpened.

Chang Jianyi's shoulders stiffened.

For one breath, both sides understood the same thing at once. Dorm 333 had been hidden there. The summon had failed. Qian Lei's best chance had turned into a shout announcing their position.

Qian Lei stared at the Gate as if it had betrayed three generations of his ancestors. "This is not the time to be busy," he whispered in a collapsing voice.

Lan Xuanyu did not scold him.

There was no room left for scolding.

The silver-patterned grass still held the Gate's unstable edge, keeping the fading door from vanishing completely. The golden-patterned grass coiled around Liu Feng's waist and arm, and the White Dragon Spear in Liu Feng's hand trembled as if a sleeping eye had opened along the blade. Dust hung between the stones. The enemy had not yet charged.

That was all the time they had.

"Move," Lan Xuanyu said.

The command was low.

Liu Feng moved first.

Outside the rubble, Lu Qianxun's face had already sunk.

The voice from behind the pile was not one of the three boys. It was a girl's voice, and for an instant his mind caught on the strangeness of it. The Maze test only held their class. No girl from another room should have been behind that pile. No fourth teammate should have been allowed.

But the thought lasted only an instant.

The important thing was simpler.

Lan Xuanyu's team was there.

The team with four plus signs.

The team that had rested while others fought.

The team Lu Qianxun had wanted to crush cleanly since the results board appeared.

"Found you," Lu Qianxun said.

His yellow soul ring rose.

Golden light tore across his body.

He grew almost visibly. His shoulders widened, his limbs lengthened, and the outline of a young student was swallowed by the pressure of a golden beast. His hair blazed bright gold, and the phantom of a lion seemed to stand with him, mane lifting in a wind that had no source.

Golden Lion King.

Even inside a simulation pod, even with only a fraction of real pain allowed, the aura that poured into the corridor made the air feel heavy.

Lu Qianxun stepped forward and punched the rubble.

Stone exploded.

The dead-end passage had kept Dorm 333 hidden a moment before. Now it became a cannon mouth. Broken pieces flew inward like a rain of hard black teeth.

Lan Xuanyu had already moved.

The battle axe taken from the stone aspect was too large for a boy his age to use gracefully, but Lan Xuanyu had never intended to make it graceful. He dragged it up with both hands and used its weight as a shield. Sparks burst where broken rock slammed into the axe face. His arms shook. The golden-patterned grass at his wrist brightened, and the force that should have knocked him over instead spread through his waist and legs.

"Feng!"

Liu Feng did not need the second half of the order.

Lan Xuanyu's shoulder struck him from below. The golden-patterned grass snapped tight, and Liu Feng's light body shot upward through the gap opened by the shattered rubble.

Ye Lingtong was already there.

A pale dragon-shaped aura wrapped her body, and her eyes found Liu Feng in midair. She had trained with Lu Qianxun's team long enough to know that the first strike in a corridor decided everything. If Liu Feng reached Chang Jianyi, their control line would break.

Her leg swept up like a dragon tail.

Liu Feng twisted.

White Dragon Spear flashed past Ye Lingtong's kick without meeting it directly. His target had never been her.

Chang Jianyi's face changed.

He lifted both hands. A strange white halo spread in front of him, quickly condensing into a stiff human-shaped figure. It had almost no features, yet the moment it appeared, Liu Feng's spear seemed to push through water. The speed that had made him famous in class slowed by a hair, then by another hair.

Those hairs saved Chang Jianyi's life in the simulation.

The White Dragon Spear still reached him.

A sharp point of pain bloomed through Chang Jianyi's shoulder. The spear pinned him backward against the stone wall, and his mouth opened soundlessly.

But he had not turned into white light.

Not yet.

"Liu Feng!" Qian Lei shouted.

The shout turned into a groan.

Lu Qianxun inhaled.

It was not a deep breath for a boy.

It was a beast drawing in the whole corridor.

The Golden Lion King's phantom opened its jaws.

The roar struck.

Sound became pressure.

Pressure became a hammer.

The passage did not release it. The walls caught the Golden Lion Roar and threw it back again and again, squeezing every wave thinner and harder until even dust looked as if it were being crushed. The broken stones still in the air shattered into powder. Qian Lei, who stood behind Lan Xuanyu and had already spent himself keeping the Gate open, clapped both hands over his head and fell to one knee.

Liu Feng's body froze at the wall.

The White Dragon Spear trembled in his hand, but his fingers lost command of it. The roar dug into his ears, his skull, his chest. His speed meant nothing when his nerves could not obey him.

Lan Xuanyu received the roar from the front.

For a moment, he heard nothing at all.

Not Qian Lei's groan.

Not the stones striking the ground.

Not Lu Qianxun's next step.

Only a bursting pressure filled his head. His chest felt as if it had been seized by a golden claw and squeezed inward. Something hot and heavy forced itself through his meridians, not his usual soul power, not the gentle chill of his silver-patterned grass, but a thick golden force that had always been there and had never liked being pressed down.

Lu Qianxun's Golden Lion Roar was strong.

In a narrow corridor, it was stronger.

It was both a spiritual shock and a soundwave attack, and it carried the dominance of a peak lion-type martial soul. Ordinary beast spirits would instinctively bow beneath that pressure. Many dragon spirits would be shaken by it.

But the thing sleeping inside Lan Xuanyu did not know how to bow.

Lu Qianxun did not see that yet.

He only saw Lan Xuanyu standing with the axe lowered, face pale, eyes unfocused. Liu Feng was pinned and stunned. Qian Lei was half-kneeling, nearly useless. The girl from the strange Gate had refused to appear. Dorm 333's trick had failed.

This battle should have been over.

Lu Qianxun stepped through the powder of the shattered rubble and threw his fist toward Lan Xuanyu.

The Gate behind Qian Lei had not closed.

A slender hand reached out of it.

Cold light unfolded.

Frost spread across the air between Lu Qianxun's fist and Lan Xuanyu's face. The golden fist stopped as if it had hit invisible ice. White mist burst outward, and the temperature in the dead end plunged.

Qian Lei stared.

"You came!" he blurted, almost crying from relief.

The girl who had said she was not free stepped halfway through the Gate with irritation still on her face. Her dark hair drifted in the cold air. Ice formed under her fingers.

She did not even have time to complain.

Lan Xuanyu raised his head.

His eyes were gold.

Not gold like Lu Qianxun's lion light.

Not gold like a soul ring.

A deeper, older gold burned in them, wild enough that even the ice around the Gate seemed to hesitate. The golden-patterned Blue Silver Grass in his hand changed color in the same instant. Pattern became body. The strands that had been blue with golden lines turned into pure gold, as if something beneath the skin of the martial soul had finally shown itself.

Lan Xuanyu opened his mouth.

A dragon roar erupted.

It did not sound like a child shouting.

It did not sound like a soul skill that had been practiced in class.

It was a furious answer torn out of blood and bone, a roar that struck the Golden Lion King's pressure from the front and shattered its pride. The ice wall rising from the Gate broke apart before it fully formed. The girl from beyond the Gate disappeared backward at once, retreating faster than she had appeared. The Gate rippled wildly and collapsed behind her.

Lu Qianxun took the roar with his face almost touching Lan Xuanyu's.

Blood burst from his nose.

Then from the corners of his eyes.

Then from his ears.

His golden hair lost its savage lift, and the phantom lion behind him twisted as if an invisible hand had struck its spine. He stumbled backward step after step, eyes wide not with pain alone, but with something he had never expected to feel in front of Lan Xuanyu.

Fear.

Ye Lingtong's Celestial Body collapsed.

The dragon-tail kick she had been holding toward Liu Feng softened mid-motion. Her face went blank, and she fell as though all strength had been pulled from her bones.

Chang Jianyi, still pinned at the wall, could not move either.

Liu Feng moved.

The White Dragon Spear answered the roar.

A clear white cry rang from the spearhead, thinner than Lan Xuanyu's roar but sharper, and a foot-long blade of white light stretched from the spear tip. Liu Feng's stunned eyes cleared enough for one thing: attack.

The spear drove forward.

Chang Jianyi turned into white light.

Liu Feng tore the weapon free, dropped, spun, and ran along the wall as if gravity had loosened around him. The Golden Lion Roar had crushed him a moment before, but the white light on the spear now pulled him onward. Ye Lingtong was already collapsing. Lu Qianxun was still staggering, blood marking his face.

Liu Feng did not hesitate.

One thrust.

White light swallowed Lu Qianxun.

A second thrust.

White light swallowed Ye Lingtong.

Then there was only dust, silence, and Lan Xuanyu swaying where he stood.

From the girl's refusal to the end of Lu Qianxun's team, the fight had lasted barely ten seconds.

Qian Lei's mouth remained open.

His first thought was that he should say something heroic.

His second thought was that his legs would not stand long enough to support anything heroic.

"Xuanyu?" Liu Feng said.

Lan Xuanyu's golden eyes dimmed.

The pure-gold grass loosened.

His small body folded forward.

Liu Feng dropped the spear and caught him before he struck the ground.

Lan Xuanyu did not wake.

For one terrible breath, Liu Feng felt the memory of the earlier coma crawl up his back. Qian Lei's expression changed in the same way. They had won. They had eliminated the strongest rival-team block in their path. The Maze had not ended. Lan Xuanyu's body had not dissolved into white light, which meant he was still counted as alive inside the test.

But his breathing was shallow, and his face was pale.

"Carry him," Qian Lei said hoarsely.

"I am carrying him," Liu Feng snapped, because fear needed somewhere to go.

Qian Lei swallowed. "Then carry faster. That roar just told every team nearby where we are."

Liu Feng lifted Lan Xuanyu over his back.

The White Dragon Spear returned to his hand, still carrying a faint one-foot white edge that had not fully disappeared. He looked at it for half a second, unable to understand why it felt so light and so sharp at the same time.

There was no time to understand.

They ran.

---

The observation room had gone quiet.

A moment earlier, the teachers had been watching several screens at once, measuring routes, judging energy use, calculating which team had fallen into which trap. The Maze was supposed to reveal actual-combat judgment, not merely soul-power numbers. It had already done that through Dorm 333's hiding strategy and through Dorm 336's separate controlled route.

Then the screen showing the rubble dead end had filled with gold, ice, and white light.

Mu Zhongtian's hand gripped the back of a chair.

He had seen Lan Xuanyu unconscious before. He had watched a simulation accident nearly break children who should only have been learning. The image of Lan Xuanyu collapsing in Liu Feng's arms made his scalp tighten.

"Body check after exit," he said at once.

"Of course," the vice president replied.

The short, stout man still smiled, but the smile had thinned. His eyes were fixed on the frozen frame that had captured Lan Xuanyu's gold-lit face a breath before the roar.

Ji Hongbin did not speak for several seconds.

That silence was more frightening than his usual scolding.

A teacher behind them whispered, "Was that really Lan Xuanyu? His soul power is only..."

"Soul power did not explain it," Ji Hongbin said.

No one argued.

The teachers had seen the Gate.

They had seen a hand emerge from somewhere beyond the simulation.

They had seen an intelligent person respond to Qian Lei's summon, refuse, intervene, and retreat. They had seen Lan Xuanyu amplify Liu Feng before, but this was different. The Golden Lion Roar should have ended the exchange. Instead it had provoked something that made Lu Qianxun's Golden Lion King recoil as if it had touched a higher bloodline.

The vice president's fingers tapped against his sleeve.

"Interesting," he murmured.

Ji Hongbin's eyes cut toward him.

"Dangerous," Mu Zhongtian said.

"Both," the vice president said pleasantly. "Children who are merely safe do not shake a screen like that. Children who shake a screen like that must be checked properly. Do not let anyone leave without routine inspection. Lan Xuanyu first. Also record Qian Lei's Gate data and Liu Feng's spear change. Full scoring review after the Maze closes."

His gaze moved across the other screens.

On one of them, Dorm 336 was nowhere near the rubble dead end.

Yan Shuo, Song Yichen, and Luo Haoran were moving through a separate branch where the walls formed a bent triangle of passages. The sound from the Golden Lion Roar and the dragon roar had reached them only as a dull tremor through layers of Maze stone, no clearer than distant thunder under the floor.

Luo Haoran's head turned.

"Something big," he whispered.

Song Yichen tightened his shield. "Close?"

Yan Shuo stood still for half a breath.

Warm copper-red flame lay thin along his shoulders and wrists, not flaring, not boasting. Pale-gold motes drifted inside the heat, and a white-gold line clung to the feather edges of Dawnfeather Mantle like sunrise caught on a blade. The tremor had passed through the soles of his feet and into the frame of his back. His first instinct was to map it.

His second was to refuse the bait.

The Maze loved curiosity.

The Maze punished children who mistook every loud sound for an opportunity.

"Not our fight," Yan Shuo said.

Luo's eyes flicked toward him.

Yan Shuo lifted two fingers and pointed toward the darker left passage. "The tremor came through too many walls. If we chase it, we give up our route and arrive late to someone else's battlefield. We hold our own line."

Song nodded once.

It was a small thing, the kind of decision no scoreboard number showed by itself. A month earlier, Luo might have darted three steps ahead to check the sound. Song might have planted himself too heavily and forced the team to pivot around him. Yan might have answered pressure with wider flame than the passage needed.

Now none of them wasted motion.

Luo vanished down the left passage, low and quick. Threadwind Mantis lines flickered around his arms. He returned before Song's next breath finished.

"Two students. Tired. One control, one assault. They heard it too. Coming fast and messy."

"Let them enter," Yan said.

Song set his Stoneplate Tortoise shield at an angle rather than flat across the corridor. The angle mattered. A flat shield would stop the first body and jam the second into a collision that might crack Song's stance. The angled shield invited the first attacker to slide along it.

The tired assault-type student took the invitation because he did not see it as one.

He rushed in with his martial soul already bright, eyes fixed behind him as if whatever had roared in the Maze was still chasing him. Song's shield met him with a scraping boom and turned his momentum sideways. Luo's mantis edge flashed low, not to cut deeply but to force the student's foot to lift at the wrong instant.

Yan Shuo's flame line closed the third side of the triangle.

It was not a fire wall.

It was thinner and more precise than that: a curved feather of copper-red heat edged in pale gold, placed exactly where the student wanted to recover balance. The heat kissed close enough for the simulation to register danger. The light along its edge made the boundary unmistakable. The student flinched away from it and turned his shoulder into Song's shield.

Song pushed.

White light swallowed the first student.

The second tried to throw a binding ring of soul power around Song's legs.

Luo interrupted the casting motion with a tap to the wrist.

Yan did not increase output. He shifted the flame feather half a step, cutting off the retreat route without touching Luo's shoulder. The pale-gold motes in the heat swirled inward, tidying the edge before it could spill. The hidden adapting rhythm in his body drank the pressure of the movement and returned it as better balance through his spine and heels.

The control student saw the shield in front, the mantis line to the side, and the clean fire-light behind.

He chose wrong.

Luo struck.

The second white light rose.

The corridor returned to quiet.

Yan let the Mantle dim at once. Heat withdrew from the wall without leaving a careless scorch in the simulation stone.

Song exhaled. "You still want to ignore that sound?"

"Even more," Yan said.

Luo gave a short laugh, then stopped when he heard distant footsteps from another direction. "This test really does hate peace."

"Then we make our peace smaller," Yan answered. "Small enough to carry."

In the observation room, a teacher who had been watching the Dorm 336 screen nodded despite himself.

"Clean," he said. "Not as shocking as Dorm 333, but clean."

Ji Hongbin heard him.

"Clean survives," he said. "Shock wins if it can stay alive after shocking people. Keep watching both."

The vice president's eyes had already returned to the screen where Liu Feng was carrying Lan Xuanyu.

---

Dorm 333 did not get far before trouble found them again.

Liu Feng ran first, Lan Xuanyu on his back, the White Dragon Spear slanting behind him. Qian Lei stumbled along beside them, one hand pressed to his head and the other clutching the Summoning Coin as if the coin could apologize if squeezed hard enough.

"Left," Liu Feng said.

"Right," Qian Lei said at the same time.

They both froze for half a heartbeat.

Footsteps echoed ahead.

Then a familiar voice shouted, "There!"

Jin Xiang appeared at the bend with two teammates behind him.

Flame-colored feathers flickered around his arms, and his eyes lit up the moment he saw Liu Feng carrying an unconscious Lan Xuanyu. Dorm 333 looked exactly like prey. Qian Lei's face was white. Liu Feng's breathing was too fast. Lan Xuanyu hung unmoving over Liu Feng's back.

Jin Xiang laughed once.

It was not a pleasant laugh.

He had wanted this since lunch, since the results board, since Dorm 333 had somehow turned weak totals into four plus signs and protection. He had wanted to humiliate them in actual combat where no teacher's mysterious plus mark could stand between teams.

"Liu Feng," he said, "you still have the courage to run toward me?"

Qian Lei's eyes darted behind Jin Xiang.

Then behind himself.

Then to the White Dragon Spear's fading light.

His brain, which had failed to summon properly at the worst possible time, decided it had to make up for it.

He suddenly widened his eyes in terror and shouted past Jin Xiang, "Run! Lu Qianxun's team is coming!"

Jin Xiang's expression changed.

His two teammates also looked past Dorm 333 by instinct.

Lu Qianxun's name weighed differently inside the Elite Junior Class. Jin Xiang feared Liu Feng less than he feared wasting strength against Liu Feng while Lu Qianxun arrived behind him. He had no reason to believe Lu's team had already disappeared. No ordinary student would believe that Dorm 333 had eliminated them.

"Move!" Jin Xiang barked.

His Flaming Golden Bird wings spread in a burst of hot metallic feathers, shielding his back as he turned. His teammates followed at once.

For half a breath, Qian Lei looked amazed that the lie had worked.

Liu Feng did not waste the half breath.

The White Dragon Spear in his hand still carried that strange lingering white edge. He could feel it fading, feel the borrowed sharpness thinning with every moment after Lan Xuanyu's roar, but it had not vanished yet.

He lowered his body and shot forward.

Jin Xiang trusted his wings.

Against Liu Feng as he usually was, that trust might have been enough.

Against this spear, at this moment, it was not.

White light pierced through flame-colored feathers. Jin Xiang's eyes went wide. Before he could turn his anger into a curse, the simulation judged the injury fatal and swallowed him in light.

Liu Feng passed him and struck twice more.

Two more flashes.

Jin Xiang's team vanished from the Maze.

Qian Lei stumbled to a stop beside Liu Feng and stared at the fading white light.

Then, because fear and triumph had tangled inside him until they were impossible to separate, he gave Liu Feng a shaky grin.

"Tell the truth," Qian Lei said. "That felt good, right?"

Liu Feng looked at Lan Xuanyu's pale face over his shoulder.

The grin that almost formed on his own face disappeared.

"Run," he said.

Qian Lei stopped joking.

They ran again.

---

The Maze continued to grind children against choices.

Some teams heard the roars and charged toward the sound, arriving only in time to find empty corridors and the risk of meeting other hunters. Some slowed too much and were caught from behind. Some fought bravely and lost because bravery did not tell them when to retreat.

Dorm 336 stayed alive through smaller decisions.

They did not chase the legendary noise.

They did not announce their position with unnecessary flame.

They took one narrow junction, surrendered another, and used a false heat trace to send a pursuing pair into a dead end where a Maze mechanism forced them to waste strength. When another tired team tried to squeeze through Song's shield side, Luo cut the timing and Yan's feather-edged fire sealed the opening for just long enough.

No one watching would call it miraculous.

That was the point.

Yan Shuo's progress had become visible not only in how fiercely he could burn, but in how often he no longer needed to. His soul power was denser than it had been when he first entered the academy. His body carried pressure differently. The Dawnflame Kite's living response no longer felt like a borrowed wing draped over his shoulders; it felt like a growing pattern threaded through breath, spine, wrist, and step. Even when the Mantle was low, even when no wide soul skill burned, the ring and soul spirit were not asleep. They were part of how he judged distance, how fast heat returned to center after use, how cleanly his light-edged fire withdrew before touching Song or Luo.

The team still made mistakes.

Luo overreached once and had to twist hard enough that his ankle stung in the pain setting. Song took a shoulder impact too squarely and grunted through the shield. Yan let one flame edge climb half a finger higher than intended before cutting it down, and the correction left a hot line of fatigue across his back.

But the mistakes did not break formation.

That was growth too.

In a test built to reward the last survivor and the cleverest battlefield judgment, Dorm 336 proved that the month had not been empty. They were not protected by four plus signs. They were not hidden behind miracle secrecy. They were three students carrying one strong public score, two improving teammates, and a route that became cleaner every time pressure tried to make it messy.

Far away in the Maze, Dorm 333 kept running with an unconscious Lan Xuanyu.

The two routes did not touch.

---

When the final simulation signal came, every pod in the room released almost at once.

White ceiling light replaced Maze darkness.

The smell of dust and hot stone vanished.

Children sat up inside their pods with the stunned expressions of people returning from a place where fear had been real enough to teach their bodies even if the wounds had not followed them out.

Mu Zhongtian went directly to Lan Xuanyu.

Liu Feng had barely pushed his pod open before Mu was already beside Dorm 333's row. Qian Lei scrambled out after him, face still pale, and pointed with unnecessary panic.

"Teacher Mu, Xuanyu fainted inside, but he did not get eliminated, and then he woke a little, and then he said nothing, and then..."

"Move aside," Mu Zhongtian said.

Qian Lei moved aside so quickly he nearly tripped.

Lan Xuanyu's pod opened.

He blinked up at the teacher with a pale face and eyes that looked unfocused for a moment before settling.

"Teacher Mu," he said softly.

Mu Zhongtian checked his breathing first, then his pupils, then the pulse of soul power he could sense through the child's wrist. The tension in his face eased only a little.

"Do you feel uncomfortable?"

Lan Xuanyu thought about it.

His body felt strange.

Not bad.

Strange.

There was a faint warm current in him, as if something that had been blocked had rushed through once and left the channel wider than before. The little vortex at his core seemed to turn with a clearer rhythm. His limbs were tired, but not hollow. His chest ached, yet beneath the ache there was a quiet sense of strength that had not been there that morning.

He did not know how to explain any of that.

"A little tired," he said. "But I am fine."

Mu Zhongtian stared into his eyes.

For an instant, he thought he saw gold flash deep inside them.

Then it was gone.

"Sit still," Mu said. "You will be checked again. Do not run around."

Lan Xuanyu nodded obediently.

Yan Shuo's pod opened two rows away.

He sat up more slowly than Luo, faster than Song. Heat withdrew from his shoulders in a controlled breath, leaving only a faint warmth under his skin. The fatigue across his back pulsed in time with his heartbeat, the familiar cost of clean restraint rather than reckless release.

His first look went to his teammates.

Song was rubbing his shoulder and pretending it did not hurt.

Luo was flexing his ankle with a grimace he tried to hide.

Both were alive in the test's judgment until the end signal.

Yan let himself exhale.

His second look went to Lan Xuanyu.

He saw Mu Zhongtian standing over him. He saw Qian Lei hovering like a frightened bird. He saw Liu Feng gripping the White Dragon Spear so tightly his knuckles had gone white even though the weapon had already returned to ordinary simulation calm.

Yan did not know what had happened.

He knew only that his childhood friend looked pale, that Dorm 333's faces carried the kind of fear that came after victory rather than defeat, and that Teacher Mu's attention was sharp enough to make questions inappropriate.

He stepped down from the pod and stopped where he was.

Concern did not have to become intrusion.

Across the room, Jin Xiang did not share that philosophy.

His pod slammed open.

"Liu Feng!" he roared. "You despicable brat!"

Several students turned at once.

Jin Xiang jumped out and rushed toward Dorm 333, face red with fury. The memory of the spear through his Flaming Golden Bird wings was clearly still burning in him more than any simulated wound.

Liu Feng turned his head.

He looked too tired to argue and too satisfied to apologize properly.

"Stop!"

Ji Hongbin's voice cracked through the room harder than any pod release signal.

Jin Xiang froze.

The Demon King walked over, and the fire in Jin Xiang's face lost half its heat before Ji even reached him.

Ji Hongbin lifted one hand and pointed at Jin Xiang.

"You underestimated your enemy. You trusted what he wanted you to trust. You turned your back because one sentence frightened you more than the opponent in front of you. Who do you blame?"

Jin Xiang's mouth opened. "Teacher Ji, I..."

"Not satisfied?" Ji Hongbin's tone became colder. "If this were a real battlefield, you would already be dead. Do dead people complain that the enemy was despicable?"

No one laughed.

Ji's gaze swept across the class.

"Remember this. A simulated world is not a toy just because your bodies return afterward. One day, the enemy in front of you will not wait for your sense of fairness. They will lie. They will hide. They will run. They will strike from behind if you offer your back. If you cannot treat the life you have inside the simulation as real, then when the real battlefield comes, you will not live long enough to regret it."

The room was silent.

Even Jin Xiang lowered his head.

Yan Shuo listened without moving.

He thought of the distant tremor under the Maze floor. He thought of Luo wanting to chase, Song asking if it was close, his own instinct trying to map the sound before judgment caught it. Teacher Ji's lesson was not only for Jin Xiang. It was for anyone who thought strength excused curiosity, anger, or pride.

At the other side of the room, Lu Qianxun had already emerged from his pod.

He said nothing.

His eyes were fixed on Lan Xuanyu.

Others in the class merely looked confused. They had not seen how Dorm 333 could possibly have beaten Lu Qianxun's team, much less Jin Xiang's. Lu knew enough to be more unsettled than confused. The roar inside the Maze had not left his memory behind with the simulation dust. His meridians still seemed to echo with it, as if the Golden Lion King inside him had taken a step backward and could not understand why.

Ye Lingtong stood near him, pale and quiet.

Chang Jianyi looked thoughtful in a way that made his shoulder seem to hurt again though no wound remained.

The vice president walked forward with his usual warm smile.

Beside him, Ji Hongbin's expression remained severe.

Mu Zhongtian finished his first check of Lan Xuanyu and stood, still close enough to stop the boy if he tried to move too quickly.

The vice president glanced around the class.

"The actual-combat test result," Ji Hongbin announced, "first place: Lan Xuanyu, Liu Feng, and Qian Lei's group."

The room erupted.

"First?"

"Dorm 333?"

"How?"

"They were hiding!"

"They beat Jin Xiang?"

"What about Lu Qianxun's team?"

The questions crashed over each other until Ji Hongbin's eyes narrowed. The volume died by half at once.

Qian Lei stared as if someone had told him the Gate of Summoning had finally learned manners.

Liu Feng's hand tightened again, but this time the tremor in his fingers looked like excitement.

Lan Xuanyu blinked.

Champion.

A proper counterattack.

The three words did not erase the ache in his chest, but they made Liu Feng and Qian Lei's faces shine. For them, who had spent so long at the bottom before he came, this result was not a number. It was proof that they had not imagined becoming a team.

Yan Shuo watched from Dorm 336's row.

He did not know the method.

He did not need to know it to understand the weight of the result.

Dorm 333 had entered the Maze with the class doubting their total score, mocking their protection, waiting for them to be humiliated. They had come out first in actual combat.

Yan glanced at Lan Xuanyu and gave the smallest nod when their eyes briefly met.

Well done.

Lan Xuanyu's answering look was tired, startled, and grateful all at once.

No explanation crossed the space between them.

That was fine.

Some doors were not public doors.

The vice president raised a hand, and the remaining whispers faded.

"Complete results will be announced after review," he said. "All of you may return to rest or cultivate. Do not neglect recovery. Today's lesson is not over just because the pods are open."

Students began to move, still whispering despite themselves. Some looked at Dorm 333 with disbelief. Some looked at Lu Qianxun and quickly looked away. A few glanced toward Yan Shuo, as if wondering how the class monitor would react to another team's impossible first place.

Yan did not perform a reaction for them.

He turned to Song and Luo first.

"Ice your ankle," he told Luo.

Luo made a face. "It is not that bad."

"Then ice the not-that-bad ankle before Teacher Ji makes you run on it tomorrow."

Song snorted despite his sore shoulder.

The small exchange steadied Dorm 336 more than a speech would have. Their test had not been a miracle, but it had been real. They had survived. They had improved. They had earned whatever score would come from their route without borrowing another dorm's secrets.

Behind them, Ji Hongbin's voice sounded again.

"Lan Xuanyu, stay."

Lan Xuanyu looked up.

Liu Feng and Qian Lei stopped at once, excitement turning into worry.

The vice president smiled at Lan Xuanyu as if the entire Maze had merely been an interesting question placed before a student.

Yan Shuo heard the public words and nothing more.

He saw Teacher Mu remain nearby. He saw Ji Hongbin and the vice president waiting. He saw Lan Xuanyu straighten despite the pallor in his face.

Then Song touched Yan's shoulder lightly with the edge of his knuckles.

"We should go," Song said.

Yan nodded.

Dorm 336 left with the rest of the class.

Behind them, Lan Xuanyu remained.

The Maze test was over.

For him, another door was opening.

---

Far away on Heaven Dou Planet, frost still clung to a girl's fingertips.

Dong Qianqiu stood in silence for a long time after the Gate connection broke. The room around her was quiet. No Maze dust. No Golden Lion pressure. No white spear light. Only the memory of a roar remained, rolling through her body as if it had known where to strike.

She had answered the summon with refusal.

She had still reached through.

Then that roar had come.

Her palm closed slowly.

Ice cracked and fell in glittering pieces.

Why did it feel familiar?

No one in Heaven Luo Academy's public classroom heard that question.

No one in Dorm 336 heard it.

The Gate's far side remained a secret.
