- Ye Lingtong has reached rank 10 and has obtained her first ring/skill.

## 4. Yan Shuo integration plan

Yan Shuo should be present where school/public context supports him and absent where Lan-family privacy requires it.

### Public school run and first clash

- Yan Shuo belongs naturally in the attack-type group.
- He can be on the field during the joint physical run.
- He should not outrun Ye Lingtong; her canon first-place result stays intact.
- He can run in the front cluster through warmth, breath control, and steady legs, but without a ring or flashy fire.
- He notices Lan Xuanyu choosing a steady middle pace and understands it emotionally as caution, not as the whole secret.
- When Ye Lingtong tries the shoulder throw, Yan Shuo can start forward but stop because the teachers are present and because he has learned not to rush blindly.
- During the sudden heavy-weight event, Yan Shuo can feel a brief, strange heaviness in his chest. This is a pressure-resistance exposure, not knowledge.
- His Fire Phoenix holds shape with effort; no one notices.

### Injury aftermath

- Yan Shuo should not celebrate Ye Lingtong being hurt.
- His good heart matters: he is angry that she attacked his friend, but uncomfortable with real injury.
- He can check on Lan Xuanyu later and hear only Lan Xuanyu's child-level confusion.
- Ye Lingtong can resent both boys in different ways: Lan Xuanyu as the one who humiliated her, Yan Shuo as the attack-class witness who did not laugh but also did not bow.

### Half-year school routine

Recommended Yan Shuo state by end of term:

- Starts Chapter 5 at innate level 6, no ring.
- Trains steadily under academy and Lin Mei.
- Corrected by author feedback: rank 7 after half a year was too slow. Yan Shuo reaches rank 7 early, rank 8 through sustained training, rank 9 while the term still has weeks left, and rank 10 bottleneck before final exam week.
- Reaches Rank 10 first bottleneck before final exam week.
- Still does not obtain Dawnflame Kite yet in Chapter 5.
- Develops better breath-fire control, stamina discipline, and anti-pressure steadiness.
- Learns that attack-type training includes patience, footwork, restraint, and safe output.

### Final exam

- Yan Shuo is also Rank 10 by final exam week but remains ringless at the first bottleneck. Keep him in a constrained no-ring/ringless practical route and let him show competence without stealing Ye Lingtong's yellow-ring spotlight or the canon Lan Xuanyu rematch.
- He can have a brief parallel practical exam against an unnamed or minor attack-type student to show growth.
- His result should be good but not unbelievable: strong control for a level-6-start child, not top of all combat.
- Ye Lingtong still chooses Lan Xuanyu.
- Yan Shuo witnesses the Celestial Dragon skill fail against Lan Xuanyu's water whirlpool.
- His pressure seed reacts more clearly than before: breath catches, shoulders heat, Fire Phoenix tightens, then steadies.
- He must not diagnose superior dragon suppression.
- He must not release Phoenix pressure or counter it.

### Lan family private aftermath

- Yan Shuo should not be inside the Lan home during Ye Feng's visit.
- The chapter may still include Lan family private scenes from Lan Xiao/Lan Xuanyu side, as Chapter 4 already did, but Yan Shuo must not learn those details.
- Yan Shuo can learn only safe aftermath: Lan Xuanyu was tired, ate a lot, was absent/resting, or seems changed.
- Yan Shuo can feel a faint after-pressure when he next sees Lan Xuanyu, but this remains subtle and unnoticed.

### Ending bridge

If Chapters 23-24 are included:

- Yan Shuo may see Lan Xuanyu at the last school day before holiday.
- Ye Lingtong's apology/check-in can remain between her and Lan Xuanyu, with Yan Shuo nearby but not taking over.
- Yan Shuo can hear about Lan Xuanyu's family trip in a child-safe way.
- End with the holiday/travel edge, not the spacecraft scene itself.

## 6. Character handling notes

- He remains gentle, innocent, and confused by conflict.
- He is cautious because of Lan Xiao's warnings.
- His combat wins are accidental or caused by hidden nature, not battle skill mastery.
- His private growth after Ye Feng's pressure is important and should remain his canon pillar.

### Ye Lingtong

- Proud, fast, trained, and talented.
- Her father-pressure and desire for recognition drive much of the conflict.
- She is not evil. She is a proud child who has never learned how to lose quietly.
- Her injury and humiliation should be uncomfortable, not comedic cruelty.
- Her later apology/check-in should be awkward and childlike, not instant friendship.

### Yan Shuo

- Protective but learning restraint.
- Good-hearted enough to dislike Ye Lingtong's bullying and still worry when she is hurt.
- Competitive but not arrogant enough to steal every stage.
- He must develop his own attack-class school life, not only orbit Lan Xuanyu.
- His pressure-resistance seed can show through breath, heat, hunger/tiredness, and instinctive steadiness.

### Adults

- Teachers should be practical and sometimes limited by what they can see.
- Lin Mei should continue grounding Yan Shuo's fire through breath and restraint.
- Yan Ke can handle records, safety, school logistics, and parental worry.
- Lan Xiao and Nan Cheng protect Lan Xuanyu's privacy.
- Ye Feng is stern and dangerous but not malicious; his mistake comes from instinctive martial-soul reaction and investigative pressure.
