# THE ADAPTATION TALENT — the OC's hidden engine

Pattern learned from `soul_land_4_fire_phoenix` → `bible/ADAPTATION_TALENT_LOCAL.md`
(the useful part, adapted to this serial — not copied wholesale).

**Purpose:** define the OC's native, womb-born adaptation foundation without
ever turning it into a system cheat or a publicly known ability.

## 1. Core definition

The OC has a passive, always-working adaptation foundation.

It is:

- **native-born — from the womb (PRE2);**
- **not meta knowledge (R8);**
- **unnamed in-story by characters;**
- **not a voice, screen, system, or explicit skill (PRE4);**
- **not public knowledge;**
- **not full immunity;**
- **not instant mastery.**

It works by helping body, breath, recovery, and training memory adjust under
repeated pressure — the same engine the fire-phoenix serial runs, expressed
as a village boy's growth.

## 2. Early-life expression (village childhood)

- steadiness other toddlers don't have;
- body learning to shrug heat, cold, bumps, hunger;
- breath and movement correcting before anyone explains;
- recovery and appetite changes the village notices but can't name;
- fire-adjacent tolerance growing around his lizard soul.

No character calls this a "talent" by name unless later deliberately
discovered on page.

## 3. Training expression

Improves through: repetition · correct pressure · recovery · food ·
hard work · danger · the awakening itself · soul-ring growth.
It converts experience into better fit rather than granting free victory.

## 4. Costs and limits

Still needs: sleep · food · time · careful training · body safety.
Wrong pressure can cause injury or instability. Growth is powerful but not
free. **The No-Fix Law (PRE3) governs it: nothing about its ceiling is
pre-written.**

## 5. Martial soul interaction

Adaptation helps integrate the **Ground Fire Dragon Lizard** soul as rings
arrive — compatibility and recovery only; it cannot bypass all bottlenecks
or replace the work. Ring-by-ring policy is not yet ruled (POWER_LAW).

## 6. Hard bans

- Never a System: no windows, levels, menus, voices, quest text. (PRE4)
- Never instant mastery, never immunity, never a free win.
- Never named aloud by a character before an earned on-page discovery.
- Never described with exact percentages/figures in prose (R11).
