# STORY_ARCS — the shape of the books

**Direction rule (R12):** the canon road in parallel + natural
consequences. Arcs emerge on page — nothing is pre-written, no villain
sentence exists (that format is banned).

## Book 0 — the village (written)

Chapter 1: birth foundation — "Two Beginnings". ✅
Chapter 2: awakening day — the OC's fire lizard, Tang San's canon moment,
the friend beat, the mother at the door. ✅
Chapter 3: three months — expectation, the shut door, the want spoken,
earning begins (no promise, no quota). ✅
Chapter 4: the road out — canon departure; the boy stays (author: "No"
to him taking the road). ✅

## Book 0 continues (not written)

- The friendship years beside canon: hill, river, blacksmith's door.
- Canon's three months of blacksmithing — comes canon-shaped when it
  comes.
- Expectation building in the village (D1 butterfly — natural, no
  invented ceremonies).
- The road out: Jack takes Tang San to Nuoding — the OC's place on that
  road is his to earn on page.

## Book 1+ — not sketched

No entrance, no trial, no climb is written down yet. The No-Fix Law
forbids sketching an endpoint "just for now".

## Banned repair moves (learned from the author's fire-phoenix project)

- No invented ceremony or reward scene to "prove" the OC matters.
- No using the OC to replace or shortcut a canon beat.
- No forced set-pieces — consequences stay natural.
