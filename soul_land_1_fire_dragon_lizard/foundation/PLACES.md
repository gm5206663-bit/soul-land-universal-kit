# PLACES — where things happen

## Holy Spirit Village (圣魂村) — home

~300 households, south of Nuoding, Fasinuo Province, Heaven Dou.
Elder Old Jack. West side: Tang Hao's forge, the crudest house. North-end
house: the OC and his mother (design). The wide wooden house at the
center: the spirit hall where the awakening happened (Chapter 1).

## The road out
