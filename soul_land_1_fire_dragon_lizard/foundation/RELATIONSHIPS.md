# RELATIONSHIPS — who knows what

**Author knowledge is never character knowledge.** If a character hasn't
earned it on page, they don't have it.

| Who | Knows | Doesn't know |
|---|---|---|
| Chen Xing | mechanics of ranks/rings (true world facts); that he adapts under pressure — feels it, has no name for it; his own name and his father's trade | any plot; what the talent "is"; Tang San's hammer |
| Tang San | the OC is his best friend; the OC runs hot and heals fast (grew up beside it) | the word "adaptation"; anything about the plot |
| Xiu (his mother) | her son has always been warm, fast-healing, different — she's been reading it for years; he asked for the road at the lamp; the tin is road-money with no date on it | what it's called; Su Yuntao's verdict beyond the village news; which road the tin is for |
| Chen Jian (his father) | his son's hands, his measuring eye, the boy's questions at the bench | the talent; the road-talk; anything about the plot |
| Tang Hao | the boy works the bellows exact; mended the woodpile unseen; asks questions about the metal; comes when the door is open | the boy's want; anything about the talent; that the boy watches the lane |
| Old Jack | a listener worth telling stories to; a worker at the woodpile; "then we see" was all he ever promised | the tin; whether a second boy will ever be on that road |
| Su Yuntao | "mid soul power, fire beast, attack type" — filed and left | anything else about the boy |
| the village | the OC woke a fire lizard; Tang San lit the room like a window | anyone's secrets; the hammer |

## Three leaks that kill a firewall

1. Suspecting is not knowing.
2. The reader's canon memory never comes out of a character who hasn't
   seen it.
3. Information never arrives earlier than the page earns it.

Every speaking character gets checked against this table before drafting.
New knowledge? This table moves the same turn the page earns it.
