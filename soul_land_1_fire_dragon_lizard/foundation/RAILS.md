# RAILS — how every line of prose sounds

- **Third-limited, the OC's point of view, past tense, plain and clear.**
  Short sentences at the beats. Real dialogue. No literary stacking —
  the author's own chapters are the model.
- Numbers and figures live in `STATUS.md`, never in prose.
- Panels (`【…】`) only on update/gain beats. Chapter 1 has none; its
  `## Footer` carries state instead — same as the author's own chapters.
- No System words. No stat screens. The talent is felt, not tabulated.
- Technique grows Low → Mid → High. Nothing is ever "final".
- Canon scenes: quote the spirit of the source, don't lift long passages.
- No line may fix the future — no "he would never again", no prophecy of
  his endpoint.
- Chapter shape: plain title (`# Chapter 1: …`), prose, `---`,
  `## Footer` with state.
