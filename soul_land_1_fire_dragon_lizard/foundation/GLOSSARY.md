# GLOSSARY — terms as this serial uses them

- **Adaptation Talent** — the OC's womb-born talent. Passive, always on.
  Never called anything in-story. No System.
- **Ground Fire Dragon Lizard** — the OC's martial soul (the author's
  exact name). Fire beast. Beast family canon-receipted (SL4 ch154).
- **No-Fix Law** — *nothing is fixed; things go naturally.* No ceiling,
  no endpoint, anywhere in this serial.
- **Holy Spirit Village (圣魂村)** — home. Tang San's village.
- **Innate soul power** — what the crystal reads at awakening. Ours: mid
  (5–7). Tang San's: full (canon).
- **Womb talent** — the adaptation started before birth.
- **Direction (R12)** — canon road + natural consequences. The
  blank-sentence "spine" format was rejected by the author and is banned.
- **Fire-phoenix serial** — the author's SL4 line, where the same beast
  name appears as a summon. Shared name, zero crossover.
- **Coverage file** — written before every chapter; records which canon
  was consumed.
