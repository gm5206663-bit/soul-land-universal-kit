### TALENT CANON (operating rules — upgraded 2026-09-04 from the SL3 Level 0–10 system)
**FUNDAMENTAL LAW (verbatim):** *"The ability does not create greatness. It reveals,
refines, and develops what already exists."* The talent is an eternal self-development
engine embedded in Keal's existence. It does not grant power; it transforms potential
into capability — and his journey never stops.

**IT CAN:** improve efficiency · remove weaknesses · optimize existing abilities ·
accelerate natural improvement · develop unused potential · build resistance through
exposure · improve compatibility · refine control and mastery. (It MAY also reveal,
derive, and evolve new *applications* from an existing foundation — "cannot create from
nothing" never meant "can never create anything new.")

**IT CANNOT:** give random powers · break the world's laws · ignore energy requirements
(hunger, sleep, strain) · instantly grant knowledge · replace training · skip experience
· create an impossible ability with no foundation.

**NON-SENSE (absolute):** the talent is a self-evolving optimization, not a voice. It
never speaks, warns, narrates, or "delivers insight." All agency is Keal's. **Banned
phrasings** (they turn it into a system voice — config `voice_tics`): "a picture
assembled itself" · "the answer arrived fully formed" · "his body computed" · "the
certainty came before the evidence." Show it as *embodied perception* — sharper noticing,
faster reading, a glance, a reflex, a corrected movement.

**TIER-MIRRORING (why it is human-level):** the talent has no independent power ladder —
it scales with the holder and **never exceeds them.** Keal occupies the **mortal tier**,
so the talent operates at mortal scope: it optimizes mortal body, mind, and skill, pushing
him toward the edge of what a human can be — it cannot, on its own, produce the
supernatural (camouflage, the staff, Evolution!). That supernatural layer is PART B (the
Miraculous), a different thing. **This is the mechanical proof of THE DISTINCTION and of
"never a superpower."** (Tier: mortal → superhuman → supernatural → cosmic → divine;
Keal = mortal. Development within the tier: it matures as he matures.)

**THE FIVE STAGES (how an adaptation happens):** 1 *Observation* (it reads the situation —
a faster opponent, a cold room, a new crowd) · 2 *Analysis* (what is the limiting factor) ·
3 *Adjustment* (body/mind begins the optimization) · 4 *Integration* (it becomes natural,
no conscious effort) · 5 *Evolution* (enough accumulated adaptations compound into deeper
change). Not every scene runs all five; most show 1–3.

**THE SPEED LAWS:** greater challenge → greater adaptation potential · repeated exposure is
required (one encounter rarely matters) · the closer something is to his foundation, the
faster it adapts · meaningful pressure is required (no challenge, no major change).

**FAILURE AS DATA:** a loss is information — it traces the weakness and opens a pathway; it
does not instantly make him stronger.

**CONSCIOUS / UNCONSCIOUS:** most of it runs as constant background optimization (recovery,
efficiency, environmental adjustment); under pressure he can *direct* it, and the result
depends on his foundation.

**THE LIMITS:** Law of Conservation (major improvement costs energy/time/food) · Law of
Foundation (the better the foundation, the greater the result) · Law of Individuality (his
path is his alone) · Law of Integration (new capabilities must integrate naturally).

**THE GAINS LAW:** every consolidated gain must alter something later on-page — perception,
difficulty, speed, cost, or tactics. A gain that changes nothing downstream is a trophy,
not an instrument.

  transform runs on a hard **3-hour timer**, and de-transforming **strips the powers
