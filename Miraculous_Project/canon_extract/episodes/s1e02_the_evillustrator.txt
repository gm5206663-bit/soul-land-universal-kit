## THE WRONGED
- Nathaniel Kurtzberg — a Dupont student with a crush on Marinette, left-handed,
  comic artist; his signature watermark is an exclamation mark.
- His comic is about a superhero alter ego ("Super Nathan") who saves Marinette.
- This episode is Nathaniel's birthday.

## OPENING (the fantasy)
- Cold open: Nathaniel imagines himself as "Super Nathan": at Dupont, Lady Wifi
  pauses Marinette, Stormy Weather freezes her in ice; Super Nathan drops in,
  erases the umbrella with his tablet, boxes the two villains, frees Marinette;
  she calls him "Super Nathan" and they exchange love confessions.
- Reality: he was doodling the comic in Ms. Mendeleiev's science class.
- Olga Mendeleiev catches him drawing, is disgusted, sends him to Principal
  Denis Damoclès' office.
- He trips over Mylène's backpack; his bag spills; the comic drops.
- Chloé picks up the book, mocks Nathaniel AND Marinette to Sabrina (the class
  overhears). Olga tells Nathaniel to go to the office.
- Nathaniel takes the book back; Chloé rips out the LAST page.

## THE AKUMA
- Hawk Moth, sensing Nathaniel's emotions, sends an akuma; it lands on his
  STYLUS. Deal: power in exchange for "a couple of items he is missing"
  (the Miraculouses). Nathaniel accepts → the Evillustrator.
- Villain form = his own drawing of himself as a superhero (the akumatized
  victim's drawing becomes the form — first time this is shown).
- Power: a tablet + stylus that DRAW (create) and ERASE objects; the drawings
  come to life (berets, a giant hairdryer, barriers, boxing gloves, a saw
  blade, a ball-and-chain, a giant heel). Weakness: he needs LIGHT — he cannot
  draw properly in the dark (clouds over the moon; the broken light switch).

## SCHOOL BEATS
- Particle physics research presentations: groups — Nino + Adrien + Alya;
  Chloé + Sabrina + Marinette. Chloé complains, wants to work alone with
  Sabrina; Olga: deal with it. Alya offers to be Marinette's wingwoman
  (regarding Adrien).
- Locker: Chloé and Sabrina at Chloé's locker — Chloé decides she isn't
  helping; Sabrina readily agrees to do the work (canon: Chloé has had Sabrina
  do her homework since grade school). Marinette confronts: what the two have
  isn't friendship; best friends don't take advantage. Chloé: she is Sabrina's
  ONLY friend, so Sabrina has to listen. Marinette: she'd rather have no
  friends than be used. Chloé leaves (her hair, better things to do).
- Sabrina thanks Marinette and declares her her NEW BEST FRIEND. They go to
  the library to work.

## LIBRARY FIGHT
- In the library, Chloé and Sabrina argue about the assignment; Chloé bribes
  Sabrina with a Gabriel beret; several berets fall on Chloé's head, then a
  giant hairdryer appears and chases Chloé around the library.
- Adrien sees this and transforms into Cat Noir. Marinette runs, hides,
  transforms into Ladybug.
- Ladybug lassos the hairdryer, rides it, tries to control it (Cat Noir
  puns); she knocks it down; Cat Noir smashes it with his staff.
- They find the Evillustrator on the second level; they chase; he draws a
  barrier and escapes.

## CHLOÉ'S ROOM (Le Grand Paris)
- Ladybug + Cat Noir go to Chloé's room to learn why she is targeted.
- Chloé is defacing Nathaniel's drawing of himself with Marinette.
- "Everyone adores me" [dialogue-verified]. Ladybug calls her "adorable"
  (sarcastic); Chloé doesn't hear the sarcasm, takes it as a compliment,
  follows Ladybug taking selfies. Cat Noir watches.
- Ladybug finds the defaced photo and leaves in a huff, telling Cat Noir to
  watch Chloé without her.

## MARINETTE'S ROOM + THE INVITATION
- Ladybug detransforms; throws her purse; Tikki flies out. Marinette rants
  about Chloé; Tikki tells her to calm down.
- Marinette remembers her project: missed calls, all from Sabrina.
- The Evillustrator erases a window in her room and enters.
- He is NOT there to harm her — he attacked Chloé because she is cruel and
  selfish. He invites her to his BIRTHDAY PARTY on the Seine.
- The invitation bears his exclamation-mark watermark; she says yes.
- Ladybug calls Cat Noir: Evillustrator is in love with Marinette and promised
  not to harm Chloé so long as she attends. Plan: Cat Noir protects Marinette
  (and gets the stylus from Evillustrator); Ladybug handles the rest.
- Chloé tries to get Cat Noir back inside to do her physics homework; he
  turns her away and goes to Marinette's house.
- Sabrina, on the door stoop: she completed Marinette's GEOGRAPHY homework as a
  show of friendship; Marinette accepts; Sabrina wants to start the physics
  project now; Marinette can't (something really important); Sabrina gets mad,
  compares Marinette to Chloé (vague excuses), takes the homework back, leaves.
- Cat Noir jumps to Marinette's doorstep — introduces himself to Marinette
  (unmasked, in front of her — FIRST time they meet as Marinette/Cat Noir),
  flirts heavily, kisses her hand, calls her "little lady" (FR: "princess"),
  says she gets to be "[his] Ladybug"; tells her to get Evillustrator's stylus
  away from him and he'll do the rest; runs off. Marinette giggles.
- [canon development: first time Marinette meets Adrien as his alter ego.]

## THE PARTY (evening, Seine, near Notre-Dame)
- Marinette arrives at the Evillustrator's ship by Notre-Dame (the party is on
  a boat).
- Hawk Moth interrupts the Evillustrator (causing him pain in the arm),
  warning him to get the Miraculouses; he hastily agrees.
- Marinette wishes him happy birthday; he shows her around.
- Clouds cover the moon, blocking his light; he gets angry (he can't draw
  properly in the dark); he draws an illuminated globe and puts it atop a
  miniature Eiffel Tower on the deck.
- They sit on a bench; musical notes float off his tablet into the night.
- Marinette sees Cat Noir on the rooftops; she distracts the Evillustrator;
  Cat Noir gets into position (hops down from the Pont de l'Archevêché and
  hides).
- Marinette convinces him to let her draw something; her hand is on the
  stylus when Cat Noir's shoes make a noise; she grabs the stylus and runs;
  Cat Noir reveals himself.
- The Evillustrator is hurt — he thought Marinette liked him; compares her to
  Chloé. Marinette: everyone is comparing her to Chloé tonight.

## THE BETRAYAL + THE FINAL FIGHT (Chloé's room again)
- The Evillustrator kicks Cat Noir's staff, knocking the stylus from
  Marinette's hand; he grabs the stylus, lands on the illuminated globe,
  hastily draws a CUBE trapping Cat Noir + Marinette; erases part of the boat
  (a hole; it sinks) and goes after Chloé — his promise broken.
- Marinette angles Cat Noir's baton, lifting the cube; they escape onto the
  streets of Paris.
- Cat Noir: "you can thank me later" [dialogue-verified]. Marinette (to
  Tikki): he should be thanking HER — she freed them. She retransforms and
  follows Cat Noir back to Le Grand Paris.
- Chloé practices crying in front of her mirror, lying about how traumatic the
  attack was; she stops when even she stops believing it.
- The Evillustrator erases her door (she hides under the bed); erases the
  closet and clothes (she shrieks); erases the bed ("he has won"); sends a
  giant heel to step on her; Ladybug breaks the heel.
- The Evillustrator monologues: they should be on his side, Chloé is awful;
  Chloé refutes with delusions ("everyone adores me").
- Three large punching gloves: one hits Cat Noir, one misses Ladybug, one
  heads for Chloé. Ladybug destroys the glove before it hits Chloé and throws
  her yo-yo at the Evillustrator; he puts up a glass barrier.
- He draws a spinning saw blade at Ladybug; it bounces off her spinning yo-yo
  toward Chloé; Cat Noir throws his staff, stopping it just in time, breaks
  the staff in two, throws both halves; one breaks a LIGHT, forcing the
  Evillustrator to move — Ladybug tells Cat Noir about the weakness.
- Cat Noir Cataclysms the light switch, but the Evillustrator erases the
  switch before the wall comes down. He attaches a ball-and-chain to Cat
  Noir's leg and erases the ground beneath him; Cat Noir hangs into the room
  BELOW Chloé's.
- Hawk Moth intervenes, demanding the Cat Miraculous.
- Lucky Charm: a small BOUNCY BALL. Ladybug throws it, breaking ALL the lights
  in the room; the Evillustrator shields his face, open; Ladybug grabs him
  with the yo-yo; the stylus drops; she catches it and BREAKS it. Cat Noir's
  ring signals one minute left. The ball-and-chain disappears; Cat Noir climbs
  back in. Ladybug catches the akuma, de-evilizes it, returns Chloé's room
  (and her own window) to normal.

## THE NEXT DAY (denouement)
- Alya interviews Chloé about last night's attack; Chloé complains the heroes
  made matters worse. Marinette overhears and laughs.
- Sabrina, wearing the Gabriel beret, believes Chloé's lies; she and Chloé
  (really just Sabrina) will do the project; she goes to tell Chloé her
  geography homework is ready.
- Marinette closes her locker; Adrien appears behind her and startles her;
  he asks about Cat Noir; she stutters, calls Adrien dreamy and "the most
  awesome of awesome" [dialogue-verified]; he takes it as her being a Cat Noir
  fan, pats her shoulder, leaves. Tikki: she just called ADRIEN "awesome" to
  his face; she needs better control over her emotions.
- End card: a new comic in Nathaniel's sketchbook — about Ladybug.
