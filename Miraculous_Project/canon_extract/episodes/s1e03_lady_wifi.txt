# Distilled canon beats — write from this, never paste (Z2). Paraphrase unless marked.

## THE WRONGED
- Alya Césaire — the teen vlogger/journalist, Marinette's best friend.
- Grievance: SUSPENDED a week for "nosiness"/trespassing in Chloé's locker — she was
  trying to PROVE who Ladybug is (she's falsely convinced it's Chloé).
- Motivation: "I will expose the lies of anyone who tries to cover up the truth."

## SETUP (Day 1)
- Literature class (Miss Caline Bustier's class). Alya cuts LADYBUG'S FACE out of a
  photograph and holds it up to classmates' faces, looking for the perfect fit.
  She stops on Chloé; Chloé points at Miss Bustier (who is standing there). The whole
  class is watching; Alya puts the picture away.
- Miss Bustier asks if Marinette is still in the girls' room (she's been absent).
- After school: Alya calls Marinette → voicemail. She sees Chloé pulling
  LADYBUG-THEMED items (a costume + a yo-yo) out of her locker into her bag.
- Alya (falsely) concludes Chloé = Ladybug. She drags Nino along to explain.
  Nino is skeptical: Chloé is too narcissistic to be a hero. (Nino notes Adrien also
  disappears for hours when you call; he blames Mr. Agreste's strictness.)
- Alya sees a TVi news report (reporter: Nadja Chamack): Ladybug saved Paris again
  (the Evillustrator fight; Cat Noir in the background). She recognizes the YO-YO in
  Ladybug's hand as the one Chloé took from her locker → tells Nino (whispered):
  Chloé is Ladybug. Nino laughs.
- Marinette comes home; Alya has dropped off her book bag + tablet at the bakery with
  Sabine, with a NOTE: "Call me. I know who the REAL Ladybug is!!" (FR original).
  Marinette is a little panicked; she can't call Alya back — no signal (the service is
  being updated; a workman on the roof confirms). Marinette does homework, falls asleep
  at her desk.

## SUSPENSION + AKUMA (Day 2)
- Morning: Marinette wakes to find she's already an hour LATE for school.
- Nino distracts Chloé while Alya tries to photograph the locker "evidence." Sabrina
  and Kim catch Alya snooping. Alya + Chloé end up in the Principal's office.
- Mr. Damoclès gives Alya detention; Chloé, citing privacy + the theft policy and her
  father's name (Mayor André Bourgeois), gets Alya SUSPENDED for a week.
- Alya storms out just as Marinette runs in. Alya swears to expose Chloé / tarnish her
  reputation as Ladybug.
- Hawk Moth (in the lair) senses Alya crying + her anger; sends the akuma. It lands on
  her CELLPHONE (the one with the charm Marinette recognizes) → transforms her into
  Lady Wifi. The deal (per the episode's own quote): Hawk Moth — "Lady Wifi, I heard
  through the grapevine that you're seeking to unmask Ladybug... I'd like to help you
  if you agree to help me too." Alya: "I will expose the lies of anyone who tries to
  cover up the truth. Sign me up!" (i.e., he helps her expose the truth/unmask
  Ladybug; she brings him the Ladybug + Cat Noir Miraculouses — the standard deal.)

## LADY WIFI'S POWER (the akumatized phone)
- PAUSE: freezes a person with a pink PAUSE icon (freezes Damoclès in his seat;
  freezes Chloé).
- VIDEO RECORDING: opens a recording icon to BROADCAST to the public (the "unmasking").
- TELEPORT: travels phone-to-phone / to a ringing phone (teleports into a room when
  the phone rings).
- LOCK icon: locks doors (every floor door in the hotel).
- Weakness: needs a SIGNAL — no signal in the basement; the signal receiver on the
  hotel roof is the source.

## MARINETTE + THE PROJECTOR
- Marinette sneaks into Miss Bustier's class; disrupts it by shouting "What?!" twice
  (learning Alya was suspended for trespassing in "Ladybug's" locker). Miss Bustier
  sends her to the Principal's office → finds Mr. Damoclès FROZEN with a pink pause
  icon. Lady Wifi uses the school PROJECTOR to play a recording of herself: she
  introduces herself as "the deliverer of truth" and promises to show ALL OF PARIS who
  Ladybug really is. Marinette recognizes Alya from the charm on her phone → transforms
  into Ladybug to save her best friend.
- Miss Bustier dismisses the class for safety. Adrien runs to the restroom to transform
  into Cat Noir (reasoning Lady Wifi will visit Chloé if she really is Ladybug).

## THE HOTEL (Le Grand Paris is a HOTEL)
- That evening: Cat Noir (running on all fours like a cat) waits on a rooftop across
  from Le Grand Paris, watching Chloé's window. He finds her DRESSED AS LADYBUG,
  practicing her yo-yo — he panics, thinking he fell for Chloé. The real Ladybug appears
  beside him and explains Chloé is just a huge fan.
- Chloé's phone rings; Lady Wifi teleports from the phone into Chloé's room. She
  FREEZES Chloé (pause icon) and opens the video recording to broadcast Chloé's
  "unmasking" to the public. Cat Noir + Ladybug arrive; Lady Wifi realizes her MISTAKE
  (Chloé isn't Ladybug). She tries to freeze them; they escape into the stairwell and
  LURE her toward the basement (no signal).
- Lady Wifi catches on and sets a trap. The heroes run back up; every floor door has a
  LOCK icon. (Cat Noir suggests they get to know each other OUTSIDE the costume;
  Ladybug brushes him off — "more important things to worry about.")
- They reach an unlocked floor → the hotel's DINING AREA: a phone on every table.
  Lady Wifi can teleport around the room via the phones. Ladybug starts smashing the
  phones with her yo-yo. Lady Wifi flees into the KITCHEN and locks the door, trapping
  Ladybug inside with her. Cat Noir uses the SERVICE ELEVATOR to get into the kitchen.
- Lady Wifi corners Ladybug against the wall, LOCKS her hands down, re-opens the video
  recording, and tries to peel off Ladybug's MASK — it appears GLUED on (magically;
  can't be removed by physical contact). Cat Noir arrives in the food elevator to rescue
  her, but Lady Wifi TRAPS him in the FREEZER and locks the door. The impact KNOCKS THE
  RING off Cat Noir's finger → he de-transforms into ADRIEN (a forced de-transform) and
  can't find the ring. Plagg REFUSES to help — it's too cold (Plagg's cosmic energy is
  flash-frozen; the improper de-transform hit the smaller kwami harder).
- Hawk Moth advises Lady Wifi: force Ladybug to use LUCKY CHARM. When her time runs out,
  the recording will broadcast her de-transforming, and Lady Wifi can take her
  Miraculous. Lady Wifi UNLOCKS Ladybug's hands but leaves the recording ON, then
  disappears, leaving Ladybug in the kitchen to rescue Cat Noir from the locked freezer.
- Chloé escapes the building and joins her father Mayor Bourgeois + the police outside.
  Her phone rings; Lady Wifi teleports from the phone onto the hotel ROOF, forcing PARIS
  to WATCH as Ladybug tries to save Cat Noir.

## THE WIN (Lucky Charm = a FROZEN MEAL)
- Kicking the freezer door doesn't work. Ladybug uses LUCKY CHARM → a frozen meal
  appears. Using her lucky vision she spots: the NON-MICROWAVABLE icon on the box, the
  LOCK icon on the freezer door, and a nearby MICROWAVE. She puts the
  non-microwaveable dinner in the microwave → it BLOWS UP → DIFFUSES THE SIGNAL. The
  doors unlock just as Adrien, nearly frozen to death, FINDS HIS RING and transforms
  back into Cat Noir. Ladybug opens the door; he passes out and falls on top of her
  (worn from the cold), then wakes quickly and realizes she used Lucky Charm, shortening
  their time to defeat Lady Wifi.
- The kitchen door is still locked; Ladybug suggests blowing up the SIGNAL RECEIVER on
  the roof. Cat Noir covers the camera icon with a pot and rides the food elevator out
  while Ladybug waits in the kitchen. Lady Wifi, suspicious, returns and chases Cat
  Noir onto the roof. He distracts her by throwing his STAFF at her, then uses
  CATACLYSM to destroy the signal receiver. Lady Wifi loses her powers; the recording +
  lock icons in the kitchen dissolve; Ladybug's earrings beep down to TWO SPOTS.
  Ladybug escapes the kitchen to the roof, yanks the phone out of Lady Wifi's hand with
  her yo-yo, smashes it on the ground, CATCHES the akuma, and returns everything to
  normal with MIRACULOUS LADYBUG.

## THE CLOSET + THE AFTERMATH
- Lady Wifi turns back into Alya, thrilled to see the heroes. But Ladybug is about to
  de-transform, so they escape the roof before Alya can interview them. They run into
  the hotel lobby; Ladybug ducks inside a CLOSET. Cat Noir tries to stop her, promising
  not to tell anyone who she is; she says "nobody must know who we really are, not even
  us." The closet door swings between them just as her transformation wears off; Cat
  Noir is tempted to open the door but, conflicted, decides NOT to betray her trust —
  he leaves, closing the door for her.
- Adrien runs outside and opens his jacket; Plagg flies out and questions him. Adrien:
  "the love of his life" was right there. Plagg asks why he shut the door for her;
  Adrien: "I can't think when I'm with Ladybug."

## THE NEXT DAY (denouement)
- Marinette + Tikki talk about secret identities on the balcony; both agree it's safer
  for her being Ladybug to remain a secret.
- Alya appears on the balcony with a NEW phone. She has noticed that when she draws a
  costume over a picture of ADRIEN, he kind of looks like Cat Noir. Marinette is
  OFFENDED that Alya would compare Adrien to someone like Cat Noir. Marinette jokes that
  Alya should send the Adrien photos to her; she GRABS Alya's phone and chases her
  around the balcony, both laughing.

## PLOT DEVELOPMENTS / TRIVIA ANCHORS
- Takes place in SEPTEMBER (Chloé says Halloween is next month, after telling off
  Aurore's outfit).
- Confirms Ladybug + Cat Noir don't know each other's secret identity (until Cat Noir
  suggests they might know each other in normal life; Ladybug finds it hard to believe).
- Cat Noir runs on ALL FOURS like a cat (Miraculous holders develop the movement
  patterns of their jewel's animal).
- A Miraculous holder's MASK is magically GLUED on — can't be removed by physical
  contact.
- The improper/forced de-transform danger: the ring knocked off, the flash-freeze,
  Plagg (smaller) hit harder, cosmic energy frozen.
- If Lady Wifi had removed Ladybug's earrings, Marinette's identity would have been
  revealed to Hawk Moth and all of Paris.
- The episode was WRITTEN after "The Evillustrator" but in-story takes place BEFORE it
  (Lady Wifi appears in Nathaniel's dream in The Evillustrator alongside Stormy Weather)
  — a canon-acknowledged ordering quirk; our AU follows PRODUCTION order (this is ch3,
  after our ch2 Evillustrator), so the dream reference in ch2 stands as canon did.
- The photo Alya cuts Ladybug's face out of: an old promotional picture (Korean version)
  / updated picture (FR/EN).
- The building the fight happens in: the hotel (Le Grand Paris is a hotel).
- EPISODE QUOTE (the deal): Hawk Moth: "Lady Wifi, I heard through the grapevine that
  you're seeking to unmask Ladybug... I'd like to help you if you agree to help me too."
  Alya: "I will expose the lies of anyone who tries to cover up the truth. Sign me up!"
