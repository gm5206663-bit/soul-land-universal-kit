## THE WRONGED
- Rose Lavillant — Marinette's best friend (with Juleka Couffaine), the "princess" type,
  romantic, perfumed.
- Grievance: Chloé tears up the love letter Rose wrote to a visiting prince and mocks her.
  Rose is heartbroken, crying, writing a second letter on a bench by the Seine.
- Hawk Moth's offer: the power that NO ONE can resist her perfume.

## SETUP (the sick kwami — the load-bearing thread)
- Cold open: TVi news recap of the PREVIOUS fight — Jean Duparc, akumatized as the "Magician
  of Misfortune," wanted to make the Eiffel Tower vanish. (First akuma fought OFFSCREEN.)
- Marinette notices TIKKI IS SICK (the "kwami flu" — first and only time a kwami is sick).
  Tikki wants a doctor; Marinette won't risk a doctor seeing a kwami. Tikki names a healer
  (Wang Fu) but Marinette fears he'll get suspicious. She's late for school; promises to take
  Tikki to the healer AFTER class.
- ⚠️ This is WHY Marinette cannot transform for most of the episode, and the seed of the
  "a sick kwami can still transform, but it is dangerous" reveal (see PLOT DEVELOPMENTS).

## SCIENCE CLASS (the win-seed)
- Class with Ms. Mendeleiev. Rose watches the news on her phone: PRINCE ALI, crowned prince
  of the (fictional) ACHU KINGDOM, is in Paris for an official visit to run a TOY DRIVE for
  kids in the city hospital. Smitten, Rose decides to write him a letter.
- Marinette arrives just as Rose is about to write. Rose sprays her perfume on the letter;
  Ms. Mendeleiev, annoyed Rose wasn't paying attention, sends her to the Principal's office.
- 🔑 ONCE ROSE IS GONE, Ms. Mendeleiev DEMONSTRATES TO THE CLASS THAT ROSE'S PERFUME IS
  FLAMMABLE. (This chemistry fact is the key to the whole victory — Ladybug will use fire.)

## THE LOST KWAMI (the letter + the "toy")
- After class: Adrien finally shows up outside school. Alya pushes Marinette to give him her
  notes; Marinette (remembering her promise to Tikki) tries to excuse herself; Alya doesn't
  believe her and forces her over. Marinette trips on a backpack and LAUNCHES TIKKI into the
  air; Adrien catches Marinette before she falls.
- Adrien + Nino leave. Marinette opens her purse — TIKKI IS MISSING. Next to her, Chloé +
  Sabrina head to the limo and find Tikki on the ground. Chloé mistakes her for a TOY and
  decides to give "it" to Prince Ali at Le Grand Paris.
- Rose arrives to ask Chloé to deliver her letter; Chloé mocks her and the letter and TEARS
  IT TO PIECES. The heartbroken Rose tries to gather the scraps. Marinette spots Tikki in
  Chloé's hand, panics, tries to claim the "toy" is hers; Chloé refuses; the limo drives off.

## AKUMA (the bench by the Seine)
- Rose, crying, sits on a bench under the Seine and writes a second letter; tears splash it.
- Hawk Moth senses her pain; sends the akuma to her perfume bottle; offers the power no one
  can resist; Rose accepts → akumatized as PRINCESS FRAGRANCE.
- VILLAIN POWER: a perfume GUN. PINK spray = mind-control (obedience); GREEN spray = a
  stinking repulsion smell. She can propel herself on the spray like a rocket, and blanket
  an area in a mind-controlling perfume FOG.

## RESCUE BEAT 1 — Marinette, civilian (Le Grand Paris)
- Marinette tries the doorman, claiming to be Chloé's friend; he refuses ("Chloé has no
  friends"). She finds a motorcycle helmet + cape + an old pizza box in the trash, disguises
  as a pizza girl, and gets in. In the elevator she's caught by Chloé, who — disgusted by
  the idea of pizza — slams the door button; Marinette runs up the stairwell.
- Meanwhile Princess Fragrance arrives, sprays the doorman (pink) into serving her, and enters.

## THE EVENT (top floor, live on TVi)
- Prince Ali greeted by Mayor André Bourgeois; the mayor mentions FIREWORKS on the Seine in
  front of the hotel, launched from a BOAT. Chloé is introduced to the prince and gives him
  the "toy" (Tikki); the prince tucks Tikki into his jacket pocket. Nadja interviews the
  prince.
- Princess Fragrance exits the service elevator; sprays a GREEN stink on Chloé; the
  MIND-CONTROLLED PAPARAZZI bow to her; she announces the prince will be hers once she sprays
  him.
- Without Tikki, Marinette CANNOT transform. She fires a CONFFETTI burst from a synthesizer
  to stop her from shooting the prince. Adrien, watching the live broadcast in his room,
  transforms into Cat Noir.

## FIGHT 1 — inside the hotel
- The group (Marinette, the prince, his chaperone Soraya Khan, the mayor, Chloé) hide in a
  hotel room (reinforced door). Perfume is sprayed under the door; Marinette VACUUMS it up
  before it reaches the prince. Princess Fragrance propels on the spray and unplugs the
  vacuum; the mayor bolts (with prince/chaperone/Chloé); she makes a perfume FOG.
- Marinette slips out through a brainwashed waiter; the mayor, holding the stairwell door, is
  caught in the fog and MIND-CONTROLLED.
- Cat Noir barges in just as she's about to spray the prince; a quick fight; he slides the
  prince, chaperone, and Chloé out of the hotel on his staff into the chaperone's car.
  Princess Fragrance chases on a rocket-spray; Marinette follows on a scooter.

## FIGHT 2 — the car, then the bridge
- The car rolls down its windows to vent the stink; she sprays EVERYONE IN THE CAR (the
  prince, and Cat Noir once he's in — Cat Noir is MIND-CONTROLLED). Tikki has escaped the
  prince's pocket to the car floor; Marinette finds her, apologizes for not taking her to the
  healer, and rushes her to Wang Fu's.
- 🔑 TIKKI (recovered by Fu) tells Marinette where the fight is. Despite the sick kwami,
  MARINETTE TRANSFORMS into Ladybug (the dangerous choice) and goes to the PONT DES ARTS.
- At the bridge, Princess Fragrance is about to "wed" the mind-controlled prince and hang a
  LOVE LOCK; Ladybug yo-yo-knocks the lock into the river. Under Hawk Moth's command she
  sends the mind-controlled servants — INCLUDING CAT NOIR — to fetch Ladybug's earrings, and
  takes the prince away.
- On the Le Grand Paris ROOF she sprays the SKY — a perfume fog over all of Paris ("our
  kingdom").
- 🔑 Ladybug realizes everyone in Paris (including her) is about to be enslaved. She REMEMBERS
  Ms. Mendeleiev's FLAMMABILITY warning from chemistry class, and sees the mayor's
  FIREWORKS BOAT. She tricks Cat Noir into shoving her off the roof onto the boat; she pulls
  the staff to haul him up; he uses CATACLYSM to stop her escape; she trips him onto the
  fireworks CONTROL PANEL — the fireworks LAUNCH into the sky; she drops him in the river.
- The FIREWORKS EXPLODE INTO THE PERFUME FOG and burn it away (fire defeats the perfume).
- Lucky Charm = a BALLOON. She threads it on a straw, jams it in the perfume gun's mouth
  (it inflates), yo-yo-snatches and breaks the gun, catches + purifies the akuma, tosses the
  balloon → Miraculous Ladybug cleanses the city and everyone (including Rose) is restored.

## DENOUEMENT
- Rose detransforms, delighted to see Prince Ali; Ladybug leaves the prince alone with "his
  biggest fan." Rose confesses he's one of the kindest people she's ever met. Chloé shoves
  Rose aside, declaring she's glad the prince is safe; the prince REFUSES Chloé ("pick up
  where we left off") — he's off to the kids' hospital with his new friend Rose. Chloé rages
  as Rose + the prince go watch the fireworks at the Pont des Arts.

## PLOT DEVELOPMENTS (canon facts that constrain the AU)
- A separated kwami can defend itself by using its small size + cuteness to LOOK LIKE A TOY
  / plush (Tikki "stuffs up").
- A kwami can get SICK (first/only). Wang Fu heals Tikki by "reading energies."
- 🔑 For a holder to transform SAFELY, the kwami's cosmic energy must flow freely and connect
  to the owner. A sick kwami CAN still transform — but it is DANGEROUS (severe/harmful
  effects, per later seasons). So BOTH the holder and the kwami must be strong enough.
- Ladybug and Cat Noir do NOT fight as a team this episode (Cat Noir is turned against her) —
  one of the six S1 episodes with no "Pound it!" and one of the four where Hawk Moth has no
  closing monologue.
- Cat Noir is MIND-CONTROLLED and tries CATACLYSM on Ladybug (echoes the later Dark Cupid).
- Chloé hates pizza (revealed). The word "kwami" is spoken on-page (one of two S1 episodes).
- Master Fu's first appearance (S1 order); a character sings outside a performance (the
  mind-controlled crowd).
