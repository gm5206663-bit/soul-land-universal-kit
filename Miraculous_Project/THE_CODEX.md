## §LAWS (permanent — the checkers enforce the enforceable ones)
**L-1 BUTTERFLY LAW — the Natural-Divergence Law (upgraded 2026-09-04 from the SL3
foundation).** The butterfly is not a pet. It does not sit on canon's shoulder.
- **Canon is ORE, not fate.** World-facts (power rules, places, identities, who-is-where)
  are true until our changes touch them. Canon's PLOT is one dead timeline's path — ours is
  alive. We mine the ore; we do not obey it for outcomes. (checks: zero_tolerance Z2 — 12-gram gate)
- **DIVERGENCE COMPOUNDS.** Every D-row is a standing change to the initial conditions,
  pressing on every thread it touches — not just the chapter it was born in. When our state
  implies a different outcome than canon, **OURS WINS.** No snap-backs: "canon's own beat
  respected" is a defect wearing a virtue's name. (checks: divergence_engine D1–D3)
- **MAGNITUDE LADDER.** Every butterfly is tagged in the footer: *dust* (a word) · *current*
  (a scene) · *bend* (an outcome) · *break* (a canon event now DEAD — never resurrect it) ·
  *STORM* (wholly original, no canon anchor). A canon-full streak while bends/breaks ride is
  the forcing-smell. Original things are the job, not the garnish.
- **THE SYMMETRY CLAUSE.** Forcing divergence is the same disease as forcing canon. If our
  causes honestly imply canon's outcome, taking it is derivation, not betrayal — and it stands.
- **THE ADAPTATION TEST.** For every canon beat, ask: *what does this scene look like
  differently because Keal is in it?* If the answer is "the same," the beat has not been
  adapted. Replaying a canon scene with Keal silent in the corner is FORCING CANON — a failure
  state. (the writer's per-beat test)
- **SCOPED HIERARCHY.** Canon's ranking does not survive him being in the room: any line that
  makes another character "the strongest / the best / the one who…" in a group he belongs to
  must be scoped (except Keal) where our facts imply it — the class, not the city. (the writer's
  per-line test)
- **COVERAGE.** Canon episodes are not our chapter breaks: one chapter spends as much ore as its
  scenes need — a whole episode, half of one, or a single beat. Lockstep 1:1 is a smell, not a
  rule. (checks: none — the writer's pacing mandate)
- **Every chapter shows ≥3 cause→effect butterflies**, each with a visible cause, none silent,
  all listed in the footer *Butterflies This Chapter*. (checks: divergence_engine D4)
