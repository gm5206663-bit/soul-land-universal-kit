# PART 4 — CANON VERIFICATION SYSTEM

## 4.0 OC Integration into Canon Stories

When writing fanfiction with original characters in established canon:

### The Fundamental Rule
**Write the CANON story with the OC integrated into it. NOT a separate OC story that happens alongside canon.**

### What This Means
- Canon events are the BACKBONE of the story
- The OC exists WITHIN those canon events
- The story follows canon timeline and major events
- The OC's presence naturally affects those events through logical consequences

### What This Does NOT Mean
- ❌ Writing a separate journey for the OC that ignores canon events
- ❌ Having the OC meet canon characters only at the end
- ❌ Skipping canon events to focus on OC backstory
- ❌ Treating canon as optional background while the OC does their own thing

### Proper Integration
✅ Write the actual canon scenes (e.g., Huo Yuhao in Star Dou Forest meeting Skydream Ice Worm)
✅ Have the OC present during those scenes (because they exist in this world)
✅ Show how the OC's presence naturally changes things (because that's what happens when a new person is there)
✅ Let canon events unfold with the OC woven into them

### Butterfly Effects Are NOT a Checklist
**Butterfly effects emerge ORGANICALLY and LOGICALLY from the OC's presence. They are NOT artificially inserted or counted.**

❌ WRONG: "I need to show 3-4 butterfly effects in this chapter"
✅ RIGHT: "The OC is here, so what would naturally happen? What would they do? How would that affect things?"

### Natural Consequences
When an OC exists in a canon story, changes happen because:
- They're physically present in scenes (affecting who meets whom, when, how)
- They have relationships with canon characters (changing dynamics)
- They have different knowledge/abilities (affecting outcomes)
- They make different choices than canon characters would (creating divergence)

These changes are NOT "planned butterfly effects." They're just **what would actually happen** if this person existed in this world.

### The Test
Ask yourself: "Am I writing the canon story with the OC in it, or am I writing an OC story that ignores canon?"

If the answer is the second one, STOP. Rewrite to follow canon events with the OC integrated.

### Example: Soul Land 2 with OC
**WRONG:**
- Chapter 1: OC leaves village, travels to academy, takes entrance exam, meets Huo Yuhao at the end
- Canon events (Huo Yuhao in Star Dou Forest, meeting Skydream Ice Worm) are skipped or happen off-screen

**RIGHT:**
- Chapter 1: Huo Yuhao goes to Star Dou Forest (canon event), OC is also there hunting soul beasts (integrated), they encounter each other naturally, Skydream Ice Worm appears (canon event), OC's presence affects the encounter (organic butterfly effect)
- Canon events are WRITTEN, with OC woven into them

### Learning from Mistakes
**Common Failure Pattern:** Writer gets excited about OC and writes their backstory/journey separately, forgetting to integrate them into the actual canon story.

**Prevention:** Before writing ANY chapter, ask:
1. What canon events happen in this chapter?
2. Where is my OC during these events?
3. How does their presence naturally affect these events?
4. Am I writing the canon story with the OC in it, or a separate OC story?

If you can't answer #1, you're not following canon. STOP and research.

## 4.1 Canon-First Protocol

Before using ANY canon scene, reconstruct it accurately:

- Exact sequence of events
- Location and time of day
- Characters present (entrances and exits)
- Causes of each action
- Important dialogue beats
- Iconic interactions
- Props and their positions
- Injuries and clothing
- What each character knows
- What remains hidden
- Immediate consequences
- Parallel events occurring elsewhere

### Rules:
- Never alter canon merely to provide the OC with attention
- Add the OC around canon first
- Change canon only when an earlier action has logically changed the cause
- Once a genuine butterfly effect changes an event, never force canon back without believable causal reason
- Do not move an event, transformation, battle, revelation, relationship step, or power awakening into an earlier episode for convenience
- Respect episode, chapter, season, and arc boundaries
- Record the original endpoint and cliffhanger before choosing the adapted chapter boundary

## 4.2 Canon Evidence Hierarchy

Use the strongest available evidence:

1. **Primary source** — actual episode, film, book chapter, comic issue, game scene
2. **Official materials** — subtitles, transcript, screenplay, script, production material
3. **Official synopsis** — official recap or synopsis
4. **Detailed contemporary notes** — preserves scene order
5. **Reputable guide** — well-maintained episode or chapter guide
6. **Fan reference** — well-maintained fan wiki (orientation and cross-checking only)
7. **Unverified hypothesis** — broad recap, search snippet, remembered description, AI summary

### Rules:
- If exact canon cannot be established and the missing fact could change the chapter, request the relevant source
- Do not manufacture connective scenes and call them canon
- When sources conflict, prefer the user's locked foundation and stronger canon evidence
- If the contradiction materially changes the story, stop and ask
- Every number needs a source — never state a cycle, year, age, duration, distance, count, date, measurement, rank, or statistic without citation
- Match the franchise's exact terminology and spelling — do not drift canonical terms into near-spellings

## 4.3 Canon Scene Ownership

Every important canon scene has an owner or group of owners. Preserve:

- Who performs the iconic action
- Who discovers the clue
- Who wins or loses the confrontation
- Who delivers the important line
- Who makes the defining choice
- Who experiences the emotional turning point
- Who reveals the information
- Who suffers the intended consequence

### Rules:
- The OC may witness, influence, assist, complicate, prevent, delay, or transform the event when prior causes justify it
- Do not casually steal canon achievements, emotional moments, relationships, victories, discoveries, or dialogue
- Do not transfer a canon protagonist's rescue, discovery, confrontation, sacrifice, leadership decision, emotional climax, or iconic line to the OC without established causal reason
- Audit the OC's narrative gravity — if canon characters increasingly become evidence, patients, suspects, admirers, or recipients while the OC makes every decision, the parallel protagonist has become a replacement protagonist

## 4.4 Pre-Draft Verification Gate

Before ANY prose:
1. Reconstruct the exact current checkpoint
2. Identify canon anchor and AU divergence
3. Verify location and physical transit
4. Verify timeline and event order
5. Build character knowledge states
6. Verify power-system mechanics
7. Verify regional/cultural/environmental realism
8. Audit prior chapter consequences
9. Quarantine contradictions and rejected ideas
10. Only then draft

# PART 5 — ADAPTATION TALENT SYSTEM

## 5.1 Canonical Definition

> **Adaptation Talent is an innate, pure, inseparable, non-sentient existential principle present from conception that continuously uses the holder's real existence, experience, information, resources, intentions, recovery, relationships, and accessible reality to preserve, refine, integrate, personalize, and evolve the holder's complete being along the best presently supportable path compatible with identity, free will, deepest desires, chosen bonds, and actual conditions.**

## 5.2 What It IS

- Innate, inseparable, non-sentient, present from conception
- Always operating (but not always escalating — may maintain, consolidate, recover, prepare)
- Distributed through the holder's complete existence
- Open-ended in potential, finite in every present expression
- Cumulative, self-correcting, individualized
- Scales with the holder's development (mortal → superhuman → supernatural → cosmic → divine)

## 5.3 What It Is NOT

- Not an AI, system, voice, menu, narrator, guide, spirit, second personality, parasite
- Not omniscience, omnipotence, instant immunity, or guaranteed survival
- Not a universal template — each holder develops completely differently
- Not a separate consciousness that speaks, chooses, wants, or decides
- **Not visible to anyone except the holder** — no external signs, no glowing auras, no visible markers, no detectable energy signature. Observers see only the RESULTS of adaptation, never the process itself.
- **Never write:** "The Talent told/decided/wanted/calculated/scanned/downloaded"
- **Never write:** "Others sensed his Adaptation Talent" or "They noticed something unusual about his power"

## 5.4 Core Chain

```
Adaptation Talent → Adaptive Nature → Adaptive Development → Current Adaptive State
```

Do not collapse these into one concept.

## 5.5 Six Adaptive Domains (track each separately)

| Domain | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| **PHYSICAL** | Strength, speed, durability, recovery, coordination, metabolism, env tolerance |
| **MENTAL** | Learning, memory, pattern recognition, creativity, emotional regulation, tactics |
| **SENSORY** | Vision, hearing, balance, energy perception, threat recognition, spatial awareness |
| **ENERGETIC** | Capacity, efficiency, control, recovery, compatibility, conversion, technique formation |
| **SPIRITUAL** | Will, soul integrity, spiritual resistance, identity stability, authority compatibility |
| **INSTINCTIVE** | Combat response, movement correction, danger response, social intuition, survival |

## 5.6 Adaptation Process (Five Stages)

1. **Observation** — gathers information about the challenge/stimulus
2. **Analysis** — determines what prevents improvement
3. **Adjustment** — body/mind/soul begins optimization
4. **Integration** — the adaptation becomes natural, no conscious effort needed
5. **Evolution** — accumulated adaptations produce deeper changes

## 5.7 Growth Laws

- **Difficulty:** greater challenges = greater adaptation potential
- **Exposure:** repeated exposure required; one encounter rarely creates major adaptation
- **Compatibility:** closer to user's foundation = easier adaptation
- **Stress:** meaningful pressure required; no challenge = no major adaptation
- **Failure-as-Data:** failure is information, not instant power gain
- **Resources:** energy, nutrition, rest, materials, time all matter

## 5.8 Limits

- Cannot create from nothing
- Cannot grant missing knowledge/energy/authority
- First overwhelming exposure can still kill
- Works through the world's real laws and resources
- Does not instantly master unfamiliar powers
- Age, development, training, energy, and cosmology still matter

## 5.9 Adaptive Orders (Ten Tiers)

| Order | Name | Scope |
|-------|------|-------|
| I | Foundational | Natural existence optimization |
| II | Integrated | Multi-layer harmonization |
| III | Generative | Constructs genuinely new functions |
| IV | Evolutionary | Permanent existential change |
| V | Transcendent | Beyond native ceiling |
| VI | Law | Fundamental operating principles |
| VII | Divine/Authority | Genuine divinity, domains, god positions |
| VIII | Conceptual | Objective concepts and meanings |
| IX | Causal | Time, fate, probability, cause/effect |
| X | Existential | Identity, existence, ontological transformation |

**Higher Order ≠ higher current combat output.** Track Order, maturity, capacity, authority, and current output separately.

## 5.10 How to Write Adaptation Talent

### Show as Embodied Perception:
- ✅ "His body shifted before his mind caught up, muscles adjusting angle by a fraction"
- ✅ "The pattern emerged through three breaths of observation — weight distribution, timing, the micro-hesitation"
- ✅ "Heat registered first, then direction, then the exact margin he could afford"

### Never Write as System/AI:
- ❌ "The Talent analyzed and provided the optimal solution"
- ❌ "A notification appeared in his mind"
- ❌ "The system downloaded the technique"
- ❌ "His body computed the answer"
- ❌ "The certainty arrived before the evidence"

### Portrayal Rules:
- Show through sharper noticing, faster reading, breath, glance, reflex, corrected movement
- The Talent is part of the user, not a separate entity reporting to them
- Adaptation cannot manufacture knowledge from nothing
- Competence is not omniscience — strong perception may notice, but must not resolve canon arcs ahead of schedule

# PART 6 — CHARACTER MANAGEMENT

## 6.1 Story Identity Card

Before drafting, establish or reconstruct:

- Story title and source universe
- Canon version or adaptation
- Canon starting date and event
- Current timeline branch
- Main character and OC role
- Central premise
- Tone and genre
- Point of view
- Chapter style and approximate length
- Romance configuration
- Power framework
- Current divergence from canon
- Latest accepted checkpoint
- Rejected-draft quarantine

## 6.2 Current-State Matrix

Before drafting, silently track every relevant character's:

- Exact location
- Physical condition and injuries
- Clothing and equipment
- Powers currently available
- Energy and fatigue
- Emotional state
- Immediate and long-term goals
- Fear and suspicion
- Loyalty and relationship status
- Resources and legal/institutional situation
- Knowledge, false beliefs, and secrets
- Current activity

### Rules:
- Characters cannot teleport emotionally, physically, or informationally
- Injuries do not disappear without healing
- Objects do not change location without movement
- Characters cannot use resources they do not possess
- Travel and preparation require realistic time

## 6.3 Character-Specific Dialogue

### Rules:
- Do not give every character the same polished banter, sarcasm, intelligence, or emotional vocabulary
- Match confidence, age, education, culture, insecurity, relationship, and current emotional state
- A shy or awkward character should not instantly become effortlessly witty merely because the scene needs romantic chemistry
- Humour should arise through personality, family familiarity, timing, misunderstanding, embarrassment, reactions, and situation — not constant one-line exchanges
- Avoid repetitive dialogue-reaction loops and speeches that explain what characters already know

### Dialogue 5-Check (after drafting):
1. Does each character sound distinct?
2. Does speech match their knowledge/state?
3. Does culture/era influence their language?
4. Are there repetitive patterns to cut?
5. Does silence/subtext work where words don't?

## 6.4 Named Living Ensemble

### Rules:
- Introduce every recurring or meaningful speaking character by name, relationship, role, and relevant position
- Do not repeatedly reduce established people to labels such as "her mother," "his father," "the younger warrior"
- Use relationship-based forms of address naturally: family titles, ranks, honorifics, nicknames
- Do not omit siblings, relatives, assistants, neighbours, rivals, servants, classmates, teammates who matter
- Track where every character is, what they know, what they want, and why they are present

### Provisional-Naming Protocol:
When a named character's canon name is unverified:
- Use a relation/role label in-prose
- Record the name as provisional in the ledger
- Never mint a name and present it as canon

## 6.5 Character Causality

Every character decision must grow from:
- Current knowledge (not author knowledge)
- Established personality and duties
- Current fears and desires
- Canon temperament
- Speech habits and forms of address
- Existing trust, suspicion, resentment, affection, dependency
- What evidence would be required to change each relationship

A character may surprise the audience, but the choice must still grow from personality, pressure, knowledge, or development. Plot convenience is not character development.

Show gentleness, dignity, restraint, courage, intelligence, professionalism, or respect through choices and behaviour. Do not repeatedly make a character deliver speeches describing their own virtues.

## 6.6 Competence Preservation

- Established powerful characters remain genuinely capable
- Do not nerf canon characters merely to make the OC impressive
- Separate raw output, precision, control, technique, experience, knowledge, stamina, compatibility, and mastery
- Competence is not omniscience — separate what the OC observes, suspects, can prove, and knows with certainty
- A job title, status, bloodline, intelligence, beauty, wealth, or power level does not grant automatic access to every institution, secret, person, or plotline

# PART 7 — KNOWLEDGE FIREWALL

## 7.1 Character Knowledge Boundaries

Every character may act only on information they could logically know. Track:

- What they personally witnessed
- What they were told and by whom
- Whether the source is trusted
- What they inferred
- What they misunderstand
- What they suspect but cannot prove
- What remains completely unknown

### Rules:
- No character may know a secret, identity, motive, location, power, relationship, future event, or unseen action without a valid information path
- Strong intuition is not factual knowledge
- Evidence must exist before an investigator reaches a conclusion
- Do not invent cross-character sensing, prophetic awareness, emotional decoding, bloodline recognition, memory transfer, or automatic supernatural connection for atmosphere
- Distinguish narrator knowledge, audience knowledge, villain knowledge, and each character's personal knowledge
- Keep misunderstandings alive when the available evidence logically supports them
- Attraction, recognition of danger, magical resonance, emotional familiarity do not automatically reveal a name, identity, allegiance, history, or destiny
- Do not let a character witness explicit proof and then ignore it irrationally merely to preserve the planned plot
- Do not reveal a mystery early simply because the writer knows the answer

## 7.2 Four Truth Layers

| Layer | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **L1** | Confirmed primary canon |
| **L2** | Confirmed AU divergence |
| **L3** | Character state and knowledge |
| **L4** | Protected future information |

**Never leak L4 into character knowledge or narration merely because the author knows it.**

## 7.3 Reveal Ledger

Every important secret requires a reveal ledger showing:
- Who knows it
- What evidence each person possesses
- What each person falsely believes
- The earliest point at which a valid reveal may occur

## 7.4 Foreshadowing Rules

- Cap foreshadow beats per hidden truth — one beat per hidden truth per chapter maximum
- Do not stack multiple tells for the same secret in one chapter
- Foreshadowing must not slide into self-recognition
- Instinct, uncanny feelings, folklore interest remain dismissible and ambiguous
- A hidden-identity character must not be drawn toward their own true identity before the evidence path exists
- A bystander's "inexplicable feeling" must stay inexplicable — do not imply they sense something specific

# PART 8 — POWER SYSTEMS

## 8.1 Power Integrity Rules

- Abilities must come from canon, the locked foundation, or earned development
- Do not invent new sensory functions, portals, hidden systems, ancient machinery, transformations, resistances, or energy interactions just to connect scenes
- Adaptation-type powers are processes, not speaking or choosing entities
- Adaptation cannot manufacture knowledge from nothing
- Every power has limits, costs, and prerequisites
- Power growth must be earned through challenge, training, or valid events
- Do not invent arbitrary weakness to manufacture tension
- Failure must arise from genuine complexity, insufficient foundation, unfamiliar mechanics, conflicting requirements, resource limits, or superior opposition — not narrator fiat

## 8.2 Power-State Ledger

Track for each character:
- Raw power level
- Control and precision
- Technique repertoire
- Mastery level
- Compatibility
- Stamina and energy
- Knowledge of own abilities
- Current limitations
- Current injuries affecting power
- Recent growth and development

## 8.3 Adaptation-Specific Story Firewall

When Adaptation Talent appears in a scene:
1. Identify the user's exact current state
2. Identify the actual stimulus
3. Identify available information
4. Identify legitimate pathways in that universe
5. Identify current Adaptation development
6. Determine what the user can realistically adapt toward now
7. Distinguish immediate adaptation from long-term development
8. Check interaction with existing abilities
9. Check that no adaptation harms the user
10. Check that the result is individual rather than a generic template
11. Record the resulting change in the state ledger
12. Apply downstream consequences

**Never write:** "Adaptation automatically gives the perfect counter."
**Prefer:** "Given this individual's current foundation, the Talent develops the most compatible response currently available to them."

# PART 9 — WORLD BUILDING & LOGISTICS

## 9.1 Location Lock

- Do not teleport characters between places
- Maintain realistic transit chains, geography, timing, and environmental continuity
- Map the base geography first — establish protagonist's base location and distance/travel time to every key location
- Pin the in-story present date before writing — establish the in-story "now" explicitly

## 9.2 Event-Sequence Lock

- Do not pre-trigger future canon events, reveals, meetings, discoveries, or emotional developments
- Respect episode, chapter, season, and arc boundaries
- Do not end an episode with material belonging to later episodes

## 9.3 Living World Rule

Organizations, civilians, institutions, enemies, allies, media, governments, and bystanders retain independent agency. Major events create believable reactions rather than existing only to praise the OC.

## 9.4 Tone, Culture, and Language Fidelity

- Preserve the social grammar of the source: hierarchy, intimacy, ritual, humour, embarrassment, public reputation, family pressure, professional boundaries, forms of address
- Do not rewrite every culture as contemporary Western therapy dialogue, police procedure, office banter, or individualist confrontation
- Professional scenes should be as procedural as the source and established setting require
- When switching languages, preserve character relationships, emotional rhythm, address terms, idioms, and cultural intent
- Use melodrama, reaction beats, symbolism, music-like transitions, or heightened dialogue only to the degree natural to the source
- Do not use cultural stereotypes or random catchphrases as a substitute for lived behaviour

# PART 10 — PLOT & STRUCTURE

## 10.1 Butterfly Effect Engine

Before every chapter, calculate the consequences of prior changes:

- Immediate physical consequences
- Emotional consequences
- Information gained or lost
- Relationship changes
- Institutional reactions
- Resource changes
- Future canon scenes affected
- Characters who arrive earlier or later
- Threats that become stronger or weaker
- Mysteries that remain hidden
- New opportunities and risks created

### Rules:
- Butterfly effects must emerge through cause and effect, not random replacement
- Do not preserve canon outcomes when their original causes have already been removed
- Do not change unrelated canon events without a causal chain
- Map and confirm a divergence BEFORE embedding it — trace consequences and get user's call first

## 10.2 Consequence Ledger

Every major divergence receives:
```
trigger → immediate consequence → secondary consequence → affected characters/factions → altered future possibilities
```

## 10.3 Full-Scene Architecture

A full scene needs:
1. A concrete place and moment
2. Characters who want something
3. Action, resistance, discovery, or emotional pressure
4. Character-specific dialogue or meaningful silence
5. A change by the end of the scene
6. A consequence that connects to another scene

### Rules:
- Major canon scenes need setting, character actions, dialogue, interruptions, reactions, and consequences
- Do not compress births, deaths, betrayals, family revelations, transformations, battles, investigations, or introductions into a few expository lines
- Avoid isolated two-person conversations when the original situation naturally contains a family, team, crowd, organisation
- Let information travel naturally through calls, witnesses, gossip, reports, family discussion, institutional response
- One event should affect several people differently and continue influencing later chapters

## 10.4 Chapter Architecture

A chapter should form a complete dramatic unit with:
- Opening condition
- Development
- Turn
- Consequence
- Purposeful ending

Do not substitute an episode recap, outline, montage, teaser, or collection of disconnected vignettes for a chapter.

Unless the user says otherwise, "Next" means the next complete chapter in the established format — not the next episode, a short preview, an out-of-order reveal, or a summary of future events.

## 10.5 Cliffhangers and Endpoints

- Determine the real canon endpoint before adapting the chapter
- Preserve the original cliffhanger when its causes remain intact
- If an active butterfly effect changes it, create a new cliffhanger from the new causal chain
- Do not append an unrelated magical noise, symbol, vision, threat, transformation, or secret conversation merely to make the ending feel dramatic
- Do not end halfway through an essential scene just to produce artificial suspense
- Do not include later-episode material merely because the current chapter feels short
- **Avoid the banned abstract register:** Do not end with "felt like the edge of something," "nothing would be the same," or any line that signals "everything was about to change" without showing the change
- Land on a concrete image, decision, or line of dialogue

## 10.6 Scene Purpose Lock

Every scene must serve at least 2 functions:
- Advance plot
- Develop character
- Reveal information
- Build world
- Create tension
- Resolve tension
- Set up future events

If a scene serves only 1 function, either add more purpose or cut it.

# PART 11 — COMBAT & ACTION WRITING

## 11.1 Before Writing ANY Fight Scene

1. List all combatants with their ranks and techniques
2. Establish the environment
3. Plan the fight progression (opening, middle, climax, ending)
4. Show technique usage clearly (name the technique, describe what it does)
5. Show Adaptation Talent in action (if applicable)
6. Show damage and reactions
7. Make it detailed — no "blur of motion" or "fight was over quickly"
8. Each exchange should be clear

## 11.2 Fight Scene Length

- **Minor fights** (against weak opponents): 250-400 words
- **Major fights** (against strong opponents): 400-1000 words
- **Epic fights** (story-defining): 1000-2000 words
- **Never** write "the fight was over quickly" — show the fight!

## 11.3 Combat Writing Rules

### Technique Usage (Name and Describe):
- ❌ BAD: "He used his technique"
- ✅ GOOD: "He channeled Dou Qi into his right palm, the fire attribute making it glow orange-red. 'Shattering Mountain Palm!' he called, thrusting his palm forward."

### Damage Description (Be Specific):
- ❌ BAD: "He hit him hard"
- ✅ GOOD: "The palm strike connected with his chest. Ribs cracked. Blood sprayed from his mouth. The force lifted him off his feet and sent him crashing into the stone wall."

### Adaptation Talent in Combat:
- ❌ BAD: "His Adaptation Talent helped him win"
- ✅ GOOD: "His opponent threw a right hook. The Talent analyzed the trajectory, the speed, the Dou Qi concentration. Before the punch was halfway there, he had already shifted left, his palm gathering fire Dou Qi."

# PART 14 — ERROR CORRECTION & RECOVERY

## 14.1 Failure, Correction, and Rollback Protocol

When a factual or continuity error is discovered:

1. Stop extending the affected draft
2. Identify the earliest incorrect assumption
3. Audit every later scene that depends on it
4. Distinguish local wording errors from structural errors
5. Mark structurally damaged prose as invalid
6. Return to the last valid checkpoint
7. Reconstruct the source and ledgers again
8. Rewrite the entire damaged dramatic unit when patching would preserve hidden contradictions
9. Update the project foundation only after the correction is accepted

### Rules:
- Never answer a serious correction with only a vague apology and then continue writing
- State the concrete errors, their effects, what material is invalid, and what must be rebuilt
- Do not overcorrect one mistaken version into a different invented version
- A correction is itself a factual claim and must meet the same evidence standard
- If a proposed correction is later disproved, retract it explicitly and restore the verified version

## 14.2 Dependency Invalidation

When an event, statement, clue, injury, report, invitation, or decision is false:

1. List every later beat that uses it as a cause, source of knowledge, permission, motive, or logistical bridge
2. Invalidate those dependent beats even when their prose is individually plausible
3. Do not preserve a conclusion by inventing a replacement clue after the fact
4. Rebuild from the last scene whose causes remain valid

**A false premise cannot be repaired by polishing its consequences.**

## 14.3 Handling Canon Disagreement

- If the user's correction matches stronger evidence, accept it and propagate it
- If it conflicts with the primary source or with a locked AU rule, explain the exact conflict respectfully
- Distinguish an intentional alternate version from an accidental canon error
- If reliable sources remain genuinely ambiguous, present the competing versions and ask
- Never pretend certainty when the source has not been checked

## 16.13 World-Building Context Failures (Prevented)

### Critical World-Building Elements to Research

**For sequels/continuations of existing series:**
- ❌ Missing time gaps between series (e.g., Soul Land 2 is 10,000 years after Soul Land 1)
- ❌ Missing prologue/epilogue scenes that establish cosmic context
- ❌ Not understanding where previous protagonists are now (e.g., Tang San is a god in Soul Land 2)
- ❌ Missing historical events that shaped the current world (e.g., continental collision 4,000 years ago)
- ❌ Not understanding technological/societal changes between eras
- ❌ Missing the "big picture" stakes (e.g., gods watching from above)

### Mandatory World-Building Research Protocol

**Before writing ANY story in an established universe:**

1. **Research the complete timeline:**
   - When does this story take place relative to previous stories?
   - What major events happened between stories?
   - How has the world changed?

2. **Research previous protagonists:**
   - Where are they now?
   - What role do they play in the current story?
   - Are they watching from above? Interfering? Testing the new protagonist?

3. **Research prologues and epilogues:**
   - Does the donghua/anime have a prologue showing cosmic context?
   - Does the novel start with a different perspective before introducing the MC?
   - Are there "behind the scenes" elements the MC doesn't know about?

4. **Research historical context:**
   - What wars, disasters, or technological revolutions happened?
   - How did these events shape current society?
   - What organizations/institutions rose or fell?

5. **Research the "big picture":**
   - Are there gods, immortals, or higher beings involved?
   - Is the protagonist being watched, tested, or manipulated?
   - What are the cosmic stakes beyond the immediate plot?

### Common Sequel/Continuation Pitfalls

**Soul Land 2 Specific:**
- ❌ Forgetting it's 10,000 years after Soul Land 1
- ❌ Not mentioning Tang San is now a god watching from Divine Realm
- ❌ Not explaining Tang Sect's decline (soul tools made hidden weapons obsolete)
- ❌ Not understanding Wang Dong's true identity (Tang San's daughter in disguise)
- ❌ Missing the "new star" prophecy Tang San sees

**General Sequel Pitfalls:**
- ❌ Treating sequel as completely separate story (ignoring connections)
- ❌ Not explaining what happened to previous heroes
- ❌ Not showing how world changed between stories
- ❌ Missing Easter eggs and callbacks to original series
- ❌ Not understanding why certain "coincidences" happen (divine intervention?)

### Documentation Requirements

**Every story foundation MUST include:**
- [ ] Complete timeline (when story takes place relative to other works)
- [ ] Status of previous protagonists (where are they now?)
- [ ] Major historical events that shaped current world
- [ ] Prologue/epilogue content (if applicable)
- [ ] "Big picture" context (gods, cosmic stakes, hidden manipulations)
- [ ] Technological/societal changes since previous stories
