## 5. The two refusals (the moral spine)

Both are load-bearing. Neither may be softened, retconned, or given a fourth option.

1. **Year 468** — the night-walker dies at his edge. He does not draw its returned years into
   the well. Reason available to him at the time: *a keeper keeps.*
2. **Year 746** — the old king puts itself into the valley soil at the end of a natural life,
   in a valley made of 391 harvested great cultivations. He does not take a single year.

**Kill count: zero, across all fifteen chapters and 775 years. Permanent.**

## 6. Ground-language vocabulary (fixed at eleven words)

*here · home · safe · danger · gone · cold · many · one · come · stay*

and the eleventh, invented by the found one: a single hard blow meaning
*I am here, I am answering, I am one of the ones who stays.*

Taps (night-walker) → pulses (him) → stamped feet (the found one, then the fringe young,
then the hundred at the door). The drum outlives everyone who invented it.
