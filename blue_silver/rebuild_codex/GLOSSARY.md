both are correct: the language grows.

## Spirit Hall vocabulary

The bureaucratic language is deliberate and load-bearing. It is how he survives.

| Term | Meaning | First use |
|---|---|---|
| grid nineteen | the glade's Hall designation | `[on page]` Ch2:216 |
| surveyed-and-unresolved | the surveyor's marking, year ~193; holds 147 years | `[on page]` Ch5:138 |
| sealed-pending | ~400 years old, never justified | `[on page]` Ch10 |
| exhausted habitat | the reclassification at year 672 that gets the refugees hunted out | `[on page]` Ch12 |
| the bend | the westward shift of the rot; Pell refuses it | `[on page]` Ch8 |
| consolidated seal | Berrit Ohn's permanent filing, in her own name, ≈2590 DC | `[on page]` Ch15 |

**The serial's central irony, stated plainly:** he is protected by paperwork. Not by power,
concealment, or guardians. Six people who never met him each did a small correct thing with
a document.
