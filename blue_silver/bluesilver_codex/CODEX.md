## 1. The story in one breath
In the deep era of Star Dou Forest, ~800 years before the world hears the name of a boy with
twin souls, a single blade of Blue Silver Grass — the commonest, weakest plant in the world,
a plant that by every law should never wake — opens its eyes. It has no right to be what it
is. And it carries a talent that rewrites every part of its own existence — body, senses,
mind, energy, and bloodline — so that "impossible" is simply a word the world has not yet
learned to apply to him.

## 2. The premise and why it is impossible
- Ordinary Blue Silver Grass has no soul power and no mind. **[canon]** A grass becoming a
  soul beast is vanishingly rare; a grass that *wakes* is unheard of. **[canon + premise]**
- The blade's waking is the serial's single miracle, and it is caused: the adaptation talent
  was at work on him from before his first root. Nothing about him is luck; everything is
  the talent's doing, priced in his own substance.
- Plant-type soul beasts cultivate slowly and cannot protect themselves until ~1,000 years
  of cultivation. **[canon]** He crosses that line absurdly early because the talent is
  exactly the kind of force canon itself recognizes: mutated and gifted beings outrun their
  years all over the books (see ADAPTATION_TALENT.md). His class is an age-grade, never a cap.

## 4. Where he stands now (end of Chapter 13)
He is the **Blue Silver King of the Sunweave line** — the grass's first wild King in an
age — and he is no longer only at the seam. The road here: the old one passed (~375); the
found one answered him in ground-language (*I am here*, Chapter 10) and passed old at the
door (~590s, Chapter 11), leaving the door a people; he kept even the liar, caging only
the act; and at the crest (~690s, Chapter 12) the King gate broke open — the King body,
the first words and first sight — the serpent of the band below was caged, spoken to, and
released, the deep band withdrew its testing, and the Empress read him for the first
time (the two-way wire). Then, in the settled King years (Chapter 13), the deep-country
pull resolved into a direction — the place the old one turned its head toward every dawn —
and he earned the rooted grass's first true travel: drawn and re-rooted, season by
season, always a rooted thing (the adaptation's greatest visible miracle, [design on
canon base]; the canon King stays rooted). He left the keeping to the door-people, gave
his word to return, and the long going carried him to the edge of the **Blue Silver
Valley — the Emperor Valley** — the line's ancient heart and the deep-country myth made
real, where the wire to the Empress runs at full strength (her body deeper toward the
core; the meeting is the near next beat, still unstaged). (Detail: BLADE_STATUS.md.)

## 9. The power law, one page
- **Cultivation class is an age-grade, never a cap** — established by the user and carried
  through ADAPTATION_TALENT.md.
- Canon's gifted beasts sit above their years: Three-Eyed Golden Lion (~15k years ≈ 100k
  power), Evil Tree Monster devourers, Dark Blue Silver Grass, Thunder Hell Prison Vine,
  devourer whale kings, royal bloodlines. **[canon]** The blade is such a being by the
  talent. His class exists so the world can marvel at how young he is for how strong he is.
- Ring colors (canon): white 10+, yellow 100+, purple 1k+, black 10k+, red 100k+,
  orange 200k+, gold 1M+. A **black ring** would form on him if he were cut down now — it
  measures his accumulated cultivation (ten-thousand-year class), not his power.
- His honest rungs (now, with numbers): ≈10,850 carried years at ~775s actual (~14×) — the
  ten-thousand-year class, the Blue Silver King of the Sunweave line, and his POWER still
  runs above his age-grade even here. Beasts of his own class no longer outrank him by the
  ledger alone (the serpent of the band below was held at his becoming — by ground and
  keeping, never by killing — and released); the deep country's true ancients (tens of
  thousands of years, climbing toward the Empress's grade) remain above him, and fire,
  flood, drought, and greater teeth can still end one rooted thing. The King gate is
  crossed; the ladder is not gone. Never a cap.

## 11. Standing laws that can never be broken
1. English only, everywhere. No Chinese characters in any file.
2. Canon first, receipts honest: **[canon]** / **[design]** tags tell the truth; original
   deep beings are never passed off as canon creatures; no canon character or event staged
   (guard rails in WORLD.md).
3. No system voice, no free energy, no immunity. Gains permanent and compounding. Bloodline
   is the talent's deepest work.
4. No invented mechanics as world rules. When he cannot do something, the reason is real.
5. Adaptation never self-lobotomizes: grief and loss are not adapted away; he is never
   benched or powerless; he lives the ordinary life of his kind, warm not edgy.
6. No speech or human reasoning until earned by the King evolution (the mantis never spoke;
   the old one speaks only in taps; narrator-zoom interiors are wordless renderings).
7. His class never caps him in prose; a century-old soul beast is not a joke; he is
   stronger than his level, and the prose may never undercut that.

## 13. Emotional architecture (the arithmetic)
The serial's key is set early and never reversed: *everyone he loves will end, and he will
not, and that is the price of being the grass that lives.* The mantis taught it to him
directly; the sanctuary teaches it again and again; the old one holds it over him now. The
adaptation — which rewrites body, memory, and bloodline — cannot rewrite this. That limit is
deliberate: it keeps the talent from reading as omnipotent, and it makes the wound A Yin
must one day know to truly reach him. (Detail: RELATIONSHIPS.md.)
