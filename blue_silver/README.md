# BLUE SILVER — a Soul Land pre-canon serial (working title, renameable)

A new fan fiction set in the world of **Soul Land (Douluo Dalu)**, hundreds of years before
the original story begins. New characters live naturally inside the canon world; no canon
event is bent or erased.

## The premise (one paragraph)
In the deep era before the story of Tang San — when the continent is quieter and the great
forests still belong to the soul beasts — an impossible thing happens in **Star Dou Forest**:
a single blade of **Blue Silver Grass**, the most common weed in the world, a plant that by
every law of the world should never become a soul beast at all, opens its eyes. It has no
right to be what it is. And it is one: a real elemental mutant — the **Sunweave** (core
LIFE/vitality · developed wood · light + water through photosynthesis · partial earth) —
and it has a talent that effects EVERY part of its existence — body, senses, mind, energy,
and the very deepening of that mutation — so that "impossible" may simply be a word the
world hasn't learned to apply to him yet.

## How we work (rules carried from the failed project, made into law here)
1. **Canon first.** Every staged scene is checked against the source before it is written.
   When canon is unknown, we say so — we never invent silently and call it verified.
2. **The OC lives inside the world.** Ordinary life (place, neighbors, hunger, seasons) is
   story, not an inconvenience. No invented rules exist to bench or elevate the OC.
3. **Restraint comes from character and logic**, never from fake mechanics.
4. **No lines out of a canon character's mouth** that they could not say.
5. **Verifiable depth in every character.** Everything derived is caused on-page.
6. **English only** — the user reads no Chinese characters.
