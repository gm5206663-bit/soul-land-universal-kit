# WORLD — era, timeline, and canon rails
Status: living. Every claim carries a tag: **[canon]** (verified against source),
**[fan]** (commonly repeated, verify before relying on it), **[assumption]** (our guess),
**[AU]** (our invention, clearly ours).

## The era: hundreds of years before Soul Land (SL1)
- We stand in the **deep pre-SL1 era** of the Douluo continent — before almost every famous
  faction of SL1's time is relevant to the story. The land has two great empires and its soul
  masters, but the famous houses, academies, and Halls of SL1's day are not yet the stage of
  our tale. **[assumption — low collision by design]**
- Our story is told almost entirely inside **Star Dou Forest**: the largest and oldest
  soul-beast forest on the continent, home to soul beasts from ten-year weeds to the
  hundred-thousand-year monarchs. **[canon]**

## Canon facts this story rests on
- **Blue Silver Grass** is the most common plant on the continent — a small blue-green weed
  with no soul power, considered worthless. **[canon]**
- A soul beast's **"cultivation age" (xiuwei) is not the same as its actual years alive** —
  the terms differ in canon, and we use them carefully. **[canon]**
- Soul-beast power tiers run by cultivation age: ten-year, hundred-year, thousand-year,
  ten-thousand-year, hundred-thousand-year (the "hundred-thousand-year gate" is where a beast
  may choose to take human form). **[canon]**
- Plants live long and cultivate slowly; a plant that is a *soul beast* at all is rare, and a
  **Blue Silver Grass that is a soul beast is effectively unheard of** — that is our premise,
  and it is supposed to feel impossible. **[canon + AU premise]**
- **A Yin** is the hundred-thousand-year **Blue Silver Empress** — queen of the Blue Silver
  Grass line, the greatest of her kind. She loves every blade of Blue Silver Grass. In our era
  she is alive, rooted in Star Dou Forest, long before she takes human form. **[canon]**
- **No mechanical "one Emperor at a time" law.** Canon shows multiple Blue Silver bearers
  existing (A Yin revived while Tang San also carried the Blue Silver Emperor martial soul),
  so the line is not ruled by a single-being law. King / Emperor are titles of reverence and
  authority, not legal positions. **[canon — user-corrected]**
- In SL1 proper there is a **Blue Silver King** — an ~85,000-year elder who leads the Blue
  Silver Grass after the Empress leaves the forest, and who later gives himself to awaken
  Tang San's royal bloodline. **[canon — guard rail below]**
- The Empress's **Blue Silver Domain** lets her feel and move through Blue Silver Grass over a
  wide area — she is connected to her blades. **[canon — basis for how she senses the OC]**
- A Yin does not take human form until the generation of Tang Hao (roughly twenty to thirty
  years before SL1 begins). Until that choice, she remains a rooted Empress in the forest.
  **[canon — approximate, per Baike/A Yin synopses]**

## Anchor dates (verified against SL1 canon, Douluo Calendar)
- **2631 DC** — Tang San is born. **[canon]**
- **2637 DC** — Tang San, six, awakens his twin martial souls (Blue Silver Grass that is
  secretly the Blue Silver Emperor, and the Clear Sky Hammer). The original story begins.
  **[canon]**
- **A Yin's human-form era** — she is a 100,000-year Blue Silver Empress who takes human
  form (hua xing / chong xiu — human-form rebirth) only in Tang Hao's generation. Canon-corrected order: she transforms FIRST,
  then enters the human world (a transformed 100,000-year beast must live among humans to
  re-cultivate) and meets Tang Hao and Tang Xiao by chance. She reveals her nature as the
  Empress to Tang Hao (~2624, Tang Hao age 38; she was roughly level 59), and her sacrifice
  follows around Tang San's infancy (~2631). Her rooted forest life therefore ends **at her
  transformation, some years before ~2621** — the exact year is not stated in canon (earlier
  notes saying "rooted until ~2631" are revised). **[canon — corrected]** Everything before
  her transformation era has centuries of free room for our tale.

## Guard rails (canon collisions we must never cause)

- **Our OC becomes a COMPLETE King at the gate — and only the verified grade is canon.** The
  canon King's grade (novel ch.134): intelligence, human language, telepathy, true speech,
  bloodline sensing; the awakening/ring-condensation role belongs to the SL1-era elder King and
  is never staged in our era (it simply sleeps). The wider ten-power kit (domain sub-anchor,
  sensory synapse, vitality funneling, flora isolation, hive-mind, etc.) has NO canon source and
  is kept only as labelled [design] territory (BEAST_LAW §10 ledger) — never asserted as canon.
  The GOING (Ch13) is likewise [design on canon base]: the canon King is rooted and immobile;
  his earned travel is never claimed as canon grass behavior.
  Two-way root with A Yin: her domain reads him as a King; his verified bloodline sensing
  recognises her line in return.

1. **The elder Blue Silver King is already here.** The King who later awakens Tang San
   (novel ch. 134; donghua ep. 111) is an 85,000+ year, male, rooted Blue Silver Grass who
   served the Empress, and he is alive in our deep era. Arithmetic forbids our OC from being
   him (our OC is born ~800 years pre-SL1; that elder's cultivation already spans
   tens of thousands of years). **Decision locked (foundation gate): let it go naturally** —
   in the deep era the elder is an ancient presence of a far region, a legend our OC may
   hear of; no forced meeting, no turf war, any face-to-face contact left for the story to
   earn on its own or never. Wherever the story goes, it must never stage the elder's SL1
   role (his self-gift to awaken Tang San's royal bloodline). See OPEN.md and THE_BLADE.md.
2. **A Yin's fate is not ours to touch.** Her human-form choice, Tang Hao, Tang Xiao, and the
   sacrifice are canon events of ~2621–2631. We may feel her departure as resonance at most;
   we never stage or reroute her canon story, and our deep-era tale should naturally reach
   its own end before her transformation era unless the user decides otherwise.
3. **No Spirit Hall / empire politics invented as fact.** The human world stays far away and
   lightly drawn until it earns a scene; anything staged must be verified first.

## Extra verified notes that shape the story
- Plant-type soul beasts cultivate slowly and are slow to gain intelligence; grass in
  particular is the world's commonest, weakest plant. **[canon]** Our OC is doubly impossible.
- Even the 85,000-year Blue Silver King is **rooted and immobile**; he speaks telepathically
  and with true speech, knows human language, shows joy and tears, wears a human face formed
  in his vines. **[canon]** So rootedness does not mean silence or mindlessness at high tier —
  it means the world comes to you, or you reach it by power your bloodline has earned. There
  is no grass "network" for ordinary grass (it carries nothing to sense); the Emperor's
  domain reading the grass is hers alone.
- **Our OC reached across that rootedness at the King (Chapter 13)** — the GOING, a whole
  rooted body drawn and re-rooted season by season. This is the serial's design, not canon:
  the canon King stays rooted and immobile, and our King's travel is never claimed as canon
  grass behavior (BEAST_LAW §10 ledger; ADAPTATION_TALENT M4). It is the adaptation's
  greatest visible miracle, priced in his own substance — each release of old earth answered
  by a new rooted reach ahead.
- The Blue Silver Emperor's domain **resonates with surrounding Blue Silver Grass**: sensing,
  control, and (at the ultimate level) drawing on all grass's life force. **[canon]** The user's
  premise — the Empress can sense her impossible King through the grass — is a natural
  extension of this, and powers their meeting. **[user design on canon base]**
- King and Emperor are **cultivation-evolution tiers** and titles of reverence, not single
  office slots; canon shows coexisting royal-line bearers. No one-at-a-time law. **[canon +
  user correction]**
- **King-evolution threshold locked for the serial (user, 2026-09): the ten-thousand-year
  class → Blue Silver King** (Soul Land wiki reading). Our OC's King evolution is that gate;
  the SL1-era elder King (85,000+) is a separate off-stage being who kept cultivating long
  past it. The Emperor apex (~100,000 + royal blood, A Yin's grade) is beyond this serial's
  end-horizon. [ADAPTATION_TALENT.md]
