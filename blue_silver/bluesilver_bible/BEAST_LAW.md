# BEAST LAW — how soul beasts live, grow, and gain power (canon dossier)
Comprehensive reference for writing the beast life accurately. Compiled 2026-09 from
Baidu Baike ("Soul Beast", "Blue Silver Grass", "Tang Hao", "Ah Yin"), the Soul Land wiki
("Soul Beast", "Blue Silver Grass", "Spirit Beasts", "Soul Rings", "Soul Skills", "Immortal
Herbs", "Tree Monster", "Blue Silver King", "Tang San/Plot"), and web compendia.
Tags: **[canon]** (consistent across sources / from source material) · **[fan]**
(commonly repeated; verify before it carries prose) · **[design]** (our choice).
Rules that only matter in later books (SL2+) are marked; the deep-era tale is SL1-era rules.

## 1. What a soul beast is
- A soul beast is any animal **or plant** on the Douluo continent that possesses soul power
  (the beast version of a soul master's power). **[canon]**
- Soul beasts were the planet's first inhabitants; they carry soul power, spiritual power,
  and bloodline power, like soul masters do. **[canon]**
- They cultivate extremely slowly. That slowness is canon and central to the story. **[canon]**
- Powerful soul beasts are the "more powerful animals or plants" of the plane; in the great
  forests, spirit-essence density is what lets beasts grow. Star Dou Forest is the largest
  and oldest such place on the continent. **[canon]**

## 2. Age: cultivation age vs. actual age
- **Cultivation age (xiuwei) is not actual lived age.** Cultivation can be raised by means
  other than time (dense spirit-essence places, treasures, devouring, lucky environs).
  A beast's ring is graded by **cultivation**, and the ring "does not represent its actual
  age." **[canon]** E.g., immortal herbs in the Ice-Fire Yin Yang Well grow ~10x fast; the
  Three-Eyed Golden Lion accelerates neighbors; eating a thousand-year Purple Zoysia adds
  1,000 years to a beast's cultivation. **[canon, later books]**
- Appearance: for most beasts, higher cultivation shows in the body (size at the
  thousand-year level, etc.); beasts of pure, powerful bloodline change less as they evolve
  because their bodies are already near-perfect. **[canon]**
- Growth tiers (by cultivation): **ten-year** · **hundred-year** · **thousand-year** ·
  **ten-thousand-year** · **hundred-thousand-year** (and beyond: 200,000+ "Great Beasts";
  million-year is later-books lore). **[canon]**

## 3. Bottlenecks — a beast's road is not smooth
- Soul beasts do not simply grow with years: **every hundred years of cultivation is a
  barrier**, and the barrier repeats at each thousand, ten-thousand, and hundred-thousand
  step, with difficulty escalating each order. That is why ancient beasts do not all end up
  at the hundred-thousand level. **[canon]**
- So a grass like our OC, if it were ordinary, would stall again and again across the
  centuries — good: the adaptation's work is precisely to keep his road open when the world's
  bottlenecks would close it. **[design on canon base]**

## 4. How a soul beast grows stronger
- Beasts grow by absorbing **the essence of heaven and earth** (and, for rooted plants, the
  nutrients of the soil and water) over long time. **[canon]** Plants in particular "absorb
  the essence of heaven and earth and the nutrients in the soil to continue to grow."
  **[canon — Tree Monster page]**
- Some beasts speed their growth by **devouring** other soul beasts or treasures (the ocean
  whale kings reached near-million by devouring). **[canon, later-books context]** Devouring
  is an available, morally heavy road in the beast world — a theme to keep in the drawer,
  not to lean on early. **[design]**
- Time in the right place is the humble road. Our blade's whole first age is this road:
  sun, water, soil, stillness, and centuries. **[design]**

## 5. Intelligence milestones (deeply relevant to voice)
- Young soul beasts are far less intelligent than humans and mature slowly. Only around
  **100,000 years of cultivation** do they reach human-level intelligence and the ability to
  speak. A 10,000-year beast may have the intellect of a small child. **[canon]**
- **Exceptions:** soul beasts of "noble or royal" unique bloodlines (or those helped by
  heaven-and-earth treasures) can speak and think like humans far earlier. **[canon]**
- The Blue Silver royalty is exactly such an exceptional bloodline: the SL1 Blue Silver King
  (85,000+) has full intelligence, knows human language, speaks telepathically and aloud,
  and even cries with joy. **[canon]**
- For us this means: ordinary creatures of his early life (the mantis included) cannot speak
  words; their minds are smaller and warmer. And our OC is NOT of the royal bloodline — he
  is a COMMON-line grass that woke, an impossibility canon offers no door for (royalty is a
  separate bloodline). His waking and early mind are the PREMISE itself, carried by the
  talent. He does NOT speak early and never will: his words arrive only at the King gate,
  where full intelligence, telepathy, and true speech are the **canon grade of every King**
  (see §10 — the King authority set), not an exception and not royal inheritance. The
  talent's part is carrying him to that gate. [premise + user-supplied King canon]

## 6. Skills and abilities — what a beast can do
- A soul beast carries an **innate ability** born with it, plus abilities that develop with
  cultivation. What humans call "soul skills" are literally "derived from the abilities of
  soul beasts" — a ring grants the killer the beast's abilities as skills. **[canon]**
- A soul ring grants one skill per ring normally; a **100,000-year ring grants two**;
  million-year rings more (later-books rule). **[canon]** This is the "how do beasts get
  skills" answer: **they are born with and grow innate abilities; they do not "equip"
  skills the way soul masters do.** High-tier beasts may wield domain-like abilities of
  their own (e.g., the Titan Giant Ape's Gravity Control Domain). **[canon]**
- Plant beasts do fight: tree monsters at ~1,000 years produce real attacks; the Monster
  Eyed Tree grows eyes after 10,000 years, each eye adding strength. **[canon]**
- For our grass the early truth is humbler: for his first centuries he has no "skill" at
  all by beast standards — he has root-memory, scar-logic, and the ground-listening (reading
  soil, water, and the footfall-tremors of everything above him through his roots), which
  are adaptation-grown *ways of being*, not combat skills. Any ability he later gains
  (binding reach, blade-hardening, a true innate skill at the class gates) must be presented
  as his bloodline's innate abilities maturing through the adaptation. A grass "network"
  sense is NOT among them — ordinary grass carries nothing to sense, and gathering the life
  and information of surrounding Blue Silver Grass is an Emperor-domain ability (A Yin),
  far future if ever earned. **[canon + design on canon base]**

## 7. What hunters take (the danger behind the story)
- Killing a soul beast yields a **soul ring**: the beast's accumulated cultivation plus its
  innate ability. Ring grade = beast's cultivation. Ring colors follow cultivation classes:
  **white = ten-year class (10+ yrs), yellow = hundred-year (100+), purple = thousand-year
  (1,000+), black = ten-thousand-year (10,000+), red = hundred-thousand-year (100,000+)** —
  so a hundred-year-class grass would yield a **yellow** ring, not white. (Orange 200,000+
  and gold million-year are later-books; God-bestowed and attribute-colored rings are
  later-books too.) **[canon]**
- A **soul bone** may drop on death (chance below 100,000 years; **guaranteed at
  100,000+**). A plant beast has no bones by body-shape; canon plants still yield their
  equivalents (e.g., A Yin left her right-leg soul bone), so don't overthink this for us —
  it only matters if a hunter ever kills a royal grass, a far-future catastrophe arc.
  **[canon]**
- A beast at 100,000+ can also **sacrifice** itself: willingly give ring + bone to one it
  acknowledges, expending all cultivation (A Yin's choice). **[canon]** Relevant only as the
  distant, painful shape of the Empress's fate — which we never stage.
- Souls of 10,000+ beasts can deliver **soul shock** on absorption attempts — the beast's
  will can hurt or kill its killer. **[canon]** A quiet dignity: even in death the old ones
  are not prey without teeth.

## 8. The hundred-thousand-year decision and tribulation
- At ~100,000 years a soul beast faces the great fork (this is the "mature period"): take
  **human form** (hua xing / chong xiu — human-form rebirth) and re-cultivate as a person, or **stay a beast** and face the
  beast road. On the beast road, SL1-era law gives a beast only ~1,000 more years of life at
  the 100,000 mark unless it breaks through into the "ferocious beast" tier; every 100,000
  years after that carries a **heavenly tribulation** that kills most. (Later books let
  beasts live on past 100,000 by surviving tribulation every 100,000; 200,000+ are called
  Great Beasts.) Once a beast has lived past the 100,000-year choice as a beast, it can no
  longer choose human-form rebirth — only extraordinary means reopen it (e.g., Snow
  Empress sealed her origin with a 100,000-year immortal herb and reborn at 700,000).
  **[canon — Soul Land wiki Soul Beast + Soul Rings: Transformation/Million-Year; Baike]**
  This is why A Yin chose human rebirth, and why 100,000-year beasts are so few.
- Beast→human mechanics (Baike): cannot revert; original body becomes the "martial soul";
  must re-cultivate soul power from scratch; rings of the first seven stages form on their
  own; cultivation is faster than a human's; **they must live among humans** to advance;
  below soul power ~60 ("juvenile stage") high soul masters can see through them; past ~60
  ("mature stage") even Titled Douluo cannot. **[canon]**
- Our OC's road is the **beast road** (user-locked). Canon keeps that road open with power
  and longevity — and charges tribulations at the great thresholds. The adaptation is
  exactly the kind of anomaly that might change how a beast meets those thresholds; how it
  plays out is story, not setting. **[design]**

## 9. Plant-type soul beasts (his kind)
- Plant beasts **grow slower and are more fragile when young** than animal beasts: animals
  have fighting strength from ten years, plants usually cannot protect themselves until
  ~1,000 years of cultivation. **[canon — Tree Monster page]**
- Plants also **develop intelligence slowly** — the "cognitive development notoriously slow"
  fact Tang San's world knows. **[canon]**
- Plant vs animal difference canon singles out: **vitality/life force**. Plant soul beasts
  hold life force beyond any animal's — which is why A Yin, a plant, could leave a seed and
  regrow after sacrifice, while beast sacrifices end in death. **[canon — SL1 ch.266
  translation]**
- **Mobility is species-dependent.** The Blue Silver King is rooted and immobile even at
  85,000 years. **[canon]** But tree monsters grow feet at ~3,000 years and can move.
  **[canon]** Conclusion: rootedness is the grass's nature, not a universal plant law; any
  future mobility for our blade must be earned as something *unusual for a grass* — a
  bloodline evolution only the adaptation could buy. **[canon base + design]**
  **RESOLVED IN-STORY (Chapter 13):** the GOING — the whole rooted body drawn and
  re-rooted, season by season, always a rooted thing — landed as that earned exception,
  the adaptation's greatest visible miracle. It is [design on canon base], never claimed as
  canon grass behavior (the canon King stays rooted; BEAST_LAW §10 B. NOT VERIFIED).
- Plants with enough nourishment/wisdom become plant-type soul beasts proper; true "immortal
  herbs" must also cross a tribulation/lightning calamity on the way. **[canon — Immortal
  Herbs page]** Blue Silver Grass is canonically counted among plant-type soul beasts
  (though not immortal herbs) — meaning **a Blue Silver Grass that is a soul beast is a real
  thing in the world, just vanishingly rare and slow to rise.** Good: the OC's miracle is
  not "a grass soul beast exists," it is "one woke up awake, in a century, and is still
  climbing." **[canon + design]**
- Plant soul beasts may grow from common plants over time; some reproduce as their kind does
  (seeds/roots). Canon's royal grass line continues through seeds (A Yin's regrowth) and
  bloodline inheritance. **[canon]**

## 10. Blue Silver Grass — the species file
- The **most common plant in the world** and the most resilient: it grows everywhere, is
  famously hard to kill, and was even used to terraform other planets (SL3 lore). It is the
  plant-type with the most prosperous **life energy** despite looking like a weak weed.
  **[canon]**
- Ordinary Blue Silver Grass has no soul power and no mind — a plain plant, "trash" in human
  eyes. Its royal members are the opposite. **[canon]**
- **Evolution path (beast side):** Blue Silver Grass that cultivates for long ages can
  evolve toward **Blue Silver King**, then **Blue Silver Emperor** at the apex. Thresholds:
  - **Soul Land wiki: 10,000 years → Blue Silver King; 100,000 → Blue Silver Emperor.**
  - Baidu Baike (Blue Silver Grass entry): 70,000+ years → Blue Silver King; 100,000+ →
    Blue Silver Emperor.
  Both agree the Emperor step sits at the 100,000-year gate. **RESOLVED FOR THE SERIAL
  (user lock, 2026-09): the ten-thousand-year class is our OC's King-evolution threshold**
  (the wiki reading), because an 800-year actual life cannot reach a 70,000+ class, and the
  adaptation talent is exactly the engine that lets him cross 10,000 early. The Baike figure
  and the 85,000+ SL1 King (a King-grade grass that kept cultivating) remain on record as the
  sterner/continued readings. **[user decision over the disputed sources]**
- **Known members (canon):**
  - **A Yin** — the Blue Silver Empress, ~100,000-year grade; "queen" of the grass line;
    loved all Blue Silver Grass; reached her human-form decision in the SL1 generation; left
    a seed; later revived and rejoined the royal line alongside her son's inherited blood.
    **[canon]** Distinguishing mark from birth: a **golden tint** in her blades (she was born
    "different" — the grass is not always born equal). **[A Yin plot reading, useful]**
  - **The Blue Silver King (SL1)** — 85,000+ years, male, rooted, vine-body with a human
    face; served the Empress; in SL1's own time he performs Tang San's second awakening and
    gifts the 25,000+ year ring; subordinate reverence to the royal blood. **[canon]** He is
    a separate being from our OC: the OC's King evolution (at the 10,000-year class) happens
    centuries before SL1 and coexists with the elder off-stage — no one-at-a-time rule.
  - **An unnamed Blue Silver Emperor of SL2** — 190,000+ years, near his next tribulation;
    chose to become a spirit soul for a soul master. **[canon, later book]** Shows the grass
    line does not end with A Yin and that King/Emperor are **cultivation-evolution tiers +
    reverential titles**, not single office slots. **[canon — supports user's correction]**

## 11. Star Dou context for our era
- Star Dou is a layered forest: outer bands hold the young (ten- and hundred-year) beasts
  that soul masters farm; the deep interior holds the old; the core is for the hundred-
  thousand monarchs. **[canon]** Our glade sits in the middle depth — inside the hunting
  pressure of outer edges, beneath the true power of the core.
- Plant beasts in the deep era include tree monsters/monster-eyed trees, lone bamboo,
  demon-eye trees, thunder vines, and rare grass royalty — plant life is a real society of
  the forest, not scenery. **[canon-flavored; specifics as needed per scene]**

## 12. Rails and freedoms this dossier gives the OC
**Canon constraints (write inside these):**
1. Ordinary grass does not wake up. His waking is the impossible event the whole serial
   hangs on. Canon offers NO door for a common grass to wake (royal blood is a separate,
   inherited line; he has none) — so his waking and early mind are the PREMISE, carried
   wholly by the talent. He is never "made royal"; the talent refines his own mutated
   common line.
2. Plant cultivation is slow; plant beasts are fragile until ~1,000 years. His early ages
   are genuinely dangerous, no matter what he is.
3. Beasts his size and age cannot speak or reason as he does — his solitude is real.
4. Bottlenecks close on the beast road every order of magnitude; nothing he gains is free of
   the world's walls.
5. Roots hold him. Even the mightiest grass in canon is rooted. Movement, if ever, is an
   evolution beyond his species — the adaptation's greatest visible miracle.
**Canon freedoms (story can use without inventing):**
1. Royal-type grass can be born different (A Yin's gold) and can think and speak young — but
   he is NOT royal and never becomes so. The talent deepens his own mutated line over ages
   (design); his speech arrives only at the King gate, where it is the KING'S CANON GRADE
   (full intelligence and true speech — every King's), never an early royal-style exception.
2. Grass life force is supreme; regrowth from root/seed is a canon plant miracle — his
   resilience themes are grounded.
3. The Emperor-domain resonance (canon, A Yin's domain) is the meeting wire to the
   Empress — her domain reads grass, and he will one day be grass that is no longer
   ordinary. His own sensing is root-and-ground based through all his pre-King centuries (no
   ordinary-grass network). At the King gate he gains the King's local dominion
   canonically — see §10 above (sub-anchor to the Emperor's domain).
4. Devouring, treasures, spirit-dense places exist as accelerants — but slow is his truth.
5. Beast-road endings (tribulation, sacrifice, spirit-soul contract, human-form) are all
   legitimate, story-decided fates — none decreed now.
