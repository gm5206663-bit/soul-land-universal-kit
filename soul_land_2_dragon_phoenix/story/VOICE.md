# Voice, scenes and terminology

## Narrative approach

Use natural close-third with deliberate transitions. Chapter 1’s current plan
stays with Yuhao. A fact shown to the reader in his viewpoint does not become
Jingheng’s knowledge. Background enters when an object, decision or exchange
makes it relevant; it is not an opening recital of relatives and cultivation.

Jingheng asks concrete questions, enjoys useful results and has room for ordinary
humor or impatience. He does not speak like a doctrine manual, rank other people
as protagonists or predict events he has never learned about. Adults need not
all be solemn; peers need not all be awed.

## Scene construction

A scene should change a decision, understanding, relationship, tactical situation
or necessary location. Give each participant something of their own to want.
Objects being handled, an interrupted explanation or a familiar correction can
carry history without a lecture. Use texture when it serves the scene, not as
an endlessly repeated food/teasing/training formula.

Canon remains present in original prose and in meaningful order. Compress
routine and expand consequential changes. A fic chapter may use several source
parts or only part of one; it is neither an exhaustive translation substitute
nor a checklist of copied paragraphs.

## Mechanical targets

| Measure | Working target |
|---|---|
| Chapter length | Approximately 2,400–3,400 words; scene needs govern final length |
| Sentence average | Roughly 14–18 words; root ceiling remains twenty-five |
| Longest sentence | No sentence over sixty words |
| Repeated construction | Avoid habitual “the way”; root cap is two per chapter |
| Prose presentation | No system window, mechanical skill list or repeated full stat panel |

These are craft targets, not newly invented author quotations. A technical pass
cannot establish naturalness, originality or characterization. Read the actual
prose before publication; do not run an automatic sentence rewrite as approval.
