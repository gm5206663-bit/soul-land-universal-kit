# Jingheng with Mu En and Xuan Zi

## Mu En: close grandmaster

Mu En is an established close figure, not a distant title the child first meets
in Chapter 1. Jingheng is comfortable sitting nearby, returning to an unfinished
question or admitting that a solution failed. Respect and familiarity coexist.
Mu En notices when a technically neat answer avoids the boy’s real concern.

His instruction gives the gifted child room to explain and demonstrate, then
corrects what the result actually shows. He knows the observed developmental
route and private twin manifestations. That does not give him the external
Talent manual or every hidden mechanism of future growth.

Jingheng is aware of an old health difficulty and has made limited non-invasive
observations. He does not possess a complete private diagnosis or a constructed
cure. Affection is a motive to investigate, not a medical mechanism. Later
source review, access, consent and real work determine what help becomes possible;
neither automatic recovery nor forced original death is selected.

## Xuan Zi: familiar elder, independent person

Xuan Zi’s bond includes practical observation, food, warning and correction.
He asks what Jingheng noticed before comparing it with what he himself saw.
The child is allowed to reason; the adult need not become incompetent to make
that reasoning impressive.

Familiarity does not reduce Xuan Zi to eating and drinking, erase his faults or
make him a permanent escort. He has independent duties and decisions. His
private-training knowledge has an actual route, not an assumption that all
elders share a single omniscient family mind.

## The wider circle

Other elders require their own contact and observation history before receiving
a special personal bond. The parents’ standing facilitates access but is not
proof everyone has taught the boy. Institutional work still happens, leaving
room for peers, independent problems and ordinary childhood.

The established warmth is real. It is not payment for every desired answer,
a blood transfer or involuntary optimization of loved ones.
