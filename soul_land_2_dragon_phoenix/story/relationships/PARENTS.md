# Jingheng with Yan Shaozhe and Cai Mei’er

## Present relationship

He is their biological son and has grown up with affection, provision, correction
and real expectations. He knows their ordinary habits as well as their authority.
Neither parent is only an instructor, a source of a martial soul or a resource dispenser.
These are original relationship designs within the author’s fixed parentage.

## With his father

Jingheng brings Yan Shaozhe questions and explanations he expects to be tested.
His father asks for a useful result rather than praising a clever description.
An unfinished explanation can be returned to after institutional work; a refusal
has a reason, not the implication that a child should never ask.

Jingheng respects him without ceremonial worship. He wants his father to notice
a genuine improvement and can be annoyed when a quick claim is challenged.
Yan Shaozhe has observed the actual training and workshop history. His later
judgment must reflect that evidence, even where he disagrees about doctrine,
priority or access. His son’s successful tool does not automatically settle
every institutional argument or convert all of his opinions.

## With his mother

Cai Mei’er knows the boy behind his practiced confidence. Her corrections test
whether an improvement remains useful from an awkward stance, interrupted
preparation or poor recovery—not merely whether it looks impressive in a clean drill.
She listens to complaints without necessarily accepting their conclusions.

Affection is ordinary and particular: noticing equipment that needs attention,
checking whether he has eaten, recognizing when he is trying to skip a less
interesting step. These habits do not make her universally stricter or limit
her to physical training. Jingheng knows her preferences, not every institutional
or family secret. Her own judgment and responsibilities continue outside his concerns.

## As a household

The parents can disagree about an actual risk without becoming caricatures or
requiring a unified response to every question. They have supplied organized
hunts, cultivation support and authorized instruction; the boy is not permanently
sheltered with all achievements confined to paper.

The current field/tool-testing outing is approved on demonstrated capability,
route boundaries and companion arrangements. No invisible parent or ancestor
stands at the stream solely to preserve the original rescue. Travel permission,
workshop allocation and ownership are real, limited authorizations.

## Continuity to carry forward

The parents know his two souls and advanced development. Their observed history
cannot reset in a later canon conversation. He trusts their care while retaining
his own choices and age-appropriate irritation. Neither love nor power grants
complete knowledge, an external Talent or automatic agreement.
