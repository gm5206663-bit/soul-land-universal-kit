# Bei Bei, Tang Ya and the first Yuhao meeting

## Before the stream

Bei Bei and Tang Ya know Jingheng through the academy/social circle. Their
existing ease is enough for the approved field/tool-testing outing without
inventing a past shared hunt, Tang Sect membership or new kinship. They know
he is a capable Soul King with workshop experience. They have not received
the private second-soul disclosure.

Bei Bei keeps his judgment and ability. A brief disagreement, practical division
of attention or understood joke can show their acquaintance without a biography.
Tang Ya has her own humor, interest in the fish and Tang Sect aims. She is not
a second Bei Bei or someone automatically authorized to recruit the deans’ son.

## Yuhao is a stranger

The first meeting is the source-anchored stream encounter, with Jingheng beside
both companions. There was no Duke-household visit, childhood friendship,
pre-opening intelligence report or tracking of Yuhao’s route.

Jingheng can notice fatigue, worn clothes or equipment and make a limited inference.
He cannot know private abuse, parentage, innate reading, the full maternal history
or a future bond on sight. Yuhao chooses what to say and whether to accept help.
Food and conversation do not create a permanent loyalty debt.

## The meal as a four-person scene

Track approach, seating, fish, tools, speech and what each participant actually
hears. Let the boys’ different assumptions emerge through a practical choice:
what can be replaced, what help means, why accepting it may feel difficult.
Yuhao stays competent; Jingheng need not be cruel to make inequality visible.

The current Chapter 1 plan ends with an actual shared-route decision. The exact
dialogue and meal distribution remain unwritten. This arrangement replaces any
automatic copy of solitary departure followed by stealth protection.

## The next danger

Their knowledge of Jingheng’s competence persists. He is not sent away by
coincidence or made helpless so an ordinary threat can reproduce Yuhao’s source
injury. N06–N09 have been read for the later forest causes, but the private
spiritual encounter still needs its own revised scene/witness plan.
