is explained in [DEVELOPMENT_DERIVATION.md](../foundation/DEVELOPMENT_DERIVATION.md),
not disguised as a mathematical proof. The original-species labels are explicit;
no unique canonical beast or later-era institution is claimed off-page.

The choice gives his background substantial consequences without granting every
ancestor’s learned knowledge, a third soul, divinity, infinite energy, automatic
mental immunity or a cure aura. It is neither an ordinary-child reset nor a
ceiling on all possible later development.
