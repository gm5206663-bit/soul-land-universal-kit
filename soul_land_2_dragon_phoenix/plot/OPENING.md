**Plan only. No chapter prose or consumed fic source units exist.**
The numerical build is established; the remaining visual-source work is in
[open work](../operations/OPEN_WORK.md). Drafting remains blocked until that work
and the real readiness gate are resolved.

## Chapter 1: a meeting that changes the route

**POV:** Yuhao close-third throughout. **Boundary:** departure, journey and stream
meeting through the actual post-meal route decision. Finish before the Wind
Baboon attack or Skydream encounter. The extra person needs space to affect the
meeting without cramming every later event into the same chapter.

| Scene | Working words | Dramatic job | What must change by its end |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Departure | 350 | Establish Yuhao’s resolve and selectively relevant past | He commits to his chosen journey |
| Journey and stream | 450 | Show travel cost and practical fishing/cooking competence | An immediate need and place for the encounter exist |
| Four-person meeting and meal | 1,450 | Introductions, distinct personalities, food and guarded exchange | Strangers become actual acquaintances, not automatic loyal companions |
| Offer and route decision | 550 | Let each person respond to what is really known | A shared-route arrangement is a real choice, not copied stealth-follow staging |

The total working target is 2,800 words, not a compulsory exact length. Relevant
1.1–1.4 beats are planned; part of 1.4 lies beyond this endpoint. Read units are
not automatically consumed units. The [structured plan](CHAPTER_001.json) records
scene IDs, source status and the same boundary.

## Jingheng’s contribution

Use one practical, controlled act plus a familiar exchange with Bei Bei/Tang Ya.
It should show confidence, experience or an assumption worth noticing—not trigger
a list of every ring and relative. A small engineering or handling detail can
carry real history. He notices fatigue or worn gear, not the whole Duke household.
Yuhao’s own competence and right to withhold personal history remain visible.

Bei Bei and Tang Ya know Jingheng is capable. They have independent judgment,
reasons for traveling and priorities in the conversation. Their ease with him
is not permission to forget the boy’s actual strength during the next threat.
The exact dialogue and physical fish distribution wait for prose; do not invent
those as already written continuity.

## Where later story can grow

| Route | Established starting difference | Decision still earned later |
|---|---|---|
| Shrek return | Jingheng returns to familiar people while Yuhao meets them anew | Formal admission, classes and actual public reactions |
| Workshop | Existing practical pupil and repeated tools | New inventions, certification and institutional response |
| Family and elders | Known advanced development and close relationships | Specific disagreements, disclosure and help that really works |
| Ma Xiaotao | Existing personal concern and limited permitted observations | Condition research, changed incident causes and any future relationship |
| Hidden ancestry | Real potential, no ancestor contact or identity disclosure to boy | Source-reconciled revelation and personal response |
| Teamwork/tournaments | Actual competence and other people’s agency | Team membership, roles and outcomes on the shared clock |

No final romance, godhood, sect allegiance, elder death/cure or final team is
preselected. Challenge can require information, an interface, material, precision,
coordination or a real choice. It does not need a permanent nerf. Once a problem
has become routine, allow it to be routine rather than repeating a solved conflict.
