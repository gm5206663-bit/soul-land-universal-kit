## Recording a written scene

Before drafting, choose start/end event, place, POV, participants and elapsed time.
Afterward record what was actually said/done, witnesses, knowledge gained,
relationship changes, material/item transfers, injuries/recovery, learning,
adaptive integration and downstream consequences. Update live state once and
regenerate its views. A damaged tool stays damaged; a learned lesson remains
learned; a disclosed secret remains known to its witnesses.

There are currently **zero written scene events**. This accurate zero is not an
absence of authored off-page history. A correction to design is recorded as a
correction, not fictional amnesia or a quiet return to unchanged canon.

## Promises and foreshadowing

No narrative promise has yet been written. Useful possible motifs include a
corrected tool working cleanly, an elder noticing a changed question, the gap
between offering and deciding for someone, or two manifestations becoming a
personal style. A gesture need not be a secret bloodline clue.

When prose establishes a promise, record its scene, who can know it, relevance,
prerequisites, earliest payoff and actual payoff/retirement on the shared clock.
No prophecy of a pairing, divinity, universal cure, bond transfer or final team
is part of the foundation. Revise changed setups rather than forcing old payoffs.
