# Combat doctrine — proposed implementation

He has genuine fighting talent, good instruction and a developed foundation.
Do not make him forget his abilities because the original battle was difficult
for someone else. Do not convert that principle into automatic victory.

## Distinct training lines

- Movement, distance, body coordination and recovery under changing terrain.
- Spear line: grip, alignment, reach, defense, feints, pressure and committed attack.
- Phoenix line: actual manifestation, movement and verified native effects.
- Soul-power precision, output, duration, transition and restraint.
- Engineering interface: sensing, deployment, hand use, timing and ammunition/energy.
- Opponent reading: observable cues and tested inference, not hidden-stat vision.
- Teamwork: others’ decisions and independent abilities, not coordinated servants.

The lines can integrate into a personal style through real practice. Suitable
techniques may fuse, but no inherited Grey Wolf grade ladder, fixed percentage
meter or unrelated named footwork is granted. Record stages of actual learning.

## Feat ledger

Separate burst, sustained, assisted, unassisted and environment-specific output.
A comparison must name the kind of opponent and conditions; a blanket “Soul King
killer” claim is not a substitute for a fight. Opening feats are unassigned.
Once old tasks become easier through genuine growth, later scenes must show it.

## First encounter consequences

Novel 1.3–1.4 gives a stream meal before the forest entrance. If Jingheng is
present and observes risk, his concern and ability must affect what he offers
or does. Do not send him away by coincidence to preserve a later injury. The
Wind Baboon/Skydream sequence needs its own primary reading before replanning.
