# Resources and cultivation economy

The author locks continuous top-level resources, teaching and guidance.
Do not invent an impoverished childhood or missing basic instruction.

## Supply classes — implementation and record discipline

| Input | Credible access route | Record before claiming use |
|---|---|---|
| Nutrition and recovery | Parents, academy household and appropriate provisions | Type, purpose, schedule, result; no fictional need to starve |
| Cultivation instruction | Parents / grandmaster / appropriate faculty | Actual technique, access, prerequisites and correction history |
| Training facilities | Authorized academy access | Which space, permission, stage-appropriate conditions |
| Medicine and special materials | Real institutional stock and explicit grants | Identity, compatibility, amount, consequence |
| Hunts and protection | Appropriate adults and actual route | Donor availability, hunt, final act, absorption and integration |
| Workshop resources | Authorized engineering faculty / family support | Materials, design access, workshop safety and results |
| High-value treasures | Specific owner and grant only | Do not turn “top resources” into every named treasure in the setting |

The current ledger records real provision classes, five hunts and workshop
allocation; the opening inventory records actual completed devices. No Golden
Tree access, immortal herb, soul bone or named rare-metal treasury is silently
added. Unknown cumulative weights are not invented as a gram-to-rank equation.

## Demand versus efficiency

Keep four independent questions: How much useful input is available? How much
is assimilated? What is being constructed? What must be integrated before
higher output is safe? A denser dragon–phoenix foundation can need more total
energy while using it more efficiently and advancing much faster.

No fixed XP equation or rank-per-meal coefficient is canon. Diagnostic averages
cannot substitute for actual nonlinear cultivation and ring gates. Do not use
“more expensive foundation” as a permanent tax that nullifies all his advantages.

Record resource uses in state/resource_events.json. There are no invented
consumption totals or magical replenishment rules in the current ledger.
