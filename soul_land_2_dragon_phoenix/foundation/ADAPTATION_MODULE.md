## 1. Identity and source architecture

Native human holder in Soul Land II. The Talent is inseparable, non-sentient and
present from conception. It is neither martial soul, a third soul slot, a ring,
a soul core, an external spirit nor a detachable blessing. No IATS/IODS is granted.

| Source | What it contributes | What it does not grant |
|---|---|---|
| Body and native soul | Embodiment, perception, cultivation substrate | An adult’s experience or automatic invulnerability |
| Inheritance | Actual parental/ancestral potential | Every ancestral phenotype and learned memory |
| Two martial souls | Distinct native manifestations and their trained paths | Two independent cultivation bars |
| Adaptation Talent | Whole-existence maintenance, integration, development, evolution and valid construction | Free resources, unseen facts or unlimited present output |
| Teaching / lived practice | Information, skill, correction, experience | Instant creative mastery from observation alone |
| Equipment / institutions | Real interfaces and access | Ownership of an entire institution’s power |

## 2. Prenatal foundation

Apply master §§6.1/32: developmental timing, inherited expression, organs, brain,
nervous system, energetic/soul architecture where supported, maternal exchange
and compatibility can all matter. The mother–child connection can support a
healthier pregnancy and persistent real improvements; it does not grant Cai Mei’er
the Talent. Powerful parents and real resources permit a materially different
foundation from a deprived holder. No unsupported prenatal soul-ring or godhood claim.

## 3. Childhood and awakening

Movement, language, attention, emotion, senses, energy compatibility, recovery,
social experience and adaptive method accumulate before martial-soul awakening.
Awakening expands expression; it does not activate a previously absent Talent.
Actual martial-soul cultivation follows the setting’s access rules. The years
cannot be erased because they occur off-page. Evidence lives in DEVELOPMENT.md.

## 4. Soul Land application

- Body and soul development can support more ambitious absorption than a normal
  same-age benchmark, but evaluate the actual donor, intent, energy and integration.
- A ring’s gifts must be identified at their source and expressed through this
  holder’s own souls/body. Another holder’s element graft or ring-aging mechanism
  does not automatically transfer to Jingheng.
- Absorbed rings, traits, bloodline changes, learned techniques and equipment
  retain separate sources. Possibility is not an achieved opening ability.
- Breakthroughs recalibrate the connected whole; they do not multiply every
  statistic equally. Later scenes must reflect lower costs and easier old tasks.
- Ring age is not automatically increased by time or an unnamed “adaptive energy”.
  Any special aging route needs its own input, mechanism and ledger.
- Spirit-soul infrastructure and later-era systems cannot be imported into the
  opening just because another Soul Land holder uses them.

## 5. Whole-existence scope in this upbringing

Body: structure, endurance, coordination, recovery, resistance with actual exposure.
Mind: learning chain, memory, attention, reasoning; hidden facts still require input.
Emotion: regulation and understanding; play, pride, grief and attachment remain real.
Soul: strength, compatibility, stability and control where the world supports them.
Energy: access, flow, storage, efficiency and valid conversions with real substrates.
Souls/bloodlines: refinement, harmonization, personalization and earned new routes.
Skills: reproduction is distinct from conditioning, application and invention.
Interfaces: safe tool handling, feedback, use and communication—not free external mutation.
Relations: better participation, not involuntary optimization of loved ones.

## 6. Economy and challenge

Substantial construction consumes substantial inputs. Good assimilation reduces
waste; dense foundation can still require more total supply. The author explicitly
provides top resources and instruction. Do not add hunger, exhaustion, instability
or slow growth without an actual excess, incompatibility, injury or supply failure.
Do not assume exposure automatically means resistance or that every hunt must
nearly kill him. Neither artificial nerfing nor free success follows the doctrine.

## 7. Present vs future

Opening Adaptive Order/maturity are not assigned yet. They cannot be converted
into a combat rank. Constructive growth beyond inheritance is possible through
real prerequisites, not a ban saying the Talent only refines old things.
Sovereign laws, divinity, derivatives and conceptual adaptation are future
conditional work, not childhood inventory.

Use the log schema in state/adaptation_events.json. Each real milestone names
cause, prior foundation, input, response, cost, integration, scope and later proof.
The ledger now contains explicitly authored original off-page history under R17.
It is not a claim that canon depicts this holder or that chapters were published.
The unassigned Order label does not negate actual achieved construction; it is
not a combat-rank conversion, hard cap or excuse to ignore recorded abilities.
