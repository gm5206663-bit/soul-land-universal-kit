# Voice, POV and scene texture

## Jingheng

Concrete questions, natural humor, impatience when earned, and comfort around
trusted people. He can be articulate without sounding like an adult doctrine
manual. His thoughts concern his world and actual experience, not protagonist
rankings, future canon or a plot he has read.

## Family and peers

Do not make every important adult solemn and every peer awed. Familiarity gives
room for interruption, correction, unfinished conversations and ordinary preferences.
Ma Xiaotao needs life beyond symptoms; Tang Ya and Bei Bei remain distinct.
Yuhao’s reserve and practical competence belong to him.

## POV

Use clear close-third and deliberate transitions. A reader-facing Yuhao scene
may establish a fact that Jingheng does not know. Do not leak that fact into the
OC’s next thought. If the viewpoint changes, track which observations travel
through dialogue and which remain private.

## Useful scene textures

An object being handled while people speak; a shared meal with different assumptions;
a familiar correction understood without a lecture; an adjustment with a visible
result; an offer with space to decline. These are working approaches, not mandatory
scene recipes. Avoid repeated food/teasing/training beats as a substitute for plot.
