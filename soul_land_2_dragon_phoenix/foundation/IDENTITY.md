# Identity and family

## Working identity — delegated design

**Yan Jingheng · 言景衡**. Male, native-born human. Same generation and around the
same age as Yuhao. Exact opening age is a design calculation anchored to the
novel’s explicit eleven-year-old Yuhao, not an author-quoted birthday.
Surname follows Yan Shaozhe’s 言. No title, royal status or divine office comes
with the name. The working book title describes the approved foundations, not
a final evolved species or accepted transformation.

## Family — author’s branch

Father: Yan Shaozhe. Mother: Cai Mei’er. Long Xiaoyao and Ye Xishui are designated
his great-grandparents by the author. Preserve that branch genealogy; do not
silently replace it with a different generation count from a wiki.
Secondary sources conflict on the intermediate generations. Before hidden-family
history appears in prose, compare the original revelation and record the exact
branch difference. This does not authorize personal contact with those ancestors.

Ancestry gives potential, not recalled lives, class-nine schematics or automatic
possession of every ancestral martial soul. The approved pair is explicit.

## Appearance — proposed, not a locked model

Age-appropriate, healthy, coordinated and visibly well cared for. A balanced,
expressive face and practical academy/travel clothes suit the upbringing. Do not
reuse Fire Phoenix’s female disguise, copper-red makeover, portrait or costume.
Hair, eye color and transformation tells should be finalized with the martial-
soul expression ledger rather than borrowed from another OC. No adult body or
sexualized presentation is justified by advanced development.

No illness, adoption secret, missing childhood memory or reincarnation has been
introduced to explain away the author’s strong starting conditions.
