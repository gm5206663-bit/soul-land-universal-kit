# Canon access and authority

## Spine

Original Soul Land II novel by Tang Jia San Shao. Working English split-unit
spine: licensed Wuxiaworld edition, whose Chapter 1 has parts 1.1–1.4.
The official WebNovel book has different platform sequence numbers. Store both
printed title/unit and platform ID; never silently subtract one and call it a map.

## Other media

Official Tencent manhua book 649513 and official WeTV donghua series
wu7vz4vgfi8ugan are separate adaptations. Track catalog order, displayed chapter
label and URL content ID independently. A title page or player listing does not
prove its visual scene contents. No equation “novel chapter = manhua chapter = episode”.

The maternal spear is an explicit author-adopted donghua foundation. Other
adaptation differences require a recorded comparison, not a silent composite canon.
Novel order is the default spine pending specific author rulings, not a license
to ignore meaningful adaptation evidence the author requested.

## Access rules

Use licensed/official available material or material legitimately supplied by the
author. Do not bypass payment, access restrictions or fetch novel chapters from
unofficial redistribution sites. Do not publicly store full copyrighted chapters,
scans or episode downloads. Keep concise original summaries and source locations.

Wikis and encyclopedias can locate references; they cannot settle source beats,
unknown martial-soul details, exact ranks or family generations alone.
A source returning HTTP 200 with no article body is an empty lead, not a receipt.

## Claim scopes

SOURCE_RECEIPTS.json specifies read body / partial comparison / metadata only /
secondary pointer / unavailable. CANON_LEDGER.md records claims and conflicts.
Scene-level use requires a reviewed body or visual unit and exact location.
A missing verification is not proof a claim is false, and a plausible inference
is not a verified scene. Escalate a genuine blocked access need; do not invent it.
