# Conflict architecture — challenge without manufactured weakness

## Interpersonal

A gifted, securely raised child can misunderstand what another person wants from
an offer. An adult can disagree over a real risk without secretly hating him.
A peer can value his company without accepting his solution. Resolve the actual
disagreement; do not repeat a learned lesson forever.

## Technical and tactical

A task can require information, a material, a particular interface, coordination,
precision under time pressure or an invention not yet constructed. Use actual
causal requirements rather than invented fatigue whenever a strong character
would otherwise solve a routine problem. Once a problem has genuinely become
routine, let it be routine.

## Institutional

Time, permission, ownership, public consequences and competing duties remain
real even when resources are excellent. A better invention can change incentives
without instantly persuading every person or replacing every existing system.
Canon opinions need to respond to the OC’s actual historical results.

## Moral and emotional

He can want approval, feel anger, worry about a loved one or hesitate over a
choice. Adaptation helps real emotional development; it does not delete identity
or grant omniscience. Do not invent permanent cruelty or incompetence to make
him seem flawed.

No artificial cultivation ceiling, unexplained instability, total secrecy tax,
compulsory near-death hunt or repeated failure to use known powers is built into
this foundation. Conversely, potential cannot substitute for a missing achieved
capability or a real external help mechanism.
