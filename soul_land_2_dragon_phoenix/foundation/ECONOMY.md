# Economy, ownership and access

[RESOURCES.md](RESOURCES.md) owns the cultivation-input logic. This file covers
ownership and practical exchange; no unsupported coin conversion is introduced.

The child has excellent provision and guidance. That means he is not repeatedly
unable to afford ordinary food or basic materials. It does not mean he owns
academy stores, can pledge his parents’ money without consent or can gift every
rare object mentioned in the source.

## Distinct budgets

Household provision · training allocation · workshop materials · damaged/replaced
components · hunt organization · travel supplies · personal possessions · formal
academy property. Record a transfer when ownership or allowed use changes.
A borrowed device remains borrowed; a consumed reagent cannot appear unused in
the next scene. Expensive failed work is real but not evidence the child must
be starved or deprived to keep the story challenging.

## Learning through making

Strong aptitude can reduce waste and improve precision without making all craft
free. Materials, tool wear, design access and iterative work need a route.
A reliable invention can have institutional value, but do not invent sales,
patents, public reputation or industrial scale before the actual result exists.

## Interaction with Yuhao

An offer can be practical without treating money as an answer to every personal
problem. Establish what Jingheng possesses, what he is authorized to give and
what Yuhao chooses to accept. Do not retroactively debit a vast amount from an
unwritten family treasury merely to advertise generosity.
