## Operating rules

A good observer may infer a limited fact and still be wrong about its cause.
Perceiving an unusual aura is not identifying a hidden god, inherited line or
future plot. Suppression/disclosure requires a concrete act and witness.
No automatic lie detection, mind reading or academy-wide privacy conspiracy.
In particular, a direct reader statement about ancestry is not a character memory.
