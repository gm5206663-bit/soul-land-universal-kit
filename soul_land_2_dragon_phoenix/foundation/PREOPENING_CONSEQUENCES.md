# Pre-opening consequences — dispositions for Chapter 1

**Scope:** working original history and the decisions required before the stream
chapter. This is not a declaration that all later canon outcomes are solved.

## Family and institution

The household knows the real advanced development. Parents allocate teaching,
hunts and appropriate access around actual duties. They do not suddenly discover
a gifted son in Chapter 1. Relevant faculty recognize his workshop work; ordinary
students need not know every privately trained ability. Privacy has witnesses,
not an institution-wide omniscient conspiracy.

Fan Yu has an existing pupil with strong practical talent. That changes normal
workshop conversation, including He Caitou’s acquaintance with him. It does not
supply the broad shared-detection interface in N10–N12 or erase Yuhao’s later
independent opportunity. Parents cannot repeat blanket dismissals that ignore
their own observed history; disagreements can instead concern scope, doctrine,
access, training balance or actual results.

## Ma Xiaotao

Jingheng knows her closely and has noticed difficult control/recovery episodes.
He has asked questions and helped with permitted observation and accessible
flow checks under adult supervision. His experience with his own phoenix is
relevant input, not proof the two souls have the same underlying condition.
No permanent change of her martial soul or verified cure is part of the opening.
No automatic failure of every future method is ruled either.

Do not write an untouched copy of the later lake incident. A future scene needs
the actual original passage, treatment history, trigger, witnesses and current
possibilities. If earlier assistance changes its causes, its result changes.
The supporting character still has a life beyond being a patient; no pairing
has been decided. Do not add intimate treatment as a romantic shortcut.

## Mu En

The boy knows an elderly close grandmaster with an old health difficulty, not
its complete source-level diagnosis. Non-invasive observations do not identify
every damaged structure or authorize risky reconstruction in another person.
No permanent cure has been constructed at opening. A genuine later route can
be researched; his canonical death is not imposed regardless of changed causes.
The Talent is the boy’s, not a passive external optimization aura.

## Stream and forest

The boy’s authorized field trip has a real purpose. He does not need a sixth
ring and has not followed Yuhao from the Duke household. Bei Bei and Tang Ya
know he is a capable Soul King, not a helpless child whose known ability they
forget during an ordinary threat.

At the meal he can offer company/help without diagnosing private history. The
Chapter 1 decision must respond to Yuhao’s actual preference; a new shared route
is the working choice, not forced stealth following. If the Wind Baboon attack
is retained later, ordinary danger is handled at the party’s real competence.
No contrived distraction or canon-mandated shoulder wound is installed.

N07–N09 show that Skydream selects for a spiritual-type host and uses a large
spiritual effect. Neither automatic OC eligibility nor immunity follows. The
later bond/relic encounter is a separate scene problem requiring its complete
coverage and causal plan, not a hidden promise at the end of Chapter 1.
