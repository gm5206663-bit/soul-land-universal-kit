# Development derivation — an authored history, not a canon calculator

## Authority and honesty

R01/R06/R07 delegate the native child’s development; R17 authorizes the preflight
work. The accepted checkpoint is **delegated fiction design**, not a new verbatim
author rank choice and not a claim that the original novel contains Jingheng.
The novel supplies world constraints. The Talent master supplies operating law.
Neither supplies a function that uniquely calculates an OC’s rank from ancestry.

The original individuals, donor labels, nominal cultivation ages, dates, tests
and devices in this route are authored fanfiction. They are labeled DESIGN01.
Do not call those entries primary-canon receipts. The resulting numbers are
continuity choices supported by a complete causal route, not experimental data
or a scientifically proven optimum. The author can revise them.

## Why this is materially stronger

Conception through awakening supplies real body, soul, sensory, cognitive,
emotional and energetic foundations. Gifted parents and the author’s dragon–
phoenix inheritance provide a viable starting substrate. Excellent care and
regulated maternal provision pay for prenatal work; childhood adds real play,
instruction and practice. The Talent does not wait until six to begin.

Awakening’s full-innate reading does not erase the denser foundation behind it.
That foundation makes an ambitious first ring plausible in this branch. Each
later stage compounds actual body/soul/control development and improved use of
resources. Hunts, education and recovery are supplied rather than absent for
convenience. Increased demand is real; it does not cancel those advantages.

This route contains no constant ranks-per-year law. Later stages cost more;
resources, integration and increasingly useful practice also change. The final
rank is the consequence of these authored milestones, not a number covertly
borrowed from another holder. High rank does not add a certificate or godhood.

## Absorption safeguards

Before the first ring, the boy’s supporting body/soul architecture is unusually
developed from the full prenatal/childhood history, not merely a normal child
with a strong surname. Parents use graduated energy/tolerance exercises and
compatible donors. Each ring is integrated before later loads. Before the black
rings, dedicated will/concentration and controlled soul-pressure work supplement
the reinforced body. The adult organizes and contains a hunt but cannot absorb
in the boy’s place or prove compatibility by title alone.

The reviewed ordinary first-ring benchmark in N09 is comparison data. This
exceptional route needs the above actual supporting history; it is not a new
universal safe limit. Nominal donor ages do not grow by calendar arithmetic.

## Choices compared and retained

An ordinary resource-poor baseline was rejected because it contradicts the premise.
A high Soul Elder/Soul Ancestor opening is not imposed to keep the OC at the
original students’ level. A still-higher opening is not categorically forbidden,
but is not silently granted without a different historical route. This build
is a working branch, not the Talent’s maximum or a law about every gifted child.

Phoenix-led rings are retained for the current integration cycle. The spear
is genuinely practiced and can share the developed foundation; its next ring
selection remains an active future project, not a requirement to repeat the
original forest hunt or an inability to use the mother’s soul.

## Feats versus implications

Authored off-page demonstrations establish the narrow functions listed in the
current checkpoint. Later prose must demonstrate relevant consequences. No
blanket claim of defeating every higher-rank fighter, curing another person,
resisting a million-year spiritual wave or knowing private history follows.
No published chapter has been retroactively invented by this development ledger.
