## What inheritance can carry

Potential, actual inherited traits and martial-soul foundations are distinct
from learned knowledge. No ancestor meetings, secret lessons or engineering
memories are inserted. Character knowledge of the hidden tree remains governed
by [the firewall](KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALL.md), not this author-facing diagram.
