## Numbering traps already found

- WebNovel platform sequence 2 displays original Chapter 1, whereas Wuxiaworld
  splits it into 1.1–1.4. Sequence numbers and printed chapter numbers differ.
- Tencent’s catalog row 1 is a prologue at cid 2. Row 2 is displayed chapter
  `1 灵眸少年` at cid 3. The URL returned by the initial mobile cid 2 lead was
  the prologue, not the first Yuhao chapter. Do not reuse the search snippet’s
  label without inspecting the destination.
- An official episode listing establishes an episode ID, not where a scene starts.

## Per-chapter footer format

Novel: edition + exact part(s), scene/beat boundaries.
Manhua: edition + displayed chapter + platform ID + panel/page range; or not used.
Donghua: edition/season + episode ID + timestamps; or not used.
Status: verified / partial / not reviewed / not used, separately for each medium.
Adaptation choice: what changed, why, and which author decision permits it.
Never fill an empty mapping with a plausible-looking number.
