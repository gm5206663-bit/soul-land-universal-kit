## Authority and use

The author establishes the family, trusted-circle upbringing and existing close
bonds. The personal routines and tensions below are **delegated working designs**,
not verified canon scenes or quotes from the author. No fic scene has been written.
These designs should guide consistent action without being recited as exposition.

## The family is not four interchangeable teachers

His father can explain why a release is wasteful; his mother can insist that a
promising movement survive an awkward recovery. These are working scene
approaches, not a universal restriction on either parent’s expertise. Mu En can
recognize when a technically correct answer avoids the boy’s real concern.
Xuan Zi can bring a discussion back to what happened under actual conditions.
None of them becomes the exclusive owner of one emotional or technical function.

Their son is exceptionally gifted. That changes their expectations and the
opportunities they provide. It does not make him head of the household or
require his parents to win every disagreement. Their responsibilities also
explain scheduling; invented neglect is not a necessary source of drama.

## Security, independence and privacy

A securely loved child can accept correction and still resent a particular rule.
He can choose to ask for help without becoming weak. Equally, institutional
access is not permission to disclose another person’s treatment, workshop work
or private history. [Knowledge boundaries](KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALL.md) apply even to
his closest bonds. Exact witnesses to the two martial souls remain to be finalized.

## Ma Xiaotao is a person, not a condition

Their closeness should exist in ordinary exchanges as well as concern over evil
fire. She can be busy, competitive, funny, impatient and concerned for him in
return. Do not make each meeting another medical inspection. Do not turn her
into a reward for the boy’s powers, and do not erase the existing closeness
merely because the future has not been selected.

## Change must accumulate

If he listens to someone’s preference once, later scenes must remember it.
If he discovers that a resource-rich answer overlooks another person’s choice,
that lesson can alter his next offer. Characters do not reset to their original
attitudes each time canon reaches a familiar conversation.

No affection percentages, compulsory romances or destiny labels are assigned.
Use [continuity](CONTINUITY.md) to record actual changes after prose exists.
