# Character — causes before labels

**Status: delegated character design; not a verbatim author personality list.**

## The person at the opening

Jingheng has the ease of a child who expects a safe place to return to. He has
been corrected by formidable people who also know when he last ate, what he is
trying to build and which explanation he will challenge. Titles alone do not
silence him. He can be respectful without performing worship.

He enjoys understanding how an action works. In a workshop this becomes attention
to a failed component, a question about a circuit or satisfaction when an awkward
motion becomes clean. In a fight it becomes active observation and adjustment,
not an internal laboratory report. His confidence has a history of effort and
results; it need not be loud or apologetic.

He is lively with people he trusts and willing to compete. Adaptation improves
regulation, recovery and understanding; it does not remove irritation, fear,
pride, attachment or the wish to be praised. He remains a native child with
limited experience, not an adult strategist pretending to be young.

## Relationships shape expression

- With Mu En: familiarity and affection beneath genuine respect. He knows a
  person, not only a legendary title; he does not therefore know all the man’s secrets.
- With his parents: security plus expectations. Their son can disagree without
  making them caricatures who instantly abandon duties or principles.
- With Ma Xiaotao: a particular, long-standing closeness. Concern is personal,
  not a newly acquired crush or a compulsory sibling relationship.
- With Bei Bei/Tang Ya: proposed pre-existing acquaintance sufficient to travel
  together; the exact shared history and purpose of this trip require a short ledger.
- With Yuhao: a new acquaintance. He may notice fatigue or worn clothing without
  diagnosing an entire abused childhood or treating the boy as a charity project.

## Productive tensions, not inserted handicaps

He can understand scarcity as a fact before understanding its daily emotional
weight. He can want to solve a problem with access or expertise when the other
person first needs to retain a choice. These are possible scene tensions,
not mandatory humiliations or permanent social incompetence. Growth must alter
later behavior; do not reset a learned lesson to manufacture another conflict.

## Voice

Concrete questions, natural humor and occasional impatience. Vary responses with
company and stakes. No constant aphorisms, smug forecasts, ceremonial dialogue
or cold diagnosis of everyone in the room. His private thought cannot cite the
novel, compare himself to protagonists or use knowledge he has never acquired.
