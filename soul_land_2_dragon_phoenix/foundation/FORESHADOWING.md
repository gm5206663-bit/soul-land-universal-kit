# Foreshadowing and promises

There are no written narrative promises yet. Proposed motifs below are not
prophecies or accepted future outcomes.

## Useful working motifs

A tool working more cleanly after an earlier correction; a familiar elder noticing
a changed question; the difference between offering something and deciding for
someone; the comfort of a known place versus a new traveler’s reserve; two soul
expressions becoming a personal style through actual work.

Use a detail only if it serves the present scene. A hand gesture need not be a
secret bloodline clue. Do not turn every phoenix/dragon description into a
promise of divine authority. Hidden ancestry can be handled later without
making an eleven-year-old speak ominous lore he has never learned.

## Promise register standard

ID · exact promise or repeated detail · chapter/scene introduced · who can know
it · intended relevance · earliest possible payoff · prerequisites · author
boundary · actual payoff or retirement. Relative timing is computed from the
shared clock, not written independently in two documents.

## Exclusions

No future Ma pairing, Mu En outcome, godhood, universal cure, Skydream transfer,
final team or sect allegiance is promised. If a later source consequence changes,
review the setup rather than forcing the old payoff at any cost. If a promise
cannot be paid off within the chosen story, deliberately revise or retire it
with a receipt; do not leave it as unexplained destiny.
