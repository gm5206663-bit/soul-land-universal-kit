# Writing — natural canon-led fiction

1. Canon is researched before prose. Use the original novel’s dramatic flow,
   not a copied paragraph template or chapter-by-chapter retranslation.
2. No lengthy OC childhood chronicle. Background appears where present action,
   observation or conversation makes it relevant.
3. Canon and OC share a clock. Do not omit load-bearing canon events while the
   camera watches repeated OC training. Do not recite every routine event either.
4. Expand meaningful changes; compress routine. A fic chapter may consume several
   source units or part of one. Exact coverage explains the choice.
5. Every scene needs a purpose: a decision, new understanding, altered relation,
   tactical consequence or necessary transition. No prestige cameos for their own sake.
6. Use natural close-third with clear scene transitions. No unsignaled head-hopping
   to distribute private knowledge. Match actual canon voices, not wiki caricatures.
7. Plain, varied prose and real dialogue. No repetitive aphorisms, constant
   micro-fragments, skill lists or identical awed reactions.
8. Starting style target, adapted from the guide: about 2,400–3,400 words, average
   sentence length roughly 14–18, no sentence above 60 words, no habitual “the way”
   construction. These are agent craft targets, not newly quoted author commands.
9. No diegetic system windows. A private state ledger is not a power in his head.
   Relevant changes may be summarized outside prose, not repeated on every page.
10. Achievements must change later action; no forgetting powers for suspense.
11. Closeness is shown in behavior without forced romance. Child depiction stays
   age-appropriate. Native intelligence is not lived adult wisdom.
12. Source footer names exact novel units, manhua edition/chapter/panels and
   donghua season/episode/timestamps actually used. Mark unreviewed/not-used entries.

A prose gate measures mechanical features, not originality, naturalness, consent,
faithful characterization or canon correctness. Authored review remains necessary.
