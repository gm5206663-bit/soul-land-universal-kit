# Foundation — a native life, not a spectator inserted into canon

## Premise and story position

The protagonist is a biological son of Yan Shaozhe and Cai Mei’er in this
fanfiction branch. His conception already changes their lives; his birth is not
an invisible patch which leaves eleven years of family history untouched.
His native identity, two martial souls and complete-existence adaptation develop
inside a real community of powerful but busy, partial and sometimes mistaken people.

Huo Yuhao remains a person with his own history and agency. Jingheng does not
know the Duke’s household, the future, Skydream or hidden identities merely
because the author knows them. The two first meet when Jingheng is with Tang Ya
and Bei Bei. The novel’s own first stream encounter supplies the researched anchor.

## Authority and classifications

AUTHOR_RULINGS.json owns exact author evidence. CURRENT_STATE.json owns the
machine-readable checkpoint. SOURCE_RECEIPTS.json owns source access/claim scope.
Design documents explain proposals; they do not create accepted feats.

Use these labels consistently:
- **author-lock**: explicit words or a selected option.
- **delegated-design**: a choice the author asked the agent to make; revisable.
- **primary-read**: the specific original narrative body actually reviewed.
- **official-metadata**: a real chapter/episode listing, not a scene review.
- **secondary-pointer**: wiki/encyclopedia orientation requiring primary confirmation.
- **research-pending**: not known, not equivalent to false or zero.
- **future-conditional**: possible only if the later causal route is earned.

## What is extraordinary, already

Top resources, sustained teaching, gifted parents, ancestral potential and the
Talent’s prenatal/childhood work cannot disappear into a decorative biography.
His body, learning, control, soul, energy handling, recovery and emotional life
must begin from the actual accumulated foundation. These advantages are not a
license to invent missing knowledge, godhood, every ancestor’s abilities or free fuel.

## Canon and consequences

Novel order is the spine. The manhua and donghua are explicitly mapped adaptations,
not interchangeable unit numbers. The maternal spear is an author-adopted donghua
foundation. A chapter records what it actually used in each medium, including
“not used” or “not yet verified” instead of fabricated source numbers.

Canon is present on the page in original prose; it is not pasted source text or
an exhaustive translation substitute. Compress routine; expand meaningful
character, tactical, relationship and butterfly changes. No one-source-unit-per-fic-
chapter rule is imposed. Do not repeat the source’s sentences to imitate its voice.

## Stage boundary

The foundation package exists. The numerical opening state and cross-medium
scene mapping are unfinished. That distinction is deliberate and tested.
No Chapter 1, no accepted rank, no released cover, no promised future pairing.
