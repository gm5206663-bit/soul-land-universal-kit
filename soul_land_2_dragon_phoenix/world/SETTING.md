# Setting, institutions and practical resources

## Opening geography

The source opening moves from Yuhao’s departure through a six-day journey to a
stream meal and the Star Dou Forest approach. Jingheng has not followed him from
the Duke household. Exact route conversions differ among English renderings;
no disputed distance is used as a movement feat.

| Place | Function in this story | Practical boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Duke household | Yuhao’s history and departure | Not Jingheng’s childhood home or an abuse dossier he has read |
| Journey route | Establishes fatigue, resolve and practical skills | Travel precedes the meal; no instant academy/forest jump |
| Stream | First four-person meeting | Track approach, positions, food, tools, speech and actual knowledge |
| Forest approach | Next causal problem after the meal | Competent companions must affect ordinary danger |
| Shrek household/training spaces | Existing home, resources and relationships | Permission and schedules remain real |
| Fan Yu’s workshop/testing area | Actual craft, correction and completed work | Authorized access and safe testing, not an unlocked storehouse |

In action, establish terrain, line of sight, reach, cover, exits and equipment
access. Flight is movement through actual space, not teleportation. A stored
tool must be retrieved unless its mechanism explicitly works otherwise.

## Institutions

**Shrek:** a household and academy environment, not the boy’s private kingdom.
Parents, elders and faculty keep their duties and disagreements. His opening
position is a household-raised training/workshop pupil; no automatic inner-court
enrollment or numbered professional title is claimed. His ability is real
without such a badge. Formal later admission needs its own route.

**Tang Sect:** Tang Ya and Bei Bei keep their aims. Traveling with them does not
make Jingheng a member, inheritor or financier. Joining would need an invitation,
informed choice and consequences for his existing commitments.

**External households and organizations:** private history requires disclosure.
Ancestry alone creates neither allegiance nor secret contact. Reputation spreads
through actual witnesses, public results, reports or rumor; no enemy receives
an author-level file simply to make a threat competitive.

## Provision and ownership

| Supply | Established route | Result / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Food, rest, recovery | Household and appropriate medical provision | Real supported development, not manufactured deprivation |
| Cultivation and training | Parents, close elders and authorized instructors | Actual teaching, correction and stage-appropriate practice |
| Hunts | Appropriate adults organize travel and containment | Jingheng supplies the final act, absorption and his own supporting capacity |
| Workshop materials/tools | Authorized allocation under Fan Yu | Making, failed parts, repairs and repeatable completed devices |
| Travel equipment | Owned devices and authorized household provision | Only recorded actual contents; no invisible treasury |
| Named treasures | No blanket grant | A soul bone, immortal herb, Golden Tree access or rare-metal stock needs its own real transfer |

Household money, academy stores, workshop allocation, borrowed property and the
boy’s possessions are separate. Consumed material stays consumed; borrowed items
remain borrowed. Excellent resources mean ordinary food and teaching are not
artificial obstacles, not that every object can be gifted without consent.

The [resource history](../state/resource_events.json) records actual supply classes.
Unknown cumulative weights are not guessed. No fixed gram-to-rank or rank-per-meal
formula exists in this design. The [development route](../power/DEVELOPMENT.md)
explains how demand, efficient assimilation and integration work together.

## Unequal circumstances at the stream

Jingheng can offer practical help without making money an answer to every personal
problem. Yuhao’s dignity, competence and ability to refuse remain real. A useful
contrast may concern what each assumes can be replaced or why accepting an offer
feels different. The scene does not require cruelty, incompetence or instant gratitude.
