# Research findings and limits — 2026-10-22

## Findings that changed the plan

1. Direct licensed novel reading places the stream approach/meeting in 1.3–1.4,
   before the next unit titled Skydream Iceworm. A first meeting after the bond
   would not be the researched initial encounter selected by this plan.
2. Yuhao’s eleven years and five cultivated years are explicit in 1.2; do not
   casually call twelve the verified opening age.
3. Chapter numbers need edition identifiers. WebNovel platform sequence 2 is
   printed Chapter 1; Wuxiaworld splits Chapter 1. Tencent row/cid numbers add a
   prologue offset. None is an arithmetic substitute for reading.
4. The wiki Episode 1 lead returned a page with no article body. HTTP success
   alone is not source verification. The real official WeTV listing was located.
5. Secondary engineering descriptions distinguish proficiency, power and tool
   class while marking several table ranges inferred. Those ranges cannot become
   hard restrictions on an exceptional holder without primary verification.
6. Cai’s spear is a secondary-identified donghua design explicitly accepted for
   this fic, not a claim that the novel named her martial soul.
7. Secondary genealogy labels disagree. The author’s stated family branch is
   preserved; the original revelation must be checked before a genealogy scene.
