# Yan Shaozhe and Cai Mei’er — two full parental relationships

**Author premise:** both are his parents. **Working design:** the specific habits
and conversations below. Their precise ages, work schedules and private history
are not manufactured as canon facts.

## With his father

Jingheng is used to asking Yan Shaozhe questions that other children might not
ask an important adult. He expects an answer, a correction or a clear refusal,
not ceremonial praise. Affection can show in his father noticing an unfinished
explanation and returning to it after work. It does not require constant public
indulgence or granting every request.

Training can involve Jingheng trying to explain an adjustment before showing it.
His father expects a useful result, not a clever-sounding answer. When the boy
actually improves a method, the adults should notice. A parent who has seen real
engineering results cannot later speak as though his son’s work never happened.
That is an institutional consequence to resolve, not an automatic wholesale
conversion of Yan Shaozhe’s opinions.

## With his mother

Cai Mei’er has her own judgment, affection, limits and responsibilities. She is
not merely the source of the second martial soul. A useful working emphasis is
that she asks whether an improvement remains useful when conditions are untidy:
an awkward stance, an interrupted preparation, an exhausted teammate.
This does not limit her to physical training or make her universally stricter
than his father.

A familiar small action—a correction of his equipment, checking whether he has
actually eaten, listening to a complaint without accepting its conclusion—can
carry more history than a paragraph of praise. He should know her ordinary
preferences without somehow knowing every institutional or family secret.

## As a household

They do not need to agree before having individual relationships with their son.
Disagreements about a hunt, workshop access or travel should concern actual
capability and risk. Do not manufacture a permanently sheltered child whose
entire remarkable training exists only on paper. Conversely, parental love does
not make every dangerous request sensible.

Their approval of the opening trip needs the completed capability/trip ledger.
No unseen parent or secret bodyguard is placed at the stream solely to preserve
the original fight or rescue. No secret ancestor visit is part of this household.
