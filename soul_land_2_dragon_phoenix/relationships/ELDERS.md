# Mu En, Xuan Zi and the elder circle

## Mu En — an established grandmaster bond

The author has already made Mu En his grandmaster and a very close figure.
Do not stage their first introduction in Chapter 1 or make the child behave as
though he only knows a distant title. Familiarity can appear in his willingness
to sit nearby, finish a question begun earlier or admit that a solution failed.
These are working scene designs, not a claim that the novel contains this OC.

Mu En can guide an extraordinary child without having the complete universal
Talent manual. What he knows comes from instruction, observation and disclosure.
A bodily improvement is not proof that he understands the Talent’s highest
orders, and closeness does not make Jingheng privy to all hidden history.

His health requires causal treatment. If the child has observed symptoms or
asked about them, record who explained what and what was actually attempted.
Neither “grandmaster must die because canon” nor “adaptation automatically cures
him through affection” is acceptable. The underlying injury and available
mechanisms need primary research before an outcome is chosen.

## Xuan Zi — affection without sanitizing the person

The established elder-circle bond can support a practical lesson, shared food,
a warning or a correction. Do not reduce the man to a joke about eating and
drinking, or make closeness erase his faults and independent decisions.
A working design is that he asks what Jingheng noticed before explaining what
he himself saw. This gives the child room to reason without making the adult
incompetent to advertise the boy’s perception.

## Other elders

An institution is not one omniscient family group. Record each elder’s actual
contact, observations and private knowledge before assigning a special bond.
No generic “all elders know everything” sentence should replace the witness
ledger. The parents’ position can facilitate introductions; it does not mean
everyone has personally trained him.

Warmth, safety and guidance can be real without erasing academy duties. They
must also leave room for Jingheng to spend time with peers, solve ordinary
problems and make decisions of his own.
