# Ma Xiaotao — close now, future undecided

## Fixed boundary

The author makes them already very close and leaves the future open. That is
not permission to turn the bond into a distant acquaintance, a betrothal, an
existing adult romance or a compulsory sibling relationship. The present child
must be depicted in an age-appropriate way.

## Working texture

They know enough of each other to recognize when a familiar answer is evasive.
She may challenge an overconfident claim or tease a workshop habit; he can take
an interest in her day that is not another demand for a demonstration.
A useful scene gives her something she wants independently of him, then shows
how their existing familiarity changes the exchange.

He can be pleased by her attention, irritated when she dismisses a concern or
worried when something is wrong. Adaptation helps him understand and regulate
those feelings without making them synthetic. Her affection or concern must not
be translated into a numerical relationship meter.

## Evil-fire continuity

Because the relationship predates Chapter 1, ask what he has actually witnessed.
Do the parents know he noticed? Has he asked a question, tried an unsuitable idea,
or been told why a particular approach does not address the cause? These are
specific research/design duties. No failed treatment has been invented merely
to keep the source plot unchanged.

Distinguish observing distress, reducing an immediate symptom, supporting
recovery, treating the cause and creating a permanent safe evolution. A bright
phoenix or an evil-dispelling spear does not prove all five. The Talent is his,
not hers; any help must operate through real contact, work or a valid mechanism.

## Future handling

Preserve moments of mutual attention without planting a fixed romantic prophecy.
If a future direction is selected, record the author’s decision and the actual
relationship development. No future pairing is silently decided in an arc plan,
a family joke or a supposedly harmless character label.
