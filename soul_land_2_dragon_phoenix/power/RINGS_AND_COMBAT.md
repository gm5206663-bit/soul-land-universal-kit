## The spear and the integrated style

The ringless spear is not dormant. Grip, alignment, reach, defense, feint, pressure,
committed attack and recovery have been taught and applied. Mother-guided spear
work and father-guided phoenix release combine with elder-corrected movement,
observation, restraint and soul-tool handling.

Phoenix-Spear Concord is the practiced original dual-expression route described
in [power foundations](FOUNDATIONS.md). It uses one cultivated reserve, not
automatic martial-soul fusion. The next spear-ring selection must bring a real
benefit and compatible progression; it should not be postponed indefinitely to
keep a familiar source fight intact.

## Reliable training and use

The opening history includes controlled projectile interception, a marked aerial
course with safe landings, a placed screen protecting a selected training target
and decisive handling of ordinary low-year threats. These are concrete training
scopes, not a claim of unrestricted battlefield dominance.

| Combat question | Scene handling |
|---|---|
| What is known? | Observable cues and actual inference, not hidden-stat vision |
| Where can he act? | Established terrain, reach, clearance and line of sight |
| What is sustainable? | Distinguish burst, repeated, assisted and unassisted output |
| What does the opponent do? | Give actual ability, motives, information and reactions |
| What does the party do? | Teamwork includes other people’s choices, not obedient servants |
| What has improved? | Easier familiar tasks and lower waste must persist after growth |

Seeing a form once is not its hidden energy route. First reproduction, trained
execution, applied mastery and invention remain distinct. No borrowed footwork,
Grey Wolf grade ladder or fixed mastery percentage is imported.
