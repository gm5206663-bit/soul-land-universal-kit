# Soul engineering and actual equipment

## The learning route

Jingheng has authorized workshop instruction under Fan Yu during later childhood.
Fine control, developed perception, memory, reserves and adaptive feedback make
real education productive. Materials are allocated; components fail, work and
are revised. No ancestor secretly teaches him and no blueprint arrives through
inherited memory.

He independently constructs and repairs focused emitters and protective assemblies
within his practiced scope. Material preparation, core inscription, flow matching,
loss detection and safe load tests are actual parts of the work. A revised
flow regulator is repeated successfully rather than declared mastered from one
gesture. Contributions to more-complex supervised prototypes are not portable
finished devices he silently carries.

## Competence, output and certificate

Actual craft, available soul power, finished device function and formal certification
are separate. Fan Yu has assessed repeated construction, operation and repair.
No external numbered certificate is asserted: the later certification lead returned
only its public teaser, not the full exam/grading rule. Absence of a badge does
not erase demonstrated exceptional competence.

Reviewed N10–N12 establish secured testing access, power/control, stationary-shell
versus direct-output tools, targeting, detection range, material cost and aiming.
The reported theoretical tenfold statement is not a universal multiplier on every
one of Jingheng’s tools. Native early Soul Land II has no imported modern computer
or later-era battle-armor solution here.

Clothes, food, water, dressings and maintenance material are finite practical
provision. A stored item must be retrieved. Borrowing, consumption, damage, repair
or gifting changes the record. No soul bone, immortal herb, ancestor artifact or
secret weapon has been silently added.

## Other people still matter

He Caitou knows the workshop acquaintance and actual repeated work. Relevant
teachers cannot reset to thinking it never happened. Fan Yu can value a capable
pupil and still recognize Yuhao’s broad shared spiritual detection as a distinct
opportunity. Precision craft is not that interface. If a related function is
constructed later, it needs actual contact, learning and a recorded mechanism.
