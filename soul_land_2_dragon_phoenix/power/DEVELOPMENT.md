# Development from conception to the opening

## Status of this history

This is accepted **working original-fiction history under delegation**, not a
new author quotation, a canon account of Jingheng or a numerical prediction
uniquely calculated from the Talent. The author can revise the route. Canon
supplies world constraints; the master supplies operating law. Neither contains
a function that uniquely computes an OC’s rank from ancestry.

[development_events.json](../state/development_events.json) owns detailed milestones.
The [opening dossier](../OPENING_BUILD.md) is generated from the current checkpoint.
No milestone is retroactively counted as a published scene.

## The causal route

Months are a planning grid measured from birth, not an absolute year or chosen
birthday. The prenatal entry ends at birth. Later rows include the work between
milestones, not an instantaneous rank gained on a date.

| Month / phase | What actually happens | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Prenatal–0 | Strong native inheritance, appropriate maternal nutrition/rest and regulated exchange | Harmonious body/soul starting substrate; mother does not acquire the Talent |
| 0–60 | Care, play, taught exercises, language, social life and rest | Coordination, attention, emotional regulation, recovery and energy compatibility |
| 72 | Private awakening with parents, Mu En and Xuan Zi | Two approved manifestations and full-innate reading on a developed foundation |
| 73 | Compatible first hunt, final act and absorption | First ring integrated; post-integration rank twelve |
| 85 | Sustained cultivation and second compatible ring | Post-integration rank twenty-two; aimed output separately practiced |
| 98 | Third stage, movement training and workshop foundations | Post-integration rank thirty-two; actual flight coordination and making |
| 113 | Fourth stage with body/soul preparation | First black ring integrated; post-integration rank forty-two |
| 120 | Continued consolidation, twin drills and repeated completed devices | Stable practiced Concord, useful engineering and repair work |
| 128 | Higher-cost cultivation, compatible fifth hunt and integration | Post-integration rank fifty-one and controlled high-output gift |
| 132 | Four further months of cultivated progress and mixed practical tests | The opening checkpoint and loadout |

There is no constant ranks-per-year rule. Later stages cost more; actual supply,
integration, practice quality and usefulness also change. A high rank does not
automatically add professional certification, adult experience or godhood.

## Why the background has substantial effects

The foundation is not an ordinary child with famous surnames. The prenatal and
pre-awakening years develop real supporting body/soul architecture. Good care
and instruction are available, and later breakthroughs improve the connected
whole. Dense demand is paid by actual resources rather than invoked to cancel
all the promised advantages.

Hunts are organized and contained by suitable adults. Jingheng performs the
final act and absorbs through his own supporting capacity. Before black rings,
controlled soul-pressure, will and concentration work supplement strengthened
body/channel structure. Integration, recovery and repeated use precede increased
loads. Adult status cannot absorb the ring in his place or prove compatibility.

The ordinary first-ring benchmark discussed in N09 is comparison data, not a new
universal safe limit. This exceptional authored route needs its supporting
history. Nominal donor ages do not increase by human calendar arithmetic.

## Decisions made rather than left blank

The ordinary resource-poor route was rejected because it erases the premises.
A lower opening is not imposed merely to keep him level with ordinary students.
A still-higher opening is not categorically forbidden, but would require a
revised developmental route rather than a number silently changed in a panel.
The present build is neither a proven optimum nor the Talent’s maximum.

The current integration cycle is phoenix-led. The spear is useful, practiced and
supported by the same developed whole; a compatible next ring route remains a
real future project, not an excuse to leave it unused forever or repeat the
source forest hunt as though he needed his first ring.
