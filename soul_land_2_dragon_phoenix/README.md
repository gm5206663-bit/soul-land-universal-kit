# The Dragon and the Phoenix

**Soul Land II fanfiction by Gaurav Meena · foundation stage · Chapter 1 unwritten**

Yan Jingheng, the native son of Yan Shaozhe and Cai Mei’er, grows up within Shrek
with two parental martial-soul foundations and Adaptation Talent from conception.
He first meets Huo Yuhao while traveling with Tang Ya and Bei Bei. His upbringing
must change what he can do—and what happens around him—without taking away the
other characters’ lives and choices.

been integrated into the subject files above, not kept as competing live copies.
