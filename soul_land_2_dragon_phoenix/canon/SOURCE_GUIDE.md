# Canon sources, claims and access limits

## Source policy

The original Soul Land II novel is the narrative spine. The licensed Wuxiaworld
English edition splits Chapter 1 into 1.1–1.4. Official Tencent manhua and WeTV/
Tencent donghua are separate adaptations, not interchangeable chapter numbers.
The maternal spear is an explicit author adoption; other adaptation differences
need actual comparison, not an unexplained composite canon.

Use accessible official/licensed material or legitimately supplied source material.
Do not bypass payment, account/location restrictions or fetch unofficial chapter
redistributions. Keep concise own-word findings and locations, not full novel
text, scans or downloaded episodes. Wikis are orientation, not scene receipts.

## Claims that remain bounded

Yuhao’s eleven-year age, rank-ten starting point and five cultivated years are
source facts, not the OC’s numerical derivation. Ordinary awakening/full-innate
benchmarks constrain the world without proving every exceptional limit. Distance
translations and hidden genealogy remain reconciliations, not guessed arithmetic.
A later engineer-class title/teaser does not establish an exam or certificate.

## Status labels and chapter footer

**Author-lock:** actual author decision. **Delegated design:** revisable original
choice. **Primary-read:** the specific complete narrative reviewed.
**Official metadata:** listing/reader identity without scene review.
**Partial:** only the stated accessible portion. **Secondary:** orientation.
**Unverified:** genuinely missing evidence. **Not used:** reviewed or located
material not consumed by that chapter’s prose.

For every written chapter record the edition, exact novel parts and beat boundary;
manhua displayed chapter/platform ID and reviewed panel range; donghua episode
ID and reviewed timestamps. Mark not-used or unreviewed explicitly. Explain an
adaptation difference and the author decision supporting it. No guessed numbers
or fiction blueprint can substitute for missing source review.
